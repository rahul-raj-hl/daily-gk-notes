# 📌 Mithila Cultural Council (Mithila Sanskriti Parishad)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Primary Objective**: Promote and preserve **Maithili language, literature, and cultural heritage** in Jharkhand and surrounding regions.
- **Constitutional Status**: Maithili is an Indo-Aryan language listed in the **8th Schedule of the Indian Constitution** (added via the **92nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003**).
- **Literary Figurehead**: **Vidyapati** (known as *Maithil Kokil*), famous for his devotional poems dedicated to Lord Shiva and Radha-Krishna.
- **Cultural Identity**: Deeply tied to the Mithila region (spanning parts of Bihar, Jharkhand, and Nepal) and globally renowned for **Madhubani (Mithila) Paintings**.

---

## 📖 Key Cultural & Linguistic Highlights

### 1. Structural Overview of Maithili Language & Region

| Aspect | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Language Family** | Indo-Aryan (Bihari group) |
| **Primary Region** | Mithila region (Northern/Eastern Bihar, parts of Jharkhand, Southern Nepal) |
| **Script** | Tirhuta / Mithilakshar (historically), Devanagari (currently in official use) |
| **Constitutional Inclusion** | 8th Schedule (92nd Amendment Act, 2003, along with Bodo, Dogri, and Santhali) |
| **Famous Art Form** | Madhubani / Mithila Painting (featuring natural dyes and geometric patterns) |

---

### 2. Major Works & Contributions

- **Vidyapati (1352–1448)**: Authored *Padavali*, *Kirtilata*, and *Kirtipataka*. Revered as the foundational figure of Maithili literature.
- **Jyotirishwar Thakur**: Authored *Varna Ratnakara* (14th century), one of the earliest prose works in any Indo-Aryan language.
- **Nagarjun (Yatri)**: Prominent modern poet and writer who wrote in both Maithili (under the pen name *Yatri*) and Hindi.
