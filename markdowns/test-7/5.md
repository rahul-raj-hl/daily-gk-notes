# 📌 Volcanism & Internal Geomorphic Processes

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Core Cause**: Volcanism is an **endogenic (internal) process** resulting from molten rock (**magma**) rising from the Earth's interior (mantle/crust) to the surface due to pressure differentials and tectonic activity[cite: 1].
- **Magma vs. Lava**: **Magma** refers to molten rock inside the Earth's surface[cite: 1], whereas **Lava** refers to molten rock once it breaks through and reaches the Earth's surface[cite: 1].
- **Key Tectonic Settings**: Occurs primarily at **Divergent boundaries** (mid-ocean ridges), **Convergent boundaries** (subduction zones), and intraplate **Hotspots** (mantle plumes)[cite: 1].
- **Exogenic Exclusion**: External forces like surface erosion, weather/atmospheric disturbances, or glacial movements **do not cause** volcanism[cite: 1].

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Detailed Breakdown

### 1. Mechanism of Volcanism

Volcanism includes all processes by which solid, liquid, or gaseous materials are forced out from the Earth's interior onto its surface[cite: 1].

- **Origin of Magma**: High temperatures and pressure changes in the asthenosphere cause partial melting of mantle rocks[cite: 1].
- **Ascent**: Being lower in density than surrounding solid rock, magma rises upward through fractures and crustal weaknesses[cite: 1].
- **Eruption & Landform Creation**: Upon reaching the surface, lava solidifies to build landforms like composite volcanoes, shield volcanoes, cinder cones, and flood basalt plateaus[cite: 1].

---

### 2. Endogenic vs. Exogenic Forces

| Category | Primary Energy Source | Key Processes | Connection to Volcanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Endogenic Forces** (Internal) | Radioactivity, rotational friction, primordial heat in Earth's interior | Tectonism, Volcanism, Earthquakes[cite: 1] | **Direct Cause**: Drives magma movement and volcanic eruptions[cite: 1]. |
| **Exogenic Forces** (External) | Solar energy and gravitational force | Weathering, Surface Erosion, Glacial Movement, Mass Wasting[cite: 1] | **No Direct Cause**: Shapes surface landforms externally but cannot initiate volcanism[cite: 1]. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Feature | Magma | Lava |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Beneath the Earth's surface (Intrusive)[cite: 1]. | On or above the Earth's surface (Extrusive)[cite: 1]. |
| **Cooling Rate** | Cools slowly, forming coarse-grained intrusive rocks (e.g., **Granite**). | Cools rapidly, forming fine-grained extrusive rocks (e.g., **Basalt**). |
| **Associated Landforms** | Batholiths, Laccoliths, Sills, and Dikes. | Volcanoes, Lava Domes, and Lava Plateaus[cite: 1]. |
