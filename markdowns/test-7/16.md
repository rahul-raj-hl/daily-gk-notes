# 📌 Sarojini Naidu (1879–1949)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **INC Presidency**: First **Indian** woman to become President of the Indian National Congress at the **Kanpur Session in 1925** (*Annie Besant was the overall first woman President in 1917*).
- **First Woman Governor**: Served as the Governor of **United Provinces (now Uttar Pradesh)** from 1947 until her death in 1949.
- **Literary Title**: Popularly known as **"Nightingale of India" (Bharat Kokila)** for her lyrical poetry.
- **Birth & Background**: Born on **13 February 1879** in **Hyderabad** to Aghornath Chattopadhyay and Barada Sundari Devi.

### 2. Major Literary Works

Sarojini Naidu was a prolific poet whose works depicted Indian culture, nature, and patriotism:

* **The Golden Threshold** (1905) – Her first collection of poems.
* **The Bird of Time** (1912) – Contains famous poems like *Guerdon* and *In the Bazaars of Hyderabad*.
* **The Broken Wing** (1917) – Includes poems like *The Gift of India*.
* **The Sceptred Flute** (1948) & **The Feather of the Dawn** (1961 - published posthumously).
