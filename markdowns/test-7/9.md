# 📌 Sjoerd Marijne Appointed Indian Women's Hockey Chief Coach

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **New Appointment**: **Sjoerd Marijne** appointed as the Chief Coach of the **Indian Women’s Hockey Team** by Hockey India[cite: 1].
- **Historic Achievement**: Previously coached the Indian women's hockey team to a **historic 4th place finish at the Tokyo 2020 Olympics** (India's best-ever Olympic performance in women's hockey)[cite: 1].
- **Key Upcoming Target**: **FIH Women’s Hockey World Cup Qualifiers** (8–14 March 2026, Hyderabad, Telangana)[cite: 1].
- **Training Base**: National Coaching Camp setup at **Sports Authority of India (SAI), Bengaluru**[cite: 1].

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Key Highlights

### 1. Re-Appointment Highlights

- **Strategic Objective**: To bolster the team's competitiveness, tactical coordination, and physical conditioning ahead of key international campaigns[cite: 1].
- **Institutional Support**: Joint initiative backed by **Hockey India**, the **Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports (MYAS)**, and the **Sports Authority of India (SAI)**[cite: 1].
- **Major Tournaments**: Focus lies on the upcoming World Cup Qualifiers and preparation for future multi-sport global events[cite: 1].
