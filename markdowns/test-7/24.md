# 📌 Global Conference on Millets (Shree Anna)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Host Venue**: **New Delhi** (Inaugurated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi at Subramaniam Hall, NASC Complex, Pusa).
- **Theme / Identity**: Promoted as **Shree Anna** (Mother of all grains) to position India as the **Global Hub for Millets**.
- **Context**: Organized in line with the UN declaring **2023 as the International Year of Millets (IYM 2023)**, an initiative proposed by India and supported by 72 countries.
- **Key Deliverables**: Release of a customized postal stamp, ₹75 commemorative coin, and a digital compendium of Indian millet standards by FSSAI.

### 1. Classification & Regional Names of Millets in India

| Category | Common English Name | Indian / Regional Name | Key Nutritional & Agronomic Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Major Millet** | Pearl Millet | **Bajra** | Highly drought-tolerant; rich in Iron & Zinc. |
| **Major Millet** | Sorghum | **Jowar** | High dietary fiber; rich in Potassium and Calcium. |
| **Major Millet** | Finger Millet | **Ragi** | Highest **Calcium** content among all cereals. |
| **Minor Millet** | Foxtail Millet | **Kangni / Kakun** | Rich in complex carbohydrates; low Glycemic Index (GI). |
| **Minor Millet** | Kodo Millet | **Kodon** | High antioxidant and fiber content; suitable for diabetics. |
| **Minor Millet** | Barnyard Millet | **Sanwa** | Highest Iron content among minor millets. |

| Strategic Point | Fact / Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Largest Producer** | **India** is the largest producer (~20% of global production) and 2nd largest exporter of millets in the world. |
| **Top Producer State** | **Rajasthan** leads total millet production in India, followed by Maharashtra and Karnataka. |
