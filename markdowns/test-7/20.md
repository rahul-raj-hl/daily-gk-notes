# 📌 COSPAR-ISRO Vikram Sarabhai Medal

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **2024 Recipient**: **Dr. Anil Bhardwaj** (Director, Physical Research Laboratory - PRL, Ahmedabad).
- **Conferring Bodies**: Jointly awarded by **COSPAR** (Committee on Space Research) and **ISRO** (Indian Space Research Organisation).
- **Assembly Location**: Presented at the **45th COSPAR Scientific Assembly** in Busan, South Korea (July 2024).
- **Award Significance**: Recognizes outstanding contributions to space research in developing countries.
