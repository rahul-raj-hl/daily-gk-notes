# 📌 Redox Reactions: Oxidation and Reduction

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Redox Reaction**: A reaction where **Oxidation** and **Reduction** occur simultaneously.
- **Oxidation**: Addition of oxygen / removal of hydrogen / **loss of electrons** / increase in oxidation state.
- **Reduction**: Removal of oxygen / addition of hydrogen / **gain of electrons** / decrease in oxidation state.
- **Reaction Analysis**:
  $$\text{2PbO(s)} + \text{C(s)} \longrightarrow \text{2Pb(s)} + \text{CO}_2\text{(g)}$$
  - **Lead oxide ($\text{PbO}$)** loses oxygen $\rightarrow$ **$\text{PbO}$ is being reduced to $\text{Pb}$**.
  - **Carbon ($\text{C}$)** gains oxygen $\rightarrow$ **$\text{C}$ is being oxidized to $\text{CO}_2$**.
- **Common Exam Trap**: Reactants ($\text{PbO}$ and $\text{C}$) undergo oxidation/reduction, **NOT the products** ($\text{Pb}$ and $\text{CO}_2$).

---

## 📖 Key Definitions & Agents Matrix

### 1. Oxidation vs. Reduction

| Classical & Modern Criteria | Oxidation | Reduction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Oxygen Transfer** | Gain of Oxygen | Loss of Oxygen |
| **Hydrogen Transfer** | Loss of Hydrogen | Gain of Hydrogen |
| **Electron Transfer (OIL RIG)** | **O**xidation **I**s **L**oss of electrons | **R**eduction **I**s **G**ain of electrons |
| **Oxidation Number** | Increases | Decreases |

---

### 2. Identifying Agents in a Reaction

| Term | Role in Reaction | In $\text{2PbO} + \text{C} \rightarrow \text{2Pb} + \text{CO}_2$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Substance Oxidized** | Gains oxygen / loses electrons | **Carbon ($\text{C}$)** |
| **Substance Reduced** | Loses oxygen / gains electrons | **Lead Oxide ($\text{PbO}$)** |
| **Oxidizing Agent** | Causes oxidation (itself gets reduced) | **Lead Oxide ($\text{PbO}$)** |
| **Reducing Agent** | Causes reduction (itself gets oxidized) | **Carbon ($\text{C}$)** |

| Strategic Point | Fact / Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **OIL RIG Rule** | **O**xidation **I**s **L**oss, **R**eduction **I**s **G**ain of electrons. |
| **Reactant Identification Rule** | The substance oxidized/reduced and the agents are **ALWAYS reactants**, never products. |
| **Rancidity & Corrosion** | Everyday examples of slow oxidation reactions in presence of air and moisture. |
| **Redox in Metallurgy** | Carbon ($\text{C}$) or Carbon Monoxide ($\text{CO}$) is widely used as a **reducing agent** to extract metals from oxides. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Statement | Status | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lead ($\text{Pb}$) is getting reduced** | ❌ **Incorrect** | Lead ($\text{Pb}$) is the *product* of reduction. Lead oxide ($\text{PbO}$) is the substance being reduced. |
| **Carbon Dioxide ($\text{CO}_2$) is getting oxidized** | ❌ **Incorrect** | $\text{CO}_2$ is the *product* of oxidation. Carbon ($\text{C}$) is the substance being oxidized. |
| **Carbon ($\text{C}$) is getting oxidized** | ✅ **Correct** | Carbon gains oxygen to form $\text{CO}_2$. |
| **Lead Oxide ($\text{PbO}$) is getting reduced** | ✅ **Correct** | $\text{PbO}$ loses oxygen to form $\text{Pb}$. |

---

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic for Agents: "Opposite Role Rule"**
> - The substance **getting oxidized** acts as the **REDUCING AGENT**.
> - The substance **getting reduced** acts as the **OXIDIZING AGENT**.

### PYQ 1

In the reaction $2\text{PbO(s)} + \text{C(s)} \rightarrow 2\text{Pb(s)} + \text{CO}_2\text{(g)}$, which of the following statements are CORRECT?
(I) Carbon is getting oxidized.  
(II) Lead oxide is getting reduced.  
(III) Lead is getting oxidized.  
(IV) Carbon dioxide is getting oxidized.

- [ ] (III) and (IV)
- [ ] (I) and (IV)
- [x] (I) and (II)
- [ ] (II) and (III)

> Carbon gains oxygen (oxidized) and Lead Oxide loses oxygen (reduced). Statements (III) and (IV) incorrectly name products instead of reactants.

### PYQ 2

Which of the following processes represents Reduction?

- [ ] Gain of oxygen
- [ ] Loss of electrons
- [x] Gain of electrons
- [ ] Loss of hydrogen

> Reduction is defined as the gain of electrons or addition of hydrogen/loss of oxygen.

---

### PYQ 3

In the reaction $\text{CuO} + \text{H}_2 \rightarrow \text{Cu} + \text{H}_2\text{O}$, which substance acts as the reducing agent?

- [ ] $\text{CuO}$
- [x] $\text{H}_2$
- [ ] $\text{Cu}$
- [ ] $\text{H}_2\text{O}$

> $\text{H}_2$ gains oxygen and gets oxidized, thereby acting as the reducing agent.

---

### PYQ 4

The process of addition of oxygen to a substance or removal of hydrogen from a substance is called:

- [x] Oxidation
- [ ] Reduction
- [ ] Corrosion
- [ ] Hydrolysis

> Oxidation in classical terms involves addition of oxygen or removal of hydrogen.

$$\text{MnO}_2 + 4\text{HCl} \rightarrow \text{MnCl}_2 + 2\text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{Cl}_2$$

- [ ] $\text{MnO}_2$
