# 📌 Poona Pact (1932)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Signing Date & Place**: **September 24, 1932** at **Yerwada Central Jail, Pune**[cite: 1].
- **Key Signatories**: Signed on behalf of the Depressed Classes by **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar**[cite: 1] and on behalf of upper-caste Hindus by **Madan Mohan Malaviya** (while **Mahatma Gandhi** was fasting inside jail)[cite: 1].
- **Core Outcome**: Abandoned the **separate electorate** for the Depressed Classes (introduced by Ramsay MacDonald's Communal Award)[cite: 1] in exchange for **increased reserved seats** in provincial and central legislatures under a **joint electorate**[cite: 1].

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Background

### 1. Historical Timeline & Context

- **August 1932 (Communal Award)**: British Prime Minister **Ramsay MacDonald** announced separate electorates for Muslims, Sikhs, Europeans, and the **Depressed Classes**[cite: 1].
- **Gandhi's Fast Unto Death**: Mahatma Gandhi opposed separate electorates for the Depressed Classes, believing it would permanently divide Hindu society[cite: 1]. He began a fast-unto-death at Yerwada Jail on September 20, 1932[cite: 1].
- **The Agreement**: To save Gandhi's life, leaders negotiated with Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, culminating in the signing of the Poona Pact on September 24, 1932[cite: 1].

---

### 2. Electorate System Comparison

| Feature | Communal Award (Separate Electorate) | Poona Pact (Joint Electorate with Reservation) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Voting Right** | Only members of the Depressed Classes voted for Depressed Class candidates[cite: 1]. | All voters in a constituency voted for candidates reserved for Depressed Classes[cite: 1]. |
| **Provincial Seats** | **71 seats** allotted[cite: 1]. | **148 seats** reserved (more than double)[cite: 1]. |
| **Central Legislature** | Standard allocation. | **18% of total seats** reserved in the Central Legislature[cite: 1]. |
| **Primary Stage** | Single-tier voting[cite: 1]. | Two-tier primary election by Depressed Class voters to panel 4 candidates[cite: 1]. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Term / Event | Key Concept | Difference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Separate Electorate** | Only voters of a specific community vote for candidates of that community[cite: 1]. | Introduced by Morley-Minto (1909) & Communal Award (1932)[cite: 1]. |
| **Joint Electorate** | All voters vote for candidates, even if a seat is reserved for a specific category[cite: 1]. | Adopted under the Poona Pact for Depressed Classes[cite: 1]. |
