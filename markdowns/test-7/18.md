# 📌 Black Soil (Regur / Black Cotton Soil)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Formation**: Formed by the weathering of **basaltic lava rocks** (Deccan Trap region).
- **Distribution**: Covers ~15% of India's land area across the **Deccan Plateau** (Maharashtra, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh).
- **Moisture Retention**: Known for high water-retaining capacity; develops deep cracks during summer (*self-ploughing property*).
- **Mineral Profile**:
  - **Rich in**: Calcium carbonate, **Lime, Iron, Magnesium, Alumina, and Potash**.
  - **Deficient in**: **Phosphorus, Nitrogen, and Organic matter (Humus)**.
- **Key Crop**: Highly suited for **Cotton**, Sugarcane, Tobacco, and Groundnut.

---

## 📖 Key Properties & Mineral Matrix

### 1. Soil Composition & Mineral Profile

| Category | Elements Present / Status |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Abundant Minerals** | Lime ($CaCO_3$), Iron, Magnesium, Alumina, Potash |
| **Deficient Elements** | **Phosphorus**, **Nitrogen**, **Organic Matter (Humus)** |
| **Texture** | Fine-grained / Clayey (~50% clay content) |
| **Special Characteristic** | "Self-Ploughing" (expands when wet, contracts/cracks when dry) |

---

### 2. Geographic Distribution

- **Deccan Trap Region**: Major parts of Maharashtra, Malwa Plateau (MP), Saurashtra & Kutch (Gujarat).
- **River Basins**: Basins of Godavari, Krishna, Narmada, and Tapi rivers.

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Strategic Point | Fact / Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Alternative Names** | Regur Soil, Black Cotton Soil, Tropical Black Earth, Tropical Chernozem. |
| **Color Origin** | Black color is due to the presence of **Titaniferous Magnetite** and iron compounds. |
| **Self-Ploughing Mechanism** | Deep cracks form during dry seasons, allowing aeration of the soil. |
| **pH Nature** | Slightly alkaline to neutral in nature (pH ~7.0–8.5). |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Soil Type | Key Mineral Richness | Key Deficiencies | Major Crop |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Black Soil** | **Lime, Iron, Magnesium, Alumina** | Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Humus | **Cotton** |
| **Alluvial Soil** | Potash, Lime | Nitrogen, Phosphorus | Wheat, Rice |
| **Red Soil** | Iron (gives red color) | Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Lime, Humus | Millets, Pulses |
| **Laterite Soil** | Iron, Aluminum | Lime, Silica, Nitrogen, Potash | Tea, Coffee, Cashew |
