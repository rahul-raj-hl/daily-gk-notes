# 📌 Climate Resilient Watershed Programme & NAFCC

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Primary Initiative**: **Climate Resilient Watershed Programme** operates under the **National Adaptation Fund for Climate Change (NAFCC)**[cite: 1].
- **Core Focus**: Community-led **water harvesting**, groundwater recharge, soil moisture conservation, and climate-resilient agriculture in semi-arid and drought-prone regions[cite: 1].
- **About NAFCC**: Established in **2015** under the **Ministry of Environment, Forest, and Climate Change (MoEFCC)**[cite: 1].
- **Implementing Agency**: **NABARD** (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development) acts as the National Implementing Entity (NIE) for NAFCC projects.

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Structural Breakdown

### 1. Key Features of the Climate Resilient Watershed Programme

The programme targets vulnerable ecological zones to mitigate drought impact and ensure sustainable water management[cite: 1].

- **Community-Led Water Harvesting**: Focuses on building village-level water security through rainwater harvesting structures, check dams, and farm ponds[cite: 1].
- **Resource Management**: Promotes soil moisture preservation and sustainable cropping patterns to withstand climate variations[cite: 1].
- **Grassroots Participation**: Engages local communities in planning, construction, and maintenance to ensure long-term operational sustainability[cite: 1].

| **Launch Year** | **2015** (Central Sector Scheme)[cite: 1] |

| **Target Sectors** | Agriculture, Water Resources, Forestry, Livestock, and Coastal Ecosystems[cite: 1] |
