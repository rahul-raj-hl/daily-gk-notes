# 📌 Vedanta Philosophy: Advaita & Dvaita Schools

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Advaita Vedanta**: Non-dualistic philosophy founded by **Adi Shankaracharya** (8th Century CE)[cite: 1]. Asserts that the individual self (**Atman**) and the supreme reality (**Brahman**) are identical[cite: 1].
- **Dvaita Vedanta**: Dualistic philosophy founded by **Madhvacharya** (13th Century CE)[cite: 1]. Asserts that God (**Vishnu/Brahman**) and individual souls (**Atman**) are eternally separate entities[cite: 1].
- **Prasthanatrayi**: The three foundational texts of Vedanta commented on by both masters—**Upanishads**, **Bhagavad Gita**, and **Brahma Sutras**[cite: 1].

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Comparative Matrix

### 1. The Core Philosophy

Vedanta (meaning the "end or culmination of the Vedas") focuses on the relationship between **Brahman** (Ultimate Reality), **Atman** (Individual Soul), and **Jagat** (The Material World).

- **Advaita (Non-Dualism)**: The world is an illusion (**Maya**); liberation (*Moksha*) is achieved through Knowledge (**Jnana Yoga**) when one realizes *Aham Brahmasmi* ("I am Brahman")[cite: 1].
- **Dvaita (Dualism)**: God is the supreme ruler; liberation is achieved through devotion (**Bhakti Yoga**) and grace of Lord Vishnu[cite: 1].

---

### 2. Sub-Schools of Vedanta Comparison Table

| Philosophy / Sub-School | Key Proponent | Century | Core Doctrine (Atman vs Brahman) | Primary Path to Moksha |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Advaita** (Non-Dualism) | **Adi Shankaracharya**[cite: 1] | 8th Century CE[cite: 1] | Atman and Brahman are **identical** (One)[cite: 1] | **Jnana** (Knowledge) |
| **Vishishtadvaita** (Qualified Non-Dualism) | **Ramanujacharya** | 11th Century CE | Atman is part of Brahman, but distinct in qualities | **Bhakti** (Devotion) |
| **Dvaita** (Dualism) | **Madhvacharya**[cite: 1] | 13th Century CE[cite: 1] | Atman and Brahman are **completely distinct**[cite: 1] | **Bhakti** (Surrender to Vishnu)[cite: 1] |
| **Shuddhadvaita** (Pure Non-Dualism) | **Vallabhacharya** | 15th-16th Century CE | World is real; Atman & Brahman are pure and identical | **Pushtimarga** (Grace/Devotion) |
| **Dvaitadvaita** (Dualistic Non-Dualism) | **Nimbarkacharya** | 12th Century CE | Soul is both different and non-different from God | **Bhakti** (Radha-Krishna) |
