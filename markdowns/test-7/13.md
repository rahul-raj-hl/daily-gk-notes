# 📌 Kadalekai Parishe & Cultural Fairs

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Festival Name**: **Kadalekai Parishe** (Traditional Groundnut Fair)[cite: 1].
- **Location**: **Basavanagudi, Bengaluru, Karnataka**[cite: 1].
- **Associated Temples**: **Bull Temple (Dodda Basavana Gudi)** and **Dodda Ganesha Temple**[cite: 1].
- **Deity**: **Nandi Basava** (Sacred Bull of Lord Shiva), worshiped as the guardian deity of agriculture and fertility[cite: 1].
- **Season/Timing**: Celebrated during the **Kartika month** (October–November), coinciding with the groundnut harvest[cite: 1].

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Cultural Breakdown

### 1. Key Highlights of Kadalekai Parishe

- **Historical Origin**: Dates back over 500 years to the **Vijayanagara Empire** era[cite: 1].
- **Tradition**: Local farmers offer their first harvest of groundnuts (*kadalekai*) to Nandi to seek blessings for crop protection, prosperity, and soil fertility[cite: 1].
- **Sacred Sequence**: Devotees conventionally pay homage at the **Dodda Ganesha Temple** before visiting the main **Bull Temple**[cite: 1].
