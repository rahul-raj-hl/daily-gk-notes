# 📌 Khaled el-Enany & UNESCO Overview

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **New Leadership**: **Khaled el-Enany** (former Tourism and Antiquities Minister of Egypt) nominated/appointed as the **Director-General** of UNESCO[cite: 1].
- **About UNESCO**: Founded on **16 November 1945** to promote international peace and security through education, science, culture, and communication[cite: 1].
- **Headquarters**: **Paris, France**.
- **India’s Association**: Founding member since **1946** (formalized full membership in 1948)[cite: 1]; hosts two UNESCO Category-1/regional offices in New Delhi[cite: 1].

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Structural Breakdown

### 1. Key Highlights of UNESCO Leadership & Governance

- **Director-General Designation**: Khaled el-Enany was selected by UNESCO's Executive Board for his prominent background in cultural heritage preservation and Egyptology[cite: 1].
- **Core Mission**: Enhancing global cooperation across primary pillars: **Education, Natural & Social Sciences, Culture, and Communication/Information**[cite: 1].
- **Major Publications**:
  - *Global Education Monitoring Report* (GEM Report)[cite: 1]
  - *UNESCO Science Report*[cite: 1]
  - *State of the World’s Forests* (jointly with FAO)
