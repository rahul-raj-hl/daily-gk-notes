# 📌 Gupta Empire: The Golden Age of India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Historical Designation**: The Gupta period (approx. 320–550 CE) is widely recognized as the **Golden Age of India**[cite: 2].
- **Core Pillars**: Marked by extraordinary achievements in **Sanskrit literature, mathematics, astronomy, art, and temple architecture**[cite: 2].
- **Key Scholars**: **Aryabhata** (astronomy/math), **Varahamihira** (astronomy), and **Kalidasa** (Sanskrit literature)[cite: 2].
- **Key Monuments**: Ajanta Cave Paintings (narrative murals), Dashavatara Temple (Deogarh), and the Iron Pillar of Delhi[cite: 2].

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Cultural Breakdown

### 1. Contributions of the Gupta Period

- **Science & Mathematics**: **Aryabhata** authored *Aryabhatiya* (concepts of zero, pi approximation, rotation of Earth) and **Varahamihira** authored *Brihat Samhita*[cite: 2].
- **Sanskrit Literature**: **Kalidasa** composed classics like *Abhijnanashakuntalam*, *Meghaduta*, and *Raghuvamsha*[cite: 2]. **Vishakhadatta** wrote *Mudrarakshasa* and *Devichandraguptam*.
- **Art & Architecture**: Introduction of classical Nagara style temple architecture, intricate bronze sculptures (Sultanganj Buddha), and mural art at the **Ajanta Caves**[cite: 2].
- **Religious Harmony**: Coexistence and flourishing of **Hinduism, Buddhism, and Jainism** under tolerant patronage[cite: 2].

---

### 2. Major Rulers & Achievements

| Ruler | Title / Epithet | Major Achievement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chandragupta I** | *Maharajadhiraja* | Founded the Gupta Era (319–320 CE); married Lichchhavi princess Kumaradevi. |
| **Samudragupta** | *"Napoleon of India"* (V.A. Smith) | *Prayag Prashasti* (Allahabad Pillar inscription) composed by court poet Harisena; expert Veena player. |
| **Chandragupta II** | *Vikramaditya* | Defeated the Shakas; Chinese traveler **Fa-Hien** visited India during his reign; hosted Nine Gems (*Navaratnas*)[cite: 2]. |
| **Kumaragupta I** | *Mahendraditya* | Founded the world-famous **Nalanda University**. |
| **Skandagupta** | *Vikramaditya* | Successfully repulsed the invasion of the **Hunas**. |
