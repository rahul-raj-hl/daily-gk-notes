# 📌 Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) & Border Security Forces

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Designated Jurisdiction**: Primary responsibility is guarding the open **Indo-Nepal** and **Indo-Bhutan** borders[cite: 1].
- **Administrative Control**: Functions directly under the **Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)**, Government of India[cite: 1].
- **Year of Establishment**: Raised in **1963** (post the 1962 Indo-China War) as the *Special Service Bureau*[cite: 1].
- **Motto**: *Service, Security, and Brotherhood* (*Seva, Suraksha aur Bandhutva*).

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Structural Breakdown

### 1. Key Operational Roles of SSB

SSB operates as an elite border guarding unit and a frontline paramilitary organization[cite: 1].

- **Border Surveillance**: Prevents illegal crossings, human trafficking, contraband smuggling, and cross-border criminal activity along open borders[cite: 1].
- **Civic Action Programs (CAP)**: Conducts community assistance, medical camps, and infrastructure support to build goodwill and intelligence networks among border citizens[cite: 1].
- **First Line of Defense**: Maintains vigilance and security readiness during peace and wartime along assigned international frontiers[cite: 1].

### 2. Central Armed Police Forces (CAPF) & Border Allocations

| Force | Primary Deployment Area / Mandate | Governing Ministry |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB)** | **Indo-Nepal & Indo-Bhutan Borders**[cite: 1] | Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)[cite: 1] |
| **Border Security Force (BSF)** | Indo-Pakistan & Indo-Bangladesh Borders | Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) |
| **Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP)** | Indo-China (Tibetan) Border | Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) |
| **Assam Rifles (AR)** | Indo-Myanmar Border & North-East Internal Security | Operational: Indian Army / Admin: MHA |
| **Central Industrial Security Force (CISF)** | Public Sector Undertakings, Airports, Metro Rail, Critical Infra[cite: 1] | Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)[cite: 1] |
| **Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF)** | Internal Security, Anti-Naxal Operations, Counter-Insurgency | Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) |
