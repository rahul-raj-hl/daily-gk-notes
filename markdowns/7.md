# 📌 Constitutional Writs

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Writs are constitutional remedies issued by the Supreme Court and High Courts.
- They protect Fundamental Rights and ensure lawful administration.
- The Constitution provides five types of writs.
- Habeas Corpus is the most important writ for protecting personal liberty.

---

## 📖 Constitutional Basis

### Article 32

*One-liner:* Supreme Court's power to enforce Fundamental Rights.

- Can issue all five writs.
- Called the "Heart and Soul of the Constitution" by Dr. B. R. Ambedkar.

### Article 226

*One-liner:* High Courts have wider writ jurisdiction.

- Can enforce Fundamental Rights.
- Can also enforce other legal rights.
- Wider scope than Article 32.

---

## ⚖️ Types of Writs

### 🧍 Habeas Corpus

*Meaning:* **"You may have the body."**

*One-liner:* Protects a person from illegal detention.

- Court orders the detained person to be produced.
- If detention is unlawful, immediate release is ordered.
- Can be issued against the Government, police, or even a private individual.

---

### 📝 Mandamus

*Meaning:* **"We Command."**

*One-liner:* Directs a public authority to perform its legal duty.

- Issued against public officials or public bodies.
- Cannot be issued against the President or Governor for official acts.
- Cannot be issued against private individuals.

---

### ❌ Certiorari

*Meaning:* **"To be Certified."**

*One-liner:* Quashes an order of a lower court or tribunal.

- Corrects jurisdictional errors.
- Issued after an order has been passed.

---

### 🛑 Prohibition

*Meaning:* **"To Forbid."**

*One-liner:* Stops a lower court or tribunal from exceeding its jurisdiction.

- Preventive in nature.
- Issued before the final order is passed.

---

### 👑 Quo Warranto

*Meaning:* **"By What Authority?"**

*One-liner:* Challenges a person's legal right to hold a public office.

- Prevents illegal occupation of public offices.
- Can be filed by any citizen.

---

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Writ | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Habeas Corpus | Illegal detention |
| Mandamus | Perform public duty |
| Certiorari | Quash illegal order |
| Prohibition | Stop proceedings |
| Quo Warranto | Challenge public office |

---

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Topic | Remember |
|------|----------|
| Article 32 | Supreme Court (Fundamental Rights only) |
| Article 226 | High Courts (Fundamental Rights + Other Legal Rights) |
| Certiorari | Quashes completed order |
| Prohibition | Stops proceedings before decision |
| Habeas Corpus | Personal liberty |

---

## 🎯 Memory Trick

👉 **"Body – Duty – Cancel – Stop – Authority"**

- 🧍 Body → Habeas Corpus
- 📝 Duty → Mandamus
- ❌ Cancel Order → Certiorari
- 🛑 Stop Proceedings → Prohibition
- 👑 Authority → Quo Warranto

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Which writ is issued to protect personal liberty?

- [x] Habeas Corpus
- [ ] Mandamus
- [ ] Certiorari
- [ ] Quo Warranto

> **Explanation:** Habeas Corpus protects individuals from illegal or arbitrary detention.

---

### Q2

Which Article empowers the Supreme Court to issue writs for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights?

- [ ] Article 226
- [ ] Article 356
- [x] Article 32
- [ ] Article 368

> **Explanation:** Article 32 guarantees the right to constitutional remedies through the Supreme Court.

---

### Q3

Which writ commands a public authority to perform its legal duty?

- [ ] Habeas Corpus
- [x] Mandamus
- [ ] Prohibition
- [ ] Certiorari

> **Explanation:** Mandamus directs a public authority to discharge its legal obligation.

---

### Q4

Which writ is preventive in nature?

- [ ] Habeas Corpus
- [ ] Mandamus
- [ ] Certiorari
- [x] Prohibition

> **Explanation:** Prohibition prevents a lower court or tribunal from exceeding its jurisdiction before a final decision.

---

### Q5

Which writ questions the authority of a person holding a public office?

- [ ] Certiorari
- [ ] Habeas Corpus
- [x] Quo Warranto
- [ ] Mandamus

> **Explanation:** Quo Warranto challenges the legal authority of a person occupying a public office.
