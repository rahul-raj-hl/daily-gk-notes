# 📌 Ashoka's Policy of Dhamma & Propagation Methods

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **All of the above**.
- **Nature of Ashoka's Dhamma**: Not a formal religion or dogmatic creed; it did not involve worship of a specific deity or performance of sacrifices. It was a **universal code of moral conduct, social ethics, and humanism** inspired by the teachings of the Buddha.
- **Paternal Conception of Kingship**: Ashoka viewed his subjects as his children (*"All men are my children"* — Separate Kalinga Edict I), believing it was his duty as a father to instruct and care for their welfare.
- **Key Propagation Methods**:
  1. **Appointment of Special Officers**: Created the office of **Dhamma Mahamattas** (Major Rock Edict V) to preach and enforce moral conduct.
  2. **Inscriptions on Rock and Pillar Edicts**: Inscribed messages in the vernacular language (**Prakrit**) using scripts easily understood by common people (**Brahmi, Kharosthi, Aramaic, and Greek**).
  3. **Oral Recitation**: Directed state officials (**Yuktas, Rajukas, and Pradesikas**) to read out the inscriptions to illiterate subjects during regular administrative tours.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Core Principles of Ashoka's Dhamma

*The moral framework outlined across the Major Rock Edicts:*

- **Ahimsa (Non-violence)**: Strict prohibition of animal slaughter for sacrificial rituals and in the royal kitchen (Major Rock Edict I).
- **Social Ethics & Tolerance**:
  - Respect and obedience towards parents, elders, and teachers.
  - Proper and humane treatment of slaves, servants, and the poor.
  - Generosity and alms to Brahmanas, Shramanas, and ascetics.
  - Religious harmony: Respect for all religious sects and avoidance of praising one's own faith while condemning others (Major Rock Edict XII).
- **Public Welfare (Lokahit)**: Construction of rest houses, digging of wells, planting shade trees along highways, and establishing botanical medicinal centres for humans and animals (Major Rock Edict II).

---

### 2. Mechanisms Used to Spread Dhamma

*Administrative and communicative channels deployed across the empire:*

#### A. The Office of Dhamma Mahamatta
- Created in the **14th year of his coronation** (documented in **Major Rock Edict V**).
- Entrusted with propagating Dhamma, resolving moral/family disputes, fostering inter-faith goodwill, and checking arbitrary imprisonment or abuse by local magistrates.

#### B. The Ashokan Edicts (Epigraphy)
- **Language**: Mostly composed in **Prakrit** (Magadhi variant) for widespread comprehension.
- **Scripts Used**:
  - **Brahmi Script**: Used in the vast majority of the Indian subcontinent (read left to right).
  - **Kharosthi Script**: Used in the Northwestern frontier (e.g., Mansehra and Shahbazgarhi in modern Pakistan, read right to left).
  - **Aramaic and Greek Scripts**: Used in the extreme northwest and Afghanistan (e.g., Kandahar Bilingual Inscription).
- **Placement**: Engraved on prominent public mountain faces, crossroads, pilgrimage routes, and highly polished monolithic sandstone pillars (Chunar sandstone).

#### C. Dhamma Yatras & Missionary Missions
- Replaced royal pleasure and hunting tours (*Vihara Yatras*) with tours of righteousness (**Dhamma Yatras** — Major Rock Edict VIII).
- Sent international peace and diplomatic missions (recorded in Major Rock Edict XIII) to Hellenistic kings (Antiochus II of Syria, Ptolemy II of Egypt, Antigonus Gonatas of Macedonia, Magas of Cyrene, Alexander of Epirus) and dispatched his children, **Mahendra and Sanghamitra**, to **Sri Lanka (Ceylon)** with a sapling of the sacred Bodhi Tree.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Concept | Historical Fact | Common Trap / Misconception |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nature of Dhamma** | **Universal moral/ethical code** (Ahimsa, respect, tolerance) | Confusing it with a strict orthodox Buddhist religious doctrine with formal rituals |
| **Deity Worship in Dhamma** | **No worship of gods or sacrificial rituals** | Believing Dhamma introduced new religious ceremonies |
| **Name used in Edicts** | Mostly styled as ***Devanampiya Piyadassi*** ("Beloved of the Gods") | Assuming all edicts uniformly used the personal name "Ashoka" |
| **Decipherer of Script** | **James Prinsep (1837)** | Confusing Prinsep with Alexander Cunningham (Father of Indian Archaeology) |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "MAHAMATTAS, MONOLITHS & MORALS MADE ASHOKA'S DHAMMA"**
> - **M**ahamattas $\rightarrow$ Officers appointed to preach
> - **M**onoliths $\rightarrow$ Edicts on rocks and pillars in local scripts
> - **M**orals $\rightarrow$ Code of conduct (no animal sacrifice, respect parents, religious tolerance)
