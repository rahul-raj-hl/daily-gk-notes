# 📌 SI Base Units & The Mole (Amount of Substance)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Amount of substance**.
- **Definition of Mole ($\text{mol}$)**: The mole is the SI base unit used to measure the **amount of substance**.
- **Avogadro's Constant ($N_A$)**: Exactly one mole contains **$6.02214076 \times 10^{23}$** elementary entities (atoms, molecules, ions, or electrons).
- **The 7 Fundamental SI Units**: Length ($\text{m}$), Mass ($\text{kg}$), Time ($\text{s}$), Electric Current ($\text{A}$), Thermodynamic Temperature ($\text{K}$), Luminous Intensity ($\text{cd}$), and Amount of Substance ($\text{mol}$).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The 7 Fundamental SI Base Quantities and Units

*The foundational measurement framework defined by the International System of Units (SI):*

| Base Physical Quantity | SI Unit Name | SI Unit Symbol | Physical Meaning / Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Amount of Substance** | **Mole** | $\text{mol}$ | Quantity containing $6.022 \times 10^{23}$ particles |
| **Thermodynamic Temperature** | **Kelvin** | $\text{K}$ | Absolute measure of thermal energy |
| **Electric Current** | **Ampere** | $\text{A}$ | Flow of electric charge per second |
| **Luminous Intensity** | **Candela** | $\text{cd}$ | Perceived power of light emitted by a source |
| **Mass** | **Kilogram** | $\text{kg}$ | Quantity of matter in an object |
| **Length** | **Meter** | $\text{m}$ | Distance travelled by light in vacuum in $1/299792458\text{ s}$ |
| **Time** | **Second** | $\text{s}$ | Duration of $9,192,631,770$ periods of Caesium-133 radiation |

---

### 2. Supplementary & Derived Units in the Options

- **Temperature**: Measured in **Kelvin ($\text{K}$)** in the SI system (commonly expressed in Celsius $^\circ\text{C}$ or Fahrenheit $^\circ\text{F}$).
- **Pressure of a Gas**: Measured in **Pascal ($\text{Pa} = \text{N/m}^2$)** in SI (also measured in $\text{bar}$, $\text{atm}$, or $\text{torr}$).
- **Electrical Conductivity**: Measured in **Siemens per meter ($\text{S/m}$)** or $\text{mho/m}$.
- **Electrical Conductance**: Measured in **Siemens ($\text{S}$)** or $\Omega^{-1}$.

---

### 3. The Mole Concept & Standard Molar Volume

*Key mathematical formulations of the mole:*

- **Number of Moles ($n$)**:

$$n = \frac{\text{Given Mass } (m)}{\text{Molar Mass } (M)} = \frac{\text{Number of Particles } (N)}{N_A}$$

- **Molar Volume at STP**: At Standard Temperature and Pressure ($0^\circ\text{C}$ and $1\text{ atm}$), $1\text{ mole}$ of any ideal gas occupies **$22.4\text{ Litres}$** ($22.4\text{ dm}^3$).

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

The mole ($\text{mol}$) is used to measure which of the following?

- [ ] Temperature of a substance
- [ ] Pressure of a gas
- [ ] Conductivity of a substance
- [x] Amount of substance

> The mole ($\text{mol}$) is the standard SI base unit that measures the amount of substance containing an elementary entity count equal to Avogadro's constant.

---

### PYQ 2

Which of the following is the SI unit of luminous intensity?

- [ ] Lumen
- [ ] Lux
- [x] Candela
- [ ] Watt

> Candela ($\text{cd}$) is the fundamental SI base unit used to measure the luminous intensity of a light source in a given direction.

---

### PYQ 3

What is the volume occupied by one mole of an ideal gas at standard temperature and pressure (STP)?

- [ ] $11.2\text{ L}$
- [x] $22.4\text{ L}$
- [ ] $44.8\text{ L}$
- [ ] $2.24\text{ L}$

> By Avogadro's law, one mole of any gas occupies exactly $22.4\text{ litres}$ ($22,400\text{ cm}^3$) at STP ($0^\circ\text{C}$ and $1\text{ atm}$).

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Which of the following physical quantities is NOT one of the 7 fundamental SI base quantities?

- [ ] Electric Current
- [ ] Luminous Intensity
- [x] Pressure
- [ ] Thermodynamic Temperature

> Pressure is a derived physical quantity ($\text{Force}/\text{Area}$, measured in Pascals), whereas current, luminous intensity, and temperature are fundamental base quantities.

---

### Q2

The unit 'Siemens' ($\text{S}$) is the SI derived unit for measuring:

- [ ] Electrical Resistance
- [ ] Electrical Resistivity
- [x] Electrical Conductance
- [ ] Capacitance

> Electrical conductance (the reciprocal of resistance, $G = 1/R$) is measured in Siemens ($\text{S}$ or $\Omega^{-1}$).

---

### Q3

How many atoms are present in exactly $0.5\text{ moles}$ of pure Carbon-12?

- [ ] $6.022 \times 10^{23}$ atoms
- [x] $3.011 \times 10^{23}$ atoms
- [ ] $1.204 \times 10^{24}$ atoms
- [ ] $1.505 \times 10^{23}$ atoms

> Number of atoms = $n \times N_A = 0.5 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23} = 3.011 \times 10^{23}\text{ atoms}$.

---

### Q4

Which of the following represents the correct SI unit for Plane Angle and Solid Angle respectively?

- [ ] Degree and Steradian
- [x] Radian and Steradian
- [ ] Gradian and Radian
- [ ] Radian and Candela

> The two supplementary dimensionless SI units are the Radian ($\text{rad}$) for plane angles and the Steradian ($\text{sr}$) for solid angles.
