# 📌 Sundaram Balachander & Famous Indian Instrumentalists

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Veena**.
- **Sundaram Balachander (S. Balachander)**: Renowned Indian **Carnatic classical Veena virtuoso**, film director, actor, and composer.
- **Instrument Specialty**: Played the **Saraswati Veena** in a distinctive style characterized by long continuous glides (*meend* / *gamakas*) pulled across multiple frets on a single pluck.
- **Awards & Recognition**: Awarded the **Padma Bhushan** in 1982 for his immense contribution to Indian classical music.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Classification of Indian Musical Instruments (Natya Shastra)

*Bharata Muni’s ancient four-fold classification (*Chaturvidha Vadya*):*

1. **Tata Vadya (Chordophones / Stringed Instruments)**:
   - Plucked / Bowed string instruments.
   - *Examples*: **Veena**, **Sitar**, **Sarod**, **Santoor**, **Sarangi**, **Violin**, **Surbahar**, **Tanpura**.
2. **Sushira Vadya (Aerophones / Wind Instruments)**:
   - Blown through reeds or air columns.
   - *Examples*: **Bansuri (Flute)**, **Shehnai**, **Nadaswaram**, **Harmonium**.
3. **Avanaddha Vadya (Membranophones / Percussion with Skin)**:
   - Struck drums with animal skin or synthetic membranes.
   - *Examples*: **Tabla**, **Mridangam**, **Pakhawaj**, **Khol**, **Dholak**, **Ghatam** *(percussion pot)*, **Kanjira**.
4. **Ghana Vadya (Idiophones / Solid Instruments)**:
   - Resonant solid metal or wood struck without membranes.
   - *Examples*: **Manjira (Taal)**, **Jaltarang**, **Ghatam**, **Kartal**, **Ghungroo**.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Instrument Variant | Key Maestro | Musical Form / Tradition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Saraswati Veena** | **S. Balachander**, **Doraiswamy Iyengar** | South Indian (Carnatic Music) |
| **Rudra Veena** | **Asad Ali Khan**, **Z. M. Dagar** | North Indian (Dhrupad Hindustani Music) |
| **Vichitra Veena** | **Gopal Krishan**, **Radhika Mohan Maitra** | Fretless plucked Hindustani chordophone |
| **Santoor vs. Sarod** | **Shivkumar Sharma (Santoor)** vs. **Amjad Ali Khan (Sarod)** | Santoor = Struck box zither; Sarod = Fretless plucked lute |
