# 📌 Union Budget 2025–26: Infrastructure & Urban Development Initiatives

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Both Statements 1 and 2**.
- **Statement 1 (Urban Challenge Fund)**: **Correct**. An **Urban Challenge Fund** with an allocation of **₹1 lakh crore** was announced to support bankable projects, modernize cities, and develop them as economic growth hubs through challenge-mode financing.
- **Statement 2 (Modified UDAN Scheme)**: **Correct**. The **Modified UDAN (Ude Desh ka Aam Naagrik) Scheme** was introduced to expand regional air connectivity to **120 new destinations**, including tier-2/tier-3 cities and tourist circuits.
- **Strategic Focus**: Aims to integrate multi-modal transportation, improve transit-oriented urban planning, and strengthen regional economic corridors.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Highlights of Urban Development (Union Budget 2025–26)

- **Urban Challenge Fund (₹1 Lakh Crore)**:
  - Designed on a competitive, challenge-based financing framework for state governments and municipal corporations.
  - Focuses on water supply, sewage treatment, solid waste management, and transit-oriented development (TOD).
  - Promotes "Cities as Growth Hubs" through economic clustering and peri-urban planning.
- **Transit-Oriented Development (TOD)**:
  - Financial incentives for urban planning reforms in 14 large cities with populations over 30 lakh.
  - Formulating frameworks for municipal bond issuance and credit enhancement to make urban local bodies (ULBs) financially self-reliant.
- **PM Awas Yojana – Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0)**:
  - Addresses housing needs of 1 crore urban poor and middle-class families with an investment of ₹10 lakh crore (including central assistance of ₹2.2 lakh crore over 5 years).

---

### 2. Modified UDAN Scheme & Civil Aviation Push

- **Target Coverage**: Expanding air connectivity to **120 underserved and unserved destinations** across India.
- **Focus Areas**:
  - Hilly, northeastern, and island regions to facilitate tourism and emergency medical logistics.
  - Upgradation of existing airstrips, water aerodromes, and helipads.
- **Viability Gap Funding (VGF)**: Continued targeted financial support to airline operators to keep regional airfares affordable for the common citizen.
