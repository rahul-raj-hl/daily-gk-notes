# 📌 Yamuna River System & Major Tributaries

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Chambal River**.
- **Yamuna River (Overview)**: The largest and longest right-bank tributary of the **Ganga** ($1,376\text{ km}$).
- **Origin**: Rises from the **Yamunotri Glacier** on the southwestern slopes of the **Bandarpunch Range** in Uttarkashi district, Uttarakhand.
- **Confluence**: Merges with the Ganga at **Triveni Sangam** in **Prayagraj (Allahabad), Uttar Pradesh**.
- **Primary Right-Bank (Peninsular) Tributaries**: **Chambal, Sindh, Betwa, Ken, Tons**.
- **Direct Ganga Tributaries in Options**:
  - **Gomti River**: Direct left-bank tributary of Ganga (originates at Fulhar Jheel / Gomat Taal, Pilibhit, UP).
  - **Gandak River**: Direct left-bank tributary of Ganga (rises in Nepal Himalayas).
  - **Son River**: Direct right-bank tributary of Ganga (originates at Amarkantak Plateau, MP).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Classification of Yamuna's Tributaries

*Division based on Himalayan (Upper Course) and Peninsular (Plains) affluents:*

#### A. Right-Bank (Peninsular) Tributaries
- **1. Chambal River**:
  - **Origin**: **Janapav Hills** (near Mhow/Indore, Vindhyan Range, MP).
  - **Confluence**: Joins Yamuna at **Pachnada near Etawah / Bhareh (UP)**.
  - **Notable Features**: Famous for **badland topography (Chambal ravines)** and the **National Chambal Gharial Sanctuary**.
  - **Major Dams**: Gandhi Sagar (MP), Rana Pratap Sagar (Rajasthan), Jawahar Sagar (Rajasthan), Kota Barrage (Rajasthan).
  - **Key Tributaries**: Banas, Kali Sindh, Parbati, Shipra, Kunu.
- **2. Sindh River**:
  - **Origin**: Malwa Plateau (Vidisha district, MP).
  - **Confluence**: Merges with Yamuna in Jalaun/Etawah district, UP.
- **3. Betwa River (Ancient Vetravati)**:
  - **Origin**: Raisen/Bhopal district (Vindhya Range, MP).
  - **Confluence**: Meets Yamuna near **Hamirpur, UP**.
  - **Major Dams**: Rajghat Dam, Matatila Dam, Parichha Dam.
- **4. Ken River (Ancient Karnavati)**:
  - **Origin**: Kaimur Hills (Jabalpur/Katni district, MP).
  - **Confluence**: Merges with Yamuna at Chilla village near **Banda/Fatehpur, UP**.
  - **Significance**: Ken passes through **Panna National Park**; forms part of the **Ken-Betwa River Interlinking Project**.

#### B. Left-Bank / Himalayan Tributaries
- **1. Tons River**:
  - Yamuna's **largest Himalayan tributary**; carries more water than the main stream before the plains.
  - Meets Yamuna below **Kalsi near Dehradun (Uttarakhand)**.
- **2. Hindon River**:
  - Originates in the Upper Shivalik range (Saharanpur, UP).
  - Flows entirely as a rain-fed plain river before joining Yamuna near **Noida/Ghaziabad (UP)**.
- **3. Giri & Hanuman Ganga**: Mountain torrents draining Himachal Pradesh and Garhwal.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Question Type | Correct Answer | Common Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Largest Tributary of Ganga** | **Yamuna River** | Ghaghara (largest by water volume discharge) |
| **Largest Tributary of Yamuna** | **Tons River** (by volume) / **Chambal** (longest/largest basin) | Betwa / Ken |
| **Right-Bank Tributary of Ganga** | **Son River** & **Yamuna River** | Chambal (joins Yamuna, not Ganga directly) |
| **Sind vs. Sindh** | **Sindh** (tributary of Yamuna in MP/UP) | **Indus (Sindhu)** (Trans-Himalayan major river) |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which of the following is a tributary of the Yamuna River?

- [x] Chambal River
- [ ] Gomti River
- [ ] Gandak River
- [ ] Son River

> The Chambal River originates in the Vindhya Range and joins the Yamuna near Etawah. Gomti, Gandak, and Son are direct tributaries of the Ganga.

---

### PYQ 2

Which of the following rivers is known for creating extensive gully erosion and badland ravines in Central India?

- [ ] Son River
- [ ] Gomti River
- [x] Chambal River
- [ ] Ghaghara River

> The Chambal basin is characterized by severe accelerated gully erosion, forming the famous Chambal Badlands (Bhihads).

---

### PYQ 3

At which of the following places does the Chambal River merge with the Yamuna River?

- [ ] Prayagraj
- [ ] Hamirpur
- [x] Pachnada / Etawah
- [ ] Kannauj

> The Chambal joins the Yamuna at Pachnada near the borders of Etawah and Bhind districts.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Which of the following dams is NOT constructed across the Chambal River?

- [ ] Gandhi Sagar Dam
- [ ] Rana Pratap Sagar Dam
- [ ] Jawahar Sagar Dam
- [x] Matatila Dam

> Matatila Dam is built across the **Betwa River** in Lalitpur district (UP), while Gandhi Sagar, Rana Pratap Sagar, and Jawahar Sagar are built on the Chambal.

---

### Q2

The historic city of Gwalior and the town of Datia are situated in the drainage basin of which Yamuna tributary?

- [ ] Ken River
- [x] Sindh River
- [ ] Tons River
- [ ] Hindon River

> The Sindh River flows north-northeast through Gwalior, Shivpuri, Datia, and Bhind in Madhya Pradesh before joining the Yamuna.

---

### Q3

The river 'Banas' (also known as *Van Ki Asha*) is a major left-bank tributary of which river?

- [ ] Betwa
- [x] Chambal
- [ ] Yamuna
- [ ] Narmada

> Banas originates in the Khamnor Hills of the Aravalli Range and flows entirely within Rajasthan to join the Chambal River.

- [ ] Ramganga

> The Gomti River is an alluvial plain river originating from Fulhar Jheel (Gomat Taal) in Pilibhit district, Uttar Pradesh.
