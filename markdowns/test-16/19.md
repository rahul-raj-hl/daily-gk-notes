# 📌 The Tripuri Session (1939) & The Subhas Chandra Bose Re-election

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Both (A) and (R) are correct, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A)**.
- **Assertion (A)**: **True**. Subhas Chandra Bose was elected Congress President for the second consecutive time in **1939 at the Tripuri Session** (near Jabalpur, MP), having previously presided over the **Haripura Session in 1938**.
- **Reason (R)**: **True**. Mahatma Gandhi actively endorsed and backed **Dr. Pattabhi Sitaramayya** as his candidate after Maulana Abul Kalam Azad withdrew from the race.
- **Why R is NOT the explanation**: Bose was re-elected *despite* Gandhiji's opposition, not *because* of it. Bose secured **1,580 votes** against Sitaramayya’s **1,377 votes**, leading Gandhiji to famously declare: *"Pattabhi's defeat is more mine than his."*

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Background to the Presidential Contest (1939)

- **Ideological Divide**: 
  - Subhas Chandra Bose represented the radical, left-wing, socialist faction of the Congress advocating an immediate mass ultimatum to Britain as World War II loomed in Europe.
  - The "Old Guard" (Gandhian faction including Sardar Patel, Rajendra Prasad, and J.B. Kripalani) favored a gradualist, negotiated constitutional approach.
- **The Election (January 1939)**:
  - Traditionally, Congress presidents were chosen unanimously by consensus.
  - Bose refused to step aside and insisted on a democratic contest against the Working Committee's nominee, Pattabhi Sitaramayya.
  - Bose won the election by a margin of 203 votes.

---

### 2. The Tripuri Crisis & Bose's Resignation

- **Govind Ballabh Pant Resolution**:
  - At the Tripuri session in March 1939, G.B. Pant moved a resolution expressing full confidence in Mahatma Gandhi and mandating that the Congress Working Committee (CWC) must be constituted strictly in accordance with Gandhiji's wishes.
- **Deadlock**:
  - Gandhi refused to recommend names for the CWC, stating that Bose should form his own executive body according to his program.
  - Bose wanted a united leadership and refused to nominate a one-sided left-wing working committee that lacked the confidence of the Old Guard.
- **Resignation & Successor (April 1939)**:
  - At the All India Congress Committee (AICC) meeting in Calcutta, Bose resigned as Congress President.
  - **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** was elected as the interim Congress President to replace him.
- **Formation of the Forward Bloc (May 1939)**:
  - Following his resignation, Netaji formed the **All India Forward Bloc** within the Congress party at Makur, Unnao (UP) to unite all left-wing and progressive nationalist elements.
