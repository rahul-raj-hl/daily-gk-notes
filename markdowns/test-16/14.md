# 📌 Lakes of India: Wular Lake & Important Water Bodies

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Wular Lake**.
- **The Record**: **Wular Lake** is the **largest freshwater lake in India** (and among the largest in Asia).
- **Location & Origin**: Located in the **Bandipora district of Jammu and Kashmir**; formed due to **tectonic activity**.
- **Primary Inflow / Drainage**: Fed by the **Jhelum River**, which enters the lake from the south and flows out to the north.
- **Historical Name & Feature**: Anciently known as ***Mahapadma Saras***; contains an artificial island called **Zaina Lank** built by King **Zain-ul-Abidin** in the 15th century.
- **Environmental Status**: Designated as a **Ramsar Wetland Site** of International Importance in **1990**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Breakdown of Lakes in the Options

- **1. Wular Lake (Jammu & Kashmir)**:
  - **Type**: Natural freshwater lake of **tectonic origin**.
  - **Key Details**: Acts as a natural flood reservoir for the Jhelum River in the Kashmir Valley.
- **2. Dal Lake (Jammu & Kashmir)**:
  - **Type**: Freshwater oxbow/glacio-fluvial urban lake located in Srinagar.
  - **Notable Features**: Known as the *"Jewel in the crown of Kashmir"*; famous for **Shikaras**, floating gardens (*Radh*), and houseboats.
- **3. Chilika Lake (Odisha)**:
  - **Type**: Largest coastal **brackish water lagoon** in India (and second-largest in the world).
  - **Notable Features**: Designated as India's **1st Ramsar Site (1981)** alongside Keoladeo National Park; home to the endangered **Irrawaddy Dolphin**; Nalabana Bird Sanctuary is situated within it.
- **4. Pulicat Lake (Andhra Pradesh & Tamil Nadu border)**:
  - **Type**: Second-largest **brackish water lagoon** in India.
  - **Notable Features**: Separated from the Bay of Bengal by the barrier island of **Sriharikota** (home to ISRO's Satish Dhawan Space Centre); famous for the annual Flamingo Festival.

### 2. Major Categories of Lakes in India

*Key geographic classification and representative examples:*

| Lake Category | Formation / Characteristics | Prominent Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Freshwater (Tectonic)** | Formed by crustal warping / tectonic movements | **Wular Lake** (J&K) |
| **Freshwater (Oxbow/Fluvial)** | Cut-off river meanders | **Kanwar Lake / Kabartal** (Bihar), **Kolleru Lake** (AP) |
| **Freshwater (Glacial / High Altitude)** | Formed by glacial scour and moraine dams | **Tso Lhamo / Cholamu** (Sikkim), **Pangong Tso** (Ladakh) |
| **Crater / Meteorite Lake** | Impact crater from a meteorite strike | **Lonar Lake** (Buldhana, Maharashtra) |
| **Brackish / Coastal Lagoon** | Sea water enclosed by sand spits and barriers | **Chilika Lake** (Odisha), **Pulicat Lake** (AP/TN) |
| **Inland Saline Lake** | Salt-encrusted endorheic inland basin | **Sambhar Lake** (Rajasthan), **Didwana** (Rajasthan) |
| **Artificial / Man-Made Lake** | Reservoir formed by masonry/earthen damming | **Gobind Ballabh Pant Sagar** (Rihand Dam, UP/MP), **Dhebar Lake** (Rajasthan) |
