# 📌 Human Diseases: Bacterial Pathogens vs. Non-Infectious Conditions

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Asthma**.
- **Nature of Asthma**: A **chronic, non-communicable inflammatory disease** of the respiratory airways triggered by allergens (dust, pollen, animal dander, cold air) or fungal spores (*Aspergillus fumigatus*). It is **not caused by a bacterial pathogen**.
- **Bacterial Diseases in the Options**:
  - **Typhoid**: Caused by the bacterium ***Salmonella typhi*** (confirmed via **Widal Test**).
  - **Plague**: Caused by the bacterium ***Yersinia pestis*** (transmitted by rat fleas, *Xenopsylla cheopis*).
  - **Diphtheria**: Caused by the bacterium ***Corynebacterium diphtheriae*** (prevented by the **DPT vaccine**).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Classification of Common Bacterial Diseases in Humans

*Major infectious diseases caused by bacteria, their causative pathogens, and target organs:*

| Disease | Causative Bacterium | Primary Target Organ / Symptoms | Diagnostic Test / Vaccine |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Typhoid** | *Salmonella typhi* | Small intestine; sustained high fever, stomach pain | **Widal Test** |
| **Plague** (Black Death) | *Yersinia pestis* | Lymph nodes (Bubonic), Lungs (Pneumonic) | Vector: *Xenopsylla cheopis* |
| **Diphtheria** | *Corynebacterium diphtheriae* | Upper respiratory tract; grey false membrane in throat | **Schick Test** / **DPT Vaccine** |
| **Tuberculosis (TB)** | *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* | Lungs; chronic cough, blood in sputum, weight loss | **Mantoux Test** / **BCG Vaccine** |
| **Cholera** | *Vibrio cholerae* | Gastrointestinal tract; severe "rice-water" diarrhea | Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS) |
| **Tetanus (Lockjaw)** | *Clostridium tetani* | Nervous system; painful muscle spasms, locked jaw | **DPT Vaccine** / ATS Injection |
| **Pertussis (Whooping Cough)**| *Bordetella pertussis* | Respiratory tract; severe paroxysmal coughing fits | **DPT Vaccine** |
| **Pneumonia** | *Streptococcus pneumoniae* | Alveoli of lungs; alveoli fill with fluid | Sputum culture |
| **Leprosy (Hansen's Disease)** | *Mycobacterium leprae* | Peripheral nerves, skin lesions | Lepromin test / MDT therapy |
| **Syphilis** | *Treponema pallidum* | Reproductive organs / systemic sores | **VDRL Test** |

---

### 2. Broad Pathogen-Wise Classification of Human Diseases

*Quick reference across various disease types:*

- **Viral Diseases**: AIDS (HIV), Polio, Rabies, Hepatitis, Influenza, Dengue, Chikungunya, Smallpox, Chickenpox, Measles, Mumps.
- **Protozoan Diseases**: Malaria (*Plasmodium*), Kala-azar (*Leishmania donovani*), Amoebiasis (*Entamoeba histolytica*), Sleeping Sickness (*Trypanosoma*).
- **Fungal Diseases**: Ringworm (*Microsporum, Trichophyton*), Athlete's Foot (*Tinea pedis*), Scabies.
- **Allergic / Chronic Non-Communicable**: **Asthma**, Rheumatoid Arthritis, Diabetes, Hypertension.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Disease Name | True Causative Category | Common Misconception |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Asthma** | **Allergic / Chronic Non-Communicable** | Mistaken for a bacterial/viral lung infection |
| **Malaria** | **Protozoan** (*Plasmodium*) | Frequently mistaken for bacterial or viral |
| **Polio** | **Viral** (Poliovirus) | Confused with bacterial paralysis |
| **Rabies** | **Viral** (Rhabdovirus / Lyssavirus) | Confused with bacterial infection from animal bites |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which of the following is NOT a bacterial disease?

- [ ] Typhoid
- [ ] Plague
- [ ] Diphtheria
- [x] Asthma

> Asthma is an allergic and chronic inflammatory condition of the airways, whereas Typhoid, Plague, and Diphtheria are caused by bacterial pathogens.

---

### PYQ 2

The 'Widal Test' is widely utilized in medical diagnostics to confirm which infectious disease?

- [ ] Tuberculosis
- [x] Typhoid
- [ ] Cholera
- [ ] Malaria

> The Widal test detects specific antibodies against *Salmonella enterica* serotype Typhi in patients suffering from typhoid fever.

---

### PYQ 3

Which of the following combined vaccines is administered to infants to provide immunity against Diphtheria, Whooping Cough, and Tetanus?

- [ ] BCG Vaccine
- [ ] MMR Vaccine
- [x] DPT Vaccine
- [ ] OPV

> The DPT triple-antigen vaccine immunizes against *Diphtheria*, *Pertussis* (whooping cough), and *Tetanus*.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Which of the following human diseases is caused by the bacterium *Mycobacterium leprae* and primarily damages peripheral nerves?

- [ ] Syphilis
- [ ] Anthrax
- [x] Leprosy (Hansen's Disease)
- [ ] Tetanus

> Leprosy, also named Hansen's Disease, is a chronic granulomatous bacterial infection affecting the skin, peripheral nerves, and upper respiratory tract.

---

### Q2

The vector responsible for transmitting the bubonic plague bacterium (*Yersinia pestis*) to humans is:

- [ ] Female Anopheles mosquito
- [ ] Sandfly (*Phlebotomus*)
- [x] Xenopsylla cheopis (Rat flea)
- [ ] Tsetse fly (*Glossina*)

> Bubonic plague spreads to humans primarily through the bites of infected rodent fleas (*Xenopsylla cheopis*).

---

### Q3

The BCG (*Bacillus Calmette–Guérin*) vaccine is administered to provide active immunization against which bacterial disease?

- [ ] Cholera
- [x] Tuberculosis
- [ ] Diphtheria
- [ ] Pertussis

> The BCG vaccine is an attenuated live bacterial vaccine developed to protect against *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*.

---

### Q4

Which of the following diseases is caused by a Protozoan and NOT by a bacterium?

- [ ] Pneumonia
- [ ] Cholera
- [ ] Plague
- [x] Kala-azar

> Kala-azar (Visceral Leishmaniasis) is caused by the protozoan parasite *Leishmania donovani* and transmitted by sandflies.
