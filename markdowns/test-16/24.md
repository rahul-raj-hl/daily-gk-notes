# 📌 Article 22: Protection Against Arrest and Detention

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **1 only**.
- **Statement 1 is Correct**: **Article 22** grants protection against arrest and detention in certain cases.
- **Statement 2 is Incorrect**: Article 22 is available to **both citizens and foreigners/aliens** (with the exception of **enemy aliens**, who are excluded from ordinary punitive protections).
- **Two Broad Parts of Article 22**:
  1. **Punitive Detention (Ordinary Criminal Law)**: Protection for a person arrested after committing an alleged offence.
  2. **Preventive Detention**: Detention of a person without a formal trial to prevent future unlawful acts.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Safeguards under Ordinary Law (Punitive Detention — Art 22(1) & 22(2))

*Rights guaranteed to an arrested individual under ordinary criminal procedure:*

- **Right to be Informed**: Must be informed of the grounds of arrest as soon as possible.
- **Right to Consult Legal Counsel**: Right to consult and be defended by a legal practitioner of choice.
- **Right to be Produced before a Magistrate**: Must be produced before the nearest Judicial Magistrate within **24 hours** of arrest (excluding journey time).
- **Right to Release after 24 Hours**: Cannot be detained in custody beyond 24 hours without explicit authorization from the Magistrate.

> **Exceptions to Punitive Safeguards (Article 22(3))**:
> - These safeguards do **NOT** apply to:
>   1. An **enemy alien** (citizen of a country at war with India).
>   2. A person arrested or detained under any law providing for **preventive detention**.

---

### 2. Safeguards under Preventive Detention (Art 22(4) to 22(7))

*Constitutional limits on preventive detention laws:*

- **Maximum Initial Detention**: Cannot exceed **3 months** unless an **Advisory Board** (consisting of persons who are, have been, or are qualified to be High Court judges) reports that there is sufficient cause for extended detention.
- **Communication of Grounds**: The grounds of detention must be communicated to the detenu at the earliest (unless the detaining authority considers it against public interest to disclose facts).
- **Right of Representation**: The detenu must be afforded the earliest opportunity to make a representation against the detention order.

---

### 3. Fundamental Rights: Citizens Only vs. Both Citizens and Foreigners

*Essential constitutional distinction:*

| Rights Available to **Citizens Only** | Rights Available to **Both Citizens & Foreigners** (Except Enemy Aliens) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Article 15**: Prohibition of discrimination on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth | **Article 14**: Equality before law and equal protection of laws |
| **Article 16**: Equality of opportunity in public employment | **Article 20**: Protection against conviction for offences (ex-post facto, double jeopardy, self-incrimination) |
| **Article 19**: 6 basic freedoms (Speech, Assembly, Association, Movement, Residence, Profession) | **Article 21**: Protection of life and personal liberty |
| **Article 29**: Protection of language, script, and culture of minorities | **Article 21A**: Right to elementary education |
| **Article 30**: Right of minorities to establish and administer educational institutions | **Article 22**: **Protection against arrest and detention in certain cases** |
| | **Article 23 & 24**: Prohibition of human trafficking, forced labour & child labour |
| | **Article 25 to 28**: Freedom of religion and conscience |

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Article 22 Provision | Time Limit / Benchmark | Key Authority / Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Production before Magistrate** | **Within 24 hours** | Excludes time necessary for the journey |
| **Preventive Detention limit** | **3 Months** | Extension requires approval of an **Advisory Board** |
| **Advisory Board Composition** | High Court Judge qualification level | Evaluates necessity of detention beyond 3 months |
| **Exception to Article 22 Rights** | **Enemy Aliens** & **Preventive Detenus** | Denied rights under Article 22(1) and 22(2) |
| **Non-applicability of Art 22** | Civil arrests, tax default, deportation | Applies only to criminal/quasi-criminal offences |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Constitutional Right | Scope / Eligibility | Common Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Article 22** | **Both Citizens and Foreigners** | Assuming it is restricted to Indian citizens only |
| **Enemy Alien Status** | Denied Art 22(1) & 22(2) protections | Confusing enemy aliens with friendly foreign tourists |
| **44th Amendment Act (1978)** | Proposed reducing preventive detention to 2 months | **Not yet brought into force** (the operative period remains **3 months**) |

Which of the following statements is/are correct regarding Article 22 of the Indian Constitution?
1. The Fundamental Right conferred by this Article protects persons against arrests and detention in certain cases.
2. This Fundamental Right is guaranteed to the citizens of India only.

Choose the correct answer using the codes given:

- [x] 1 only
- [ ] 2 only
- [ ] Both 1 and 2
- [ ] Neither 1 nor 2

> Statement 1 is correct as Article 22 deals with protection against arrest and detention. Statement 2 is incorrect because Article 22 is available to both Indian citizens and foreigners.

### PYQ 2

An arrested person must be produced before the nearest magistrate within what time period under Article 22(2)?

- [ ] 12 hours
- [x] 24 hours
- [ ] 48 hours
- [ ] 72 hours

> Article 22(2) mandates that every person arrested and detained in custody shall be produced before the nearest magistrate within 24 hours, excluding travel time.

---

### PYQ 3

Which of the following Fundamental Rights in the Indian Constitution is available EXCLUSIVELY to Indian citizens?

- [ ] Article 14
- [ ] Article 21
- [ ] Article 22
- [x] Article 19

> Article 19 (the six fundamental freedoms) is available exclusively to citizens of India, while Articles 14, 21, and 22 are accessible to both citizens and foreigners.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Under Article 22(4), what is the maximum duration for which a person can be held under a Preventive Detention law without obtaining the opinion of an Advisory Board?

- [ ] 1 month
- [ ] 2 months
- [x] 3 months
- [ ] 6 months

> Under current operational provisions, a person cannot be detained under preventive detention for more than 3 months without Advisory Board confirmation.

---

### Q2

The safeguards against arbitrary arrest under Article 22(1) and 22(2) do NOT apply to which of the following categories of persons?

- [ ] Women and children
- [ ] Friendly foreign tourists
- [x] Enemy aliens and persons detained under preventive detention laws
- [ ] Government servants on official duty

> Under Article 22(3), ordinary criminal safeguards do not apply to enemy aliens or persons arrested under preventive detention statutes.

---

### Q3

Under which constitutional authority does Parliament hold the power to prescribe the maximum period for which any person may be detained under preventive detention?

- [ ] Article 20
- [ ] Article 21
- [x] Article 22(7)
- [ ] Article 32

> Article 22(7) empowers the Parliament to prescribe by law the circumstances and maximum duration of detention under any preventive detention statute.

---

### Q4
