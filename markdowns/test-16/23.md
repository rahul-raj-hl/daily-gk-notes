# 📌 Arya Samaj & Swami Dayanand Saraswati (1875)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **1875**.
- **Founding Details**: Established on **10 April 1875** at **Bombay (Mumbai)** by **Swami Dayanand Saraswati** (headquarters later shifted to **Lahore in 1877**).
- **Core Slogan**: *"Go Back to the Vedas"* (*Vedo ki Aur Lauto*), declaring the four Vedas as infallible and the fountainhead of all true knowledge.
- **Key Tenets**: Promoted **monotheism**, rejected idol worship, polytheism, animal sacrifice, priestcraft, child marriage, and hereditary caste hierarchy.
- **Magnum Opus**: Composed the seminal philosophical work ***Satyarth Prakash*** (The Light of Truth) in Hindi in 1875.
- **Socio-Religious Reforms**: Introduced the **Shuddhi Movement** (reconversion of converted individuals back to Hinduism) and championed female education and widow remarriage.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Swami Dayanand Saraswati: Life & Philosophy

- **Early Life**: Born as **Mool Shankar Tiwari** on 12 February 1824 in Tankara (Kathiawad, Gujarat).
- **Spiritual Guru**: Disciple of the blind Vedic scholar **Swami Virjanand Saraswati** at Mathura, who instructed him to purge Hinduism of post-Vedic corruption.
- **Swaraj Ideal**: He was the first to proclaim *"India for Indians"* and use the term **"Swaraj"**, stating that good government is no substitute for self-government.
- **Major Literary Works**:
  - *Satyarth Prakash* (1875)
  - *Veda Bhashya Bhumika* (Introduction to the Commentary on the Vedas)
  - *Rigvedadi Bhashya Bhumika*
  - *Sanskrit Vidhi*

### 3. The DAV vs. Gurukul Educational Split (1892–1893)

*Following Dayanand's death in 1883, the Arya Samaj split into two wings regarding educational policy:*

- **1. DAV (Dayanand Anglo-Vedic) Faction / College Party**:
  - **Leaders**: **Lala Hansraj** and **Lala Lajpat Rai**.
  - **Philosophy**: Favored a synthetic modern curriculum combining Western science and English education along with Vedic studies.
  - **First Institution**: Established the first **DAV High School/College at Lahore (1886)** with Lala Hansraj serving as its honorary principal.
- **2. Gurukul Faction / Orthodox Party**:
  - **Leaders**: **Swami Shraddhanand (Mahatma Munshi Ram)** and Pandit Lekh Ram.
  - **Philosophy**: Favored traditional Sanskrit education and ancient Gurukul-style ascetic discipline, rejecting English medium instruction.
  - **First Institution**: Established the **Gurukul Kangri at Haridwar (1902)**.
