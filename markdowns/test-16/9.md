# 📌 Baisakhi Festival, Sikh History & Regional Harvest Festivals

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **April** (traditionally celebrated on **13th or 14th April** every year).
- **Dual Significance**:
  1. **Historical/Religious Milestone**: Commemorates the formation of the **Khalsa Panth** by the tenth Sikh Guru, **Guru Gobind Singh**, at Anandpur Sahib on **Baisakhi Day in 1699**.
  2. **Agricultural Milestone**: Marks the celebration of the **Rabi crop harvest** (especially wheat) across Punjab, Haryana, and Northern India.
- **Astronomical Context**: Marks the first day of the solar calendar month of *Vaisakha* (Mesha Sankranti / Solar New Year).
- **Cultural Folk Performances**: Celebrated with traditional Punjabi folk dances: **Bhangra** (by men) and **Giddha** (by women).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Historical & Religious Foundation of Khalsa (1699)

- **Event Location**: Anandpur Sahib (Punjab), on Baisakhi day, 13 April 1699.
- **The Panj Pyare (Five Beloved Ones)**: Guru Gobind Singh baptized the first five initiates:
  - *Bhai Daya Ram* (Daya Singh)
  - *Bhai Dharam Das* (Dharam Singh)
  - *Bhai Himmat Rai* (Himmat Singh)
  - *Bhai Mohkam Chand* (Mohkam Singh)
  - *Bhai Sahib Chand* (Sahib Singh)
- **The Five Ks (Panj Kakar)**: Mandated articles of faith for baptized Sikhs:
  1. **Kesh** (Uncut hair)
  2. **Kangha** (Wooden comb)
  3. **Kara** (Iron/Steel bracelet)
  4. **Kachera** (Cotton undergarment)
  5. **Kirpan** (Curved dagger/sword)
- **Titular Suffixes**: Assigned the name **"Singh"** (Lion) to all male Sikhs and **"Kaur"** (Princess) to all female Sikhs.

| **Pana Sankranti (Maha Vishuba)**| **Odisha** | Odia New Year; distribution of *Pana* beverage |
| **Bisu** | **Karnataka (Tulu Nadu)** | Tuluva New Year |
| **Jur Sital** | **Bihar / Mithila** | Maithili New Year |

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Which of the following is NOT one of the 'Five Ks' (Panj Kakar) prescribed by Guru Gobind Singh during the Baisakhi of 1699?

- [ ] Kesh
- [ ] Kangha

- [ ] Kirpan

> The Five Ks are *Kesh* (uncut hair), *Kangha* (wooden comb), *Kara* (iron bracelet), *Kachera* (cotton undergarment), and *Kirpan* (dagger). *Kurta* is not one of the five articles.
