# 📌 Sattriya Dance & Classical Dance Forms of India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Assam**.
- **Origin & Creator**: Introduced in the **15th century CE** by the great Vaishnavite saint and social reformer **Mahapurusha Srimanta Sankaradeva** as part of the *Ekasarana Dharma* (Neo-Vaishnavite movement).
- **Etymology**: Derived from **Sattra** (Vaishnavite monasteries in Assam, particularly on **Majuli Island**), where it was traditionally preserved and performed by male celibate monks (*Bhokots*).
- **Classical Recognition**: Recognized as the **8th Classical Dance of India** by the **Sangeet Natak Akademi** on **15 November 2000**.
- **Key Themes**: Centers around episodes of **Lord Krishna and Radha**, derived from texts like the *Bhagavata Purana*.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Elements and Characteristics of Sattriya

- **Core Repertoire**:
  - **Mati Akhara**: The foundational set of 64 ground-level exercises based on body posture, balance, and agility.
  - **Ankiya Naat (Bhaona)**: Traditional one-act dance-drama plays composed by Sankaradeva and his disciple Madhavadeva (e.g., *Rukmini Haran*, *Parijat Haran*, *Kaliya Daman*).
  - **Ojapali**: An ancient choral storytelling tradition of Assam incorporated into Sattriya performances.
- **Duality of Styles**:
  - **Tandava (Masculine / Energetic)**: Performed with vigor, broad leaps, and power (*Paurushik Bhangi*, such as *Khol Bayan*).
  - **Lasya (Feminine / Graceful)**: Gentle, fluid body movements and lyrical facial expressions (*Stri Bhangi*).
- **Musical Instruments**:
  - **Khol** (two-headed terracotta drum)
  - **Taal** (Manjira / cymbals: *Bhortal*, *Pati Taal*)
  - **Bahi** (Bamboo flute), Violins, and Harmonium.
- **Costumes & Materials**:
  - Made of indigenous Assamese silk: **Pat Silk** (mulberry silk) and **Muga Silk** (golden silk).
  - Traditional Assamese jewellery: *Golpata*, *Dhulbiri*, *Biri Moni*, *Jonbiri*, *Lokaparo*, and *Muthi-kharu*.

### 2. The 8 Classical Dances Recognized by Sangeet Natak Akademi

| Classical Dance | State of Origin | Key Pioneer / Revivalist / Origin Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sattriya** | **Assam** | **Srimanta Sankaradeva** (Sattras of Majuli) |
| **Bharatanatyam** | **Tamil Nadu** | Devadasi tradition / E. Krishna Iyer, Rukmini Devi Arundale |
| **Kathakali** | **Kerala** | Dance-drama with vibrant facial makeup & headdresses (*Kireetam*) |
| **Mohiniyattam** | **Kerala** | "Dance of the Enchantress" / Swathi Thirunal, Vallathol |
| **Kathak** | **Uttar Pradesh / North India** | Gharana system (Lucknow, Jaipur, Banaras) / Pandit Birju Maharaj |
| **Odissi** | **Odisha** | Mahari & Gotipua traditions / Kelucharan Mohapatra (*Tribhanga* posture) |
| **Manipuri** | **Manipur** | Raas Leela / Lai Haraoba / cylindrical skirt (*Kumil*) |
| **Kuchipudi** | **Andhra Pradesh** | Siddhendra Yogi / Brass plate dancing (*Tarangam*) |

*(Note: The Ministry of Culture also recognizes **Chhau** (Purulia, Seraikella, Mayurbhanj) as a classical dance, bringing the total to 9 under its cultural purview).*
