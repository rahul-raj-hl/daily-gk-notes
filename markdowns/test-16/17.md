# 📌 Salkhan Fossil Park & Geo-Heritage Sites in India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Sonbhadra** (Uttar Pradesh).
- **Exact Location**: Officially named **Sonbhadra Fossil Park** (or *Salkhan Murtiya Fossil Park*), located near **Salkhan village on SH-5A** in Sonbhadra district, Uttar Pradesh.
- **Geological Marvel**: Preserves fossilized microbial ring structures called **Stromatolites**, dating back approximately **1.4 to 1.5 billion years** (Mesoproterozoic era / Semri Group of Vindhyan Supergroup).
- **Global Recognition**: Included in **UNESCO's Tentative List of World Heritage Sites** as a world-class geological heritage phenomenon (predating the Yellowstone National Park stromatolite formations).
- **Sonbhadra District Distinction**: The **only district in India that borders four distinct states** (Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, and Bihar).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. What are Stromatolites?

*The earliest evidence of complex biological life on Earth:*

- **Biological Origin**: Layered accretionary structures formed in shallow water by the trapping, binding, and cementation of sedimentary grains by microbial biofilms, specifically **Cyanobacteria (blue-green algae)**.
- **Photosynthetic Milestone**: These primitive photosynthetic microorganisms played a critical role in generating the **Great Oxidation Event (GOE)**, which oxygenated the Earth's early prebiotic atmosphere and made aerobic life possible.
- **Scientific Value**: The fossils preserved at Salkhan are among the world's most intact and extensive visible formations of Mesoproterozoic life.
