# 📌 Government Schemes and Initiatives for Pensioners

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **SAMPANN** stands for **System for Accounting and Management of Pension**.
- Launched by the **Department of Telecommunications (DoT)** under the Ministry of Communications.
- Primary Purpose: **Online management and disbursal of pensions** directly into bank accounts.
- Provides digital grievance redressal (complaint solving) and real-time pension tracking.
- Integrated with **DigiLocker** to store e-PPOs (Electronic Pension Payment Orders) and Form 16.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. SAMPANN Portal Overview

*Integrated digital portal for telecom retirees.*

- **Full Form**: **System for Accounting and Management of Pension**.
- **Nodal Department**: Department of Telecommunications (DoT), Ministry of Communications.
- **Key Function**: Processes, sanctions, and disburses (pays out) pensions directly to bank accounts without middle-tier banks.
- **Digital Services**: Online complaint tracking, digital profile management, and easy download of Form 16 and Gratuity Orders via DigiLocker.

---

### 2. Other Important Pension Portals in India

*Related portals frequently asked in SSC/DSSSB exams.*

- **SPARSH** (*System for Pension Administration - Raksha*): Portal for **Defense Pensioners** (Army, Navy, Air Force) by Ministry of Defence.
- **BHAVISHYA**: Online pension tracking system for **Central Government Civil Pensioners**.
- **Jeevan Pramaan**: Digital Life Certificate (DLC) service using Aadhaar biometric authentication for all pensioners.
- **CPENGRAMS**: Dedicated online complaint portal for central government retirees.
