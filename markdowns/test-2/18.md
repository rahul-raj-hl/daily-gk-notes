# 📌 eSanjeevani Telemedicine Initiative

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **eSanjeevani** is the national **telemedicine** (online medical consultation) platform launched by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.
- Divided into two main verticals (branches): **eSanjeevani OPD** and **eSanjeevani AB-HWC**.
- **eSanjeevani OPD** connects **patients directly with doctors** (Patient-to-Doctor model) via video calls.
- **eSanjeevani AB-HWC** connects rural Health and Wellness Centres with specialist doctors in urban hospitals (Doctor-to-Doctor model) using a **hub-and-spoke model**.
- Developed by C-DAC (Centre for Development of Advanced Computing), Mohali.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Structure of eSanjeevani

*Two-tier national teleconsultation system.*

- **eSanjeevani OPD** (*Patient-to-Doctor*):
  - Virtual platform allowing patients to book appointments and consult doctors online from home.
  - Eliminates physical hospital visits, making primary healthcare accessible in remote areas.
- **eSanjeevani AB-HWC** (*Doctor-to-Doctor*):
  - Links patients visiting Ayushman Bharat Health and Wellness Centres (AB-HWCs) with specialist doctors.
  - Operates on a **Hub-and-Spoke model** (HWCs act as spokes; district hospitals/medical colleges act as hubs).

---

### 2. Strategic Importance

*Strengthening digital health infrastructure.*

- **Nodal Ministry**: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW).
- **Technical Partner**: Designed and developed by **C-DAC Mohali**.
- **Urban-Rural Division**: Bridges the rural healthcare gap by giving villagers access to urban super-specialist doctors without travelling.
- **Pandemic Role**: Played a critical role during COVID-19 by ensuring remote medical consultation without physical virus transmission risks.
