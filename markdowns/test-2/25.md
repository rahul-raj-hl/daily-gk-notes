# 📌 Indigenous Robotics Innovations & SCORP

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **SCORP** is India's **first legged mobile manipulator** (a walking robot with a robotic arm).
- Developed by **xTerra Robotics**, a deep-tech startup incubated at **IIT Kanpur**.
- Combines legged mobility (walking like an animal) with intelligent manipulation (handling tools/objects).
- Designed for complex, dangerous, and unstructured environments like **defence**, **industrial plants**, and **disaster management**.
- xTerra Robotics also developed **SVAN M2**, recognized as **India's first commercial quadruped** (four-legged) robot.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. SCORP Robot Overview

*India's first indigenous legged mobile manipulator.*

- **Developer**: **xTerra Robotics** (incubated at IIT Kanpur).
- **Key Feature**: Combines quadruped legs (four-legged movement) with a dexterous robotic arm (*manipulator*).
- **Environment**: Built to operate in rough, uneven, or hazardous terrains where wheeled or tracked robots cannot go.
- **Applications**: Defense surveillance, bomb disposal, industrial inspection, and search-and-rescue operations during disasters.

---

### 2. Other Key Indian Robotics Developments

*Related milestones in Indian robotics and AI.*

- **SVAN M2**: India's first commercial quadruped (four-legged) robot developed by xTerra Robotics.
- **Vyommitra**: Female humanoid robot developed by **ISRO** for the unmanned Gaganyaan space mission.
- **Daksh**: Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV) developed by **DRDO** for locating and handling explosive materials safely.
- **Robo-Cap**: Robotics initiative for hazardous sewer cleaning (*e.g., Bandicoot robot by Genrobotics*).
