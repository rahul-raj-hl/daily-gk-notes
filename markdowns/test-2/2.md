# 📌 Agricultural Revolutions in India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Agricultural Revolutions are **special programmes (initiatives)** started to increase the production of a particular crop or product.
- Most revolutions are identified by **colours**, making them easy to remember.
- SSC frequently asks **Colour ↔ Product**, **Father ↔ Revolution**, and **Revolution ↔ Sector**.
- Green, White, Yellow, Blue, Pink, Brown, Grey and Golden Revolutions are the most important for exams.
- Learn the **product, father (if any), and memory trick** for quick revision.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### What is an Agricultural Revolution?

*A revolution means a major increase (rapid growth) in the production of a particular agricultural product.*

- Started to increase production and make India self-sufficient (able to meet its own needs).
- Uses better seeds, technology, irrigation and modern farming methods.
- Different colours represent different sectors.

---

### 🌾 Green Revolution

*Focused on increasing food grain production.*

- Product: **Food Grains (especially Wheat and Rice)**
- Period: **1960s**
- Father of Green Revolution (India): **M. S. Swaminathan**
- Helped India become self-sufficient in food grains.

---

### 🥛 White Revolution

*Focused on increasing milk production.*

- Product: **Milk**
- Also called **Operation Flood**
- Father of White Revolution: **Verghese Kurien**
- Made India the world's largest milk producer.

---

### 🌻 Yellow Revolution

*Focused on increasing oilseed production.*

- Product: **Oilseeds**
- Examples: Mustard, Groundnut, Sunflower, Soybean
- Reduced dependence on imported edible oils.

---

### 🐟 Blue Revolution

*Focused on fisheries (fish farming) and aquaculture.*

- Product: **Fish**
- Includes inland and marine fisheries.
- Increased fish production and exports.

---

### 🌸 Golden Revolution

*Focused on horticulture (fruit, vegetables, flowers, honey).*

- Product: **Horticulture**
- Includes fruits, vegetables, flowers and honey.
- Improved agricultural exports.

---

### 🦐 Pink Revolution

*Focused on meat and shrimp production.*

- Product: **Shrimp (Prawns) and Meat**
- SSC usually asks **Pink Revolution = Shrimp**.

---

### 🤎 Brown Revolution

*Focused on leather, cocoa and sometimes non-conventional energy in different contexts.*

- SSC generally accepts **Leather**.
- Some books may mention **Cocoa**.
- Follow the latest SSC trend: **Brown → Leather**.

---

### ⚙️ Grey Revolution

*Focused on fertilizer production.*

- Product: **Fertilizers**
- Increased availability of fertilizers for farming.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Revolution | Don't Confuse With |
|------------|--------------------|
| Green | Food grains, not vegetables |
| White | Milk, not eggs |
| Blue | Fish, not water conservation |
| Yellow | Oilseeds, not pulses |
| Pink | Shrimp/Meat, not flowers |
| Brown | Leather, not fertilizers |
| Grey | Fertilizers, not cement |
| Golden | Horticulture, not wheat |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

### 🌈 Colour Story

🟢 **Green** fields → **Food Grains**

⚪ **White** glass → **Milk**

🔵 **Blue** sea → **Fish**

🟡 **Yellow** mustard → **Oilseeds**

🩷 **Pink** shrimp → **Shrimp & Meat**

🤎 **Brown** shoes → **Leather**

⚙️ **Grey** fertilizer bag → **Fertilizers**

🥇 **Golden** fruits → **Horticulture**
