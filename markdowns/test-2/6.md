# 📌 Folk Dances and Festivals of Sikkim

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Losoong** (also called **Namsoong**) marks the **Sikkimese New Year** and the end of the harvest season.
- Celebrated mainly by the **Bhutia** and **Lepcha** communities of Sikkim.
- **Kali Topi Dance** (Black Hat Dance) is a famous traditional folk dance performed during Losoong.
- **Chaam Dance** is a ritualistic masked dance (dance performed wearing traditional masks) performed by Buddhist monks.
- Celebrated in December/January (10th month of the Tibetan lunar calendar).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Festivals of Sikkim

*Important cultural events and harvest celebrations.*

- **Losoong / Namsoong**: Sikkimese New Year celebrated by Bhutia (Losoong) and Lepcha (Namsoong) communities.
- **Saga Dawa**: Major Buddhist festival celebrating the birth, enlightenment, and death of Lord Buddha.
- **Pang Lhabsol**: Festival unique to Sikkim, celebrated to worship **Mount Kanchenjunga** (the guardian deity/god protecting Sikkim).
- **Lhabab Dhuechen**: Marks the descent (coming down) of Lord Buddha from heaven to earth.

---

### 2. Traditional Folk Dances of Sikkim

*Performances that showcase Sikkim's rich heritage (cultural history).*

- **Kali Topi Dance** (*Black Hat Dance*): Performed during Losoong wearing distinctive black hats; signifies victory of good over evil.
- **Chaam Dance**: Masked dance performed in Buddhist monasteries (places where monks live and pray) during religious festivals.
- **Singhi Chham** (*Snow Lion Dance*): Performers dress up as the mythical snow lion, representing peace and purity.
- **Yak Chham**: Dance performed in honor of the yak (a long-haired high-altitude bull) which is crucial for survival in cold mountains.
- **Maruni Dance**: Popular traditional dance performed during Tihar (Diwali) by the Nepali community in Sikkim.
