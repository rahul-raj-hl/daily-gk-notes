# 📌 Sessions of Parliament (Summoning, Adjournment, Prorogation & Dissolution)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Summoning**: Done by the **President** (Article 85) to call a parliamentary session.
- Maximum gap between two sessions **cannot exceed 6 months** (Parliament must meet at least twice a year).
- **Adjournment & Adjournment Sine Die**: Declared **ONLY by the Presiding Officer** (Speaker in Lok Sabha / Chairman in Rajya Sabha), NOT the President.
- **Prorogation**: Done by the **President** to officially end a session of the House.
- **Dissolution**: Done by the **President** to end the life of the Lok Sabha (*Rajya Sabha is a permanent body and cannot be dissolved*).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Devices for Conducting & Terminating Parliamentary Sittings

*Mechanisms used to control house sittings and sessions.*

- **Summoning**: The process of calling members of Parliament to meet. Issued from time to time by the **President**.
- **Adjournment**: Suspends the work in a sitting for a **specified time** (hours, days, or weeks).
- **Adjournment Sine Die** (*Without fixing a day*): Terminating a sitting of Parliament for an **indefinite period** (no fixed date set for reassembly).
  - *Authority*: Exercised solely by the **Presiding Officer** (Speaker/Chairman).
- **Prorogation**: Terminates **not just a sitting, but an entire session** of the House. Issued via notification by the **President** (usually a few days after Adjournment Sine Die).
- **Dissolution**: Ends the **life of the existing House** (applies only to Lok Sabha). Results in fresh general elections.

---

### 2. Overview of Annual Sessions

*Regular parliamentary meeting schedules in India.*

- **Budget Session**: Held between **February and May** (longest session).
- **Monsoon Session**: Held between **July and September**.
- **Winter Session**: Held between **November and December** (shortest session).

| Action / Device | Authorised Authority | What It Terminates | Key Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Summoning** | **President** | *Starts a session* | Max gap between sessions = 6 months |
| **Adjournment** | **Presiding Officer** | A Sitting (for fixed time) | Specified hours, days, or weeks |
| **Adjournment Sine Die** | **Presiding Officer** | A Sitting (indefinitely) | No date fixed for next meeting |
| **Prorogation** | **President** | An entire **Session** | Does not affect pending bills |
| **Dissolution** | **President** | Life of **Lok Sabha** | Rajya Sabha is NEVER dissolved |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Parameter | Adjournment / Adjournment Sine Die | Prorogation | Dissolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Who Declares?** | **Presiding Officer** (Speaker / Chairman) | **President** | **President** |
| **What is Ended?** | Only a **sitting** (day/work) | An entire **session** | Life of the **House** (Lok Sabha) |
| **Effect on Pending Bills?** | No effect (bills remain alive) | No effect (bills remain alive) | Bills may lapse based on position |
| **Applicable To** | Both Lok Sabha & Rajya Sabha | Both Lok Sabha & Rajya Sabha | **Lok Sabha ONLY** |

> 'Sine Die' means terminating a sitting for an indefinite period without appointing a day for reassembly.

- [ ] Adjournment
- [ ] Adjournment Sine Die

> Prorogation is done by the President to terminate a session of the House.

### Q3

- [ ] It is governed directly by the Prime Minister
- [ ] It has a fixed term of 4 years
- [x] It is a permanent house not subject to dissolution
- [ ] Its members are directly elected by citizens

> Rajya Sabha is a permanent body and is not subject to dissolution; one-third of its members retire every two years.

Under which Article of the Indian Constitution is the President empowered to summon, prorogue, and dissolve the Parliament?

- [ ] Article 72
- [ ] Article 78
