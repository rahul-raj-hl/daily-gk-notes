# 📌 Famous Books and Authors (Sports & History)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Chandresh Narayanan** authored *"75 Years of Indian Sports – Game, Guts, Glory"*.
- The book highlights key milestones across Indian sports since Independence (1947–2022).
- Related sports books frequently tested in SSC include *"Nation at Play"* by Ronojoy Sen.
- Political memoires like *"The Accidental Prime Minister"* were authored by Sanjaya Baru.
- Books and authors form a core part of the Static GK syllabus across all SSC and DSSSB exams.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Sports Literature & Overview

*Key books recording Indian sporting achievements.*

- **75 Years of Indian Sports – Game, Guts, Glory**: Authored by sports journalist **Chandresh Narayanan**.
- **Nation at Play: A History of Sport in India**: Written by historian **Ronojoy Sen**.
- **Sunny Days**: Famous autobiography written by **Sunil Gavaskar**.
- **Playing It My Way**: Autobiography of **Sachin Tendulkar** (co-authored with Boria Majumdar).
- **The Test of My Life**: Autobiography written by cricketer **Yuvraj Singh**.

---

### 2. Major Autobiographies of Indian Sportspersons

*Frequently asked autobiographies in competitive exams.*

- **Unbreakable**: Autobiography of boxer **M.C. Mary Kom**.
- **Ace Against Odds**: Autobiography of tennis player **Sania Mirza**.
- **Serve to Win**: Written by tennis legend **Novak Djokovic**.
- **Straight from the Heart**: Autobiography of World Cup-winning captain **Kapil Dev**.
- **Mind Master**: Written by Five-time World Chess Champion **Viswanathan Anand**.
