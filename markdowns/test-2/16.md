# 📌 Indian Shooting Milestones & ISSF World Championships

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Anish Bhanwala** created history by winning a **Silver medal** in the **Men's 25m Rapid Fire Pistol** event at the ISSF World Championships in Cairo, Egypt.
- First-ever individual medal won by an Indian pistol shooter in an Olympic category event at the ISSF World Championships.
- Anish hails from **Sonipat, Haryana**.
- India won a total of **5 medals** (1 Gold, 2 Silver, 2 Bronze) at the Cairo meet.
- **ISSF** (*International Shooting Sport Federation*) is headquartered in **Munich, Germany**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Anish Bhanwala's Achievement

*Historic milestone in Indian pistol shooting.*

- **Event**: Men's 25m Rapid Fire Pistol (*an Olympic shooting category*).
- **Medal Won**: **Silver Medal** (scored 28 hits in the final round).
- **Venue**: Cairo, Egypt (ISSF World Championships).
- **Significance**: Became the first Indian individual pistol shooter to win a World Championship medal in an Olympic event.
- **Other Records**: Youngest Indian gold medallist at the 2018 Commonwealth Games (won gold at age 15).

---

### 2. International Shooting Sport Federation (ISSF) & Indian Governing Body

*Core administrative bodies for global and Indian shooting sports.*

- **ISSF** (*International Shooting Sport Federation*):
  - **Full Form**: International Shooting Sport Federation
  - **Established**: **1907**
  - **Headquarters**: **Munich, Germany**
- **NRAI** (*National Rifle Association of India*):
  - Governing body for shooting sports in India.
  - **Founded**: **1951** (first president was Lok Sabha Speaker **G.V. Mavlankar**).
