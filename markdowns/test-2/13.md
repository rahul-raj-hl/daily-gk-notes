# 📌 International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **IAEA** was created in **1957** as an autonomous (independent) organization within the UN system.
- Often called the world's **"Atoms for Peace and Development"** organization.
- Headquartered in **Vienna, Austria**.
- Main Goal: Promotes the **peaceful use of nuclear energy** and prevents its use for military weapons.
- Works closely with member nations under the **Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT)**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Objectives & Functions

*Core mandate of the IAEA.*

- **Promoting Peaceful Use**: Helps countries use nuclear science for power generation (electricity), medicine (cancer treatment), and agriculture.
- **Safeguards & Verification** (*Inspection System*): Sends inspectors to nuclear facilities to ensure nuclear fuel is **not diverted** for making atomic bombs.
- **Safety and Security**: Sets global safety standards for nuclear power plants to prevent accidents (like Chernobyl or Fukushima).

---

### 2. How Safeguards Work (Assertion & Reason Logic)

*Understanding how IAEA enforces its mandate.*

- **Assertion (A)**: IAEA promotes the peaceful use of nuclear technology.
- **Reason (R)**: IAEA conducts regular **safeguards inspections** to verify that civilian nuclear programs are not misused for military weapons.
- **Why R explains A**: Inspections are the practical mechanism (working tool) through which international trust is built, ensuring nuclear technology remains safe and peaceful.
