# 📌 International Organizations & Their Flagship Reports

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **International Labour Organization (ILO)** publishes the **World Social Protection Report (WSPR)**.
- Established in **1919** under the Treaty of Versailles; headquartered in **Geneva, Switzerland**.
- **International Monetary Fund (IMF)** publishes the **World Economic Outlook** and **Global Financial Stability Report**.
- **World Health Organization (WHO)** publishes the **World Health Report** (Headquarters: Geneva, Switzerland).
- **United Nations Security Council (UNSC)** has **15 members** (5 permanent P5 + 10 non-permanent members).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. International Labour Organization (ILO)

*Specialized agency of the UN focused on worker rights and social justice.*

- **Establishment**: Founded in **1919** (became the first UN specialized agency in 1946).
- **Headquarters**: **Geneva, Switzerland**.
- **Primary Function**: Sets international labor standards, promotes decent work (fair working conditions), and advocates for social protection (social security benefits).
- **Key Reports**:
  - **World Social Protection Report (WSPR)**
  - **Global Wage Report**
  - **World Employment and Social Outlook (WESO)**

---

### 2. Other Important UN Bodies & Reports

*Major organizations frequently tested in SSC exams.*

- **International Monetary Fund (IMF)**:
  - **Established**: 1944 (Bretton Woods Conference).
  - **Headquarters**: **Washington, D.C., USA**.
  - **Key Reports**: **World Economic Outlook (WEO)**, **Global Financial Stability Report**.
- **World Health Organization (WHO)**:
  - **Established**: 1948 (World Health Day celebrated on April 7).
  - **Headquarters**: **Geneva, Switzerland**.
  - **Key Reports**: **World Health Report**, **Ambient Air Pollution Report**.
- **United Nations Security Council (UNSC)**:
  - **Role**: Maintains international peace and security.
  - **Structure**: 15 total members — **5 Permanent Members** (P5: China, France, Russia, UK, USA) with Veto Power (power to block decisions) and **10 Non-Permanent Members** elected for 2-year terms.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Organization | Similar-Sounding Report | Actual Publishing Body |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **World Economic Outlook** | Often confused with World Bank | **IMF** (International Monetary Fund) |
| **Global Competitive Report** | Often confused with World Bank | **WEF** (World Economic Forum) |
| **World Development Report** | Often confused with UNDP | **World Bank** |
| **Human Development Index (HDI)** | Often confused with World Bank | **UNDP** (UN Development Programme) |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "Labour for Social Protection in Geneva"**

- **Labour** $\rightarrow$ **ILO** (International Labour Organization)
- **Social Protection** $\rightarrow$ **World Social Protection Report**
- **Geneva** $\rightarrow$ **Headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland**
