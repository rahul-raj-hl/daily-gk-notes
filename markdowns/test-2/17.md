# 📌 Article 255 & Requirements as to Recommendations and Assents

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Article 255** saves a law from becoming invalid (null and void) if a required prior recommendation was missed during introduction.
- A law remains **fully valid** as long as the necessary assent (approval) is given later by the Governor or President.
- Serves as a **procedural safeguard** to avoid invalidating laws due to minor administrative mistakes.
- Applies to bills requiring prior permission, such as **Money Bills** and financial bills.
- Protects the smooth working of Parliament and State Assemblies.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Purpose of Article 255

*A legal safety net for procedural lapses.*

- **Core Rule**: No Act of Parliament or State Legislature will be invalid *only* because a required prior recommendation/sanction was not taken before introducing the bill.
- **Condition for Validity**: The bill must receive the required **subsequent assent** (later approval) from either the **Governor** or the **President**.
- **Why it Matters**: Ensures legislative work is not wasted due to minor technical errors or missing administrative permissions beforehand.

---

### 2. Types of Assent under Article 255

*When and how later assent cures a procedural mistake.*

- **Governor's Assent**: Validates a state law if the Governor's prior recommendation was missing at introduction time.
- **President's Assent**: Validates a law if the President's prior recommendation was missing, or if the bill was later reserved for and approved by the President.
- **Covered Bills**: Money bills, financial bills, or bills affecting trade, commerce, or state powers.

| Requirement Type | Initial Status | Final Outcome | Legal Validity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prior Recommendation** | Missing / Forgotten | Assent granted later by Governor/President | **VALID** (under Article 255) |
| **Final Assent** | Granted later | Bill becomes an official Act | **VALID** |
| **Final Assent** | Denied / Refused | Bill fails to become law | **INVALID** |

| **Article 201** | Bills reserved for President | Options available to President on reserved state bills |
| **Article 255** | Requirement as to recommendations | **Saves laws from invalidity** if prior sanction was missed |
