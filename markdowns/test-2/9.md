# 📌 Constitutional Articles Related to GST

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Article 269A** empowers the Government of India to **levy and collect** Goods and Services Tax (GST) on inter-State trade or commerce.
- Introduced into the Indian Constitution through the **101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016**.
- **Article 246A** gives concurrent power (shared power) to both Parliament and State Legislatures to make laws regarding GST.
- **Article 279A** empowers the President of India to constitute the **GST Council**.
- Under Article 269A, the tax collected on inter-state supplies (Integrated GST / IGST) is **apportioned** (divided) between the Centre and the States based on the GST Council's recommendation.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Article 269A (Levy and Collection of GST in Inter-State Trade)

*Empowers the Centre to collect tax on cross-border trade within the country.*

- **Scope**: Covers supplies of goods or services taking place in the course of **inter-State trade or commerce** (including imports into India).
- **Collection Body**: The tax is levied and collected directly by the **Government of India** (Central Government).
- **Distribution**: The tax collected is apportioned between the Union and the States according to principles framed by Parliament on the recommendation of the GST Council.
- **Consolidated Fund Exemption**: The state's apportioned share does **not** form part of the Consolidated Fund of India.

---

### 2. Other Key Constitutional Articles for GST

*Core articles created by the 101st Amendment Act, 2016.*

- **Article 246A** (*Special Provision for GST*): Grants explicit authority to Parliament and State Assemblies to pass GST laws. Parliament retains exclusive power for inter-State supplies.
- **Article 279A** (*GST Council*): Mandates the creation of a joint forum (GST Council) headed by the **Union Finance Minister** to decide rates, rules, and exemptions.
- **Article 266**: Deals with the **Consolidated Fund of India** and Public Account.
- **Article 268A**: Formerly dealt with Service Tax levied by Union and collected/appropriated by States (omitted after GST implementation).

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Constitutional Provision | Article 246A | Article 269A | Article 279A |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Main Function** | Legislative Power (law making) | Executive Levy & Collection (IGST) | Institutional Body (GST Council) |
| **Tax Domain** | Both Intra-state & Inter-state GST | Exclusively Inter-state trade & imports | Policy, Rates, & Exemptions |
| **Primary Beneficiary** | Union and State Governments | Union levies; shared with destination state | Recommends policy for both Centre & States |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "Power at 246A, Collection at 269A, Council at 279A"**

- **246A** $\rightarrow$ **P**ower to make GST laws (*Parliament & States*)
- **269A** $\rightarrow$ **C**ollection of Inter-State GST (*Centre collects*)
- **279A** $\rightarrow$ **C**ouncil formed to recommend rates (*GST Council*)
