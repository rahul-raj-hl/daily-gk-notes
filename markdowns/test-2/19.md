# 📌 National Youth Day and Viksit Bharat Young Leaders Dialogue

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **National Youth Day** is celebrated every year on **12th January** to mark the birth anniversary of **Swami Vivekananda**.
- **Viksit Bharat Young Leaders Dialogue 2026** is a national platform that connects youth directly with national leadership.
- Prime Minister Narendra Modi addresses the concluding session on National Youth Day in **New Delhi**.
- Aligns with the vision to engage **1 lakh non-political youth** in politics and governance.
- Held from **9th to 12th January**, involving over 50 lakh participants across the country.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Viksit Bharat Young Leaders Dialogue

*National platform for youth-led national development ideas.*

- **Objective**: Create structured engagement between India's youth and top leadership for nation-building.
- **Selection Process**: A 3-stage merit-based contest comprising a digital quiz, essay challenge, and state-level vision presentations.
- **Youth Interaction**: Around 3,000 selected delegates (including international diaspora) interact directly with the Prime Minister.
- **Thematic Focus**: Features youth-led presentations across 10 thematic areas like *Design for Bharat* and *Tech for Viksit Bharat*.

---

### 2. National Youth Day & Swami Vivekananda

*Background and key historical facts.*

- **Date**: **12th January** (Declared by the Government of India in 1984, first celebrated in 1985).
- **Inspiration**: **Swami Vivekananda** (born 12 January 1863 in Kolkata; original name **Narendranath Datta**).
- **Key Historical Event**: Famous speech at the **World's Parliament of Religions in Chicago** on **September 11, 1893**.
- **Key Organizations**: Founded the **Ramakrishna Math** and **Ramakrishna Mission** (1897) in Belur Math, West Bengal.
- **Famous Motto**: *"Arise, awake, and stop not till the goal is reached."*
