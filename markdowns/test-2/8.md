# 📌 Basketball Rules & Violations

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- A **dribble ends** as soon as a player touches the ball with **both hands simultaneously** (at the same time) or lets the ball rest in one hand.
- Starting to dribble again after stopping is a violation called **Double Dribble**.
- **3-Second Rule**: An offensive player cannot stay inside the opponent's restricted area (*key* or *paint area*) for more than **3 seconds**.
- **8-Second Rule**: A team must move the ball across the center line (frontcourt) within **8 seconds** of gaining possession.
- **24-Second Shot Clock**: A team must attempt a shot at the basket within **24 seconds** of taking control of the ball.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Dribbling & Ball Handling Rules

*How players are allowed to move with the ball.*

- **Dribble**: Bouncing the ball continuously using **one hand at a time** while walking or running.
- **Ending a Dribble**: Occurs when both hands touch the ball at once or when the ball comes to a complete rest in a player's hand.
- **Double Dribble** (*Illegal dribble*): Dribbling with both hands together, or stopping a dribble and then dribbling a second time.
- **Travelling** (*Walking with the ball*): Moving one's feet illegally without dribbling the ball.
- **Out of Bounds**: A player or ball is out of bounds if they touch the boundary line (**sideline** or **endline**) or ground outside it.

---

### 2. Common Time Violations

*Time limits imposed to keep the game fast and active.*

- **3-Second Violation**: Staying inside the opponent's restricted area (key area near the basket) for more than 3 consecutive seconds while on offense.
- **5-Second Violation**: Holding the ball for 5 seconds when closely guarded by a defender without passing, shooting, or dribbling.
- **8-Second Violation**: Failing to move the ball from the backcourt (own half) to the frontcourt (opponent's half) within 8 seconds.
- **24-Second Violation** (*Shot Clock*): Failing to attempt a field goal (shot at the rim) before the 24-second timer runs out.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Basketball Violation | Explanation | Penalty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Double Dribble** | Dribbling again after stopping, OR using both hands together | Loss of ball possession (turnover) |
| **Travelling** | Taking extra steps without bouncing the ball | Loss of ball possession (turnover) |
| **Backcourt Violation** | Passing the ball back into own half after crossing center line | Loss of ball possession (turnover) |
| **Foul (Personal)** | Illegal physical contact (hitting, pushing, holding) | Free throws or throw-in for opponent |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "3 in the Paint, 8 across the Line, 24 to Shoot"**

- **3** $\rightarrow$ **3-Second Rule** (Max time inside restricted key area)
- **8** $\rightarrow$ **8-Second Rule** (Time to cross half-court line)
- **24** $\rightarrow$ **24-Second Rule** (Total shot clock time limit)
