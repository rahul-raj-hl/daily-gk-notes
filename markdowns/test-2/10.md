# 📌 FIFA World Cup History and Future Editions

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **2026 FIFA Men's World Cup** is jointly hosted by three nations: **USA, Mexico, and Canada**.
- First time in tournament history that **48 teams** will participate (increased from 32 teams).
- **Mexico** becomes the first country to host/co-host the Men's World Cup **3 times** (previously in 1970 and 1986).
- **Canada** hosts the Men's World Cup for the **first time**.
- The 32-team format was used continuously from **1998 to 2022**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. 2026 FIFA Men's World Cup Key Facts

*Historic expansion and North American joint hosting.*

- **Host Nations**: USA, Mexico, and Canada (first tournament hosted across 3 countries).
- **Team Expansion**: Expanded from 32 teams to **48 teams**.
- **Previous Host History**:
  - **USA**: Previously hosted in **1994**.
  - **Mexico**: Previously hosted in **1970** and **1986**.
  - **Canada**: First-time host.
- **Format History**: 32-team format was introduced at the **1998 FIFA World Cup** in France and ended with the 2022 tournament in Qatar.

---

### 2. Major FIFA World Cup Milestones

*Important past and future editions tested in exams.*

- **First FIFA World Cup (1930)**: Held in **Uruguay**; winner was **Uruguay** (runner-up: Argentina).
- **Most Successful Nation**: **Brazil** with **5 titles** (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002).
- **2022 FIFA World Cup**: Hosted by **Qatar**; won by **Argentina** (defeated France in the final).
- **2030 FIFA World Cup**: Main hosts are **Spain, Portugal, and Morocco** (with centenary opening matches in **Uruguay, Argentina, and Paraguay**).
- **Governing Body**: **FIFA** (*Fédération Internationale de Football Association*), established in **1904**, headquartered in **Zurich, Switzerland**.
