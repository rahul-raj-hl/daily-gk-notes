# 📌 Renewable and Non-Renewable Energy Sources

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Renewable Energy** comes from natural sources that replenish (refill) naturally and never run out (*inexhaustible*).
- Key Examples: **Solar energy**, **Wind energy**, **Hydropower** (water power), **Geothermal energy**, and **Biomass**.
- **Non-Renewable Energy** comes from sources that take millions of years to form and will eventually get exhausted (finish up).
- Key Examples: **Coal**, **Petroleum (oil)**, **Natural Gas**, and **Nuclear Fuels (Uranium)**.
- Thermal power plants mostly burn fossil fuels like coal, making them non-renewable energy producers.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Statement Analysis

*Evaluating each option from the question:*

- **Statement 1: Electricity produced from solar (Correct)**
  - *Explanation*: Solar energy comes directly from sunlight (*photovoltaic cells*) and is completely inexhaustible (never runs out).
- **Statement 2: Electricity produced from thermal plants (Incorrect)**
  - *Explanation*: Conventional thermal plants burn fossil fuels (*coal, petroleum, natural gas*) which are non-renewable and release greenhouse gases.
- **Statement 3: Electricity produced from coal (Incorrect)**
  - *Explanation*: Coal is a fossil fuel formed from buried plant matter over millions of years, making it a non-renewable source.
- **Statement 4: Electricity produced from wind (Correct)**
  - *Explanation*: Wind power uses natural airflow to rotate turbines and produce clean, renewable electricity.

---

### 2. Classification of Energy Sources

*How energy resources are categorized in science.*

- **Renewable Energy** (*Non-conventional / Clean energy*):
  - Natural resources that naturally replenish over a short period.
  - Causes minimal to zero pollution or carbon emissions.
  - Examples: Solar, Wind, Hydroelectric, Tidal, Geothermal, Biomass.
- **Non-Renewable Energy** (*Conventional energy*):
  - Limited resources that take millions of years to form beneath the Earth.
  - Burning them produces carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) and causes air pollution.
  - Examples: Coal, Crude Oil / Petroleum, Natural Gas, Nuclear fuel.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Parameter | Renewable Energy | Non-Renewable Energy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alternative Term** | **Non-Conventional** Energy Sources | **Conventional** Energy Sources |
| **Environmental Impact** | Eco-friendly (Low/Zero emissions) | High pollution (Releases $CO_2$, $SO_2$) |
| **Availability** | Inexhaustible (Never runs out) | Exhaustible (Will run out) |
| **Cost** | High initial setup cost, low running cost | Low initial setup cost, high fuel cost |
