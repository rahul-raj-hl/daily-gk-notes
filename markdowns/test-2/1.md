# 📌 Coalbed Methane (CBM)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Coalbed Methane (CBM) is a natural gas found trapped inside coal seams.
- The Jharia and Bokaro coalfields in Jharkhand have large reserves of CBM.
- CBM mainly contains methane (CH₄), which is a clean-burning fuel.
- It is used for electricity generation, industries, domestic fuel, and CNG.
- CBM is an unconventional source of natural gas because it is extracted from coal beds instead of conventional gas reservoirs.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### What is Coalbed Methane (CBM)?

*CBM is methane gas naturally stored inside coal seams.*

- Methane (CH₄) is the main component of CBM.
- It remains trapped in coal due to underground pressure.
- The gas is extracted by drilling wells into coal seams.
- CBM is called an unconventional natural gas because its source is coal.

---

### Formation of CBM

*CBM forms during the process of coal formation.*

- Plants buried millions of years ago gradually changed into coal.
- During this process, methane gas was produced.
- The methane remained trapped in tiny pores and cracks of coal.
- Water present in coal seams keeps the gas under pressure.

---

### Major CBM Areas in India

*India has significant CBM reserves in coal-bearing regions.*

| Coalfield | State |
|-----------|-------|
| Jharia | Jharkhand |
| Bokaro | Jharkhand |
| Raniganj | West Bengal |
| Sohagpur | Madhya Pradesh |
| Son Valley | Madhya Pradesh & Uttar Pradesh |
| North Karanpura | Jharkhand |

---

### Uses of Coalbed Methane

*CBM is an important clean energy source.*

- Used to generate electricity.
- Used as industrial fuel.
- Supplied for cooking and heating.
- Can be converted into Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) for vehicles.
- Produces less pollution than coal.

---

### Advantages of CBM

*CBM is cleaner and environmentally friendly.*

- Burns with less smoke and pollution.
- Reduces methane release into the atmosphere.
- Helps utilize coal resources more efficiently.
- Supports India's energy security.

## 🎯 Memory Trick

**"CBM = Coal ke Beech Methane"**

👉 **C** = Coal  
👉 **B** = Bed (Coal Seam)  
👉 **M** = Methane

Remember:
**Coal → Methane → CBM**
