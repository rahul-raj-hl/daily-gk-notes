# 📌 Coalbed Methane (CBM)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Coalbed Methane (CBM) is a **Natural Gas (Methane Gas - CH₄)** found inside **Coal Layers (Coal Seams)**.
- It is an **Unconventional Fuel (Non-traditional source of natural gas)**.
- Jharia and Bokaro coalfields in **Jharkhand** have large CBM reserves.
- CBM is a **Clean Fuel (produces less pollution)** than coal.
- SSC usually asks: **CBM → Location, State, Uses, Type of Fuel.**

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### What is Coalbed Methane (CBM)?

*CBM is methane gas trapped inside coal deposits.*

- **Methane (CH₄)** is the main gas present in CBM.
- It is stored inside **Coal Layers (Coal Seams)**.
- It is extracted by **Drilling Wells (boring deep holes into the ground)**.
- It is a type of **Natural Gas**.

---

### Where is it found?

*CBM occurs in coal-rich regions.*

- Major reserves are found in **Jharia Coalfield (Jharkhand)**.
- Also found in **Bokaro Coalfield (Jharkhand)**.
- Other CBM areas include:
  - Raniganj (West Bengal)
  - Sohagpur (Madhya Pradesh)
  - North Karanpura (Jharkhand)

---

### Uses of CBM

*CBM is used as a clean source of energy.*

- Used to produce **Electricity (Power Generation)**.
- Used as **Cooking Fuel**.
- Used in **Industries**.
- Used as **Vehicle Fuel (after processing)**.
- Produces **less smoke and pollution** than coal.

---

### Advantages of CBM

*CBM is an eco-friendly energy source.*

- Cleaner than coal.
- Reduces greenhouse gas release if captured.
- Provides an additional source of natural gas.
- Helps reduce dependence on imported fuel.

---

- **Important Coalfields:** Jharia & Bokaro
