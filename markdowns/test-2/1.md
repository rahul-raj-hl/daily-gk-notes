# 📌 Coalbed Methane (CBM)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Coalbed Methane (CBM) is a **natural gas** mainly made of **Methane (CH₄)**.
- It is found inside **coal seams (layers of coal)**.
- The **Jharia and Bokaro coalfields** in Jharkhand have large CBM reserves.
- CBM is a **clean-burning fuel (less pollution)** than coal.
- SSC usually asks **where it is found**, **its use**, and **major coalfields**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### What is Coalbed Methane (CBM)?

*A natural gas trapped inside coal layers.*

- CBM stands for **Coalbed Methane**.
- It is mainly made of **Methane (CH₄)** gas.
- It is stored inside **coal seams (layers of coal)**.
- It is extracted by drilling wells into coal beds.
- It is an **unconventional natural gas (gas obtained differently from normal natural gas)**.

---

### Where is it Found?

*CBM occurs in coal-bearing regions.*

- Major reserves are found in **Jharia Coalfield (Jharkhand)**.
- Also found in **Bokaro Coalfield (Jharkhand)**.
- Other CBM-bearing basins include:
  - Raniganj (West Bengal)
  - Sohagpur (Madhya Pradesh)
  - Son Valley (Madhya Pradesh & Uttar Pradesh)
  - Godavari Valley (Telangana)

---

### Uses of CBM

*Used as a clean source of energy.*

- Electricity generation.
- Cooking fuel.
- Industrial fuel.
- Fertilizer and chemical industries.
- CNG-like fuel for vehicles after processing.

---

### Why is CBM Important?

*Provides cleaner energy than coal.*

- Produces less smoke and pollution.
- Reduces greenhouse gas release when captured.
- Helps meet India's energy demand.
- Makes use of methane that would otherwise escape into the atmosphere.

---

### Jharia & Bokaro Coalfields

*Important coalfields of India.*

- Located in **Jharkhand**.
- Jharia is famous for **high-quality coking coal (coal used in steel making)**.
- Bokaro is India's **second-largest coking coal field**.
- Both are important for **Coalbed Methane (CBM)** production.

## 🎯 Memory Trick

**"Coal ke Bed mein Methane → CBM"**

Think of a **bed made of coal** with **Methane gas sleeping inside it**.

👉 **Coal + Bed + Methane = CBM**
