# 📌 Coalbed Methane (CBM)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Coalbed Methane (CBM) is a **natural gas** mainly made of **Methane (CH₄)**.
- It is found inside **coal seams (layers of coal)**.
- **Jharia** and **Bokaro** coalfields in **Jharkhand** have major CBM reserves.
- CBM is a **clean-burning fuel (produces less pollution)** than coal.
- Jharia is India's **largest coking coal (coal used for making steel)** field.
- SSC mainly asks **CBM → Found in**, **Coalfield → State**, and **Coalfield → Mineral**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### What is Coalbed Methane (CBM)?

*A natural gas trapped inside coal layers.*

- Coalbed Methane (CBM) is an **unconventional natural gas (gas extracted differently from normal natural gas)**.
- It mainly contains **Methane (CH₄)**.
- It remains trapped inside **coal seams (layers of coal)**.
- It is extracted by drilling wells into coal beds.
- It is cleaner than burning coal directly.

---

### Where is CBM Found in India?

*CBM occurs in coal-bearing regions.*

- **Jharia Coalfield (Jharkhand)** – Largest producer of coking coal and rich in CBM.
- **Bokaro Coalfield (Jharkhand)** – Second-largest coking coal field and rich in CBM.
- **Raniganj Basin (West Bengal)** – India's first commercial CBM production.
- **Sohagpur Basin (Madhya Pradesh)** – Important CBM reserve.
- **Godavari Valley (Telangana)** and **Son Valley (MP & UP)** also contain CBM.

---

### Uses of Coalbed Methane

*CBM is an important source of clean energy.*

- Used for electricity generation.
- Used as industrial fuel.
- Used for cooking after processing.
- Used in fertilizer and chemical industries.
- Can be compressed and used as vehicle fuel.

---

### Why is CBM Important?

*It is a cleaner and efficient source of fuel.*

- Produces less smoke than coal.
- Reduces methane leakage into the atmosphere.
- Helps increase India's energy security (ability to meet its own energy needs).
- Makes better use of coal resources.

---

### Jharia & Bokaro Coalfields

*Two of India's most important coalfields.*

- Both are located in **Jharkhand**.
- **Jharia** is famous for **high-quality coking coal**.
- **Bokaro** is India's **second-largest coking coalfield**.
- Both have significant **Coalbed Methane (CBM)** reserves.
- Jharia is also known for its long-running underground coal fires.

## 🎯 Memory Trick

### **"Coal ke BED mein METHANE sota hai."**

Imagine a **bed made of coal**, and **Methane gas is sleeping inside it.**

👉 **Coal + Bed + Methane = Coalbed Methane (CBM)**

To remember locations:

**"JB = Jharkhand's Best CBM"**

- **J** → Jharia
- **B** → Bokaro
