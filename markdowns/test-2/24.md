# 📌 Padma Awards & Bibek Debroy's Legacy

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Shri Bibek Debroy** received the **Padma Bhushan** award *posthumously* (after death) in **2025** for **Literature and Education**.
- Eminent economist, scholar, and former **Chairman of the Economic Advisory Council to the Prime Minister (EAC-PM)**.
- Famous for translating the **unabridged (complete) Mahabharata and Ramayana** from Sanskrit into English.
- **Padma Awards** are announced annually on the eve of **Republic Day** (January 25) by the **President of India**.
- **Civilian Hierarchy**: Bharat Ratna (1st) $\rightarrow$ Padma Vibhushan (2nd) $\rightarrow$ Padma Bhushan (3rd) $\rightarrow$ Padma Shri (4th).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Contributions of Shri Bibek Debroy

*Eminent economist, scholar, and translator.*

- **Padma Bhushan Award (2025)**: Conferred posthumously in the field of **Literature and Education**.
- **Key Administrative Role**: Served as the Chairman of the **Economic Advisory Council to the Prime Minister (EAC-PM)**.
- **Literary Works**: Translated full unabridged versions of ancient Sanskrit epics including the **Mahabharata**, **Ramayana**, and major **Puranas** into English.
- **Economic Expertise**: Authored numerous papers on economic reforms, railway restructuring, and legal reforms in India.

---

### 2. Hierarchy of Indian Civilian Awards

*The highest honors given by the Union Government.*

- **Bharat Ratna**: Highest civilian award in India (established in 1954).
- **Padma Vibhushan**: Second-highest civilian award (given for *exceptional and distinguished service*).
- **Padma Bhushan**: Third-highest civilian award (given for *distinguished service of a high order*).
- **Padma Shri**: Fourth-highest civilian award (given for *distinguished service*).
- **Presentation**: Presented by the **President of India** at Rashtrapati Bhavan.
