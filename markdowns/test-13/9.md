# 📌 Nominal GDP vs Real GDP & National Income

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Nominal GDP**: Total value of goods and services produced in an economy calculated using **current market prices** (without removing inflation effect).
- **Real GDP**: Total value of goods and services calculated using **constant base year prices** (adjusted for inflation).
- **Base Year in India**: Currently **2011-12** (used for calculating Real GDP in India).
- **GDP Deflator**: Measures the price level change (inflation) in an economy, calculated as $\frac{\text{Nominal GDP}}{\text{Real GDP}} \times 100$.
- **Real GDP** reflects true physical output growth, whereas **Nominal GDP** can increase purely due to rising prices (price rise/inflation).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Types of GDP

*Different measures used to evaluate economic output.*

- **Nominal GDP (GDP at Current Prices)**:
  - Calculated using prices prevailing in the market during the current year.
  - Does NOT remove the impact of price rise (inflation adjustment).
  - Formula: $\text{Nominal GDP} = \text{Current Year Quantity} \times \text{Current Year Price}$.
- **Real GDP (GDP at Constant Prices)**:
  - Calculated using fixed prices of a selected base year.
  - Removes the effect of price changes (inflation adjusted) to show actual production growth.
  - Formula: $\text{Real GDP} = \text{Current Year Quantity} \times \text{Base Year Price}$.
- **Potential GDP**: The maximum theoretical output an economy can produce when all resources (labor and capital) are fully used.
- **Actual GDP**: The actual real-time output produced by an economy at any given period.

---

### 2. GDP Deflator

*Tool used to measure general price change (inflation).*

- Expresses the ratio of Nominal GDP to Real GDP as a percentage.
- Formula: $\text{GDP Deflator} = \left( \frac{\text{Nominal GDP}}{\text{Real GDP}} \right) \times 100$.
- A GDP deflator greater than $100$ indicates price rise (inflation) compared to the base year.

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Feature | Nominal GDP | Real GDP |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Price Used** | **Current Market Prices** | **Constant Base Year Prices** |
| **Inflation Effect** | Included (Not adjusted) | Excluded (Adjusted for inflation) |
| **Economic Growth Truth** | Can give a false impression due to high prices | Shows true picture of actual physical output |
| **Value** | Generally higher during inflation | Generally lower during inflation |
| **Base Year Used** | Not required | Requires a fixed **Base Year (2011-12)** |

---

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Term Pair | Key Difference |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Nominal GDP vs Real GDP** | **Nominal GDP** uses current prices; **Real GDP** uses base year constant prices. |
| **GDP Deflator vs CPI** | **GDP Deflator** covers ALL produced goods/services; **CPI (Consumer Price Index)** covers only consumer goods basket. |
| **Real GDP vs Potential GDP** | **Real GDP** is actual output produced; **Potential GDP** is maximum possible output at full employment. |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic**: **"NOMINAL = NOW, REAL = REGULAR (BASE)"**
> - **NOMINAL** $\rightarrow$ Uses **NOW** (Current market prices)
> - **REAL** $\rightarrow$ Uses **REGULAR BASE** (Constant base year prices)
