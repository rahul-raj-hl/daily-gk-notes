# 📌 Cabinet Mission Plan (1946) & Interim Government

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Cabinet Mission (1946)**: High-powered British mission consisting of **Pethick-Lawrence**, **Stafford Cripps**, and **A.V. Alexander**.
- **Rejection of Plan**: The Muslim League initially accepted the plan in June 1946. On **29 July 1946**, it withdrew acceptance after Congress secured a clear majority in Constituent Assembly elections.
- **Direct Action Day**: Passed a resolution on July 29, calling for **Direct Action Day** on **August 16, 1946**, to demand Pakistan.
- **Interim Government**: Formed under **Jawaharlal Nehru** on **September 2, 1946**. The Muslim League joined later on **October 26, 1946**, to obstruct Congress operations rather than work together.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Cabinet Mission Proposals & Withdrawal

*Events leading to the withdrawal of acceptance by the Muslim League.*

- **Members**: Sent by British PM Clement Attlee; included Lord Pethick-Lawrence (Secretary of State), Sir Stafford Cripps, and A.V. Alexander.
- **Key Recommendations**:
  - Rejected the demand for an independent Pakistan.
  - Proposed a weak Centre with 3 groups of provinces (Group A, B, and C).
  - Recommended forming a **Constituent Assembly** and an **Interim Government**.
- **Withdrawal Cause**: In July 1946 elections, Congress won 205 out of 296 general seats. Fearing permanent Congress domination in the Assembly, Jinnah and the League withdrew support on **29 July 1946**.

---

### 2. Interim Government (1946)

*Formation of the temporary national cabinet before Independence.*

- **Initial Swearing-in**: Sworn in on **September 2, 1946**, with **Jawaharlal Nehru** as Vice President of the Executive Council.
- **League’s Entry**: Lord Wavell persuaded the Muslim League to join on **October 26, 1946**.
- **Liaquat Ali Khan's Portfolio**: Became the **Finance Minister**, creating hurdles for other ministries through budget restrictions.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Assertion / Reason Question | True / False Status | Reason for Relation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Assertion (A)**: In 1946, Muslim League withdrew acceptance of Cabinet Mission Plan. | **TRUE** | League withdrew on July 29, 1946 due to Assembly election results. |
| **Reason (R)**: Muslim League joined the interim government in 1946. | **TRUE** | League joined on October 26, 1946 to oppose Congress internally. |
| **Relationship** | **(R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)** | Joining the government in October did not cause the plan's withdrawal in July. |

> **Mnemonic for Chronology**: **"MISSION LEAVES DIRECTLY BEFORE INTERIM ENTRY"**
> - **MISSION** → Cabinet **Mission** Plan (March 1946)
> - **LEAVES** → League **Withdraws** acceptance (July 29, 1946)
> - **DIRECTLY** → **Direct** Action Day (August 16, 1946)
> - **INTERIM** → **Interim** Government sworn in (Sept 2, 1946)
> - **ENTRY** → League **Entry** into Interim Govt (Oct 26, 1946)
