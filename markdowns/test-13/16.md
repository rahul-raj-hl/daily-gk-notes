# 📌 Brihadisvara Temple at Gangaikonda Cholapuram & Rajendra Chola I

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Rajendra Chola I** (ruled c. 1014–1044 CE).
- **The Temple**: The grand Brihadisvara Temple at **Gangaikonda Cholapuram** (Ariyalur district, Tamil Nadu) was commissioned by **Rajendra Chola I** to celebrate his triumphant military expedition to North India and the Ganga river basin.
- **Title Assumed**: After defeating the Pala ruler Mahipala I and bringing the sacred waters of the Ganga south, he assumed the title **"Gangaikonda Chola"** (*The Chola who took the Ganges*).
- **New Imperial Capital**: He established **Gangaikonda Cholapuram** as the new Chola capital, shifting it from Thanjavur.
- **UNESCO Recognition**: It forms a vital part of the **"Great Living Chola Temples"** UNESCO World Heritage Site (along with Thanjavur and Darasuram).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Architectural Highlights of Gangaikonda Cholapuram Temple

- **Dravidian Masterpiece**: Dedicated to **Lord Shiva**, the temple houses one of the largest monolithic Shiva Lingams in South India (approx. 4 meters high).
- **The Vimana (Tower)**:
  - Stands at an imposing height of about 55 meters (182 feet).
  - Unlike the sharp, straight-sided pyramidal Vimana of Thanjavur built by his father, Rajendra's Vimana features a **graceful concave curve with feminine contours**.
- **The Chola Gangam Lake (*Cholagangam*)**:
  - A massive artificial irrigation reservoir built by Rajendra I near the capital, celebrated as the *"liquid pillar of victory"*.

---

### 2. Great Living Chola Temples (UNESCO World Heritage Site)

The group consists of three architectural marvels:

1. **Brihadisvara Temple at Thanjavur**:
   - Built by: **Rajaraja Chola I** (completed c. 1010 CE).
   - Dedicated to: Lord Shiva.
   - Distinctive feature: 66-meter tall straight-edged pyramidal Vimana topped with an 80-tonne monolithic cupola.
2. **Brihadisvara Temple at Gangaikonda Cholapuram**:
   - Built by: **Rajendra Chola I** (c. 1035 CE).
   - Distinctive feature: Graceful curved Vimana and detailed sculptural friezes.
3. **Airavatesvara Temple at Darasuram**:
   - Built by: **Rajaraja Chola II** (12th Century CE).
   - Distinctive feature: Chariot-shaped Mandapa (*Ratha*) and musical stone steps (*Singing Steps*).

---

### 3. Key Achievements of Rajendra Chola I

- **Naval Supremacy**: Maintained a powerful blue-water navy, turning the Bay of Bengal into a *"Chola Lake"*.
- **Overseas Conquests**: Conquered the whole of Sri Lanka (captured King Mahinda V), the Maldives, and launched successful maritime naval expeditions against the **Srivijaya Empire** (Sumatra, Malay Peninsula, and Java).
- **Trade Missions**: Sent trade and diplomatic embassies to the **Song Dynasty** court in China.
