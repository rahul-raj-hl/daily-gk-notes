# 📌 2026 ICC Men's T20 World Cup - Host Nations & Tournament Overview

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **1 and 3 only** (**India** and **Sri Lanka**).
- **Host Nations**: The 2026 ICC Men's T20 World Cup was co-hosted by **India and Sri Lanka**.
- **Tournament Dates**: Held from **7 February to 8 March 2026**.
- **Format & Scale**: 20 national teams contested across 55 matches in 8 venues.
- **Final Match Venue**: **Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad**.
- **Champion**: **India** successfully defended their title, defeating New Zealand by 96 runs in the final.
- **Player of the Tournament**: **Sanju Samson** (321 runs).
- **Player of the Final**: **Jasprit Bumrah** (4/15 in 4 overs).
