# 📌 Water Disinfection & Chemical Properties of Elements

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Chlorine**.
- **Chlorination**: The process of adding chlorine or chlorine compounds (such as bleaching powder or sodium hypochlorite) to water to kill pathogenic microorganisms.
- **Mechanism**: Chlorine destroys microbes by weakening and damaging their **cell membrane**, penetrating inside, and disrupting **cellular respiration and DNA activity**.
- **Alternative Chemical Disinfectants for Water**: Ozone ($\text{O}_3$), Bleaching powder ($\text{CaOCl}_2$), Potassium permanganate ($\text{KMnO}_4$), and Chloramines.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Water Purification with Chlorine

- **Action in Water**:
  - When dissolved in water, chlorine forms **hypochlorous acid ($\text{HOCl}$)** and **hypochlorite ions ($\text{OCl}^-$)**, which are powerful oxidizing agents that neutralize bacteria and viruses.
- **Residual Protection**:
  - A key advantage of chlorination over UV irradiation or boiling is that it leaves a **residual amount of disinfectant** in the water supply network, preventing recontamination during transit.

---

### 2. Properties of Other Substances in the Options

- **Ethanol ($\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}$)**:
  - Commonly known as **ethyl alcohol** or grain alcohol.
  - A clear, colorless, volatile, and flammable liquid.
  - Used primarily in alcoholic beverages, hand sanitizers, and antiseptic solutions (at 70% concentration).
- **Phosphorus ($\text{P}$, Atomic Number 15)**:
  - The name literally translates to *"I carry light"* (exhibits phosphorescence/chemiluminescence).
  - **White Phosphorus**: Highly reactive, has a characteristic **garlic odor**, and spontaneously catches fire in air at ~30°C.
  - **Storage**: Must be stored **immersed under water** to isolate it from atmospheric oxygen.
- **Teflon (PTFE - Polytetrafluoroethylene)**:
  - A synthetic fluoropolymer of tetrafluoroethylene ($\text{CF}_2=\text{CF}_2$).
  - Discovered accidentally by **Roy J. Plunkett** in 1938.
  - Noted for its high chemical resistance, high melting point, and very low coefficient of friction; widely used in **non-stick cookware coatings** and waterproof gasket insulation.
