# 📌 Regional & Longitudinal Divisions of the Himalayas

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Location**: A series of parallel mountain ranges stretching from North-West to South-East north of the Indian peninsula.
- **Longitudinal Ranges**: Main divisions are **Trans-Himalayas**, **Greater Himalayas (Himadri)**, **Lesser Himalayas (Himachal)**, **Outer Himalayas (Shiwalik)**, and **Eastern Hills (Purvanchal)**.
- **Regional Divisions**: Divided west-to-east based on river valleys: **Punjab**, **Kumaon**, **Nepal**, **Sikkim**, and **Assam** Himalayas.
- **Key River Boundaries**: **Indus**, **Sutlej**, **Kali**, **Kosi**, **Teesta**, and **Dihang (Brahmaputra)** rivers mark the regional borders.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Longitudinal (North-to-South) Divisions

*Divisions from north to south separated by deep valleys.*

- **Trans-Himalayas**: Ranges north of the main Himalayas (includes Karakoram, Ladakh, Zaskar).
- **Greater Himalayas (Himadri)**: The highest continuous northern range with snow-capped peaks.
- **Lesser Himalayas (Himachal)**: Middle mountain range located between Himadri and Shiwaliks.
- **Shiwalik (Outer Himalayas)**: The outermost, low-height southern foothills made of fresh sediments.
- **Purvanchal (Eastern Hills)**: Eastern bend of the Himalayas along the north-eastern border.

---

### 2. Regional (West-to-East) Divisions

*Divisions from west to east bounded by major rivers.*

- **Punjab Himalayas**: Region lying between the **Indus and Sutlej rivers**.
- **Kumaon Himalayas**: Region located between the **Sutlej and Kali rivers**.
- **Nepal Himalayas**: Region located between the **Kali and Teesta rivers**.
- **Sikkim Himalayas**: Region located between the **Kosi and Teesta rivers**.
- **Assam Himalayas**: Region located between the **Teesta and Dihang (Brahmaputra) rivers**.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Feature | Longitudinal Division | Regional Division |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direction** | North to South (Parallel Ranges) | West to East (Horizontal Stretch) |
| **Separated By** | Valleys (Duns) & Thrust faults | River Basins / River Valleys |
| **Example** | Himadri, Himachal, Shiwalik | Punjab, Kumaon, Nepal, Assam |

---

| Division | Bounding Rivers |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Kumaon Himalayas** | Between **Sutlej** and **Kali** rivers |
| **Nepal Himalayas** | Between **Kali** and **Teesta** rivers |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

The part of the Himalayas lying between Sutlej and Kali rivers is known as:

- [ ] Punjab Himalayas
- [x] Kumaon Himalayas
- [ ] Nepal Himalayas
- [ ] Assam Himalayas

> The stretch of the Himalayas between Sutlej and Kali rivers is called the Kumaon Himalayas (located mostly in Uttarakhand).

---

### PYQ 2

Which two rivers mark the boundaries of the Assam Himalayas?

- [ ] Indus and Sutlej
- [ ] Kali and Teesta
- [x] Teesta and Dihang
- [ ] Sutlej and Kali

> The Assam Himalayas lie between the Teesta river in the west and the Dihang (Brahmaputra) river in the east.

---

### PYQ 3

Which region of the Himalayas is situated between the Indus and Sutlej rivers?

- [x] Punjab Himalayas
- [ ] Kumaon Himalayas
- [ ] Nepal Himalayas
- [ ] Sikkim Himalayas

> The westernmost part of the Himalayas lying between Indus and Sutlej rivers is traditionally known as the Punjab Himalayas.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

The part of the Himalayas lying between the Kali and Teesta rivers is known as the:

- [ ] Kumaon Himalayas
- [x] Nepal Himalayas
- [ ] Assam Himalayas
- [ ] Punjab Himalayas

> The Nepal Himalayas span across the region between the Kali river in the west and the Teesta river in the east.

---

### Q2

Which river marks the boundary between the Sikkim Himalayas and the Assam Himalayas?

- [ ] Kosi
- [ ] Kali
- [x] Teesta
- [ ] Dihang

> The Teesta river flows between the Nepal/Sikkim region and marks the beginning of the Assam Himalayas.

---

### Q3

The outermost (southernmost) range of the Himalayas is called:

- [ ] Himadri
- [ ] Himachal
- [x] Shiwalik
- [ ] Purvanchal

> The Shiwalik range is the outermost and youngest southern foothill range of the Himalayas.

---

### Q4

Which region of the Himalayas is bounded by the Kosi and Teesta rivers?

- [ ] Punjab Himalayas
- [ ] Kumaon Himalayas
- [x] Sikkim Himalayas
- [ ] Assam Himalayas

> The Sikkim Himalayas lie in the eastern zone bounded by the Kosi and Teesta river systems.
