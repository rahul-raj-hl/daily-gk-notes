# 📌 Guru Kelucharan Mohapatra & Odissi Classical Dance

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Odissi**.
- **Guru Kelucharan Mohapatra**: Regarded as the **legendary maestro and revivalist of Odissi dance**, who revived and structured the art form in the 20th century.
- **First Recipient from Odisha**: He was the **first person from Odisha** to receive the prestigious **Padma Vibhushan** (in the year 2000).
- **Major Awards Conferred**:
  - **Padma Shri** (1972)
  - **Sangeet Natak Akademi Award** (1966)
  - **Padma Bhushan** (1989)
  - **Padma Vibhushan** (2000)
- **Institution Founded**: Founded the premier Odissi dance school **"Srjan"** in Bhubaneswar in 1993.
- **Key Characteristics of Odissi**: Known for the signature **Tribhanga posture** (three-body bend) and **Chowk posture** (square stance).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Odissi Dance Form & Guru Kelucharan Mohapatra

- **Origins & Revival**:
  - Odissi originated in the temples of Odisha and is rooted in the ancient Sanskrit treatise *Natya Shastra*.
  - Historically performed by **Maharis** (temple dancers at the Jagannath Temple, Puri) and young male dancers known as **Gotipuas**.
  - During the colonial era, the dance declined significantly. Guru Kelucharan Mohapatra, along with other scholars, codified the classical techniques, postures, and repertoire to establish its modern classical standing.
- **Key Classical Postures in Odissi**:
  - **Chowk**: A balanced, square-like masculine stance symbolizing Lord Jagannath.
  - **Tribhanga**: A feminine, three-bend posture at the neck, torso, and knee, inspired by temple sculptures (such as those at Konark Sun Temple).
- **Famous Disciples of Guru Kelucharan Mohapatra**:
  - Sanjukta Panigrahi, Sonal Mansingh, Kumkum Mohanty, Madhavi Mudgal, and Protima Gauri Bedi.

---

### 2. Overview of Classical Dances in the Options

- **Bharatnatyam (Tamil Nadu)**:
  - Deeply rooted in the *Devadasi* temple tradition; known for crisp geometric footwork and expressive abhinaya.
  - Prominent exponents: Rukmini Devi Arundale, Padma Subrahmanyam, Yamini Krishnamurthy.
- **Kathakali (Kerala)**:
  - A classical dance-drama featuring elaborate colorful facial makeup (*vesham*), headgear, and stories from the *Ramayana* and *Mahabharata*.
  - Accompanied by percussion instruments like the *Chenda* and *Maddalam*.
- **Kathak (North India / Uttar Pradesh)**:
  - Characterized by rhythmic footwork (*tatkar*), rapid pirouettes (*chakkars*), and storytelling gestures.
  - Prominent exponents: Pandit Birju Maharaj, Sitara Devi, Shambhu Maharaj, Lachhu Maharaj.
- **Odissi (Odisha)**:
  - Distinctive lyricism, soft torso movements, and devotion to Lord Jagannath and the poetry of Jayadeva (*Gita Govinda*).
