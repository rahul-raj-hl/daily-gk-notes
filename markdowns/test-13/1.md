# 📌 Impulse and Impulsive Force

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Impulsive Force**: A very large force acting on an object for a very small time duration (time interval).
- **Impulse ($I$)**: The total effect of an impulsive force, calculated as $F \times t$.
- **Impulse-Momentum Theorem**: Impulse equals the total change in momentum ($\Delta p$) of an object.
- **SI Unit**: Newton-second ($\text{N}\cdot\text{s}$) or kilogram meter per second ($\text{kg}\cdot\text{m}\cdot\text{s}^{-1}$).
- **Quantity Type**: Vector quantity (has both magnitude and direction).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Understanding Impulse & Impulsive Force

*Key definitions and physical meaning.*

- **Impulsive Force**: A sudden, strong force that changes rapidly from zero to maximum and back to zero in a very short time.
- **Impulse**: The measure of the overall effect produced by an impulsive force during a specific time interval.
- **Formula**: $\text{Impulse} = \text{Force} \times \text{Time} = \text{Change in Momentum}$ ($\Delta p$).
- **Units**: SI unit is $\text{N}\cdot\text{s}$ (or $\text{kg}\cdot\text{m}\cdot\text{s}^{-1}$); CGS unit is $\text{g}\cdot\text{cm}\cdot\text{s}^{-1}$ (or $\text{dyne}\cdot\text{s}$).

---

### 2. Practical Applications (Exam Favorite Points)

*Everyday examples where time duration is increased to reduce impact force.*

- **Cricket Fielder**: Pulls hands backward while catching a fast ball to increase impact time, reducing the impact force on hands.
- **Vehicles & Shock Absorbers**: Springs and shockers increase time of impact during bumps, reducing force transferred to passengers.
- **Glassware Packaging**: Bubble wrap and straw cushions increase impact time during transit, preventing glass breakage.
- **High Jump Landing**: Athletes land on foam beds or sand to extend landing time and reduce injury risk.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Force** | Rate of change of momentum ($\frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$) | Newton ($\text{N}$) |
