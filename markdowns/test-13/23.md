# 📌 Dhamek Stupa (Sarnath) & Buddhist Architecture

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Both I and II**.
- **Statement I is CORRECT**: The **Dhamek Stupa** at **Sarnath** (near Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh) is one of the most prominent, revered, and well-preserved Buddhist monuments in India.
- **Statement II is CORRECT**: The present stupa structure was built in **500 CE** (during the Gupta era) to replace and enlarge an earlier cylindrical brick stupa originally commissioned by **Mauryan Emperor Ashoka in 249 BCE**.
- **Historical Significance of Sarnath**: Marks the historic Deer Park (*Rishipatana / Mrigadava*) where **Gautama Buddha delivered his first sermon** to his first five disciples, known as the **Dharmachakrapravartana** (*Turning the Wheel of Law*).
- **Architectural Style**: Cylindrical tower rising to a height of **43.6 meters** and a diameter of **28 meters**, with lower stone walls featuring intricate Gupta geometric and floral arabesque carvings.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Four Sacred Buddhist Pilgrimage Sites (*Chaar Mahasthan*)

*The core life milestones of Gautama Buddha:*

- **1. Lumbini (Nepal)**: Place of Buddha's **Birth** (*Janma*).
- **2. Bodh Gaya (Bihar)**: Place of **Enlightenment** (*Nirvana / Sambodhi*) beneath the Bodhi Tree.
- **3. Sarnath (Uttar Pradesh)**: Site of the **First Sermon** (*Dharmachakrapravartana*).
- **4. Kushinagar (Uttar Pradesh)**: Place of **Death / Final Departure** (*Mahaparinirvana*).

---

### 2. Architecture and Relics at Sarnath

*Key monuments situated within the Sarnath archaeological complex:*

- **Dhamek Stupa**:
  - The name *Dhamek* is derived from the Sanskrit word *Dharmarajika* or *Dharma*.
  - Characterized by solid stone facing covering the lower foundation, adorned with classic Gupta floral creepers, svastika swastikas, birds, and lotus petals.
- **Chaukhandi Stupa**:
  - Located nearby; commemorates the exact spot where Lord Buddha first met his five ascetic companions (*Panchavargiya bhikshus*).
  - Topped with an octagonal tower built by Mughal ruler Akbar to commemorate his father Humayun's visit.
- **The Ashokan Lion Capital**:
  - Erected by Emperor Ashoka at Sarnath (3rd century BCE).
  - Adopted as the official **National Emblem of the Republic of India** on **26 January 1950** (along with the motto *"Satyameva Jayate"* from the Mundaka Upanishad).
