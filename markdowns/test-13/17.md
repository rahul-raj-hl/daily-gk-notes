# 📌 National Organ Donation Rankings 2025 (NOTTO / SOTTO Data)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Tamil Nadu** (Ranked **1st** nationally with **267 organ donations** in 2025).
- **Top 3 States Nationally (2025)**:
  - **1st Position**: **Tamil Nadu** — 267 donations
  - **2nd Position**: **Telangana** — 205 donations
  - **3rd Position**: **Karnataka** — 198 donations (State's highest-ever figure)
- **4th & 5th Positions**: **Maharashtra** (153 donations) and **Gujarat** (152 donations).
- **Pioneering State**: Tamil Nadu was the first Indian state to establish a structured deceased-donor organ transplantation model (TRANSTAN) and grant state honors for deceased organ donors.
- **National Organ Donation Day**: Observed annually on **August 3** by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. State Performance Breakdown (2025 Data)

*Overview of the top-performing states in deceased organ donation:*

- **Tamil Nadu (1st Rank)**:
  - Maintained its leadership position in India's cadaveric organ transplant program by recording **267 organ donations** in 2025.
  - Supported by the *Transplant Authority of Tamil Nadu (TRANSTAN)*, strong public hospital coordination, and widespread public awareness.
- **Telangana (2nd Rank)**:
  - Secured the second spot nationally with **205 donations**, driven by its dedicated state initiative *Jeevandan*.
- **Karnataka (3rd Rank)**:
  - Achieved a record high of **198 donations** in 2025, surpassing its previous best of 178 donations in 2023.
  - Managed by the *State Organ and Tissue Transplant Organisation (SOTTO)* under the *Jeevasarthakathe* program.
- **Maharashtra & Gujarat**:
  - Maharashtra reported **153 donations**, ranking fourth.
  - Gujarat closely followed with **152 donations**, ranking fifth.

---

### 2. Institutional Framework for Organ Donation in India

*National and state coordination mechanisms:*

- **Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act (THOTA), 1994**:
  - Primary legislative framework regulating the removal, storage, and transplantation of human organs and tissues in India, as well as preventing commercial dealings.
- **Three-Tier Institutional Hierarchy**:
  - **NOTTO (National Organ and Tissue Transplant Organisation)**: Apex body in New Delhi under the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), MoHFW, overseeing nationwide registries and organ allocation networks.
  - **ROTTO (Regional Organ and Tissue Transplant Organisation)**: Regional nodal centers coordinating across multiple states.
  - **SOTTO (State Organ and Tissue Transplant Organisation)**: State-level authorities managing hospital procurement, waitlists, and organ retrieval coordination.
