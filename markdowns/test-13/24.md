# 📌 Census of India 2011 - Decadal Population Growth Rates & Demographics

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Lakshadweep** (recorded a decadal growth rate of **$6.23\%$**, the lowest among all Union Territories).
- **National Decadal Growth Rate (2001–2011)**: **$17.7\%$** (India's total population rose to $\approx 1.21\text{ billion}$).
- **State with the Lowest (Negative) Growth Rate**: **Nagaland** ($-0.58\%$, only state with negative population growth).
- **State with the Highest Growth Rate**: **Meghalaya** ($27.95\%$).
- **UT with the Highest Growth Rate**: **Dadra and Nagar Haveli** ($55.88\%$).
- **UT with the Lowest Growth Rate**: **Lakshadweep** ($6.23\%$).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Growth Rates of the Union Territories in the Options (2001–2011)

*Decadal growth percentage comparison:*

- **Lakshadweep**:
  - Growth Rate: **$6.23\%$** (Lowest among all UTs).
  - Total Population: **$64,429$** (Smallest UT by population and area).
  - Low growth factors: Geographical isolation in the Arabian Sea, high female literacy ($87.9\%$), and strict demographic stability.
- **Andaman and Nicobar Islands**:
  - Growth Rate: **$6.86\%$** (Second lowest among UTs).
  - Total Population: **$380,581$**.
  - Influenced by restricted migration policies and tribal area protection regulations.
- **Chandigarh**:
  - Growth Rate: **$17.19\%$**.
  - Total Population: **$1,055,450$**.
  - High urban immigration from neighboring Punjab, Haryana, and Himachal Pradesh.
- **Puducherry**:
  - Growth Rate: **$28.08\%$**.
  - Total Population: **$1,244,464$**.
  - Attracted inter-state economic and educational migration.

---

### 2. Overview of Extreme Demographic Indicators (Census 2011)

- **Overall Population**:
  - Most Populous State: **Uttar Pradesh** ($\approx 19.98\text{ crore}$).
  - Least Populous State: **Sikkim** ($\approx 6.10\text{ lakh}$).
  - Most Populous UT: **Delhi (NCT)** ($\approx 1.67\text{ crore}$).
  - Least Populous UT: **Lakshadweep** ($64,429$).
- **Sex Ratio (Females per 1000 Males)**:
  - Highest (State): **Kerala** ($1,084$).
  - Lowest (State): **Haryana** ($879$).
  - Highest (UT): **Puducherry** ($1,037$).
  - Lowest (UT): **Daman and Diu** ($618$).
