# 📌 Adi Brahmo Samaj & Evolution of the Brahmo Movement

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Debendranath Tagore**.
- **First Split of Brahmo Samaj (1866)**:
  - The original Brahmo Samaj split into two factions due to ideological differences between the conservative and radical wings.
  - **Adi Brahmo Samaj**: Retained and led by **Debendranath Tagore** (focused on Upanishadic/Vedic Hindu reform).
  - **Brahmo Samaj of India (*Bhartiya Brahmo Samaj*)**: Formed by **Keshab Chandra Sen** (advocated radical social reforms and cosmopolitan multi-faith inclusions).
- **Original Foundation**: Founded by **Raja Ram Mohan Roy** in **1828** as *Brahmo Sabha* (later renamed *Brahmo Samaj*).
- **Tattvabodhini Sabha (1839)**: Founded by Debendranath Tagore to study India's past and propagate Vedanta, merged with Brahmo Samaj in 1842.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Timeline and Splits of the Brahmo Movement

*Understanding the progression from Brahmo Sabha to multiple factions:*

- **1828 – Foundation of Brahmo Sabha**:
  - Established by **Raja Ram Mohan Roy** and Dwarkanath Tagore in Calcutta.
  - Preached pure monotheism (*Ekeshwaravada*), worship through prayers and meditation without idol worship, and campaigned vigorously against evil practices like **Sati** (abolished by William Bentinck via Regulation XVII in 1829).
- **1843 – Revival by Debendranath Tagore**:
  - After Ram Mohan Roy's death in Bristol (1833), Debendranath Tagore revitalized the Samaj.
  - Introduced the *Brahmo Covenant* (formal initiation creed) and published the journal ***Tattvabodhini Patrika***.
- **1866 – The First Schism (Split)**:
  - **Cause**: Keshab Chandra Sen wanted radical changes—inter-caste marriages, inclusion of teachings from the Bible and Quran, and complete rejection of the sacred thread. Debendranath preferred a moderate approach rooted strictly in Hindu monotheism.
  - **Outcome**:
    - Debendranath's faction became the **Adi Brahmo Samaj** (*"Original" Brahmo Samaj*).
    - Keshab's group established the **Brahmo Samaj of India** (*Bhartiya Brahmo Samaj*).
- **1878 – The Second Schism (Sadharan Brahmo Samaj)**:
  - **Cause**: Keshab Chandra Sen married off his minor 13-year-old daughter to the minor Maharaja of Cooch Behar according to orthodox Hindu rituals, directly violating the *Native Marriage Act (Civil Marriage Act) of 1872* which he himself had championed.
  - **Outcome**: Disillusioned radical followers like **Anand Mohan Bose** and **Shibnath Shastri** broke away from Keshab to establish the **Sadharan Brahmo Samaj** in 1878.

---

### 2. Prominent Reformers in the Options

- **Raja Ram Mohan Roy**:
  - Regarded as the **"Father of the Indian Renaissance"** and "Maker of Modern India".
  - Established the *Atmiya Sabha* (1814) and *Brahmo Samaj* (1828).
- **Debendranath Tagore**:
  - Father of Rabindranath Tagore; founded *Tattvabodhini Sabha* (1839) and led the *Adi Brahmo Samaj* (1866). Given the title **"Maharshi"**.
- **Keshab Chandra Sen**:
  - Powerful orator who spread the Brahmo ideology outside Bengal to Bombay and Madras. Founded the *Brahmo Samaj of India* (1866) and *Naba Bidhan* (1880).
- **Atmaram Pandurang**:
  - Founded the **Prarthana Samaj** in Bombay in **1867** (under the inspiration of Keshab Chandra Sen).
