# 📌 Kopra Reservoir - Chhattisgarh's First Ramsar Site

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Kopra Reservoir**.
- **First Ramsar Site of Chhattisgarh**: **Kopra Reservoir**, located in the **Bilaspur district**, has been designated as the state's very first wetland of international importance under the Ramsar Convention.
- **Wetland Type**: A freshwater wetland shaped by natural hydrological flows and human-made structures, primarily rain-fed and supported by small seasonal streams.
- **Ecological Role**: Serves as a vital ecological hotspot supporting resident and migratory avifauna, regional biodiversity, groundwater recharge, and local rural livelihoods.
- **Ramsar Convention Overview**: Signed on **February 2, 1971**, in **Ramsar, Iran** (celebrated annually as **World Wetlands Day**).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Ecological Significance of Kopra Reservoir

- **Location & Hydrology**: Situated in a semi-rural landscape of Bilaspur district, Chhattisgarh. It captures monsoon surface runoff and maintains regional hydrological balance.
- **Biodiversity Habitat**: Acts as a crucial refuge and foraging ground for thousands of migratory birds navigating the Central Asian Flyway during winter months.
- **Community Benefits**: Supports regional agriculture, local freshwater fisheries, flood mitigation, and aquifer replenishment.

---

### 2. Major Water Bodies in the Options

- **Hasdeo Bango Reservoir (Minimata Bango Dam)**:
  - Built across the **Hasdeo River** (a major tributary of the Mahanadi) in the Korba district of Chhattisgarh; one of the largest multipurpose water projects in the state.
- **Gangrel Reservoir (Ravishankar Sagar Dam)**:
  - Constructed across the **Mahanadi River** in Dhamtari district, Chhattisgarh; supplies water to the Bhilai Steel Plant and Raipur.
- **Kopra Reservoir**:
  - Located in **Bilaspur district**; officially recognized as **Chhattisgarh's 1st Ramsar Site**.
