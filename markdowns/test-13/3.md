# 📌 Sangam Kingdoms (Cheras, Cholas, and Pandyas)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Three Main Dynasties**: The Sangam Age was dominated by the **Cholas**, **Cheras**, and **Pandyas** in South India.
- **Cholas**: Ruled central/northern Tamil Nadu with capital at **Uraiyur**; emblem was the **Tiger**.
- **Cheras**: Controlled central/northern Kerala & Kongu region with capital at **Vanji**; emblem was the **Bow and Arrow**.
- **Pandyas**: Ruled from **Madurai** with main port at **Korkai** (famous for pearl fishery); emblem was the **Fish**.
- **Key Rulers**: **Karikala** (Chola) won the Battle of Venni; **Senguttuvan** (Chera) introduced the Pattini cult.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Cholas

*Rulers of Cholamandalam (Central & Northern Tamil Nadu).*

- **Capital**: **Uraiyur** (near Tiruchirapalli).
- **Port Town / Secondary Capital**: **Puhar** (Kaveripattinam) served as a major port and alternate royal home.
- **Royal Emblem**: **Tiger**.
- **Greatest Ruler**: **Karikala**, who defeated a combined group (confederacy) of Cheras, Pandyas, and 11 minor chiefs at the **Battle of Venni**.
- **Key Feature**: Maintained an effective navy and flourished in trade.

---

### 2. The Cheras

*Rulers of Central/Northern Kerala and Kongu region of Tamil Nadu.*

- **Capital**: **Vanji**.
- **Major Ports**: **Musiri** and **Tondi** on the west coast.
- **Royal Emblem**: **Bow and Arrow**.
- **Greatest Ruler**: **Senguttuvan** (also known as the *Red Chera* or *Good Chera*).
- **Achievements of Senguttuvan**:
  - Mentioned in the Tamil epic **Silapathikaram** for his Himalayan trip (expedition).
  - Started the **Pattini cult** (worship of Kannagi as the ideal wife).
  - First South Indian ruler to send an official messenger (embassy) to China.
- **Economic Base**: Heavily dependent on rich trade with the Romans.

---

### 3. The Pandyas

*Rulers of Southern Tamil Nadu.*

- **Capital**: **Madurai**.
- **Major Port**: **Korkai**.
- **Royal Emblem**: **Fish**.
- **Famous For**: **Pearl fishery** and **chank diving** (shell gathering).
- **Social & Religious Life**: Sati, caste system, and idol worship were common; adopted Vedic sacrifice rituals and supported Brahmin priests.
