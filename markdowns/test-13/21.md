# 📌 Lysosomes & Cell Organelles (Suicidal Bags of the Cell)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Lysosome**.
- **Suicidal Bag of the Cell**: **Lysosomes** are called the *"suicidal bags"* or *"garbage disposal system"* of the cell because they contain powerful **hydrolytic enzymes** (acid hydrolases) that can digest and destroy the cell's own damaged components or the entire damaged cell (*autolysis*).
- **Discovered By**: Belgian cytologist **Christian de Duve** in **1955** (awarded the Nobel Prize in 1974).
- **Enzymatic pH**: Contains around 50 different digestive enzymes (proteases, lipases, nucleases, glycosidases) that function optimally in an **acidic pH ($\sim 4.5\text{–}5.0$)**.
- **Membrane Structure**: Enclosed by a **single lipid bilayer membrane** that prevents the hydrolytic enzymes from leaking into the neutral cytoplasm.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Biological Functions of Lysosomes

*Essential cellular clean-up and defense mechanisms:*

- **1. Intracellular Digestion (Heterophagy / Phagocytosis)**:
  - Fuses with phagosomes or endosomes to break down foreign invaders (bacteria, viruses) and food particles engulfed by the cell.
- **2. Autophagy (Cellular Recycling)**:
  - Digests worn-out or damaged cell organelles (e.g., non-functional mitochondria) and recycles their basic molecular building blocks.
- **3. Autolysis (Self-Destruction / Programmed Cell Death)**:
  - During severe cell injury, infection, or starvation, lysosomes burst open, releasing digestive enzymes into the cytoplasm, which completely digests the parent cell.
- **4. Lysosomal Storage Diseases**:
  - Genetic deficiency of specific lysosomal enzymes causes harmful accumulation of undigested molecules (e.g., **Tay-Sachs disease**, **Gaucher's disease**, **Niemann-Pick disease**).

---

### 2. Overview of Other Cell Organelles in the Options

*Distinct cellular nicknames and primary roles:*

- **Mitochondria ("Powerhouse of the Cell")**:
  - Double-membrane organelle responsible for cellular respiration and generation of cellular energy currency in the form of **ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate)**.
  - Contains its own circular DNA and 70S ribosomes (semi-autonomous organelle).
- **Golgi Apparatus / Golgi Body ("Post Office / Traffic Police of the Cell")**:
  - Discovered by **Camillo Golgi** (1898); consists of flat membranous sacs called cisternae.
  - Modifies, sorts, packages, and ships proteins and lipids; also plays a direct role in the **formation of primary lysosomes**.
- **Ribosomes ("Protein Factories of the Cell")**:
  - Discovered by **George Palade** (1955); non-membrane-bound granular structures composed of rRNA and proteins.
  - The universal site of **protein synthesis / translation**.

### Q2

The digestive enzymes found inside lysosomes are broadly classified as:

- [ ] Oxidoreductases
- [ ] Transferases
- [x] Hydrolytic enzymes (Acid Hydrolases)
- [ ] Polymerases

> Lysosomal enzymes are hydrolytic enzymes (acid hydrolases) that require an acidic pH to cleave chemical bonds by adding water.

- [ ] Lysosome
- [x] Ribosome
