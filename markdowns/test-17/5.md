# 📌 Chitra-Vichitra Fair & Tribal Heritage of Gujarat

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Gujarat**.
- **Location & Venue**: Held annually at the confluence of the **Sabay and Wakal rivers** in the **Sabarkantha district** of Gujarat.
- **Key Tribes Involved**: Predominantly celebrated by the **'Garasia'** and **'Bhil'** tribal communities.
- **Timing**: Celebrated shortly after the **Holi festival**, usually falling in the **March–April** period, marking a major cultural gathering and local trade milestone.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Features of the Chitra-Vichitra Fair

- **Cultural Significance**: One of the most vibrant and historically significant tribal fairs in western India, drawing thousands of indigenous participants adorned in traditional, colorful attire.
- **Economic & Social Hub**: Serves as a major marketplace for **local trade**, handicrafts, and agricultural goods, alongside folk songs and traditional dances recounting ancestral folklore.
