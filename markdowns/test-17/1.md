# 📌 Rigvedic Rivers & Their Ancient Names (Sapta Sindhu)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Parushini** (or **Parushni** / *Iravati*).
- **Vedic Identity**: In the Rigvedic texts (notably the *Nadistuti Sukta* in Mandala 10), the river **Ravi** is referred to as **Parushni**.
- **Historical Significance**: The legendary **Battle of the Ten Kings (*Dasrajan Yuddha*)** was fought on the banks of the **Parushni River**, where King **Sudas** of the Bharata tribe emerged victorious against a confederacy of ten tribal kings.
- **Other Options in the Question**:
  - **Askini (Asikni)**: Ancient Vedic name for the **Chenab** river.
  - **Shutudri (Sutudri)**: Ancient Vedic name for the **Sutlej** river.
  - **Kalindi**: Traditional/epic name for the **Yamuna** river.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Nadistuti Sukta & The Sapta Sindhu

*The Rigveda mentions numerous rivers flowing through the north-western part of the Indian subcontinent (the region termed 'Sapta Sindhu' — the Land of Seven Rivers):*

| Modern River Name | Rigvedic / Ancient Name | Greek Classical Name | Key Exam Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ravi** | **Parushni / Iravati** | *Hydraotes* | Site of the Battle of Ten Kings (*Dasrajan*) |
| **Chenab** | **Asikni (Askini)** | *Akesines* | Largest tributary of the Indus by volume |
| **Jhelum** | **Vitasta** | *Hydaspes* | Site of Battle of the Hydaspes (Alexander vs. Porus, 326 BCE) |
| **Sutlej** | **Shutudri / Sutudri** | *Zadadres / Hesidros* | Originates from Rakshastal near Lake Manasarovar |
| **Beas** | **Vipasa / Vipash** | *Hyphasis* | Alexander's army refused to advance beyond this river |
| **Indus** | **Sindhu** | *Indos* | Most prominently mentioned river in the Rigveda |
| **Ghaggar / Hakra** | **Saraswati (Naditama)** | — | Holiest Rigvedic river (*Naditama* = Best of Rivers) |
| **Ganges (Ganga)** | **Ganga** | *Ganges* | Mentioned only **once** in the Rigveda |
| **Yamuna** | **Yamuna / Kalindi** | *Diamouna* | Mentioned **three times** in the Rigveda |

---

### 2. Trans-Indus (Afghan) Rivers Mentioned in the Rigveda

*Western tributaries of the Indus documented in Rigvedic hymns:*

- **Kubha**: Modern **Kabul** River (Afghanistan).
- **Krumu**: Modern **Kurram** River.
- **Gomal (Gomati)**: Modern **Gomal** River.
- **Suvastu**: Modern **Swat** River.

- [ ] Kurram River

### Q4

- [x] 1 time
- [ ] 3 times
- [ ] 10 times
- [ ] Never mentioned

> The river Ganga is mentioned only once (in the 10th Mandala's *Nadistuti Sukta*), indicating that the core focus of early Rigvedic geography was centered around the Sapta Sindhu basin rather than the Gangetic plain.
