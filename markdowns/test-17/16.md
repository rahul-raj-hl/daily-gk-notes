# 📌 Operation Sagar Bandhu & Indian Humanitarian Operations

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Operation Sagar Bandhu**.
- **Operation Mission**: **Operation Sagar Bandhu** was launched by the Indian Armed Forces (led by the Indian Navy and Coast Guard) as a Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (**HADR**) mission in response to the devastation caused by **Cyclone Ditwah**.
- **Strategic Doctrine**: Conducted in line with India's vision of **SAGAR** (*Security and Growth for All in the Region*) and the **"Neighbourhood First"** policy, establishing India as the first responder in the Indian Ocean Region (IOR).
- **Core Activities**: Deployment of naval relief supplies, emergency medicines, freshwater generation units, evacuation efforts, and mobile medical teams.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. India as the Net Security Provider & First Responder (SAGAR Policy)

- **SAGAR Framework**: Unveiled by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in 2015, emphasizing collective maritime security, disaster management, and regional cooperation across the Indian Ocean littoral states.
- **HADR Capabilities**: The Indian Navy routinely deploys amphibious transport docks (such as *INS Jalashwa*), destroyers, and landing ship tanks (LSTs) loaded with HADR bricks, food, clothing, and field hospital units during maritime crises.
