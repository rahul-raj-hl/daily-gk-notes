# 📌 Census 2011: Urbanization & Population Distribution

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **31 percent** (exact figure: **31.16%**).
- **Rural vs. Urban Breakdown (Census 2011)**:
  - **Urban Population**: **31.16%** (~377.1 million people / 37.71 crore).
  - **Rural Population**: **68.84%** (~833.5 million people / 83.35 crore).
- **Total Population of India (2011)**: **1,210.85 million** (1.21 billion / ~121.08 crore).
- **Decadal Growth Rate (2001–2011)**:
  - Overall National Growth: **17.70%**
  - Urban Population Growth: **31.80%**
  - Rural Population Growth: **12.30%**

### 2. Definition of an Urban Area (Census of India)

*For Census purposes, an urban area is classified under two heads:*

1. **Statutory Towns**: All places with a municipality, corporation, cantonment board, or notified town area committee.
2. **Census Towns (CT)**: Non-statutory settlements that satisfy all the following **three criteria**:
   - A minimum total population of **5,000**.
   - At least **75% of the male main working population** engaged in non-agricultural pursuits.
   - A population density of at least **400 persons per sq. km** (1,000 per sq. mile).

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "31 IN CITIES, 69 IN VILLAGES"**
> - **~31%** $\rightarrow$ Reside in urban towns and cities
> - **~69%** $\rightarrow$ Reside in rural villages
