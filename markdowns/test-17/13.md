# 📌 Poverty Alleviation Programs & The Fifth Five-Year Plan

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Fifth Five-Year Plan** (1974–1978/79).
- **Core Dual Objectives**:
  1. **Garibi Hatao** (*Removal of Poverty* / Poverty Alleviation).
  2. **Attainment of Self-Reliance** (*Atmanirbharta*).
- **Key Architect / Model**: Drafted and prepared by **D.P. Dhar**.
- **Major Welfare Scheme Launched**: The **Minimum Needs Programme (MNP)** was introduced in **1974** during the first year of the Fifth Plan to provide basic essential services (health, water supply, rural education, housing).
- **Abrupt Termination**: The Fifth Plan was terminated one year early in **1978** by the newly elected **Janata Party Government** led by Morarji Desai, who subsequently introduced the **Rolling Plan (1978–1980)**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Highlights of the Fifth Five-Year Plan (1974–1978)

- **Target vs. Actual Growth**:
  - Target Growth Rate: **4.4%**
  - Actual Growth Rate: **4.8%** (Deemed a successful plan despite high inflation and the 1973 global oil shock).
- **Twenty-Point Programme (TPP)**: Introduced by Prime Minister Indira Gandhi in **July 1975** to improve the quality of life, strengthen social justice, and accelerate poverty reduction.
- **Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)**: Established on **2 October 1975** under the recommendations of the **M. Narasimham Working Group** (via the RRB Ordinance / Act of 1976) to expand institutional credit to rural poor, small farmers, and agricultural labourers.
- **National Highway System**: Introduced to boost national infrastructure and transport connectivity.

---

### 2. Comprehensive Comparison of Key Five-Year Plans

*Essential focus areas and landmarks across Five-Year Plans:*

| Five-Year Plan | Duration | Model / Key Leader | Primary Objective / Slogan | Major Milestones / Schemes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st FYP** | **1951–1956** | **Harrod-Domar Model** | Agriculture, irrigation & power projects | Bhakra Nangal, Hirakud, Damodar Valley dams; 5 IITs established |
| **2nd FYP** | **1956–1961** | **P.C. Mahalanobis Model** | Rapid industrialization & heavy capital goods | Steel plants at Rourkela, Bhilai, Durgapur; Atomic Energy Commission |
| **3rd FYP** | **1961–1966** | Gadgil Yojana / Chakravarty Model | Self-reliant & self-generating economy | Bokaro Steel Plant, FCI, CACP; failed due to 1962 & 1965 wars |
| **Plan Holidays** | **1966–1969** | Annual Plans | Crisis management & food security | Launch of the **Green Revolution** (HYV seeds) |
| **4th FYP** | **1969–1974** | Ashok Rudra & A.S. Manne Model | Growth with stability & progressive self-reliance | Nationalization of 14 Banks (1969); Pokhran-I (*Smiling Buddha*, 1974) |
| **5th FYP** | **1974–1978** | **D.P. Dhar Model** | **Garibi Hatao (Poverty Removal) & Self-Reliance** | **Minimum Needs Programme (1974)**; Twenty-Point Programme (1975); RRBs (1975) |
| **Rolling Plan** | **1978–1980** | Gunnar Myrdal concept / D.T. Lakdawala | Flexible short-term annual adjustments | Introduced by Janata Party under Morarji Desai |
| **6th FYP** | **1980–1985** | Input-Output Model | Direct attack on poverty & economic modernization | **IRDP, NREP, TRYSEM, DWACRA**; NABARD & EXIM Bank founded (1982) |
| **7th FYP** | **1985–1990** | Pranab Mukherjee Model | Food, Work, and Productivity | Jawahar Rozgar Yojana (JRY, 1989); SEBI formed (1988) |
| **8th FYP** | **1992–1997** | Rao-Manmohan Model | Human resource development & LPG reforms | Launch of **LPG Reforms (1991)**; Pradhan Mantri Rozgar Yojana (PMRY, 1993) |

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Establishment of NABARD & EXIM Bank** | **Sixth Five-Year Plan (1982)** | Confusing with the 5th FYP |
| **Green Revolution Launch** | **Plan Holiday (1966–1969)** | Confusing with the 3rd or 4th FYP |
| **14 Major Commercial Banks Nationalized** | **Fourth Five-Year Plan (1969)** | Confusing with the 1980 nationalization of 6 banks (6th FYP) |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "5 MEIN GARIBI HATAO, 6 MEIN NABARD LAO"**
> - **5th Plan** $\rightarrow$ **Garibi Hatao** & Minimum Needs Programme (MNP)
> - **6th Plan** $\rightarrow$ Institutional expansion (**NABARD**, EXIM Bank, IRDP)
