# 📌 Yuva AI for All & The 'Kaushal Rath' Mobile Programme

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Yuva AI for All**.
- **The Initiative**: The Government launched the **'Kaushal Rath'** mobile programme under the **'Yuva AI for All'** national literacy drive to democratize AI education across the grassroots level.
- **What is Kaushal Rath?**: A specialized **mobile AI-enabled digital laboratory-on-wheels** designed to travel directly to schools, colleges, ITIs, and polytechnics to provide hands-on AI literacy and practical demonstrations to students and youth.
- **Course Framework**: 'Yuva AI for All' offers an accessible **four-hour self-paced introductory course** covering fundamental concepts, real-world applications, and ethics of Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Components of 'Yuva AI for All' & 'Kaushal Rath'

- **National AI Literacy Focus**: Geared towards demystifying AI concepts for young students, bridging the digital divide between Tier-1 metro hubs and Tier-2/3 cities, rural areas, and industrial training institutes (ITIs).
- **Core Pillars Covered in the 4-Hour Course**:
  - Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
  - Ethical AI and responsible usage frameworks.
  - Everyday industrial and social applications of AI tools.
- **Mobility & Outreach**: The *Kaushal Rath* eliminates infrastructure constraints in semi-urban and rural schools by bringing high-tech computing labs and AI learning modules directly to the student doorstep.
