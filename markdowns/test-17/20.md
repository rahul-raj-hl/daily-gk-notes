# 📌 The Ghadar Movement: Armed Uprising & External Networks

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A**.
- **Assertion (A) is True**: The **Ghadar Party** explicitly aimed to overthrow British colonial rule through a nationwide **armed rebellion and mutiny** of Indian soldiers, strategically timing their insurrection with Britain's involvement in **World War I (1914–1918)**.
- **Reason (R) is True**: The movement was heavily and secretly funded by Indian expatriates, immigrant labourers, wealthy donors (such as Baba Gurdit Singh, Pandit Kanshi Ram), and international revolutionary networks based abroad across the United States, Canada, and Europe.
- **Why R is NOT the Correct Explanation of A**: 
  - External financial backing and logistical networks enabled the operational functioning of the party, but the *ideological decision* to wage an armed revolution during WWI arose from their **anti-imperialist revolutionary philosophy** and the geopolitical opportunity of Britain being militarily stretched and vulnerable during the World War.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Strategy of WWI Armed Insurrection

*Why the Ghadar Party launched an armed uprising during World War I:*

- **Capitalizing on Britain's Vulnerability**: The outbreak of World War I in August 1914 led to a substantial withdrawal of British troops from India to European and Middle Eastern battlefronts. Ghadar leaders saw this as a historic window of opportunity:
  > *"England's difficulty is India's opportunity."*
- **Call for Armed Mutiny**: Thousands of Ghadarites left North America aboard ships (such as the *Tosa Maru*) to return to India and incite mutinies among Indian soldiers stationed in military cantonments (Lahore, Ferozepur, Rawalpindi, Meerut).
- **Target Date**: The pan-India military uprising was originally scheduled for **21 February 1915** under the leadership of **Rash Behari Bose** and **Sachindra Nath Sanyal**, before being compromised by police intelligence infiltrator Kirpal Singh.

---

### 2. Financial & Logistical Backing Abroad

*How the revolutionary machinery was sustained outside India:*

- **Immigrant Community Contributions**: Punjabi Sikh, Hindu, and Muslim immigrant farmers, lumber-mill workers, and small traders in Oregon, California, and British Columbia contributed substantial portions of their wages to the party treasury managed by **Pandit Kanshi Ram**.
- **The Berlin Committee Connection**: The **Berlin Committee for Indian Independence** (led by Virendranath Chattopadhyaya and Bhupendranath Datta) coordinated with the **German Foreign Office** under the **"Hindu-German Conspiracy"** (Zimmermann Plan) to channel funds, ammunition, and logistical support to Ghadar networks.
- **San Francisco Center**: The party purchased its own printing press at **Yugantar Ashram** (San Francisco) to mass-distribute the revolutionary weekly *Ghadar* free of charge across global Indian diaspora networks.
