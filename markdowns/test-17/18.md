# 📌 MoSPI New Identity, "Saankhyikee" Mascot & Statistics Framework

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI)**.
- **New Mascot**: MoSPI unveiled its official mascot named **“Saankhyikee”** (derived from the Sanskrit/Hindi word *Saankhyiki* meaning *Statistics*) along with a redesigned modern logo to represent data-driven governance, transparency, and statistical literacy.
- **Core Role of MoSPI**: The apex central agency responsible for the development, coordination, and integrity of India’s official statistical system, national accounts statistics (GDP/GVA), Consumer Price Index (CPI), and Index of Industrial Production (IIP).
- **National Statistics Day**: Celebrated annually in India on **29 June** to mark the birth anniversary of the *Father of Indian Statistics*, **Prof. Prasanta Chandra (P.C.) Mahalanobis**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Structure and Wings of MoSPI

*MoSPI operates with two main wings:*

- **1. Statistics Wing (National Statistical Office - NSO)**:
  - Formed in **2019** by merging the **Central Statistics Office (CSO)** and the **National Sample Survey Office (NSSO)** on the recommendations of the **Rangarajan Commission**.
  - Responsible for computing national income (GDP), compiling macroeconomic indices like **CPI (Combined)** and **IIP**, and conducting Periodic Labour Force Surveys (PLFS) and Annual Surveys of Industries (ASI).
- **2. Programme Implementation Wing**:
  - Monitors the implementation of Central Infrastructure Projects costing ₹150 Crore and above.
  - Oversees the execution of the **Member of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS)**.
  - Tracks progress of the **Twenty Point Programme (TPP)**.

---

### 2. Major Statistical Indices & Surveys Released by MoSPI

| Indicator / Publication | Frequency | Base Year | Purpose / Scope |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Gross Domestic Product (GDP)** | Quarterly & Annual | **2011–12** | Measures total economic value of goods and services produced |
| **Consumer Price Index (CPI - Combined)** | Monthly | **2012 = 100** | Retail inflation measure used by RBI Monetary Policy Committee |
| **Index of Industrial Production (IIP)** | Monthly | **2011–12 = 100** | Measures physical output growth in Mining, Manufacturing & Electricity |
| **Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)** | Quarterly (Urban) & Annual (Rural+Urban) | — | Key national survey on employment, unemployment, and labor participation |

> **Crucial Distinction**: Wholesale Price Index (**WPI**) is **NOT** published by MoSPI. WPI is released by the **Office of the Economic Adviser, Ministry of Commerce and Industry** (Base Year: 2011–12).

| Index / Concept | Releasing Body | Common Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CPI (Consumer Price Index)** | **MoSPI (NSO)** | Confusing with RBI or Ministry of Labour (Labour Bureau releases CPI-IW) |
| **WPI (Wholesale Price Index)** | **Ministry of Commerce & Industry** | Mistakenly selecting MoSPI |
| **SDG India Index** | **NITI Aayog** | Confusing with MoSPI (MoSPI provides the National Indicator Framework) |
| **MPLADS Implementation** | **MoSPI** | Confusing with Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs or Rural Development |
