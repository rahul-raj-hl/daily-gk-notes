# 📌 Reactivity Series of Metals & Displacement Reactions

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Only Statement 1 is true**.
- **Statement I is True**: When an iron nail/piece ($\text{Fe}$) is placed in a blue copper sulfate ($\text{CuSO}_4$) solution, a **reddish-brown coating of metallic copper** deposits onto the iron surface, and the blue solution gradually turns **pale light green** due to the formation of ferrous sulfate ($\text{FeSO}_4$).
- **Statement II is False**: **Iron ($\text{Fe}$) is more reactive than copper ($\text{Cu}$)** in the metal activity series. Therefore, iron displaces copper from its salt solution, not the other way around.
- **Chemical Equation**:

$$\text{Fe (s)} + \text{CuSO}_4\text{ (aq, Blue)} \longrightarrow \text{FeSO}_4\text{ (aq, Pale Green)} + \text{Cu (s, Reddish-Brown)}$$

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Reactivity (Activity) Series of Metals

*Arrangement of metals in the decreasing order of their chemical reactivity / electropositive nature:*

| Metal Name | Symbol | Position / Relative Reactivity | Reaction with Water / Acids |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Potassium** | $\text{K}$ | **Most Reactive** | Reacts violently with cold water |
| **Sodium** | $\text{Na}$ | Highly Reactive | Reacts vigorously with cold water |
| **Calcium** | $\text{Ca}$ | Highly Reactive | Reacts with cold water (floats due to $\text{H}_2$ bubbles) |
| **Magnesium** | $\text{Mg}$ | Reactive | Reacts with hot water / steam |
| **Aluminium** | $\text{Al}$ | Moderate | Reacts only with steam |
| **Zinc** | $\text{Zn}$ | Moderate | Displaces $\text{Fe, Pb, Cu, Ag}$ from solutions |
| **Iron** | $\text{Fe}$ | **More reactive than Cu** | Reacts slowly with steam; displaces $\text{Cu}$ |
| **Lead** | $\text{Pb}$ | Low | Reacts very slowly with acids |
| **[Hydrogen]** | $[\text{H}]$ | *Non-metal reference standard* | Reference standard ($E^\circ = 0.00\text{ V}$) |
| **Copper** | $\text{Cu}$ | **Less reactive than Fe** | Does NOT displace $\text{H}_2$ from dilute acids |
| **Mercury** | $\text{Hg}$ | Very Low | Noble/unreactive |
| **Silver** | $\text{Ag}$ | Very Low | Unreactive |
| **Gold** | $\text{Au}$ | **Least Reactive (Noble)** | Dissolves only in Aqua Regia |
| **Platinum** | $\text{Pt}$ | **Least Reactive (Noble)** | Dissolves only in Aqua Regia |

---

### 2. Principles of Single Displacement Reactions

- **Golden Rule**: A metal placed **higher** in the reactivity series can displace any metal positioned **below it** from its aqueous salt solution.
- **Why Statement II Fails**: Copper lies below Iron ($\text{Cu} < \text{Fe}$); hence, copper can **never** displace iron from a ferrous sulfate solution ($\text{Cu} + \text{FeSO}_4 \longrightarrow \text{No Reaction}$).
- **Zinc with Copper Sulfate**:

$$\text{Zn (s)} + \text{CuSO}_4\text{ (aq, Blue)} \longrightarrow \text{ZnSO}_4\text{ (aq, Colourless)} + \text{Cu (s, Reddish-Brown)}$$

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Reaction / Phenomenon | Initial Colour | Final Observation / Product |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$\text{Fe} + \text{CuSO}_4$** | Blue ($\text{CuSO}_4$) | **Pale Green** ($\text{FeSO}_4$) + **Reddish-brown deposit** ($\text{Cu}$) |
| **$\text{Zn} + \text{CuSO}_4$** | Blue ($\text{CuSO}_4$) | **Colourless** ($\text{ZnSO}_4$) + **Reddish-brown deposit** ($\text{Cu}$) |
| **$\text{Cu} + \text{AgNO}_3$** | Colourless ($\text{AgNO}_3$) | **Blue** ($\text{Cu(NO}_3)_2$) + **Shining Silver deposit** ($\text{Ag}$) |
| **$\text{Cu} + \text{FeSO}_4$** | Pale Green ($\text{FeSO}_4$) | **No Reaction** (as $\text{Cu}$ is less reactive than $\text{Fe}$) |
| **Reaction with Dilute $\text{HCl}$** | Metals above $[\text{H}]$ | Liberate **$\text{H}_2$ gas** with a 'pop' sound |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Scenario | Outcome | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Iron nail in $\text{CuSO}_4$** | **Displacement occurs** | $\text{Fe}$ is above $\text{Cu}$ in activity series |
| **Copper coin in $\text{FeSO}_4$** | **No reaction occurs** | $\text{Cu}$ is below $\text{Fe}$; cannot displace $\text{Fe}$ |
| **Copper in dilute $\text{HCl}$** | **No reaction / No $\text{H}_2$ gas** | $\text{Cu}$ lies below Hydrogen |
| **Zinc in dilute $\text{H}_2\text{SO}_4$** | **Vigorous reaction ($\text{H}_2 \uparrow$)** | $\text{Zn}$ lies above Hydrogen |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Consider the following statements regarding the reactivity series of metals:
- **Statement I**: A piece of iron placed in a solution of copper sulfate gradually develops a reddish layer.
- **Statement II**: Copper is more reactive than iron and replaces it in the solution.

Which of these statements is/are correct?

- [ ] Only Statement 2 is true
- [x] Only Statement 1 is true
- [ ] Both statements are true
- [ ] Both statements are false

> Statement 1 is true because iron displaces copper, depositing reddish-brown copper on the iron. Statement 2 is false because iron is more reactive than copper.

---

### PYQ 2

When an iron nail is immersed in an aqueous solution of copper sulphate for half an hour, the colour of the solution changes from blue to:

- [ ] Colourless
- [ ] Dark Blue
- [x] Light Green
- [ ] Yellow

> The solution turns light green due to the formation of ferrous sulphate ($\text{FeSO}_4$).

---

### PYQ 3

Which of the following metals will NOT displace hydrogen gas from dilute hydrochloric acid ($\text{HCl}$)?

- [ ] Zinc
- [ ] Iron
- [ ] Magnesium
- [x] Copper

> Copper lies below hydrogen in the reactivity series and therefore cannot reduce $\text{H}^+$ ions to release $\text{H}_2$ gas from dilute acids.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

What is observed when a strip of Zinc metal is added to a blue copper sulfate solution?

- [ ] The solution turns dark yellow
- [x] The blue colour fades and becomes completely colourless
- [ ] The solution turns blood red
- [ ] No chemical reaction takes place

> Zinc displaces copper to form zinc sulfate ($\text{ZnSO}_4$), which is a clear, colourless solution, alongside reddish copper precipitation.

---

### Q2

Which of the following elements is the LEAST reactive metal according to the chemical reactivity series?

- [ ] Silver
- [ ] Copper
- [ ] Iron
- [x] Gold

> Gold ($\text{Au}$) and Platinum ($\text{Pt}$) are noble metals situated at the bottom of the reactivity series.

---

### Q3

A student added copper turnings to an aqueous solution of ferrous sulfate ($\text{FeSO}_4$). What change in colour will be observed?

- [ ] The solution turns deep blue
- [ ] The solution turns colourless
- [x] No colour change occurs (no reaction)
- [ ] The solution turns dark black

> Because copper is less reactive than iron, no displacement reaction takes place and the solution remains unchanged.

---

### Q4

Which metal floats on the surface of water during its reaction with cold water because the bubbles of hydrogen gas formed stick to its surface?

- [ ] Sodium
- [ ] Potassium
- [x] Calcium
- [ ] Iron

> Calcium reacts less violently with water than sodium/potassium; the heat evolved is insufficient for hydrogen to catch fire, causing the calcium metal to float as $\text{H}_2$ bubbles stick to it.
