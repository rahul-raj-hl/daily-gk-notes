# 📌 Impeachment Procedure of the President of India (Article 61)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Constitutional Provision**: Prescribed under **Article 61** of the Constitution of India as a quasi-judicial procedure.
- **House of Origin**: Impeachment charges can be initiated in **either House of Parliament** (Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha).
- **Notice & Initiation**: Requires a **14-day advance written notice** signed by at least **one-fourth ($1/4^{\text{th}}$ / 25%) of the total membership** of the initiating House.
- **Investigative Function**: The second House acts strictly as a **fact-finding body** to investigate the framed charges (or cause them to be investigated), rather than serving as a prosecuting authority.
- **Removal Threshold**: Requires a **two-thirds majority of the total membership of both Houses**, making the impeachment process exceptionally stringent and rare.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Constitutional Parameters of Impeachment

*Core structural pillars of the Article 61 impeachment mechanism:*

| Parameter | Constitutional Mandate | Legal Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sole Constitutional Ground** | **"Violation of the Constitution"** | Specific ground defined under Article 61(1) |
| **Initiating House** | **Either Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha** | Either House can prefer the initial charges |
| **Notice Requirement** | **14 days' written notice** | Prior notice served directly to the President |
| **Resolution Initiation Quorum** | **$\ge 1/4^{\text{th}}$ of total members** | Signed by at least 25% members of initiating House |
| **Nature of Investigating House** | **Fact-finding / Quasi-judicial body** | Examines charges; allows President right to defense |
| **Voting Majority in Each House** | **$\ge 2/3^{\text{rd}}$ of TOTAL membership** | Highest voting threshold in the Indian Constitution |

---

### 2. Functional Role: Fact-Finding Body vs. Prosecuting Authority

- **Fact-Finding Role**: When one House frames charges against the President, the other House is tasked with investigating those charges to verify their veracity.
- **Quasi-Judicial Safeguard**: It operates as an impartial investigative tribunal. It does not prosecute or file criminal charges against the President; rather, it assesses whether the constitutional violations framed by the first House are substantiated.
- **Right to Legal Representation**: The President is constitutionally guaranteed the right to appear in person or be represented by an authorized representative or counsel (such as the Attorney General of India) during the investigation.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Notice Signature** | **$\mathbf{1/4^{\text{th}}}$ of Total Members** | Must sign the resolution to introduce it |
| **2. Advance Warning** | **14 Days** | Minimum formal notice given to the President |
| **3. 1st House Voting** | **$\mathbf{2/3^{\text{rd}}}$ of Total Membership** | Must pass before reaching the second House |
| **4. 2nd House Action** | **Fact-finding investigation** | Checks factual accuracy of charges |
| **5. 2nd House Voting** | **$\mathbf{2/3^{\text{rd}}}$ of Total Membership** | President stands removed on the day of passage |
| **Participation Anomaly** | **Nominated MPs participate** | Elected MLAs do NOT participate |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Constitutional Aspect | Correct Fact | Common Trap / Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Notice Quorum** | **$1/4^{\text{th}}$ (25%) of total members** | Confusing with standard parliamentary quorum ($1/10^{\text{th}}$) |
| **Investigating House Role** | **Fact-finding body** | Assuming the House acts as a prosecuting agency |
| **Majority Required** | **$2/3^{\text{rd}}$ of TOTAL membership** | Confusing with $2/3^{\text{rd}}$ of members present and voting |
| **House of Origin** | **Either House of Parliament** | Confusing with Vice-President's removal (Rajya Sabha only) |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "1/4TH INITIATES, 14 DAYS WARNS, 2/3RD OF TOTAL SEALS THE VOTE"**
> - **$1/4^{\text{th}}$** $\rightarrow$ Members needed to sign notice
> - **14 Days** $\rightarrow$ Notice period
> - **$2/3^{\text{rd}}$ Total** $\rightarrow$ Supermajority needed in both Houses
