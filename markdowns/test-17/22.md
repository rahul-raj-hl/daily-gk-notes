# 📌 Pandit Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande & Hindustani Music

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Pandit Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande** (1860–1936).
- **Major Masterwork**: Compiled the monumental 6-volume series ***Kramik Pustak Malika***, which documented, standardized, and notated hundreds of classical compositions, *bandishes*, and ragas.
- **The 10-Thaat Classification**: Systematized North Indian (Hindustani) classical ragas into **10 parent scales (Thaats)**: *Bilawal, Kalyan, Khamaj, Kafi, Asavari, Bhairav, Bhairavi, Todi, Poorvi, and Marwa*.
- **Institutional Legacy**: Established premier institutions including the **Bhatkhande Music Institute / University** in Lucknow (originally *Marris College of Music*, 1926) and the **Madhav Music College** in Gwalior.
- **Notation System**: Developed the widely used **Bhatkhande Swarlipi** (musical notation system), standardizing musical pedagogy across India.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Modernizers of Hindustani Music: Bhatkhande vs. Paluskar

*The two legendary pioneers who revolutionized Indian classical music in the 20th century:*

| Feature / Dimension | **Pandit Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande** (1860–1936) | **Pandit Vishnu Digambar Paluskar** (1872–1931) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Contribution** | Theoretical framework, raga grammar, notation & documentation | Mass performance, spiritualization & democratization of music |
| **Raga Classification** | Classified ragas into **10 Thaats** | Emphasized *Raga-Ragini* and devotional singing |
| **Seminal Books** | • ***Kramik Pustak Malika*** (6 vols)<br>• ***Hindustani Sangeet Paddhati***<br>• ***Lakshya Sangeetam*** | • ***Sangeet Bal Prakash***<br>• Composed tune for *"Raghupati Raghav Raja Ram"* |
| **Institutions Founded** | • **Marris College of Music, Lucknow** (1926)<br>• Madhav Music College, Gwalior | • **Gandharva Mahavidyalaya**, Lahore (1901)<br>• Gandharva Mahavidyalaya, Mumbai (1911) |
| **Notation System** | **Bhatkhande Notation System** (horizontal lines below/above for *komal/tivra*) | **Paluskar Notation System** (distinct signs for *matras* and beats) |

### 2. The 10 Thaats of Hindustani Classical Music (Bhatkhande System)

*The 10 parent scales that encompass all primary Hindustani ragas:*

| Thaat Name | Note Characteristics (*Swaras*) | Representative / Ashraya Ragas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bilawal** | All 7 pure/natural notes (*Shuddha Swaras*) — Indian equivalent of Western Major Scale | Raga Alhaiya Bilawal, Deskar |
| **Kalyan** | Only *Tivra Ma* (sharp 4th), all other notes *Shuddha* | Raga Yaman, Bhupali, Shuddha Kalyan |
| **Khamaj** | *Komal Ni* (flat 7th), all other notes *Shuddha* | Raga Khamaj, Des, Tilak Kamod |
| **Kafi** | *Komal Ga* and *Komal Ni* (flat 3rd & 7th) | Raga Kafi, Bageshree, Bhimpalasi |
| **Asavari** | *Komal Ga, Dha, Ni* (flat 3rd, 6th & 7th) — Natural Minor Scale | Raga Asavari, Jaunpuri, Darbari Kanada |
| **Bhairav** | *Komal Re* and *Komal Dha* (flat 2nd & 6th) | Raga Bhairav, Ramkali, Ahir Bhairav |
| **Bhairavi** | All 4 flat notes (*Komal Re, Ga, Dha, Ni*) | Raga Bhairavi, Malkauns, Bilaskhani Todi |
| **Todi** | *Komal Re, Ga, Dha* + *Tivra Ma* | Raga Miyan Ki Todi, Gujari Todi |
| **Poorvi** | *Komal Re, Dha* + *Tivra Ma* + *Shuddha Ga, Ni* | Raga Poorvi, Puriya Dhanashree |
| **Marwa** | *Komal Re* + *Tivra Ma* + *Shuddha Ga, Dha, Ni* (Omits *Pa*) | Raga Marwa, Puriya, Sohini |

### 3. Other Key Personalities in the Options

- **Ustad Allauddin Khan** (1862–1972): Legendary multi-instrumentalist (sarod maestro), composer, and founder of the **Maihar Gharana**; teacher of Pandit Ravi Shankar, Ustad Ali Akbar Khan, and Annapurna Devi.
- **Swami Haridas** (16th Century): Renowned saint-musician of Vrindavan; pioneer of the **Dhrupad** style and spiritual guru of court musicians **Miyan Tansen** and **Baiju Bawra**.
