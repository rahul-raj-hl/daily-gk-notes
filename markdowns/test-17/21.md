# 📌 Sattriya Dance & Srimanta Sankaradeva

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Sattriya**.
- **Origin & Creator**: Introduced in the **15th century CE** (around 1450–1500) by the great Vaishnavite saint, polymath, and social reformer **Mahapurush Srimanta Sankaradeva** (along with his foremost disciple **Madhavadeva**).
- **Associated Institution**: Nurtured and preserved in the **Sattras** (neo-Vaishnavite monasteries) and **Namghars** (prayer halls) across **Assam** (especially on Majuli Island).
- **Classical Recognition**: Recognized as the **8th Classical Dance of India** by the **Sangeet Natak Akademi** on **15 November 2000**.
- **Key Theatrical Form**: Integral to the Assamese one-act religious plays known as **Ankiya Nat** (incorporating songs known as **Borgeet** written in the literary language *Brajavali*).

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The 8 Classical Dances of India (Sangeet Natak Akademi)

*Complete reference of classical dances recognized by the apex national academy:*

| Dance Form | State of Origin | Core Theme / Associated Tradition | Key Exponents |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sattriya** | **Assam** | Neo-Vaishnavite Bhakti movement (*Ekasarana Dharma*) | Bapuram Barbhayan, Jatin Goswami, Ghanakanta Bora |
| **Bharatanatyam** | **Tamil Nadu** | *Dasiyattam*, temple devadasis, Shaivite/Vaishnavite | Rukmini Devi Arundale, Yamini Krishnamurthy, Padma Subrahmanyam |
| **Kathak** | **Uttar Pradesh** / North India | Storytelling (*Katha kahe so Kathak*), Radha-Krishna *leela* | Pandit Birju Maharaj, Sitara Devi, Shambhu Maharaj, Lachhu Maharaj |
| **Kathakali** | **Kerala** | Dance-drama, elaborate facial makeup (*Aharya*), epic battles | Kalamandalam Gopi, Guru Kunchu Kurup |
| **Mohiniyattam** | **Kerala** | Dance of the enchantress (*Mohini*), graceful *Lasya* movements | Sunanda Nair, Bharati Shivaji, Kanak Rele |
| **Kuchipudi** | **Andhra Pradesh** | Bhagavata Mela, *Tarangam* (dancing on brass plate edge) | Yamini Krishnamurthy, Raja & Radha Reddy, Vempati Chinna Satyam |
| **Odissi** | **Odisha** | Mahari & Gotipua traditions; *Tribhanga* posture, Jagannath Bhakti | Kelucharan Mohapatra, Sanjukta Panigrahi, Sonal Mansingh |
| **Manipuri** | **Manipur** | *Raasleela* (Radha-Krishna), *Pung Cholom*, cylindrical drum skirt (*Kumin*) | Guru Bipin Singh, Jhaveri Sisters (Nayna, Suverna, Ranjana, Darshana) |

### 2. Stylistic Elements & Anatomy of Sattriya

- **Two Distinct Traditions**:
  - **Gayan-Bhayanar Nach**: Performed by singers (*Gayans*) and percussionists playing the barrel drum (*Bhayans*).
  - **Kharmanar Nach / Natua Nach**: Distinct theatrical dance performances.
- **Masculine vs. Feminine Aspects**:
  - **Tandava (Paurashik)**: Energetic, vigorous masculine movements (e.g., *Ora*, *Chali*).
  - **Lasya (Stree)**: Gentle, graceful, lyrical feminine movements.
- **Musical Accompaniments & Costumes**:
  - **Instruments**: **Khol** (two-headed earthen asymmetrical drum), **Tala** (cymbals), and Flute.
  - **Costumes**: Traditional Assamese silk textiles—**Pat Silk** and **Muga Silk**; male dancers wear the *Dhoti* and *Paguri* (turban), while female dancers wear the three-piece *Ghuri*, *Chador*, and *Kanchi* (waist cloth) along with traditional silver filigree Assamese jewellery (*Kopaali*, *Muthi-kharu*).

| Dance Form | State | Common Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
