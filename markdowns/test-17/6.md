# 📌 Sheetal Devi & Able-Bodied Archery Team Qualification

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Sheetal Devi**.
- **The Historic Milestone**: Became the **first para-athlete in Indian sports history** to qualify for the national **able-bodied archery squad** (competing in the compound archery category at the Asia Cup Stage 3 in Jeddah).
- **Technique & Unique Feat**: Born with **phocomelia** (a rare congenital condition leading to the absence of arms), Sheetal shoots compound bows using her **legs, feet, and shoulder-modified release aids**.
- **Training Base**: Hails from Kishtwar, Jammu & Kashmir; trains primarily at the **Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board (SMVDSB) Sports Complex** in Katra, J&K.
- **Notable Awards**: Conferred the prestigious **Arjuna Award** by the President of India in recognition of her outstanding performance in international sports.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Sheetal Devi's Athletic Achievements

- **Asian Para Games (Hangzhou, China - 2023)**:
  - Won **two Gold Medals** (Women's Individual Compound Open and Mixed Team Compound) and **one Silver Medal** (Women's Doubles).
  - First Indian woman to secure two gold medals at a single edition of the Asian Para Games.
- **World Archery Para Championships**:
  - Won the Silver Medal in the women's open compound section in Pilsen, Czech Republic.
- **Paris Paralympics**:
  - Clinched the **Bronze Medal in the Mixed Team Compound Open** event alongside Rakesh Kumar.
- **Global Recognition**:
  - Named the **Best Youth Athlete of the Year** by the Asian Paralympic Committee (APC) and ranked as the World No. 1 para-compound archer.
