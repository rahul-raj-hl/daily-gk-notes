# 📌 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) & Millennium Development Goals (MDGs)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Genesis & Evolution**:
  - The process to create the SDGs was initiated in **2012** at the **United Nations Conference on Sustainable Development (Rio+20)** in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil.
  - The **17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** were officially adopted by the UN General Assembly in **September 2015** under the resolution titled *"Transforming our world: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development"*.
  - Entered into force on **1 January 2016** with a target deadline of **2030** (**Agenda 2030**).
- **Predecessor Framework**: Succeeded the **Millennium Development Goals (MDGs)**, which comprised **8 goals** implemented over a 15-year timeframe from **2000 to 2015**.
- **Scope & Targets**: Encompasses **17 Goals**, **169 Targets**, and **231 unique Indicators** applied universally to all member countries (developed and developing alike).
- **Nodal Agency in India**: **NITI Aayog** is the nodal institution responsible for coordinating and monitoring SDG implementation in India via the **SDG India Index**.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Complete List of all 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

*The 17 interconnected goals must be remembered in sequential order:*

| Goal Number | Target Name | Core Objective / Focus |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **SDG 1** | **No Poverty** | End poverty in all its forms everywhere |
| **SDG 2** | **Zero Hunger** | End hunger, achieve food security and improved nutrition, promote sustainable agriculture |
| **SDG 3** | **Good Health and Well-Being** | Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages |
| **SDG 4** | **Quality Education** | Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and lifelong learning opportunities |
| **SDG 5** | **Gender Equality** | Achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls |
| **SDG 6** | **Clean Water and Sanitation** | Ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all |
| **SDG 7** | **Affordable and Clean Energy** | Ensure access to affordable, reliable, sustainable, and modern energy for all |
| **SDG 8** | **Decent Work and Economic Growth** | Promote sustained, inclusive economic growth, full employment, and decent work |
| **SDG 9** | **Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure** | Build resilient infrastructure, promote inclusive industrialization, and foster innovation |
| **SDG 10** | **Reduced Inequalities** | Reduce inequality within and among countries |
| **SDG 11** | **Sustainable Cities and Communities** | Make cities and human settlements inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable |
| **SDG 12** | **Responsible Consumption & Production**| Ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns |
| **SDG 13** | **Climate Action** | Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts |
| **SDG 14** | **Life Below Water** | Conserve and sustainably use oceans, seas, and marine resources |
| **SDG 15** | **Life on Land** | Protect, restore, and promote sustainable use of terrestrial ecosystems and forests |
| **SDG 16** | **Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions** | Promote peaceful and inclusive societies, provide access to justice for all |
| **SDG 17** | **Partnerships for the Goals** | Strengthen global partnership for sustainable development |

### 2. Comparison: MDGs (2000–2015) vs. SDGs (2016–2030)

| Parameter | Millennium Development Goals (MDGs) | Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operational Period** | **2000 – 2015** (15 Years) | **2016 – 2030** (15 Years) |
| **Adopted At** | UN Millennium Summit (New York, 2000) | UN Sustainable Development Summit (New York, 2015) |
| **Number of Goals** | **8 Goals** (18 Targets) | **17 Goals** (169 Targets) |
| **Applicability Scope** | Targeted primarily at **developing countries** | **Universally applicable** to all developed & developing nations |
| **Core Pillars** | Focused predominantly on social/human development | Integrates three dimensions: **Social, Economic, and Environmental** |
| **Origin Point** | UN Millennium Declaration (2000) | Formulated at **Rio+20 Earth Summit (2012)** |

---

### 3. SDG Monitoring in India: NITI Aayog SDG India Index

- **NITI Aayog Framework**: Released periodically to track state and UT performance across individual SDGs.
- **Score Classification Categories**:
  - **Aspirant**: Score $0 – 49$
  - **Performer**: Score $50 – 64$
  - **Front Runner**: Score $65 – 99$
  - **Achiever**: Score $100$
- **Top Performing States**: **Kerala** and **Uttarakhand** consistently rank among the top-performing states on overall composite SDG scores.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Term / Goal Number | Associated Focus | Common Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SDG 5** | **Gender Equality** | Confusing with SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities) |
| **SDG 6** | **Clean Water & Sanitation** | Confusing with SDG 14 (Life Below Water) |
| **SDG 13** | **Climate Action** | Confusing with SDG 15 (Life on Land) |
| **SDG 14 vs. SDG 15** | **SDG 14 = Aquatic/Marine Life** | **SDG 15 = Terrestrial/Land Ecosystems** |
| **MDGs vs. SDGs Count** | **MDGs = 8 Goals** | **SDGs = 17 Goals** |
