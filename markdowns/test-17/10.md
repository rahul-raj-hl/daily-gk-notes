# 📌 Champaran Satyagraha (1917) & 'Gandhi in Champaran'

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **D.G. Tendulkar** (Dinannath Gopal Tendulkar).
- **The Book**: ***Gandhi in Champaran***, published by the Publications Division (Ministry of Information and Broadcasting), is an authoritative historical chronicle detailing Mahatma Gandhi's first civil disobedience/satyagraha experiment in India in **1917**.
- **D.G. Tendulkar's Legacy**: A monumental biographer of Mahatma Gandhi, best known for his encyclopedic 8-volume biography titled ***Mahatma: Life of Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi***.
- **Champaran Milestone**: The **Champaran Satyagraha (1917)** in Bihar was Mahatma Gandhi's **first major Satyagraha movement in India**, launched to liberate indigo farmers from the oppressive **Tinkathia System** ($3/20^{\text{th}}$ cultivation mandate).

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Champaran Satyagraha (1917): Key Historical Facts

*Background, mechanics, and outcomes of Gandhi's first mass movement on Indian soil:*

- **Invitation by Raj Kumar Shukla**: A local indigo farmer (*ryot*), **Raj Kumar Shukla**, met Mahatma Gandhi at the **Lucknow Congress Session (1916)** and persistently persuaded him to visit Champaran to witness the exploitation of tenant farmers.
- **The Oppressive System**:
  - **Tinkathia System**: European planters legally bound tenant farmers to grow indigo on $\frac{3}{20}\text{th}$ portion of their prime agricultural land.
  - **Sharhabeshi & Tawan**: When synthetic German chemical dyes rendered natural indigo unremunerative, British planters demanded heavy illegal exactions (*Sharhabeshi* = rent enhancements, *Tawan* = lump-sum cancellation penalties) to release farmers from their contracts.
- **Gandhi’s Core Associates in Champaran**:
  - **Dr. Rajendra Prasad**, **J.B. Kripalani**, **Mahadev Desai**, **Narhari Parikh**, **Brij Kishore Prasad**, **Anugrah Narayan Sinha**, and **Mazhar-ul-Haq**.
- **Outcome & Commission of Inquiry**:
  - The British government appointed the **Champaran Agrarian Committee** (with Gandhi as an official member).
  - The **Champaran Agrarian Act, 1918** abolished the Tinkathia system, and planters were forced to refund **25%** of the illegally extracted money to the farmers.
- **The Title 'Mahatma'**: Impressed by Gandhi's success at Champaran, **Rabindranath Tagore** bestowed the title of **"Mahatma"** upon him.
