# 📌 Spread of Buddhism & Chronology Across East Asia

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Chronological Order of Spread**: **China (1st century CE) $\rightarrow$ Korea (4th century CE) $\rightarrow$ Japan (6th century CE)**.
- **China**: Introduced via the **Silk Road trade routes** during the Han Dynasty (~1st Century CE).
- **Korea**: Transmitted from China to the Korean Three Kingdoms (*Goguryeo, Baekje, Silla*) in the 4th Century CE.
- **Japan**: Introduced officially via Korean emissaries from Baekje in the **6th Century CE** (Asuka Period).
- **Sri Lanka**: Earlier spread during Emperor Ashoka's reign in the 3rd Century BCE via his children **Mahendra and Sanghamitra**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Chronological Timeline in East Asia

*Step-by-step geographical transmission of Mahayana Buddhism.*

- **China (1st Century CE)**:
  - Reached China via northern Silk Road routes through Central Asia.
  - Indian monks like **Kashyapa Matanga** and **Dharmaratna** translated Buddhist scriptures at the **White Horse Temple** (*Baima Temple*) under Emperor Ming of Han.
- **Korea (4th Century CE)**:
  - Monks from China introduced Buddhism to Goguryeo (372 CE) and Baekje (384 CE).
  - Adopted as state religion to unify the kingdoms.
- **Japan (6th Century CE)**:
  - King Seong of Baekje (Korea) sent a statue of Buddha and sutras to Emperor Kinmei of Japan (~552/538 CE).
  - Strongly championed during the reign of **Prince Shotoku**.

---

### 2. Spread to Southern Asia (Theravada Route)

*Missionary efforts during Emperor Ashoka's reign (3rd Century BCE).*

- **Sri Lanka**: Mahendra and Sanghamitra brought a branch of the sacred **Bodhi Tree** to Anuradhapura.
- **South-East Asia (Suvarnabhumi)**: Monks **Sona and Uttara** were dispatched by Ashoka to Myanmar/Thailand regions.
