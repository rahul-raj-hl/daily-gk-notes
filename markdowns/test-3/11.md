# 📌 Gandhian Environmental Thought & T.N. Khoshoo

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **T.N. Khoshoo** compiled Mahatma Gandhi's ecological ideas in the book *"Mahatma Gandhi and the Environment: Analysing Gandhian Environmental Thought"*.
- The book contextualizes Gandhi’s core philosophy for modern environmental challenges like **climate change** and **resource depletion**.
- Features a foreword written by **Dr. R. K. Pachauri**, former Chairman of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC).
- **Dr. T.N. Khoshoo** was a distinguished environmental scientist and served as Secretary to the Department of Environment, Government of India.
- Highlights famous Gandhian environmental ideals, such as *"The Earth has enough for everyone's need, but not for anyone's greed."*

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Themes of Gandhian Environmentalism

*Core principles analyzed in the book.*

- **Simplicity and Need-Based Living**: Promoting minimal consumption to prevent resource depletion.
- **Swaraj & Self-Sufficiency**: Localized production and village-based economies to reduce industrial carbon footprints.
- **Ahimsa (Non-Violence)**: Extended towards nature, living organisms, and ecosystems.
- **Trusteeship**: Viewing natural resources as a trust for present and future generations rather than private property.

---

### 2. About Dr. T.N. Khoshoo

*Renowned Indian environmentalist and scientist.*

- **Full Name**: Triloki Nath Khoshoo.
- **Key Positions**: Served as the Director of the National Botanical Research Institute (NBRI) and Secretary in the Ministry of Environment and Forests.
- **Awards**: Awarded the **Padma Bhushan** (1992) for his outstanding contributions to environmental research and conservation policy.
