# 📌 Global Biodiversity & Megadiverse Countries

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Brazil** holds the **highest biodiversity** of any country in the world.
- It is home to approximately **10% of the world's known species**, including over 30,000 plant species, 1,300 bird species, and 3,000 fish species.
- Supported by the vast **Amazon Rainforest**, Cerrado savanna, and Atlantic Forest ecosystems.
- **Megadiverse Countries**: A group of 17 nations identified by Conservation International that house the majority of Earth's species.
- **India** ranks among the world's megadiverse countries (housing 4 major biodiversity hotspots).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Why Brazil Leads Global Biodiversity

*Ecological factors driving species richness in Brazil.*

- **Amazon Rainforest**: World's largest tropical rainforest, acting as a massive biological storehouse.
- **Geographical Scale & Varied Habitats**: Spans diverse biomes like the Amazon basin, Pantanal wetlands, Cerrado, and Caatinga.
- **High Endemism**: Exceptional number of species that are native to Brazil and found nowhere else on Earth.

---

### 2. Top Megadiverse Countries

*Ranking of nations with the richest species diversity:*

1. **Brazil** 🥇 (*Highest worldwide*)

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Term | Definition | Key Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Megadiverse Country** | Nation housing a large proportion of Earth's total species | **Brazil**, India, Indonesia |
| **Biodiversity Hotspot** | Region with high endemism under severe threat (*Norman Myers term*) | **Western Ghats**, Indo-Burma |
| **Endemic Species** | Species restricted to a specific geographical location | Lion-tailed Macaque (*Western Ghats*) |
