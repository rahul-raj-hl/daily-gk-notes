# 📌 Reh Festival & Tribal Culture of Arunachal Pradesh

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Reh Festival** is celebrated annually in **February** by the **Idu-Mishmi tribe** of **Arunachal Pradesh**.
- The festival is dedicated to the **Divine Mother Nanyi-Inyit**, believed to be the creator and protector of the Mishmi people.
- Involves traditional rituals, feasts, and community dances (*such as the Igu dance by priests*) to invoke peace, prosperity, and a bountiful harvest.
- **Other Notable Arunachal Tribal Festivals**:
  - **Solung** $\rightarrow$ Adi Tribe
  - **Si-Donyi** $\rightarrow$ Tagin Tribe
  - **Saru / Nechi-Dow** $\rightarrow$ Hrusso (Aka) Tribe
  - **Myoko / Dree** $\rightarrow$ Apatani Tribe

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Reh Festival & Idu-Mishmi Beliefs

*Cultural significance and rituals.*

- **Community**: **Idu-Mishmi** (one of the three sub-tribes of the Mishmi ethnic group residing in Dibang Valley and Lower Dibang Valley).
- **Deity Worshipped**: **Divine Mother Nanyi-Inyit**.
- **Timing**: Celebrated annually during the month of **February**.
- **Key Rituals**:
  - **Androp**: Invocation performed by the *Igu* (shaman priest).
  - **Naya**: Folk dance executed by men, women, and children.
  - **Feasting & Sacrifice**: Mithun (Gayal) and pigs are offered to seek divine blessings for crops and well-being.

---

### 2. Prominent Tribes & Festivals of Arunachal Pradesh

*Essential mapping of tribes to their major cultural festivals:*

- **Idu-Mishmi Tribe**: Celebrates **Reh**.
- **Adi Tribe**: Celebrates **Solung** and **Etor** (*associated with the Ponung dance*).
- **Tagin Tribe**: Celebrates **Si-Donyi** (*worships Earth/Si and Sun/Donyi*).
- **Hrusso (Aka) Tribe**: Celebrates **Saru** and **Nechi-Dow**.
- **Apatani Tribe**: Celebrates **Dree** (*agricultural*) and **Myoko** (*friendship/animistic*).
- **Nyishi Tribe**: Celebrates **Nyokum Yullo**.
- **Monpa Tribe**: Celebrates **Losar** (*Tibetan New Year*).
