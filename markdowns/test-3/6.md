# 📌 AI Hackathon 2025 & Critical Mineral Exploration

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **AI Hackathon 2025** was themed **"Mineral Targeting using Artificial Intelligence"**.
- Organized alongside India's first-ever **Exploration Licence (EL)** auction on March 13, 2025, in **Goa**.
- Objective: Use Artificial Intelligence (AI) and advanced geological datasets to **discover hidden mineral deposits**.
- Organized under the aegis of the **Ministry of Mines**, Government of India.
- The auction featured **13 critical and strategic mineral blocks** spread across 10 states.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. AI Hackathon 2025 & Mineral Exploration

*Leveraging technology for deep-seated mineral discovery.*

- **Theme**: **"Mineral Targeting using Artificial Intelligence"**.
- **Core Focus**: Employs Machine Learning (ML) algorithms and satellite/geophysical datasets to locate critical minerals (*like Lithium, Cobalt, Nickel, and Rare Earth Elements*).
- **Ministry**: Ministry of Mines, Government of India.
- **Venue**: Launched in **Goa** on March 13, 2025.

---

### 2. Exploration Licence (EL) Regime in India

*New legal framework for private sector mining exploration.*

- **Legislative Basis**: Introduced through the **Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Amendment Act, 2023** (*MMDR Act*).
- **Purpose**: Encourages private exploration agencies to discover deep-seated and critical minerals.
- **Auction Scale**: First tranche auctioned **13 mineral blocks** across 10 Indian states.
- **Key Target Minerals**: Lithium, Beryllium, Titanium, Niobium, Tantalum, and Rare Earth Elements (REEs).

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Mineral License Type | Key Feature | Primary Stage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Exploration Licence (EL)** | Granted for prospecting and reconnaissance of deep-seated minerals | Early stage exploration |
| **Mining Lease (ML)** | Granted for actual commercial extraction/mining of minerals | Operational extraction stage |
| **Composite Licence (CL)** | Combined license for exploration and subsequent mining lease | Intermediate two-in-one stage |
