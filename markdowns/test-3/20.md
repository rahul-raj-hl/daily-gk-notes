# 📌 Bharatiya Nyaya Samhita (BNS) 2023 & New Criminal Laws

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Bharatiya Nyaya Samhita (BNS), 2023** consolidates and amends provisions related to **Criminal Substantive Law** in India.
- It replaces the colonial-era **Indian Penal Code (IPC), 1860**.
- Enacted alongside two other major criminal reform codes:
  - **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023** $\rightarrow$ Replaces Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), 1973.
  - **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023** $\rightarrow$ Replaces Indian Evidence Act, 1872.
- The three new criminal laws came into official force across India on **July 1, 2024**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Classification of New Criminal Laws

*Mapping old colonial statutes to the newly enacted Indian legal codes:*

- **Bharatiya Nyaya Samhita (BNS), 2023**:
  - *Branch*: **Criminal Substantive Law**
  - *Replaces*: **Indian Penal Code (IPC), 1860**
  - *Scope*: Defines offences, criminal liability, and prescribes punishments for crimes.
- **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023**:
  - *Branch*: **Criminal Procedural Law**
  - *Replaces*: **Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), 1973**
  - *Scope*: Governs investigation procedures, arrests, bail, and court trial mechanics.
- **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023**:
  - *Branch*: **Law of Evidence**
  - *Replaces*: **Indian Evidence Act, 1872**
  - *Scope*: Regulates admissibility of evidence, witness testimony, and digital/electronic records in court.

---

### 2. Major Legal Reforms Introduced in BNS

*Key changes to modernize Indian criminal jurisprudence:*

- **Sedition Repealed**: Section 124A (Sedition) of the IPC has been abolished and replaced by provisions punishing acts endangering the sovereignty, unity, and integrity of India.
- **Community Service**: Introduced for the first time as a formal punishment option for minor offences.
- **Organized Crime & Terrorism**: Expressly defined and penalized within the general penal code.
- **Mob Lynching**: Explicitly criminalized with severe penalties up to life imprisonment or capital punishment.

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "N-S-A = Nyaya, Suraksha, Sakshya"**

- **NYAYA (BNS)** $\rightarrow$ **Substantive Penal Code** (*Punishment for Crimes*)
- **SURAKSHA (BNSS)** $\rightarrow$ **Procedure / Security** (*Police & Trial Procedure*)
- **SAKSHYA (BSA)** $\rightarrow$ **Evidence / Proof** (*Proof in Court*)
