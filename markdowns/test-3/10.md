# 📌 Terms of Credit & Sources of Credit

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Terms of Credit** represent the contractual conditions under which a loan is sanctioned.
- The 4 major components of Terms of Credit are: **Interest rate**, **Collateral**, **Documentation required**, and **Mode of repayment**.
- **Source of credit** refers to *where* the loan comes from (e.g., Banks, Cooperatives, Moneylenders). It is **NOT** a component of the terms of credit.
- **Collateral** is an asset pledged by the borrower as security until the loan is repaid.
- **Formal Credit Sector** is supervised by the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Components of Terms of Credit

*Essential conditions defined in every credit agreement.*

- **Interest Rate**: The cost of borrowing charged by the lender as a percentage of the principal amount.
- **Collateral**: Asset (such as land, house, gold, vehicle, fixed deposits) owned by the borrower that serves as security to the lender until the loan is repaid.
- **Documentation Required**: Proof of identity, income verification, employment details, and property ownership papers required to establish legal enforceability.
- **Mode of Repayment**: Specific timeline, installments, and method (cash, cheque, bank transfer) agreed upon for repaying the principal and interest.

---

### 2. Sources of Credit: Formal vs Informal Sector

*Categorizing lending sources in the economy.*

- **Formal Sector**:
  - Includes **Commercial Banks** and **Cooperative Societies**.
  - Charges lower and fixed interest rates.
  - Regulated and supervised by the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**.
  - Requires proper documentation and collateral.
- **Informal Sector**:
  - Includes **Moneylenders, traders, relatives, and friends**.
  - Charges exorbitant interest rates without regulation.
  - No strict supervisor (leads to debt traps).

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Component / Term | Definition | Included in Terms of Credit? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Interest Rate** | Percentage price charged on borrowed principal | **YES** |
| **Collateral** | Security asset pledged by the borrower | **YES** |
| **Documentation** | Legal paperwork verifying identity/income | **YES** |
| **Mode of Repayment** | Schedule and method of returning the loan | **YES** |
| **Source of Credit** | Entity/Institution providing the loan (Bank/Moneylender) | **NO** |

---

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Parameter | Terms of Credit | Sources of Credit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meaning** | Conditions/rules attached to a loan contract | Institutions or individuals granting the loan |
| **Examples** | Interest rate, Collateral, Repayment mode | Banks, Cooperatives, Moneylenders |
| **Supervision** | Governed by individual loan agreements | Formal sources supervised by the **RBI** |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "C-I-D-R sets the Credit Terms"**

- **C** $\rightarrow$ **C**ollateral
- **I** $\rightarrow$ **I**nterest rate
- **D** $\rightarrow$ **D**ocumentation
- **R** $\rightarrow$ Mode of **R**epayment

*(Source is WHERE you go, NOT WHAT you agree to!)*
