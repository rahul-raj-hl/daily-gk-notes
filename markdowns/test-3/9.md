# 📌 Prof. Arunoday Saha & Padma Shri 2025

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Prof. Arunoday Saha** was honoured with the **Padma Shri in 2025** for his contributions to **Literature and Education**.
- He belongs to the northeastern state of **Tripura**.
- An acclaimed economist, educationist, and literary figure, he served as the founding Vice-Chancellor of **Tripura University**.
- Recognized for his extensive literary works in Bengali literature and economic studies.
- **Padma Shri** is India's **fourth-highest civilian award**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Profile of Prof. Arunoday Saha

*Eminent economist and literary personality from Tripura.*

- **State**: **Tripura** (Northeastern India).
- **Award**: **Padma Shri (2025)** in the field of **Literature and Education**.
- **Academic Contributions**: Served as the first Vice-Chancellor of Tripura University after it became a Central University.
- **Literary Base**: Built a vast readership in India and abroad through his insightful Bengali books, essays, and economic commentary.

---

### 2. State Overview: Tripura

*Key geographical and cultural facts.*

- **Capital**: Agartala.
- **Borders**: Bordered by Bangladesh on three sides (north, south, and west).
- **Official Languages**: **Bengali** and **Kokborok**.
- **Civilian Honors**: Awarded on the eve of **Republic Day** by the **President of India**.
