# 📌 Quit India Movement & Flag Hoisting at Gowalia Tank

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Aruna Asaf Ali** hoisted the Indian National Congress flag at **Gowalia Tank Maidan**, Bombay on **9th August 1942**.
- Popularly called the **"Grand Old Lady"** of the Indian Independence Movement.
- **Quit India Resolution** was passed on **8th August 1942** at the Bombay session of the All-India Congress Committee (AICC).
- Mahatma Gandhi gave the historic slogan **"Do or Die"** (*Karo ya Maro*) at Gowalia Tank Maidan.
- Gowalia Tank Maidan was later renamed **August Kranti Maidan** to honor the movement.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Highlights of Quit India Movement (1942)

*Major mass movement for complete freedom.*

- **Launch Date**: Resolution passed on **8th August 1942**; movement began on **9th August 1942**.
- **Famous Flag Hoisting**: **Aruna Asaf Ali** presided over the AICC session after major leaders were arrested and hoisted the tricolor flag at Gowalia Tank Maidan.
- **Slogan Creators**:
  - **"Do or Die"**: Given by **Mahatma Gandhi** in his speech.
  - **"Quit India"**: Coined by socialist leader **Yusuf Meherally** (former Mayor of Bombay).
- **Immediate Causes**: Failure of the **Cripps Mission** (1942) which offered Dominion Status instead of full independence, and dragging India into **World War II** without public consent.

---

### 2. Operation Zero Hour & Underground Movement

*British crackdown and leadership during the movement.*

- **Operation Zero Hour**: British police launched a midnight raid on August 9, 1942, arresting top Congress leaders (Gandhi, Nehru, Patel, Azad) before the movement could be formally organized.
- **Underground Leaders**: Aruna Asaf Ali, Ram Manohar Lohia, Jayaprakash Narayan, and Sucheta Kripalani operated secretly.
- **Congress Radio**: **Usha Mehta** established an underground secret radio station to broadcast news across the country.
