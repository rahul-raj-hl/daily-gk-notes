# 📌 Major Anglo Wars & Chronology in Modern Indian History

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Third Carnatic War** (**1756–1763**): Fought between the British and French; ended with the Battle of Wandiwash (1760) and the **Treaty of Paris (1763)**.
- **First Anglo-Mysore War** (**1767–1769**): Fought between Hyder Ali of Mysore and the British; ended with the **Treaty of Madras (1769)**.
- **First Anglo-Maratha War** (**1775–1782**): Began with the Treaty of Surat and ended with the **Treaty of Salbai (1782)**.
- **First Anglo-Sikh War** (**1845–1846**): Fought between the Sikh Empire and East India Company; ended with the **Treaty of Lahore (1846)**.
- **Correct Chronology**: **(b) Third Carnatic War $\rightarrow$ (a) First Anglo-Mysore War $\rightarrow$ (c) First Anglo-Maratha War $\rightarrow$ (d) First Anglo-Sikh War**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Chronological Timeline of Major Anglo Wars

*Sequence of British expansion across the Indian subcontinent.*

- **Third Carnatic War (1756–1763)**: British commander Sir Eyre Coote defeated French commander Comte de Lally at the **Battle of Wandiwash (1760)**, ending French political ambitions in India.
- **First Anglo-Mysore War (1767–1769)**: Hyder Ali successfully defeated the British-Nizam alliance and forced the British to sign a defensive alliance (*Treaty of Madras*).
- **First Anglo-Maratha War (1775–1782)**: Triggered by internal succession disputes among Marathas; ended with the **Treaty of Salbai**, guaranteeing 20 years of peace between Marathas and the British.
- **First Anglo-Sikh War (1845–1846)**: Fought after Maharaja Ranjit Singh's death; led to the **Treaty of Lahore** and the cession of Jammu and Kashmir to Gulab Singh under British suzerainty.

---

### 2. Ending Treaties of Key Anglo Wars

*Important treaties frequently tested in SSC exams.*

- **Carnatic Wars**:
  - 1st Carnatic War (1746–1748) $\rightarrow$ **Treaty of Aix-la-Chapelle** (1748)
  - 2nd Carnatic War (1749–1754) $\rightarrow$ **Treaty of Pondicherry** (1754)
  - 3rd Carnatic War (1756–1763) $\rightarrow$ **Treaty of Paris** (1763)
- **Anglo-Mysore Wars**:
  - 1st Mysore War $\rightarrow$ **Treaty of Madras** (1769)
  - 2nd Mysore War $\rightarrow$ **Treaty of Mangalore** (1784)
  - 3rd Mysore War $\rightarrow$ **Treaty of Seringapatam** (1792)
  - 4th Mysore War $\rightarrow$ Death of Tipu Sultan (1799)
