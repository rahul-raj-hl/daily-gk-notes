# 📌 President of 80th UN General Assembly Session

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Annalena Baerbock** of **Germany** was elected President of the **80th session** of the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA).
- Nominated by Germany under the **Western European and Other States Group (WEOG)** regional rotation.
- Elected during the **72nd plenary meeting** of the UN General Assembly.
- **UN General Assembly (UNGA)** is one of the **6 principal organs** of the United Nations, headquartered in **New York, USA**.
- Founded in **1945** as the main deliberative, policymaking, and representative organ of the UN.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. UN General Assembly Presidency & Regional Rotation

*How the President of the UN General Assembly is elected.*

- **Regional Groups**: Presidency rotates annually among 5 regional groups:
  1. African Group
  2. Asia-Pacific Group
  3. Eastern European Group
  4. Latin American and Caribbean Group (GRULAC)
  5. Western European and Other States Group (WEOG)
- **Role**: Presides over Assembly sessions, opens and closes plenary meetings, directs discussions, and enforces parliamentary rules of procedure.

---

### 2. Overview of United Nations General Assembly (UNGA)

*Key organizational facts.*

- **Principal Organ**: Main deliberative body representing all 193 UN Member States with equal voting power (*1 country = 1 vote*).
- **Headquarters**: **New York City, USA**.
- **Founding Year**: **1945** (*UN Charter*).
- **Indian Milestone**: **Vijaya Lakshmi Pandit** was elected as the first female President of the UN General Assembly during its 8th session in **1953**.
