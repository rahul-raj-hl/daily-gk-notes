# 📌 Judo Belt Ranks, Dan System & Competition Area Guidelines

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Statement 1 is Correct**: A **red-and-white panelled belt** (*Kohaku obi*) is worn by judokas holding the **6th, 7th, and 8th Dan** ranks.
- **Statement 2 is Correct**: A shared safety zone of at least **1 meter** must be maintained between adjacent competition areas according to International Judo Federation (IJF) rules.
- **Founding of Judo**: Founded in **1882** by **Jigoro Kano** in Tokyo, Japan (*Kodokan Judo*).
- **Highest Belt Rank**: A **solid red belt** is reserved for **9th Dan and 10th Dan** masters.
- **Black Belts**: Worn for ranks from **1st Dan to 5th Dan**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Statement Analysis

*Evaluating rules governed by the International Judo Federation (IJF):*

- **Statement 1: A red-and-white belt is associated with judokas from Sixth dan to Eighth dan levels.**
  - *Correct.* Judokas holding 1st through 5th Dan wear a solid **black belt**. For 6th, 7th, and 8th Dan, practitioners may wear a **red-and-white panelled belt** (*Kohaku obi*) as a mark of distinction.
- **Statement 2: A shared safety zone of at least 1 meter must be maintained between adjoining competition areas.**
  - *Correct.* Official IJF mat layout regulations specify a minimum **1-meter shared safety zone** between two adjoining tatami mat competition areas to ensure safety and prevent collisions.

---

### 2. Overview of Judo Belt & Dan Rank Hierarchy

*Mastery hierarchy beyond beginner levels.*

- **Kyu Ranks** (*Beginner to Advanced Student*): Colored belts (White, Yellow, Orange, Green, Blue, Brown).
- **Dan Ranks** (*Advanced / Black Belt Ranks*):
  - **1st Dan to 5th Dan**: Solid **Black Belt** (*Kuro obi*).
  - **6th Dan to 8th Dan**: Alternating **Red-and-White Belt** (*Kohaku obi*).
  - **9th Dan & 10th Dan**: Solid **Red Belt** (*Aka obi*).
