# 📌 Vijayanagara Empire & Krishna Deva Raya

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Krishna Deva Raya** (reigned 1509–1529 CE) assumed the title **'Yavanrajya Sthapanacharya'** (*Restorer of the Yavana/Turkic Kingdom*).
- Belonged to the **Tuluva Dynasty**; under his leadership, the Vijayanagara Empire reached its golden age in territorial expansion, military power, and culture.
- Defeated the Sultan of Bidar/Gulbarga and reinstated Mahmud Shah on his throne, which earned him this specific title.
- Other famous titles held by him include **Andhra Bhoja**, **Mooru Rayara Ganda**, and **Kannada Rajya Rama Ramana**.
- Authored famous literary works like **Amuktamalyada** (in Telugu) and **Jambavati Kalyanam** (in Sanskrit).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Krishna Deva Raya & His Royal Titles

*Significant titles conferred on the greatest emperor of Vijayanagara.*

- **Yavanrajya Sthapanacharya**: Assumed after defeat of the Bahmani/Sultanate forces and restoring the rightful sultan in Bidar.
- **Andhra Bhoja**: Awarded for his patronage of Telugu literature and arts.
- **Abhinava Bhoja / Sakala Kala Bhoja**: Celebrated as a great scholar king, comparable to King Bhoja of Paramara dynasty.
- **Ashtadiggajas**: Court adorned by eight famous Telugu poets, including **Tenali Ramakrishna** and **Allasani Peddana**.

---

### 2. Dynasty Chronology of Vijayanagara Empire

*The four dynasties that ruled Vijayanagara (1336–1646 CE).*

1. **Sangama Dynasty** (1336–1485 CE): Founded by Harihara I and Bukka I.
2. **Saluva Dynasty** (1485–1505 CE): Founded by Saluva Narasimha.
3. **Tuluva Dynasty** (1505–1570 CE): Founded by Tuluva Narasa Nayaka; famous ruler **Krishna Deva Raya**.
4. **Aravidu Dynasty** (1570–1646 CE): Founded by Tirumala Raya following the **Battle of Talikota (1565)**.
