# 📌 Major Mountain Passes in India (Bara-lacha Pass & Himalayan Passes)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Bara-lacha Pass** (*Bara-lacha La*) is located in the **Zanskar Range** of the **Himalayas** (not Western Ghats).
- It connects the **Lahaul district in Himachal Pradesh** with the **Leh district in Ladakh** on the Manali–Leh highway.
- Known as the **"Pass with crossroads"** because roads from Ladakh, Spiti, Lahaul, and Zanskar meet here.
- Source of the **Bhaga River** (which joins Chandra River to form the **Chenab River**).
- Major Western Ghats passes include **Thal Ghat**, **Bhor Ghat**, and **Pal Ghat**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Statement Analysis

*Evaluating statements regarding Bara-lacha Pass:*

- **Statement (i): Bara-lacha Pass is a high mountain pass in the Western Ghats mountain range.**
  - *False.* It is a high-altitude pass located in the **Himalayas** (Zanskar Range) at an elevation of ~4,890 meters.
- **Statement (ii): Bara-lacha Pass connects Lahaul district in Himachal Pradesh to Leh district in Ladakh.**
  - *True.* It acts as a vital connector along the **Manali–Leh Highway** linking Lahaul (HP) with Leh (Ladakh).

---

### 2. Key Himalayan Passes in North India

*Important mountain passes frequently tested in SSC exams.*

- **Bara-lacha La**: Connects Lahaul (Himachal Pradesh) $\rightarrow$ Leh (Ladakh).
- **Rohtang Pass**: Connects Kullu Valley $\rightarrow$ Lahaul & Spiti Valleys (Himachal Pradesh).
- **Shipki La**: Connects Himachal Pradesh $\rightarrow$ Tibet (Sutlej River enters India through this pass).
- **Zoji La**: Connects Srinagar (Kashmir Valley) $\rightarrow$ Leh (Ladakh).
- **Nathu La**: Connects Sikkim $\rightarrow$ Tibet (famous Silk Route trade pass).
- **Bumdila / Lipulekh**: **Lipulekh** (Uttarakhand) connects to Tibet (used for Kailash Mansarovar Yatra).
