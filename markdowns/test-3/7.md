# 📌 Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft (AMCA)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **AMCA** (*Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft*) is India's indigenous **5th-generation stealth fighter jet** project.
- **Designer & Manufacturer**: Designed by the **Aeronautical Development Agency (ADA)** under **DRDO**, with manufacturing led by **Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL)**.
- **Key Features**: Twin-engine, single-seat, all-weather multirole combat aircraft featuring stealth characteristics (*low radar cross-section and internal weapons bay*).
- **Engine Partnership**: India and **France** agreed to co-develop a **120 kN-class jet engine** with 100% transfer of technology (ToT) for the AMCA Mark 2 variant.
- **Government Approval**: Cleared by the Cabinet Committee on Security (CCS) in 2024 with a prototype development cost of ₹15,000 crore.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Program Features

*Technical specifications of India's stealth fighter.*

- **Generational Classification**: AMCA is a **5th-generation stealth fighter** (*not 6th generation, which includes AI-assisted laser/hypersonic capabilities*).
- **Design Configuration**: Twin-engine, single-seat, stealth multirole aircraft.
- **Stealth Capabilities**: Low Radar Cross-Section (RCS), internal weapons bay, and advanced composite materials.
- **Two Planned Variants**:
  - **AMCA Mark 1**: Uses imported GE F414 engines.
  - **AMCA Mark 2**: Uses the newly co-developed **120 kN high-thrust engine** built with France.

---

### 2. International Collaboration & Defense Agencies

*Organisations powering the indigenous jet project.*

- **ADA & DRDO**: Lead design and research agencies.
- **HAL** (*Hindustan Aeronautics Limited*): Lead manufacturing agency.
- **Safran (France)**: Foreign partner for joint development and 100% Technology Transfer (ToT) of the 120 kN engine.
