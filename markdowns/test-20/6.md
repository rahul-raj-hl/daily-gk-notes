# 📌 UMMID Initiative: Prevention & Management of Inherited Genetic Disorders

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Statements**: **I, II, and III are all correct**.
- **Acronym Expansion**: **UMMID** stands for **Unique Methods of Management of Inherited Disorders**.
- **Nodal Agency**: A national health-biotechnology initiative conceptualized and funded by the **Department of Biotechnology (DBT)**, Ministry of Science and Technology.
- **Launch Year**: Launched in **September 2019** to address the growing public health burden of congenital and inherited genetic disorders in India.
- **Integrated Approach**: Unifies **screening, prenatal diagnosis, genetic counselling, multidisciplinary prevention, medical training, and public awareness** under a single public-health framework.
- **Infrastructure Pillar**: Established specialized **NIDAN Kendras** (*National Inherited Diseases Administration Kendras*) in government hospitals to deliver accessible clinical genetics services.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Objectives & Components of the UMMID Scheme

| Component | Strategic Focus | Operational Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NIDAN Kendras** | Clinical & Diagnostic Facilities | Setting up dedicated *National Inherited Diseases Administration Kendras* in premier government hospitals to provide clinical genetics, prenatal screening, and post-natal testing. |
| **Skill Development (Training)** | Human Resource Capacity | Training government clinicians in Medical Genetics, Biochemical Genetics, and Cytogenetics to expand specialist coverage. |
| **Aspirational Districts Screening**| Population Screening | Conducting systematic screening of pregnant women and newborns in selected Aspirational Districts for common inherited genetic conditions (e.g., Thalassemia, Sickle Cell Anaemia). |
| **Genetic Counselling** | Preventive Public Health | Providing pre-marital, pre-conception, and post-natal genetic counselling to families with a history of congenital defects. |

---

### 2. Common Inherited Disorders Targeted Under Public Health Genetics

| Disorder Category | Example Diseases | Mode of Inheritance / Etiology |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hemoglobinopathies** | **Thalassemia**, **Sickle Cell Anaemia** | Autosomal recessive mutations affecting haemoglobin production or structure. |
| **Inborn Errors of Metabolism (IEM)**| **Phenylketonuria (PKU)**, **Galactosemia**, **MSUD** | Enzyme deficiencies impairing metabolic conversion of amino acids/sugars. |
| **Neuromuscular Disorders** | **Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD)**, **SMA** | X-linked or autosomal mutations causing progressive muscle degeneration. |
| **Chromosomal Aneuploidies** | **Down Syndrome (Trisomy 21)**, **Turner Syndrome** | Abnormal chromosome number occurring during meiotic cell division. |
