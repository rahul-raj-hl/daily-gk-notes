# 📌 Project UDAYAK: 37th Raising Day & Strategic Border Infrastructure

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Event & Venue**: The **37th Raising Day** of **Project UDAYAK** was celebrated on **June 01, 2026**, at **Doomdooma, Assam**.
- **Parent Body**: Executed by the **Border Roads Organisation (BRO)** under the Ministry of Defence.
- **Geographic Jurisdiction**: Entrusted with maintaining over **1,457 km** of strategic road network spanning the easternmost districts of **Arunachal Pradesh** (Anjaw, Lohit, Dibang Valley, Longding, Tirap, Changlang) and adjoining parts of **Assam**.
- **Strategic Importance**: Critical for military logistics, heavy troop mobility, and civilian connectivity along the **Line of Actual Control (LAC)** with China and the **Indo–Myanmar international border**.
- **Key Infrastructure Projects**: Involves constructing **border fencing infrastructure** along the Indo–Myanmar border, along with vital bridges, strategic tunnels, all-weather roads, and forward helipads.
