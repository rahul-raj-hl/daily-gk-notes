# 📌 Satwiksairaj Rankireddy & Chirag Shetty: Historic Singapore Open 2026 Title

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Historic Milestone**: On **May 31, 2026**, **Satwiksairaj Rankireddy** and **Chirag Shetty** became the **first Indian men's doubles pair** to win the prestigious **Singapore Open** title.
- **Final Encounter**: In the final, the Indian duo defeated Indonesia's **Fajar Alfian** and **Muhammad Fikri** with a score of **18-21, 21-17, 21-16** in a match lasting **1 hour and 13 minutes**.
- **Career Milestone**: This victory marked their **ninth career BWF World Tour title** and their **third Super 750 crown**.
- **Ending the Title Drought**: Ended a two-year title drought, with their last tournament victory coming at the **Thailand Open in 2024** (having registered four runner-up finishes in the interim).
- **Semifinal Breakthrough**: Entered the tournament as the **World No. 4 pair** and reached the final by defeating South Korea's reigning world champions **Kim Won-ho** and **Seo Seung-jae**.
- **Major Pedigree**: The duo are also reigning **Asian Games** and **Commonwealth Games** champions.
