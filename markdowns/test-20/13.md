# 📌 Satwiksairaj Rankireddy & Chirag Shetty: Historic Singapore Open 2026 Title

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Statements**: **1, 2, and 3 are all correct**.
- **Historic Milestone**: **Satwiksairaj Rankireddy** and **Chirag Shetty** became the **first Indian men's doubles pair** to win the prestigious **Singapore Open** title.
- **Final Encounter**: In the final match, the Indian pair defeated the Indonesian duo of **Fajar Alfian** and **Muhammad Fikri** with a score of **18-21, 21-17, 21-16**.
- **Career Titles**: This victory marks their **9th career BWF World Tour title** and their **3rd Super 750 crown**.
- **Semifinal Breakthrough**: The World No. 4 Indian pair advanced to the final following a victory over reigning world champions **Kim Won-ho** and **Seo Seung-jae** of South Korea.
- **Previous Title**: Prior to this tournament, the duo's last title triumph was at the **Thailand Open in 2024**.
