# 📌 Indian Civil Aviation Trends: Bengaluru Overtakes Mumbai in Domestic Traffic

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Historic Ranking Shift**: In **April 2026**, **Bengaluru Airport (Kempegowda International Airport - BLR)** overtook **Mumbai Airport (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport - BOM)** to become **India's 2nd busiest domestic airport**.
- **Domestic Passenger Volumes**: Bengaluru handled **3.28 million domestic passengers** in April 2026 compared to Mumbai's **2.89 million**.
- **Mumbai Domestic Contraction**: Mumbai recorded a **12% year-on-year (YoY) decline** in domestic passenger traffic during April.
- **Overall Passenger Traffic**: Mumbai retained its status as the **2nd busiest airport overall** (domestic + international combined) handling **4.00 million total passengers**, compared to Bengaluru's **3.68 million total passengers**.
- **Structural Cause**: Mumbai Airport faces severe **slot saturation** operating primarily as a **single-runway system** at any given time (due to cross-runway geometry), constraining capacity additions.
- **Future Relief & Expansion**: The upcoming **Navi Mumbai International Airport (NMIAL)** is projected to surpass CSMIA Mumbai in flight movements and passenger volume by the early-to-mid **2030s**.
