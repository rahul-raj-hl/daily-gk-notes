# 📌 Barda Wildlife Sanctuary: Geography, Drainage & Conservation

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **I only** (Statements II and III are incorrect).
- **Location**: Located in the **Saurashtra peninsula of Gujarat** (spanning Porbandar and Devbhumi Dwarka districts), *not in the Trans-Himalayan region*.
- **River Drainage**: Drained by seasonal rivers and streams, prominently the **Bileshvary and Joghri rivers**, which ensure perennial water holes.
- **Dam-Based Water Sources**: Contains vital freshwater reservoirs including the **Khambala and Fodara dams**, which sustain its rich wildlife and forest canopy.
- **Strategic Ecological Significance**: Designated as the proposed **second home for Asiatic Lions (*Panthera leo persica*)** under **Project Lion** to decongest Gir National Park and mitigate epidemic risks.
- **Vegetation Type**: Predominantly composed of **Tropical Thorn Forest, Dry Deciduous Scrub, and Bamboo groves** characteristic of the Kathiawar-Gir dry deciduous forest ecoregion.
