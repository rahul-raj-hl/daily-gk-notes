# 📌 Cuttack Rupa Tarakasi: Silver Filigree Craft & GI Tag

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Craft Identity**: **Rupa Tarakasi** (*Chandi Tarakasi*) is regarded as one of India’s finest and most exquisite **silver filigree** traditions.
- **Geographical Epicenter**: **Cuttack, Odisha** (historically renowned as the *"Silver City of India"*).
- **Core Technique**: The craft involves drawing high-purity silver into hair-thin **fine silver wires** (*Tara*) and shaping or twisting them into intricate, delicate lacework and ornamental designs (*Kasi*).
- **Geographical Indication (GI) Tag**: Accorded the **GI Tag** by the Geographical Indications Registry (Chennai) under the *Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999*, protecting traditional artisanal knowledge from imitation.
- **Cultural Significance**: Extensively used during Cuttack's famous **Durga Puja (Chandi Medha)** to construct silver tableaus/backdrops and ornaments for deities, as well as during Bali Yatra.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Art & Technique of Rupa Tarakasi

| Process Stage | Craft Technique / Step | Description & Materials Used |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Smelting & Purifying** | High-grade silver smelting | Pure silver is melted in clay crucibles and mixed with a small trace of copper/alloy for workability. |
| **2. Wire Drawing (*Tara*)** | Thinning wire through gauge dies | The ingot is rolled and manually pulled through wire-drawing plates into threads as fine as human hair. |
| **3. Twisting & Crimping** | Hand-crimping and flattening | Two or more fine threads are twisted together, flattened, and shaped into miniature coils, petals, or geometric motifs. |
| **4. Framework & Soldering** | Setting within outer silver frame | Delicate filigree spirals are filled into a sturdier outer silver frame and soldered using borax powder (*Suhaga*). |
| **5. Cleaning & Polishing** | Acid washing and burnishing | Polished and washed with natural acidic solutions (like *reetha* or lemon juice) to reveal a gleaming white luster. |
