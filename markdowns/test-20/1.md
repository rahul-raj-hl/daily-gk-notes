# 📌 Indian Women's Hockey Team: Salima Tete Named Captain for FIH Nations Cup

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Captain**: Midfielder **Salima Tete** was named captain of the 20-member Indian women's hockey squad.
- **Tournament**: **FIH Women's Nations Cup 2025–26**, scheduled to take place in **Auckland, New Zealand**.
- **Goalkeeping Core**: Experienced goalkeepers **Savita** (former captain) and **Bichu Devi Kharibam**.
- **Defensive & Midfield Pillars**: Anchored by senior players including **Sushila Chanu Pukhrambam**, **Nikki Pradhan**, **Ishika Chaudhary**, and **Jyoti**.
- **Forward Line Leadership**: **Navneet Kaur** was designated to lead the forward line and attacking unit.
- **Strategic Vision**: The tournament serves as critical preparation and qualification pathway toward the **FIH World Cup** and the upcoming **Asian Games**.
