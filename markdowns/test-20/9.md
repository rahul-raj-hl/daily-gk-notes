# 📌 Mega AI Centre of Excellence (CoE) at IIT Patna, Bihta

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Option**: **"The funding involves a collaborative effort with industry partners contributing ₹12 crore."**
- **Location & Land**: Established across **11 acres** within the permanent IIT Patna campus at **Bihta, Bihar** (*not 25 acres*).
- **Total Project Outlay**: **₹60 crore** (*not ₹100 crore*), financed through a collaborative tripartite public-private partnership (PPP) model:
  - **Central Government**: ₹24 crore ($40\%$)
  - **Government of Bihar**: ₹24 crore ($40\%$)
  - **Industry Partners**: ₹12 crore ($20\%$)
- **Primary Industry Partner**: **Tiger Analytics** (*not Google*).
- **Lead Academic Institution**: **IIT Patna** (spearheading R&D, algorithm development, and capacity building).
- **Core Focus Areas**: High-performance computing (HPC) and applied AI solutions tailored to address regional challenges: **flood forecasting**, **crop disease detection**, and **tele-healthcare delivery in remote areas** (*not space research*).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Tripartite Collaborative Funding Structure

| Stakeholder Entity | Financial Contribution (₹) | Percentage Share | Strategic Role & Responsibility |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Central Government** | **₹24 Crore** | **$40\%$** | Capital assistance, national AI mission alignment, and supercomputing infrastructure access. |
| **Government of Bihar** | **₹24 Crore** | **$40\%$** | State-level data integration, regional deployment pipelines, and administrative support. |
| **Industry Partners** *(e.g., Tiger Analytics)* | **₹12 Crore** | **$20\%$** | Industry mentorship, commercial AI deployment, datasets, and applied technical tools. |
| **Total Project Cost** | **₹60 Crore** | **$100\%$** | Complete financial corpus for setup, computational hardware, and operational cycles. |

### 2. Core Applied AI Verticals & Regional Impact

| Domain Vertical | Applied AI / Machine Learning Implementation | Target Regional Problem Solved |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Disaster Resilience & Hydrology** | Predictive hydrological modeling and flood inundation simulation using satellite and sensory feeds. | Mitigates devastating recurring seasonal floods across North Bihar (Kosi, Gandak, Bagmati basins). |
| **Agritech & Plant Diagnostics** | Computer vision-based disease identification via smartphone imaging and precision yield analysis. | Assists smallholder farmers in early crop pest detection and optimizing fertilizer/pesticide usage. |
| **Remote Tele-Healthcare** | Diagnostic AI triage, automated screening, and regional language tele-consultation portals. | Bridges the specialist doctor deficit in remote and rural primary health centers across Bihar. |
| **High-Performance Computing (HPC)** | GPU clusters and high-throughput machine learning pipelines for complex multi-modal data processing. | Fuels indigenous technological R&D and deep-tech startup incubation at IIT Patna. |
