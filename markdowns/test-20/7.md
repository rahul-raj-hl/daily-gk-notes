# 📌 Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO): Structure & Mandate

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Statements**: **I and II only** (Statement III is incorrect because the headquarters are in **Rome, Italy**, not Geneva, Switzerland).
- **Establishment**: Founded on **16 October 1945** in Quebec City, Canada, as a specialized agency of the United Nations.
- **World Food Day**: Celebrated globally every year on **16th October** to commemorate the founding date of the FAO.
- **Headquarters**: Located in **Rome, Italy** (alongside other UN food agencies: WFP and IFAD).
- **India's Status**: **India is a founding member** of the FAO, maintaining close collaboration across agricultural research, crop diversity, and nutrition programs since 1945.
- **Membership & Reach**: Comprises **194 member nations**, 1 member organization (the European Union), and 2 associate members, operating across more than 130 countries worldwide.
- **Core Mandate**: Defeating hunger, achieving food security for all, and ensuring high-quality nutrition through sustainable agriculture, forestry, and fisheries management.

---

### 2. The Three Rome-Based UN Food Agencies

| Agency Acronym | Full Organization Name | Founding Year | Core Operational Role |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **FAO** | **Food and Agriculture Organization** | **1945** | Policy guidance, agricultural knowledge, global statistics, and standard-setting for food systems. |
| **WFP** | **World Food Programme** | **1961** | Emergency food assistance, humanitarian relief in conflict zones, and logistics (Nobel Peace Prize 2020). |
| **IFAD** | **International Fund for Agricultural Development** | **1977** | Financial institution providing concessional loans and grants for rural poverty eradication and smallholder farming. |

---

### 3. Flagship Reports & Initiatives Published by the FAO

| Publication / Initiative | Key Focus Area & Policy Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SOFI Report** (*State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World*) | Joint annual flagship assessment tracking global progress toward ending hunger and malnutrition. |
| **SOFA Report** (*State of Food and Agriculture*) | In-depth annual evaluation of agricultural economics, rural labor, and sustainable agri-food systems. |
| **GIAHS** (*Globally Important Agricultural Heritage Systems*) | Recognizes traditional agro-ecosystems (e.g., Kuttanad Below Sea Level Farming, Koraput Traditional Agriculture in India). |
| **Codex Alimentarius Commission** | Joint standard-setting body run by **FAO and WHO** to ensure international food safety and trade fairness. |
