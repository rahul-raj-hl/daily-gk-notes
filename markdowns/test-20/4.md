# 📌 Removal of Judges & The Judges (Inquiry) Act, 1968

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Constitutional Basis**: Governed by **Article 124(4) & (5)** (for Supreme Court Judges) and **Article 217(1)(b)** (for High Court Judges) of the Constitution of India.
- **Enabling Legislation**: The procedure for investigation and proof of the misbehaviour or incapacity of a judge is regulated by **The Judges (Inquiry) Act, 1968**[cite: 5].
- **Constitutional Grounds for Removal**: Only two grounds:
  1. **Proved misbehaviour**[cite: 5]
  2. **Incapacity**[cite: 5]
- **Inquiry Committee Structure**: A statutory 3-member independent fact-finding body comprising[cite: 5]:
  1. A Judge of the Supreme Court (or Chief Justice of India).
  2. A Chief Justice of a High Court.
  3. A distinguished jurist.
- **Principles of Natural Justice**: The concerned judge is framed with specific charges and given a **reasonable opportunity to present a defence** and cross-examine witnesses[cite: 5].
- **Parliamentary Passage**: Requires a **Special Majority** in both Houses of Parliament in the same session, followed by an order of removal signed by the **President of India**[cite: 5].

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Step-by-Step Procedure for the Removal of a Judge

| Step | Stage of Procedure | Authority / Body Involved | Procedural Requirement & Working |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Introduction of Motion** | Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha | • Signed by at least **100 members** if introduced in Lok Sabha.<br>• Signed by at least **50 members** if introduced in Rajya Sabha. |
| **2** | **Admission of Motion** | Speaker (LS) / Chairman (RS) | Presiding officer may admit the motion or refuse to admit it. |
| **3** | **Constitution of Inquiry Committee** | Speaker / Chairman | A **3-member committee** is formed to investigate the allegations as an independent fact-finding body[cite: 5]. |
| **4** | **Inquiry & Defence** | 3-Member Committee & Concerned Judge | The committee frames charges; the judge is served notice and given reasonable opportunity to submit a written statement and defence[cite: 5]. |
| **5** | **Submission of Report** | Inquiry Committee | • If charges are **not substantiated**, the proceedings terminate.<br>• If **guilty of proved misbehaviour or incapacity**, the report is tabled in Parliament[cite: 5]. |
| **6** | **Parliamentary Debate & Voting** | Lok Sabha & Rajya Sabha | Must be passed by each House by a **Special Majority**: Majority of total membership of the House **AND** a majority of not less than $2/3\text{rd}$ of members present and voting[cite: 5]. |
| **7** | **Presidential Order** | President of India | President issues an order for the removal of the judge. |

---

### 2. Composition of the Statutory 3-Member Inquiry Committee

*Under Section 3(2) of The Judges (Inquiry) Act, 1968:*

| Member | Designated Category | Appointing Authority |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Member 1** | **Judge of the Supreme Court** (including the Chief Justice of India) | Nominated by the Speaker (LS) / Chairman (RS) |
| **Member 2** | **Chief Justice of a High Court** | Nominated by the Speaker (LS) / Chairman (RS) |
| **Member 3** | **Distinguished Jurist** | Selected on the basis of eminent legal scholarship |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Constitutional Feature | Removal of a Judge | Impeachment of President (Art 61) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grounds** | **Proved misbehaviour or incapacity**[cite: 5] | **Violation of the Constitution** |
| **Notice Requirement** | $100$ (LS) / $50$ (RS) | $1/4\text{th}$ of total members of the House |
| **Majority Required** | Special Majority ($2/3\text{rd}$ present & voting + absolute majority)[cite: 5] | Special Majority of $\mathbf{2/3\text{rd}\text{ of total membership}}$ of the House |
| **Role of Chief Justice** | Inquires as part of the 3-member committee | Presides over Supreme Court, not the legislative vote |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "100 IN LOK, 50 IN RAJYA, 3 INVESTIGATE, 2/3rd VOTE TO VACATE"**
> - **100 in Lok** $\rightarrow$ 100 members in Lok Sabha for motion
> - **50 in Rajya** $\rightarrow$ 50 members in Rajya Sabha for motion
> - **3 Investigate** $\rightarrow$ 3-Member Inquiry Committee (SC Judge + HC CJ + Jurist)[cite: 5]
> - **2/3rd Vote** $\rightarrow$ Special Majority to pass address to President[cite: 5]

- [ ] Violation of the Constitution

> Under the Judges (Inquiry) Act, 1968, a removal motion must be signed by at least 100 members in the Lok Sabha or at least 50 members in the Rajya Sabha[cite: 5].
