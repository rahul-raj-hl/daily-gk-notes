# 📌 Nasha Mukt Vidyalaya & Student Mental Health Initiatives

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Nasha Mukt Vidyalaya Portal**: Tracks institutional compliance and tobacco/drug-free school protocols via **UDISE+** (Unified District Information System for Education Plus).
- **Mental Health & Psychosocial Support**: Directly integrated through the national **Manodarpan helpline (844 844 0632)** under the Ministry of Education.
- **School Felicitations & Awards**: A total of **12 schools** were felicitated across four distinct National Education Policy (NEP 2020) categories: **Foundational, Preparatory, Middle, and Secondary**.
  - **1st Prize**: ₹50,000
  - **2nd Prize**: ₹25,000
  - **3rd Prize**: ₹15,000
- **Demographic Reach**: The school education system in India connects with nearly **24.69 crore children** and directly influences **15–20 crore households**.
- **Legislative Milestone**: Highlights the prohibition of **electronic cigarettes (e-cigarettes / ENDS)** enacted through *The Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Act, 2019*.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Pillars of the Drug-Free School & Student Welfare Ecosystem

| Pillar / Component | Implementing Mechanism | Core Function & Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nasha Mukt Vidyalaya Portal** | Integrated with **UDISE+** | Real-time monitoring and reporting of substance-free school campus guidelines across all recognized schools in India. |
| **Manodarpan Initiative** | National Helpline: **844 844 0632** | Provides 24/7 tele-counselling and psychosocial guidance to students, teachers, and parents to support emotional well-being and stress management. |
| **School Category Awards** | **4 NEP Tiers**: Foundational (Ages 3–8), Preparatory (8–11), Middle (11–14), Secondary (14–18) | Recognizes 12 model schools creating proactive awareness and peer-support mechanisms against substance abuse. |
| **Statutory Tobacco Control** | COTPA (2003) & PECA (2019) | Enforces the ban on sale of tobacco/nicotine products within 100 yards of educational institutions and complete prohibition of e-cigarettes. |
