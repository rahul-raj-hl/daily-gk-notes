# 📌 Aluminium Smelting Industry in India: Key Corporations & Plant Locations

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Aluminium Smelting in India**: Aluminium is the **second most important metallurgical industry** in India after iron and steel, prized for being light, resistant to corrosion, highly malleable, and a good conductor of heat and electricity.
- **Key Industry Players & Main Plants**:
  - **BALCO (Bharat Aluminium Company Ltd.)**: Established in **1965**; major integrated plant located at **Korba (Chhattisgarh)**; now part of Vedanta Resources.
  - **HINDALCO (Hindalco Industries Ltd.)**: Flagship metals company of the Aditya Birla Group; prime smelter set up at **Renukoot (Sonbhadra district, Uttar Pradesh)** in **1962**.
  - **INDAL (Indian Aluminium Company Ltd.)**: Founded in **1938**; major smelter located at **Hirakud (Odisha)**; later merged into Hindalco.
  - **NALCO (National Aluminium Company Ltd.)**: Established in **1981** as a central Public Sector Enterprise (CPSE); operates one of Asia's largest integrated complexes with bauxite mines/alumina refinery at **Damanjodi (Koraput, Odisha)** and smelter at **Angul (Odisha)**.
- **Primary Raw Material**: **Bauxite** ($\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 \cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$); roughly 4 to 6 tonnes of bauxite yield 2 tonnes of alumina, which produce 1 tonne of aluminium.

### 2. Aluminium Manufacturing Flow

| Stage | Process Name | Raw Material & Inputs | Output Product |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Bauxite Mining** | Raw bauxite ore extracted from open-cast mines | Crushed Bauxite Ore ($4\text{ to }6\text{ tonnes}$) |
| **2** | **Bayer Process (Refining)** | Digestion with caustic soda ($\text{NaOH}$) under pressure | Calcined **Alumina ($\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$)** ($~2\text{ tonnes}$) |
| **3** | **Hall-Héroult Process (Smelting)** | Electrolytic reduction using molten **cryolite ($\text{Na}_3\text{AlF}_6$)** and massive electric currents | Molten **Aluminium Metal** ($~1\text{ tonne}$) |

- **Critical Locational Factors**: Regular supply of **cheap, uninterrupted electric power** and assured sources of **bauxite raw material** are the two primary determinants of aluminium plant locations.

### Q2

Which of the following substances is added to alumina during the electrolytic Hall-Héroult reduction process to lower its melting point and enhance electrical conductivity?

- [ ] Dolomite
- [ ] Gypsum
- [x] Cryolite
- [ ] Limestone

> Cryolite ($\text{Na}_3\text{AlF}_6$) is mixed with alumina during smelting to lower the operational melting temperature from over $2000^\circ\text{C}$ to around $950^\circ\text{C}$, significantly reducing energy consumption.
