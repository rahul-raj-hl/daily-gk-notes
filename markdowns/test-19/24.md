# 📌 Janjatiya Garima Utsav 2026: Technology, Genomics & Tribal Healthcare

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Event & Date**: The national workshop for **Janjatiya Garima Utsav 2026** was convened in **New Delhi on 14th May 2026**.
- **Theme**: The inaugural week was celebrated under the theme **"Technology as a Development Driver"**.
- **Genomic & Health Initiatives (CSIR-IGIB)**: Presented by **Dr. Souvik Maiti** (Director, CSIR-IGIB), highlighting flagship projects:
  - **GUaRDIAN initiative** (Genomics for Understanding Rare Diseases: India Alliance Network)
  - **Indian Breast Cancer Genome Atlas (IBCGA)**
  - **PHENOME India** (Longitudinal health monitoring and cohort phenomics)
- **Traditional Knowledge Preservation (CSIR-TKDL)**: Led by **Smt. Viswajanani Sattigeri** (Director, CSIR-TKDL), focusing on the digital documentation and intellectual property protection of tribal ethno-medicinal formulations.
- **Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination**: Commemorating **Bhagwan Birsa Munda**, with a focus on indigenous CRISPR-based gene-editing therapeutic pathways presented by **Dr. Debojyoti Chakraborty**.
