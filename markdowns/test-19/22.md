# 📌 Sweden's Royal Order of the Polar Star: PM Narendra Modi's Honour

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **The Award**: Prime Minister **Narendra Modi** was conferred with Sweden's prestigious **Royal Order of the Polar Star** (*Kungliga Nordstjärneorden*).
- **Highest Distinction**: It is the highest honor that can be awarded to a **Head of Government** by the Scandinavian Kingdom of **Sweden**.
- **Historical Inception**: The order was officially instituted in the year **1748** (founded by King Frederick I of Sweden) to recognize exceptional personal endeavors, civic contributions, and public duties for Swedish and international interests.
- **Milestone Count**: This conferment marks Prime Minister Narendra Modi's **31st international state honour**.
- **Bilateral Visit**: PM Modi was received by his Swedish counterpart, Prime Minister **Ulf Kristersson**, at Gothenburg Airport during his bilateral visit to Sweden.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Facts About the Royal Order of the Polar Star

| Parameter | Details & Significance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Conferring Nation** | **Kingdom of Sweden** (Scandinavian country in Northern Europe) |
| **Institution Year** | **1748** (Established by King Frederick I) |
| **Motto of the Order** | *"Nescit Occasum"* (Latin for *"It knows no decline"* or *"It never sets"*) |
| **Primary Objective** | Conferred on foreign nationals and members of the Royal Family for civic merits, devotion to duty, science, literature, and strengthening bilateral ties. |
| **Significance for India** | Recognizes the deepening strategic partnership between India and Sweden in innovation, green transition, and trade. |
