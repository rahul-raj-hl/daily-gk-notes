# 📌 CERT-In & Cybersecurity Framework in India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **WhatsApp** (WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business).
- **The Event**: In April 2021, India's premier cybersecurity agency **CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)** issued a **"high" severity advisory** warning users of vulnerabilities in WhatsApp that could allow attackers to execute arbitrary code or access sensitive information.
- **Affected Builds**:
  - **Android**: Versions prior to **v2.21.4** of WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business.
  - **iOS**: Versions prior to **v2.21.32** of WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business.
- **Agency Identity & Parent Body**: CERT-In operates under the **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)**, Government of India.
- **Statutory Mandate**: Designated as the national nodal agency under **Section 70B of the Information Technology (IT) Act, 2000** to respond to computer security incidents, issue vulnerability advisories, and guard Indian cyberspace.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Structure & Core Functions of CERT-In

*Key statutory responsibilities of CERT-In under Section 70B of the IT Act, 2000:*

| Functional Area | Core Responsibilities & Operations |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Incident Response & Monitoring** | Serves as the national nodal agency to track, collect, analyze, and monitor cybersecurity incidents across the country. |
| **Early Warning & Advisories** | Issues real-time alerts and severity advisories regarding software vulnerabilities, malware threats, phishing scams, and ransomware. |
| **Vulnerability Coordination** | Collaborates with domestic and global software vendors, telecom providers, and enterprises to patch software flaws before exploitation. |
| **Emergency Incident Containment** | Formulates rapid emergency measures for handling major cybersecurity intrusions and cyber-attacks on national infrastructure. |
| **Guidelines & Compliance** | Mandates reporting of cybersecurity incidents by service providers, intermediaries, data centers, and corporate bodies within specified timeframes (e.g., within 6 hours). |

---

### 2. Major Cybersecurity Organizations & Initiatives in India

| Organization / Initiative | Parent Body / Nodal Framework | Core Mandate / Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CERT-In** | Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) | National nodal agency for cyber incident response, technical advisories, and cyber defense coordination. |
| **NCIIPC** *(National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre)* | National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO) / PMO | Dedicated specifically to protecting **Critical Information Infrastructure (CII)** (Power, Banking, Telecom, Defense, Transport) under **Section 70 of IT Act**. |
| **I4C** *(Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre)* | Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) | Handles citizen-centric cybercrime complaints, online financial fraud portals (**cybercrime.gov.in**), and law enforcement coordination. |
| **Cyber Swachhta Kendra** *(Botnet Cleaning & Malware Analysis Centre)* | CERT-In / MeitY | Provides free botnet detection and malware removal tools for citizens and organizations. |
| **National Cyber Security Strategy** | National Security Council Secretariat (NSCS) | Comprehensive policy blueprint aimed at securing national digital assets and critical infrastructure. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Organization | Key Mandate Focus | Common Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CERT-In** | **General technical incident response & software vulnerability advisories** | Confusing with NCIIPC (which is strictly for Critical Infrastructure) |
| **NCIIPC** | **Critical Information Infrastructure protection (Power, Banking, etc.)** | Confusing with MHA's cybercrime portal |
| **I4C (MHA)** | **Cyber fraud reporting & citizen crime investigation** | Assuming CERT-In directly investigates local police financial frauds |
| **Section 70 vs. Section 70B** | • **Section 70**: Critical Information Infrastructure (NCIIPC)<br>• **Section 70B**: Appointment and powers of CERT-In | Mixing up the section numbers in the IT Act, 2000 |

> **Mnemonic: "CERT COUNTERS CRIME, NCIIPC PROTECTS POWER, I4C INVESTIGATES CITIZEN FRAUD"**
