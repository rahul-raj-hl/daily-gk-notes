# 📌 L&T and Exail Defence Partnership: Unmanned MCM Suite for Indian Navy

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Strategic Collaboration**: **Larsen & Toubro (L&T)** acts as the prime contractor, while **Exail** (France) serves as the global technology partner[cite: 1].
- **Programme Scope**: The partnership provides an advanced **Unmanned Mine Countermeasure (MCM) Suite** to participating shipyards for the **Indian Navy's upcoming programme for 12 Mine Counter Measure Vessels (MCMVs)**[cite: 1].
- **System Capabilities**: Features autonomous and remotely operated robotic underwater systems designed to safely **detect, classify, identify, and neutralise naval mines from a stand-off distance** without endangering crewed vessels[cite: 1].
- **Indo-French Strategic Ties**: Reinforces bilateral **Indo-French defence cooperation** by transferring state-of-the-art naval technology and building a self-reliant domestic naval defence ecosystem[cite: 1].
- **National Policy Alignment**: Closely supports the Government of India's **Aatmanirbhar Bharat** and **Make in India** initiatives through domestic co-production and industrial capacity development[cite: 1].

### 2. Operational Phases of Modern Mine Countermeasure (MCM) Systems

| Stage | Operational Function | Robotic / Sonar Mechanism Involved |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Detection** | High-resolution sonar scanning | Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUVs) scan the sea floor using synthetic aperture sonar (SAS) to spot suspicious submerged anomalies[cite: 1]. |
| **2. Classification** | Acoustic profiling & shape separation | Onboard AI processors differentiate potential mine-like objects from natural seabed rocks or harmless debris[cite: 1]. |
| **3. Identification** | Close-range optical & acoustic verification | Remotely Operated Vehicles (ROVs) inspect the target with optical cameras and high-frequency sonars[cite: 1]. |
| **4. Neutralisation** | Safe stand-off detonation | Expendable Mine Disposal Systems (EMDS) or robotic charges detonate the mine safely from a remote distance[cite: 1]. |
