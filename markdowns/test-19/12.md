# 📌 Global Places in News: Wuhan, Natanz & Beirut

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Wuhan**:
  - Capital of **Hubei Province**, People's Republic of China.
  - Major commercial and transportation hub situated at the confluence of the **Yangtze River** and the **Han River**.
- **Natanz**:
  - Located in the **Isfahan Province** of central Iran.
  - Globally known as Iran’s primary underground **uranium enrichment nuclear facility**.
- **Beirut**:
  - Capital and largest city/port of **Lebanon**, situated on the Mediterranean coast.
  - Site of the massive 2020 port explosion triggered by improperly stored **ammonium nitrate ($\text{NH}_4\text{NO}_3$)**.
