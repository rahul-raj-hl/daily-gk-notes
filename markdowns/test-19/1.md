# 📌 Raja Ram Mohan Roy: Pioneer of Modern Education & Renaissance in India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Raja Ram Mohan Roy**.
- **Father of Modern India / Indian Renaissance**: Raja Ram Mohan Roy was the first Indian social reformer to advocate for Western, scientific, and English education as an essential medium for spreading rationalism, enlightenment, and modern ideas across the country.
- **Educational Milestones**:
  - Assisted **David Hare** in founding the **Hindu College in Calcutta (1817)** (which later evolved into Presidency College).
  - Founded the **Anglo-Hindu School (1822)** to teach English and modern sciences alongside Indian philosophy.
  - Established the **Vedanta College (1825)** offering courses combining indigenous Vedic learning with Western physical sciences.
- **Key Social & Institutional Achievements**:
  - Founded the **Atmiya Sabha (1815)** and the **Brahmo Samaj (1828)** to promote monotheism and eradicate idol worship, superstition, and caste dogma.
  - Spearheaded the crusade that led to the abolition of Sati via Regulation XVII of 1829 by Governor-General Lord William Bentinck.

### 2. Raja Ram Mohan Roy’s Major Publications & Journals

*Pioneering journalistic and literary works:*

- ***Tuhfat-ul-Muwahhidin* (A Gift to Monotheists)** (1803–1804): Written in Persian with an Arabic introduction, arguing against polytheism and defending rational monotheism.
- ***Precepts of Jesus* (1820)**: Separated the moral and ethical message of the New Testament from its miracles and dogmas.
- ***Sambad Kaumudi* (1821)**: Bengali weekly newspaper advocating social reform, widow remarriage, and the abolition of Sati.
- ***Mirat-ul-Akhbar* (1822)**: The first Persian weekly newspaper published in India, focusing on domestic and international socio-political affairs.
