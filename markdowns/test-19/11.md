# 📌 King Amoghavarsha I & Acharya Jinasena: Rashtrakuta Golden Age

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Amoghavarsha I (800–878 CE / 814–878 CE)**: Also known as **Nrupatunga**, he was the greatest and longest-reigning ruler of the **Rashtrakuta Dynasty** (reigned for over 64 years).
- **Spiritual Mentorship & Jainism**: Amoghavarsha I was a devout patron and follower of **Digambara Jainism**; the eminent Digambara monk and scholar **Acharya Jinasena** served as his chief spiritual preceptor (*guru*).
- **Literary Masterpiece**: Amoghavarsha I authored ***Kavirajamarga*** (co-authored with court poet Sri Vijaya), the earliest available work on poetics, rhetoric, and grammar in the **Kannada language**.
- **Founding of Manyakheda**: Built the new imperial Rashtrakuta capital at **Manyakheda (modern Malkhed in Kalaburagi/Gulbarga district, Karnataka)**.
- **Works of Jinasena**: Composed ***Parsvabhudaya*** (a poetic biography of 23rd Tirthankara Parshvanatha using Kalidasa's *Meghaduta* meters), ***Adipurana*** (biography of Rishabhanatha, later completed by his disciple Gunabhadra), and ***Mahapurana***.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Contributions: Amoghavarsha I vs. Acharya Jinasena

| Historical Figure | Role / Distinction | Major Literary & Cultural Contributions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Amoghavarsha I (Nrupatunga)** | • Greatest Emperor of the Rashtrakuta Dynasty<br>• Compared to Ashoka for his religious tolerance and love for peace | • Authored ***Kavirajamarga*** (earliest known Kannada literary work on poetics).<br>• Authored ***Prashnottara Ratnamalika*** (Sanskrit philosophical work).<br>• Shifted the capital from Mayurkhandi to **Manyakheda (Malkhed)**.<br>• Renounced worldly attachments in his later years to embrace Jain asceticism under Jinasena. |
| **Acharya Jinasena** | • Preeminent Digambara Jain monk, philosopher, and Sanskrit scholar<br>• Chief spiritual preceptor (*Rajaguru*) to Amoghavarsha I | • Composed ***Parsvabhudaya*** (biography of Tirthankara Parshvanatha).<br>• Initiated ***Adipurana*** (life of the first Tirthankara Rishabhanatha and Bharata Chakravartin).<br>• Co-authored the encyclopedic Digambara text ***Mahapurana***.<br>• Authored ***Jayadhavala tika*** (commentary on Jain canonical philosophy). |

### 2. Major Dynastic Rulers of the Rashtrakutas

*Chronological sequence of prominent Rashtrakuta sovereigns:*

| Ruler | Reign Period | Key Historical Significance & Achievements |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Dantidurga** | 735–756 CE | **Founder of the Rashtrakuta Empire**; performed the *Hiranyagarbha* (Golden Womb) ritual at Ujjain after defeating the Chalukyas of Badami. |
| **Krishna I** | 756–774 CE | Uncle of Dantidurga; built the rock-cut monolithic **Kailash Temple (Cave 16) at Ellora**. |
| **Govinda III** | 793–814 CE | Great military conqueror; expanded the empire into North India during the **Tripartite Struggle** against Gurjara-Pratiharas and Palas. |
| **Amoghavarsha I** | **814–878 CE** | **Longest reign (64 years)**; patronized Jainism, Kannada literature, and established **Manyakheda**. |
| **Krishna III** | 939–967 CE | Last great Rashtrakuta ruler; defeated the Cholas at the famous **Battle of Takkolam (949 CE)** and erected a victory pillar at Rameswaram. |

---

### 3. Arab Travelers' Accounts of Amoghavarsha's Empire

- **Sulaiman al-Tajir** (visited India in 851 CE during Amoghavarsha’s reign):
  - Ranked the **Rashtrakuta Empire (Balhara)** as one of the **four greatest empires of the contemporary world**, alongside the **Abbasid Caliphate (Baghdad), the Roman/Byzantine Empire, and the Chinese Empire**.
- **Al-Masudi** (visited in 10th century):
  - Reaffirmed the titles of the Rashtrakuta rulers as *Vallabharaja* (contracted into the Arabic *Balhara*).

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Historical Attribute | Correct Figure / Ruler | Common Trap / Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Founder of Dynasty** | **Dantidurga** | Confusing with Amoghavarsha I (the greatest ruler, not founder) |
| **Builder of Kailash Temple** | **Krishna I** | Mistakenly attributing Ellora Kailash Temple to Amoghavarsha |
| **Author of *Kavirajamarga*** | **Amoghavarsha I / Sri Vijaya** | Confusing with Pampa (author of *Vikramarjuna Vijaya*) |
| **Author of *Adipurana*** | **Jinasena** | Confusing with Pampa (who wrote the Kannada version *Adipurana*) |
| ***Pampa, Ranna, and Ponna*** | **Three Gems of Kannada Literature (*Ratnatraya*)** | Confusing them with Rashtrakuta kings |
