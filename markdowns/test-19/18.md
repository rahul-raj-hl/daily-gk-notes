# 📌 Moons of the Solar System: Galilean Satellites & Planetary Features

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Triton**: The largest moon of **Neptune** (*not Jupiter*), notable for its unique **retrograde orbit** (orbits in the direction opposite to Neptune's rotation) and active cryovolcanoes.
- **The Galilean Moons of Jupiter**: Discovered by **Galileo Galilei in 1610**, Jupiter's four largest natural satellites are **Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto**.
  - **Ganymede**: The **largest moon in the Solar System** (larger than the planet Mercury and the dwarf planet Pluto).
  - **Europa**: Possesses a smooth ice shell covering a vast **subsurface liquid water ocean**, making it a primary target in the search for extraterrestrial life.
  - **Io**: The most **volcanically active body** in the entire Solar System.
  - **Callisto**: Heavily cratered, ancient ice-rock surface.
- **Jupiter's Key Features**: Largest planet in the Solar System, primarily composed of **hydrogen and helium**, featuring a massive magnetic field and the **Great Red Spot** (a giant anticyclonic storm lasting hundreds of years).

### 1. Major Moons of the Outer Planets

*Overview of prominent natural satellites across the giant planets:*

| Planet | Major Moons / Satellites | Defining Features & Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jupiter** | • **Ganymede**<br>• **Europa**<br>• **Io**<br>• **Callisto** | • **Ganymede**: Largest moon in Solar System; has its own magnetic field.<br>• **Europa**: Global subterranean liquid water ocean under ice crust.<br>• **Io**: Extreme sulfur volcanism caused by tidal heating.<br>• **Callisto**: Most heavily cratered object known. |
| **Saturn** | • **Titan**<br>• **Enceladus**<br>• **Mimas**<br>• **Iapetus** | • **Titan**: Second-largest moon in Solar System; dense nitrogen atmosphere and liquid methane/ethane lakes.<br>• **Enceladus**: Ice geysers ejecting water vapor from a subsurface ocean. |
| **Uranus** | • **Titania**<br>• **Oberon**<br>• **Miranda**<br>• **Ariel** | • **Miranda**: Extreme canyon cliffs (*Verona Rupes*).<br>• Named after characters from works of William Shakespeare and Alexander Pope. |
| **Neptune** | • **Triton**<br>• **Proteus**<br>• **Nereid** | • **Triton**: Largest moon of Neptune; retrograde orbit; captured Kuiper Belt object; geysers of nitrogen gas. |
| **Mars** *(Inner Planet)* | • **Phobos**<br>• **Deimos** | • Small, irregularly shaped captured asteroids.<br>• **Deimos** is the smallest known moon in the Solar System. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Moon Pair / Names | Correct Host Planet | Common Exam Trap / Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Triton vs. Titan vs. Titania** | • **Triton**: Neptune<br>• **Titan**: Saturn<br>• **Titania**: Uranus | Mixing up the three similarly named moons |
| **Europa vs. Ganymede** | • **Europa**: Subsurface liquid water ocean<br>• **Ganymede**: Largest moon in Solar System | Swapping their standout planetary traits |
| **Moons of Mars vs. Jupiter** | • **Mars**: Phobos & Deimos<br>• **Jupiter**: Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto | Confusing small Martian moons with Jovian satellites |
| **Planets Without Moons** | **Mercury & Venus** | Assuming all planets have at least one moon |
