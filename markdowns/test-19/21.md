# 📌 AIGCC State of Investor Climate Transition in Asia Report (2025)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Reporting Body & Edition**: Released as the **7th annual "State of Investor Climate Transition in Asia" report** by the **Asia Investor Group on Climate Change (AIGCC)**.
- **Scale of Assessment**: Analysed **240 institutional investors** managing a combined total of **$123 trillion** in assets under management (AUM).
- **Physical Climate Risk Disclosure**: Surged from **29% in 2023 to 61% in 2025**, driven by escalating concerns over the economic fallout of extreme weather disasters.
- **Universal Risk Recognition**: **100% of surveyed AIGCC members** now recognize climate change as a **material financial risk**.
- **Net Zero Commitments**: Approximately **74% of member investors** have established Net Zero portfolio emissions targets, while **58%** have set interim emissions reduction milestones.
- **Top Investment Frontier**: **Energy storage** emerged as the single highest climate investment priority, with investor interest doubling from **40% in 2023 to 82% in 2025**.
- **Key Implementation Gaps**: Less than one-third of investors have concrete strategies regarding fossil fuels, and only **11%** currently have a defined "just transition" framework.
