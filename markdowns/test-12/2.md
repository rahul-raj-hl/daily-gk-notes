# 📌 PM SHRI Schools Scheme: NEP 2020 Showcase & Education Infrastructure

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Full Name of the Scheme**: **PM SHRI** stands for **PM Schools for Rising India** (approved by the Union Cabinet in September 2022).
- **Core Vision**: Upgrades existing schools into **exemplar schools** to showcase the implementation of all key pillars of the **National Education Policy (NEP) 2020**.
- **Coverage Target**: Aims to develop more than **14,500 schools** across India over a 5-year period ($2022\text{--}2023$ to $2026\text{--}2027$).
- **Jurisdictional Scope**: Not restricted to central government schools (like KVs or JNVs); covers schools managed by the **Central Government, State/UT Governments, local bodies, and private/aided entities**.
- **Nature of Funding**: Implemented as a **Centrally Sponsored Scheme**, where financial costs are shared between the Central and State/UT governments.
- **NEP 2020 Headline Target**: Aims to achieve **100% Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER)** in school education from preschool to secondary level by **2030**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Pillars and Features of PM SHRI Schools

| Parameter | Core Feature & Policy Implementation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Exemplar Status** | Acts as model institutions providing leadership and mentorship to other schools in their vicinity. |
| **Pedagogical Approach** | Focuses on **experiential, holistic, inquiry-driven, and competency-based learning** as envisioned in NEP 2020. |
| **Green School Framework** | Incorporates eco-friendly elements including solar panels, LED lighting, nutrition gardens, and waste/water management systems. |
| **Technological Integration** | Equipped with smart classrooms, digital libraries, science and math labs, and vocational training facilities. |
| **Early Vocational Exposure** | Embeds 21st-century skill development, coding, computational thinking, and internship linkages with local artisans/industries. |

---

### 2. Major Goals of National Education Policy (NEP) 2020

| Target Indicator | Target Year | Policy Mandate & Educational Metric |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **100% GER in School Education** | **2030** | Universal access from pre-primary to Grade 12. |
| **50% GER in Higher Education** | **2035** | Expansion of vocational and higher education capacity. |
| **Universal Foundational Literacy & Numeracy** | **2026–27** | Through the national **NIPUN Bharat Mission** (Grade 3 benchmark). |
| **New Curricular Structure** | — | Replaces the old $10+2$ system with the **$5+3+3+4$ structure** (Foundational, Preparatory, Middle, Secondary). |
| **Medium of Instruction** | — | Mother tongue / local language / regional language at least until Grade 5. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Feature | Correct Provision | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **School Eligibility** | Central, State, UT, and local body schools | Believing PM SHRI is restricted only to Kendriya Vidyalayas / Navodaya Vidyalayas |
| **Funding Pattern** | **Centrally Sponsored** (Cost shared) | Assuming it is a 100% Centrally Funded (Central Sector) scheme |
| **School GER vs. Higher Ed GER** | • **School GER (100%)**: Target **2030**<br>• **Higher Education GER (50%)**: Target **2035** | Swapping the target years 2030 and 2035 |
| **PM SHRI vs. Vidyanjali** | • **PM SHRI**: Infrastructure & NEP model schools<br>• **Vidyanjali**: Community & CSR volunteer mentoring portal | Confusing school upgrade schemes with volunteer portals |
