# 📌 Cheetah Conservation: IUCN Status, Subspecies & Project Cheetah

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **International Cheetah Day**: Observed globally on **4th December** every year (established in 2010 by Dr. Laurie Marker, founder of the Cheetah Conservation Fund, in memory of a cheetah cub named *Khayam*).
- **Global IUCN Red List Status**:
  - **Cheetah (Overall / African Cheetah - *Acinonyx jubatus*)**: Classified as **Vulnerable (VU)** on the IUCN Red List.
  - **Asiatic Cheetah (*Acinonyx jubatus venaticus*)**: Classified as **Critically Endangered (CR)** (surviving only in small fragmented populations in Iran).
  - **Northwest African Cheetah (*Acinonyx jubatus hecki*)**: Classified as **Critically Endangered (CR)**.
- **Extinction & Reintroduction in India**:
  - Declared **extinct in India in 1952** (last recorded three Asiatic cheetahs were shot in Koriya, Chhattisgarh in 1947 by Maharaja Ramanuj Pratap Singh Deo).
  - **Project Cheetah** (world's first intercontinental large wild carnivore translocation project) was launched on **17 September 2022**, translocating African cheetahs from **Namibia (8 cheetahs)** and **South Africa (12 cheetahs)** to **Kuno National Park**, Madhya Pradesh.
  - **Gandhi Sagar Wildlife Sanctuary** (MP) has been prepared as the second home for translocated cheetahs.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Comparative Matrix: African Cheetah vs. Asiatic Cheetah

| Biological Parameter | African Cheetah (*Acinonyx jubatus jubatus*) | Asiatic Cheetah (*Acinonyx jubatus venaticus*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IUCN Red List Status** | **Vulnerable (VU)** | **Critically Endangered (CR)** |
| **Global Wild Population** | $\approx 6,500 - 7,100$ individuals across African savannahs. | Extremely low ($< 40-50$ individuals surviving in Iran). |
| **Physical Stature & Coat** | Slightly larger, heavier build, brighter tawny yellow coat with distinct solid spots. | Slightly smaller, slender build, paler fawn-colored coat, thicker fur/neck ruff in winter. |
| **CITES Listing** | **Appendix I** | **Appendix I** |
| **Wildlife Protection Act, 1972 (India)** | **Schedule I** (Highest legal protection) | **Schedule I** (Declared extinct in India in 1952) |
| **Primary Reintroduction Site** | **Kuno National Park (Sheopur, MP)** | — |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Species / Subspecies | IUCN Status | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Cheetah (Global / African)** | **Vulnerable (VU)** | Marking "Endangered" or "Critically Endangered" for the general species |
| **Asiatic Cheetah** | **Critically Endangered (CR)** | Selecting "Vulnerable" for the Asiatic subspecies |
| **Tiger vs. Cheetah** | • Tiger = **Endangered (EN)**<br>• Cheetah = **Vulnerable (VU)** | Swapping the status of Tiger and Cheetah |
| **Leopard vs. Snow Leopard** | • Leopard = **Vulnerable**<br>• Snow Leopard = **Vulnerable** | Assuming Snow Leopard is critically endangered |
| **International Cheetah Day vs. Tiger Day** | • Cheetah Day = **4 December**<br>• Tiger Day = **29 July**<br>• Lion Day = **10 August** | Confusing wildlife observance days |
