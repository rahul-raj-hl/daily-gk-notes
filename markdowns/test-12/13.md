# 📌 Refraction of Light: Frequency Invariance & Wave Dynamics

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Constant Property**: When light transitions from one optical medium to another (e.g., from air into glass or water), its **frequency ($f$ or $\nu$) remains unchanged / constant**.
- **Reason for Invariance**: Frequency is an **intrinsic characteristic of the light source** emitting the wave. The number of wave crests entering a boundary per second must equal the number of wave crests leaving it per second.
- **Properties that Change**:
  - **Speed ($v$)**: Changes depending on the optical density/refractive index ($\mu$) of the medium ($v = \frac{c}{\mu}$).
  - **Wavelength ($\lambda$)**: Changes proportionally with speed ($\lambda = \frac{v}{f} = \frac{\lambda_0}{\mu}$).
  - **Amplitude ($A$) & Intensity ($I$)**: Generally decrease due to partial reflection and absorption at the interface.
- **Wave Speed Equation**: Governed by $$v = f \cdot \lambda$$ Because frequency $f$ remains constant, any reduction in speed ($v$) results in a proportional decrease in wavelength ($\lambda$).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Behavior of Light Wave Parameters During Refraction

| Wave Parameter | Behavior During Transition (Air $\rightarrow$ Denser Medium) | Governing Physical Law / Formula |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Frequency ($f$)** | **Remains Constant / Unchanged** | Characteristic of the source; determines photon energy ($E = h\nu$). |
| **Speed ($v$)** | **Decreases** | $v = \frac{c}{\mu}$ (Slows down in optically denser media). |
| **Wavelength ($\lambda$)** | **Decreases** | $\lambda = \frac{\lambda_{\text{air}}}{\mu}$ (Directly proportional to speed). |
| **Phase ($\phi$)** | **Remains Constant** | No phase change occurs during pure refraction. |
| **Intensity ($I$)** | **Decreases** | Part of the incident energy is reflected or absorbed at the interface. |
| **Direction of Propagation** | **Bends towards the normal** | Snell's Law: $\mu_1 \sin i = \mu_2 \sin r$. |

---

### 2. Physical Basis: Rare to Denser vs. Denser to Rare Medium

| Medium Transition | Speed ($v$) | Wavelength ($\lambda$) | Frequency ($f$) | Ray Bending Behavior |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Rarer to Denser** *(e.g., Air to Water/Glass)* | Decreases | Decreases | **Constant** | Bends **towards** the normal ($i > r$) |
| **Denser to Rarer** *(e.g., Glass to Air/Water)* | Increases | Increases | **Constant** | Bends **away from** the normal ($r > i$) |

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Dimension / Metric | Physical Parameter / Formula | Key Exam Clue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Quantity Unchanged in Refraction** | **Frequency ($f$ / $\nu$)** | Most frequent optics question |
| **Quantity Unchanged in Reflection** | **Speed, Frequency, and Wavelength** | Medium does not change |
| **Relation between $v$, $f$, and $\lambda$** | $v = f \lambda$ | Fundamental wave equation |
| **Photon Energy Equation** | $E = h\nu = hf$ | Planck's equation |
| **Refractive Index of Vacuum / Air**| $\mu \approx 1.0$ | Baseline standard |
| **Refractive Index of Water** | $\mu = \frac{4}{3} \approx 1.33$ | Speed in water $= 2.25 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$ |
| **Refractive Index of Crown Glass**| $\mu = 1.5$ | Speed in glass $= 2.0 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$ |
| **Refractive Index of Diamond** | $\mu = 2.42$ *(Highest common)* | Lowest speed of light $\approx 1.24 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$ |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Parameter Pair | Comparison / Distinction | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frequency vs. Wavelength** | • Frequency is **constant** during refraction.<br>• Wavelength **changes** proportionally with speed. | Assuming wavelength remains constant when light enters water/glass |
| **Refraction vs. Reflection** | • In **Refraction**: Speed & wavelength change, frequency is constant.<br>• In **Reflection**: Speed, wavelength, and frequency all remain constant. | Mixing up reflection (same medium) with refraction (different media) |
| **Color Perception** | Color of light depends on its **frequency**, though conventionally quoted by its vacuum wavelength. | Assuming color changes inside glass when wavelength compresses |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

When light enters from air into a medium, its speed changes. Which of the following properties of light remains constant during this transition?

- [ ] Wavelength
- [x] Frequency
- [ ] Speed
- [ ] Amplitude

> Frequency is determined solely by the source of the wave and represents the number of cycles generated per second; it remains constant across different media.

---

### PYQ 2

When a ray of monochromatic light passes from air into water, which of the following characteristics decreases?

- [ ] Frequency only
- [x] Both speed and wavelength
- [ ] Wavelength only
- [ ] Speed only

> In an optically denser medium like water ($\mu = 1.33$), the speed of light decreases ($v = \frac{c}{\mu}$), which causes the wavelength to decrease proportionally while frequency remains invariant.

---

### PYQ 3

A ray of light traveling from a rarer medium to a denser medium bends:

- [x] Towards the normal
- [ ] Away from the normal
- [ ] Along the normal without deviation
- [ ] Back into the same medium

> According to Snell's law, when light slows down upon entering a denser medium, the angle of refraction becomes smaller than the angle of incidence ($r < i$), bending the ray towards the normal.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

If a light wave of frequency $6 \times 10^{14}\text{ Hz}$ enters a glass slab of refractive index $1.5$, what will be its frequency inside the glass?

- [ ] $4 \times 10^{14}\text{ Hz}$
- [x] $6 \times 10^{14}\text{ Hz}$
- [ ] $9 \times 10^{14}\text{ Hz}$
- [ ] Zero

> Because the frequency of an electromagnetic wave is invariant upon entering a new optical medium, it remains unchanged at $6 \times 10^{14}\text{ Hz}$.

---

### Q2

The energy of a photon of light is directly dependent upon which of the following properties?

- [ ] Amplitude
- [ ] Wavelength in the medium
- [x] Frequency
- [ ] Velocity in the medium

> According to Planck's quantum equation $E = h\nu$, the energy of a photon depends directly and solely on its frequency ($\nu$).

---

### Q3

When light is reflected from a smooth polished plane mirror back into the same medium, which of the following changes?

- [ ] Speed
- [ ] Frequency
- [ ] Wavelength
- [x] Direction of propagation

> In reflection, because the wave remains in the same medium, its speed, wavelength, and frequency remain identical; only the direction of propagation changes.

---

### Q4

What is the speed of light in a medium whose absolute refractive index is $1.5$? (Speed of light in vacuum $= 3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$)

- [ ] $1.5 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$
- [x] $2.0 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$
- [ ] $2.25 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$
- [ ] $3.0 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$

> Using the formula $v = \frac{c}{\mu}$:
$$v = \frac{3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}}{1.5} = 2.0 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$$
