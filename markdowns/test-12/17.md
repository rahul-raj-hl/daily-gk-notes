# 📌 Faxian (Fa-Hien): Journey, Observations & The Gupta Golden Age

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Royal Patron / Reign**: The famous Chinese Buddhist monk **Faxian (Fa-Hien)** visited India during the reign of **Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya)** (~$375\text{--}415\text{ CE}$).
- **Primary Objective**: Traveled on foot from China to India via the Silk Road between **$399\text{ and }412\text{ CE}$** to secure original authentic manuscripts of the **Vinaya Pitaka** (Buddhist monastic rules).
- **Famous Travelogue**: Authored ***Fo-Kwo-Ki*** (*A Record of Buddhist Kingdoms*), documenting the socioeconomic stability, religious tolerance, and public welfare under Gupta rule.
- **Unique Historiographical Note**: Interestingly, Faxian **never mentions the name of Emperor Chandragupta II directly** anywhere in his travelogue, focusing purely on Buddhism, moral conduct, and public life.
- **Key Social Observations**:
  - The society was peaceful, vegetarianism was widespread, and crime was virtually non-existent without harsh bodily punishments or capital punishment (only fines and mutilation for repeated treason).
  - People used **cowrie shells (*kaudi*)** as common market currency for everyday small transactions.
  - Documented the practice of untouchability regarding the **Chandalas** (who had to strike a piece of wood when entering cities to warn others).

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Comparative Matrix: Major Chinese Buddhist Travellers to India

| Chinese Traveller | Period of Visit | Indian Monarch / Reign | Famous Account / Book | Primary Purpose & Destinations |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Faxian (Fa-Hien)** | **$399\text{--}412\text{ CE}$** | **Chandragupta II (Gupta Empire)** | ***Fo-Kwo-Ki*** (*Record of Buddhist Kingdoms*) | To collect authentic copies of Buddhist Vinaya Pitaka; visited Pataliputra, Mathura, Sarnath, Bodh Gaya, Tamralipti. |
| **Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang)** | **$629\text{--}645\text{ CE}$** | **Harshavardhana (Pushyabhuti) & Narasimhavarman I (Pallava)** | ***Si-Yu-Ki*** (*Buddhist Records of the Western World*) | Studied at Nalanda University under Acharya Shilabhadra; attended the Kannauj Assembly and Prayag Maha Moksha Parishad. |
| **Yijing (I-Tsing)** | **$671\text{--}695\text{ CE}$** | **Later Guptas / Post-Harsha Period** | ***A Record of the Buddhist Religion as Practised in India and the Malay Archipelago*** | Studied at Nalanda for 10 years; brought back hundreds of Sanskrit texts and translated Vinaya texts. |
| **Song Yun** | **$518\text{--}521\text{ CE}$** | **Huna Rule (Mihirakula / Toramana era in Gandhara)** | *Travel Accounts of Song Yun* | Sent by Empress Dowager of Northern Wei; explored Udyana and Gandhara. |

### 2. Major Gupta Emperors & Distinguishing Milestones

| Gupta Emperor | Historical Titles | Landmark Historical Milestone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chandragupta I** | *Maharajadhiraja* | Founded the **Gupta Era (319–320 CE)**; married Licchavi princess Kumaradevi (issued King-Queen type gold coins). |
| **Samudragupta** | *Kaviraja*, *Sarva-raj-ochchetta*, *Ashvamedha-parakrama* | Termed the **"Napoleon of India"** by V.A. Smith; his military conquests are inscribed on the **Prayag Prashasti (Allahabad Pillar)** by Harisena. |
| **Chandragupta II** | *Vikramaditya*, *Sakari* (Destroyer of Sakas), *Simhavikrama* | Annihilated the Western Kshatrapas (Sakas) in Gujarat; maintained the famous **Navaratnas (Nine Jewels)** including **Kalidasa** and **Varahamihira**; **Faxian visited during his reign**. |
| **Kumaragupta I** | *Mahendraditya*, *Shakraditya* | Founded the ancient **Nalanda Mahavihara (University)** in modern-day Bihar; performed Ashvamedha sacrifices. |
| **Skandagupta** | *Vikramaditya*, *Kramaditya* | Repulsed the savage **Huna invasion**; repaired the **Sudarshana Lake embankment** in Saurashtra (Junagadh Inscription). |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Traveller / Feature | Faxian (Fa-Hien) | Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang) | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Emperor's Reign** | **Chandragupta II (Gupta)** | **Harshavardhana (Vardhana)** | Swapping Faxian $\leftrightarrow$ Harsha and Xuanzang $\leftrightarrow$ Chandragupta II |
| **Century of Visit** | **Early 5th Century CE (400s)** | **Early 7th Century CE (600s)** | Mixing up the chronological order (Faxian came 200+ years earlier) |
| **Study at Nalanda** | **No** (Nalanda was not yet built) | **Yes** (Studied at Nalanda under Shilabhadra) | Stating that Faxian studied at Nalanda University |
| **Royal Name Mention** | **Never mentions the king's name** | **Extensively documents Harsha** | Assuming Faxian praised Chandragupta II by name |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "F-G and H-H: FAXIAN TO GUPTA, HIUEN-TSANG TO HARSHA"**
> - **F $\rightarrow$ G**: **F**axian visited **G**upta Empire (Chandragupta II)
> - **H $\rightarrow$ H**: **H**iuen-Tsang (Xuanzang) visited **H**arsha
