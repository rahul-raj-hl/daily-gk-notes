# 📌 Lodi Dynasty: The First Afghan Dynasty of the Delhi Sultanate

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Ethnic Origin**: The **Lodi Dynasty** was the **first Afghan (Pashtun) dynasty** to rule over the Delhi Sultanate (1451–1526 CE).
- **Dynastic Sequence**: It was the fifth and **final dynasty of the Delhi Sultanate**, succeeding the Sayyid Dynasty and ending with the establishment of the Mughal Empire.
- **Founder**: **Bahlul Lodi** founded the dynasty in **1451 CE**, replacing the last Sayyid ruler, Alam Shah.
- **Greatest Ruler**: **Sikandar Lodi** (reigned 1489–1517 CE), who founded the city of **Agra in 1504 CE**, shifted the capital there in 1506 CE, and introduced the **Gaz-i-Sikandari** measurement unit.
- **Fall of the Dynasty**: Ended at the **First Battle of Panipat (21 April 1526)**, where Babur defeated **Ibrahim Lodi**, marking the demise of the Delhi Sultanate.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Chronology of Delhi Sultanate Dynasties & Ethnic Origins

| Dynasty | Period | Ethnic / Tribal Origin | Founder |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Mamluk / Slave Dynasty** | **1206–1290 CE** | **Ilbari Turk** | Qutb-ud-din Aibak |
| **Khalji Dynasty** | **1290–1320 CE** | **Turko-Afghan (Khalaj tribe)** | Jalal-ud-din Khalji |
| **Tughlaq Dynasty** | **1320–1414 CE** | **Turko-Indian (Qaraunah Turks)** | Ghiyas-ud-din Tughlaq |
| **Sayyid Dynasty** | **1414–1451 CE** | **Claimed Arab lineage (Descendants of the Prophet)** | Khizr Khan |
| **Lodi Dynasty** | **1451–1526 CE** | **Pure Afghan / Pashtun (Lodi/Ghilzai tribe)** | **Bahlul Lodi** |

---

### 2. Major Lodi Rulers & Historical Contributions

| Ruler | Reign | Major Achievements & Historical Milestones |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Bahlul Lodi** | **1451–1489 CE** | • Founder of the First Afghan Empire in India.<br>• Treated Afghan nobles as equals (*"Primus inter pares"* / first among equals) rather than subjects; sat on carpets instead of a high throne.<br>• Annexed the **Sharqi Kingdom of Jaunpur**.<br>• Issued the copper coin known as **Bahloli**. |
| **Sikandar Lodi** *(Nizam Khan)* | **1489–1517 CE** | • Most competent administrator and poet (wrote Persian verses under the pen name **Gulrukhi**).<br>• **Founded the city of Agra in 1504 CE** and shifted the capital to Agra in 1506 CE.<br>• Introduced **Gaz-i-Sikandari** (32-digit unit for land measurement).<br>• Authored/commissioned the Ayurvedic medical translation **Lahjat-i-Sikandar Shahi** and musical treatise **Farhang-i-Sikandari**.<br>• Re-imposed Jizya and destroyed Hindu temples (e.g., Nagarkot). |
| **Ibrahim Lodi** | **1517–1526 CE** | • Last Sultan of the Delhi Sultanate.<br>• Adopted an autocratic stance against proud Afghan nobles, leading to rebellions.<br>• Defeated by Rana Sanga of Mewar at the **Battle of Khatoli (1518)**.<br>• His governor of Punjab, **Daulat Khan Lodi**, and uncle **Alam Khan** invited Babur to invade India.<br>• Killed on the battlefield at the **First Battle of Panipat (1526)**. |

- [x] Bahlul Lodi
