# 📌 Dudhsagar Falls: Mandovi River & Major Waterfalls of India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct River**: **Mandovi River** (also known as the *Mahadayi* / *Mhadei* River).
- **Geographic Location**: Located on the border of **Goa and Karnataka** in the Western Ghats (Sanguem taluka, South Goa).
- **Etymology**: The name **"Dudhsagar"** literally translates to **"Sea of Milk"**, describing the white, frothy appearance of the four-tiered cascade as it rushes down the vertical cliff face.
- **Physical Dimensions**: It is a four-tiered waterfall with a total height of approximately **$310\text{ metres}$ ($1,017\text{ feet}$)** and an average width of $30\text{ metres}$, making it one of the tallest waterfalls in India.
- **Protected Area**: Situated inside the **Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary and Mollem National Park**.
- **Railway Landmark**: The spectacular **Madgaon–Belagavi (South Western Railway)** rail route crosses a stone arched viaduct bridge directly in front of the waterfall.
- **Lifeline of Goa**: The Mandovi River, along with the **Zuari River**, forms the lifeline of Goa's ecology and inland navigation (connecting at the Mormugao port via the *Cumbharjua Canal*).

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Major Waterfalls of India: River & State Matrix

| Waterfall Name | River | State | Key Distinguishing Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | **Mandovi (Mahadayi)** | **Goa** | Four-tiered fall (~$310\text{ m}$) inside Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary / Mollem NP. |
| **Jog Falls (Gersoppa / Mahatma Gandhi Falls)** | **Sharavati** | **Karnataka** | Untiered plunge waterfall consisting of four cascades: *Raja, Roarer, Rocket, and Rani*. |
| **Kunchikal Falls** | **Varahi** | **Karnataka** | Highest tiered waterfall in India (~$455\text{ m}$), located in Shimoga district. |
| **Nohkalikai Falls** | Rain-fed stream | **Meghalaya** | Tallest plunge-type waterfall in India (~$340\text{ m}$) near Sohra (Cherrapunji). |
| **Shivasamudram Falls** | **Kaveri** | **Karnataka** | Segregated into *Gaganachukki* & *Bharachukki*; site of Asia's first major hydroelectric plant (1902). |
| **Dhuandhar Falls** | **Narmada** | **Madhya Pradesh** | Located at Bhedaghat (Jabalpur) carving through spectacular white **Marble Rocks**. |
| **Hundru Falls** | **Subarnarekha** | **Jharkhand** | Major scenic fall near Ranchi (~$98\text{ m}$). |
| **Chitrakote Falls** | **Indravati** | **Chhattisgarh** | Known as the **"Niagara of India"** due to its expansive horseshoe-shaped width in Bastar. |
| **Vajrai Falls** | **Urmodi** | **Maharashtra** | Three-tier waterfall located near Satara (near Kaas Plateau). |
| **Kapildhara / Doodh Dhara** | **Narmada** | **Madhya Pradesh** | Sacred waterfalls situated near Amarkantak origin point. |

### 2. River Systems in Options: Hydro-Geographical Details

| River Name | Origin / Source | Flow Direction & Outflow | Key Features / Projects |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mandovi River** | Bhimgad (Western Ghats, Belagavi, Karnataka) | **West-Flowing** $\rightarrow$ Arabian Sea | Known as the **"Lifeline of Goa"**; subject of the *Mahadayi Water Disputes Tribunal* (Goa, Karnataka, Maharashtra). |
| **Sharavati River** | Ambuthirtha (Tirthahalli, Shimoga, Karnataka) | **West-Flowing** $\rightarrow$ Arabian Sea | Hosts the famous **Jog Falls** and the **Linganamakki Dam** hydroelectric project. |
| **Bhadra River** | Gangamoola (Varaha Parvatha, Western Ghats, Karnataka) | **East-Flowing** $\rightarrow$ Joins Tunga to form **Tungabhadra** (Krishna basin) | Hosts the **Bhadra Wildlife Sanctuary** and **Bhadra Reservoir (Lakkavalli)**. |
| **Ulhas River** | Rajmachi hills (Sahyadri range, Maharashtra) | **West-Flowing** $\rightarrow$ Arabian Sea (Vasai Creek / Thane Creek) | Vital freshwater source for the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR). |
