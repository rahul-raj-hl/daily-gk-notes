# 📌 MGNREGS Transition to VB G RAM G: Workdays Expansion & Scheme Evolution

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Scheme Identity & Renaming**: The Union Cabinet approved the renaming of the **Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS)** to **Viksit Bharat - Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission - Gramin (VB G RAM G)**.
- **Expansion of Guaranteed Workdays**: The statutory wage employment guarantee has been enhanced from **100 days to 125 days** per rural household per financial year.
- **Nodal Ministry**: The scheme continues to be centrally administered and monitored by the **Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD)**, Government of India.
- **Evolutionary Trajectory**:
  - **2005**: Enacted via the *National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (NREGA)*.
  - **2009**: Renamed as *MGNREGA* (on 2 October 2009 to honor Mahatma Gandhi).
  - **Recent Reform**: Rebranded under the Viksit Bharat umbrella as *VB G RAM G* alongside an expanded 125-day livelihood safety net.
- **Core Statutory Mandates**:
  - Legal right to work for adult members of rural households willing to do unskilled manual labour.
  - Mandatory provision of employment within **15 days of application**, failing which the state must pay an **unemployment allowance**.
  - Minimum of **one-third (33%)** of total participating beneficiaries must be women.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Evolution Timeline: Rural Employment Guarantee in India

| Milestone Stage | Official Scheme Title | Key Features / Statutory Additions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enactment (2005)** | **NREGA (2005)** | Passed by Parliament on 23 August 2005; launched from Anantapur (Andhra Pradesh) on 2 February 2006. |
| **First Rebranding (2009)**| **MGNREGA** | Renamed on **2 October 2009** by prefixing "Mahatma Gandhi"; institutionalized 100 days of guaranteed wage work. |
| **Current Restructuring** | **VB G RAM G** | **Viksit Bharat - Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission - Gramin**; increased guaranteed days to **125 days/year**. |

---

### 2. Operational Framework & Statutory Provisions

| Implementation Parameter | Statutory Mandate / Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Guaranteed Workdays** | **125 days per household annually** (enhanced from the previous 100 days). |
| **Distance Benchmark** | Work must be provided within a **$5\text{ km}$ radius** of the village; if beyond $5\text{ km}$, an additional $10\%$ wage is paid for transport/living costs. |
| **Unemployment Allowance** | Payable by State Governments if employment is not provided within **15 days** of applying. |
| **Wage-to-Material Ratio** | Mandated ratio of **$60:40$** (60% unskilled wages to 40% material & skilled wages) maintained at the Gram Panchayat level. |
| **Social Audit Mechanism** | Gram Sabhas are legally mandated to conduct compulsory **Social Audits** of all projects. |
| **Wage Indexation** | Wage rates are notified by the Central Government annually under Section 6(1) and indexed to the **Consumer Price Index for Agricultural Labourers (CPI-AL)**. |
