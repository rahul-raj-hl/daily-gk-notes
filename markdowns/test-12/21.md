# 📌 Fisheries: Culture Fishing vs. Capture Fishing & Blue Revolution

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Term**: The rearing and breeding of fish in artificially controlled and managed freshwater bodies (such as ponds, tanks, and raceways) is known as **Culture Fishing (or Pisciculture / Aquaculture)**.
- **Core Distinction**:
  - **Culture Fishing**: Involves human intervention through controlled breeding, artificial feeding, water quality maintenance, disease management, and harvesting.
  - **Capture Fishing**: Involves catching naturally living wild aquatic organisms from open natural water bodies (oceans, seas, rivers, natural lakes) without prior rearing.
- **Water Salinity Types**:
  - **Freshwater Aquaculture**: Carried out in ponds, lakes, and rivers (e.g., Indian Major Carps: *Catla, Rohu, Mrigal*).
  - **Brackishwater Aquaculture**: Carried out in coastal estuaries and lagoons (e.g., Tiger shrimp, Mud crab).
  - **Mariculture**: Cultivation of marine organisms in deep sea pens or coastal ocean enclosures (e.g., Seaweed, Oysters, Pompano).
- **Flagship Government Scheme**: **Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)** launched in May 2020 to accelerate the **Blue Revolution** in India.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Classification Matrix: Major Types of Fishing Practices

| Classification Base | Fishing Sub-Type | Operational Definition | Typical Aquatic Species Involved |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **By Management & Production Method** | **Culture Fishing (*Pisciculture*)** | Controlled cultivation, selective stocking, feeding, and harvesting of aquatic organisms in artificial/regulated environments. | Catla, Rohu, Mrigal, Grass Carp, Tilapia, Pangasius |
| | **Capture Fishing** | Harvesting wild fish populations from unrestricted natural aquatic ecosystems without prior stocking or feeding. | Sardines, Mackerel, Hilsa, Bombay Duck, Tuna |
| **By Water Environment** | **Freshwater Fisheries** | Undertaken in waters with salinity $< 0.5\text{ ppt}$ (canals, ponds, reservoirs, rivers). | Rohu, Catla, Magur, Singhi, Common Carp |
| | **Brackish Water Fisheries** | Undertaken in estuarine mixing zones with salinity $0.5 - 30\text{ ppt}$ (mangroves, coastal lagoons). | Tiger Shrimp (*Penaeus monodon*), Milkfish, Mullet |
| | **Marine Fisheries (*Mariculture*)** | Undertaken in oceanic/sea waters with salinity $> 30\text{ ppt}$ along the coastline and Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ). | Pomfret, Mackerel, Sardine, Tuna, Cuttlefish |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Terminology Pair | Core Difference | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Culture Fishing vs. Capture Fishing** | • **Culture**: Artificial rearing and breeding in managed ponds.<br>• **Capture**: Extracting naturally occurring wild fish from open waters. | Assuming capture fishing involves pond management |
| **Aquaculture vs. Pisciculture** | • **Aquaculture**: Culturing **all** aquatic flora and fauna (fish, prawns, crabs, seaweed, oysters).<br>• **Pisciculture**: Specifically the culturing of **fin-fish** only. | Using pisciculture when referring to prawns or seaweed |
| **Mariculture vs. Aquaculture** | • **Mariculture**: Sub-branch of aquaculture in **marine / sea water** environments.<br>• **Aquaculture**: Umbrella term encompassing freshwater, brackish, and marine waters. | Assuming mariculture includes freshwater pond farming |

> **Mnemonic: "CULTURE CARES AND BREEDS, CAPTURE CHASES AND CATCHES"**
> - **Culture** $\rightarrow$ Controlled breeding, feeding, and pond care (Pisciculture)
> - **Capture** $\rightarrow$ Direct harvesting from natural wild waters without care
> - **Blue Revolution** $\rightarrow$ Water / Fish production boost
