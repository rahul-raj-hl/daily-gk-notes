# 📌 Sangam Age: Cheran Senguttuvan, the Chera Dynasty & the Pattini Cult

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Identity of Senguttuvan**: **Cheran Senguttuvan** (often celebrated as the **"Red Chera"** or "Good Chera") was the most illustrious and powerful king of the **Chera Dynasty** during the Sangam period (2nd Century CE).
- **Institution of Pattini Cult**: He is historically credited with founding the **Pattini Cult** (worship of **Kannaki** as the ideal chaste goddess of fidelity and justice).
- **Himalayan Expedition Legend**: According to literary traditions in *Silappadikaram* and *Padirruppattu*, he led a military expedition to the **Himalayas** to fetch a sacred stone for sculpting the idol of Kannaki, bathing the stone in the holy River Ganga.
- **Literary Connection (*Silappadikaram*)**: The author of the celebrated Tamil epic *Silappadikaram*, the Jain monk **Ilango Adigal**, was the younger brother of Cheran Senguttuvan.
- **The Muvendars (Three Crowned Kings)**: The Cheras ruled predominantly over modern **Kerala and parts of western Tamil Nadu (Kongu region)**, alongside their contemporary rival dynasties, the **Cholas** and **Pandyas**.

### 1. Comparative Matrix: The Sangam Muvendar (Three Crowned Dynasties)

| Dynasty | Core Region / Geography | Capital City | Chief Seaport(s) | Royal Emblem | Most Celebrated Ruler |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Cheras** | Kerala coast & Western Tamil Nadu (Kongu) | **Vanji / Karur** | **Muziris** & **Tondi** | **Bow and Arrow** | **Cheran Senguttuvan** (*Red Chera*) |

| **Pandyas** | Southern Tamil Nadu (Vaigai / Tamraparni basins) | **Madurai** | **Korkai** (*famed for pearl fishery*) | **Carp / Twin Fish** | **Nedunjeliyan** (*Hero of Talaiyalanganam*) |

---

### 2. Landmark Contributions of Cheran Senguttuvan

| Feature / Accomplishment | Historical Detail & Significance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **The Pattini Cult** | Institutionalized the formal worship of Kannaki (the central tragic heroine of *Silappadikaram*) as the embodiment of chastity (*Pattini*). |
| **Consecration Ceremony** | Rulers from neighboring regions, including **King Gajabahu I of Sri Lanka**, attended the grand consecration of the Kannaki temple. |
| **Naval Supremacy** | Regarded as a formidable naval power who suppressed coastal piracy in the Arabian Sea; celebrated in texts as *Kadal Pirakottiya Senguttuvan* ("the one who drove back the sea/pirates"). |
| **Literary Epics** | His heroic deeds form the subject of the 5th decade of **Padirruppattu** (composed by poet Paranar) and the *Vanjikandam* section of **Silappadikaram**. |
