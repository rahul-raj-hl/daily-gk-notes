# 📌 Cabinet Mission Plan 1946: Proposals, Members & Grouping System

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Arrival & Purpose**: Sent to India in **March 1946** by British Prime Minister Clement Attlee to formulate a mechanism for the transfer of power and devise a framework for framing India's future Constitution.
- **Three Cabinet Ministers (Members)**:
  1. **Lord Pethick-Lawrence** (Chairman / Secretary of State for India)
  2. **Sir Stafford Cripps** (President of the Board of Trade)
  3. **A. V. Alexander** (First Lord of the Admiralty)
- **Rejection of Complete Partition**: The Plan explicitly **rejected the demand for a sovereign, separate Pakistan**, recommending instead a united Indian Federation with a weak Centre and substantial provincial autonomy.
- **Three-Tier Provincial Grouping**:
  - **Group A (Hindu-majority)**: Madras, Bombay, Central Provinces, United Provinces, Bihar, and Orissa.
  - **Group B (Western Muslim-majority)**: Punjab, North-West Frontier Province (NWFP), and Sindh.
  - **Group C (Eastern Muslim-majority)**: Bengal and Assam.
- **Constituent Assembly Formation**: Set up a **389-member Constituent Assembly** (296 from British India + 93 from Princely States) elected through indirect proportional representation by the provincial legislative assemblies.
- **Subsequent Developments**: Due to conflicting interpretations of the compulsory grouping clause between the Congress and the Muslim League, the plan broke down, leading to the **Mountbatten Plan (3 June 1947)** and the **Indian Independence Act of 1947**.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Three Cabinet Members & Their Portfolios

| Cabinet Mission Member | British Cabinet Portfolio | Role in the Mission |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lord Pethick-Lawrence** | **Secretary of State for India** | **Chairman / Leader** of the Mission |
| **Sir Stafford Cripps** | **President of the Board of Trade** | Chief constitutional drafter (previously led Cripps Mission 1942) |
| **A. V. Alexander** | **First Lord of the Admiralty** | Senior British Cabinet member representing naval and defence affairs |

---

### 2. The Three-Section Provincial Grouping Scheme

| Group Section | Demographic Profile | Provinces Included | Powers & Procedure |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Group A** | **Hindu-majority Provinces** | Madras, Bombay, UP, CP, Bihar, Orissa | Met separately to draft their own provincial constitutions and common group constitution. |
| **Group B** | **Muslim-majority (North-West)** | Punjab, NWFP, Sindh *(along with British Baluchistan)* | Drafted western regional constitutions; had provision to opt-out after the first general election. |
| **Group C** | **Muslim-majority (North-East)** | Bengal and Assam | Met jointly to draft provincial constitutions for the eastern sector. |

---

### 3. Core Proposals of the Cabinet Mission Plan

| Constitutional Aspect | Recommendation / Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Union Powers (Centre)** | A loose Union of India with authority limited to **Foreign Affairs, Defence, and Communications**, along with the power to raise necessary finances. |
| **Residuary Powers** | All remaining subjects and residuary powers were vested in the **Provinces and Princely States**. |
| **Constituent Assembly Composition** | Total **389 Seats**: 292 from Provincial Assemblies, 4 from Chief Commissioners' Provinces (Delhi, Ajmer-Merwara, Coorg, British Baluchistan), and 93 from Princely States. |
| **Interim Government** | Proposed setting up an **Interim Government** with 14 Indian members holding all portfolios (sworn in on **2 September 1946** headed by Jawaharlal Nehru). |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Constitutional Mission / Plan | Year | Primary Focus / Stance on Partition | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Cripps Mission** | **1942** | Offered **Dominion Status** after WWII; right of provinces to secede | Confusing Cripps Mission (1942) with Cabinet Mission (1946) |
| **Cabinet Mission Plan** | **1946** | **Rejected Pakistan**; created 389-member Constituent Assembly | Assuming Cabinet Mission accepted the creation of Pakistan |
| **Mountbatten Plan (June 3 Plan)**| **1947** | **Accepted Partition** and creation of two independent Dominions | Confusing the plan that formed the Assembly (Cabinet Mission) with the plan that partitioned India |
| **Assam's Grouping** | **Group C** | Grouped with Bengal despite having a Congress government | Assuming Assam was placed in Group A with other Hindu-majority areas |
