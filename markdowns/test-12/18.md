# 📌 Agro-Climatic Zones of Rajasthan: ICAR Classification & Agricultural Geography

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Total Agro-Climatic Zones in Rajasthan**: Divided into **10 distinct agro-climatic zones** by the **Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)** under the National Agricultural Research Project (NARP).
- **Classification Criteria**: Demarcated based on **rainfall patterns, temperature, soil characteristics, topography, cropping systems, and irrigation water availability**.
- **National Context**:
  - **Planning Commission of India**: Delineated **15 broad Agro-Climatic Zones** across India (Rajasthan falls under Zone 6: *Trans-Gangetic Plains Region* and Zone 14: *Western Dry Region*).
  - **ICAR (NARP framework)**: Delineated India into **127 agro-climatic sub-zones**, of which Rajasthan comprises **10 sub-zones**.
- **Extreme Zones by Area**:
  - **Largest Zone by Geographic Area**: **Zone I-C** (*Hyper-Arid Partially Irrigated Zone* – Bikaner, Jaisalmer, Churu).
  - **Smallest Zone by Geographic Area**: **Zone IV-B** (*Humid Southern Plain* – Banswara, Dungarpur, Pratapgarh).

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The 10 Agro-Climatic Zones of Rajasthan (ICAR / NARP Model)

| Zone Code | Official Agro-Climatic Zone Name | Districts Covered (Representative Area) | Major Soil Type | Dominant Crops Cultivated |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Zone I-A** | **Arid Western Plain** | Barmer, Jodhpur | Desert sandy soils | Bajra, Moth bean, Moong, Guar, Cumin, Isabgol |
| **Zone I-B** | **Irrigated North-Western Plain** | Sri Ganganagar, Hanumangarh | Alluvial & desert soils (Canal irrigated) | Wheat, Cotton, Mustard, Gram, Kinnow (Citrus) |
| **Zone I-C** | **Hyper-Arid Partially Irrigated Zone** *(Largest)* | Bikaner, Jaisalmer, Churu | Desert sandy dune soils | Bajra, Moth bean, Groundnut, Mustard, Wheat |
| **Zone II-A** | **Transitional Plain of Inland Drainage** | Sikar, Jhunjhunu, Nagaur | Sandy loam to desert soils | Bajra, Pulses, Mustard, Barley, Fenugreek |
| **Zone II-B** | **Transitional Plain of Luni Basin** | Jalore, Pali, Sirohi | Grey brown alluvial & sandy loam | Bajra, Castor, Mustard, Cumin, Isabgol |
| **Zone III-A** | **Semi-Arid Eastern Plain** | Jaipur, Ajmer, Dausa, Tonk | Sandy loam to loam soils | Bajra, Sorghum (Jowar), Mustard, Wheat, Gram |
| **Zone III-B** | **Flood-Prone Eastern Plain** | Alwar, Bharatpur, Dholpur, Karauli, Sawai Madhopur | Rich alluvial soils | Mustard (*highest producer*), Wheat, Bajra, Arhar |
| **Zone IV-A** | **Sub-Humid Southern Plain & Aravalli Hills** | Bhilwara, Rajsamand, Chittorgarh, Udaipur | Red & black loamy soils | Maize, Jowar, Wheat, Gram, Groundnut |
| **Zone IV-B** | **Humid Southern Plain** *(Smallest)* | Banswara, Dungarpur, Pratapgarh | Medium black & red soils | Maize, Paddy (Rice), Soybean, Wheat, Gram |
| **Zone V** | **Humid South-Eastern Plain (*Hadoti*)** | Kota, Baran, Bundi, Jhalawar | Deep black cotton soils (*Vertisols*) | Soybean, Coriander, Mustard, Garlic, Wheat, Citrus |

---

### 2. National Framework: Planning Commission vs. ICAR / NARP

| Institutional Authority | Number of Zones | Foundation / Focus Criteria |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Planning Commission (1989)** | **15 Zones** | Macro-level regional resource planning based on broad physiography, climate, and water resources. |
| **ICAR / NARP Framework** | **127 Zones** | Micro-level agricultural research and extension planning customized to local agro-ecological niches. |
| **National Bureau of Soil Survey (NBSS&LUP)** | **20 Agro-Ecological Regions (AERs)** | Based on landform, soil type, bioclimatic conditions, and Length of Growing Period (LGP). |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Classification Body / Metric | Total Number of Zones | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Agro-Climatic Zones in Rajasthan (ICAR)** | **10** | Marking 8, 12, or 14 for Rajasthan |
| **Agro-Climatic Zones in India (Planning Commission)** | **15** | Confusing Rajasthan's count (10) with India's macro zones (15) |
| **Agro-Climatic Zones in India (ICAR / NARP)** | **127** | Confusing Planning Commission (15) with ICAR (127) |
| **Agro-Ecological Regions in India (NBSS&LUP)** | **20 (with 60 sub-regions)** | Swapping agro-climatic zones with agro-ecological zones |
