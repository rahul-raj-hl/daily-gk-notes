# 📌 Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar (RVP): Structure, Categories & Governance

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Evaluation of Assertion & Reason**:
  - **Assertion (A) is TRUE**: The **Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar (RVP)** is the highest unified national award established by the Government of India to recognize outstanding contributions in Science, Technology, and Innovation.
  - **Reason (R) is FALSE**: The award is **NOT** administered by the Ministry of Culture. It is coordinated by the **Ministry of Science and Technology** along with the **Office of the Principal Scientific Adviser (PSA)** to the Government of India.
  - **Correct Option**: **A is true, but R is false.**
- **Consolidation of Awards**: Instituted to rationalize and replace multiple fragmented science awards (including the Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize structure) under a unified national framework.
- **Announcement & Ceremony Dates**:
  - **Announced On**: **National Technology Day (11 May)**.
  - **Conferred On**: **National Space Day (23 August)** (commemorating the soft landing of Chandrayaan-3 on the Moon's South Pole).
- **Four Distinct Categories**: **Vigyan Ratna**, **Vigyan Shri**, **Vigyan Yuva - Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar**, and **Vigyan Team**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Four Award Categories of Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar

| Category Name | Maximum Awards / Year | Eligibility & Criterion | Age Limit |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: |
| **1. Vigyan Ratna (VR)** | **Maximum 3** | Recognizes **lifetime achievements and contributions** made in any field of science and technology. | **No age bar** |
| **2. Vigyan Shri (VS)** | **Maximum 25** | Recognizes **distinguished and pioneering contributions** to a specific field of science and technology. | **No age bar** |
| **3. Vigyan Yuva: Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar (VY-SSB)** | **Maximum 25** | Recognizes **exceptional young scientists** who have shown extraordinary potential and impact in S&T. | **Up to 45 Years** |
| **4. Vigyan Team (VT)** | **Maximum 3 teams** | Recognizes a **collaborative team of three or more** scientists/researchers for breakthrough achievements. | **No age bar** |

---

### 2. Governance, Selection & Domain Coverage

| Operational Parameter | Provision / Procedure |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Administering Authority** | Ministry of Science and Technology & Office of the Principal Scientific Adviser (PSA). |
| **Apex Selection Committee** | **Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar Committee (RVPC)**, chaired by the **Principal Scientific Adviser (PSA)**. |
| **Number of S&T Domains** | Open across **13 Domains**: Physics, Chemistry, Biological Sciences, Mathematics & Computer Science, Earth Science, Medicine, Engineering, Agricultural Science, Environmental Science, Technology & Innovation, Atomic Energy, Space Science & Exploration, and Others. |
| **Inaugural Vigyan Ratna Awardee** | Eminent biochemist and former IISc Director **Prof. Govindarajan Padmanabhan** (awarded in the inaugural 2024 edition). |
| **Award Package** | A **Sanad (Certificate)** signed by the President of India and a **Medallion** (no cash component). |
