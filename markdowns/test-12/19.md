# 📌 Constituent Assembly Committees: Steering Committee & Leadership Matrix

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Steering Committee Chairman**: **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** served as the Chairman of the **Steering Committee** in the Constituent Assembly of India.
- **Role of the Steering Committee**: Responsible for planning, coordinating, managing the day-to-day agenda, and regulating the legislative and constitution-making proceedings of the Assembly.
- **Other Committees Chaired by Dr. Rajendra Prasad**:
  - **Rules of Procedure Committee**
  - **Finance and Staff Committee**
  - **Ad-hoc Committee on the National Flag**
- **Dual Role of Dr. Rajendra Prasad**: Elected as the **Permanent President of the Constituent Assembly** on **11 December 1946** (succeeding the temporary president, Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha) and subsequently elected as the **first President of independent India (1950–1962)**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Major Committees of the Constituent Assembly & Their Chairpersons

| Major Committee | Chairperson | Key Constitutional Mandate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Steering Committee** | **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** | Managed business, schedule, and coordinated between different sub-committees. |
| **Rules of Procedure Committee** | **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** | Framed internal procedural and voting rules for the Assembly. |
| **Drafting Committee** *(7 Members)* | **Dr. B. R. Ambedkar** | Scrutinized and drafted the formal text of the Constitution of India. |
| **Union Powers Committee** | **Jawaharlal Nehru** | Defined the jurisdiction and powers of the Central / Union Government. |
| **Union Constitution Committee** | **Jawaharlal Nehru** | Framed structural provisions relating to the Union Executive and Parliament. |
| **States Committee** *(Committee for negotiating with States)* | **Jawaharlal Nehru** | Negotiated representation and terms of accession with Princely States. |
| **Provincial Constitution Committee** | **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel** | Framed model constitutional provisions for provincial / state governance. |
| **Advisory Committee on Fundamental Rights, Minorities and Tribal & Excluded Areas** | **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel** | Apex advisory body overseeing fundamental liberties and minority safeguards. |

---

### 2. Key Sub-Committees & Notable Minor Committees

| Sub-Committee / Minor Committee | Chairperson | Functional Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fundamental Rights Sub-Committee** | **J. B. Kripalani** | Formulated the core charter of justiciable Fundamental Rights (Part III). |
| **Minorities Sub-Committee** | **H. C. Mookerjee** | Devised safeguards and political protections for religious/linguistic minorities. |
| **Order of Business Committee** | **Dr. K. M. Munshi** | Determined the day-to-day legislative agenda and priority of motions. |
| **House Committee** | **B. Pattabhi Sitaramayya** | Managed accommodation and residential amenities for members. |
| **Ad-hoc Committee on the National Flag** | **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** | Finalized the design of the Tricolour (adopted on **22 July 1947**). |
| **Committee on Functions of the Constituent Assembly** | **G. V. Mavalankar** | Chaired sessions when the Assembly functioned purely as the Dominion Legislature. |
| **Special Committee to Examine Draft Constitution** | **Sir Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar** | Legal review of clauses prepared by the Drafting Committee. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Committee / Body | Chairperson | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Steering Committee** | **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** | Mistaking Ambedkar or Nehru for the head of the Steering Committee |
| **Drafting Committee** | **Dr. B. R. Ambedkar** | Confusing the Drafting Committee with the Steering Committee |
| **Order of Business Committee** | **Dr. K. M. Munshi** | Confusing Order of Business (Munshi) with Steering Committee (Prasad) |
| **Advisory Committee on FR & Minorities** | **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel** | Confusing the main committee (Patel) with the Fundamental Rights Sub-Committee (Kripalani) |
| **States Committee** | **Jawaharlal Nehru** | Assuming Sardar Patel headed the States Committee due to integration of states |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic Rules for Assembly Chairpersons:**
> - **"UNION & STATES" $\rightarrow$ NEHRU** (*Union Powers, Union Constitution, States Committee*)
> - **"PROVINCE & RIGHTS" $\rightarrow$ PATEL** (*Provincial Constitution, Advisory on Fundamental Rights & Minorities*)
> - **"PROCEDURE & STEERING" $\rightarrow$ RAJENDRA PRASAD** (*Rules of Procedure, Steering, Flag*)
> - **"DRAFTING" $\rightarrow$ AMBEDKAR** (*7-Member Drafting Committee*)

### Q2

The Ad-hoc Committee on the National Flag, which approved the adoption of the Indian Tricolour, was headed by:

- [ ] Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
- [x] Dr. Rajendra Prasad
- [ ] Sarojini Naidu
- [ ] C. Rajagopalachari

> The Ad-hoc Committee on the National Flag was chaired by Dr. Rajendra Prasad; the flag was formally adopted by the Constituent Assembly on 22 July 1947.

---

### Q3

The 'States Committee' appointed for negotiating terms and accession with the Princely States was presided over by:

- [ ] Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
- [x] Jawaharlal Nehru
- [ ] V. P. Menon
- [ ] Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

> The States Committee (Committee for Negotiating with States) was chaired by Jawaharlal Nehru, whereas the overall integration of states at the ministry level was driven by Sardar Patel and V. P. Menon.

---

### Q4

When the Constituent Assembly met as the Dominion Legislature (Parliament of India), the session was presided over by:

- [ ] Dr. Rajendra Prasad
- [x] G. V. Mavalankar
- [ ] S. Radhakrishnan
- [ ] M. A. Ayyangar

> The Constituent Assembly performed dual functions: when it met as a Constitution-making body, it was chaired by Dr. Rajendra Prasad; when it met as the provisional legislative body, it was chaired by G. V. Mavalankar.
