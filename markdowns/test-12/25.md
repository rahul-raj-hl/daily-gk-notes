# 📌 Nile River Basin: Drainage Network, 11 Riparian States & Hydrology

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Country Not in the Nile Basin**: The Nile River does **not** flow through **Chad**.
- **Hydrological System of Chad**: Chad belongs to the completely separate, landlocked, endorheic **Lake Chad Drainage Basin** (primarily fed by the **Chari and Logone Rivers**).
- **Longest River in the World**: The Nile spans approximately **$6,650\text{ km}$ ($4,132\text{ miles}$)**, flowing northward across eastern and northeastern Africa into the **Mediterranean Sea**.
- **The 11 Riparian Countries of the Nile Basin**:
  1. **Egypt**
  2. **Sudan**
  3. **South Sudan**
  4. **Uganda**
  5. **Ethiopia**
  6. **Kenya**
  7. **Tanzania**
  8. **Rwanda**
  9. **Burundi**
  10. **Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC)**
  11. **Eritrea**
- **Two Main Tributary Branches**:
  - **White Nile**: Originates in the equatorial Great Lakes region (**Lake Victoria / Lake Luvironza in Burundi**).
  - **Blue Nile**: Originates in the Ethiopian Highlands (**Lake Tana**), supplying $\approx 80-85\%$ of the Nile’s water and sediment during the flood season.
  - **Confluence Point**: The White Nile and Blue Nile merge at **Khartoum**, the capital city of **Sudan**.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Comparative Matrix: White Nile vs. Blue Nile vs. Atbara

| River Branch | Source / Origin | Riparian Journey | Contribution to Main Nile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **White Nile** | **Lake Victoria** (headwaters trace to the Kagera/Luvironza River in Burundi/Rwanda) | Flows through Uganda, South Sudan (passes through the vast **Sudd Swamp**), and into Sudan. | Provides a **steady, continuous base flow** year-round ($\approx 15-20\%$ total annual volume). |
| **Blue Nile (*Abay*)** | **Lake Tana** (Ethiopian Highlands) | Rushes through the Ethiopian plateau gorges into Sudan to join the White Nile at **Khartoum**. | Supplies **$\approx 80-85\%$ of the total water volume and fertile silt/alluvium** during the summer monsoon flood season. |
| **Atbara River (*Black Nile*)** | North of Lake Tana (Ethiopia) | Meets the main Nile at Atbara city in Sudan; last major tributary before the Mediterranean Sea. | Highly seasonal; dries down to pools in dry months and rushes fiercely during monsoons. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Geographical Entity | Description / Basin | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chad vs. Sudan / South Sudan** | • **Sudan/South Sudan**: Integral parts of the **Nile Basin**<br>• **Chad**: Part of the landlocked **Lake Chad Basin** | Selecting Sudan or South Sudan as non-Nile nations |
| **Lake Victoria vs. Lake Tana** | • **Lake Victoria**: Source of the **White Nile**<br>• **Lake Tana**: Source of the **Blue Nile** | Swapping the source lakes between Blue and White Nile |
| **Longest vs. Largest River** | • **Longest**: **Nile** (Length)<br>• **Largest**: **Amazon** (Discharge volume & Basin area) | Marking Nile as the largest river by water volume |
| **Lake Nasser vs. Lake Victoria** | • **Lake Nasser**: Artificial reservoir on Nile created by Aswan Dam (Egypt/Sudan)<br>• **Lake Victoria**: Natural Great Lake source of White Nile | Confusing the man-made reservoir with the source lake |

>   - **W**hite Nile $\rightarrow$ Lake **V**ictoria

> - **Khartoum**: Where White and Blue marry into the main Nile stream!

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

'Nile river' does not flow through which of the following countries?

- [ ] Sudan
- [x] Chad
- [ ] Uganda
- [ ] Egypt

> The Nile basin spans 11 countries (Egypt, Sudan, South Sudan, Uganda, Ethiopia, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, DRC, Eritrea). Chad lies to the west and drains internally into the endorheic Lake Chad basin.

---

### PYQ 2

The Blue Nile and the White Nile meet at which of the following cities?

- [ ] Cairo
- [ ] Alexandria
- [x] Khartoum
- [ ] Juba

> The White Nile flowing from Lake Victoria and the Blue Nile descending from the Ethiopian highlands converge at Khartoum, the capital of Sudan.

---

### PYQ 3

Which of the following lakes is the source of the Blue Nile River?

- [ ] Lake Victoria
- [ ] Lake Tanganyika
- [x] Lake Tana
- [ ] Lake Albert

> Lake Tana, situated in the northwestern Ethiopian Highlands, is the official source of the Blue Nile (*Abay River*).

- [ ] Nile River

- [x] Chari River

> The Chari River (along with its tributary, the Logone) provides over $90\%$ of the water flowing into Lake Chad.
