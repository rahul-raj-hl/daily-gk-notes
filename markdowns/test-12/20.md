# 📌 Panchayati Raj Committees: Ashok Mehta Committee & The Two-Tier Model

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Two-Tier System Recommendation**: The **Ashok Mehta Committee (1977)** recommended replacing the traditional three-tier system with a **two-tier system of Panchayati Raj**.
- **The Two Tiers Proposed**:
  1. **Zila Parishad** at the district level (to be the executive body and made responsible for planning).
  2. **Mandal Panchayat** below it, covering a group of villages with a population of $15,000\text{ to }20,000$.
- **Historical Context**: Appointed in **December 1977** by the **Janata Party Government** under Prime Minister Morarji Desai, submitting its report in August 1978 with 132 recommendations.
- **Key Breakthrough Proposals of Ashok Mehta Committee**:
  - Official participation of **political parties** at all levels of panchayat elections.
  - Constitutional recognition of Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs).
  - Compulsory powers of taxation to mobilize financial resources.
  - Establishment of **Nyaya Panchayats** headed by a qualified judge.
  - A Minister for Panchayati Raj in the State Council of Ministers.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Comparative Matrix: Major Committees on Panchayati Raj

| Committee Name | Year Appointed | Government in Power | Key Structural Recommendation & Focus |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee** | **1957** | Congress (Nehru Govt.) | Recommended **3-tier system** (*Gram Panchayat $\rightarrow$ Panchayat Samiti $\rightarrow$ Zila Parishad*); pioneer of 'Democratic Decentralisation'. |
| **Ashok Mehta Committee** | **1977** | Janata Party (Morarji Desai Govt.) | Recommended **2-tier system** (*Zila Parishad $\rightarrow$ Mandal Panchayat*); district as first point of decentralisation; political party participation. |
| **G. V. K. Rao Committee** | **1985** | Planning Commission | Recommended making **Zila Parishad the pivotal body**; introduced post of **District Development Commissioner (DDC)**; warned against "grass without roots" bureaucracy. |
| **L. M. Singhvi Committee** | **1986** | Congress (Rajiv Gandhi Govt.) | Recommended **Constitutional recognition, protection & preservation** of PRIs; Gram Sabha as base of direct democracy; Nyaya Panchayats. |
| **Thungon Committee** | **1988** | Sub-committee of Parliamentary Consultative Committee | Recommended **Constitutional status**, 3-tier system, fixed 5-year tenure, Zila Parishad as planning agency. |
| **Gadgil Committee** | **1988** | Policy and Programmes Committee | Recommended **3-tier system**, direct elections, reservation for SC/ST and women, State Finance Commission, State Election Commission (blueprint of 73rd Amendment). |

### 2. Balwant Rai Mehta (3-Tier) vs. Ashok Mehta (2-Tier)

| Parameter | Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957) | Ashok Mehta Committee (1977) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **System Structure** | **3-Tier Structure** | **2-Tier Structure** |
| **Apex Level (District)** | **Zila Parishad** (Advisory & supervisory role) | **Zila Parishad** (Executive body & primary planning authority) |
| **Intermediate Level** | **Panchayat Samiti** at Block level (Executive body) | *Abolished / Merged* |
| **Grassroots Level** | **Gram Panchayat** at Village level | **Mandal Panchayat** (Group of villages, 15,000–20,000 pop.) |
| **Political Parties** | Non-party basis (no official party symbols) | Direct official participation of political parties |

| Committee / Feature | Key Distinction | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ashok Mehta vs. Balwant Rai Mehta** | • Balwant Rai = **3-tier** (Village, Block, District)<br>• Ashok Mehta = **2-tier** (Mandal, District) | Selecting Balwant Rai for 2-tier system |
| **L. M. Singhvi vs. Balwant Rai Mehta** | • Balwant Rai = Recommended democratic decentralisation<br>• L. M. Singhvi = Recommended **Constitutional status** | Crediting Balwant Rai with constitutionalizing PRIs |
| **G. V. K. Rao vs. Ashok Mehta** | • Ashok Mehta = Mandal Panchayat<br>• G.V.K. Rao = District Development Commissioner (DDC) | Mixing up administrative appointments with tier models |
