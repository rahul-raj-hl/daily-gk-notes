# 📌 Galwan War Memorial: Bharat Ranbhoomi Darshan & Strategic Border Heritage

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Development Initiative**: The world's highest war memorial at Galwan was developed under the **Bharat Ranbhoomi Darshan initiative**.
- **Geographic Location**: Situated near the **KM-120 post** along the strategic **Durbuk–Shyok–Daulat Beg Oldie (DS-DBO) Road** in Eastern Ladakh.
- **Commemoration**: Built to honor the supreme sacrifice of the **20 Indian Army soldiers** (including Col. B. Santosh Babu, MVC posthumous) of the 16 Bihar Regiment and accompanying units who laid down their lives during the fierce hand-to-hand combat in the **Galwan Valley clash on 15 June 2020**.
- **Architectural Elements**: Constructed using **red and black granite** (symbolizing valor and supreme sacrifice), featuring **20 bronze statues**, an eternal flame (*Amar Jawan Jyoti*), a museum, an art gallery, and motifs of the *Trishul* and *Damru*.
- **Inauguration**: Officially inaugurated by Union Defence Minister **Rajnath Singh** to promote patriotic border tourism and document India's military resolve along the Line of Actual Control (LAC).
