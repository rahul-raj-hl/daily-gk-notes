# 📌 Five Year Plans of India & NITI Aayog

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Planning Commission**: Set up on **March 15, 1950** (extra-constitutional, advisory body) with the Prime Minister as Chairman.
- **Five Year Plans (FYPs)**: Total of **12 Five Year Plans** executed in India from 1951 to 2017.
- **Third Five Year Plan (1961–66)**: Aimed at establishing a **self-reliant and self-generating economy** (known as Gadgil Yojana).
- **Plan Holidays (1966–69)**: Annual plans introduced following the failure of the 3rd Plan due to wars (1962, 1965) and severe droughts.
- **NITI Aayog**: Established on **January 1, 2015**, replacing the Planning Commission with a bottom-up cooperative federalism approach.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Overview of Five Year Plans (1st to 12th)

*Key models, objectives, and historical events associated with each plan.*

- **1st FYP (1951–56)**: Based on **Harrod-Domar Model**; focused on **agriculture**, irrigation, and power (Bhakra Nangal dam built).
- **2nd FYP (1956–61)**: Based on **Mahalanobis Model**; focused on **rapid industrialization** and heavy industries (Bhilai, Durgapur, Rourkela steel plants).
- **3rd FYP (1961–66)**: Known as **Gadgil Yojana**; objective was **self-reliant and self-generating economy**; failed due to Sino-Indian War (1962), Indo-Pak War (1965), and severe drought (1965-66).
- **Plan Holidays (1966–69)**: Three annual plans; Green Revolution launched during this period.
- **4th FYP (1969–74)**: Focused on **growth with stability** and progressive self-reliance; 14 major banks nationalized (1969) & Pokhran-I test (1974).
- **5th FYP (1974–78)**: Objective was **Garibi Hatao** (poverty removal) and self-reliance; drafted by D.D. Dhar; terminated early by Janata Party.
- **Rolling Plan (1978–80)**: Introduced by Morarji Desai's Janata Party government.
- **6th FYP (1980–85)**: Focused on **poverty alleviation and technological self-reliance**; NABARD established (1982).
- **7th FYP (1985–90)**: Focused on **food, work, and productivity**; private sector given priority over public sector for the first time.
- **Annual Plans (1990–92)**: Due to economic crisis and political instability; **LPG Reforms (1991)** launched under Dr. Manmohan Singh & P.V. Narasimha Rao.
- **8th FYP (1992–97)**: Focused on **human resource development** (education, health, employment); highest growth rate recorded till then.
- **9th FYP (1997–2002)**: Focused on **growth with social justice and equity**; launched during 50th year of Independence.
- **10th FYP (2002–07)**: Aimed to **double per capita income** in 10 years and reduce poverty by 15%.
- **11th FYP (2007–12)**: Prepared by C. Rangarajan; objective was **"Towards Faster and More Inclusive Growth"**.
- **12th FYP (2012–17)**: Last plan; objective was **"Faster, More Inclusive and Sustainable Growth"** (Target growth rate: 8%).

---

### 2. NITI Aayog (National Institution for Transforming India)

*Features of India's modern think tank.*

- **Established Date**: **January 1, 2015** (replaced Planning Commission via Cabinet resolution).
- **Nature**: Non-constitutional, non-statutory extra-constitutional body acting as a **Policy Think Tank**.
- **Core Philosophy**: **Cooperative Federalism** (bottom-up approach, unlike top-down approach of Planning Commission).
- **Organizational Structure**:
  - **Chairperson**: Prime Minister of India.
  - **Governing Council**: Chief Ministers of all States + Lt. Governors of Union Territories.
  - **Vice-Chairperson & CEO**: Appointed by the Prime Minister.
- **Key Reports / Indices**: School Education Quality Index (SEQI), State Health Index, Composite Water Management Index, India Innovation Index.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Body / Concept | Planning Commission | NITI Aayog |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Approach** | Top-down approach (Centre to State) | **Bottom-up approach** (State participation) |
| **Power to Allocate Funds** | Had power to allocate financial funds | **No power to allocate funds** (Finance Ministry does) |
| **Role of States** | Limited participation | Full involvement through Governing Council |

---

| Term / Period | Key Difference |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Plan Holiday (1966–69)** | 3 annual plans introduced after failure of 3rd FYP. |
| **Rolling Plan (1978–80)** | Introduced by Janata Party after ending 5th FYP early. |
