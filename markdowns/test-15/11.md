# 📌 Vijayanagar Empire Architecture & Ruins

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Founding**: Founded in **1336 AD** by brothers **Harihara I** and **Bukka I** on the banks of the **Tungabhadra River**.
- **Capital**: **Hampi** (located in modern Karnataka).
- **Ruins Discovery**: The ruins of Hampi (Vijayanagar) were brought to light in 1800 by **Colonel Colin Mackenzie**.
- **Architecture Style**: The architectural style evolved here is called **Provida** (Provida style).
- **Mandapa**: A *Mandapa* is not a style name, but an open pillar hall/pavilion used for keeping deities and hosting rituals.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Statement Analysis

- **Statement 1: Ruins brought to light by Colin Mackenzie.**
  - *Correct.* Colonel Colin Mackenzie (an engineer and antiquarian of the East India Company) made the **first survey map** of Hampi in 1800.
- **Statement 2: A new style of architecture called 'Mandapa' was produced.**
  - *Incorrect.* The new style developed under Vijayanagar rulers is called **Provida style**. A *Mandapa* refers to a pillared hall or open pavilion within a temple complex.

---

### 2. Architectural Features & Dynasties

*Key artistic elements and ruling families.*

- **Provida Style Features**:
  - Intricately carved tall pillars with **sculpted horses** (Yali) as the most common symbol.
  - Addition of *Kalyana Mandapa* (marriage halls) and *Ranga Mandapa* inside temple compounds.
  - Famous Temples: **Vittala Temple** (famous stone chariot) and **Hazara Rama Temple**.
- **Four Ruling Dynasties**:
  - **Sangama Dynasty (1336–1485 AD)**: Founded by Harihara and Bukka.
  - **Saluva Dynasty (1485–1505 AD)**: Founded by Saluva Narasimha.
  - **Tuluva Dynasty (1505–1570 AD)**: Founded by Veer Narasimha; greatest ruler was **Krishnadevaraya**.
  - **Aravidu Dynasty (1570–1650 AD)**: Founded by Tirumala.
