# 📌 Major Sports Trophies and Cups

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Rogers Cup**: A major international tournament related to **Tennis** (also known as the Canadian Open).
- **Tennis Trophies**: Davis Cup, Wightman Cup, Hopman Cup, Heineken Cup, Rogers Cup, and 4 Grand Slams.
- **Badminton Trophies**: Thomas Cup (Men), Uber Cup (Women), Sudirman Cup (Mixed), and Amrit Diwan Cup.
- **Football Trophies**: Santosh Trophy, Durand Cup, Subroto Cup, Rovers Cup, and FIFA World Cup.
- **Hockey Trophies**: Sultan Azlan Shah Cup, Agha Khan Cup, Beighton Cup, Dhyan Chand Trophy, and Rangaswami Cup.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Lawn Tennis Trophies & Cups

*Key national and international Tennis competitions.*

- **Rogers Cup**: Official Canadian Open tournament for professional tennis players.
- **Davis Cup**: World's largest annual international team event in men's tennis.
- **Wightman Cup**: Famous women's tennis tournament played between US and UK teams.
- **Hopman Cup**: International mixed-team tennis tournament.
- **Heineken Cup**: Major professional men's tennis tournament.
- **Grand Slams**: Australian Open, French Open (Roland Garros), Wimbledon, and US Open.

---

### 2. Trophies in Other Major Sports

*Comprehensive breakdown of high-frequency sports trophies.*

- **Football**:
  - **Durand Cup**: Oldest football tournament in Asia (India).
  - **Santosh Trophy**: Premier Indian national state-level football championship.
  - **Subroto Cup**: Famous inter-school football tournament in India.
  - **Rovers Cup**: Historical football tournament in India.
- **Hockey**:
  - **Beighton Cup**: Oldest hockey tournament in India (Calcutta).
  - **Sultan Azlan Shah Cup**: Annual international men's hockey tournament in Malaysia.
  - **Agha Khan Cup**: Prestigious Indian hockey tournament.
  - **Rangaswami Cup**: National Hockey Championship trophy of India.
- **Badminton**:
  - **Thomas Cup**: World Men's Team Badminton Championship.
  - **Uber Cup**: World Women's Team Badminton Championship.
  - **Sudirman Cup**: World Mixed Team Badminton Championship.
  - **Amrit Diwan Cup / Yonex Cup**: Popular national/international badminton events.
- **Golf**:
  - **Ryder Cup**: Prestigious biennial men's golf match between USA and Europe.
  - **Walker Cup**: Golf tournament between amateur teams of US and Great Britain/Ireland.
  - **Canada Cup / Eisenhower Trophy**: Major international golf team competitions.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Trophy Pair | Key Difference |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Thomas Cup vs Uber Cup** | **Thomas Cup** $\rightarrow$ Men's World Badminton; **Uber Cup** $\rightarrow$ Women's World Badminton. |
| **Davis Cup vs Ryder Cup** | **Davis Cup** $\rightarrow$ Tennis; **Ryder Cup** $\rightarrow$ Golf. |
| **Durand Cup vs Beighton Cup** | **Durand Cup** $\rightarrow$ Oldest Football tournament in Asia; **Beighton Cup** $\rightarrow$ Oldest Hockey tournament in India. |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic for Badminton**: **"THOMAS and UBER go to SUDIRMAN"**
> - **THOMAS** $\rightarrow$ **Thomas Cup** (Men)
> - **UBER** $\rightarrow$ **Uber Cup** (Women)
> - **SUDIRMAN** $\rightarrow$ **Sudirman Cup** (Mixed)
