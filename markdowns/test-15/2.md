# 📌 Removal Procedure of the Vice-President of India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Initiation House**: The removal resolution can be initiated **ONLY in the Rajya Sabha** (Article 67(b)).
- **Notice Period**: A prior notice of at least **14 days** must be given before moving the resolution.
- **Rajya Sabha Voting**: Must be passed by an **Effective Majority** (majority of all the then members of the House).
- **Lok Sabha Voting**: Must be agreed to by the Lok Sabha by a **Simple Majority**.
- **Grounds for Removal**: The Constitution **does NOT specify any grounds** for the removal of the Vice-President.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Constitutional Provisions (Article 67)

*Constitutional rules governing the removal process.*

- **Article 63**: Mentions the post of the Vice-President of India (second-highest constitutional office).
- **Article 64**: States that the Vice-President is the *ex-officio* Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
- **Article 67(b)**: Details the procedure for removal:
  - Resolution must originate **only in Rajya Sabha**.
  - Requires **14 days' advance notice** in writing.
  - Requires **Effective Majority** in Rajya Sabha + **Simple Majority** in Lok Sabha.
- **No Specific Grounds**: Unlike the President (impeached for "violation of the Constitution"), no grounds are listed for removing the Vice-President.

---

### 2. Role of the Supreme Court (Article 71)

*Judicial power over election disputes.*

- **Article 71**: The Supreme Court has exclusive power to inquire into and decide all doubts/disputes regarding the election of the Vice-President.
- **Disqualification/Malpractices**: If the election is declared void by the Supreme Court, the Vice-President ceases to hold office from the date of the decision.

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic**: **"VP belongs to UPPER HOUSE"**
> - **VP** (Vice-President) is Chairman of Rajya Sabha.
> - Therefore, removal **STARTS ONLY** in **Rajya Sabha** (Upper House) with **14 Days Notice**.
