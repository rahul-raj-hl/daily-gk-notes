# 📌 DOSTI Trilateral Maritime Exercise

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Participating Nations**: **India**, **Maldives**, and **Sri Lanka**.
- **Exercise Nature**: Trilateral Coast Guard / Naval maritime exercise.
- **Historical Background**: Started in **1991** as a bilateral exercise between **India and Maldives**.
- **Expansion**: **Sri Lanka** joined the exercise for the first time in **2012**, making it a trilateral exercise.
- **17th Edition**: Commenced in **January 2026** at Male, Maldives.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Highlights & Objectives

*Core purpose and key activities of Exercise DOSTI.*

- **Strategic Objective**: Aims to address regional maritime challenges, improve coordination, and enhance interoperability among participating forces.
- **Key Drills & Components**:
  - **MARPOL Drills**: Focused on marine pollution response and environmental safety.
  - **VBSS Drills**: Operations covering **Visit, Board, Search, and Seizure** tactics.
  - **Phases**: Conducted in both **Harbour Phase** and **Sea Phase** activities.
- **Policy Alignment**: Aligns with India's **SAGAR** (Security and Growth for All in the Region) vision and **Neighborhood First Policy**.

---

### 2. Major Joint Exercises of India with Neighboring Nations

*High-frequency defense exercises asked in SSC & DSSSB exams.*

- **Sri Lanka**:
  - **SLINEX**: Naval exercise (Sri Lanka-India Naval Exercise).
  - **Mitra Shakti**: Joint military (army) exercise.
- **Maldives**:
  - **Ekuverin**: Joint military exercise (meaning "Friends" in Dhivehi).
  - **Ekathe**: Joint exercise between naval special forces / coast guards.
- **Bangladesh**:
  - **Bongosagar**: Bilateral naval exercise.
  - **SAMPRITI**: Joint army military exercise.
- **Nepal**:
  - **Surya Kiran**: Joint military army exercise.
