# 📌 Major Sites of Indus Valley Civilization (IVC)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **First Site Discovered**: **Harappa** was the first site excavated in **1921** by **Daya Ram Sahni** (under Sir John Marshall).
- **Alternative Name**: Because Harappa was discovered first, IVC is also widely known as the **Harappan Civilization**.
- **Second Major Site**: **Mohenjodaro** was excavated in **1922** by **R.D. Banerji** in Sindh, Pakistan.
- **Urban Planning**: Famous for grid system street layouts (chessboard pattern), covered drainage systems, and burnt brick houses.
- **Time Period**: Mature Harappan phase lasted roughly from **2600 B.C. to 1900 B.C.**

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key IVC Sites and Their Discoverers

*Essential details about major Indus Valley settlements.*

- **Harappa**: Located on the banks of the **Ravi River** (Punjab, Pakistan); discovered by Daya Ram Sahni in 1921.
- **Mohenjodaro**: Meaning *"Mound of the Dead"*; located on the banks of the **Indus River** (Sindh, Pakistan); discovered by R.D. Banerji in 1922.
- **Dholavira**: Located in Rann of Kutch (Gujarat); famous for advanced water harvesting systems and unique three-part town planning.
- **Lothal**: Located on the **Bhogava River** (Gujarat); famous as the **world's oldest artificial dockyard** (port town).
- **Kalibangan**: Located on the **Ghaggar River** (Rajasthan); famous for ploughed field signs and wooden drainage.
- **Chanhudaro**: Located in Sindh, Pakistan; famous as the only Harappan city **without a citadel** (fortress).

---

### 2. Major Archaeological Findings by Site

*Frequently asked artifacts and structures.*

- **Harappa**: 6 Granaries in a row, Coffin burial (R-37 cemetery), Stone statue of dancing girl (Torso), Clay mother goddess figures.
- **Mohenjodaro**: **Great Bath**, **Great Granary** (largest structure), Bronze Dancing Girl, Pashupati Seal, Steatite Bearded Priest statue.
- **Lothal**: Dockyard, Rice husk remains, Terracotta model of ship, Fire altars, Double burial.
- **Kalibangan**: Ploughed field surface, Camel bones, Fire altars, Decorative floor bricks.
- **Kot Diji**: Tar artifacts, Bull statues, Mother goddess idols.
