# 📌 Books, Newspapers & Writings of Mahatma Gandhi

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Author of 'Pathway to God'**: Written by **Mahatma Gandhi** (published in 1971 as a compilation of his spiritual thoughts).
- **Autobiography**: *The Story of My Experiments with Truth* (written originally in Gujarati).
- **Major Books**: *Hind Swaraj* (1909), *Satyagraha in South Africa*, and *Key to Health*.
- **Key Newspapers**: *Indian Opinion* (South Africa), *Young India* (English), *Navajivan* (Gujarati), and *Harijan* (English/Hindi).
- **Basic Life Facts**: Born on **2nd October 1869** at Porbandar, Gujarat; assassinated on **30th January 1948**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Books Written by Mahatma Gandhi

*Literary works and personal writings.*

- **Pathway to God**: Focuses on inner spiritual journey, truth, self-realization, and devotion to God.
- **The Story of My Experiments with Truth**: Official autobiography covering his life from childhood up to 1921.
- **Hind Swaraj (Indian Home Rule)**: Written in 1909 on a ship from London to South Africa, outlining his views on self-rule and critique of modern civilization.
- **Satyagraha in South Africa**: Detailed record of his early non-violent resistance movements against racial discrimination in South Africa.
- **Annasakti Yoga**: His interpretation and commentary on the Bhagavad Gita.

---

### 2. Major Newspapers & Journals Started by Gandhi

*Publications used to spread social and political awareness.*

- **Indian Opinion (1903)**: Started in South Africa in English, Tamil, Gujarati, and Hindi to fight racial prejudice.
- **Young India (1919–1931)**: Weekly journal in English used to share nationalist thoughts and non-violent resistance principles.
- **Navajivan (1919–1931)**: Weekly journal in Gujarati and Hindi.
- **Harijan (1933)**: English weekly founded to promote social equality and upliftment of marginalized communities (untouchables/Dalits).
