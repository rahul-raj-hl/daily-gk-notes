# 📌 Indo-German Agricultural Programme & Dairy Plant in Mandi

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Dairy Plant Location**: Under the Indo-German programme, a dairy development plant was established at **Chakkar** in Mandi district.
- **Programme Nature**: A collaborative initiative between India and Germany focused on agriculture, dairy processing, and rural development.
- **Core Objective**: To promote modern dairy farming techniques, improve milk yields, and boost local farmers' income.
- **State Context**: Located in Himachal Pradesh, where livestock rearing and milk production form a key part of rural economy.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Indo-German Project at Chakkar

*Collaborative agricultural development in Mandi district.*

- **Plant Location**: **Chakkar** (near Sundernagar in Mandi district, Himachal Pradesh).
- **Project Scope**: Started as an integrated agricultural development scheme combining cattle breeding, fodder production, and milk processing.
- **Key Function**: Modern milk collection, chilling, and processing facility to supply quality dairy products.
- **Economic Impact**: Improved market access and fair pricing for dairy farmers across the Balh Valley and surrounding Mandi regions.

---

### 2. Strategic Context of Other Options

*Key Mandi district landmarks frequently asked in state exams.*

- **Sundernagar**: Major industrial and educational town in Mandi district; famous for the Balh Valley.
- **Pandoh**: Famous for the **Pandoh Dam** built on the Beas River under the Beas-Sutlej Link project.
- **Slapper**: Location where water from the Beas River discharges into the Sutlej River via the Dehar Power House.
