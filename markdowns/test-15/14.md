# 📌 Declaration of Bihar as Naxal-Free State

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Naxal-Free Status**: **Bihar** was officially declared Naxal-free following the surrender of its last armed Maoist, **Suresh Koda**.
- **Location of Surrender**: The last armed insurgent surrendered in **Munger district**, confirmed by DIG Rakesh Kumar.
- **Affected Region History**: In 2012, up to **23 districts** in Bihar were categorized as Left-Wing Extremism (LWE) affected.
- **Zero Incidents**: Zero Naxalite violence incidents were recorded in the state throughout 2025.
- **Rehabilitation Policy**: Surrendered cadre receive economic and vocational benefits under the Central Government's **Surrender and Rehabilitation Policy**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Security Milestones in Bihar

*Key facts and timeline leading to Naxal eradication.*

- **Last Armed Surrender**: **Suresh Koda**, the final armed Maoist cadre in the region, laid down arms in Munger.
- **Official Announcement**: Confirmed by **DIG Rakesh Kumar** (Munger Range).
- **Active Squad Count**: No armed Naxalite squad remains operational across any of Bihar's 23 previously affected districts.
- **2025 Enforcement Record**:
  - Zero Naxalite violent incidents reported.
  - **220 Naxalites** arrested during joint security operations in 2025.
- **Historical Comparison**: In 2012, 23 out of 38 districts in Bihar were classified under the Security Related Expenditure (SRE) scheme for Left-Wing Extremism.

---

### 2. Left-Wing Extremism (LWE) Control Framework

*Central schemes and policies combating Naxalism in India.*

- **Surrender and Rehabilitation Policy**: Provides immediate financial assistance, monthly stipend for vocational training, and legal aid to former insurgents.
- **Operation SAMBAV & Security Focus**: Joint operations by State Police, CRPF, and CoBRA units to eliminate armed strongholds (e.g., Chakraborty and Bhimbandh forests).
- **SAMADHAN Strategy**: The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) overarching strategy for LWE:
  - **S**- Smart Leadership
  - **A**- Aggressive Strategy
  - **M**- Motivation and Training
  - **A**- Actionable Intelligence
  - **D**- Dashboard-based Key Performance Indicators
  - **H**- Harnessing Technology
  - **A**- Action Plan for each Theatre
  - **N**- No access to Financing
