# 📌 Peshwas of the Maratha Empire

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Last Peshwa**: **Baji Rao II** was the last ruling Peshwa of the Maratha Empire (reigned 1795–1818).
- **First Peshwa**: **Moropant Trimbak Pingle** was appointed as the first Peshwa by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.
- **First Hereditary Peshwa**: **Balaji Vishwanath** made the Peshwa office hereditary and supreme.
- **Treaty of Bassein (1802)**: Signed by Baji Rao II with the British East India Company, accepting the Subsidiary Alliance.
- **Abolition of Peshwaship**: Following defeat in the **Third Anglo-Maratha War (1817–18),** the British abolished the Peshwa post and exiled Baji Rao II to Bithoor (near Kanpur).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Chronology of Prominent Peshwas

*Key rulers and their contributions to the Maratha Empire.*

- **Balaji Vishwanath (1713–1720)**: First hereditary Peshwa; helped Chhatrapati Shahu consolidate power and secured Farrukhsiyar's Farman.
- **Baji Rao I (1720–1740)**: Son of Balaji Vishwanath; famous for expanding the empire Northward, winning the **Battle of Palkhed (1728)** against Nizam, and the slogan *"Let us strike at the trunk of the withered tree."*
- **Balaji Baji Rao / Nana Saheb I (1740–1761)**: Maratha power reached its peak under him; Signed the **Sangola Agreement (1750)** making Peshwa the real ruler; Third Battle of Panipat (1761) occurred during his reign.
- **Madhav Rao I (1761–1772)**: Restored Maratha power after the disaster of Panipat.
- **Raghunath Rao (1773–1774)**: Uncle of Narayan Rao who assassinated his nephew for power, leading to the **First Anglo-Maratha War**.
- **Madhav Rao II / Sawai Madhavrao (1774–1795)**: Infant Peshwa backed by the **Barabhai Council** led by **Nana Phadnavis** ("Maratha Machiavelli").
- **Baji Rao II (1795–1818)**: Son of Raghunath Rao; signed the Treaty of Bassein (1802) and was defeated in the Third Anglo-Maratha War, ending Peshwa rule.

---

### 2. End of Peshwa Rule & Adopted Son

*The fall of the Maratha Confederacy and its link to 1857 Revolt.*

- **Third Anglo-Maratha War (1817–1818)**: British forces under Lord Hastings defeated Baji Rao II.
- **Pension & Exile**: Baji Rao II was dethroned, his territories were annexed to Bombay Presidency, and he was exiled to Bithoor (Kanpur) on a British pension.
- **Nana Sahib (Dhondu Pant)**: Adopted son of Baji Rao II. Lord Dalhousie refused to pay him the pension under the **Doctrine of Lapse**, driving Nana Sahib to lead the **Rebellion of 1857 in Kanpur**.
