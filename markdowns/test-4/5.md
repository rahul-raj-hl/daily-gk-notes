# 📌 Food Preservation Techniques & Common Preservatives

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Preservatives**: **All (i), (ii), and (iii)** — **Sugar**, **Vinegar**, and **Common Salt** ($\text{NaCl}$) are widely used natural food preservatives.
- **Mechanism of Salt & Sugar**: Act via **osmosis / plasmolysis**, drawing water out of microbial cells to dehydrate and inhibit bacteria and fungi.
- **Mechanism of Vinegar**: Contains **acetic acid** ($\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$, $4\text{--}8\%$), lowering the $\text{pH}$ to create an acidic environment that prevents microbial survival and growth.
- **Common Chemical Preservatives**: **Sodium Benzoate** and **Sodium Metabisulphite** are widely used synthetic chemical preservatives in processed foods (jams, squashes).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Mechanisms of Common Natural Preservatives

*How traditional kitchen preservatives prevent food spoilage.*

- **Common Salt ($\text{NaCl}$)**:
  - **Process**: Known as *salting* or *curing*.
  - **Action**: Increases osmotic pressure, drawing water out of food and microbial cells through **osmosis** (plasmolysis).
  - **Common Uses**: Preserving meat, fish, raw mangoes, amla, and tamarind; preparing pickles.
- **Sugar**:
  - **Process**: High concentrations of sucrose bind free moisture.
  - **Action**: Reduces water activity ($a_w$) and induces hypertonic dehydration of microorganisms.
  - **Common Uses**: Jams, jellies, marmalades, and fruit preserves.
- **Vinegar (Acetic Acid)**:
  - **Composition**: $4\text{--}8\%$ dilute aqueous solution of acetic acid.
  - **Action**: Drastically lowers $\text{pH}$ (increases acidity); denatures essential enzymes in bacteria and spoilage organisms.
  - **Common Uses**: Pickling vegetables, chutneys, sauces, and meat marinades.

---

### 2. Overview of Food Preservation Methods

*Core classification of preservation techniques.*

- **Physical Methods**:
  - **Thermal Treatment**: Boiling, pasteurization (heating milk to $72^\circ\text{C}$ for 15–30 seconds and rapidly chilling).
  - **Refrigeration / Freezing**: Slows down enzymatic activity and microbial growth.
  - **Dehydration / Sun Drying**: Removes moisture to halt bacterial metabolism.
- **Chemical Methods**:
  - **Natural**: Salt, sugar, vinegar, mustard oil, spices.
  - **Synthetic**: Sodium benzoate, Potassium metabisulphite, Calcium propionate, Sorbic acid.
