# 📌 Atal Tunnel (Rohtang Tunnel) & BRO Key Infrastructure Projects

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Incorrect Statement (Altitude)**: Atal Tunnel is situated at an altitude of approximately **10,171 feet (3,100 meters)**, NOT 5,000 feet.
- **Length**: **9.02 km**, making it the world's longest single-tube highway tunnel above 10,000 feet.
- **Connectivity**: Provides **all-weather connectivity** between Manali and Lahaul-Spiti Valley.
- **Distance Reduction**: Reduces the road distance between Manali and Leh by approximately **46 to 50 km** (saves ~4 to 5 hours of travel time).
- **Constructing Agency**: Built by the **Border Roads Organisation (BRO)** under the Ministry of Defence.
- **Renaming**: Renamed from Rohtang Tunnel to **Atal Tunnel** in honor of former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Engineering & Geographic Features

*Specifications of the Atal Tunnel.*

- **Location**: Built under the **Rohtang Pass** in the **Pir Panjal Range** of the Himalayas, connecting Manali (Kullu Valley) to Lahaul and Spiti Valley in Himachal Pradesh.
- **Length & Altitude**: Total length is **9.02 km**, bored at an elevation of **3,100 meters (10,171 ft)** above sea level.
- **Strategic Importance**:
  - Provides year-round, secure movement of military logistics and troops toward Ladakh and the LAC.
  - Bypasses hazardous snowbound routes across the Rohtang Pass, which remained closed for 5–6 months during winter.
- **Constructing Agency**: Executed by the **Border Roads Organisation (BRO)**.

---

### 2. About Border Roads Organisation (BRO)

*Strategic infrastructure developer.*

- **Established**: **7 May 1960** by Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru.
- **Nodal Ministry**: **Ministry of Defence**.
- **Role**: Builds and maintains border roads, bridges, tunnels, and airfields across India's border zones and friendly neighboring nations (such as Bhutan, Myanmar, and Tajikistan).
- **Key Projects**:
  - *Project Deepak* / *Project Himank*
  - **Umling La Pass** (World's highest motorable road at 19,024 ft in Ladakh)
  - **Sela Tunnel** (Arunachal Pradesh)
  - **Shinku La Tunnel** (under construction to connect Himachal Pradesh to Ladakh)

# 📌 Atal Tunnel (Rohtang Tunnel) & Strategic Infrastructure

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Incorrect Statement / Actual Altitude**: The Rohtang Tunnel is situated at an elevation of over **10,000 feet (approx. 10,171 ft / 3,100 m)**, NOT 5,000 feet.
- **Renaming**: Renamed **Atal Tunnel** in honor of former Prime Minister **Atal Bihari Vajpayee**.
- **Constructing Agency**: Constructed by the **Border Roads Organisation (BRO)** under the Ministry of Defence.
- **Distance & Time Reduced**: Shortens the road distance between Manali and Leh by approximately **46–50 km** and reduces travel time by 4 to 5 hours.
- **All-Weather Route**: Guarantees year-round road connectivity to the previously isolated **Lahaul and Spiti Valley** in Himachal Pradesh.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Engineering & Geographical Facts

*Vital specifications of the Atal Tunnel.*

- **Location**: Built under the **Rohtang Pass** in the **Pir Panjal Range** of the Himalayas in Himachal Pradesh.
- **Total Length**: **9.02 km**, making it one of the longest highway tunnels in the world built above an altitude of 10,000 feet (3,000 meters).
- **Altitude**: Situated at approximately **3,100 meters (10,171 feet)** above mean sea level.
- **Shape & Technology**: Horseshoe-shaped, single-tube, bi-lane tunnel with an integrated emergency escape tunnel below the main roadway.

---

### 2. Border Roads Organisation (BRO)

*The premier defence infrastructure builder.*

- **Established**: 7 May 1960.
- **Nodal Ministry**: **Ministry of Defence**.
- **Role**: Develops and maintains critical road connectivity in border areas, strategic high-altitude terrains, and friendly neighboring nations.
- **Key High-Altitude Projects**: Atal Tunnel (HP), Sela Tunnel (Arunachal Pradesh), Umling La Pass Road (highest motorable road in Ladakh).
