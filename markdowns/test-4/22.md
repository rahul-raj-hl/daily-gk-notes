# 📌 Salal Dam & Hydroelectric Projects on Chenab River

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Primary Purpose**: **Hydroelectricity generation** (Salal Hydroelectric Power Station).
- **River**: Built across the **Chenab River**.
- **Location**: Situated in the **Reasi district** of the Union Territory of **Jammu and Kashmir**.
- **Installed Capacity**: **690 MW** ($6 \times 115\text{ MW}$ units), commissioned in two stages (Stage I in 1987 and Stage II in 1995).
- **Operated By**: National Hydroelectric Power Corporation (**NHPC**).
- **Chenab Origin**: Formed by the confluence of two headstreams, the **Chandra** and **Bhaga** rivers, near Tandi in the Lahaul and Spiti district of Himachal Pradesh.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Overview of Salal Hydroelectric Project

*Engineering details and strategic importance.*

- **Type of Project**: Run-of-the-river hydroelectric scheme constructed on a loop of the Chenab River.
- **Bilateral Context**: The design of the project was agreed upon through diplomatic discussions between India and Pakistan in 1978 under the framework of the **Indus Waters Treaty (1960)**.
- **Beneficiary States / UTs**: Supplies electricity to northern grid states including Jammu & Kashmir, Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, Himachal Pradesh, Rajasthan, and Uttar Pradesh.

---

### 2. Major Hydroelectric Projects on Chenab River

*High-yield river projects frequently asked in competitive exams.*

- **Salal Project**: 690 MW (Reasi district, J&K).
- **Dul Hasti Project**: 390 MW (Kishtwar district, J&K).
- **Baglihar Project**: 900 MW (Ramban district, J&K).
- **Ratle Project**: 850 MW (Kishtwar district, J&K).
- **Pakal Dul Project**: 1,000 MW (on the Marusudar River, a tributary of Chenab, Kishtwar district, J&K).
- **Kiru Project**: 624 MW (Kishtwar district, J&K).
- **Kwar Project**: 540 MW (Kishtwar district, J&K).
