# 📌 Rohtang Tunnel (Atal Tunnel) - Facts & Engineering Significance

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Incorrect Statement in Question**: *"It is located at an altitude of 5,000 feet"* is **incorrect**. 
- **Actual Altitude**: The tunnel is situated at an elevation of **approx. 10,171 feet (3,100 meters)** above mean sea level.
- **Official Name**: Renamed to **Atal Tunnel** in honor of former Prime Minister **Atal Bihari Vajpayee**.
- **Location**: Built under the **Rohtang Pass** in the **Pir Panjal Range** of the Himalayas, connecting **Manali** to the **Lahaul-Spiti Valley** in Himachal Pradesh.
- **Constructing Agency**: Built by the **Border Roads Organisation (BRO)** under the Ministry of Defence.
- **Length & Connectivity**: Length is **9.02 km**, reducing the road distance between Manali and Leh by approximately **46–50 km** and travel time by 4 to 5 hours while ensuring **all-weather year-round connectivity**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Engineering & Strategic Features

*Vital structural facts about Atal Tunnel.*

- **World Record Distinction**: Certified by the World Book of Records as the *longest highway tunnel above 10,000 feet (3,048 m)* in the world.
- **Length & Shape**: 
  - **Length**: $9.02\text{ km}$ (horse-shoe shaped single tube, double lane).
  - **Width**: $10.5\text{ m}$ road with a $1\text{ m}$ footpath on both sides.
  - **Emergency Escape**: Built-in emergency egress tunnel integrated directly beneath the main roadway.
- **Strategic Importance**:
  - Bypasses the snow-bound Rohtang Pass, which previously remained closed for nearly 6 months in winter due to heavy snowfall.
  - Provides year-round military logistics and civilian access to Ladakh and border posts.

---

### 2. About Border Roads Organisation (BRO)

*The executing agency for frontier infrastructure.*

- **Formed**: **7 May 1960** by Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru to develop remote northern and north-eastern border infrastructure.
- **Nodal Ministry**: Functions under the **Ministry of Defence**.
- **Role**: Builds and maintains national highways, bridges, airfield infrastructure, and strategic border tunnels (e.g., Atal Tunnel, Sela Tunnel, Zojila Tunnel).

# 📌 Atal Tunnel (Rohtang Tunnel) - Facts & Engineering

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Name**: **Atal Tunnel** (formerly known as the *Rohtang Tunnel*), named in honor of former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee.
- **Altitude**: Located at an altitude of approximately **10,171 feet (around 3,100 meters)** above mean sea level across the Pir Panjal range of the Himalayas (not 5,000 feet).
- **Constructing Agency**: Built and maintained by the **Border Roads Organisation (BRO)** under the Ministry of Defence.
- **Length**: **9.02 km**, holding the record for one of the longest highway single-tube tunnels above an altitude of 10,000 feet.
- **Strategic Impact**: 
  - Connects **Manali to the Lahaul-Spiti Valley** throughout the year.
  - Reduces the road distance between Manali and Leh by approximately **46 to 50 km** and travel time by 4 to 5 hours.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Engineering & Geographic Features

*Strategic importance and technical specifications.*

- **Location**: Built under the **Rohtang Pass** in the **Pir Panjal Range** of the Himalayas in Himachal Pradesh.
- **All-Weather Connectivity**: Previously, the Lahaul-Spiti valley remained cut off for nearly 6 months each winter due to heavy snowfall over the Rohtang Pass. The tunnel provides year-round access.
- **Shape and Design**: Horseshoe-shaped, single-tube, bi-directional tunnel featuring a built-in escape route beneath the main carriageway for emergency evacuation.
- **Maximum Speed**: Designed for vehicular speeds of up to $80\text{ km/h}$.

---

### 2. Strategic Importance for National Defence

*Border logistics and all-weather transport corridors.*

- Facilitates uninterrupted troop and logistics movements toward the border sectors of **Ladakh (Leh)**.
- Serves as a vital component of the alternative all-weather northern route along the **Manali-Leh Axis**.
