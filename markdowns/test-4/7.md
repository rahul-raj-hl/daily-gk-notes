# 📌 Gandhara School of Art & Kushana Dynasty

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Patron Dynasty**: The Gandhara School of Art emerged and flourished primarily under the **Kushana Dynasty** (1st century BCE to 4th century CE), especially during the reign of **Emperor Kanishka I**.
- **Artistic Style**: Known as **Greco-Buddhist / Greco-Roman Art** due to the fusion of Indian Buddhist spiritual themes with Greek and Roman anatomical realism and sculpting styles.
- **Medium Used**: Sculptures were predominantly carved using **Grey/Bluish-grey Schist stone**, along with **stucco** (plaster), green phyllite, and terracotta.
- **Geographical Center**: Flourished in the northwestern region of the Indian subcontinent (modern-day Peshawar, Taxila, and parts of Afghanistan/Swat Valley).
- **Key Focus**: Exclusively focused on **Buddhist themes**, creating the earliest anthropomorphic (human-like) Buddha and Bodhisattva sculptures.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Distinctive Features of Gandhara Art

*Stylistic and physical characteristics of Buddha sculptures.*

- **Greek/Roman Anatomical Realism**: Buddha is portrayed with sharp facial features, wavy/curly hair tied in a bun (*ushnisha*), well-defined muscular physique, and elongated earlobes.
- **Drapery**: Heavy, realistic pleated robes/drapes resembling a Greek toga, draped over one or both shoulders.
- **Spiritual Symbolism**: Large plain halo behind the head, *urna* (dot/mark on the forehead), and expressive meditative hand gestures (*mudras* like *Dhyana*, *Abhaya*, *Dharmachakra*, and *Bhumisparsha*).
- **Major Centers**: Taxila, Peshawar (Purushapura), Begram, Hadda, and Bamiyan (Afghanistan).

---

### 2. Comparison: Gandhara vs. Mathura vs. Amaravati Schools

*High-yield comparative matrix tested across exams.*

- **Gandhara School**:
  - Region: Northwest Frontiers (Peshawar, Taxila).
  - Patronage: **Kushana Dynasty**.
  - Religion: **Exclusively Buddhist** (Mahayana Buddhism).
  - Material: **Grey Schist Stone** & Stucco.
  - Influence: **Greco-Roman / Hellenistic**.
- **Mathura School**:
  - Region: Mathura (Uttar Pradesh).
  - Patronage: **Kushana Dynasty**.
  - Religion: **All three** (Buddhism, Hinduism, and Jainism).
  - Material: **Spotted Red Sandstone**.
  - Influence: **Purely Indigenous** (fleshy, smiling Buddha with shaven head and energetic posture).
- **Amaravati School**:
  - Region: Krishna-Godavari Basin, Andhra Pradesh.
  - Patronage: **Satavahanas and Ikshvakus**.
  - Religion: **Buddhism** (Emphasis on narrative art and Jataka tales).
  - Material: **White Marble / Limestone**.
