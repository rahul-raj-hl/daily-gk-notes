# 📌 UNESCO Memory of the World Register & Indian Inscriptions

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **1 only** (Statement 1 is correct; Statement 2 is incorrect).
- **Inscribed Texts**: The **Bhagavad Gita** and Bharata Muni's **Natyashastra** have been inscribed onto UNESCO's Memory of the World Register.
- **India's Total Inscriptions**: India has **14 documentary heritage entries** (not 18) inscribed on the Memory of the World International Register.
- **MoW Programme Established**: Established by UNESCO in **1992** to protect, preserve, and provide universal access to the world's significant documentary heritage.
- **Other Famous Indian Inscriptions**: 30 manuscripts of the *Rig Veda* (2007), *Saiva Manuscripts of Pondicherry* (2005), *Gilgit Manuscripts* (2017), *Ramcharitmanas*, *Panchatantra*, and *Maitreyavyakarana* (2023).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. UNESCO Memory of the World (MoW) Programme

*Safeguarding humanity's recorded memory and documentary treasures.*

- **Established**: In **1992** by UNESCO.
- **Objective**:
  - Facilitate preservation of the world's documentary heritage (manuscripts, oral recordings, film archives, and printed texts).
  - Enable universal access to documentary materials of outstanding universal value.
  - Raise global awareness of the significance and fragile state of historical records.
- **India's Standing**:
  - India hosts a vast corpus of ancient manuscripts preserved across repositories like the Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute (BORI, Pune), National Archives of India, and Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts (IGNCA).

---

### 2. Key Inscribed Texts: Bhagavad Gita & Natyashastra

*Cultural and philosophical significance.*

- **The Bhagavad Gita**:
  - Forms chapters 23–40 of the *Bhishma Parva* of the ancient epic *Mahabharata*.
  - A 700-verse philosophical dialogue between Lord Krishna and Arjuna on duty (*dharma*), action without attachment (*nishkama karma*), and spiritual knowledge (*jnana/bhakti*).
- **Natyashastra (by Bharata Muni)**:
  - The foundational Sanskrit treatise on Indian performing arts, theater, dance, and music.
  - Formulated the classical **Rasa Theory** (nine aesthetic emotional states) and detailed stagecraft, dramaturgy, gestures (*mudras*), and musical modes.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| UNESCO Heritage List | Primary Scope / Examples |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Memory of the World (MoW)** | **Documentary Heritage** (Rig Veda manuscripts, Natyashastra, Gilgit texts) |
| **Intangible Cultural Heritage (ICH)** | **Living Traditions / Folk Performances** (Kumbh Mela, Garba, Chhau, Vedic Chanting) |
| **World Heritage Sites (WHS)** | **Physical Monuments / Natural Parks** (Ajanta Caves, Kaziranga, Taj Mahal) |

---

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic**: **"MOW PRESERVES PAPERS, NOT DANCES"**
> - **MOW (Memory of the World)** $\rightarrow$ **Written manuscripts/documents** (Rigveda, Gita, Natyashastra)
> - **ICH** $\rightarrow$ **Intangible traditions & performances** (Garba, Kalbelia, Ramlila)
