# 📌 Plant Mineral Nutrition - Macronutrients vs Micronutrients

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Manganese ($\text{Mn}$)** is a **micronutrient** (trace element), NOT a macronutrient.
- **Macronutrients (9 elements)**: Required in large quantities ($> 10\text{ mmole kg}^{-1}$ of dry matter). These include **Carbon ($\text{C}$), Hydrogen ($\text{H}$), Oxygen ($\text{O}$), Nitrogen ($\text{N}$), Phosphorus ($\text{P}$), Potassium ($\text{K}$), Calcium ($\text{Ca}$), Magnesium ($\text{Mg}$), and Sulphur ($\text{S}$)**.
- **Micronutrients (8 elements)**: Required in trace amounts ($< 10\text{ mmole kg}^{-1}$ of dry matter). These include **Iron ($\text{Fe}$), Manganese ($\text{Mn}$), Copper ($\text{Cu}$), Molybdenum ($\text{Mo}$), Zinc ($\text{Zn}$), Boron ($\text{B}$), Chlorine ($\text{Cl}$), and Nickel ($\text{Ni}$)**.
- **Key Function of Manganese**: Best known for its role in the **photolysis (splitting) of water** during the light reaction of photosynthesis to evolve oxygen ($\text{O}_2$).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Classification of Essential Mineral Elements

*Criteria based on quantitative dietary requirements in plant tissues.*

- **Non-Mineral Elements (Framework Elements)**:
  - **$\text{C}, \text{H}, \text{O}$**: Absorbed mainly from air ($\text{CO}_2$) and water ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$); form the basic structure of plant cells.
- **Macronutrients (Mineral Source from Soil)**:
  - **Primary Macronutrients**: Nitrogen ($\text{N}$), Phosphorus ($\text{P}$), Potassium ($\text{K}$) — *NPK*.
  - **Secondary Macronutrients**: Calcium ($\text{Ca}$), Magnesium ($\text{Mg}$), Sulphur ($\text{S}$).
- **Micronutrients / Trace Elements**:
  - Needed in minute quantities, primarily serving as enzyme activators and prosthetic groups:
  - $\text{Fe}, \text{Mn}, \text{Zn}, \text{Cu}, \text{B}, \text{Mo}, \text{Cl}, \text{Ni}$.

---

### 2. High-Yield Functions of Specific Nutrients

*Frequently tested physiological functions across competitive exams.*

- **Magnesium ($\text{Mg}$)**:
  - Central constituent ring element of the **chlorophyll molecule**.
  - Activates enzymes of respiration and photosynthesis; maintains ribosome structure.
- **Phosphorus ($\text{P}$)**:
  - Constituent of **cell membranes (phospholipids)**, nucleic acids ($\text{DNA/RNA}$), and cellular energy molecules ($\text{ATP/ADP}$).
- **Sulphur ($\text{S}$)**:
  - Present in sulfur-containing amino acids (**Cysteine and Methionine**), coenzyme A, and vitamins (thiamine, biotin).
- **Manganese ($\text{Mn}$)**:
  - Essential for **photolysis of water** in Photosystem II ($\text{PS-II}$) to liberate oxygen.
- **Zinc ($\text{Zn}$)**:
  - Required for the synthesis of **Auxin** (plant growth hormone) and activation of carbonic anhydrase.
- **Boron ($\text{B}$)**:
  - Essential for **pollen grain germination**, cell elongation, and carbohydrate/sugar translocation.

| Nutrient Pair | Distinct Role / Classification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Magnesium ($\text{Mg}$)** | **Macronutrient** $\rightarrow$ Central core of chlorophyll |
| **Manganese ($\text{Mn}$)** | **Micronutrient** $\rightarrow$ Photolysis of water in photosynthesis |

---

| Micronutrient | Specific Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Molybdenum ($\text{Mo}$)** | Constituent of **Nitrogenase** enzyme (biological nitrogen fixation) |
| **Boron ($\text{B}$)** | Required for **pollen tube germination** & sugar transport |
| **Zinc ($\text{Zn}$)** | Required for **Auxin biosynthesis** |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which one of the following mineral nutrients is not the macro-nutrient required by plants?

- [x] Manganese
- [ ] Magnesium
- [ ] Phosphorus
- [ ] Sulphur

> Manganese is a micronutrient required only in trace amounts, whereas Magnesium, Phosphorus, and Sulphur are macronutrients.

---

### PYQ 2

Which metal ion is present at the center of the porphyrin ring of a chlorophyll molecule?

- [ ] Iron ($\text{Fe}$)
- [ ] Copper ($\text{Cu}$)
- [x] Magnesium ($\text{Mg}$)
- [ ] Manganese ($\text{Mn}$)

> Magnesium ($\text{Mg}^{2+}$) is the central metal atom coordinated within the ring system of chlorophyll.

---

### PYQ 3

Which of the following elements is primarily responsible for the photolysis of water during the light reaction of photosynthesis?

- [ ] Zinc
- [x] Manganese
- [ ] Molybdenum
- [ ] Calcium

> Manganese ($\text{Mn}$) and Chlorine ($\text{Cl}$) are essential cofactors for the oxygen-evolving complex that catalyzes the photolysis of water.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Which of the following micronutrients is essential for biological nitrogen fixation as a component of the nitrogenase enzyme?

- [ ] Boron
- [ ] Zinc
- [x] Molybdenum
- [ ] Copper

> Molybdenum ($\text{Mo}$) is a critical constituent of both nitrogenase and nitrate reductase enzymes involved in nitrogen metabolism.

---

### Q2

Which of the following pairs contains only sulfur-containing amino acids synthesized by plants?

- [ ] Glycine and Alanine
- [ ] Lysine and Valine
- [x] Cysteine and Methionine
- [ ] Proline and Serine

> Cysteine and methionine are the two primary sulfur-containing amino acids synthesized in plants using sulfur absorbed as sulfate ($\text{SO}_4^{2-}$).

---

### Q3

The deficiency of which micronutrient leads to 'Little Leaf Disease' and hinders the synthesis of the plant hormone auxin?

- [ ] Iron
- [x] Zinc
- [ ] Chlorine
- [ ] Copper

> Zinc is required for the biosynthesis of auxin (indole-3-acetic acid); its deficiency results in shortened internodes and little leaf disease.

---

### Q4

Which trace element is specifically required by plants for pollen germination and carbohydrate translocation?

- [x] Boron
- [ ] Nickel
- [ ] Magnesium
- [ ] Phosphorus

> Boron facilitates pollen tube growth, pollen germination, and the movement of sugars through the phloem.
