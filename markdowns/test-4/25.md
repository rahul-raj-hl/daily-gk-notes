# 📌 Khelo India Youth Games (KIYG) 2025 - Results & Medal Standings

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Champion State (1st Rank)**: **Maharashtra** topped the overall medal tally with **158 medals** (58 Gold, 47 Silver, 53 Bronze).
- **Runner-up (2nd Rank)**: **Haryana** finished second with **117 medals** (including 39 Gold).
- **3rd Rank**: **Rajasthan** finished third with **60 medals** (24 Gold, 12 Silver, 24 Bronze).
- **Host State Performance**: **Bihar** (host state) finished in **15th place** with a total of **36 medals** (7 Gold, 11 Silver, 18 Bronze).
- **Khelo India Initiative**: Flagship grassroots sports scheme launched by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports to identify and nurture young athletic talent across India.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Khelo India Youth Games (KIYG) 2025 Overview

*Medal table highlights and host details.*

- **Host**: Bihar.
- **Top Performing State**: **Maharashtra** defended its title as the most dominant sporting state in the KIYG series.
- **Key Medal Distribution**:
  - **1st — Maharashtra**: 58 Gold, 47 Silver, 53 Bronze (Total: 158).
  - **2nd — Haryana**: 39 Gold (Total: 117).
  - **3rd — Rajasthan**: 24 Gold, 12 Silver, 24 Bronze (Total: 60).
  - **15th — Bihar**: 7 Gold, 11 Silver, 18 Bronze (Total: 36).

---

### 2. Evolution of Khelo India Youth Games

*Historical milestones of the flagship youth sports event.*

- **Inception (2018)**: Originally launched as **Khelo India School Games (KISG)** in New Delhi; Haryana won the inaugural edition.
- **Renamed (2019)**: Rechristened as **Khelo India Youth Games (KIYG)** held in Pune, Maharashtra.
- **Age Categories**: Caters to two age brackets:
  - Under-17 school students
  - Under-21 college students
- **Financial Scholarship**: Top 1,000 talented athletes identified through the games receive an annual financial assistance scholarship of **₹5 Lakh per annum for 8 years**.
