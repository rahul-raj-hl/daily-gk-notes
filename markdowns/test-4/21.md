# 📌 Brahmo Samaj & Socio-Religious Reforms of Raja Ram Mohan Roy

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Principle NOT Followed**: **Membership based on caste** — The Brahmo Samaj was staunchly anti-caste, promoting universal brotherhood and equality; admission was open to all individuals regardless of caste, creed, or nationality.
- **Founder**: **Raja Ram Mohan Roy**, widely celebrated as the *"Father of Modern Indian Renaissance"* and *"Father of Modern India"*.
- **Founding Date**: Founded originally as **Brahmo Sabha in August 1828** in Calcutta (later renamed **Brahmo Samaj**).
- **Core Creed**: **Monotheism** (worship of one eternal, formless, unsearchable God / *Nirguna Brahma*), rejection of idol worship, priesthood, animal sacrifice, and dogmatic rituals.
- **Landmark Achievement**: Ram Mohan Roy's persistent advocacy led to the historic **Abolition of Sati (Regulation XVII of 1829)** during the governor-generalship of **Lord William Bentinck**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Philosophy & Principles of Brahmo Samaj

*Core spiritual and social ideals.*

- **Religious Reform**:
  - Based purely on the monotheistic essence of the **Upanishads** and *Vedanta*.
  - Strictly forbade idol worship, images, statues, sacrifices, and ritualistic offerings inside its prayer halls.
  - Opposed the intermediary role of hereditary priests (*Brahmins*) in spiritual matters.
- **Social Reform**:
  - **Eradication of Caste Rigidity**: Advocated social equality and attacked caste hierarchies, untouchability, and dietary restrictions.
  - **Emancipation of Women**: Campaigned tirelessly against the barbaric practice of *Sati*, child marriage, and polygamy, while championing widow remarriage and female inheritance rights.
  - **Promotion of Rationalism & Education**: Supported modern scientific Western education combined with indigenous learning (helped establish Hindu College in 1817 and Vedanta College in 1825).

---

### 2. Evolution & Splits in the Brahmo Samaj

*From a localized intellectual movement to nationwide branches.*

- **Atmiya Sabha (1814)**: Founded by Ram Mohan Roy in Calcutta as a precursor to discuss monotheism and social ills.
- **Debendranath Tagore's Leadership (1842)**:
  - Founded the **Tattvabodhini Sabha (1839)** and its organ *Tattvabodhini Patrika* before merging with the Brahmo Samaj.
  - Infused the movement with systematic organizational structure and Vedic study.
- **First Split (1866)**:
  - Disagreements between conservative Debendranath Tagore and radical reformer **Keshab Chandra Sen**.
  - Debendranath's group became the **Adi Brahmo Samaj**.
  - Keshab's group formed the **Brahmo Samaj of India** (*Bharatvarshiya Brahmo Samaj*).
- **Second Split (1878)**:
  - Keshab Chandra Sen married his 13-year-old minor daughter to the Maharaja of Cooch Behar following orthodox Hindu rites, violating his own *Native Marriage Act (1872)*.
  - Disillusioned followers led by **Anandamohan Bose** and **Sivanath Sastri** broke away to establish the **Sadharan Brahmo Samaj**.
