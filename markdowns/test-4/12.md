# 📌 Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Deposit Scheme (PMGKDS) 2016

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Scheme Name**: **Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Deposit Scheme (PMGDS), 2016**.
- **Notified Year**: Notified in **December 2016** under the *Taxation and Investment Regime for Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Yojana (PMGKY), 2016*.
- **Context**: Introduced in the wake of the **demonetization drive** (November 8, 2016) to provide a window for individuals to declare undisclosed income / black money.
- **Mandatory Deposit**: Declarants were mandated to deposit **$25\%$ of the declared undisclosed income** into PMGKDS.
- **Lock-in Period & Interest**: The deposited amount had a mandatory lock-in period of **4 years** and earned **$0\%$ interest** (interest-free).
- **Tax & Penalty Rate**: Declarants paid a total tax, surcharge (Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Cess), and penalty amounting to **$50\%$** of the undisclosed income.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Structure of PMGKY & PMGKDS (2016)

*Key financial components and tax breakdown.*

- **Legislative Backing**: Introduced via the *Taxation Laws (Second Amendment) Act, 2016*.
- **Financial Breakdown of Declared Amount**:
  - **$50\%$ Total Outflow**: Paid as Tax ($30\%$), PMGK Cess ($33\%$ of tax = $9.9\%$), and Penalty ($10\%$) $\approx 50\%$.
  - **$25\%$ Mandatory Deposit**: Locked in the **PMGKDS** for 4 years without any interest.
  - **$25\%$ Retained**: Remaining portion available immediately to the declarant.
- **Conditions on Deposit**:
  - Excluded from Wealth Tax.
  - Could **not** be used as collateral/security for taking loans from banks.
  - Deposits could only be made by individuals/entities making a valid declaration under PMGKY.

---

### 2. Schemes in the Options

*Quick overview of other government schemes tested in exams.*

- **Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)**:
  - Launched in **August 2014** for universal financial inclusion (zero-balance bank accounts, RuPay debit cards, and overdraft facilities).
- **National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP / NSAS)**:
  - Launched in **1995** under the Ministry of Rural Development; provides welfare pensions to the elderly, widows, and persons with disabilities living below the poverty line (BPL).
- **Ombudsman Scheme for NBFCs**:
  - Introduced by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) in **2018** for redressal of customer complaints against Non-Banking Financial Companies.
