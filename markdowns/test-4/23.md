# 📌 "Salman Khan: The Sultan of Bollywood" & Film Biographies

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Author**: **Mohar Basu**, an award-winning Indian film journalist and pop-culture commentator.
- **Book Title**: *"Salman Khan: The Sultan of Bollywood"*.
- **Publisher**: **HarperCollins India**.
- **Context / Occasion**: Published to commemorate actor Salman Khan's **60th birthday**.
- **Nature of Book**: A comprehensive biographical tribute and cultural commentary detailing his rise to superstardom, fan phenomena, and iconic film roles.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. About the Book & Author

*Highlights of the biography and its narrative scope.*

- **Scope**: Blends film history, rare photographs, industry interviews, and fan anecdotes to explore the enduring cultural phenomenon of Salman Khan across three decades.
- **Themes**:
  - The transition from early romantic roles (*Maine Pyar Kiya*, *Hum Aapke Hain Koun..!*) to mass action commercial cinema (*Wanted*, *Dabangg*, *Sultan*, *Bajrangi Bhaijaan*).
  - Cultural sociology of the single-screen cinema audience in India.
- **Publisher**: HarperCollins India, a leading publishing house known for extensive biographical and non-fiction releases.

---
