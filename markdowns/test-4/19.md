# 📌 Rigvedic Society & Composition of Vedic Hymns

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Both (I) and (II) are false**.
- **Statement I is False**: The hymns (*Suktas*) of the Rigveda were composed by **sages (*Rishis*)**, not by the *Dasas* or *Dasyus* (who were adversaries described as non-performers of sacrifices).
- **Statement II is False**: **Priests / Sages (*Gurus*)** taught students to recite and memorize each syllable, word, and sentence—NOT the *rajas* (tribal warrior chiefs).
- **Rigveda Overview**: The oldest of the four Vedas; comprises **10 Mandalas (books)**, **1,028 Suktas (hymns)**, and **10,580 verses** composed in **Vedic Sanskrit**.
- **Oral Transmission (*Shruti*)**: Transmitted orally across generations using exact phonetic precision through the *Guru-Shishya* tradition.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Composition and Transmission of the Rigveda

*Sages, oral pedagogy, and Vedic education.*

- **The Composers (*Rishis*)**:
  - Vedic hymns (*sukta*, meaning "well-said") were seen/revealed by sages (*Rishis*).
  - Prominent male sages include **Vishvamitra, Vashistha, Gritsamada, Vishvakarma, Bharadvaja, and Atri**.
  - Several hymns were also composed by celebrated women scholars and sages (*Brahmavadinis*), such as **Gargi, Maitreyi, Lopamudra, Ghosha, and Apala**.
- **Oral Pedagogy (*Patha* system)**:
  - **Priests and preceptors** taught students through strict oral recitation (*Shruti* - that which is heard).
  - Students committed verses to memory syllable by syllable using specialized recitation styles (*Samhita-patha, Pada-patha, Krama-patha, Jata-patha, and Ghana-patha*) to prevent textual corruption.

---

### 2. Social Terms: Aryas vs. Dasas / Dasyus & Role of Rajas

*Sociological structure in the Early Vedic Period.*

- **Aryas vs. Dasas / Dasyus**:
  - The composers of hymns called themselves **Aryas** ("noble").
  - They described their opponents as **Dasas** or **Dasyus**—people who did not perform Vedic sacrifices (*ayajvan*), spoke different tongues (*mridhravac*), and worshipped different deities.
  - Later in the Vedic period, the term *Dasa* came to denote a servant or enslaved person.
- **Nature of the Early Vedic *Rajan***:
  - The *Raja* in the Early Vedic Period was a **tribal warrior chieftain** (*Gopati* / protector of cattle), not an absolute monarch.
  - They did not maintain regular standing armies or tax-collection machinery; their primary function was leading tribal cattle raids (*Gavishti*) and warfare, not scholastic teaching.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Early Vedic Group | Role & Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Rishis / Priests** | Composed and **taught Vedic hymns** to disciples |
| **Rajas / Chieftains** | **Led military expeditions** and protected tribal cattle (*Gopa*) |
| **Dasas / Dasyus** | Opponents of Aryas; did **not** perform sacrifices or compose hymns |
