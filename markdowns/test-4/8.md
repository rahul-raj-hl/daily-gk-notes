# 📌 India's First Earth System Model (ESM) & IITM Pune

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Developing Institute**: The **Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM)**, located in **Pune**, is spearheading the development of the **first Earth System Model (ESM)** for India.
- **Parent Ministry**: IITM is an autonomous research institution operating under the **Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)**, Government of India.
- **Core Purpose**: To improve climate forecasts, assess regional climate change impacts, model monsoon dynamics, and support long-term adaptation and policy planning.
- **Global Context**: Aligns with India's commitments under the **Paris Agreement** and contributes to IPCC climate assessment reports.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Earth System Model (ESM)

*State-of-the-art computational simulation for Earth's interactive spheres.*

- **Definition**: An Earth System Model (ESM) integrates the physical, chemical, and biological interactions between the atmosphere, oceans, land surface, cryosphere (ice), and biosphere.
- **Indian ESM Focus**:
  - Specially tailored to simulate and forecast the **Indian Summer Monsoon**, which is one of the most complex coupled climate phenomena globally.
  - Predicts long-term impacts on agriculture, water security, glacier melt in the Himalayas, and frequency of extreme climatic events (cyclones, droughts, heavy rainfall).

---

### 2. Major Research Institutes & Headquarters

*Key scientific institutes frequently tested in exams.*

- **Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM)**: **Pune, Maharashtra** (Established in 1962 under MoES).
- **Central Arid Zone Research Institute (CAZRI)**: **Jodhpur, Rajasthan** (Under ICAR; focused on desertification and arid zone farming).
- **National Environmental Engineering Research Institute (NEERI)**: **Nagpur, Maharashtra** (Under CSIR; focused on water, air pollution, and environmental engineering).
- **Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)**: **New Delhi** (Apex body for agricultural research and education).
