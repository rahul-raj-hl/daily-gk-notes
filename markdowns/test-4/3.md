# 📌 Bada Khayal, Sultan Hussain Sharqi & Semi-Classical Forms

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Bada Khayal Origin**: Sultan **Hussain Shah Sharqi** of Jaunpur developed **Bada Khayal** (slow-tempo Khayal) on the pattern of **Qawwali**.
- **Sharqi Dynasty of Jaunpur**: Jaunpur was renowned as the *"Shiraz of India"* due to its rich patronage of music, learning, and architecture.
- **Thumri**: Semi-classical vocal genre originating in eastern Uttar Pradesh (Bhakti movement influenced); popularized during the 19th century in the court of **Nawab Wajid Ali Shah** of Awadh.
- **Dadra**: A light classical vocal genre performed in Dadra tala, traditionally sung immediately after a Thumri.
- **Qawwali**: Sufi devotional musical form pioneered in India by **Amir Khusrau**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Sultan Hussain Shah Sharqi & Development of Khayal

*Contribution of the Jaunpur Sultanate to Indian classical music.*

- **Hussain Shah Sharqi (r. 1458–1479 CE)**: The last ruler of the Sharqi dynasty of Jaunpur; an accomplished musician, composer, and theorist.
- **Development of Bada Khayal**:
  - He structured **Bada Khayal** (vilambit/slow-tempo khayal) taking structural inspiration from the melodic flow and expressive flexibility of **Qawwali**.
  - Credited with creating several famous ragas such as *Jaunpuri Todi*, *Hussaini Todi*, and *Jaunpuri Asavari*.

---

### 2. Major Classical & Semi-Classical Forms

*High-yield vocal genres tested across competitive exams.*

- **Khayal**:
  - Meaning: Derived from the Arabic/Persian word meaning *"imagination"* or *"thought"*.
  - Types: **Bada Khayal** (slow tempo / *Vilambit*) and **Chhota Khayal** (fast tempo / *Drut*).
  - Main Patronage / Pioneers: Amir Khusrau, Sultan Hussain Sharqi, and Sadarang-Adarang (Niyamat Khan & Firoz Khan in Mughal court).
- **Thumri**:
  - Name Origin: Derived from the Hindi verb *thumakna* (graceful walking / footwork).
  - Nature: Romantic-devotional themes expressing the love of Radha and Krishna; deeply linked with the **Kathak** dance tradition.
  - Centers: Banaras (Purab Ang) and Lucknow (Lucknow Ang).
- **Dadra**:
  - Characterized by lighter rhythmic structures (6-beat or 8-beat cycle); closely related to Thumri and usually sung at the conclusion of a Thumri recital.
- **Ghazal & Qawwali**:
  - **Ghazal**: Poetic form of rhyming couplets (*sher*) and refrain originating in Arabic poetry.
  - **Qawwali**: Choral Sufi devotional singing designed to induce spiritual ecstasy (*wajad*).
