# 📌 Air Marshal Subroto Mukherjee & Indian Air Force History

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A**.
- **Father of the IAF**: **Air Marshal Subroto Mukherjee** is widely revered as the **"Father of the Indian Air Force"**.
- **First Indian Air Chief**: He took over as the **first Indian Commander-in-Chief / Chief of the Air Staff (CAS)** on **1 April 1954**.
- **Establishment of IAF**: Formally raised on **8 October 1932** as an auxiliary force of the Royal Air Force (celebrated annually as **Air Force Day**).
- **IAF Motto**: *"Nabhaḥ Sparśaṁ Dīptam"* (*"Touch the Sky with Glory"*), taken from Chapter 11, Verse 24 of the **Bhagavad Gita**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Life & Contributions of Subroto Mukherjee (1911–1960)

*The pioneer who transformed the IAF into an independent, modern force.*

- **Pioneering Cadre**: Born on 5 March 1911 in Kolkata; was among the first batch of six Indian cadets selected to train at the **Royal Air Force College, Cranwell (UK)** in 1930.
- **Historic Milestones**:
  - Commissioned into the newly formed Indian Air Force on **8 October 1932**.
  - In 1939, he became the first Indian to command a flight squadron (No. 1 Squadron) and later the first Indian to command an operational station (RAF Station Kohat).
  - Appointed the **first Indian Chief of Air Staff** on **1 April 1954**, replacing Air Marshal Sir Gerald Gibbs.
- **Modernization & Institution Building**:
  - Oversaw the transition of the IAF into the jet era (introducing Vampire, Toofani, and Mystère fighters).
  - Reorganized technical branches, flight training academies, and maintenance commands to make the force completely self-reliant.

---

### 2. Historical Timeline & Key Defence Milestones

*High-yield exam facts on the founding commanders of the Indian Armed Forces.*

- **Indian Army**:
  - First Indian Commander-in-Chief: **Field Marshal K. M. Cariappa** (took command on **15 January 1949**, celebrated as **Army Day**).
- **Indian Navy**:
  - First Indian Chief of the Naval Staff (CNS): **Vice Admiral R. D. Katari** (assumed office on **22 April 1958**; Navy Day celebrated on **4 December**).
- **Indian Air Force**:
  - First Indian Chief of the Air Staff (CAS): **Air Marshal Subroto Mukherjee** (**1 April 1954**; Air Force Day celebrated on **8 October**).
- **Only 5-Star Rank Officer in IAF**: **Marshal of the Indian Air Force Arjan Singh** (conferred 5-star rank in January 2002).
