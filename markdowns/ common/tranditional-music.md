# 📌 Classical Dances of India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- India has **8 Classical Dance Forms** recognized by the **Sangeet Natak Akademi**.
- SSC mainly asks the **Dance ↔ State**, **Important Exponent**, and **Special Feature**.
- Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kathakali and Odissi are the most frequently asked.

---

## 💃 Classical Dance Forms

### 🪔 Bharatanatyam

*One-liner:* Oldest classical dance, famous for grace and temple traditions.

- 📍 State → Tamil Nadu
- 🙏 Theme → Devotional (Bhakti)
- ⭐ Famous Exponents:
  - Rukmini Devi Arundale
  - Yamini Krishnamurthy
  - Alarmel Valli

---

### 👣 Kathak

*One-liner:* Storytelling dance known for fast footwork.

- 📍 State → Uttar Pradesh
- 🎵 Famous for → Tatkar (Footwork), Spins (Chakkars)
- ⭐ Exponents:
  - Birju Maharaj
  - Sitara Devi
  - Kumudini Lakhia

---

### 🎭 Kathakali

*One-liner:* Dance-drama with elaborate makeup and costumes.

- 📍 State → Kerala
- 🎨 Famous for → Green face makeup & mythological stories
- ⭐ Exponents:
  - Kalamandalam Gopi
  - Ramankutty Nair

---

### 🌊 Mohiniyattam

*One-liner:* Graceful feminine dance of Kerala.

- 📍 State → Kerala
- 🌸 Theme → Soft, graceful movements
- ⭐ Exponents:
  - Bharati Shivaji
  - Kalamandalam Kalyanikutty Amma

---

### 🌀 Kuchipudi

*One-liner:* Dance-drama originating from a village of the same name.

- 📍 State → Andhra Pradesh
- 🥁 Famous for → Dancing on a brass plate (Tarangam)
- ⭐ Exponents:
  - Raja & Radha Reddy
  - Yamini Krishnamurthy

---

### 🛕 Odissi

*One-liner:* Temple dance inspired by the sculptures of Odisha.

- 📍 State → Odisha
- 🌙 Famous for → Tribhangi posture
- ⭐ Exponents:
  - Kelucharan Mohapatra
  - Sonal Mansingh

---

### 🌿 Manipuri

*One-liner:* Devotional dance based on Radha-Krishna themes.

- 📍 State → Manipur
- 🌸 Famous for → Gentle and graceful movements
- ⭐ Exponents:
  - Guru Bipin Singh
  - Jhaveri Sisters

---

### 🎼 Sattriya

*One-liner:* Monastic dance tradition of Assam.

- 📍 State → Assam
- ⛩️ Introduced by → Srimanta Sankardeva
- ⭐ Theme → Vaishnavism

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Which classical dance form originated in Odisha?

- [ ] Kathak
- [ ] Bharatanatyam
- [x] Odissi
- [ ] Kuchipudi

> **Explanation:** Odissi is the classical dance form of Odisha and is famous for the Tribhangi posture.

---

### Q2

Kathak is primarily associated with:

- [ ] Rajasthan
- [ ] Madhya Pradesh
- [x] Uttar Pradesh
- [ ] Bihar

> **Explanation:** Kathak evolved in the temples and royal courts of North India, especially Uttar Pradesh.

---

### Q3

Which classical dance is famous for dancing on a brass plate?

- [ ] Bharatanatyam
- [ ] Kathakali
- [x] Kuchipudi
- [ ] Manipuri

> **Explanation:** Tarangam, performed on a brass plate, is a unique feature of Kuchipudi.

---

### Q4

Sattriya dance was introduced by:

- [ ] Birju Maharaj
- [ ] Vallathol Narayana Menon
- [x] Srimanta Sankardeva
- [ ] Kelucharan Mohapatra

> **Explanation:** Sattriya originated in the Vaishnavite monasteries of Assam under Srimanta Sankardeva.

---

### Q5

Which dance form is famous for elaborate face makeup and dance-drama?

- [ ] Kathak
- [x] Kathakali
- [ ] Mohiniyattam
- [ ] Odissi

> **Explanation:** Kathakali of Kerala is renowned for its elaborate costumes, makeup, and dramatic storytelling.
