# 📌 Census in India: Historical Evolution, Constitutional Framework & Key Provisions

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Incorrect Statement Identified**: **Option 4 is INCORRECT** because Population Census is a **Union Subject** under **Article 246** listed at **Serial Number 69** (not State List) of the **Seventh Schedule** of the Indian Constitution.
- **Historical Milestones**:
  - **Ancient/Medieval**: Reference to population counting exists in *Rig Veda* (800–600 BCE), *Arthashastra* (for taxation), and *Ain-i-Akbari* (population/industry data).
  - **First City Census**: Conducted in **1830 by Henry Walter in Dacca** (Dhaka).
  - **Second City Census**: Conducted in 1836–37 by Fort St. George.
- **Legal & Administrative Framework**:
  - Conducted under the provisions of the **Census Act, 1948**.
  - Executed by the **Office of the Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India** under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**.
- **Upcoming / Digital Census Innovations**: First digital census with self-enumeration provisions and the first-time inclusion of households headed by persons from the Transgender community.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Historical Evolution of Census in India

| Period / Timeline | Key Record / Authority | Significant Contribution / Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ancient Period (800–600 BCE)** | **Rig Veda** | Mentions early population counts. |
| **Mauryan Era (300 BCE)** | **Kautilya's Arthashastra** | Prescribed population statistics collection for state tax policy. |
| **Mughal Era (16th Century)** | **Abul Fazl's Ain-i-Akbari** | Comprehensive data on population, industry, and economic wealth. |
| **1830** | **Henry Walter (Dacca)** | **First complete census of an Indian city**. |
| **1836–1837** | **Fort St. George** | Second urban census attempt in India. |
| **1872** | **Lord Mayo** | First non-synchronous, all-India census attempt. |
| **1881** | **W.C. Plowden (Lord Ripon)** | **First synchronous, complete decennial census of India**. |

---

### 2. Constitutional & Administrative Machinery

* **Constitutional Position**:
  - **Subject**: Population Census is exclusively a **Union Subject**.
  - **Article**: **Article 246** of the Constitution of India.
  - **Schedule**: **Seventh Schedule (Union List - Entry / Serial No. 69)**.
* **Statutory Authority**: Conducted under the **Census Act, 1948**.
* **Nodal Office**: **Office of the Registrar General and Census Commissioner, India (ORGI)**, established in 1961 under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Constitutional / Historical Concept | Accurate Distinction | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |

| **Census vs. Population Control List** | • **Census**: **Union List** (Entry 69).<br>• **Population Control & Family Planning**: **Concurrent List** (Entry 20A). | Confusing Census (Union List) with Population Control (Concurrent List) |
| **First Census vs. First Synchronous Census** | • **1872 (Lord Mayo)**: First general/non-synchronous census.<br>• **1881 (Lord Ripon)**: First synchronous/complete all-India census. | Crediting Lord Mayo with the first complete synchronous census |
| **City Census (1830) vs. All-India Census (1881)** | • **1830**: First complete census of a **city** (Dacca by Henry Walter).<br>• **1881**: First **all-India** synchronous census. | Mixing up Henry Walter's city census with the nationwide census |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "CENSUS 69 IS HOME IN UNION"**
> - **69**: Entry Serial No. **69** in 7th Schedule
> - **HOME**: Governed by Ministry of **Home** Affairs
> - **UNION**: Strictly a **Union** Subject under Article 246
