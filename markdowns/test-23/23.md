# 📌 Basanti Bisht & Folk Music of Uttarakhand

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Personality**: **Basanti Bisht** is a renowned Indian folk singer hailing from **Uttarakhand**.
- **Specialization**: Celebrated for preserving and performing **'Jagar'** singing—a traditional ritualistic musical form invoking local deities.
- **Pioneer Role**: Recognized as the **first female performer of Jagar singing**, breaking long-standing gender barriers in a traditionally male-dominated art form.
- **Civilian Honor**: Awarded the **Padma Shri** in **2017** for her distinguished service to Indian folk art and cultural heritage.

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Cultural Breakdown

### 1. Jagar Tradition & Sacred Folk Art

**Jagar** is an ancient ritualistic folk performance indigenous to the Garhwal and Kumaon regions of Uttarakhand. The word *Jagar* is derived from the Sanskrit root *"Jag"*, meaning "to awaken".

- **Core Objective**: Invoking local devtas (gods/spirits) such as **Golu Devta**, **Nanda Devi**, and **Mahasu Devta** to seek divine blessings or resolve community grievances.
- **Musical Instruments Used**: Key accompaniment includes the **Hurka** (an hour-glass shaped percussion drum), **Thali** (brass plate played with sticks), and **Dhol-Damau**.
- **Social Impact**: Traditionally restricted to male singers (*Jagaris*), Basanti Bisht pioneered women's participation in this sacred art form.

### 2. Notable Folk Music & Dance Forms of Uttarakhand

| Folk Form | Category | Key Characteristics / Occasion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jagar** | Ritualistic Singing | Awakening and worshipping divine deities; accompanied by Hurka and Thali |
| **Pandav Nritya** | Folk Dance / Drama | Reenactment of scenes from Mahabharata during Diwali / post-harvest period |
| **Chholiya** | Martial Dance | Sword and shield dance performed during Kumaoni marriage processions |
| **Jhumeila & Chaunfla** | Romantic / Festive Dance | Performed by women celebrating spring season (Basant Panchami) and festivals |
| **Barada Nati** | Folk Dance | Popular group dance performed during religious festivals in Jaunsar-Bawar |
