# 📌 Fundamental Rights: Citizen-Exclusive Rights vs. Rights Available to All

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Exclusive Rights for Citizens Only**: Fundamental Rights guaranteed under **Articles 15, 16, 19, 29, and 30** are available **ONLY to Indian citizens** (not to foreign citizens or alien enemies).
- **Article 19**: Guarantees the **Six Freedoms** (Speech & Expression, Assembly, Association, Movement, Residence, and Profession) exclusively to citizens.
- **Rights Available to Both (Citizens + Foreigners)**: Fundamental Rights under **Articles 14, 20, 21, 21A, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, and 28** apply to both Indian citizens and foreigners (except enemy aliens).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Fundamental Rights Classification: Citizens vs. Foreigners

| Category | Articles Included | Brief Description / Scope | Applicability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Citizens Only** | **Article 15** | Prohibition of discrimination on grounds only of religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth. | **Indian Citizens ONLY** |
| **Citizens Only** | **Article 16** | Equality of opportunity in matters of public employment. | **Indian Citizens ONLY** |
| **Citizens Only** | **Article 19** | Protection of 6 basic freedoms (speech, assembly, association, movement, residence, trade/profession). | **Indian Citizens ONLY** |
| **Citizens Only** | **Article 29** | Protection of language, script, and culture of minorities. | **Indian Citizens ONLY** |
| **Citizens Only** | **Article 30** | Right of minorities to establish and administer educational institutions. | **Indian Citizens ONLY** |
| **Both Citizens & Foreigners** | **Articles 14, 20, 21, 21A, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28** | Right to equality before law, life and personal liberty, protection against conviction/arrest, freedom of religion, etc. | **Citizens + Non-Citizens** (Except Enemy Aliens) |

---

### 2. Breakdown of Article 19 (Six Democratic Freedoms)

Article 19 guarantees six basic rights to Indian citizens, subject to reasonable restrictions (Article 19(2) to 19(6)):

1. **Article 19(1)(a)**: Right to freedom of **speech and expression**.
2. **Article 19(1)(b)**: Right to **assemble peacefully** and without arms.
3. **Article 19(1)(c)**: Right to form **associations, unions, or co-operative societies**.
4. **Article 19(1)(d)**: Right to **move freely** throughout the territory of India.
5. **Article 19(1)(e)**: Right to **reside and settle** in any part of the territory of India.
6. **Article 19(1)(g)**: Right to practice any **profession**, or carry on any occupation, trade, or business.

*(Note: Article 19(1)(f) — Right to property — was removed by the 44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978).*

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Article / Concept | Key Difference | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Article 14 vs. Article 15** | • **Article 14**: Equality before law (Applies to **All persons**).<br>• **Article 15**: Anti-discrimination (Applies to **Citizens ONLY**). | Assuming all equality articles (14-18) apply to foreigners |
| **Article 21 vs. Article 19** | • **Article 21**: Personal Liberty (Applies to **All persons**).<br>• **Article 19**: 6 Freedoms (Applies to **Citizens ONLY**). | Confusing liberty (21) with freedom of movement/speech (19) |
| **Enemy Aliens Exclusions** | Enemy aliens do NOT get protection under **Article 22** (preventive detention rights) or fundamental rights exclusive to citizens. | Thinking foreigners get identical rights under all non-exclusive articles |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which of the following Fundamental Rights is available ONLY to citizens of India and NOT to foreigners?

- [ ] Protection of life and personal liberty (Article 21)
- [x] Freedom of speech and expression (Article 19)
- [ ] Equality before law (Article 14)
- [ ] Protection in respect of conviction for offenses (Article 20)

> Fundamental Rights under Articles 15, 16, 19, 29, and 30 are available exclusively to citizens of India. Article 19 guarantees freedom of speech and expression.

---

### PYQ 2

Which Article of the Indian Constitution prohibits discrimination against any citizen on grounds ONLY of religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth?

- [ ] Article 14
- [x] Article 15
- [ ] Article 16
- [ ] Article 17

> Article 15 provides that the State shall not discriminate against any citizen on grounds only of religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth, or any of them.

---

### PYQ 3

Which of the following Articles protects an individual against self-incrimination and double jeopardy?

- [ ] Article 19
- [x] Article 20
- [ ] Article 21
- [ ] Article 22

> Article 20 provides protection in respect of conviction for offenses, including protection against ex-post-facto laws, double jeopardy, and self-incrimination.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

A foreign national residing in India CANNOT claim which of the following Fundamental Rights?

- [ ] Right to freedom of religion under Article 25
- [ ] Protection against arrest and detention under Article 22
- [x] Equality of opportunity in matters of public employment under Article 16
- [ ] Protection of life and personal liberty under Article 21

> Article 16 (equality of opportunity in public employment) is exclusively available to Indian citizens. Foreign nationals cannot claim rights under Articles 15, 16, 19, 29, and 30.

---

### Q2

Which Constitutional Amendment Act removed the 'Right to Property' from the list of Fundamental Rights under Article 19(1)(f)?

- [ ] 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976
- [x] 44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978
- [ ] 86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002
- [ ] 91st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003

> The 44th Amendment Act, 1978 deleted the Right to Property from the list of Fundamental Rights and made it a legal/constitutional right under Article 300A in Part XII.

---

### Q3

Under Article 16, on how many grounds specifically is discrimination in public employment prohibited by the State?

- [ ] 5 grounds
- [x] 7 grounds
- [ ] 6 grounds
- [ ] 4 grounds

> Article 16(2) states that no citizen shall be ineligible for or discriminated against in respect of public employment on grounds only of religion, race, caste, sex, descent, place of birth, residence, or any of them (total 7 grounds).

---

### Q4

Which Fundamental Right remains operative and CANNOT be suspended even during the proclamation of a National Emergency under Article 352?

- [ ] Article 19
- [ ] Articles 14 and 19
- [x] Articles 20 and 21
- [ ] Articles 21 and 22

> As per the 44th Amendment Act, 1978, the rights guaranteed under Articles 20 (protection against conviction) and 21 (right to life and personal liberty) cannot be suspended even during a National Emergency.
