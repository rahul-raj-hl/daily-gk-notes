# 📌 The Rock Cycle: Processes & Rock Transformation

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Core Question Answer**: **Both Statements 1 and 2 are CORRECT**.
  - **Statement 1**: Rocks do not remain in their original form for long but undergo continuous transformation.
  - **Statement 2**: The transformation of rocks across different types is driven by **weathering, erosion, and metamorphic action**.
- **Primary Rocks**: **Igneous rocks** are known as *primary rocks* because all other rock types (sedimentary and metamorphic) are ultimately derived from them.
- **Subduction & Recycling**: Crustal rocks (igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic) can be pushed down into the mantle via **subduction** at convergent plate boundaries, melting into magma to restart the cycle.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Interrelationships in the Rock Cycle

* **Igneous Rocks**: Form directly from the cooling and solidification of molten magma or lava.
* **Sedimentary Rocks**: Formed when fragments derived from igneous, metamorphic, or existing sedimentary rocks undergo weathering, erosion, and lithification.
* **Metamorphic Rocks**: Formed when igneous or sedimentary rocks are subjected to intense heat and pressure without complete melting.
* **Magma (Re-entry)**: Any crustal rock type subducted into the Earth's interior melts down due to extreme temperatures, converting back into molten magma.

---

### 2. Transformation Mechanisms Between Rock Types

| Original State | Process / Agent | Resulting Rock Type | Explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Magma / Lava** | Cooling & Solidification | **Igneous Rocks** | Primary creation of rock material from molten earth interior. |
| **Igneous / Metamorphic** | Weathering, Erosion, & Lithification | **Sedimentary Rocks** | Broken fragments deposit, compact, and cement together. |
| **Igneous / Sedimentary** | High Heat & Intense Pressure | **Metamorphic Rocks** | Recrystallization occurs without complete melting. |
| **Any Rock Type** | Subduction into Mantle $\rightarrow$ Melting | **Magma (Re-entry)** | Crustal plates subduct at convergent zones, melting back into magma. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Process / Term | Distinct Difference | Common Exam Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Metamorphism vs. Melting** | • **Metamorphism**: Structural/chemical change in **solid state** under high heat/pressure.<br>• **Melting**: Total fluid transformation into **magma**. | Assuming metamorphic rocks go through a liquid phase |
| **Weathering vs. Erosion** | • **Weathering**: In-situ breaking down of rocks.<br>• **Erosion**: Transportation of weathered fragments by agents like wind/water. | Using weathering and erosion as interchangeable terms |
