# 📌 Patimokkha (Pratimoksha): Buddhist Monastic Code & Vinaya Rules

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Core Definition**: **Paṭimokkha** (Sanskrit: *Pratimoksha*) is the foundational code of monastic discipline in Buddhism, regulating the daily conduct, ethics, and communal harmony of ordained monks (*Bhikkhus*) and nuns (*Bhikkhunis*).
- **Textual Location**: It is embedded within the **Suttavibhaṅga**, which is a major division of the **Vinaya Piṭaka** (one of the Tripitakas).
- **Rule Count**:
  - **Bhikkhu Paṭimokkha (Monks)**: Contains **227 rules** (in Theravada tradition).
  - **Bhikkhuṇī Paṭimokkha (Nuns)**: Contains **311 rules**.
- **Special Rules for Nuns**: The Bhikkhuni Patimokkha contains **89 extra rules** with no direct equivalent in the Bhikkhu Patimokkha, formulated specifically to protect nuns from abusive/careless behavior and to prevent servitude to monks or laypeople.
- **Recitation Requirement**: Recited fortnightly by the Sangha on **Uposatha** days (Full Moon and New Moon) as a communal confession and purification rite.

| Pitaka (Basket) | Presiding Monk / Reciter | Core Subject Matter & Contents | Primary Divisions / Components |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

---

### 2. Primary Divisions of the Vinaya Piṭaka

- **Suttavibhaṅga**: Contains the basic monastic rules (*Patimokkha*) along with the origin stories (*Atthuppatti*) for each rule.
  - **Bhikkhu Vibhaṅga**: 227 rules for monks.
  - **Bhikkhuṇī Vibhaṅga**: 311 rules for nuns.
- **Khandhaka**: Details the rules governing daily monastic procedures, ordination, housing, clothing, and legal proceedings (subdivided into *Mahavagga* and *Cullavagga*).
- **Parivara**: A technical summary, cross-reference manual, and catechism of the rules for study and instruction.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Term / Text | Core Focus & Function | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vinaya Pitaka vs. Sutta Pitaka** | • **Vinaya Pitaka**: Monastic rules and code (*Patimokkha*).<br>• **Sutta Pitaka**: Sermons and doctrinal teachings of the Buddha. | Attributing monastic rules to the Sutta Pitaka |
| **Suttavibhanga vs. Sutta Pitaka** | • **Suttavibhaṅga**: A sub-text of the **Vinaya Pitaka** explaining the rules.<br>• **Sutta Piṭaka**: The main collection of Buddha's sermons. | Confusing *Suttavibhanga* with the *Sutta Pitaka* due to the word 'Sutta' |
| **Bhikkhu (227) vs. Bhikkhuni (311)** | • Monks have **227** rules.<br>• Nuns have **311** rules (more rules due to additional protective codes). | Assuming monks have more rules than nuns |
