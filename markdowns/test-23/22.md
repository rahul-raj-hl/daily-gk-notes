# 📌 Konark Sun Temple & Eastern Ganga Dynasty

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Builder**: **King Narasimhadeva I** of the **Eastern Ganga Dynasty** (13th Century CE, c. 1250 CE).
- **Architectural Style**: **Kalinga Architecture** (designed as a gigantic stone chariot of Sun God Surya with 24 wheels pulled by 7 horses).
- **Historical Nickname**: Famed as the **"Black Pagoda"** by European sailors (contrasted with the **Jagannath Temple** of Puri, called the **"White Pagoda"**).
- **UNESCO Recognition**: Designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in **1984**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Architectural Features

### 1. Architectural Highlights

- **Chariot Design**: Constructed in the shape of a colossal chariot dedicated to the Sun God, **Surya**.
- **Stone Wheels**: Features **24 elaborately carved wheels** (12 pairs representing 12 months of the year, functioning as accurate sundials) pulled by **7 galloping horses** (representing the 7 days of the week or 7 colors of light).
- **Kalinga Temple Components**:
  - **Jagamohana (Assembly Hall)**: The main surviving structure seen today.
  - **Deula (Sanctum/Vimana)**: The principal tower, which collapsed in the past.
  - **Nata Mandira (Dancing Hall)** & **Bhoga Mandira (Offering Hall)**.
- **Material**: Built using Khondalite rocks and chlorite stone structures.
