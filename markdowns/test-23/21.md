# 📌 Ajanta & Ellora Caves

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Primary Significance**: Renowned UNESCO World Heritage Sites in **Maharashtra** famous for world-class **Buddhist religious art**, rock-cut cave architecture, and Jataka tale murals.
- **Ajanta Caves**: **Exclusively Buddhist** (30 caves), famous for tempera wall paintings and Jataka stories (2nd Century BCE to 5th Century CE).
- **Ellora Caves**: **Multi-religious** (34 main caves: Buddhist, Hindu, Jain), famous for the monolithic **Kailash Temple (Cave 16)** (6th to 10th Century CE).

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Detailed Comparison

### 1. Ajanta Caves

- **Location**: Waghora River valley in Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar (Aurangabad) district, Maharashtra.
- **Religious Affiliation**: **Exclusively Buddhist** (30 rock-cut caves).
- **Patrons**: Satavahanas (earlier phase) and Vakatakas (later phase - especially Harishena).
- **Key Features**:
  - **Fresco/Tempera Paintings**: Murals depicting the life of Gautama Buddha and **Jataka Tales** (stories of Buddha's previous births).
  - **Famous Paintings**: *Padmapani* (Bodhisattva holding a blue lotus), *Vajrapani*, and the *Dying Princess*.
  - **Rediscovery**: Rediscovered in **1819** by British officer **John Smith** during a tiger hunt.

---

### 2. Ellora Caves (Verul Lena)

- **Location**: Charanandri hills, Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar (Aurangabad) district, Maharashtra.
- **Religious Affiliation**: **Multi-religious** (34 main caves open to tourists):
  - **Caves 1–12**: Buddhist Caves (6th–8th Century CE)
  - **Caves 13–29**: Hindu Caves (7th–9th Century CE)
  - **Caves 30–34**: Jain Caves (9th–10th Century CE)
- **Patrons**: Rashtrakutas, Kalachuris, and Yadavas.
- **Key Features**:
  - **Cave 16 (Kailash Temple)**: World's largest monolithic rock-cut structure, excavated top-down from a single basalt cliff under Rashtrakuta King **Krishna I**.
  - **Cave 10 (Vishvakarma Cave)**: A famous Buddhist Chaitya hall containing a seated Buddha statue.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Cave Complex | Religious Breakdown | Unique Distinguishing Factor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ajanta** | 100% Buddhist | Famous for **wall paintings** & Jataka murals |
| **Ellora** | Buddhist + Hindu + Jain | Famous for **Kailash Temple** (rock carving) |
| **Elephanta** | Predominantly Hindu (Shaivite) | Located on Gharapuri Island, Mumbai (Trimurti idol) |
| **Bagh Caves** | Exclusively Buddhist | Located in Madhya Pradesh (similar to Ajanta paintings) |
