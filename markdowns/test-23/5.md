# 📌 Gupta Empire: Administrative Hierarchy, Imperial Titles & Inscriptions

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Incorrect Statement Identified**: **Statement 3 is INCORRECT** because the **Sanchi Inscription** mentions the **Panchmandali** (a local corporate/administrative body).
- **Administrative Hierarchy**: The empire was divided into provinces called **Deshas** or **Bhuktis** (governed by *Uparikas*), which were further subdivided into districts called **Visayas** (headed by *Vishayapatis*), followed by lower units like **Vithi**, **Bhumi**, **Pathaka**, and villages (**Grama**).
- **Royal Titles**:
  - **Chandragupta II**: Adopted *Vikramaditya*, **Sakari** (destroyer of Shakas), *Devagupta*, *Devashri*, *Devaraja*, and *Param Bhagavata*.
  - **Samudragupta**: *Kaviraja*, *Ashwamedha-Parakrama*, and *Param Bhagavata*.
  - **Skandagupta**: *Vikramaditya*, *Shakropama* (Kahaum Pillar), and *Karamaditya*.
- **Girnar Rock Inscription**: Skandagupta's Girnar inscription details the breach of **Sudarshana Lake** in 455 CE and its restoration within two months by his governor **Parnadatta** and his son **Chakrapalita**.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Administrative Divisions & Officials of the Gupta Empire

| Administrative Unit | Unit Level / Hierarchy | Head Official / Governing Body | Key Responsibilities & Inscriptional Sources |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bhukti / Desha** | Province (Top Administrative Level) | **Uparika** (Appointed directly by the King) | Highest provincial status; recorded in **Damodarpur Copper Plates**. |
| **Visaya** | District | **Vishayati** / **Vishayapati** | Assisted by local boards like *Nagarasreshtin* (banker) & *Sarthavaha* (trader). |
| **Vithi / Bhumi / Pathaka** | Sub-District Level | **Ayuktakas** & **Vithi-mahattaras** | Managed local land transactions and revenue administration. |
| **Grama** | Village (Lowest Unit) | **Gramika** / **Gramadhyaksha** | Head of village administration assisted by village elders (*Gramavriddhas*). |
| **Local Corporate Body** | Village / Town Assembly | **Panchmandali** | Mentioned specifically in the **Sanchi Inscription**. |

### 3. Key Inscriptions & Administrative Mentions

- **Damodarpur Copper Plates**: Mentions that *Uparikas* held high rank in provincial administration and refers to an **Ashtakula-Adhikarana** (eight-member board).
- **Sanchi Inscription**: Records the functioning of the **Panchmandali** (corporate local council).
- **Allahabad Pillar Inscription (Prayag Prashasti)**: References an imperial advisory council known as the **Sabha**.
- **Junagadh / Girnar Inscription**: Records the flood damage to **Sudarshana Lake** in 455 CE and its reconstruction under Skandagupta's reign.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Term / Name | Accurate Historical Context | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bhukti vs. Visaya** | • **Bhukti**: Province (larger unit, headed by *Uparika*).<br>• **Visaya**: District (sub-division of Bhukti, headed by *Vishayapati*). | Reversing the administrative hierarchy between Bhukti and Visaya |
| **Sakari vs. Shakraditya** | • **Sakari**: Title of **Chandragupta II** (Conqueror of Shakas).<br>• **Shakraditya**: Title of **Kumaragupta I**. | Confusing the titles of Chandragupta II and Kumaragupta I |
| **Sudarshana Lake Builders & Repairers** | • **Constructed by**: Pushyagupta (under Chandragupta Maurya).<br>• **Conduits added by**: Tushaspha (under Ashoka).<br>• **1st Repair**: Rudradaman I (Junagadh Inscription, 150 CE).<br>• **2nd Repair**: Skandagupta / Chakrapalita (455 CE). | Attributing the initial construction of Sudarshana Lake to Skandagupta |
