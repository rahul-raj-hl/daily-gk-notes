# 📌 Thrissur Pooram: The Mother of All Poorams

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Core Question Answer**: **Thrissur Pooram** is celebrated in the state of **Kerala**.
- **Key Characteristics**: Known as the *"Mother of all Poorams"*, it features grand caparisoned elephant processions (*Aana Chamayam*), traditional percussion ensembles (*Ilanjithara Melam*), decorative umbrella switching ceremonies (*Kudamattam*), and spectacular midnight fireworks.
- **Venue**: Held annually at the **Vadakkunnathan Temple** grounds (Thekkinkadu Maidan) in Thrissur, dedicated to Lord Shiva.
- **Historical Pioneer**: Founded by **Raja Rama Varma** (popularly known as **Sakthan Thampuran**), the Maharaja of Cochin, in 1798.

### 1. Key Elements of Thrissur Pooram

| Feature | Local Term / Detail | Exam Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Founder** | **Sakthan Thampuran** (Raja Rama Varma) | Unified local temple festivals into one grand Pooram in 1798. |
| **Primary Venue** | **Vadakkunnathan Temple**, Thrissur | Ancient Shiva temple in Kerala style architecture. |
| **Participating Temples** | 10 local temples divided into 2 groups | **Paramekkavu** and **Thiruvambadi** are the main rival groups. |
| **Key Ritual / Display** | **Kudamattam** | Competitive quick-display of colorful decorative sequined umbrellas atop elephants. |
| **Musical Ensemble** | **Ilanjithara Melam** | Traditional orchestra featuring over 250 musicians playing *Chenda*, *Kombu*, and *Elathalam*. |

### 2. Major Cultural Festivals of Southern & Eastern States

* **Kerala**: Onam, Vishu, Thrissur Pooram, Theyyam, Attukal Pongala, Thirvathira.
* **Tamil Nadu**: Pongal, Thaipusam, Natyanjali Dance Festival, Jallikattu (during Pongal).
* **Karnataka**: Mysuru Dasara, Kambala (buffalo race), Ugadi, Karaga.
* **Odisha**: Raja Parba, Nuakhai, Rath Yatra (Puri), Bali Yatra.
