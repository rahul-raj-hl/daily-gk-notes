# 📌 Presiding Officers of Rajya Sabha: Chairman, Deputy Chairman & Vacancy Provisions

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Core Question Answer**: When the office of the Chairman of the Council of States (Rajya Sabha) is vacant, the duties of the office are performed by the **Deputy Chairman of the Council of States**.
- **Ex-Officio Chairman**: The **Vice President of India** is the *ex-officio* Chairman of the Rajya Sabha (Article 89).
- **Election of Deputy Chairman**: The Deputy Chairman is elected by the members of the Rajya Sabha from **amongst its own members** (Article 89(2)).
- **Vacancy vs. Absence (Article 91)**:
  - If the Chairman's office is **VACANT** (or when VP acts as President): The **Deputy Chairman** performs the duties of the Chairman. If Deputy Chairman is also vacant, a member appointed by the **President of India** performs the duties.
  - If the Chairman is **ABSENT** from a sitting: The Deputy Chairman presides. If Deputy Chairman is also absent, a person from the **Panel of Vice-Chairmen** presides.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Presiding Officers of Rajya Sabha & Constitutional Articles

| Position | How Appointed / Elected | Constitutional Article | Primary Role & Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chairman (Rajya Sabha)** | *Ex-officio* (Vice President of India, elected by Electoral College of both Houses). | **Article 89(1)** | Presides over Rajya Sabha; NOT a member of Rajya Sabha; casts vote only in case of a tie (casting vote). |
| **Deputy Chairman** | Elected by Rajya Sabha members from **amongst themselves**. | **Article 89(2)** | Presides when Chairman is absent or office is vacant; subordinate to the House, NOT to the Chairman. |
| **Panel of Vice-Chairmen** | Nominated by the **Chairman** of Rajya Sabha. | Rules of Procedure (Rajya Sabha) | Any member from the panel can preside during **absence** (NOT vacancy) of Chairman and Deputy Chairman. |

---

### 2. Difference Between 'Vacancy' and 'Absence' (Article 91)

Understanding the distinction between vacancy and absence is critical for exam questions:

| Scenario | Office of Chairman Status | Who Presides / Performs Duties? | Key Constitutional Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vacancy in Chairman's Office** | Vacant (e.g., VP resigns, dies, or acts as President) | **Deputy Chairman** | Performs all duties of the Chairman (Article 91(1)). |
| **Vacancy in Both Offices** | Both Chairman & Deputy Chairman offices are vacant | **A member of Rajya Sabha appointed by the President** | President appoints a member specifically to perform duties until election. |
| **Absence from a Sitting** | Chairman is temporarily absent (e.g., unwell) | **Deputy Chairman** | Presides over the sitting of the House (Article 91(2)). |
| **Absence of Both** | Both Chairman & Deputy Chairman are absent | **A member from the Panel of Vice-Chairmen** | If panel member is also absent, House decides who presides. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Scenario / Office | Correct Provision | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vacancy vs. Panel of Vice-Chairmen** | Members of the Panel of Vice-Chairmen can preside ONLY during **ABSENCE**, **NOT during VACANCY**. | Thinking a panel member can act when the Chairman's office is vacant |
| **Resignation of Deputy Chairman** | Deputy Chairman submits resignation letter to the **Chairman of Rajya Sabha** (Vice President). | Confusing it with submitting resignation to the President of India |
| **Casting Vote Right** | The Chairman does NOT vote in the first instance; holds a **casting vote** only in case of a tie. | Assuming the Chairman votes like a regular MP on all bills |
| **Vice President Acting as President** | When the Vice President acts as President of India, he **does NOT perform duties** of Rajya Sabha Chairman (Article 97). | Assuming VP continues presiding over RS while acting as President |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

When the office of the Chairman of Rajya Sabha is vacant, who among the following performs the duties of the Chairman?

- [ ] Speaker of Lok Sabha
- [x] Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha
- [ ] Prime Minister of India
- [ ] Senior-most member of Rajya Sabha

> Under Article 91(1), when the office of Chairman is vacant, the Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha performs the duties of the Chairman.

---

### PYQ 2

If the offices of BOTH the Chairman and the Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha fall vacant, who performs the duties of the Chairman?

- [ ] Speaker of Lok Sabha
- [ ] Chief Justice of India
- [x] A member of Rajya Sabha appointed by the President
- [ ] Leader of the House in Rajya Sabha

> Article 91(1) specifies that if both offices are vacant, the duties shall be performed by such member of the Council of States as the President may appoint for the purpose.

---

### PYQ 3

The Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha submits his resignation letter addressed to the:

- [ ] President of India
- [ ] Prime Minister of India
- [x] Chairman of Rajya Sabha
- [ ] Chief Justice of India

> Under Article 90, a member holding office as Deputy Chairman may at any time resign his office by writing under his hand addressed to the Chairman.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Which Article of the Constitution of India provides for the election of a Deputy Chairman of the Council of States (Rajya Sabha)?

- [ ] Article 85
- [x] Article 89
- [ ] Article 93
- [ ] Article 100

> Article 89(2) provides that the Council of States shall, as soon as may be, choose a member of the Council to be Deputy Chairman thereof.

---

### Q2

Can a member of the Panel of Vice-Chairmen preside over the sitting of Rajya Sabha when the office of the Chairman is VACANT?

- [ ] Yes, automatically
- [ ] Yes, if approved by the Leader of the House
- [x] No, a panel member cannot preside during a vacancy (only during absence)
- [ ] Yes, if approved by the Speaker of Lok Sabha

> Members of the Panel of Vice-Chairmen can preside ONLY when the Chairman or Deputy Chairman is ABSENT, not when the office is VACANT. During a vacancy, the President appoints a member if the Deputy Chairman is unavailable.

---

### Q3

When the Vice President of India acts as the President of India, what happens to his salary and duties as the Chairman of Rajya Sabha?

- [ ] He continues receiving salary as Chairman of Rajya Sabha
- [x] He does NOT perform the duties of Chairman and receives salary of the President
- [ ] He holds both offices simultaneously and receives dual salary
- [ ] Deputy Chairman takes over as Vice President

> As per Article 97, when the Vice President acts as President, he does not perform the duties of Chairman of Rajya Sabha and is entitled to the emoluments/salary of the President of India.

---

### Q4

Which of the following statements regarding the Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha is INCORRECT?

- [ ] He is elected by members of Rajya Sabha from among themselves.
- [ ] He ceases to hold office if he ceases to be a member of Rajya Sabha.
- [x] He is directly subordinate to the Chairman of Rajya Sabha.
- [ ] He can be removed by a resolution passed by a majority of all the then members of Rajya Sabha.

> The Deputy Chairman is NOT subordinate to the Chairman; he is directly responsible to the Rajya Sabha.
