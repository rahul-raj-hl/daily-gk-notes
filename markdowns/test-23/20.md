# 📌 Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve & Mysore Elephant Reserve

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Core Concept**: The **Mysore Elephant Reserve** forms an integral part of the larger **Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve** landscape, which is India's first biosphere reserve.
- **Key Location**: Tri-junction of **Karnataka, Kerala, and Tamil Nadu** in the Western Ghats.
- **Ecological Significance**: World's largest wild population of Asian Elephants (*Elephas maximus*) and a crucial UNESCO World Heritage Site cluster.

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Geographical Breakdown

### 1. Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve (NBR)

- **Established**: **1986** (India's 1st Biosphere Reserve).
- **UNESCO MAB Status**: Included in the **Man and the Biosphere (MAB)** program list in **2000**.
- **Total Area**: Approx. **5,520 sq. km**.
- **State Breakdown**:
  - **Karnataka**: Bandipur National Park, Nagarhole National Park, Mudumalai contiguous belt.
  - **Kerala**: Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary, Silent Valley National Park, Aralam Wildlife Sanctuary.
  - **Tamil Nadu**: Mudumalai National Park, Mukurthi National Park, Sathyamangalam Tiger Reserve.

---

### 2. Mysore Elephant Reserve

- **Location**: Primary elephant landscape of southern Karnataka covering Mysore, Chamarajanagar, and Kodagu districts.
- **Associated Protected Areas**: Bandipur, Nagarhole, BRT (Biligiri Ranganatha Swamy Temple) Tiger Reserve, and Wayanad-Mudumalai corridors.
- **Project Elephant**: Established under India's **Project Elephant (1992)** to secure elephant corridors, reduce human-elephant conflict, and safeguard habitats.

- [ ] Bandipur National Park
- [ ] Silent Valley National Park

> Periyar National Park is located further south in Kerala (Cardamom Hills) and forms part of the Periyar Elephant Reserve/Tiger Reserve, not the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve.

> The Moyar river runs through the Moyar gorge/valley dividing the Nilgiri plateau and Mudumalai region within NBR.
