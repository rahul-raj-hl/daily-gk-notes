# 📌 PT Usha & 1984 Los Angeles Olympics

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Historic Event**: **1984 Los Angeles Olympic Games** (400m Hurdles)[cite: 1].
- **Timing & Finish**: Clocked **55.42 seconds**[cite: 1], missing the bronze medal by just **0.01 seconds (1/100th of a second)**[cite: 1] to finish **4th**[cite: 1].
- **Popular Nicknames**: **"Payyoli Express"**[cite: 1] and **"Golden Girl"**.
- **Key Honors**: Arjuna Award (1983)[cite: 1], Padma Shri (1985)[cite: 1], and current President of the **Indian Olympic Association (IOA)**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept & Detailed Breakdown

### 1. The 1984 Los Angeles Performance

Pilavullakandi Thekkeparambil Usha (P.T. Usha) became the first Indian woman to reach the final of an Olympic track event during the 1984 Los Angeles Games[cite: 1].

- **Event**: Women's 400-metre Hurdles[cite: 1].
- **National Record**: Her time of **55.42 seconds** in the 1984 final remained the Indian National Record for decades[cite: 1].
- **Marginal Defeat**: Cristieana Cojocaru of Romania won the bronze medal with a timing of **55.41 seconds**, leaving Usha just 0.01 seconds short of becoming the first Indian woman to win an Olympic medal[cite: 1].
