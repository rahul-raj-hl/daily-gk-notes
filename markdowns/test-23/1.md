# 📌 Socio-Religious Reform Movements: Key Organizations & Founders

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Rajahmundry Social Reform Association**: Founded in **1878** by **Kandukuri Veeresalingam Pantulu**, widely revered as the **'Father of Renaissance in Telugu'**.
- **Servants of India Society**: Established in **1905** at Pune by **Gopal Krishna Gokhale** to train national missionaries for social service.
- **Social Service League**: Founded in **1911** at Bombay by **Narayan Malhar (N. M.) Joshi**, a prominent trade union leader and co-founder of the All India Trade Union Congress (AITUC).
- **Seva Samiti**: Established in **1914** at Allahabad by **Hridayanath Kunzru** (a key member of the Servants of India Society) to organize relief operations during disasters and epidemics.

### 2. Deep Dive into Key Reformers

#### **A. Kandukuri Veeresalingam Pantulu (1848–1919)**
- **Title**: Father of Telugu Renaissance (*Andhra Renaissance*).
- **Literary Works**:
  - Authored **"Rajasekhara Charitamu"** (1880) — considered the first modern novel in Telugu literature.
  - Published journals like *Viveka Vardhani* (1874) and *Sati Hita Bodhini* (for women's education).
- **Social Reform**: Organized the first widow remarriage in the Andhra region in December 1881. Established an orphanage and widow home in Rajahmundry.

#### **B. Gopal Krishna Gokhale (1866–1915)**
- Political guru of **Mahatma Gandhi**.
- Leader of the Moderate faction of the Indian National Congress (Presided over the **1905 Banaras INC Session**).
- Associated with the Deccan Education Society prior to founding the **Servants of India Society** in 1905.

#### **C. Narayan Malhar Joshi (1879–1955)**
- Co-founded the **All India Trade Union Congress (AITUC)** in 1920 alongside Lala Lajpat Rai.
- Served as the General Secretary of AITUC (1925–1929 and 1940–1948).
- Formed the **All India Trade Union Federation** in 1931.

#### **D. Hridayanath Kunzru (1887–1978)**
- Member of the **Servants of India Society**.
- Formed **Seva Samiti** (1914) and **Bharat Scouts and Guides**.
- Served as a member of the **States Reorganisation Commission (SRC)** in 1953 alongside Fazl Ali and K. M. Panikkar.

| **Social Service League vs. Seva Samiti** | • **Social Service League (1911)**: Founded by **N. M. Joshi** (Labor/Secular focus).<br>• **Seva Samiti (1914)**: Founded by **H. N. Kunzru** (Disaster relief focus). | Confusing N. M. Joshi and H. N. Kunzru |
