# 📌 Andhra Pradesh: Geography, Hydroelectric Projects & Mineral Wealth

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Primary Subject**: Andhra Pradesh geography, mineral reserves, borders, and major river valley projects.
- **Srisailam Dam**: Also known as **Neelam Sanjiva Reddy Sagar Dam**, constructed across the **Krishna River** on the border of Kurnool district (Andhra Pradesh) and Nagarkurnool/Mahabubnagar (Telangana) near Srisailam temple town.
- **Mineral Abundance**: Nicknamed **'Ratna Garbha'** due to its rich and diverse mineral wealth. Holds the **largest deposits of quality Chrysotile Asbestos (white asbestos)** in India.
- **Coastline & Size**: 8th largest state by area (1,60,205 sq km) and possesses the **2nd longest coastline in India (974 km)** after Gujarat.
- **Bifurcation**: Split into Telangana and residuary Andhra Pradesh under the **Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014**.

### 2. Srisailam Dam (Neelam Sanjiva Reddy Sagar Project)

- **River**: Constructed across the **Krishna River**.
- **Location**: Situated near the Srisailam temple town, straddling the Kurnool district of AP and Mahabubnagar/Nagarkurnool districts of Telangana.
- **Capacity & Dimensions**:
  - Length: **512 m** (1,680 ft).
  - Height: **145 m** (476 ft).
  - Crest Gates: **12 radial crest gates**.
  - Reservoir Area: **616 sq km**.
- **Power Production**: Functions as the **2nd largest capacity working hydroelectric station** in India.

---

### 3. Mineral Resources Profile of Andhra Pradesh

- **Chrysotile Asbestos**: Serpentine group mineral silicate fiber, commonly used in construction, roofing, and heat insulation. Andhra Pradesh leads in high-quality deposits.
- **Other Prominent Minerals**: Copper ore, Manganese, Mica (Nellore belt), Coal, Barytes (Mangampeta), and Limestone.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Geographical / Mineral Topic | Accurate Distinction | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Longest vs. 2nd Longest Coastline** | • **Gujarat**: Longest overall coastline.<br>• **Andhra Pradesh**: 2nd longest overall (longest in Peninsular East Coast). | Marking Andhra Pradesh as the overall longest coastline in India |
| **Srisailam Dam vs. Nagarjuna Sagar Dam** | • **Srisailam**: Neelam Sanjiva Reddy Sagar (Upstream, Krishna River).<br>• **Nagarjuna Sagar**: Downstream on the Krishna River (Guntur/Nalgonda). | Confusing the locations of the two major Krishna River dams |
| **Chrysotile vs. Amphibole Asbestos** | • **Chrysotile**: Serpentine series (*White Asbestos*), most common and commercially used.<br>• **Amphibole**: Includes blue/brown asbestos (more toxic). | Classifying Chrysotile under the Amphibole group |
