# 📌 Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY): Provisions & Scheme Matrix

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Option**: **Both I and II** are correct.
  - **Statement I**: The scheme is available to individuals aged **18 to 70 years** who hold a savings bank or post office account.
  - **Statement II**: Risk coverage provided is **₹2 Lakh** for accidental death and permanent total disability, and **₹1 Lakh** for permanent partial disability.
- **Launch Date & Place**: Launched on **9 May 2015** in Kolkata by Prime Minister Narendra Modi along with PMJJBY and Atal Pension Yojana (APY).
- **Premium Structure**:
  - Initially launched at **₹12 per annum**.
  - Revised to **₹20 per annum** (effective from June 2022).
  - Premium is auto-debited in a single installment from the subscriber's account on or before 1 June annually.
- **Coverage Period**: Operates on a yearly renewal basis from **1 June to 31 May** of subsequent years.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Risk Coverage and Benefit Table for PMSBY

| Event / Casualty Covered | Benefit / Insurance Sum Payable |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Accidental Death** | **₹2,00,000 (₹2 Lakh)** |
| **Total and Irrecoverable Loss of both eyes OR Loss of use of both hands or feet OR Loss of sight of one eye and loss of use of one hand or foot** | **₹2,00,000 (₹2 Lakh)** |
| **Total and Irrecoverable Loss of sight of one eye OR Loss of use of one hand or foot (Partial Disability)** | **₹1,00,000 (₹1 Lakh)** |

| **Implementing Ministry** | **Ministry of Finance** (DFS) | **Ministry of Finance** (DFS) | **PFRDA** / Ministry of Finance |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Parameter | PMSBY | PMJJBY | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Age Eligibility** | **18 – 70 Years** | **18 – 50 Years** | Mixing up the upper age limits ($70$ vs $50$) |
| **Nature of Risk** | **Accidental Death & Disability Only** | **Death due to Any Reason (Natural/Accidental)** | Believing PMSBY covers natural death or illnesses |
| **Annual Premium** | **₹20** | **₹436** | Quoting the old pre-revision rates (₹12 and ₹330) |
| **Partial Disability Cover** | **₹1 Lakh** | **No partial disability benefit** (Death only) | Assuming PMJJBY pays for disability |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "SURAKSHA SEVENTY (PMSBY 18-70), JEEVAN FIFTY (PMJJBY 18-50)"**
> - **Suraksha (PMSBY)** $\rightarrow$ Covers accidents up to **70 years**
> - **Jeevan (PMJJBY)** $\rightarrow$ Covers life entry up to **50 years**
> - **20 for Suraksha, 436 for Jeevan** $\rightarrow$ Respective annual premiums

> For permanent partial disability, such as the loss of one eye or one limb, the compensation under PMSBY is ₹1 Lakh.

### Q2

If an individual maintains multiple savings bank accounts in different banks, under how many accounts can they enroll and claim benefits under PMSBY?

- [x] One account only
- [ ] Up to two accounts
- [ ] All active accounts
- [ ] Unlimited accounts

> A person is eligible to join PMSBY through a single savings bank account only; even if multiple debits take place inadvertently, the claim remains capped at ₹2 Lakh.

### Q3

The Atal Pension Yojana (APY) provides a minimum guaranteed monthly pension starting after attaining the age of:

- [ ] 50 years
- [ ] 55 years
- [x] 60 years
- [ ] 65 years

> Under the Atal Pension Yojana, subscribers receive a guaranteed monthly pension ranging from ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 upon reaching 60 years of age.

---

### Q4

Which of the following causes of demise is NOT covered under the Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)?

- [ ] Road vehicular accident
- [ ] Snakebite leading to death
- [x] Death due to cardiac arrest / natural illness
- [ ] Drowning in a river

> PMSBY covers death and disability arising solely out of accidental injuries; death due to natural diseases, heart attacks, or illnesses is excluded (covered under PMJJBY).
