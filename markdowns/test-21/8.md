# 📌 Non-Cooperation Movement (1920–1922): Genesis, Program & Mass Phase

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **First Mass-Based Movement**: The **Non-Cooperation Movement (NCM)** was Mahatma Gandhi’s **first nationwide, mass-based political movement** against British colonial rule.
- **Launch Date**: Officially launched on **August 01, 1920** (coinciding with the tragic demise of Bal Gangadhar Tilak).
- **INC Resolutions**:
  - **Calcutta Special Session (September 1920)**: Approved the resolution to start the Non-Cooperation Movement under the presidency of **Lala Lajpat Rai**.
  - **Nagpur Annual Session (December 1920)**: Formally **ratified** the movement and defined the comprehensive Non-Cooperation program under the presidency of **C. Vijayaraghavachariar**.
- **Early Localized Precursors**: Preceded by Gandhi's localized regional struggles: **Champaran Satyagraha (1917)** in Bihar, followed in 1918 by the **Ahmedabad Mill Strike** and **Kheda Satyagraha** in Gujarat.
- **Khilafat Integration**: Merged seamlessly with the **Khilafat Movement**, uniting major leaders including **Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, Dr. M. A. Ansari, Dr. Saifuddin Kitchlew, and the Ali Brothers (Shaukat Ali & Mohammad Ali)** under a shared anti-imperialist platform.
- **Core Strategy**: Promoted the **boycott of government schools, colleges, British law courts, and foreign cloth**, while championing constructive programs like **Swadeshi, the Charkha (hand-spinning), national education institutions, and Hindu-Muslim unity**.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Evolution from Regional Satyagrahas to Nationwide Mass Movement

| Phase / Movement | Year & Region | Core Issue Addressed | Historical Nature & Outcome |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Champaran Satyagraha** | **1917** (Bihar) | Exploitative *Tinkathia* indigo farming system | First Civil Disobedience; secured tenure rights and abolition of illegal cesses. |
| **Ahmedabad Mill Strike** | **1918** (Gujarat) | Dispute over plague bonus and wage demand ($35\%$) | First Hunger Strike; arbitrated wage settlement for textile workers. |
| **Kheda Satyagraha** | **1918** (Gujarat) | Demanded revenue remission due to complete crop failure | First Non-Cooperation at local level; revenue collection suspended for poor farmers. |
| **Non-Cooperation Movement** | **1920–1922** (All-India) | Rowlatt Act, Jallianwala Bagh massacre, and Khilafat injustice | **First nationwide mass political campaign** involving peasants, students, workers, and urban middle class. |

---

### 2. Dual Dimensions of the Non-Cooperation Movement

| Program Dimension | Specific Actionable Elements | Intended Political & Economic Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Negative / Boycott Program** | • Surrender of colonial titles and honorary posts (*e.g., Gandhi returned Kaisar-i-Hind*).<br>• Boycott of government educational institutions (schools/colleges).<br>• Boycott of British law courts by lawyers and litigants.<br>• Boycott of legislative council elections under the 1919 Act.<br>• Boycott of foreign goods and picketing of foreign cloth/liquor shops. | Paralyze colonial administration through civil withdrawal of cooperation. |
| **Positive / Constructive Program** | • Promotion of **Charkha** and Khadi hand-spun fabric.<br>• Establishment of national schools and universities (*Jamia Millia Islamia, Kashi Vidyapith, Gujarat Vidyapith*).<br>• Setting up private arbitration *Panchayats* to resolve disputes.<br>• Eradication of untouchability and promotion of Hindu-Muslim communal unity.<br>• Collection for the **Tilak Swaraj Fund** (surpassed ₹1 Crore). | Build indigenous self-reliance (*Swadeshi*) and grassroot organizational discipline. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Historical Event | INC Session / Decision | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Calcutta Session (Sept 1920)** | Approved the initial proposal to launch NCM | Confusing initial approval (Calcutta) with final detailed ratification (Nagpur) |
| **Nagpur Session (Dec 1920)** | Formally **ratified** the NCM program and restructured INC | Mistaking Nagpur for the launch session |
| **Launch Date (1 Aug 1920)** | Movement commenced prior to special session approval | Assuming NCM began only after the December 1920 session |
| **Civil Disobedience (1930) vs. Non-Cooperation (1920)** | • **NCM**: Non-cooperation with administrative machinery<br>• **CDM**: Active defiance of specific colonial laws (*Salt Law*) | Mixing up the core tactics and years of the two nationwide campaigns |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "1 AUG START, CALCUTTA PROPOSES, NAGPUR RATIFIES, KHILAFAT UNITES"**
> - **1 Aug Start** $\rightarrow$ NCM formally began on 1 August 1920
> - **Calcutta Proposes** $\rightarrow$ Special Session approved NCM (Sept 1920)
> - **Nagpur Ratifies** $\rightarrow$ Annual Session detailed and ratified NCM (Dec 1920)
> - **Khilafat Unites** $\rightarrow$ Azad, Ansari, Kitchlew & Ali Brothers allied with Gandhi
