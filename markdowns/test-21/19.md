# 📌 Diphu Pass: The India-Myanmar-China Tri-Junction & McMahon Line

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Location & Tri-Junction**: **Diphu Pass** (also spelled *Diphuk Pass*) is a strategic mountain pass located in the eastern part of **Arunachal Pradesh** (Anjaw district) on the **tri-junction boundary connecting India, Myanmar (Burma), and China (Tibet)**.
- **Border Line Association**: It lies on the eastern sector of the **McMahon Line**, the demarcated border established between British India and Tibet under the **Simla Convention of 1914**.
- **Strategic Significance**: Provides a round-the-year passage across the Mishmi Hills and serves as a vital historical trade and defense approach corridor toward **eastern Assam** (Brahmaputra Valley) and northern Myanmar.
- **Key Border Boundary Definitions**:
  - **McMahon Line**: Boundary line separating **India (Arunachal Pradesh) and China (Tibet)**.
  - **Radcliffe Line**: Boundary line between **India and Pakistan** (as well as India and Bangladesh).
  - **Durand Line**: Boundary line between **Afghanistan and Pakistan** (and a small stretch with India in PoK).
  - **Palk Strait**: Narrow water body separating the Tamil Nadu coast of **India from Sri Lanka**.

### 1. Comparative Matrix: Major International Boundaries of India

| Border / Boundary Line | Connecting Countries / Regions | Historical Origin / Commission | Key Geographic Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **McMahon Line** | **India (Arunachal Pradesh) & China (Tibet)** | Negotiated by **Sir Henry McMahon** at the **Simla Accord (1914)**. | • Extends from Bhutan to Diphu Pass.<br>• Includes strategic passes: **Bum La, Sela, Diphu Pass**.<br>• China disputes this alignment. |
| **Radcliffe Line** | **India & Pakistan** / **India & Bangladesh** | Drawn by **Sir Cyril Radcliffe** (Boundary Commission, **17 August 1947**). | • Divides Punjab and Bengal.<br>• Covers high-security desert, riverine, and marshland borders. |
| **Durand Line** | **Afghanistan & Pakistan** (and India in PoK - Wakhan corridor) | Established by **Sir Mortimer Durand** and Afghan Amir Abdur Rahman (**1893**). | • Cuts across the Pashtun tribal heartland.<br>• Shortest Indian border stretch (~106 km with Afghanistan in PoK). |
| **Palk Strait & Gulf of Mannar**| **India (Tamil Nadu) & Sri Lanka** | Named after **Robert Palk**, Governor of Madras Presidency (1755–1763). | • Marine strait connecting the Bay of Bengal with Palk Bay.<br>• Home to Adam's Bridge (*Ram Setu*). |
