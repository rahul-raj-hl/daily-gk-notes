# 📌 Ancient & Early Medieval Indian Literature: Court Poets & Royal Patrons

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Bhavabhūti**: Renowned 8th-century Sanskrit dramatist and poet who served as the **court poet of King Yashovarman of Kannauj**; celebrated for masterpieces like *Uttararamacharita*, *Malatimadhava*, and *Mahaviracharita*, often compared to Kalidasa.
- **Bhāravi**: 6th-century classical Sanskrit poet famous for composing the epic *Mahakavya* **Kirātārjunīya**; served at the court of the Pallava king **Simhavishnu** (Avanisimha) at Kanchipuram as well as Western Ganga king **Durvinita**.
- **Rājaśekhara**: 9th–10th century Sanskrit poet, dramatist, and rhetorician; acted as the **guru, mentor, and court minister to Gurjara-Pratihara kings Mahendrapala I and Mahipala I**; authored the literary guide **Kāvyamīmāṃsā**, *Karpuramanjari*, and *Balaramayana*.
- **Ravikīrti**: Eminent Jain poet and the court scholar of the Badami Chalukya emperor **Pulakeshin II**; composed the famous **Aihole Inscription (Aihole Prashasti)** in 634 CE celebrating the defeat of Harshavardhana on the banks of the Narmada.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Scholar / Text | Common Exam Trap / Confusion | Correct Historical Attribution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bhavabhūti vs. Banabhatta** | Confusing the court poet of Yashovarman with the court poet of Harsha | • **Banabhatta**: Harsha's court (*Kadambari*)<br>• **Bhavabhuti**: Yashovarman's court (*Uttararamacharita*) |
| **Bhāravi vs. Bhartrihari** | Mixing up the epic poet with the author of the Shatakas | • **Bharavi**: *Kiratarjuniya*<br>• **Bhartrihari**: *Niti-Sataka*, *Vairagya-Sataka* |
| **Rajashekhara vs. Rajatarangini** | Assuming Rajashekhara wrote Rajatarangini | • **Rajashekhara**: *Kavyamimamsa*<br>• **Kalhana**: *Rajatarangini* (Chronicle of Kashmir) |
| **Aihole Inscription Attribution** | Mistaking the subject ruler for the author | Composed by **Ravikirti**, describing **Pulakeshin II** |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "BHAV-YASHO, BHAR-SIMHA, RAJA-PRATI, RAVI-PULA"**
> - **Bhav-Yasho** $\rightarrow$ **Bhavabhūti** was in the court of **Yashovarman**
> - **Bhar-Simha** $\rightarrow$ **Bhāravi** was patronized by **Simhavishnu**
> - **Raja-Prati** $\rightarrow$ **Rājaśekhara** graced the **Gurjara-Pratihara** court
> - **Ravi-Pula** $\rightarrow$ **Ravikīrti** composed the Aihole inscription for **Pulakeshin II**
