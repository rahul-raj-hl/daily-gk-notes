# 📌 Prehistoric Agriculture: Mehrgarh & Harappan Civilisation

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **First Evidence of Cotton**: **Mehrgarh** (located on the Kachi plain of Balochistan, Pakistan) has yielded the **earliest known evidence of cotton cultivation** in the world, dating back to the Neolithic period (~5000 BCE).
- **Earliest Farming Settlement**: Mehrgarh represents the earliest agricultural settlement in the Indian subcontinent (dating back to ~7000–6500 BCE), documenting the critical evolutionary transition from **food-gathering to food-producing (agriculture and pastoralism)**.
- **Earliest Cereals**: The site has provided the earliest archaeological evidence of cultivated **wheat and barley**, alongside the domestication of **sheep, goats, and cattle/buffalo**.
- **Indus Valley Civilisation (IVC) Link**: Mehrgarh is regarded as a major precursor and proto-urban developmental phase that led to the Mature Harappan Civilisation.
- **Harappan Urban Hallmarks**: Grid-pattern town planning, sophisticated underground covered drainage systems, standardized burnt bricks, and extensive overseas maritime trade with **Mesopotamia (Meluhha), Afghanistan, and Iran**.

| Archaeological Site | Geographical Location | Archaeological Period | Major Discoveries & Agricultural Remains |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Chirand** | **Saran (Bihar)** | **Neolithic** | Extensive bone and antler tools, sericulture/silk traces, cereal grains |

### 2. Major Harappan Agricultural & Urban Sites Comparison

| Harappan Site | Location / River | Key Agricultural / Economic Finds | Distinctive Urban Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kalibangan** | Rajasthan (Ghaggar River) | **Ploughed field surface** (furrow marks), mixed cropping (mustard & chickpea) | Fire altars, wooden drainage, camel bones |
| **Lothal** | Gujarat (Bhogava River) | **Rice husks / grain impressions**, terracotta model of a ship | **Tidal Brick Dockyard**, bead factory, Persian Gulf seal |
| **Rakhigarhi** | Haryana (Ghaggar-Hakra) | Granary complex, barley and wheat cultivation | **Largest Indian site of IVC**; extensive cemetery |
| **Banawali** | Haryana (Rangoi/Ghaggar) | High quality **Barley grains**, terracotta toy plough | Radial layout; lack of systematic grid streets |
| **Mohenjo-daro** | Sindh, Pakistan (Indus River) | **Woven cotton textile fragment** (dyed madder) | Great Bath, Great Granary, Bronze Dancing Girl |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Cultivation / Site Feature | Archaeological Site | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Earliest Cotton Cultivation (Neolithic)** | **Mehrgarh** | Confusing early prehistoric cultivation (Mehrgarh) with Harappan sites |
| **Woven Cotton Cloth Fragment** | **Mohenjo-daro** | Confusing raw cotton farming (Mehrgarh) with manufactured fabric trace (Mohenjo-daro) |
| **Earliest Ploughed Agricultural Field** | **Kalibangan** | Selecting Mehrgarh or Lothal instead of Kalibangan |
| **Tidal Dockyard & Rice Husk** | **Lothal** | Assuming Lothal is in Rajasthan (it is in Gujarat) |
| **Largest IVC Site in India** | **Rakhigarhi** | Confusing Dholavira with Rakhigarhi for largest area in India |

> **Mnemonic: "MEHRGARH MAKES COTTON & CORN, LOTHAL LAUNCHES SHIPS AT DAWN"**
> - **Mehrgarh Makes Cotton & Corn** $\rightarrow$ Mehrgarh yielded earliest **Cotton**, **Wheat**, and **Barley**
> - **Lothal Launches Ships** $\rightarrow$ Lothal has the tidal **Dockyard** and early rice remains
