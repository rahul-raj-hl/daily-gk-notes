# 📌 European Cities: Capitals, Non-Capitals & Geographic Profiles

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Riga**: The **capital and largest city of Latvia**, situated on both banks of the **Daugava (Western Dvina) River** near the Gulf of Riga; former major **Hanseatic League** hub renowned for having Europe's finest collection of **Art Nouveau architecture**.
- **Zagreb**: The **capital of Croatia** (located in northwestern Croatia), characterized by 18th- and 19th-century Austro-Hungarian architecture, the Gothic twin-spired **Zagreb Cathedral**, and the 13th-century **St. Mark’s Church**.
- **Rotterdam**: Major port city and the second-largest city in the **Netherlands** (situated in the Randstad metropolitan region); globally famed for its maritime infrastructure and modern architecture, but **NOT the capital city** (Amsterdam is the official capital; The Hague is the seat of government).
- **Zurich**: The largest city and premier global financial/banking center of **Switzerland**, situated at the northern end of **Lake Zurich**; **NOT the capital city** (Bern serves as the *de facto* federal capital of Switzerland).

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Country | Actual Capital | Common Exam Trap City | Key Distinguishing Factor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Switzerland** | **Bern** | **Zurich** | Zurich is the largest city and economic/banking engine, but Bern is the political capital. |
| **Netherlands** | **Amsterdam** | **Rotterdam** | Rotterdam is the chief maritime port; The Hague hosts Parliament/ICJ; Amsterdam is the constitutional capital. |
| **Latvia vs. Lithuania vs. Estonia**| • **Latvia**: Riga<br>• **Lithuania**: Vilnius<br>• **Estonia**: Tallinn | Mixing up the three Baltic capitals | Remember Baltic order from North to South: **Estonia (Tallinn) $\rightarrow$ Latvia (Riga) $\rightarrow$ Lithuania (Vilnius)**. |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "RIGA & ZAGREB WEAR THE CROWN, ZURICH & ROTTERDAM HOLD THE TOWN"**
> - **Riga & Zagreb** $\rightarrow$ True national capitals (Latvia & Croatia)
> - **Zurich & Rotterdam** $\rightarrow$ Mega-cities / Economic hubs that are **NOT** capitals (Bern & Amsterdam are the capitals)

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which of the following pairs of 'Country – Capital City' is correctly matched?

- [ ] Switzerland – Zurich
- [x] Latvia – Riga
- [ ] Netherlands – Rotterdam
- [ ] Australia – Sydney

> Riga is the capital of Latvia. The capital of Switzerland is Bern (not Zurich), the capital of the Netherlands is Amsterdam (not Rotterdam), and the capital of Australia is Canberra (not Sydney).

The city of Riga, known for having one of the finest collections of Art Nouveau architecture in Europe, is located on the banks of which river?

- [ ] Danube River
- [ ] Rhine River
- [x] Daugava River
- [ ] Vistula River

> Riga lies on both banks of the Daugava River (Western Dvina), approximately 15 km above its mouth on the Gulf of Riga.
