# 📌 Institute of Economic Growth (IEG): New Director & Institutional Profile

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **New Director Appointed**: **Professor Sabyasachi Kar** was appointed as the Director of the **Institute of Economic Growth (IEG)**, New Delhi.
- **Succession**: He succeeds **Professor Chetan Ghate** and previously served as the **RBI Chair Professor** at IEG.
- **Institutional Mandate**: Founded in **1952** by renowned economist **V. K. R. V. Rao**, IEG is a premier autonomous socio-economic research think tank located in the University of Delhi Enclave.
- **Capacity Building Role**: IEG is officially entrusted with conducting the mandatory induction and foundational training program for probationers of the **Indian Economic Service (IES)**.
- **Academic & Policy Profile**: Prof. Kar is an expert in macroeconomics, growth modeling, and political economy, having served on the **MoSPI Committee on Regional Accounts** and consulted with **NITI Aayog, the Planning Commission, and the World Bank**.
