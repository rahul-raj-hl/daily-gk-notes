# 📌 Delhi Sultanate: Dynastic Chronology & Reign Durations

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Longest Ruling Dynasty**: The **Tughlaq Dynasty** ruled for the longest duration in the history of the Delhi Sultanate (**94 years**, from **1320 to 1414 CE**).
- **Shortest Ruling Dynasty**: The **Khilji Dynasty** had the shortest reign (**30 years**, from **1290 to 1320 CE**).
- **Total Span of Delhi Sultanate**: Spanned **320 years** (from **1206 CE** to **1526 CE**), concluding with Babur defeating Ibrahim Lodhi in the First Battle of Panipat.
- **Dynastic Lineage Order**:
  1. **Mamluk / Slave Dynasty** (1206–1290 CE) $\rightarrow$ 84 years
  2. **Khilji Dynasty** (1290–1320 CE) $\rightarrow$ 30 years
  3. **Tughlaq Dynasty** (1320–1414 CE) $\rightarrow$ **94 years** *(Longest)*
  4. **Sayyid Dynasty** (1414–1451 CE) $\rightarrow$ 37 years
  5. **Lodhi Dynasty** (1451–1526 CE) $\rightarrow$ 75 years
- **Ethnic Origins**: The first four dynasties were of **Turkic** (or Turk-Indian) origin; the Lodhi Dynasty was the **first Afghan (Pashtun)** dynasty to rule Delhi.

### 2. Ranking by Duration of Rule (Descending Order)

| Rank | Dynasty | Duration | Key Historical Milestones |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1st** | **Tughlaq Dynasty** | **94 Years** | Maximum territorial expansion (Muhammad bin Tughlaq), canal irrigation network & public works (Firoz Shah Tughlaq), Timur's invasion (1398). |
| **2nd** | **Slave (Mamluk) Dynasty** | **84 Years** | Foundation of the Sultanate, construction of Qutub Minar, introduction of Iqta system and Chahalgani (Corps of Forty). |
| **3rd** | **Lodhi Dynasty** | **75 Years** | Foundation of Agra city (Sikandar Lodhi in 1504), introduction of Gaz-i-Sikandari, end of Delhi Sultanate (1526). |
| **4th** | **Sayyid Dynasty** | **37 Years** | Established following the aftermath of Timur's sack of Delhi; maintained nominal Timurid vassalage. |
| **5th** | **Khilji Dynasty** | **30 Years** | Market control regulations, price fixation, military branding (*Dagh* and *Chehra*), Deccan conquests under Malik Kafur. |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "GUL-KHILE-TO-SAIYAN-LODI (Order of Dynasties)"**
> - **Gul** $\rightarrow$ Ghulam (Slave / Mamluk) Dynasty (1206–1290)
> - **Khile** $\rightarrow$ Khilji Dynasty (1290–1320)
> - **To** $\rightarrow$ **Tughlaq Dynasty (1320–1414) — LONGEST**
> - **Saiyan** $\rightarrow$ Sayyid Dynasty (1414–1451)
> - **Lodi** $\rightarrow$ Lodhi Dynasty (1451–1526)
