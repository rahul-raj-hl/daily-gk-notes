# 📌 Panchayati Raj Institutions: 73rd Amendment & the 11th Schedule

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Total Functional Subjects**: The **11th Schedule** of the Indian Constitution entrusts **29 functional subjects/items** to the Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs).
- **Constitutional Enactment**: Inserted into the Constitution via the **73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992** (came into force on **24 April 1993**, celebrated annually as *National Panchayati Raj Day*).
- **Enabling Constitutional Article**: Governed by **Article 243G**, which empowers State Legislatures to endow Panchayats with powers and authority to function as institutions of self-government.
- **Scope of Functions**: Covers vital rural development sectors including **agriculture, minor irrigation, animal husbandry, rural housing, drinking water, roads, poverty alleviation programs, primary education, markets, and public distribution systems (PDS)**.
- **Companion Schedule (Urban)**: The **12th Schedule** (added by the **74th Amendment Act, 1992**) contains **18 functional subjects** entrusted to Urban Local Bodies (Municipalities) under **Article 243W**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Structural Architecture of the 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992

| Constitutional Dimension | Specific Provision / Article | Key Detail & Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New Part Added** | **Part IX** (*"The Panchayats"*) | Articles **243 to 243O** |
| **New Schedule Added** | **11th Schedule** | Contains **29 functional items** |
| **Devolution of Powers** | **Article 243G** | Powers, authority, and responsibilities of Panchayats |
| **Three-Tier Structure** | **Article 243B** | 1. Gram Panchayat (Village Level)<br>2. Panchayat Samiti (Block/Intermediate Level)<br>3. Zilla Parishad (District Level) *(States with population $< 20$ lakh may omit intermediate tier)* |
| **Gram Sabha** | **Article 243A** | Foundation of PRI; body consisting of all registered voters in the village |
| **Elections & SEC** | **Article 243K** | State Election Commission conducts local body polls |
| **Finances & SFC** | **Article 243I** | State Finance Commission constituted every 5 years by the Governor |
| **Reservation of Seats** | **Article 243D** | Mandatory $\ge 33\%$ ($1/3\text{rd}$) reservation for women; proportional seats for SCs and STs |

### 2. Representative Functional Subjects of the 11th Schedule (29 Subjects)

| Category | Representative Subjects / Items |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Agriculture & Natural Resources** | Agriculture (including agricultural extension), Land improvement & soil conservation, Minor irrigation, water management & watershed development, Animal husbandry, dairying & poultry, Fisheries, Social forestry & farm forestry, Minor forest produce. |
| **Rural Infrastructure** | Small scale industries (including food processing), Khadi, village & cottage industries, Rural housing, Drinking water, Fuel & fodder, Roads, culverts, bridges, ferries & other means of communication, Rural electrification (including distribution). |
| **Social & Educational Welfare** | Non-conventional energy sources, Poverty alleviation programmes, Primary & secondary education, Technical training & vocational education, Adult & non-formal education, Libraries, Cultural activities. |
| **Health, Markets & Distribution** | Markets and fairs, Health and sanitation (including hospitals, PHCs & dispensaries), Family welfare, Women & child development, Social welfare (including welfare of handicapped & mentally retarded), Welfare of weaker sections (SCs/STs), Public Distribution System (PDS), Maintenance of community assets. |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "29 FOR THE VILLAGE (11th), 18 FOR THE CITY (12th)"**
> - **11th Schedule** $\rightarrow 2 + 9 = 11 \rightarrow$ **29 Subjects** for Panchayats
> - **12th Schedule** $\rightarrow 18$ Subjects for Municipalities
> - **Article 243G** $\rightarrow$ **G** for **Gram** Panchayat powers (29 items)
> - **Article 243W** $\rightarrow$ **W** for **Ward** / Municipality powers (18 items)
