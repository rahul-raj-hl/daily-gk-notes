# 📌 Shaurya Van: Military Memorial at Jaisalmer & Defence Reforms

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Inauguration Venue**: **Shaurya Van** was inaugurated by Union Defence Minister Rajnath Singh in **Jaisalmer, Rajasthan**[cite: 1].
- **Memorial Complex**: Located within the **Thar Desert**, Shaurya Van is integrated into the **Jaisalmer War Museum** (commemorating the historic **1971 Indo-Pak War** and the Battle of Longewala)[cite: 1].
- **Core Purpose**: Dedicated to the bravery, supreme sacrifices, and valour of the Indian Armed Forces, featuring dedicated memorial groves and an immersive **light-and-sound show**[cite: 1].
- **Defence Procurement Manual (DPM) 2025**: Released by the Ministry of Defence to streamline capital and revenue procurements, enhance **indigenisation (*Aatmanirbharta*)**, expedite decision-making, and boost participation of **MSMEs and domestic start-ups** in defence manufacturing[cite: 1].

### 2. Major Defence Procurement Manual (DPM) Reforms

| Reform Area | Strategic Objective & Core Provision |
| :--- | :--- |
| **MSME & Start-up Push** | Lowers entry barriers and encourages domestic micro, small, and medium enterprises to participate in supply chains[cite: 1]. |
| **Indigenisation Mandate** | Prioritizes domestic design, development, and manufacturing (*Make in India* in Defence)[cite: 1]. |
| **PAC Procurement** | Simplified rules for **Proprietary Article Certificate (PAC)** procurements to reduce acquisition timelines[cite: 1]. |
| **Streamlined Approvals** | Eliminates redundant procedural tiers to accelerate vendor payments and contract execution[cite: 1]. |
