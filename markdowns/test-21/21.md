# 📌 National Population Policy (NPP 2000): Targets, TFR & Demographic Transition

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **First in the World**: India became the **first country in the world** to launch a nationwide **National Family Planning Programme** in **1952**.
- **National Population Policy (NPP) 2000**: Announced in February 2000 under the chairmanship of Dr. M. S. Swaminathan (drafting committee) to address unmet needs for contraception, healthcare infrastructure, and child survival.
- **Replacement-Level Fertility**: Defined as a **Total Fertility Rate (TFR) of 2.1**, which is the average number of children per woman required to keep population size constant without migration.
- **Long-Term Objective**: Achieve a **stable population by 2045** at a level consistent with sustainable economic growth and environmental protection.
- **Current Fertility Status**: According to **NFHS-5 (2019–21)**, India's national TFR declined to **2.0** (below the replacement level of 2.1).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Three-Tier Objectives of NPP 2000

| Objective Tier | Target Horizon | Specific Core Focus & Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Immediate Objective** | Short-term | Meet the unmet needs for contraception, health infrastructure, and deploy integrated service delivery for basic reproductive and child healthcare. |
| **Medium-Term Objective** | By 2010 | Bring the Total Fertility Rate (TFR) down to **replacement level (TFR = 2.1)** through vigorous implementation of inter-sectoral operational strategies. |
| **Long-Term Objective** | **By 2045** | Achieve a **stable population** at a level consistent with the requirements of sustainable economic development, social development, and environmental protection. |

---

### 2. Major Demographic Indicators & National Targets

| Demographic Indicator | Definition | NPP 2000 Target / Benchmark | Current Level (NFHS-5) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Fertility Rate (TFR)** | Average number of children born to a woman during her reproductive lifetime ($15–49$ years). | **2.1** (Replacement Level) | **2.0** *(Urban: 1.6, Rural: 2.1)* |
| **Infant Mortality Rate (IMR)** | Deaths of children under 1 year per 1,000 live births. | **Below 30** per 1,000 live births | **35.2** per 1,000 live births |
| **Maternal Mortality Ratio (MMR)** | Maternal deaths per 100,000 live births due to pregnancy-related causes. | **Below 100** per 100,000 live births | **97** per 100,000 live births (SRS) |
| **Institutional Deliveries** | Percentage of births delivered in health facilities. | **$80\%$** | **$88.6\%$** |
| **Institutional Immunization** | Universal immunization of children against vaccine-preventable diseases. | **$100\%$** | **$76.6\%$** (Full immunization) |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Term / Year | Correct Metric | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Population Stabilization Target Year** | **2045** | Confusing 2045 with 2030, 2050, or 2070 |
| **Replacement Level TFR Value** | **2.1** | Confusing replacement rate (2.1) with zero fertility or 2.0 |
| **Family Planning Launch Year** | **1952** | Reading 1959 due to printing typos in some question papers |
| **NPP 2000 Medium vs. Long Term** | • Medium: TFR 2.1 by 2010<br>• Long: Population stabilization by 2045 | Mixing up medium-term goals with long-term goals |
