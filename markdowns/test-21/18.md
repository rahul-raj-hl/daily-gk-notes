# 📌 Exercise Ocean Sky: Multinational Air Combat Exercises & IAF Deployment

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Assessment**: **Statement I is incorrect, but Statement II is correct**.
- **Host Nation & Venue**: Exercise Ocean Sky is **not hosted by India**; it is organized and hosted by the **Spanish Air and Space Force (*Ejército del Aire y del Espacio*)** at the **Gando Air Base** located in the **Canary Islands, Spain**.
- **Exercise Objectives**: Aims to enhance interoperability, air combat proficiency, and tactical cooperation among participating air forces through realistic simulations of:
  - **Defensive Counter-Air (DCA)** operations.
  - **Offensive Counter-Air (OCA)** missions.
  - Tactical electronic warfare, network-centric operations, and multi-domain warfare.
- **Indian Air Force (IAF) Participation**: The IAF deploys frontline assets including **Su-30MKI air superiority fighters**, along with combat pilots, technical specialists, aerial refuelling aircraft (*IL-78*), and mission analysts.
- **Strategic Importance**: Simulates complex, large-scale coalition air campaign scenarios in an oceanic airspace environment to test joint command, control, and tactical execution against cutting-edge NATO standard platforms.
