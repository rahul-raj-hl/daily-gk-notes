# 📌 Refraction of Light: Optical Density, Bending Rules & Prisms

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Definition of Refraction**: The phenomenon of the **bending of a light ray** as it passes obliquely from one transparent optical medium into another due to a change in its speed of propagation.
- **Rarer to Denser Medium**: When light travels from an optically rarer (less dense) medium to an optically denser medium, it slows down and **bends towards the normal**—making the **angle of refraction ($r$) smaller than the angle of incidence ($i$)** ($r < i$).
- **Denser to Rarer Medium**: When light travels from an optically denser medium to an optically rarer medium, it speeds up and **bends away from the normal**—making the **angle of refraction ($r$) greater than the angle of incidence ($i$)** ($r > i$).
- **Factors Determining Bending**: The extent of bending depends directly on:
  1. The **angle of incidence ($i$)** at the interface boundary.
  2. The **relative refractive index ($n$)** (optical density ratio) between the two media.
- **Prism & Dispersion**: A prism is a transparent optical element with flat, polished surfaces cut at precise angles; it separates composite white light into its seven constituent spectral colors (**VIBGYOR**) via wavelength-dependent differential refraction (**dispersion**).

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Refraction Dynamics Across Medium Interfaces

| Optical Transition | Velocity of Light | Direction of Bending | Angular Relationship | Everyday Manifestation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Rarer $\rightarrow$ Denser**<br>*(e.g., Air $\rightarrow$ Glass / Water)* | Decreases ($v_2 < v_1$) | **Bends towards the normal** | $\angle r < \angle i$ | A straight straw appearing bent when submerged in a water beaker. |
| **Denser $\rightarrow$ Rarer**<br>*(e.g., Glass / Water $\rightarrow$ Air)* | Increases ($v_2 > v_1$) | **Bends away from the normal** | $\angle r > \angle i$ | A swimming pool floor appearing shallower than its actual depth. |
| **Normal Incidence**<br>*(Ray along the normal, $\angle i = 0^\circ$)* | Changes velocity only | **No deviation / No bending** | $\angle r = 0^\circ$ | Light ray passes straight through undeflected. |

---

### 2. Fundamental Laws of Refraction & Optical Principles

| Optical Law / Principle | Formula / Mathematical Expression | Core Scientific Significance |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **First Law of Refraction** | — | The incident ray, the refracted ray, and the normal to the interface at the point of incidence all lie in the **same plane**. |
| **Snell's Law (Second Law)** | $$\frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \frac{n_2}{n_1} = \,^1n_2$$ | The ratio of the sine of the angle of incidence to the sine of the angle of refraction is constant for a given pair of media and color of light. |
| **Absolute Refractive Index ($n$)** | $$n = \frac{c}{v}$$ | Ratio of the speed of light in vacuum ($c \approx 3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$) to the speed of light in the given medium ($v$). |
| **Dispersion of Light** | $\text{Bending} \propto \frac{1}{\lambda}$ | Shorter wavelengths (**Violet**) refract and bend the most; longer wavelengths (**Red**) refract and bend the least. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Optical Quantity | During Refraction (Rarer to Denser) | During Refraction (Denser to Rarer) | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frequency ($f$)** | **Remains Constant** | **Remains Constant** | Assuming frequency changes with changing medium |
| **Speed of Light ($v$)** | **Decreases** | **Increases** | Confusing speed changes with frequency |
| **Wavelength ($\lambda$)** | **Decreases** | **Increases** | Forgetting $\lambda$ is directly proportional to speed ($v$) |
| **Angle of Refraction ($r$)**| **Smaller than $i$** ($r < i$) | **Greater than $i$** ($r > i$) | Swapping the angular relationships between media |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "R-D-T (Rarer to Denser = Towards), D-R-A (Denser to Rarer = Away)"**
> - **R $\rightarrow$ D $\rightarrow$ T** $\rightarrow$ **R**arer to **D**enser bends **T**owards the normal ($r < i$)
> - **D $\rightarrow$ R $\rightarrow$ A** $\rightarrow$ **D**enser to **R**arer bends **A**way from the normal ($r > i$)
> - **ROYGBIV / VIBGYOR** $\rightarrow$ **V**iolet bends maximum, **R**ed bends minimum

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

When a beam of light travels from an optically rarer medium into an optically denser medium:

- [ ] Its speed increases and it bends away from the normal
- [x] Its speed decreases and its angle of refraction is smaller than the angle of incidence
- [ ] Its frequency decreases and it travels straight without bending
- [ ] Its wavelength increases and it bends away from the normal

> In transitioning from a rarer to a denser medium, light slows down and bends toward the normal line, resulting in an angle of refraction that is smaller than the angle of incidence ($r < i$).

### PYQ 2

Which of the following properties of a light wave does NOT change when light undergoes refraction from one medium to another?

- [ ] Velocity
- [ ] Wavelength
- [x] Frequency
- [ ] Amplitude

> Frequency is an intrinsic characteristic of the light source emitting the photons; hence, it remains completely invariant when transitioning across different media.

### PYQ 3

The splitting of white light into a band of seven distinct colors upon passing through a triangular glass prism is known as:

- [ ] Total internal reflection
- [ ] Diffraction
- [x] Dispersion
- [ ] Polarization

> Dispersion is the phenomenon where polychromatic light splits into its component wavelengths because each color travels at a different speed and undergoes a different angle of deviation in glass.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

When a ray of light passes from water into air at an oblique angle, the refracted ray:

- [x] Bends away from the normal line with an angle of refraction greater than the angle of incidence
- [ ] Bends towards the normal line with an angle of refraction smaller than the angle of incidence
- [ ] Continues along the identical path with no change in direction
- [ ] Bends at an angle of $90^\circ$ irrespective of the angle of incidence

> Water is optically denser than air; therefore, as the light ray emerges into air, it speeds up and bends away from the normal ($\angle r > \angle i$).

### Q2

According to Snell's Law, the refractive index of medium 2 with respect to medium 1 ($^1n_2$) is expressed as:

- [ ] $\frac{\cos i}{\cos r}$
- [x] $\frac{\sin i}{\sin r}$
- [ ] $\frac{\tan i}{\tan r}$
- [ ] $\sin i \times \sin r$

> Snell's Law states that for light of a given wavelength, the ratio of the sine of the angle of incidence to the sine of the angle of refraction is equal to the relative refractive index ($\frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \frac{n_2}{n_1}$).

### Q3

Which color in the visible white light spectrum experiences the least amount of deviation when refracted through a triangular prism?

- [ ] Violet
- [ ] Blue
- [ ] Yellow
- [x] Red

> Red light has the longest wavelength in the visible spectrum and travels faster in glass than violet, resulting in the lowest refractive index and the least deviation.

### Q4

What happens to a light beam when it strikes the interface between two transparent media perpendicularly ($\angle i = 0^\circ$)?

- [ ] It bends at an angle of $45^\circ$
- [ ] It is completely dispersed into rainbow colors
- [x] It enters the second medium undeflected without any change in direction
- [ ] It undergoes total internal reflection back into the first medium

> For normal incidence ($\angle i = 0^\circ$), applying Snell's Law yields $\sin r = 0 \implies \angle r = 0^\circ$, meaning the light passes straight through without bending.
