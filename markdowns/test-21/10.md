# 📌 Indian Gallantry Awards: Order of Precedence & Defence Honours

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Highest Gallantry Award**: The **Param Vir Chakra (PVC)** is the **highest award in the order of precedence** for military gallantry services in India, conferred for the most conspicuous bravery in the presence of the enemy during wartime.
- **Classification of Gallantry Awards**:
  - **Wartime Gallantry Awards** (Instituted on 26 January 1950, deemed effective from 15 August 1947): **Param Vir Chakra (PVC)**, **Maha Vir Chakra (MVC)**, and **Vir Chakra (VrC)**.
  - **Peacetime Gallantry Awards** (Instituted on 4 January 1952, renamed in 1967): **Ashoka Chakra (AC)**, **Kirti Chakra (KC)**, and **Shaurya Chakra (SC)**.
- **Official Order of Precedence (Combined)**:
  1. **Param Vir Chakra** (Rank 1)
  2. **Ashoka Chakra** (Rank 2)
  3. **Maha Vir Chakra** (Rank 3)
  4. **Kirti Chakra** (Rank 4)
  5. **Vir Chakra** (Rank 5)
  6. **Shaurya Chakra** (Rank 6)
- **Announcement Schedule**: Gallantry awards are announced **twice a year**—on **Republic Day (26 January)** and **Independence Day (15 August)**.
- **First Recipient of Param Vir Chakra**: **Major Somnath Sharma** (4th Battalion, Kumaon Regiment) posthumously for his heroics in the 1947 Battle of Badgam (Kashmir).

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Comparative Hierarchy: Wartime vs. Peacetime Gallantry Awards

| Category Tier | Wartime Gallantry Award *(In the face of the enemy)* | Peacetime Gallantry Award *(Other than in the face of the enemy)* | Order of Precedence Ranking |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Tier 1 (Highest)** | **Param Vir Chakra (PVC)** | **Ashoka Chakra (AC)** *(Equivalent to PVC)* | **1st** (PVC) $\rightarrow$ **2nd** (AC) |
| **Tier 2 (Second Highest)** | **Maha Vir Chakra (MVC)** | **Kirti Chakra (KC)** *(Equivalent to MVC)* | **3rd** (MVC) $\rightarrow$ **4th** (KC) |
| **Tier 3 (Third Highest)** | **Vir Chakra (VrC)** | **Shaurya Chakra (SC)** *(Equivalent to VrC)* | **5th** (VrC) $\rightarrow$ **6th** (SC) |

### 2. Design, Symbolism & Historical Evolution

| Feature / Dimension | Param Vir Chakra (PVC) | Ashoka Chakra (AC) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Instituted On** | **26 January 1950** (with effect from 15 Aug 1947) | **4 January 1952** (originally named *Ashoka Chakra, Class I*) |
| **Renamed Year** | Retained original name | Renamed to **Ashoka Chakra** in **1967** |
| **Medal Designer** | **Savitri Khanolkar** (Eve Yvonne Maday de Maros) | Government of India |
| **Medal Motif / Design**| Circular bronze medal with 4 replicas of **Indra's Vajra** surrounding the State Emblem | Circular gold-gilt medal with **Ashoka Chakra** surrounded by a lotus wreath |
| **Ribbon Color** | Plain **purple** ribbon | **Green** ribbon divided into two equal segments by an **orange vertical line** |
| **Eligibility** | Officers and enlisted personnel across Army, Navy, and Air Force | Military personnel and **civilians** of either sex |
