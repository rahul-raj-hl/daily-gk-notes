# 📌 European Free Trade Association (EFTA) & India-TEPA

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **EFTA Member Nations (4 States)**: **Iceland**, **Liechtenstein**, **Norway**, and **Switzerland**.
- **Historical Establishment**: Founded in **1960** by the **Stockholm Convention** as an alternative economic bloc for European states that chose not to join the European Economic Community (EEC, now the EU).
- **Former Member Fact**: **Sweden** was an original founding member of EFTA in 1960 but **left in 1995** to formally join the **European Union (EU)**.
- **India–EFTA Landmark Deal**: India signed the historic **Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement (TEPA)** with the four EFTA nations.
  - **Key Commitment**: EFTA nations committed to investing **$100 Billion** into India over 15 years, generating approximately **1 Million direct jobs**.
- **Smallest Member**: **Liechtenstein** is the smallest member state by both land area and population (joined in 1991; shares a customs union with Switzerland).

---

### 2. High-Yield Distinctions: EFTA vs. EU vs. EEA

| Entity / Framework | Participating Member Countries | Relationship with the EU Single Market |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EFTA** | **4 Countries**: Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland | Operates external free trade networks globally independent of the EU. |
| **EEA (European Economic Area)**| **30 Countries**: 27 EU Member States + 3 EFTA States (*Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway*) | Grants full access to the EU’s Internal Single Market (Four Freedoms: Goods, Persons, Services, Capital). |
| **Switzerland's Unique Status** | Switzerland rejected EEA membership in a 1992 referendum; participates via **bilateral sector-specific agreements**. |
