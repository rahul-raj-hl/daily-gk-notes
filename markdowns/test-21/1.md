# 📌 Chronology of Key Events: Indian National Movement (1918–1939)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Ahmedabad Mill Strike (1918)**: One of Mahatma Gandhi's earliest mass movements following his return from South Africa; marked his first hunger strike in India over plague bonus disputes.
- **Rowlatt Acts (February 1919)**: Enacted by the Imperial Legislative Council; authorized detention of political suspects without trial and in-camera trials without juries (*"No Dalil, No Vakil, No Appeal"*).
- **Simon Commission (November 1927)**: Appointed by the British Government two years ahead of schedule to review constitutional progress under the 1919 Act; boycotted across India due to being an all-white commission.
- **Bardoli Satyagraha (1928)**: Major peasant protest in Gujarat against unfair revenue hikes; led by **Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel**, where local women conferred upon him the title **"Sardar"**.
- **Gandhi–Irwin Pact (5 March 1931)**: Signed between Mahatma Gandhi and Viceroy Lord Irwin, leading to the suspension of the Civil Disobedience Movement and Congress participation in the **Second Round Table Conference**.
- **Resignation of Congress Ministries (1939)**: In **October–November 1939**, Congress provincial ministries resigned in protest against Viceroy Lord Linlithgow dragging India into World War II without Indian consent.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Historical Event | Exact Year | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Simon Commission Appointment vs. Arrival** | • **Appointed**: Nov 1927<br>• **Arrived in Bombay**: Feb 1928 | Mixing up the appointment year (1927) with its arrival in India (1928) |
| **Gandhi's Early Satyagrahas** | • **Champaran**: 1917 (1st CDM)<br>• **Ahmedabad**: 1918 (1st Hunger Strike)<br>• **Kheda**: 1918 (1st Non-Cooperation) | Confusing the sequential order and exact tool used in each early movement |
| **Gandhi-Irwin Pact vs. Poona Pact** | • **Gandhi-Irwin**: March 1931 (with Viceroy)<br>• **Poona Pact**: Sept 1932 (Gandhi & Ambedkar) | Swapping the signatories and years of the two pacts |
| **Kheda (1918) vs. Bardoli (1928)** | • **Kheda**: 1918 (Post-monsoon crop failure)<br>• **Bardoli**: 1928 (Protest against $22\%$ tax hike) | Mixing up Gujarat's two famous peasant satyagrahas |
