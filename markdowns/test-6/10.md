# 💃 Folk Dances of Himachal Pradesh
🔴 High Yield

## 📖 Overview
• Himachal Pradesh is known for its vibrant folk dances performed during festivals, weddings, religious ceremonies, and social occasions.

### 📖 Nati Dance
• Most popular folk dance of Himachal Pradesh.
↳ Especially famous in Kullu Valley.
• Performed during:
  ↳ Dussehra
  ↳ Weddings
  ↳ Festivals
  ↳ Social celebrations
• NOT exclusive to Shimla Dussehra.
• Recognized in the Guinness Book of World Records for the largest folk dance performance.

### 📖 Kinnauri Dance
• Traditional tribal group dance of Kinnaur district.
• Performed by both men and women.
• Performed during:
  ↳ Religious ceremonies
  ↳ Festivals
  ↳ Social gatherings
• Famous during:
  ↳ Phulaich (Flower Festival)

### 📖 Other Important Folk Dances

**Chham**
• Masked dance performed by Buddhist monks (Lamas).
• Symbolizes victory of good over evil.

**Thoda**
• Martial folk dance associated with traditional archery.

**Gugga Dance**
• Performed in honour of folk deity Gugga Ji.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

❌ Nati is performed **only** during Shimla Dussehra.
✅ Nati is performed across Himachal during festivals, weddings and celebrations.

❌ Chham belongs only to Himachal.
✅ Chham is also performed in Ladakh, Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh and other Himalayan Buddhist regions.
