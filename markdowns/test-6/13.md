# 🚨 Emergency Provisions (Articles 352–360)
🔴 High Yield

## 📖 Overview
• Emergency provisions are contained in **Part XVIII** of the Indian Constitution.
↳ Covered under **Articles 352–360**.
• Three types of Emergencies:
  ↳ National Emergency (Art. 352)
  ↳ President's Rule / Constitutional Emergency (Art. 356)
  ↳ Financial Emergency (Art. 360)

---

## ⭐ Key Points

### 🇮🇳 National Emergency (Article 352)

📌 Grounds
• War
• External Aggression
• Armed Rebellion

⭐ Key Facts
• Declared by the President.
• Must be approved by Parliament within **1 month**.
• No maximum duration (can continue with parliamentary approval every 6 months).
• Centre gets greater control over States.
• Certain Fundamental Rights can be affected.
• Lok Sabha can pass a resolution for revocation.

---

### 🏛️ President's Rule (Article 356)

📌 Ground
• Failure of Constitutional Machinery in a State.

⭐ Key Facts
• Also called **Constitutional Emergency**.
• Must be approved within **2 months**.
• Maximum duration → **3 years**.
• Applies only to the concerned State.
• Fundamental Rights remain unaffected.
• Can be revoked by the President.

---

### 💰 Financial Emergency (Article 360)

📌 Ground
• Threat to India's financial stability or credit.

⭐ Key Facts
• Declared by the President.
• Must be approved within **2 months**.
• No maximum duration.
• Has **never been imposed** in India.
• President may direct reduction of salaries of:
  ↳ Government employees
  ↳ Judges
  ↳ Constitutional authorities
• Fundamental Rights are **not suspended**.

---

## 🎯 Exam Booster

• Article **352** → National Emergency.
• Article **356** → President's Rule.
• Article **360** → Financial Emergency.
• National Emergency is the **only emergency that can affect Fundamental Rights**.
• President's Rule → Maximum **3 years**.
• Financial Emergency → Never declared in India.

## 🔄 Quick Revision

| Feature | National (352) | President's Rule (356) | Financial (360) |
|---------|----------------|-------------------------|-----------------|
| Reason | War / External Aggression / Armed Rebellion | Failure of Constitutional Machinery | Financial Stability Threat |
| Maximum Duration | No limit | 3 Years | No limit |
| Fundamental Rights | Can be affected | Not affected | Not affected |
| Affects | Entire Country / Part | One State | Entire Country |
| Parliament Approval | 1 Month | 2 Months | 2 Months |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

❌ President's Rule affects Fundamental Rights.
✅ Only **National Emergency** can affect certain Fundamental Rights.

❌ Financial Emergency has been declared in India.
✅ **It has never been imposed.**

❌ Article 356 deals with National Emergency.
✅ Article **352** → National Emergency.
✅ Article **356** → President's Rule.
✅ Article **360** → Financial Emergency.

---

## 💡 Memory Trick

**"352 → 356 → 360 = Nation → State → Money"**

- **352** → Nation in danger.
- **356** → State government fails.
- **360** → Money crisis.

# 📝 Related PYQs

### PYQ 1 (SSC CGL)

**Which Emergency can affect Fundamental Rights?**

- [x] National Emergency
- [ ] President's Rule
- [ ] Financial Emergency
- [ ] None

**Why?**
Only **National Emergency (Article 352)** can affect certain Fundamental Rights.

---

### PYQ 2 (SSC CGL)

**Which Article deals with President's Rule?**

- [ ] 352
- [x] 356
- [ ] 360
- [ ] 365

**Why?**
Article **356** provides for President's Rule when constitutional machinery fails in a State.

---

### PYQ 3 (SSC CGL)

**Financial Emergency is provided under:**

- [ ] Article 352
- [ ] Article 356
- [x] Article 360
- [ ] Article 365

**Why?**
Article **360** empowers the President to proclaim a Financial Emergency.

---

### PYQ 4 (Practice)

**Which Emergency has never been imposed in India?**

- [ ] National Emergency
- [ ] President's Rule
- [x] Financial Emergency
- [ ] None

**Why?**
Since the Constitution came into force in **1950**, **Financial Emergency (Article 360)** has never been proclaimed.
