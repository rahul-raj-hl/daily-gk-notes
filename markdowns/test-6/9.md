# 📌 White Revolution 2.0

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **White Revolution 2.0** is a **cooperative-led initiative** to strengthen India's dairy sector.
- Launched by the **Ministry of Cooperation**.
- Focuses on:
  - 🥛 Increasing milk procurement
  - 👩 Women empowerment
  - 🤝 Expanding dairy cooperatives
- Funded under **National Dairy Development Programme (NPDD) 2.0**.

---

## 📖 About the Scheme

*One-liner:* Expanding dairy cooperatives and improving farmers' income.

- Ministry → **Ministry of Cooperation**
- Funding → **NPDD 2.0**
- Target → Cooperative-led dairy development
- Coverage → Across India

---

## 🎯 Objectives

*One-liner:* Increase milk collection and strengthen the dairy cooperative network.

- 🥛 Increase milk procurement by **50% in 5 years**.
- 📈 Target procurement:
  - **1,007 lakh kg/day by 2028–29**
- 🏘️ Establish **75,000 new Dairy Cooperative Societies**.
- 👩 Promote women-led participation in dairy activities.

---

## 👩 Women & Dairy

*One-liner:* Women are the backbone of India's dairy sector.

- Nearly **70% of the dairy workforce** comprises women.
- Scheme aims to:
  - Improve livelihoods.
  - Increase financial inclusion.
  - Strengthen women-led cooperatives.

---

## 🥛 National Dairy Development Programme (NPDD) 2.0

*One-liner:* Supports dairy infrastructure and cooperative strengthening.

- Implemented by the **Department of Animal Husbandry & Dairying**.
- Supports:
  - Milk collection infrastructure
  - Chilling facilities
  - Dairy cooperative development
