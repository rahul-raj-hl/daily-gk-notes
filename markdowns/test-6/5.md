# 📌 Chakri (Jammu & Kashmir)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Chakri** is a popular **folk music tradition** of **Jammu & Kashmir**.
- Known for **storytelling through music**, depicting folk tales, romance, and local events.
- Commonly performed during **weddings, festivals, and cultural celebrations**.
- SSC usually asks **Music Form ↔ State**.

---

## 📖 About Chakri

*One-liner:* Traditional storytelling folk music of Jammu & Kashmir.

- Origin → Jammu & Kashmir
- Type → Folk music
- Theme → Folk tales, romance, history, social events
- Performance → Group singing with traditional instruments
