# 📌 Indus Valley Civilization – Town Planning

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- The **Indus Valley Civilization (IVC)** is famous for its **advanced urban planning**.
- Cities followed a **Grid-pattern layout** with streets intersecting at **right angles**.
- Well-planned **covered drainage system** was one of its greatest achievements.
- Major cities: **Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Dholavira, Lothal, Kalibangan**.

---

## 🏙️ Grid-pattern Layout

*One-liner:* Streets crossed each other at right angles, forming blocks.

- Roads laid in **north-south and east-west** directions.
- Streets intersected at **90°**.
- Easy movement and systematic city planning.
- Seen in **Harappa** and **Mohenjo-daro**.

---

## 🚰 Drainage System

*One-liner:* One of the world's earliest planned sanitation systems.

- Covered underground drains.
- Every house connected to the main drain.
- Inspection holes (manholes) for cleaning.
- Excellent focus on sanitation and hygiene.

---

## 🏛️ City Planning

### 🏰 Citadel

*One-liner:* Raised fortified area for public buildings.

- Administrative and religious structures.
- Granaries
- Great Bath
- Assembly Hall

---

### 🏠 Lower Town

*One-liner:* Residential area for the common people.

- Well-planned houses.
- Courtyards and bathrooms.
- Houses usually opened into side lanes instead of main roads.

---

## 🧱 Standardized Construction

*One-liner:* Uniform baked bricks used throughout the civilization.

- Standard brick ratio: **1 : 2 : 4**
- Used for houses, drains and public buildings.
- Indicates centralized planning.
