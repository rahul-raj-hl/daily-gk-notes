# 📌 Pre-Monsoon Showers in India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Pre-monsoon showers occur **before the Southwest Monsoon** (March–May).
- They are caused by **local convection and thunderstorms**.
- Different regions have different local names.
- Frequently asked in SSC as **Local Name ↔ Region ↔ Crop Benefited**.

---

## 🌧️ Pre-Monsoon Showers

*One-liner:* Local thunderstorms that occur before the arrival of the Southwest Monsoon.

- 📅 Season → March to May
- 🌡️ Caused by intense summer heating and convection.
- 🌦️ Help reduce summer heat and prepare conditions for the monsoon.
- 🌾 Important for agriculture in many regions.

---

## 🥭 Important Local Names

### 🥭 Mango Showers

*One-liner:* Pre-monsoon rainfall that helps mangoes ripen early.

- 📍 Kerala
- 📍 Coastal Karnataka
- 🥭 Helps early ripening of mangoes.

---

### ☕ Coffee (Cherry Blossom) Showers

*One-liner:* Showers beneficial for flowering of coffee plants.

- 📍 Kerala
- 📍 Karnataka
- ☕ Important for coffee plantations.

---

### 🍃 Tea Showers

*One-liner:* Light rainfall beneficial for tea cultivation.

- 📍 Assam
- 🍃 Also benefits jute and rice.

---

### 🌩️ Norwesters (Kal Baisakhi)

*One-liner:* Violent thunderstorms occurring in eastern India before the monsoon.

- 📍 West Bengal
- 📍 Jharkhand
- 📍 Odisha
- 📍 Assam
- 💨 Wind speed may reach **60–80 km/h**.
- 🧊 Often accompanied by hailstorms.
- 🌾 Can damage standing crops.

---

### 🌪️ Bordochila (Bardoli Chheerha)

*One-liner:* Local name for Norwesters in Assam.

- 📍 Assam
- Similar thunderstorm phenomenon as Kal Baisakhi.
