# 📌 Foreign Invasions in Post-Mauryan India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- After the fall of the **Mauryan Empire (185 BCE)**, several foreign dynasties entered north-western India.
- The correct chronological order is:

**Greeks → Shakas → Parthians → Kushans**

---

## 📖 Chronological Order

### 🇬🇷 Indo-Greeks (Greeks)

*One-liner:* First foreign rulers after the Mauryas.

- 📅 Around **180 BCE**
- 👑 Important rulers:
  - Demetrius
  - Menander (Milinda)
- 🌍 Ruled north-western India.
- 📚 Menander is associated with **Milinda Panha**.

---

### 🏹 Shakas (Scythians)

*One-liner:* Defeated the Indo-Greeks and expanded into western India.

- 📅 Around **90 BCE**
- 👑 Important rulers:
  - Maues
  - Azes I
- 🌍 Ruled:
  - Gujarat
  - North-west India
  - Central India

---

### 🛡️ Parthians (Pahlavas)

*One-liner:* Brief rulers between the Shakas and Kushans.

- 📅 Around **20 CE**
- 👑 Important ruler:
  - Gondophares
- 🌍 Controlled parts of north-western India.

---

### 🐎 Kushans

*One-liner:* Built a vast empire linking India with Central Asia.

- 📅 Around **50 CE – 3rd Century CE**
- 👑 Important rulers:
  - Kujula Kadphises
  - Kanishka
- 🌍 Promoted:
  - Silk Route trade
  - Buddhism
  - Gandhara Art
