# 📌 Maritime Security Belt 2025

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Maritime Security Belt 2025** is a **joint naval exercise** conducted by:
  - 🇨🇳 China
  - 🇮🇷 Iran
  - 🇷🇺 Russia
- Conducted in the **Middle East (Gulf of Oman region)**.
- Focuses on **maritime security, anti-piracy, counter-terrorism, and naval cooperation**.

---

## 🌍 About the Exercise

*One-liner:* A trilateral naval exercise to improve maritime cooperation.

- Participants:
  - China
  - Iran
  - Russia
- Region:
  - Gulf of Oman / Northern Indian Ocean
- Nature:
  - Joint naval exercise
- Frequency:
  - Conducted periodically since **2019**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

*One-liner:* Secure important sea routes and strengthen naval coordination.

- 🚢 Combat piracy.
- 🛡️ Counter maritime terrorism.
- ⚓ Improve interoperability among navies.
- 🌍 Protect international shipping lanes.
- 🤝 Strengthen strategic cooperation.

---

## 🌊 Why is it Important?

- Gulf of Oman connects to the **Strait of Hormuz**.
- Major route for:
  - Oil exports
  - Global trade
  - Energy security
- Demonstrates growing defence cooperation among the three nations.
