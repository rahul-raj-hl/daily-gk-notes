# 📌 Kabir

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Kabir (15th Century) was one of the most important saints of the **Bhakti Movement**.
- He preached **Nirguna Bhakti** (worship of a formless God).
- Strongly opposed **caste discrimination, ritualism, idol worship, and religious intolerance**.
- Emphasized **unity of all religions** and equality of all human beings.
- His teachings are preserved in the **Bijak**, **Kabir Granthavali**, and **Guru Granth Sahib**.

---

## 📖 About Kabir

*One-liner:* A poet-saint who united people beyond caste and religion.

- Lived during the **15th century**.
- Believed in one supreme, formless God.
- Criticized blind faith and meaningless rituals.
- Stressed devotion (Bhakti), love, and self-realization.

---

## 🌿 Teachings

### 🤝 Religious Harmony

*One-liner:* Kabir believed all religions ultimately lead to the same God.

- Equality of Hindus and Muslims.
- Rejected communal divisions.
- Promoted universal brotherhood.

> "Hindus and Muslims are two vessels filled with the same wine."

---

### ⚖️ Social Equality

*One-liner:* Birth does not determine a person's worth.

- Opposed caste hierarchy.
- Condemned untouchability.
- Equality of all human beings.

---

### 🙏 Nirguna Bhakti

*One-liner:* Worship of a formless, attribute-less God.

- Rejected idol worship.
- Inner devotion is more important than rituals.
- God resides within every individual.
