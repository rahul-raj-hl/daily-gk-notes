# 🏛️ Pre-Congress Political Associations
🔴 High Yield

## 📖 Overview
• Before the formation of the **Indian National Congress (1885)**, several regional political associations emerged.
• These organizations united educated Indians, promoted political awareness, and laid the foundation for the national movement.

---

## ⭐ Important Associations

### 🏛️ Indian Association
• 📅 Founded → **1876**
• 📍 Place → **Calcutta**
• 👥 Founders:
  ↳ Surendranath Banerjee
  ↳ Ananda Mohan Bose
• 🎯 Significance:
  ↳ One of the earliest all-India political organizations.
  ↳ Prepared the ground for the Indian National Congress.

---

### 🏛️ Poona Sarvajanik Sabha
• 📅 Founded → **1870**
• 📍 Place → **Poona (Pune)**
• 👥 Founder (Exam Focus):
  ↳ Justice M.G. Ranade
• 👥 Other Founder:
  ↳ Ganesh Vasudeo Joshi
• 🎯 Significance:
  ↳ Represented public opinion before the British Government.
  ↳ Worked for political, social and economic reforms.

---

### 🏛️ Madras Mahajan Sabha
• 📅 Founded → **1884**
• 📍 Place → **Madras**
• 👥 Founders:
  ↳ M. Viraraghavachariar
  ↳ G. Subramania Iyer
  ↳ P. Ananda Charlu
• 🎯 Significance:
  ↳ One of the earliest political organizations in South India.
  ↳ Played an important role in the formation of the INC.

---

### 🏛️ Bombay Presidency Association
• 📅 Founded → **1885**
• 📍 Place → **Bombay**
• 👥 Founders:
  ↳ Badruddin Tyabji
  ↳ Pherozeshah Mehta
  ↳ K.T. Telang
• 🎯 Significance:
  ↳ Represented educated Indians of Bombay Presidency.
  ↳ Later became an important supporter of the Indian National Congress.

## ⚠️ Remember

• These organizations existed **before the INC (1885).**
• They united educated Indians and created political awareness.
• They served as the foundation of the Indian National Movement.
• **Indian National Congress was founded in 1885 by A.O. Hume** (with support from Indian leaders).
