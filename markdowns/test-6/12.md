# 👑 Vairamudi Garudotsava
🔴 High Yield

## 📖 Overview
• Vairamudi Garudotsava is a famous annual temple festival of Karnataka.
↳ Celebrated at the **Cheluvanarayana Swamy Temple**, Melukote.

## ⭐ Key Points

### 📍 Location
• Cheluvanarayana Swamy Temple.
↳ Melukote, Mandya district, Karnataka.
• Situated on **Yadugiri (Yadavgiri) Hills**.

### 🛕 Deity
• Lord Cheluvanarayana Swamy.
↳ Also known as **Thirunarayana / Ramapriya**.
• A form of **Lord Vishnu**.

### 📅 Festival
• Celebrated annually.
• Held during **Phalguna (March–April)**.
• Duration:
  ↳ 10–13 days.

### 👑 Highlight
• The deity is adorned with the legendary **Vairamudi** (diamond-studded crown).
↳ Also called **Vajramukuta**.
• Crown is kept in the **Mandya District Treasury**.
• Brought to the temple under heavy security only during the festival.
• Traditionally adorned **only at night** to avoid exposure to daylight.

### 📖 Legend
• Garuda recovered the crown from the demon **Virochana**.
• Brought it from **Kshira Sagara (Milky Ocean)** to Lord Vishnu.

### 🏛️ Temple Facts
• Architecture:
  ↳ Dravidian style.
  ↳ Hoysala & Vijayanagara influences.
• **Ramanujacharya** stayed here for 12 years.
↳ Renovated the temple and revived its traditions.
• Famous water bodies:
  ↳ Kalyani Theertha
  ↳ Akka-Tangi Kola (Sister Ponds)

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

❌ Vairamudi Garudotsava is celebrated in Andhra Pradesh.
✅ It is celebrated in **Melukote, Karnataka**.

❌ The Vairamudi crown is permanently placed on the deity.
✅ It is kept in the **Mandya District Treasury** and brought only during the festival.

❌ The crown is displayed during the day.
✅ Traditionally, it is adorned **only at night**.
