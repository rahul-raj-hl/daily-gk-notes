# 📌 Major Mineral Producing Countries

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- China dominates the production of **Coal** and **Aluminium**.
- Australia is the world's largest producer of **Iron Ore**.
- SSC frequently asks **Mineral ↔ Largest Producer**.
- Also remember a few important exports associated with these countries.

---

## ⛏️ Major Producers

### 🪨 Coal

*One-liner:* Coal is the world's most widely used fossil fuel for electricity generation.

- 🥇 Largest Producer → **China**
- 🥈 Other major producers:
  - India
  - Indonesia
  - USA
  - Australia

---

### 🏗️ Iron Ore

*One-liner:* Iron ore is the primary raw material used in steel production.

- 🥇 Largest Producer → **Australia**
- 🥈 Other major producers:
  - Brazil
  - China
  - India
  - Russia

---

### 🏭 Aluminium

*One-liner:* Aluminium is extracted from **Bauxite** and widely used due to its lightweight nature.

- 🥇 Largest Producer → **China**
- Raw Material → **Bauxite**
- Other major producers:
  - India
  - Russia
  - Canada
  - UAE

---

## 🌍 Country Highlights

### 🇨🇳 China

- Largest producer of **Coal**
- Largest producer of **Aluminium**
- World's biggest steel producer.
- Huge industrial and manufacturing base.

---

### 🇦🇺 Australia

- Largest producer of **Iron Ore**
- Major exporter of:
  - Gold
  - Uranium
  - Bauxite
  - Coal

---

### 🇧🇷 Brazil

- One of the largest producers of **Iron Ore**.
- Major exports:
  - Soybean
  - Coffee
  - Sugar
  - Iron Ore

---

### 🇷🇺 Russia

- Rich in:
  - Coal
  - Natural Gas
  - Oil
  - Nickel
  - Palladium

---

### 🇺🇸 USA

- Major producer of:
  - Natural Gas
  - Petroleum
  - Coal

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Mineral | Remember |
|----------|----------|
| Coal | China |
| Iron Ore | Australia |
| Aluminium | China |
| Bauxite | Ore of Aluminium |
| Steel | Made from Iron Ore |
| Aluminium | Extracted from Bauxite |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

👉 **"CIA"**

- **C** → Coal → **China**
- **I** → Iron Ore → **Australia**
- **A** → Aluminium → **China**

Think:
> **China dominates Coal & Aluminium, Australia dominates Iron Ore.**
