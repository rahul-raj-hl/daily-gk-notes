# 📌 State Executive (Articles 163–166)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Articles **163–166** deal with the **Governor and the State Executive**.
- The **Governor is the constitutional (nominal) head** of the State.
- Executive actions of the State Government are taken **in the name of the Governor**.
- SSC frequently asks **Article ↔ Provision**.

---

## 📖 Important Articles

### 📜 Article 163

*One-liner:* Council of Ministers to aid and advise the Governor.

- Council of Ministers headed by the **Chief Minister**.
- Advises the Governor in exercising executive powers.
- Governor acts independently only in matters where the Constitution grants discretion.

---

### 👤 Article 164

*One-liner:* Appointment of the Chief Minister and other Ministers.

- Chief Minister is appointed by the Governor.
- Other Ministers are appointed on the advice of the Chief Minister.
- Ministers hold office during the **pleasure of the Governor**.
- Council of Ministers is **collectively responsible** to the Legislative Assembly.

---

### ⚖️ Article 165

*One-liner:* Appointment of the Advocate General.

- Advocate General is appointed by the Governor.
- Highest legal officer of the State.
- Holds office during the pleasure of the Governor.
- Must be qualified to be appointed as a High Court Judge.

---

### 🏛️ Article 166

*One-liner:* Conduct of Government Business.

- **166(1)** → All executive actions of the State Government are expressed in the **name of the Governor**.
- **166(2)** → Authentication of orders made in the Governor's name.
- **166(3)** → Governor frames rules for convenient transaction of State Government business.

|----------|----------|

| 77 | Executive actions of **Union** in the name of the President |
| 163 | Council of Ministers to aid the **Governor** |
| 166 | Executive actions of **State** in the name of the Governor |
| 165 | Advocate General (State) |
| 76 | Attorney General (Union) |

👉 **164 → Ministers**

Think:

**3 → Advice**

**4 → Ministers**

**5 → Advocate**

**6 → Government Orders**

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Executive actions of the Union Government are expressed in whose name?

- [x] President
- [ ] Prime Minister
- [ ] Governor
- [ ] Chief Justice of India

> **Explanation:** Under Article 77, all executive actions of the Union are expressed in the name of the President.

---

### Q2

Which Article deals with the appointment of the Chief Minister?

- [ ] Article 163
- [x] Article 164
- [ ] Article 165
- [ ] Article 166

> **Explanation:** Article 164 provides for the appointment of the Chief Minister and other Ministers.

---

### Q3

Who is the highest legal officer of a State?

- [ ] Attorney General
- [ ] Solicitor General
- [x] Advocate General
- [ ] Chief Justice

> **Explanation:** The Advocate General is the highest law officer of a State.

---

### Q4

Who frames rules for the convenient transaction of State Government business?

- [x] Governor
- [ ] Chief Minister
- [ ] State Legislature
- [ ] High Court

> **Explanation:** Under Article 166(3), the Governor makes rules for the conduct of State Government business.
