# 💧 Vasopressin (Antidiuretic Hormone - ADH)
🔴 High Yield

## 📖 Overview
• Vasopressin, also called **Antidiuretic Hormone (ADH)**, regulates water reabsorption in the kidneys.
↳ Secreted by the **posterior pituitary gland**.

## ⭐ Key Points

### 🎯 Functions
• Acts on:
  ↳ Distal Convoluted Tubule (DCT)
  ↳ Collecting Duct

• Increases water reabsorption.
• Produces concentrated urine.
• Maintains:
  ↳ Water balance
  ↳ Blood volume
  ↳ Blood pressure

### ⚙️ Mechanism
• ADH binds to receptors on kidney tubule cells.
• Inserts **Aquaporin** (water channel) proteins into the cell membrane.
• More water is absorbed back into the bloodstream.
• Less water is lost through urine.

### ⭐ Related Hormones

🩸 **Renin**
• Released by kidneys.
• Initiates the Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System (RAAS).

⚡ **Angiotensin II**
• Raises blood pressure.
• Stimulates Aldosterone and ADH secretion.

🧂 **Aldosterone**
• Secreted by adrenal cortex.
• Increases sodium reabsorption.
• Water follows sodium.

🤱 **Oxytocin**
• Secreted by posterior pituitary.
• Causes uterine contractions and milk ejection.

## 🎯 Exam Booster
• ADH = Water reabsorption hormone.
• Acts mainly on **DCT and Collecting Duct**.
• Aquaporins are water channels inserted by ADH.
• More ADH → Less urine + Concentrated urine.
• Less ADH → More urine + Dilute urine (Diabetes Insipidus).

## 🔄 Quick Revision

| Hormone | Main Function |
|---------|---------------|
| Vasopressin (ADH) | Water reabsorption |
| Aldosterone | Sodium reabsorption |
| Renin | Starts RAAS |
| Angiotensin II | Raises Blood Pressure |
| Oxytocin | Labour & Milk Ejection |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

❌ ADH increases sodium absorption.
✅ Aldosterone primarily increases sodium absorption.

❌ ADH is secreted by the kidneys.
✅ ADH is secreted by the **posterior pituitary gland**.

❌ Aquaporins transport sodium.
✅ Aquaporins transport **water only**.

## 💡 Memory Trick

**ADH = Add Drinking water back**

- **A** → Aquaporins
- **D** → Distal tubule
- **H** → Holds water

**Which hormone regulates water reabsorption in the distal renal tubule?**

- [ ] Renin
- [x] Vasopressin (ADH)
- [ ] Oxytocin

**Why?**
ADH directly increases water reabsorption in the DCT and collecting ducts by inserting aquaporins.
