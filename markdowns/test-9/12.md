# 📌 Folk Dances of India: Bhavai, Regional Forms & Key Highlights

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Bhavai Dance**: A prominent folk dance of **Rajasthan** known for balancing 7 to 11 earthen pots or brass pitchers (*matkas*) on the head while dancing on swords, glass pieces, or metal rims.
- **Key Performing Communities**: Kalbelia, Bhil, Meena, Jat, and Kumhar communities.
- **Major Centers in Rajasthan**: Udaipur, Chittorgarh, Jodhpur, and Jaipur.
- **UNESCO Recognition**: **Kalbelia Dance** (also from Rajasthan) was inscribed on the UNESCO Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage in **2010**.

## 📖 Major Indian States & Their Famous Folk Dances

| State | Popular Folk Dances | Key Defining Feature / Highlight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rajasthan** | **Bhavai, Ghoomar, Kalbelia, Terah Taali, Kachhi Ghodi** | *Kalbelia* (Snake charmer community, UNESCO 2010); *Terah Taali* uses 13 *manjeeras*. |
| **Gujarat** | **Garba, Dandiya Raas, Tippani, Padhar** | *Garba* was inscribed on the UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage list in **2023**. |
| **Bihar** | **Jat-Jatin, Bidesia, Jhijhiya, Domkach, Kajari** | *Jat-Jatin* depicts the tender relationship and quarrels of a married couple. |
| **Himachal Pradesh** | **Nati, Chham, Kayang, Dangi** | *Kullu Nati* holds a Guinness World Record for the largest folk dance performance. |
| **Maharashtra** | **Lavani, Koli, Tamasha, Dhangari Gaja** | *Lavani* is performed to the powerful beats of the *Dholki*. |
| **Punjab** | **Bhangra, Giddha, Sammi, Kickli** | *Giddha* is performed exclusively by women; *Bhangra* by men during Harvest (Baisakhi). |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bhavai Dance vs. Bhavai Folk Theatre** | **Bhavai Dance** = Balancing dance of Rajasthan; **Bhavai Folk Drama** = Popular folk theatre of **Gujarat**. | Confusing the Rajasthani *balancing dance* with the Gujarati *folk theatre tradition*. |
| **Kalbelia vs. Ghoomar** | Both belong to **Rajasthan**, but **Kalbelia** is on the UNESCO list, whereas **Ghoomar** is the state dance. | Marking Ghoomar instead of Kalbelia for UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage recognition. |
| **Tippani vs. Matki Dance** | **Tippani** = Gujarat (Saurashtra construction workers); **Matki Dance** = Malwa region of **Madhya Pradesh**. | Swapping Tippani (Gujarat) with Matki (Madhya Pradesh). |

### PYQ 1

Bhavai is a traditional folk dance form of which Indian state?

- [ ] Gujarat
- [x] Rajasthan
- [ ] Bihar
- [ ] Madhya Pradesh

> **Explanation**: Bhavai is a famous folk dance of Rajasthan involving sensational balancing acts on sharp surfaces like glass, swords, or brass plates with pots on the head.

### PYQ 2

In which year was the 'Kalbelia' folk dance and songs of Rajasthan included in UNESCO's Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage?

- [ ] 2005
- [x] 2010
- [ ] 2015
- [ ] 2018

> **Explanation**: Kalbelia dance was inscribed on the UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2010 along with Chhau dance and Mudiyettu.

### PYQ 3

Which of the following folk dances belongs to the state of Himachal Pradesh and is famous for holding a world record for the largest group performance?

- [ ] Rouf
- [x] Nati
- [ ] Cheraw
- [ ] Giddha

> **Explanation**: Kullu Nati from Himachal Pradesh was officially acknowledged in the Guinness Book of World Records for being the largest folk dance in terms of participant numbers.

---

### PYQ 4

The 'Cheraw' dance, performed using bamboo staves, is the traditional folk dance of which state?

- [ ] Nagaland
- [ ] Meghalaya
- [x] Mizoram
- [ ] Manipur

> **Explanation**: Cheraw is a prominent folk dance of Mizoram, commonly called the Bamboo Dance, where dancers step in and out of rhythmic bamboo grids.

### PYQ 5

In December 2023, which Indian folk dance form was officially added to UNESCO's Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity?

- [ ] Lavani
- [x] Garba
- [ ] Bhangra
- [ ] Bihu

> **Explanation**: Garba of Gujarat was inscribed on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list in December 2023 during the 18th session of the Intergovernmental Committee.
