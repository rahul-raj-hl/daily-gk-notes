# 📌 Fundamental Rights During National Emergency (Articles 20 & 21)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Key Exception**: During a **National Emergency** (Article 352), the enforcement of Fundamental Rights conferred by **Articles 20 and 21 CANNOT be suspended** under any circumstances.
- **Governing Article**: **Article 359** empowers the President to suspend the right to move any court for enforcement of Fundamental Rights, **except Articles 20 and 21**.
- **Crucial Safeguard**: Added by the **44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978** to prevent misuse of emergency power (as seen during the 1975–1977 Emergency).
- **Core Rights Protected**:
  - **Article 20**: Protection in respect of conviction for offenses (*No ex-post facto law, No double jeopardy, No self-incrimination*).
  - **Article 21**: Protection of life and personal liberty (*Procedure established by law*).

---

## 📖 Key Articles & Constitutional Provisions

| Article | Core Provision | Status During National Emergency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Article 19** | 6 Fundamental Freedoms (*Speech, Assembly, Association, Movement, Residence, Profession*) | Suspended **automatically** under **Article 358**, but **only** during *External Emergency* (*War or External Aggression*). |
| **Article 20** | Protection in respect of conviction for offenses | **Cannot be suspended** under any condition (Protected by Article 359). |
| **Article 21** | Protection of life and personal liberty | **Cannot be suspended** under any condition (Protected by Article 359). |
| **Article 22** | Protection against arbitrary arrest and detention | Can be suspended by a Presidential Order under Article 359. |
| **Article 23** | Prohibition of traffic in human beings and forced labor | Can be suspended by a Presidential Order under Article 359. |
| **Article 358** | Automatic suspension of Article 19 | Operates only during War/External Aggression (Not Armed Rebellion). |
| **Article 359** | Presidential Order suspending court enforcement of Rights | Applies to both External & Internal Emergency; **Articles 20 & 21 are explicitly excluded**. |

---

## 🧠 Exam Booster: Article 358 vs. Article 359

| Feature | Article 358 | Article 359 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rights Affected** | Limited to **Article 19** only. | Covers all Fundamental Rights **except Articles 20 & 21**. |
| **Automaticity** | Suspends Article 19 **automatically** when emergency is declared. | Requires a **specific Presidential Order** to suspend enforcement. |
| **Grounds of Emergency** | Applies **only** to External Emergency (*War or External Aggression*). | Applies to **both** External (*War/Aggression*) and Internal Emergency (*Armed Rebellion*). |
| **Duration** | Operates for the **entire duration** of Emergency nationwide. | Operates for the duration specified in the order (or full emergency period). |

---

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Clarification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Suspension of Right vs. Suspension of Enforcement** | Article 358 suspends Article 19 *itself*. Article 359 suspends the *right to move court* for enforcement of specified rights, leaving the substantive rights theoretically intact. |
| **Internal Emergency Ground** | The term **"Internal Disturbance"** was replaced by **"Armed Rebellion"** via the 44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978. |
| **42nd vs. 44th Amendment** | **42nd Amendment (1976)** gave sweeping emergency powers; **44th Amendment (1978)** restored judicial safeguards and protected Articles 20 & 21. |

## 🎯 Memory Tricks

> **1. The "20-21 Lifeboat" Rule:**
> - **20** = Protection against **2**-times punishment (*Double Jeopardy*) & illegal conviction.
> - **21** = Right to **1**-and-only Life & Liberty.
> - *No emergency on Earth can drown Articles 20 & 21!*

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which of the following Fundamental Rights cannot be suspended even during a National Emergency?

- [ ] Articles 14 and 15
- [ ] Articles 19 and 20
- [x] Articles 20 and 21
- [ ] Articles 21 and 22

> **Explanation**: Under Article 359 of the Indian Constitution (as amended by the 44th Amendment Act, 1978), the right to move any court for the enforcement of rights under Articles 20 and 21 cannot be suspended under any circumstance.

---

### PYQ 2

Which Constitutional Amendment Act restricted the power of the President to suspend the enforcement of Articles 20 and 21 during a National Emergency?

- [ ] 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976
- [x] 44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978
- [ ] 86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002
- [ ] 40th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976

> **Explanation**: The 44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978 introduced vital safeguards after the 1975 National Emergency, ensuring that the enforcement of Articles 20 and 21 remains protected.

---

### PYQ 3

Article 19 of the Indian Constitution is automatically suspended during a National Emergency declared on which of the following grounds?

- [ ] Armed Rebellion
- [ ] Financial Instability
- [x] War or External Aggression
- [ ] Internal Disturbance

> **Explanation**: Under Article 358, Article 19 is automatically suspended only when National Emergency is declared on grounds of 'War' or 'External Aggression' (External Emergency), and NOT on the ground of 'Armed Rebellion'.

---

### PYQ 4

Under which Article of the Indian Constitution does the President issue an order suspending the right to move courts for enforcement of Fundamental Rights?

- [ ] Article 352
- [ ] Article 356
- [ ] Article 358
- [x] Article 359

> **Explanation**: Article 359 authorizes the President to suspend the right to approach courts for enforcement of specified Fundamental Rights (excluding Articles 20 and 21) during a National Emergency.

1. Statement 1: Article 358 extends to the whole of India automatically upon the declaration of National Emergency.
2. Statement 2: The right to move court under Article 32 for enforcement of Article 20 can be suspended if the President passes a special decree.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- [x] 1 only
- [ ] 2 only

> **Explanation**: Statement 1 is correct (Article 358 covers the whole country automatically). Statement 2 is incorrect because Article 20 cannot be suspended under any decree or Presidential order under Article 359.
