# 📌 Supreme Court Judges: Oath & Qualifications

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Oath or Affirmation**: Administered by the **President of India** (or a person appointed by him for this purpose) under **Article 124(6)** and prescribed in the **Third Schedule** of the Constitution.
- **Qualifications**: Must be a citizen of India AND meet one of these criteria:
  - Judge of a High Court for **5 years**; OR
  - Advocate of a High Court for **10 years**; OR
  - A **distinguished jurist** in the opinion of the President.
- **Minimum Age**: **No minimum age** is prescribed in the Constitution for appointment as a judge of the Supreme Court.
- **Retirement Age**: Supreme Court judges hold office until they attain the age of **65 years**.

## 📖 Supreme Court vs. High Court Judges: Core Provisions

| Parameter | Supreme Court Judge | High Court Judge |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Constitutional Article** | Article 124 | Article 217 |
| **Oath Administered By** | **President** (or person appointed by him) | **Governor** of the State (or person appointed by him) |
| **Tenure / Retirement Age** | **65 years** | **62 years** |
| **HC Judicial Experience Required** | 5 years as HC Judge | 10 years holding judicial office in India |
| **HC Advocate Experience Required**| 10 years as HC Advocate | 10 years as HC Advocate |
| **Distinguished Jurist Provision** | **Yes** (Eligible) | **No** (Not eligible for HC appointment) |
| **Resignation Submitted To** | President of India | President of India |

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Constitutional Feature | Specific Provision / Article | High-Yield Point |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Oath Schedule** | **Third Schedule** | Contains the form of oath/affirmation for SC Judges and CAG. |
| **Tenure Clause** | Article 124(2) | Holds office until attaining $65\text{ years}$ of age. |
| **Removal Process (Impeachment)** | Article 124(4) | Removed by President only after an address by Parliament passed by a **special majority** (Misbehaviour or Incapacity). |
| **Post-Retirement Bar** | Article 124(7) | A retired SC judge is **debarred from pleading/acting** in any court or authority in India. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Oath Authority** | SC Judge takes oath before the **President**; HC Judge takes oath before the **Governor**. | Thinking the Chief Justice of India administers oath to SC judges (they can only do so if explicitly delegated by the President). |
| **Distinguished Jurist Clause** | Exists **ONLY** for Supreme Court judge qualification under Article 124(3)(c). | Assuming a "distinguished jurist" can also be directly appointed as a High Court judge (Not applicable for HC). |
| **Minimum Age** | **No minimum age requirement** is fixed in the Indian Constitution for SC/HC judges. | Choosing option statements that claim $35\text{ years}$ or $40\text{ years}$ as the minimum age. |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "P5A10J - President, 5-Year Judge, 10-Year Advocate, Jurist"**
> - **P** $\rightarrow$ Oath administered by **President**
> - **5** $\rightarrow$ **5 years** experience as a High Court **Judge**
> - **A10** $\rightarrow$ **10 years** experience as a High Court **Advocate**
> - **J** $\rightarrow$ Distinguished **Jurist** in the opinion of the President

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Before entering upon his office, a judge of the Supreme Court has to make and subscribe an oath or affirmation before:

- [ ] Chief Justice of India
- [x] President or some person appointed by him
- [ ] Union Law Minister
- [ ] Prime Minister of India

> **Explanation**: According to Article 124(6), every person appointed to be a judge of the Supreme Court shall, before he enters upon his office, make and subscribe an oath before the President or some person appointed by him.

---

### PYQ 2

What is the minimum age prescribed in the Constitution of India for appointment as a Judge of the Supreme Court?

- [ ] 30 years
- [ ] 35 years
- [ ] 40 years
- [x] No minimum age is prescribed

> **Explanation**: The Constitution sets qualifications (citizenship, HC experience, or jurist status) and a retirement age ($65\text{ years}$), but prescribes no minimum age limit.

---

### PYQ 3

A person is eligible for appointment as a Judge of the Supreme Court if he has been an advocate of a High Court for at least how many years?

- [ ] 5 years
- [x] 10 years
- [ ] 15 years
- [ ] 20 years

> **Explanation**: Article 124(3)(b) states that an advocate of a High Court (or two or more such courts in succession) for at least 10 years is qualified for appointment as an SC judge.

---

### PYQ 4

A retired Judge of the Supreme Court of India is prohibited from pleading or acting in any court or before any authority within the territory of India under which Article?

- [ ] Article 123
- [x] Article 124(7)
- [ ] Article 125
- [ ] Article 126

> **Explanation**: Article 124(7) debars retired SC judges from pleading or acting in any court or before any authority within India to preserve judicial independence.

---

### PYQ 5

In which schedule of the Constitution of India is the form of oath or affirmation for a Supreme Court Judge provided?

- [ ] Second Schedule
- [x] Third Schedule
- [ ] Fourth Schedule
- [ ] Sixth Schedule

> **Explanation**: The Third Schedule contains forms of Oaths or Affirmations for Constitutional posts including SC Judges, Union Ministers, MPs, and the CAG.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Consider the following statements regarding the qualifications of Supreme Court Judges:
1. Statement 1: He/She must be a citizen of India.
2. Statement 2: A person can be appointed as an SC judge if he is a distinguished jurist in the opinion of the Governor.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- [x] 1 only
- [ ] 2 only
- [ ] Both 1 and 2
- [ ] Neither 1 nor 2

> **Explanation**: Statement 1 is correct. Statement 2 is incorrect because the distinguished jurist opinion must be in the view of the **President**, not the Governor.
