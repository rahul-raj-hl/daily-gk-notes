# 📌 Schedules of the Indian Constitution

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Total Schedules**: Originally **8** in 1950; currently **12** after constitutional amendments.
- **Key Mappings**:
  - **2nd Schedule**: Emoluments, allowances, and privileges of constitutional posts.
  - **3rd Schedule**: Forms of Oaths or Affirmations.
  - **4th Schedule**: Allocation of Rajya Sabha seats to States and UTs.
  - **6th Schedule**: Tribal area administration in **AMTM** (Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram).
  - **10th Schedule**: Anti-Defection Law (added by the 52nd Amendment, 1985).

---

## 📖 Complete Master Table of All 12 Schedules

| Schedule | Subject Matter | Key Constitutional Details / Amendment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st Schedule** | Territory & Jurisdiction | Names of States and Union Territories with their boundaries. |
| **2nd Schedule** | Emoluments & Allowances | Salaries/privileges of President, Governors, Judges, CAG, Speakers, etc. |
| **3rd Schedule** | Oaths & Affirmations | Forms of oaths for Ministers, MPs, Judges, CAG (*Excludes President/Governor oaths*). |
| **4th Schedule** | Rajya Sabha Seat Allocation | Distribution of Council of States seats to States and UTs based on population. |
| **5th Schedule** | Scheduled Areas & Tribes | Administration and control of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes (*except 6th Schedule states*). |
| **6th Schedule** | Tribal Areas (AMTM) | Special provisions for Tribal Areas in **Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram**. |
| **7th Schedule** | Division of Powers | **Union List** (100 items), **State List** (61 items), **Concurrent List** (52 items). |
| **8th Schedule** | Official Languages | **22 recognized languages** (Originally 14; added 21st, 71st, and 92nd Amendments). |
| **9th Schedule** | Land Reforms & Validation | Land reforms / Zamindari abolition; immune to judicial review (*1st Amendment, 1951*). |
| **10th Schedule** | Anti-Defection Law | Disqualification of MPs/MLAs on grounds of defection (*52nd Amendment, 1985*). |
| **11th Schedule** | Panchayats | Powers, authority, and 29 functional matters of Panchayats (*73rd Amendment, 1992*). |
| **12th Schedule** | Municipalities | Powers, authority, and 18 functional matters of Urban Local Bodies (*74th Amendment, 1992*). |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2nd vs. 3rd Schedule** | **2nd** = Money / Allowances $\rightarrow$ **3rd** = Oaths / Affirmations. | Swapping the order (remember: *Salary/Emoluments come before taking the Oath*). |
| **5th vs. 6th Schedule** | **5th** = General Scheduled Areas across India $\rightarrow$ **6th** = Specific 4 North-Eastern States (AMTM). | Including states like Manipur, Nagaland, or Arunachal Pradesh under the 6th Schedule. |
| **11th vs. 12th Schedule** | **11th** = Panchayats (29 subjects) $\rightarrow$ **12th** = Municipalities (18 subjects). | Confusing the number of subjects transferred to local self-governing bodies. |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which Schedule of the Indian Constitution contains provisions regarding the anti-defection law?

- [ ] Eighth Schedule
- [ ] Ninth Schedule
- [x] Tenth Schedule
- [ ] Eleventh Schedule

> **Explanation**: The Tenth Schedule was added by the 52nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1985, and deals with disqualification on the ground of defection.

---

### PYQ 2

Which of the following states is NOT covered under the Sixth Schedule of the Indian Constitution?

- [ ] Assam
- [ ] Meghalaya
- [x] Manipur
- [ ] Tripura

> **Explanation**: The Sixth Schedule specifically covers the tribal areas of four North-Eastern states: Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, and Mizoram (AMTM). Manipur is not included.

---

### PYQ 3

By which Constitutional Amendment Act was the Ninth Schedule added to the Constitution of India?

- [x] 1st Amendment Act, 1951
- [ ] 42nd Amendment Act, 1976
- [ ] 44th Amendment Act, 1978
- [ ] 52nd Amendment Act, 1985

> **Explanation**: The 1st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1951 introduced the Ninth Schedule to protect land reform laws from judicial challenge.

### PYQ 4

Match Column A (Schedule) with Column B (Subject):

| Column A | Column B |

| **1. Second Schedule** | A. Allocation of seats in Rajya Sabha |
| **2. Third Schedule** | B. Administration of Tribal Areas in AMTM |
| **3. Fourth Schedule** | C. Forms of Oaths or Affirmations |
| **4. Sixth Schedule** | D. Emoluments, allowances, and privileges |

- [ ] 1-A, 2-C, 3-D, 4-B

- [ ] 1-D, 2-A, 3-C, 4-B
- [ ] 1-C, 2-D, 3-A, 4-B

> **Explanation**: 2nd Schedule = Emoluments (1-D); 3rd Schedule = Oaths (2-C); 4th Schedule = Rajya Sabha seats (3-A); 6th Schedule = AMTM Tribal areas (4-B).

### PYQ 5

How many languages are currently recognized under the Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution?

- [ ] 14
- [ ] 18
- [x] 22
- [ ] 24

> **Explanation**: Originally, there were 14 languages in the 8th Schedule. Subsequent amendments added 8 more languages, taking the total count to 22.
