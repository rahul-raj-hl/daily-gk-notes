# 📌 Shamsuddin Iltutmish & The Mamluk Dynasty

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Real Founder of Delhi Sultanate**: **Shamsuddin Iltutmish** (reigned 1211–1236 CE) consolidated Muslim rule in Northern India and shifted the capital from **Lahore to Delhi**.
- **Currency Reforms**: Introduced two standard coins of the Sultanate—the **Silver Tanka** ($175\text{ grains}$) and the **Copper Jital**.
- **Administrative System**: Introduced the **Iqtadari System** (dividing the empire into *Iqtas* allocated to military commanders/nobles) and organized **Turkan-i-Chahalgani** (Group of Forty loyal slave nobles).
- **Architectural Achievements**: Completed the construction of the **Qutb Minar** and the **Quwwat-ul-Islam Mosque** in Delhi. Built his own tomb near the Qutb complex in 1235 CE.
- **Succession**: Nominated his competent daughter, **Razia Sultana**, as his successor—making her the first and only woman ruler of the Delhi Sultanate.

## 📖 Key Mamluk Rulers Comparison

| Ruler | Reign Period | Major Contributions / Achievements | High-Yield Landmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Qutb-ud-din Aibak** | 1206–1210 CE | Founder of Slave Dynasty; known as **Lakh Baksh** (giver of lakhs); died playing *Chaugan* (polo). | Started Qutb Minar; built Quwwat-ul-Islam (Delhi) & Adhai Din Ka Jhonpra (Ajmer). |
| **Shamsuddin Iltutmish** | 1211–1236 CE | Shifted capital to Delhi; introduced *Tanka* & *Jital*; created *Iqtadari system* & *Chahalgani*. | Completed Qutb Minar; saved India from Mongol invader **Genghis Khan** (1221 CE). |
| **Razia Sultana** | 1236–1240 CE | First female Muslim ruler of South Asia; discarded purdah and sat open-court (*Durbar*). | Defeated by Altunia (Governor of Bhatinda); married him before being killed at Kaithal. |
| **Ghiyas-ud-din Balban** | 1266–1287 CE | Destroyed *Turkan-i-Chahalgani*; introduced **Sijda** & **Paibos**; introduced Persian festival **Navroz**. | Established military department **Diwan-i-Arz** and policy of **"Blood and Iron"**. |

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Administrative / Historical Feature | Key Concept | High-Yield Point |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Iqtadari System** | Land grant revenue distribution system. | Land (*Iqta*) assigned to *Iqtadars/Muqtis* to maintain troops; not hereditary under Iltutmish. |
| **Turkan-i-Chahalgani** | Council of 40 Turkish slave nobles (*Chalisa*). | Formed by Iltutmish to consolidate power; later destroyed completely by Balban. |
| **Mongol Threat Neutralization** | Refused asylum to Jalal-ud-din Mangabarni (Shah of Khwarazm). | Kept Genghis Khan from invading India in 1221 CE. |
| **Caliphate Recognition** | Received investiture (*Mansur*) from Abbasid Caliph of Baghdad in 1229 CE. | Legalized his rule as the legitimate Sultan of Delhi. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Qutb Minar Construction** | **Started** by Aibak $\rightarrow$ **Completed** by Iltutmish $\rightarrow$ **Repaired/Added 5th storey** by Firoz Shah Tughlaq. | Attributing the full construction to a single ruler. |
| **First Ruler vs. Real Founder** | **Qutb-ud-din Aibak** was the first ruler, but **Iltutmish** is called the "Real Founder" of the Delhi Sultanate. | Selecting Aibak when the question asks for the "Real consolidator/founder who made Delhi the capital." |
| **Sultanate Coinage** | **Silver Tanka** & **Copper Jital** were introduced by **Iltutmish**. | Confusing them with token currency introduced much later by Muhammad bin Tughlaq. |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "T-I-Q-C - Tanka, Iqta, Qutb, Chahalgani"**
> - **T** $\rightarrow$ **Tanka** (Silver coin) & Jital
> - **I** $\rightarrow$ **Iqtadari** System
> - **Q** $\rightarrow$ **Qutb** Minar completion
> - **C** $\rightarrow$ **Chahalgani** (Group of 40)

### PYQ 1

Which Sultan of the Delhi Sultanate shifted his capital from Lahore to Delhi?

- [ ] Qutb-ud-din Aibak
- [x] Shamsuddin Iltutmish
- [ ] Razia Sultana
- [ ] Ghiyas-ud-din Balban

> **Explanation**: Iltutmish shifted the capital of the Sultanate from Lahore to Delhi, establishing Delhi as the central political seat of power.

### PYQ 2

Who among the following introduced the two coins of the Delhi Sultanate—the 'Silver Tanka' and 'Copper Jital'?

- [ ] Balban
- [x] Iltutmish
- [ ] Alauddin Khalji
- [ ] Muhammad bin Tughlaq

> **Explanation**: Iltutmish reformed the currency system by introducing pure Arabic coinage, including the $175\text{ grain}$ Silver Tanka and Copper Jital.

### PYQ 3

The 'Iqtadari System' in the Delhi Sultanate was officially introduced by which ruler?

- [ ] Qutb-ud-din Aibak
- [x] Iltutmish
- [ ] Jalal-ud-din Khalji
- [ ] Firoz Shah Tughlaq

> **Explanation**: Iltutmish introduced the Iqtadari system, dividing the empire into administrative territories called Iqtas granted to nobles and army commanders.

### PYQ 4

Which Mongol leader reached the borders of India along the Indus river during the reign of Iltutmish?

- [ ] Timur
- [x] Genghis Khan
- [ ] Hulagu Khan
- [ ] Tarmashirin

> **Explanation**: Genghis Khan pursued Khwarazmian prince Jalal-ud-din to the banks of the Indus in 1221 CE. Iltutmish wisely refused shelter to Jalal-ud-din, saving India from Mongol devastation.
