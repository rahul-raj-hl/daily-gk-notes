# 📌 Swadeshi Movement & Major Mass Movements in India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Core Answer**: The **Swadeshi Movement** (launched in 1905 against the Partition of Bengal) was known as the **Vande Mataram Movement** in coastal Andhra Pradesh.
- **Trigger**: Annoyance over the Partition of Bengal announced by **Lord Curzon** (October 16, 1905).
- **Key Regional Leader in Andhra**: **Mutnuri Krishna Rao** (editor of *Krishna Patrika*) and **Bipin Chandra Pal**, whose lecture tour in 1907 popularized the movement across Rajahmundry and Machilipatnam.
- **National Impact**: Marked the rise of **Extremist leaders** (Lal-Bal-Pal and Aurobindo Ghosh) and established boycott of foreign goods as a major political tool.

## 📖 Major Indian Freedom Movements: Comparison Matrix

| Movement | Year / Period | Immediate Trigger / Cause | Key Leaders & Regional Names | Major Outcome / Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Swadeshi Movement** | **1905 – 1911** | Partition of Bengal by Lord Curzon | Lal-Bal-Pal, Aurobindo Ghosh; known as **Vande Mataram Movement** in Andhra | Annulment of Partition in 1911; rise of indigenous industries (*Swadeshi stores*) |
| **Khilafat Movement** | **1919 – 1924** | Harsh treatment of Ottoman Caliph after WWI (*Treaty of Sèvres*) | Ali Brothers (Shaukat & Mohammad Ali), Abul Kalam Azad | Merged with Non-Cooperation Movement (1920) under Gandhi |
| **Civil Disobedience (Phase I & II)** | **1930 – 1934** | Rejection of Gandhi's 11-point demands by Lord Irwin | Mahatma Gandhi, C. Rajagopalachari, Sarojini Naidu | Dandi March (Salt Satyagraha); Gandhi-Irwin Pact (1931) |
| **Quit India Movement** | **1942** | Failure of Cripps Mission & threat of Japanese invasion | Mahatma Gandhi (*"Do or Die"*), Aruna Asaf Ali, Usha Mehta | Underground radio network; parallel governments (*Jatiya Sarkar*, *Satara*) |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vande Mataram Movement vs. Home Rule** | **Vande Mataram Movement** refers specifically to the Swadeshi Movement in Andhra Pradesh (1905–1911). | Confusing it with Tilak and Annie Besant's Home Rule Movement (1916). |
| **Partition of Bengal Dates** | Announced in **July 1905**, implemented on **October 16, 1905**, and annulled in **1911** by Lord Hardinge II. | Mixing up the implementation year (1905) with the annulment year (1911). |
| **Methanoic vs. Formic Acid / Salt March Leaders** | Sarojini Naidu led Dharsana Salt Works raid; Rajaji led Vedaranyam March; K. Kelappan led Payyanur March in Kerala. | Assuming Gandhi personally led salt marches in south India. |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

By what name was the Swadeshi Movement popularly known in the coastal Andhra region?

- [x] Vande Mataram Movement
- [ ] Khilafat Movement
- [ ] Salt Satyagraha
- [ ] Reshmi Rumal Movement

> **Explanation**: During the anti-partition agitation, Bipin Chandra Pal toured Andhra Pradesh in 1907. His speeches popularized the nationalist surge under the name "Vande Mataram Movement."

---

### PYQ 2

Who was the Viceroy of India when the Partition of Bengal was implemented in 1905?

- [ ] Lord Ripon
- [x] Lord Curzon
- [ ] Lord Hardinge
- [ ] Lord Minto

> **Explanation**: Lord Curzon announced the Partition of Bengal in July 1905 and brought it into effect on October 16, 1905, leading directly to the Swadeshi Movement.

---

### PYQ 3

Who among the following ran an underground secret radio station during the Quit India Movement of 1942?

- [ ] Aruna Asaf Ali
- [ ] Sucheta Kripalani
- [x] Usha Mehta
- [ ] Sarojini Naidu

> **Explanation**: Usha Mehta operated the clandestine "Congress Radio" from different hidden locations in Bombay to broadcast news during the 1942 Quit India struggle.

---

### PYQ 4

The Day of Partition of Bengal (October 16, 1905) was observed throughout Bengal as a day of:

- [ ] Independence
- [x] Mourning and Raksha Bandhan
- [ ] Victory
- [ ] Non-Violence

> **Explanation**: On Rabindranath Tagore's suggestion, the day was observed as *Raksha Bandhan* to symbolize brotherhood between Eastern and Western Bengal, alongside fasting and mourning.

---

### PYQ 5

Who led the Salt Satyagraha march from Tiruchirappalli to Vedaranyam in Tamil Nadu in 1930?

- [ ] K. Kelappan
- [ ] Chidambaram Pillai
- [x] C. Rajagopalachari
- [ ] Tanguturi Prakasam

> **Explanation**: Inspired by Gandhi's Dandi March, C. Rajagopalachari led the Salt March on the Tanjore coast from Trichy to Vedaranyam in April 1930.
