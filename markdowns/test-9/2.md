# 📌 Pioneering Indian Olympic Legends

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **K. D. Jadhav (Khashaba Dadasaheb Jadhav)**: First athlete from independent India to win an individual Olympic medal—a **Bronze in Wrestling** (Bantamweight category) at the **1952 Helsinki Olympics**.
- **Karnam Malleswari**: First Indian woman to win an Olympic medal—a **Bronze in Weightlifting** ($54\text{ kg}$ category) at the **2000 Sydney Olympics**.
- **Milkha Singh**: Nicknamed **"The Flying Sikh"**; won 4 Asian Games Gold medals and finished 4th in the $400\text{ m}$ final at the **1960 Rome Olympics**.
- **P. T. Usha**: Known as the **"Payyoli Express"** or **"Golden Girl"**; won 4 Gold medals at the 1986 Seoul Asian Games and missed an Olympic Bronze in the $400\text{ m}$ hurdles by $0.01\text{ seconds}$ at the **1984 Los Angeles Olympics**.

### PYQ 1

Who was the first athlete from independent India to win an individual medal at the Olympic Games?

- [ ] Milkha Singh
- [x] K. D. Jadhav
- [ ] Dhyan Chand
- [ ] Karnam Malleswari

> **Explanation**: Khashaba Dadasaheb Jadhav won the bronze medal in wrestling at the 1952 Summer Olympics in Helsinki, making him the first individual medalist for independent India.

In which sport did Karnam Malleswari win an Olympic bronze medal, becoming the first Indian woman to achieve this feat?

- [ ] Boxing
- [ ] Wrestling
- [x] Weightlifting
- [ ] Badminton

> **Explanation**: Karnam Malleswari won a bronze medal in the weightlifting $54\text{ kg}$ category at the 2000 Sydney Olympics.

### PYQ 3

Which legendary Indian sprinter is widely known by the nickname "The Flying Sikh"?

- [x] Milkha Singh
- [ ] P. T. Usha
- [ ] Gurbachan Singh Randhawa
- [ ] Ajmer Singh

> **Explanation**: Milkha Singh was given the name "The Flying Sikh" by General Ayub Khan of Pakistan after his dominant performance in a 1960 international athletic meet.

### PYQ 4

P. T. Usha missed out on winning an Olympic bronze medal in the $400\text{ m}$ hurdles at the 1984 Los Angeles Olympics by what margin?

- [ ] $0.1\text{ seconds}$
- [x] $0.01\text{ seconds}$
- [ ] $0.05\text{ seconds}$
- [ ] $0.5\text{ seconds}$

> **Explanation**: P. T. Usha finished fourth in the $400\text{ m}$ hurdles final, missing the bronze medal by just one-hundredth of a second ($0.01\text{ s}$).

- [ ] Neeraj Chopra
