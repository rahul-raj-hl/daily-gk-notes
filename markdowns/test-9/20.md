# 📌 Obituary: Charles Coste (1924–2025)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Who was he?**: French track cyclist and **world's oldest living Olympic champion**.
- **Key Achievement**: Won **Gold in the Men's Team Pursuit** at the **1948 London Olympics**.
- **Paris 2024 Role**: Served as the **penultimate torchbearer** during the Paris 2024 Opening Ceremony, passing the flame in his wheelchair to Marie-José Pérec and Teddy Riner.
- **Passing**: Died on **October 31, 2025**, at the age of **101**.
