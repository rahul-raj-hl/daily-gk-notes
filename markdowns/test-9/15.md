# 📌 CBIC Chairman Appointment & Institutional Overview

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **New CBIC Chairman**: **Vivek Chaturvedi** (1990-batch IRS C&IT officer).
- **Succeeded**: **Sanjay Kumar Agarwal** (1988-batch IRS officer), who retired in November 2025.
- **Approving Authority**: **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet (ACC)**.
- **Parent Body**: Operates under the **Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance**, Government of India.

## 📖 Comparison: Direct Taxes (CBDT) vs. Indirect Taxes (CBIC)

| Feature | Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) | Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Statutory Status** | Statutory body under **Central Boards of Revenue Act, 1963** | Statutory body under **Central Boards of Revenue Act, 1963** |
| **Tax Jurisdiction** | Income Tax, Corporate Tax, Securities Transaction Tax (STT) | Goods and Services Tax (GST), Customs, Central Excise |
| **Cadre Control** | Indian Revenue Service (IRS - Direct Taxes) | Indian Revenue Service (IRS - Customs & Indirect Taxes) |
| **Parent Department** | Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance | Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance |
| **Key Function** | Administering progressive direct taxes on income & profits | Policy formulation, border trade facilitation & GST administration |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CBIC vs. CBDT Scope** | **CBIC** handles indirect levies (*GST, Customs*); **CBDT** handles direct taxation (*Personal Income Tax, Corporate Tax*). | Swapping Chairman appointments between CBDT and CBIC in current affairs questions. |
| **CBEC to CBIC Renaming** | Renamed in **2018** after the GST implementation in July 2017. | Thinking CBIC has always existed under its current name since the 1963 Act. |
| **Constitutional vs. Statutory** | CBIC and CBDT are **Statutory Bodies** (*Central Boards of Revenue Act, 1963*), **not Constitutional**. | Confusing regulatory revenue boards with constitutional bodies like the Finance Commission (Art. 280). |

## 🎯 Memory Tricks

> **1. Mnemonic for Administrative Scope: "I-C-G"**
> - **I**ndirect Taxes
> - **C**ustoms
> - **G**ST
> *(All administered by **CBIC** under the Department of Revenue)*

> **2. Renaming Chronology:**
> - **1963**: Central Board of Excise and Customs (**CBEC**)
> - **2017**: Rollout of GST
> - **2018**: Re-designated as Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (**CBIC**)

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

The Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) functions under which Ministry of the Government of India?

- [ ] Ministry of Commerce and Industry
- [x] Ministry of Finance
- [ ] Ministry of Corporate Affairs
- [ ] Ministry of Home Affairs

> **Explanation**: CBIC is a statutory body functioning under the Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance.

---

### PYQ 2

The Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) and Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) were created under which statutory act?

- [ ] Income Tax Act, 1961
- [x] Central Boards of Revenue Act, 1963
- [ ] Finance Act, 1992
- [ ] Customs Act, 1962

> **Explanation**: Both CBDT and CBIC derive their statutory authority from the Central Boards of Revenue Act, 1963.

---

### PYQ 3

In which year was the Central Board of Excise and Customs (CBEC) renamed to Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC)?

- [ ] 2015
- [ ] 2017
- [x] 2018
- [ ] 2020

> **Explanation**: Following the introduction of GST in July 2017, CBEC was officially renamed CBIC in 2018 to reflect its mandate over GST administration.
