# 📌 Naturally Occurring Organic Acids & Their Sources

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Oxalic Acid**: Primarily found in **Tomatoes** (~50 mg per 100g) and **Spinach**.
- **Chemical Formula**: $(COOH)_2$ or $C_2H_2O_4$ (simplest dicarboxylic acid).
- **Industrial/Commercial Use**: Used as a **rust stain remover** and bleaching agent in textiles.
- **Health Connection**: High oxalic acid combines with calcium to form **calcium oxalate**, the main component of kidney stones.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Citric vs. Ascorbic Acid** | **Citric Acid** gives sour taste; **Ascorbic Acid** is Vitamin C. | Assuming Vitamin C and Citric Acid are the exact same chemical compound. |
| **Formic vs. Methanoic Acid** | Both refer to the **exact same acid** ($HCOOH$). | Treating Formic Acid (common name) and Methanoic Acid (IUPAC name) as different acids. |
| **Vinegar Concentration** | Vinegar contains **5% to 8%** Acetic Acid in water. | Thinking pure 100% acetic acid is called vinegar. |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Oxalic acid is naturally present in which of the following sources?

- [ ] Vinegar
- [ ] Tamarind
- [x] Tomato
- [ ] Sour Milk

> **Explanation**: Tomato contains oxalic acid along with citric and malic acids. Spinach is another major natural source of oxalic acid.

---

### PYQ 2

Which acid is injected into the skin when an ant bites?

- [ ] Lactic acid
- [ ] Citric acid
- [x] Methanoic acid
- [ ] Tartaric acid

> **Explanation**: Ant stings contain Methanoic acid (commonly known as Formic acid, $HCOOH$), which causes pain and irritation.

---

### PYQ 3

Which of the following acids is present in Tamarind?

- [ ] Methanoic acid
- [x] Tartaric acid
- [ ] Lactic acid
- [ ] Acetic acid

> **Explanation**: Tartaric acid is naturally found in tamarind, grapes, and unripe mangoes.

---

### PYQ 4

A 5% to 8% solution of acetic acid in water is known as _____.

- [ ] Ethanol
- [x] Vinegar
- [ ] Quicklime
- [ ] Formalin

> **Explanation**: Vinegar is an aqueous solution containing 5% to 8% acetic acid, widely used as a food preservative and flavoring agent.

---

### PYQ 5

Which organic acid gives rancid butter its characteristic unpleasant smell?

- [ ] Oxalic acid
- [ ] Malic acid
- [x] Butyric acid
- [ ] Citric acid

> **Explanation**: Butyric acid (butanoic acid) is released when butter turns rancid due to the hydrolysis of fats.
