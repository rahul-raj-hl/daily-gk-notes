# 📌 Iqta System: Evolution, Key Reforms & Rulers

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Introduced By**: **Shams-ud-din Iltutmish** introduced the Iqta system in India to organize land revenue and military administration.
- **Reformed / Restricted By**: **Alauddin Khilji (1296–1316)** severely restricted small Iqtas, expanded **Khalisa (Crown Lands)**, abolished land assignment to personal troops, and paid salaries in **cash**.
- **Abolished / Hereditary Phase**: **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** later made Iqtas hereditary, weakening central control.
- **Key Concept**: An **Iqta** was a non-hereditary revenue assignment given to officers (**Muqtis** or **Iqtadars**) in exchange for administrative and military duties.

## 📖 Ruler-Wise Evolution of the Iqta System

| Ruler | Dynasty | Key Action / Modification | Impact on Administration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Iltutmish** | Mamluk / Slave | **Introduced** Iqta system in India. | Fragmented lands among nobles (*Iqtadars*) to collect revenue and maintain troops. |
| **Ghiyas-ud-din Balban** | Mamluk / Slave | Appointed *Khwaja* (accountants) to check Iqtadar accounts. | Created central audit oversight to curb embezzlement. |
| **Alauddin Khilji** | Khilji | **Restricted Iqta**, converted Doab into *Khalisa*, introduced **cash salaries**. | Created a centralized standing army; established *Diwan-i-Mustakhraj* (department of arrears). |
| **Ghiyas-ud-din Tughlaq** | Tughlaq | Separated personal income of Muqti from military maintenance funds. | Prevented Muqtis from squeezing peasants for excess revenue. |
| **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** | Tughlaq | Made Iqtas **hereditary**. | Weakened central authority; led to military corruption. |

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Administrative Term | Meaning / Function | Key High-Yield Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Khalisa Land** | Crown land whose direct revenue went to the royal treasury (*Bait-ul-Mal*). | Maximized under Alauddin Khilji in the fertile Central Doab region. |
| **Muqti / Wali** | Holder or governor of an Iqta assignment. | Responsible for local administration, law & order, and troop maintenance. |
| **Fawazil** | Surplus revenue submitted by Muqtis to the central treasury. | Strictly audited under Balban and Alauddin Khilji. |
| **Diwan-i-Mustakhraj** | Department established by Alauddin Khilji to collect revenue arrears. | Created specifically to extract unpaid revenue from defaulting Iqtadars and intermediaries. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Introduction vs. Reform** | **Iltutmish** *introduced* Iqta; **Alauddin Khilji** *reformed/restricted* it. | Answering Iltutmish when the question asks who *reformed or restricted* the system. |
| **Iqta vs. Jagir System** | **Iqta** belongs to the Delhi Sultanate; **Jagirdari** belongs to the Mughal Empire. | Conflating Medieval Sultanate revenue assignments with Mughal Jagirs. |
| **Ownership vs. Revenue Rights** | Iqta was **NOT land ownership**; it was purely the **right to collect land revenue**. | Assuming Iqtadars became legal landowners of their assigned territory. |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which Sultan of Delhi restricted the Iqta system, converted land into Khalisa, and started paying his soldiers in cash?

- [ ] Iltutmish
- [ ] Balban
- [x] Alauddin Khilji
- [ ] Firoz Shah Tughlaq

> **Explanation**: Alauddin Khilji abolished land assignments for personal cavalry, brought the fertile Doab directly under Khalisa (crown land), and instituted cash salaries for his standing army.

### PYQ 2

Who among the following introduced the Iqta system in India?

- [ ] Qutb-ud-din Aibak
- [x] Shams-ud-din Iltutmish
- [ ] Alauddin Khilji
- [ ] Muhammad bin Tughlaq

> **Explanation**: Iltutmish introduced the Iqta system to efficiently collect revenue and distribute administrative duties across his empire.

### PYQ 3

In the medieval period under the Delhi Sultanate, what did the term 'Fawazil' denote?

- [ ] Extra payment made to nobles
- [ ] Tax levied on non-Muslims
- [x] Excess amount paid to the exchequer by the Iqtadars
- [ ] Revenue assigned for religious grants

> **Explanation**: *Fawazil* referred to the surplus revenue balance that an Iqtadar had to remit to the central treasury after deducting operational expenses and military costs.

---

### PYQ 4

Which ruler made the Iqta system hereditary in the Delhi Sultanate?

- [ ] Alauddin Khilji
- [ ] Ghiyas-ud-din Balban
- [ ] Muhammad bin Tughlaq
- [x] Firoz Shah Tughlaq

> **Explanation**: Firoz Shah Tughlaq made army posts and Iqta assignments hereditary, allowing sons or relatives to inherit rights upon an officer's death.

---

### PYQ 5

Which specialized administrative department was established by Alauddin Khilji to look after revenue collection and arrears?

- [ ] Diwan-i-Arz
- [ ] Diwan-i-Kohi
- [x] Diwan-i-Mustakhraj
- [ ] Diwan-i-Insha

> **Explanation**: *Diwan-i-Mustakhraj* was established by Alauddin Khilji to audit accounts and collect pending arrears from defaulting revenue officials and Iqtadars.
