# 📌 Makaravilakku & Important Indian State Festivals

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Makaravilakku Festival**: Celebrated annually on **Makara Sankranti (January 14)** at the **Sabarimala Ayyappa Temple** in **Kerala**.
- **Makara Jyothi**: A celestial star that appears on *Makara Samkramam* when the Sun transitions into *Makaram Rasi* (Capricorn).
- **Other Key Kerala Festivals**: **Onam** (Harvest Festival, features *Thiruvathira* & *Vallam Kali* boat race), **Vishu** (Malayalam New Year), and **Attukal Pongala**.

## 📖 Major Indian States & Their High-Yield Cultural Festivals

| State | Important Festivals | Key Defining Feature / Highlight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kerala** | **Makaravilakku, Onam, Vishu, Theyyam, Thrissur Pooram** | *Onam* features *Pookkalam* (flower rangoli) and *Vallam Kali* (Snake Boat Race). |
| **Telangana** | **Bathukamma, Bonalu, Medaram Jathara** | *Bathukamma* is a floral festival celebrated by women; *Medaram Jathara* is Asia's largest tribal fair. |
| **Tamil Nadu** | **Pongal, Aadiperukku, Thaipusam, Jallikattu** | *Pongal* is a 4-day harvest festival (*Bogi, Surya, Mattu, Kaanum Pongal*). |
| **Assam** | **Bihu (Bohag, Kati, Magh), Me-Dam-Me-Phi, Ambubachi Mela** | *Ambubachi Mela* is held at Kamakhya Temple; *Me-Dam-Me-Phi* is ancestor worship by Ahom tribe. |
| **Mizoram** | **Chapchar Kut, Pawl Kut, Mim Kut** | *Chapchar Kut* is celebrated in spring after clearing jungles (*Jhum* cultivation). |
| **Arunachal Pradesh** | **Pakke Paga Hornbill, Losar, Torgya, Solung** | *Pakke Paga Hornbill* focuses specifically on hornbill conservation (*Pakke Tiger Reserve*). |
| **Tripura** | **Garia Puja, Kharchi Puja, Ker Puja** | *Kharchi Puja* involves the worship of 14 deities (*Chaturdasa Devata*). |
| **Nagaland** | **Hornbill Festival, Sekrenyi, Moatsu, Aoling** | *Hornbill Festival* is called the "Festival of Festivals" (Dec 1–10). |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hornbill vs. Pakke Paga Hornbill** | **Hornbill Festival** = Nagaland; **Pakke Paga Hornbill Festival** = Arunachal Pradesh. | Selecting Nagaland when asked about *Pakke Paga Hornbill*. |
| **Losar vs. Losoong** | **Losar** = Tibetan New Year (Ladakh/Sikkim); **Losoong** = Sikkimese New Year (Lepcha harvest). | Treating Losar and Losoong as interchangeable. |
| **Bihu Types (Assam)** | **Bohag (Rongali) Bihu** = Spring/New Year (April); **Kati (Kongali) Bihu** = Oct/Nov; **Magh (Bhogali) Bihu** = Harvest (Jan). | Confusing the seasons/months of the three Bihu celebrations. |
