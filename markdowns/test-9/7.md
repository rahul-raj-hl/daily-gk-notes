# 📌 Olympic Rings: Colors, Continents & History

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Yellow Ring**: Represents **Asia** in traditional competitive exam mappings.
- **5 Interlocking Rings**: Symbolize the five participating continents: **Europe, Asia, Africa, The Americas, and Oceania**.
- **Designer**: Baron **Pierre de Coubertin** designed the flag in **1913**; it was officialized in **1914** and first hoisted at the **1920 Antwerp Games**.
- **Color Inclusivity Concept**: The six colors (including the white background) were chosen because **every nation's flag** contains at least one of these colors.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Yellow vs. Green Ring** | **Yellow** = Asia; **Green** = Oceania. | Swapping Yellow and Green between Asia and Australia/Oceania. |
| **Red Ring Scope** | Represents **both** North and South America together. | Thinking North and South America have separate rings. |
| **IOC Official Position** | Officially, IOC states rings do *not* map strictly 1:1 to single colors today; however, **SSC/State PSC exams strictly test traditional 1:1 mappings**. | Marking "no mapping" in competitive exams when a color-continent pair is asked. |

### PYQ 2

In which year was the Olympic flag designed by Pierre de Coubertin?

- [ ] 1896
- [x] 1913
- [ ] 1920
- [ ] 1924

> **Explanation**: Baron Pierre de Coubertin designed the Olympic symbol and flag in 1913. It was formally presented in 1914 and raised at the 1920 Antwerp Games.

Which ring color on the Olympic flag represents the continent of Oceania?

- [ ] Blue
- [ ] Black
- [x] Green
- [ ] Red

Where was the Olympic flag hoisted for the very first time during the Olympic Games?

- [ ] Athens, 1896
- [ ] Paris, 1900
- [ ] London, 1908
- [x] Antwerp, 1920

> **Explanation**: The Olympic flag made its official debut at the 1920 Summer Olympics held in Antwerp, Belgium.

### PYQ 5

In 2021, which word was added to the official Olympic motto 'Citius, Altius, Fortius'?
