# 📌 Major Indian Temples & Monuments

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Brihadeswara Temple**: Located in **Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu**. Dedicated to Lord Shiva; built by Chola Emperor **Rajaraja I** (Dravida style). Part of UNESCO's "Great Living Chola Temples."
- **Dilwara Temples**: Located on **Mount Abu, Rajasthan**. Five famous **Jain temples** carved from white marble, built by Solanki rulers/ministers (Maru-Gurjara architecture).
- **Lingaraja Temple**: Located in **Bhubaneswar, Odisha**. Dedicated to Lord Shiva (Harihara); landmark of **Kalinga style architecture** (Somavamsi dynasty).
- **Group of Monuments at Hampi**: Located in **Vijayanagara/Ballari district, Karnataka**. Capital of the Vijayanagara Empire (built by Harihara I & Bukka I); UNESCO World Heritage site featuring the Stone Chariot at the Vittala Temple.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brihadeswara (Thanjavur) vs. Gangaikonda Cholapuram** | Thanjavur was built by **Rajaraja I**; Gangaikonda Cholapuram was built by his son **Rajendra I**. | Swapping the Chola kings responsible for each Brihadeswara-style temple. |
| **Dilwara Temple Religion** | **Jainism** (5 temples dedicated to Tirthankaras). | Misidentifying it as a Hindu temple due to Solanki Hindu ruler patronage. |
| **Kalinga Style Components** | Consists of **Deula** (sanctum/tower) and **Jagamohana** (assembly hall). | Confusing Kalinga architecture terminology with Nagara terms like *Shikhara* and *Mandapa*. |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "Big Cholas Built Thanjavur, Detailed Jains Mount Abu"**
> - **Big Cholas** $\rightarrow$ **Brihadeswara** built by **Cholas** (Rajaraja I)
> - **Thanjavur** $\rightarrow$ Located in **Tamil Nadu**
> - **Detailed Jains** $\rightarrow$ **Dilwara** marble carving (Jainism)
> - **Mount Abu** $\rightarrow$ Located in **Rajasthan**

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which Chola ruler constructed the famous Brihadeswara Temple at Thanjavur?

- [ ] Rajendra I
- [x] Rajaraja I
- [ ] Parantaka I
- [ ] Karikala Chola

> **Explanation**: King Rajaraja I of the Chola Dynasty commissioned the Brihadeswara Temple around 1010 CE.

---

### PYQ 2

The world-famous Dilwara Temples located at Mount Abu belong to which religion?

- [ ] Buddhism
- [ ] Hinduism
- [x] Jainism
- [ ] Sikhism

> **Explanation**: The Dilwara Temples are a group of five Svetambara Jain temples constructed between the 11th and 13th centuries CE.

---

### PYQ 3

The iconic Stone Chariot featured on the back of the ₹50 currency note in India is located in which UNESCO World Heritage Site?

- [ ] Pattadakal
- [ ] Khajuraho
- [x] Hampi
- [ ] Mahabalipuram

> **Explanation**: The Stone Chariot is located inside the Vittala Temple complex at Hampi, Karnataka, and is featured on the ₹50 note.

---

### PYQ 4

Lingaraja Temple in Bhubaneswar is built in which specific style of architecture?

- [ ] Dravida Style
- [x] Kalinga Style (Nagara variant)
- [ ] Vesara Style
- [ ] Hoysala Style

> **Explanation**: Lingaraja Temple is a classic example of Kalinga temple architecture, characterized by its prominent *Deula* and *Jagamohana*.

---

### PYQ 5

In which present-day state is the UNESCO World Heritage Site of Hampi located?

- [ ] Tamil Nadu
- [ ] Andhra Pradesh
- [x] Karnataka
- [ ] Kerala

> **Explanation**: Hampi is located along the banks of the Tungabhadra River in east-central Karnataka.
