# 📌 Chronology of Major Socio-Political & Peasant Movements

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Chronological Sequence**:
  1. **Khilafat Movement**: **1919** (Pan-Islamic protest against the Ottoman Empire's dismemberment after WWI).
  2. **Self-Respect Movement**: **1925** (Founded by Periyar E.V. Ramasamy in Tamil Nadu to fight caste oppression).
  3. **Tebhaga Movement**: **1946–1947** (Bengal peasant struggle demanding a $\frac{2}{3}$ share of harvest for sharecroppers).
  4. **Telangana Peasant Rebellion**: **1946–1951** (Armed peasant struggle in Hyderabad State against *vetti* forced labor and feudal landlords).

## 📖 Key Socio-Political & Peasant Movements: Detailed Comparison

| Movement | Year | Region & Key Leaders | Core Objective / Demand | Key Outcome / Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Khilafat Movement** | **1919 – 1924** | All-India; Ali Brothers (Shaukat & Mohammad Ali), Abul Kalam Azad | Restore status of Ottoman Caliph | Merged with Non-Cooperation Movement (1920) under Gandhi. |
| **Self-Respect Movement** | **1925** | Tamil Nadu; E.V. Ramasamy (*Periyar*) | Anti-caste, non-Brahmin emancipation, rationalism | Paved the way for Dravidian political identity in Tamil Nadu. |
| **Tebhaga Movement** | **1946 – 1947** | Bengal; Bengal Pradesh Kisan Sabha (CPI, Krishak Praja Party) | Demand for $\frac{2}{3}$ share (*Tebhaga*) of crop for *bargadars* | Led to the passage of the Bargadari Act protecting sharecroppers. |
| **Telangana Rebellion** | **1946 – 1951** | Hyderabad State; Andhra Mahasabha, Communist Party of India | Abolition of *Vetti* (forced labor) and feudal landlords (*Deshmukhs*) | Weakened Nizam's rule; accelerated land reform legislation post-independence. |

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Movement / Organization | Founding Leader | High-Yield Fact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eka Movement (1921)** | Madari Pasi | Peasant revolt in Awadh against high rents and extraction of extra charges. |
| **Bardoli Satyagraha (1928)** | Vallabhbhai Patel | Resistance against a 22% tax hike; Vallabhbhai earned the title **"Sardar"** from local women. |
| **All India Kisan Sabha (1936)** | Swami Sahajanand Saraswati | Formed at the Lucknow session of the Indian National Congress to represent peasant demands nationally. |
| **Punnapra-Vayalar (1946)** | CPI Leaders (Kerala) | Armed uprising against the autocratic regime of C.P. Ramaswami Iyer in Travancore. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tebhaga Share Ratio** | Demanded **$\frac{2}{3}$ share for peasants** and $\frac{1}{3}$ for landlords (*Jotedars*). | Confusing it with traditional $\frac{1}{2}$ (50-50) sharecropping (*Adhiari*) system. |
| **Telangana vs. Tebhaga Timeline** | Both started in **1946**, but Telangana lasted until **1951** (post-independence armed struggle against Nizam & Razakars). | Assuming both movements ended before Independence in 1947. |
| **Self-Respect Movement vs. Justice Party** | Self-Respect Movement was launched in **1925** by Periyar; Justice Party was formed earlier in **1916** by T.M. Nair and C. Natesa Mudaliar. | Treating Justice Party and Self-Respect Movement as the exact same entity formed at the same time. |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which among the following was the primary demand of the Tebhaga Movement in Bengal (1946-47)?

- [ ] Complete abolition of the Zamindari system
- [x] Two-thirds share of the harvest for sharecroppers
- [ ] Remission of land revenue during famine
- [ ] Freedom from moneylenders' debts

> **Explanation**: Sharecroppers (*bargadars*) demanded two-thirds of the harvested crop for themselves instead of giving half to the landlords (*jotedars*).

---

### PYQ 2

Who founded the 'Self-Respect Movement' in Tamil Nadu in 1925?

- [ ] C. Rajagopalachari
- [ ] T.M. Nair
- [x] E.V. Ramasamy Naicker
- [ ] Jyotirao Phule

> **Explanation**: E.V. Ramasamy Naicker (Periyar) started the Self-Respect Movement in 1925 to promote rationalism, gender equality, and anti-caste principles.

---

### PYQ 3

Arranging the following movements chronologically:
1. All India Kisan Sabha
2. Bardoli Satyagraha
3. Self-Respect Movement
4. Tebhaga Movement

Choose the correct order:

- [ ] 2, 3, 1, 4
- [x] 3, 2, 1, 4
- [ ] 1, 2, 3, 4
- [ ] 3, 1, 2, 4

> **Explanation**: Self-Respect Movement (1925) $\rightarrow$ Bardoli Satyagraha (1928) $\rightarrow$ All India Kisan Sabha (1936) $\rightarrow$ Tebhaga Movement (1946).

Consider the following statements regarding the Telangana Peasant Movement:
1. Statement 1: It was directed against forced labor (*vetti*) and the feudal landlord system under the Nizam regime.
2. Statement 2: The uprising was actively led by the Communist Party of India and Andhra Mahasabha.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- [ ] 1 only
- [ ] 2 only
- [x] Both 1 and 2
- [ ] Neither 1 nor 2

> **Explanation**: Both statements are factually correct. The movement targeted oppressive local lords (*Deshmukhs*) and forced labor practices in the Hyderabad state under communist leadership.
