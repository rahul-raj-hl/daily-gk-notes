# 📌 Ghiyasuddin Balban & Mamluk Dynasty Administration

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Ruler**: **Ghiyasuddin Balban** (reigned 1266–1287 CE), original name **Bahauddin**.
- **Theory of Kingship**: Propounded divine right of kings—claimed to be **Zil-i-Ilahi** (Shadow of God on Earth) and recipient of **Niyabat-i-Khudai** (Vicegerency of God).
- **Court Etiquette**: Introduced Persian customs **Sijada** (prostration) and **Paibos** (kissing the monarch's feet), along with the Persian New Year festival **Nauroz**.
- **Key Reforms**:
  - Abolished **Turkan-i-Chahalgani** (The Forty / Chalisa) to consolidate royal authority.
  - Followed a policy of **"Blood and Iron"** (*Lauh-o-Rakt*) against internal rebels and Mewati bandits.
  - Separated the military department (**Diwan-i-Arz**) from the finance department (**Diwan-i-Wizarat**) and established an efficient intelligence network (**Barids**).

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Term / Custom | Meaning | Initiator / Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sijada / Sijda** | Prostration before the Sultan | Introduced by **Balban**; later restricted/abolished by **Shah Jahan**. |
| **Paibos** | Kissing the Sultan's feet | Introduced by **Balban** to establish absolute monarchical dominance. |
| **Turkan-i-Chahalgani** | Council of 40 Turkish nobles | Formed by **Iltutmish**, destroyed by **Balban**. |
| **Diwan-i-Arz vs. Diwan-i-Wizarat** | Military vs. Finance Departments | **Balban** separated military (*Arz*) from finance (*Wizarat*) to strengthen the army. |

### PYQ 1

Who among the following Delhi Sultans introduced the Persian customs of 'Sijada' and 'Paibos' in his court?

- [ ] Iltutmish
- [ ] Alauddin Khalji
- [x] Ghiyasuddin Balban
- [ ] Qutb-ud-din Aibak

> **Explanation**: Ghiyasuddin Balban introduced Sijada (prostration) and Paibos (kissing the feet of the monarch) to prove the superiority of the Sultan over the nobles.

### PYQ 2

The Persian festival of 'Nauroz' was introduced in India by which ruler of the Delhi Sultanate?

- [ ] Firuz Shah Tughlaq
- [x] Ghiyasuddin Balban
- [ ] Iltutmish
- [ ] Sikandar Lodi

> **Explanation**: Balban introduced the famous Persian New Year festival 'Nauroz' to impress his subjects with the grandeur and awe of his court.

### PYQ 3

Which Sultan of Delhi destroyed the powerful group of Turkish nobles known as 'Turkan-i-Chahalgani' or 'The Forty'?

- [ ] Jalaluddin Khalji
- [ ] Razia Sultana
- [x] Ghiyasuddin Balban
- [ ] Iltutmish

> **Explanation**: Iltutmish created 'Turkan-i-Chahalgani', but Balban completely eliminated this group to remove rival power centers and centralize authority in the hands of the Sultan.

### PYQ 4

Who among the following rulers of the Delhi Sultanate assumed the title of 'Zil-i-Ilahi' (Shadow of God)?

- [ ] Qutb-ud-din Aibak
- [x] Ghiyasuddin Balban
- [ ] Muhammad bin Tughlaq
- [ ] Bahlul Lodi

> **Explanation**: Balban adopted the title *Zil-i-Ilahi* to emphasize his divine right to rule and elevate the status of the monarch above all nobles.

### PYQ 5

The military department called 'Diwan-i-Arz' was established as an independent department during the reign of:

- [ ] Alauddin Khalji
- [ ] Iltutmish
- [x] Ghiyasuddin Balban
- [ ] Firuz Shah Tughlaq

> **Explanation**: Balban separated the military department (*Diwan-i-Arz*) from the finance department (*Diwan-i-Wizarat*) to maintain a well-equipped, centralized army to fight internal rebels and Mongol invasions.

The famous policy of 'Blood and Iron' (*Lauh-o-Rakt*) was adopted by Balban primarily to deal with:

- [ ] The South Indian kingdom of Devagiri
- [x] The Mewati raiders and internal rebels near Delhi
- [ ] The Portuguese traders on the western coast
- [ ] The revolt of the Vijayanagara Empire

> **Explanation**: Balban adopted the stern policy of 'Blood and Iron' to suppress internal insurrections, clear the forests around Delhi, and eliminate Mewati raiders.

### Q4

What was Balban's original name before he ascended the throne of the Delhi Sultanate in 1266 CE?

- [ ] Ulugh Khan
- [x] Bahauddin
- [ ] Farid Khan
- [ ] Junagadh Khan

> **Explanation**: Balban's original name was Bahauddin. He was captured by Mongols as a youth, sold as a slave to Khwaja Jamaluddin of Basra, and later purchased by Iltutmish. He was also given the title *Ulugh Khan*.

### Q5

Which Mughal Emperor later banned the practice of 'Sijada' in the royal court, which was originally introduced by Balban during the Sultanate period?

- [ ] Akbar
- [ ] Jahangir
- [x] Shah Jahan
- [ ] Aurangzeb

> **Explanation**: Shah Jahan abolished the practice of *Sijada* in court, considering it un-Islamic (as prostration is reserved only for God in Islamic tradition), and replaced it with *Chahar Taslim* and *Zaminbos*.
