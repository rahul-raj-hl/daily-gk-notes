## 🧠 Exam Booster: Key Facts

- **Order of Precedence**: Bharat Ratna (1st) $\rightarrow$ Padma Vibhushan (2nd) $\rightarrow$ Padma Bhushan (3rd) $\rightarrow$ Padma Shri (4th).
- **Institution Year**: Padma Awards were instituted in **1954**.
- **Announcement Date**: Announced every year on the eve of **Republic Day (26th January)**.
- **Maximum Sanction Limit**: Usually capped at **120 awards per year** (excluding posthumous awards and foreign/NRI recipients).

Which of the following is the correct hierarchical order of Indian civilian awards in descending order?

- [ ] Bharat Ratna $\rightarrow$ Padma Bhushan $\rightarrow$ Padma Vibhushan $\rightarrow$ Padma Shri

### PYQ 3

Which body/authority announces the Padma Awards every year on the eve of Republic Day?

- [ ] Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports
- [x] Ministry of Home Affairs

> **Explanation**: The Padma Awards recommendations are received by the Padma Awards Committee constituted by the Prime Minister, and the awards are announced by the Ministry of Home Affairs on 25th January.
