# 📌 Human Nutrition: Functions of Key Nutrients

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Carbohydrates**: Primary and immediate source of energy ($\text{4 kcal/g}$). Broken down into **glucose**.
- **Proteins**: Essential for growth, tissue repair, enzyme formation, and bodybuilding ($\text{4 kcal/g}$). Built from **amino acids**.
- **Fats (Lipids)**: Concentrated energy storage reserves ($\text{9 kcal/g}$) and structural components of cell membranes.
- **Vitamins & Minerals**: Micronutrients essential for metabolic regulation, immune system maintenance, and biological functions without directly yielding energy.

## 📖 Classification of Nutrients

| Nutrient Category | Primary Function | Monomer / Basic Unit | Daily Energy Yield | Key Food Sources |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Carbohydrates** | Primary energy provider | Monosaccharides (Glucose, Fructose) | $\sim 4\text{ kcal/g}$ | Rice, wheat, potatoes, maize, banana |
| **Proteins** | Growth, tissue repair, enzymes | Amino acids (20 types) | $\sim 4\text{ kcal/g}$ | Pulses, eggs, meat, milk, soybeans |
| **Fats (Lipids)** | Energy reserve & insulation | Fatty acids + Glycerol | $\sim 9\text{ kcal/g}$ | Butter, ghee, nuts, vegetable oils |
| **Vitamins** | Metabolic protection & immunity | Organic compounds | $0\text{ kcal/g}$ | Citrus fruits, green leafy vegetables |
| **Minerals** | Structural & regulatory support | Inorganic elements ($\text{Ca, Fe, I, Na}$) | $0\text{ kcal/g}$ | Milk, spinach, iodized salt |

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Energy Value** | Fats ($9\text{ kcal/g}$) > Carbohydrates ($4\text{ kcal/g}$) = Proteins ($4\text{ kcal/g}$) | Fats produce more than double the energy per gram compared to carbohydrates. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Carbohydrates vs. Fats for Instant Energy** | **Carbohydrates** provide immediate energy; **Fats** are long-term storage reserves. | Assuming fats are the primary instant energy source because they are calorie-dense. |
| **Energy from Vitamins** | Vitamins yield **0 calories**; they act strictly as co-enzymes/regulators. | Believing vitamins provide physical energy directly to the body. |
| **Proteins as Fuel** | Proteins are used for energy **only during prolonged starvation**. | Thinking protein's primary function is daily energy generation. |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which of the following nutrients is known as the primary 'energy-yielding' food for the human body?

- [x] Carbohydrates
- [ ] Proteins
- [ ] Vitamins
- [ ] Minerals

> **Explanation**: Carbohydrates are quickly broken down into glucose, making them the primary and most accessible energy source for cells.

---

### PYQ 2

Kwashiorkor, a severe health condition characterized by swollen bellies, is caused by the deficiency of which nutrient?

- [ ] Carbohydrates
- [ ] Fats
- [x] Proteins
- [ ] Vitamin C

> **Explanation**: Kwashiorkor occurs due to severe protein deficiency in the diet, leading to fluid retention (edema) and muscle wasting.

---

### PYQ 3

Which nutrient yields the maximum energy per gram upon oxidation?

- [ ] Carbohydrates
- [x] Fats
- [ ] Proteins
- [ ] Roughage

> **Explanation**: Fats yield approximately $9\text{ kcal/g}$, which is more than double the energy provided by carbohydrates ($4\text{ kcal/g}$) or proteins ($4\text{ kcal/g}$).

### Q1

Match Column A (Nutrient) with Column B (Primary Role):

| Column A | Column B |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Carbohydrates** | A. Tissue growth and cell repair |
| **2. Proteins** | B. Absorption of Vitamins A, D, E, K |
| **3. Fats** | C. Primary immediate fuel for respiration |
| **4. Vitamins** | D. Biological catalysts and metabolic co-factors |

- [ ] 1-A, 2-C, 3-B, 4-D
- [x] 1-C, 2-A, 3-B, 4-D
- [ ] 1-C, 2-B, 3-A, 4-D
- [ ] 1-B, 2-A, 3-C, 4-D

> **Explanation**: Carbohydrates = Primary fuel (1-C); Proteins = Growth & repair (2-A); Fats = Solvent for fat-soluble vitamins (3-B); Vitamins = Metabolic co-factors (4-D).

### Q2

Consider the following statements regarding macronutrients:
1. Statement 1: Both carbohydrates and proteins yield approximately 4 kilocalories of energy per gram.
2. Statement 2: Vitamins provide energy to human body tissues when carbohydrates are unavailable.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- [x] 1 only
- [ ] 2 only
- [ ] Both 1 and 2
- [ ] Neither 1 nor 2

> **Explanation**: Statement 1 is correct ($4\text{ kcal/g}$). Statement 2 is incorrect because vitamins produce zero energy; body tissues break down fat reserves or structural proteins when carbohydrates are unavailable.
