# 📌 Operation Hawkeye Strike & Major International Military Operations

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Operation Name**: **Operation Hawkeye Strike**.
- **Participating Countries / Forces**: Executed primarily by the **United States** via **U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM)**, supported by the **Jordanian Armed Forces**.
- **Location**: **Syria** (targeting ISIS infrastructure in central Syria).
- **Date**: **December 19, 2025**.
- **Objective**: Neutralize over 70 ISIS targets using fighter jets, attack helicopters, and artillery (deploying over 100 precision-guided munitions) to degrade terror planning capabilities.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Operation Name | Country / Location | Primary Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Operation Hawkeye Strike** | **Syria** (US & Jordan) | Targeted strikes against ISIS infrastructure in central Syria (Dec 2025). |
| **Operation Inherent Resolve** | **Iraq & Syria** (US-led Coalition) | Broader international military campaign against ISIS launched in 2014. |
| **Operation Midnight Hammer** | **Middle East** | Separate tactical operation; do not confuse with air campaign names. |
| **Operation Kaveri / Ajay / Ganga** | **Sudan / Israel / Ukraine** | Indian non-combatant evacuation operations (NEOs) for Indian citizens. |
