# 📌 Natyashastra: Authorship, Structure & Classical Arts Treatises

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Author / Compiler**: Attributed to the ancient sage **Bharata Muni**.
- **Nature of Work**: An ancient Sanskrit treatise on classical drama, dance, music, stage design, and aesthetics (*Rasa Theory*).
- **Fifth Veda**: Often referred to as the **Natya Veda** or "Fifth Veda," created by Lord Brahma by taking elements from the four Vedas:
  - *Pathya* (Words/Recitation) from **Rigveda**
  - *Geeta* (Music/Melody) from **Samaveda**
  - *Abhinaya* (Gestures/Expression) from **Yajurveda**
  - *Rasa* (Emotion/Aesthetics) from **Atharvaveda**

## 📖 Key Ancient Treatises on Music, Dance & Theatre

| Text | Author / Compiler | Core Focus / Subject Matter | Key Highlight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Natyashastra** | **Bharata Muni** | Drama, classical dance, aesthetics (*Rasa*), music | Contains 36 chapters and ~6000 slokas; defines 8 primary *Rasas*. |
| **Sangeeta Ratnakara** | **Sarangadeva** (13th Century) | Classical Indian Music (*Carnatic & Hindustani*) | Bridges ancient and medieval Indian music systems. |
| **Brihaddeshi** | **Matanga Muni** (6th–8th Century) | Musicology & Ragas | First text to explicitly define and introduce the concept of **Raga**. |
| **Abhinaya Darpana** | **Nandikesvara** | Gestures (*Mudras*) & Expressions in Classical Dance | Key manual used in Bharatanatyam and classical dance training. |
| **Silappatikaram** | **Ilango Adigal** | Tamil Epic with detailed accounts of ancient music & dance | Describes ancient Tamil musical scales and the *Isai-Natakam* tradition. |

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Concept | Associated Detail | High-Yield Point |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rasa Theory** | **8 Original Rasas** in Natyashastra | Bharata Muni listed 8 Rasas. The 9th Rasa (**Shanta Rasa** - Peace) was added later by **Abhinavagupta**. |
| **Abhinavabharati** | **Abhinavagupta** (10th Century) | The most famous commentary on Bharata's Natyashastra. |
| **Tandava vs. Lasya** | Dance Movements | **Tandava** = Vigorous, masculine dance (Shiva); **Lasya** = Gentle, graceful, feminine dance (Parvati). |
| **Vedic Roots of Music** | **Samaveda** | The oldest foundation of Indian music and vocal chanting (*Saman* melodies). |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Concept | Correct Detail | Common Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Natyashastra vs. Sangeeta Ratnakara** | **Natyashastra** (Bharata Muni) focuses primarily on *dramaturgy and theatre*; **Sangeeta Ratnakara** (Sarangadeva) focuses specifically on *musicology*. | Attributing Sangeeta Ratnakara to Bharata Muni. |
| **Origin of Raga Concept** | Explicitly defined in **Brihaddeshi** by **Matanga Muni**, not in Natyashastra. | Assuming Natyashastra first named individual Ragas as understood today. |
| **Number of Rasas** | Natyashastra mentions **8 Rasas**; the concept of **Navarasa (9 Rasas)** evolved later with Shanta Rasa. | Selecting '9 Rasas' when asked specifically about original counts in Natyashastra. |

## 🎯 Memory Tricks

> **1. Mnemonic for Elements taken from 4 Vedas for Natya Veda: "R-S-Y-A"**

> - **S**amaveda $\rightarrow$ **Song** (*Geeta*)
> - **Y**ajurveda $\rightarrow$ **Acting** (*Abhinaya*)
> - **A**tharvaveda $\rightarrow$ **Emotion** (*Rasa*)

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

The ancient Sanskrit treatise on performing arts, 'Natyashastra', was compiled by which of the following sages?

- [ ] Sage Valmiki
- [x] Bharata Muni
- [ ] Sage Kapila
- [ ] Patanjali

> **Explanation**: Natyashastra was compiled by Bharata Muni and is considered the foundational manual for classical drama, music, and dance in India.

---

### PYQ 2

Which Veda is considered the primary source of Indian classical music?

- [ ] Rigveda
- [x] Samaveda
- [ ] Yajurveda
- [ ] Atharvaveda

> **Explanation**: Samaveda consists of hymns set to musical tunes (*Saman*), making it the earliest textual foundation of Indian musicology.

---

### PYQ 3

Which medieval scholar wrote 'Sangeeta Ratnakara', one of the most authoritative texts on Indian music?

- [ ] Amir Khusrau
- [ ] Tansen
- [x] Sarangadeva
- [ ] Matanga Muni

> **Explanation**: Sarangadeva authored 'Sangeeta Ratnakara' in the 13th century during the rule of the Yadava dynasty in Devagiri.

---

### PYQ 4

The concept of 'Raga' in Indian classical music was first systematically defined in which ancient text?

- [ ] Natyashastra
- [x] Brihaddeshi
- [ ] Gita Govinda
- [ ] Meghaduta

> **Explanation**: 'Brihaddeshi', written by Matanga Muni, is the first ancient text that explicitly names and describes 'Raga' as a distinct musical framework.
