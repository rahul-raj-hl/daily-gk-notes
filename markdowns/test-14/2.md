# 📌 Muskan Kirar & Indian Archery Players

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Archery** (Women's Compound Archery).
- **Home State**: Hails from **Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh**.
- **Major International Achievements**:
  - **Gold Medalist**: Won gold in the women's compound event at the **Archery Asia Cup (Stage 1) in 2018** in Bangkok.
  - **Silver Medalist**: Clinched silver in the Women's Compound Team event at the **2018 Asian Games (Jakarta-Palembang)**.
  - **Bronze Medalist**: Won individual bronze at the **2019 World Archery Championships** in 's-Hertogenbosch, Netherlands.
- **National Honour**: Conferred with the prestigious **Eklavya Award (2018)** and **Vikram Award (2019)** by the Government of Madhya Pradesh.
- **Trained Under**: Coach **Richpal Singh Salaria** at the Madhya Pradesh State Archery Academy in Jabalpur/Bhopal.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Categories in Competitive Archery

*Understanding the two main disciplines recognized internationally:*

- **1. Recurve Archery (Olympic Discipline)**:
  - Uses traditional curved bows where the limbs curve away from the archer when unstrung.
  - Featured in the **Olympic Games**.
  - Prominent Indian Recurve Archers: **Deepika Kumari, Atanu Das, Tarundeep Rai, Ankita Bhakat, Pravin Jadhav, Dola Banerjee, Jayanta Talukdar, Limba Ram**.
- **2. Compound Archery (Non-Olympic / Asian Games Discipline)**:
  - Uses a mechanical pulley-and-cable system (*cams*) with magnifying sights and mechanical release aids.
  - Featured in the **Asian Games** and **World Archery Championships**.
  - Prominent Indian Compound Archers: **Muskan Kirar, Jyothi Surekha Vennam, Abhishek Verma, Ojas Pravin Deotale, Aditi Gopichand Swami, Prathamesh Jawkar**.
