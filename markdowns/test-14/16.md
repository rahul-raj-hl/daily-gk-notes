# 📌 Regional AI Impact Conference 2026 (Jaipur, Rajasthan)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Jaipur** (Capital city of Rajasthan).
- **Date of Event**: Held on **January 6, 2026**.
- **Objective**: Served as a key regional build-up event and precursor to the **India AI Impact Summit 2026**.
- **Core Focus Areas**:
  - Exploring the deployment of **Artificial Intelligence (AI) in public service delivery and governance**.
  - Driving economic growth, inclusive development, ethical AI frameworks, and tech-driven employment.
- **Co-located Event**: Held alongside the **Rajasthan DigiFest $\times$ TiE Global Summit 2026**, highlighting the state's vision to emerge as a premier hub for digital innovation, startups, and entrepreneurship.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Scope & Vision of the Regional AI Impact Conference 2026

*Key discussion pillars and institutional collaboration:*

- **Multi-Stakeholder Participation**: Brought together senior policymakers and administrators from both the Government of India and the Government of Rajasthan, along with technology leaders, startup founders, and academia.
- **Primary Pillars**:
  1. **AI for Citizen Services**: Enhancing last-mile public service delivery, grievance redressal, healthcare diagnostics, and smart agriculture.
  2. **Ethical AI & Data Privacy**: Formulating responsible AI governance frameworks, algorithmic transparency, and bias reduction.
  3. **Workforce Upskilling & Job Creation**: Preparing local youth for AI-era job markets and fostering digital entrepreneurship.
  4. **Regional Innovation Pipeline**: Acting as a decentralized regional feeder platform contributing insights to the apex **India AI Impact Summit 2026**.

---

### 2. Major Digital & Tech Summits in India

*National and international tech platforms:*

- **IndiaAI Mission**: Approved by the Union Cabinet with an outlay of over ₹10,300 crore to build sovereign computing capacity, AI Compute infrastructure (10,000+ GPUs), AI datasets, and indigenous foundation models.
- **Global Partnership on Artificial Intelligence (GPAI)**: India served as the Lead Chair and hosted the GPAI Annual Summit in New Delhi.
- **Rajasthan DigiFest**: An annual flagship technology event organized by the Department of Information Technology & Communication (DoIT&C), Rajasthan, to showcase regional startups, youth innovations, and digital infrastructure.
