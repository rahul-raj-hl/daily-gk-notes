# 📌 Padma Vibhushan 2025 & M. T. Vasudevan Nair

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **MT Vasudevan Nair** (Madath Thekkepaattu Vasudevan Nair).
- **Category / Field**: Awarded the **Padma Vibhushan (Posthumous)** in January 2025 for his monumental contributions to **Literature and Education**.
- **State / Language**: Renowned literary titan, novelist, screenwriter, and film director from **Kerala (Malayalam Literature)**.
- **Literary Masterpiece**: Famous for his celebrated novel ***Randamoozham* (The Second Turn)**, which retells the epic *Mahabharata* from the perspective of Bhima.
- **Other Civilian Honours**: Previously awarded the **Padma Bhushan** (2005) and **Jnanpith Award** (1995).
- **Civilian Hierarchy**: The Padma Vibhushan is the **second-highest civilian award** in the Republic of India (after the Bharat Ratna), instituted in **1954**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Literary & Cinematic Legacy of M. T. Vasudevan Nair

- **Literary Achievements**:
  - Born on **July 15, 1933**, in Kudallur, Kerala.
  - Conferred with India's highest literary honour, the **Jnanpith Award**, in **1995** and the **Kendra Sahitya Akademi Award** in 1970 for *Kaalam*.
  - Authored iconic novels such as *Naalukettu*, *Asuravithu*, *Manju*, *Kaalam*, and *Randamoozham*.
- **Contributions to Indian Cinema**:
  - Won the **National Film Award for Best Feature Film** with his directorial debut *Nirmalyam* (1973).
  - Wrote landmark screenplays including *Oru Vadakkan Veeragatha*, *Perumthachan*, *Kadavu*, and *Sadayam*, winning the National Film Award for Best Screenplay a record four times.
  - Conferred with the **J. C. Daniel Award** (Kerala's highest cinema award) in 2013.

---

### 2. Breakdown of Other Personalities in the Options

- **Osamu Suzuki**:
  - Japanese industrialist and former Chairman of Suzuki Motor Corporation.
  - Conferred with the **Padma Vibhushan** in the field of **Trade & Industry (Foreigner / Japan)**.
- **Sharda Sinha**:
  - Renowned Indian folk, Maithili, and Bhojpuri singer known affectionately as the *"Bihar Kokila"*.
  - Conferred with the **Padma Vibhushan (Posthumous)** in the field of **Art (Music)**.
- **Kumudini Lakhia**:
  - Renowned Kathak dancer and choreographer from Gujarat; founder of the *Kadamb Centre for Dance*.
  - Conferred with civilian honours in the field of **Art (Classical Dance)**.
