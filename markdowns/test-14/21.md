# 📌 Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha & Vidhan Sabha: The Legislative System of India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Union Legislature (Parliament of India)**:
  - **Lok Sabha (House of the People)**: Lower House at the Centre; members are **directly elected** by the public; 5-year term (subject to dissolution); exclusive authority over **Money Bills**.
  - **Rajya Sabha (Council of States)**: Upper House at the Centre; **permanent body** (not subject to dissolution); members serve **6-year terms** with one-third retiring every 2 years; elected **indirectly by State MLAs**.
- **State Legislature**:
  - **Vidhan Sabha (Legislative Assembly)**: Lower House at the State level; directly elected by citizens of the state; 5-year term.
  - **Vidhan Parishad (Legislative Council)**: Upper House at the State level (present in only 6 Indian states: UP, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Central Legislature: The Parliament of India

*Composed of the President of India and the two Houses (Article 79):*

#### A. Lok Sabha (Article 81) — Lower House / Popular House
- **Representation**: Represents the people of India as a whole.
- **Electoral Method**: Direct election on the basis of **Universal Adult Suffrage (Article 326)**.
- **Maximum Strength**: 550 members (530 from States, 20 from UTs). *(Anglo-Indian nominated seats abolished by the 104th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2019).*
- **Qualifications**: Minimum age of **25 years**; must be an Indian citizen.
- **Key Powers**:
  - Council of Ministers is collectively responsible **only to the Lok Sabha (Article 75(3))**.
  - Money Bills can **only be introduced in the Lok Sabha (Article 109)**.
  - Can pass a **No-Confidence Motion** to dismiss the government.

#### B. Rajya Sabha (Article 80) — Upper House / Council of States
- **Representation**: Represents the federating States and Union Territories.
- **Electoral Method**: Indirectly elected by the **elected MLAs of State Legislative Assemblies** using proportional representation by means of a single transferable vote.
- **Strength**: Maximum 250 members (238 elected + 12 nominated by the President for literature, science, art, and social service).
- **Qualifications**: Minimum age of **30 years**; must be an Indian citizen.
- **Special / Exclusive Powers**:
  - **Article 249**: Can authorize Parliament to make laws on a State List subject in the national interest.
  - **Article 312**: Can authorize the creation of one or more new **All India Services (AIS)**.

---

### 2. State Legislature: Vidhan Sabha & Vidhan Parishad

#### A. Vidhan Sabha (Legislative Assembly — Article 170)
- **Representation**: Directly elected representatives of the people within a State.
- **Strength**: Maximum 500, minimum 60 (with constitutional exceptions for smaller states like Sikkim, Goa, Mizoram).
- **Term**: 5 years (can be dissolved earlier by the Governor).
- **Minimum Age**: **25 years**.
- **Key Role**: State counterpart to the Lok Sabha; controls state finances and holds the State Council of Ministers accountable.

#### B. Vidhan Parishad (Legislative Council — Article 171)
- **Status**: Permanent Upper House at the state level (wherever created under **Article 169**).
- **Bicameral States (6)**: Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana.
- **Minimum Age**: **30 years**.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Constitutional Body / Term | Direct vs. Indirect Election | Dissolution Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lok Sabha** | **Directly Elected** by people | Dissolved every 5 years (or earlier) |
| **Vidhan Sabha** | **Directly Elected** by people | Dissolved every 5 years (or earlier) |
| **Rajya Sabha** | **Indirectly Elected** by MLAs | **Never Dissolved (Permanent)** |
| **Vidhan Parishad** | **Indirectly Elected** (Teachers, Graduates, MLAs, Local bodies) | **Never Dissolved (Permanent)** |

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Which of the following bodies is a permanent house and cannot be dissolved under any circumstances?

- [ ] Lok Sabha
- [x] Rajya Sabha
- [ ] Vidhan Sabha
- [ ] Municipal Corporation

> The Rajya Sabha is a permanent legislative chamber not subject to dissolution; one-third of its members retire every two years.

---

### Q2

Which of the following Indian states does NOT possess a bicameral legislature (Vidhan Parishad)?

- [ ] Uttar Pradesh
- [ ] Bihar
- [x] Rajasthan
- [ ] Karnataka

> Only 6 states in India currently have a Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad): Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana.

---

### Q3

The Council of Ministers at the Centre is collectively responsible to which House of Parliament?

- [ ] Rajya Sabha
- [x] Lok Sabha
- [ ] Both Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha equally
- [ ] The Supreme Court

> Under Article 75(3), the Union Council of Ministers is collectively responsible strictly to the House of the People (Lok Sabha).

---

### Q4

What is the maximum permissible strength of a State Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha) as stipulated under Article 170?

- [ ] 250 members
- [ ] 400 members
- [x] 500 members
- [ ] 550 members

> Article 170 specifies that a Vidhan Sabha shall consist of not more than 500 and not less than 60 members chosen by direct election.
