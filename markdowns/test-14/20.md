# 📌 South Indian Dynasties & The Post-Sangam Era (6th Century AD)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **The Kalabhra Interregnum**: A period in the early centuries AD where the "Kalabhras" disrupted the traditional ruling houses (Cholas, Cheras, Pandyas) of Tamil Nadu.
- **Dynastic Revival (6th Century AD)**: The decline of the Kalabhras led to the re-emergence of the Pallavas, Cholas, and Pandyas as dominant powers.
- **Architectural Legacy**: The Pallavas pioneered the **Dravidian architectural style** (e.g., Mahabalipuram), which was later perfected by the Cholas and Pandyas (e.g., Brihadeshwara Temple).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key South Indian Dynasties

#### A. Pallava Dynasty (Established $\sim$4th Century AD)
*   **Foundation**: Simhavishnu was the first prominent ruler who revived Pallava power.
*   **Expansion**: His son, **Mahendravarman-I**, expanded the kingdom northwards up to the **River Krishna**.
*   **Legacy**: Known for promoting Dravidian architecture, particularly rock-cut temples in **Mahabalipuram**.

#### B. Chola Dynasty (Ancient roots, revived 6th Century AD)
*   **Early History**: One of the oldest dynasties, dating back to the Sangam period (3rd Century BC).
*   **Revival**: **King Kadungon** revived the dynasty towards the end of the 6th century AD by defeating the Kalabhras.
*   **Later Peaks**: Under later rulers like Raja Raja Chola I and Rajendra Chola I, the empire expanded to Southeast Asia and built architectural wonders like the **Brihadeshwara Temple**.

#### C. Chalukya Dynasty (Early 6th Century AD)
*   **Foundation**: Rose to sovereignty under **Pulakesin-I**.
*   **Conflict & Reach**: Pulakesin-II became famous for resisting the invasion of the North Indian emperor **Harshavardhana**.
*   **Legacy**: Renowned for their contributions to temple architecture, specifically the **Badami Cave Temples**.

#### D. Pandya Dynasty
*   **Region**: Ruled the southernmost districts of Tamil Nadu from **Madurai**, with the **Vaigai River basin** as their heartland.
*   **Economy**: Famous for their pearl trade and flourishing maritime relations with ancient Rome and Greece.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Statement | Fact Check | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "The Cholas originated in the 6th century AD." | **Incorrect** | The Cholas existed during the **Sangam period** (3rd Century BC). King Kadungon only *revived* them in the 6th century AD. |
| "The Kalabhras were a long-standing empire." | **Incorrect** | They were a dynasty that *disrupted* established houses in early centuries AD and were eventually overthrown. |
