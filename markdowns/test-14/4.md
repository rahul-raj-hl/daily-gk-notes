# 📌 Currency Reforms of Sher Shah Suri (Rupiya) & Medieval Indian Coinage

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Rupiya**.
- **Founder of Sur Dynasty**: **Sher Shah Suri** (original name: *Farid Khan*), ruled the Sur Empire from **1540 to 1545 CE**.
- **The Silver Coin (Rupiya)**: Sher Shah introduced the standard silver coin named **Rupiya** weighing **178 grains** (approx. 11.53 grams), which became the direct precursor of the modern Indian Rupee.
- **Trimetallic Currency System**:
  - **Silver Coin**: **Rupiya** (178 grains)
  - **Gold Coin**: **Mohur** (approx. 169 grains)
  - **Copper Coin**: **Dam** (approx. 380 grains; ratio was $1\text{ Rupiya} = 64\text{ Dams}$)
- **Key Military Conquests**: Defeated Mughal Emperor Humayun in the **Battle of Chausa (1539)** and the **Battle of Kannauj/Bilgram (1540)**.
- **Grand Trunk Road (*Sadak-e-Azam*)**: Rebuilt and extended the ancient highway spanning from Sonargaon (Bengal) through Calcutta, Delhi, Lahore to Peshawar.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Administrative & Economic Reforms of Sher Shah Suri

- **Currency Standardization**:
  - Abolished previously debased mixed-metal coinage and established a tri-metallic currency standard with fixed weights, pure metals, and standard legal tender across his empire.
  - Engraved his name and titles in Arabic script and Nagari (Devanagari) script on coins.
- **Revenue Administration (Zabt System)**:
  - Introduced land classification (good, middle, bad) and fixed land revenue at one-third ($\frac{1}{3}\text{rd}$) of the average produce.
  - Introduced the twin documents: **Patta** (title deed defining the holding and assessed tax) and **Qabuliyat** (deed of agreement signed by the peasant).
  - Used the measurement standard **Gaz-i-Sikandari** (32 digits/points) with a rope made of hemp/iron chain.
- **Infrastructure & Communication**:
  - Constructed four major national highways, the most prominent being the **Sadak-e-Azam** (later renamed **Grand Trunk Road** by Lord Auckland).
  - Built **Sarais** (rest houses/inns) at every two *Kos* (approx. 8 km), equipped with separate lodging for Hindus and Muslims, postal relays (*Dak Chowkis*), and fresh water wells.
- **Architecture**:
  - Built the **Purana Qila** and *Qila-i-Kuhna Mosque* in Delhi.
  - Built the famous **Rohtas Fort** in Pakistan (a UNESCO World Heritage Site).
  - His own magnificent octagonal tomb is situated amidst an artificial lake in **Sasaram, Bihar**.

---

### 2. Overview of Medieval Coins in the Options

- **Silver Tankah & Copper Jittal**:
  - Introduced by Delhi Sultanate ruler **Shamsuddin Iltutmish** (Slave Dynasty). The *Tankah* was a pure silver coin weighing 175 grains.
- **Rupiya**:
  - Introduced by **Sher Shah Suri**; adopted and continued by Akbar and subsequent Mughal rulers, and eventually by the British East India Company.
- **Mohur**:
  - High-value gold coin standardized by Sher Shah Suri and minted extensively during the Mughal Empire.
- **Dinar**:
  - Gold coin introduced/standardized during the Delhi Sultanate (notably by **Muhammad bin Tughluq**, who also experimented with copper/brass *token currency*).
