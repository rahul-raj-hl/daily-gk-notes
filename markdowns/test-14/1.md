# 📌 Bandipur National Park & Protected Areas of Karnataka

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Karnataka**.
- **Location**: Situated in the Chamarajanagar district of **Karnataka**.
- **Establishment**: Established as a National Park and Tiger Reserve under *Project Tiger* in **1974** (originally created as Venugopala Wildlife Park by the Maharaja of Mysore in 1931).
- **Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve**: Forms an integral core part of the **Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve** (India's first biosphere reserve, est. 1986).
- **Contiguous Protected Areas**: Shares borders with **Nagarhole National Park** (Karnataka) to the northwest, **Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary** (Kerala) to the southwest, and **Mudumalai National Park** (Tamil Nadu) to the south.
- **Rivers**: Bounded by the **Kabini River** to the north and the **Moyar River** to the south.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The 5 National Parks of Karnataka

*Crucial national parks located in Karnataka:*

- **1. Bandipur National Park**:
  - Renowned for its high density of Bengal tigers, Asian elephants, and Indian gaurs.
  - Key rivers: Kabini, Moyar, and Nugu.
- **2. Nagarhole National Park (Rajiv Gandhi National Park)**:
  - Located in Kodagu and Mysuru districts; separated from Bandipur by the Kabini reservoir.
- **3. Bannerghatta National Park**:
  - Located near Bengaluru; features India's first biological park with a dedicated **Butterfly Park** and safari.
- **4. Kudremukh National Park**:
  - Located in Chikkamagaluru district in the Western Ghats (a biodiversity hotspot famous for shola grasslands and lion-tailed macaques).
- **5. Anshi National Park (Kali Tiger Reserve)**:
  - Located in Uttara Kannada district; famous for its dense evergreen forests and presence of the black panther.

---

### 2. Protected Areas in the Other States

- **Tamil Nadu**:
  - Mudumalai National Park, Mukurthi National Park, Guindy National Park, Gulf of Mannar Marine National Park, Anamalai Tiger Reserve (Indira Gandhi NP).
- **Odisha**:
  - Similipal National Park (Biosphere Reserve, famous for melanistic tigers), Bhitarkanika National Park (famous for saltwater crocodiles and Olive Ridley turtles).
- **Chhattisgarh**:
  - Indravati (Kutru) National Park, Kanger Valley National Park, Guru Ghasidas (Sanjay) National Park (54th Tiger Reserve).
