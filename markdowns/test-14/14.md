# 📌 Solung Festival, Adi Tribe of Arunachal Pradesh & Traditional Beverages

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Apong**.
- **The Festival**: **Solung** is the socio-religious agro-based harvest festival of the **Adi tribe** of **Arunachal Pradesh**, celebrated annually in the first week of **September** (spanning 5 days).
- **The Traditional Beverage (Apong)**: An indigenous, naturally fermented **rice beer** (or millet beer) prepared using traditional yeast starter cakes (*Aopo-pitha*) and roasted paddy husk ash (*Nogin*), integral to communal bonding, hospitality, and ritual offerings.
- **Deities Invoked**: Offerings and prayers are made to **Kine Nane** (Goddess of wealth and crops), **Doying Bote** (God of wisdom/heaven), and **Gumin Soyin** (deity of the household) for bumper crop yields and protection of cattle.
- **Iconic Dance**: The traditional **Ponung dance**, performed by women in a circle led by the lead singer/narrator (*Miri*), chanting mythological epics.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Solung Festival of the Adi Tribe

- **Three Main Parts of Solung**:
  1. **Soli-Lunyung**: The opening phase involving ritual animal offerings (*Mithun* / pig sacrifice), distribution of meat, and preparation of feasts.
  2. **Dorung**: The feast day where community members and relatives visit each other, exchange greetings, and drink **Apong**.
  3. **Ekop (or Lutor)**: The concluding ritual performed to erect protective charms and arrows at the village boundaries to ward off evil spirits (*Taro*).
- **Significance of Mithun (*Bos frontalis*)**: Considered a sacred bovine animal in Arunachal Pradesh and the ultimate symbol of social status and ritual wealth.

---

### 2. Traditional Rice Beverages of Northeast India

- **Apong (Arunachal Pradesh & Assam)**:
  - Prepared by the **Adi, Mising, and Nyishi** communities.
  - Divided mainly into two varieties: *Poro Apong* (dark/blackish rice beer made with ash) and *Nogin Apong* (milky white rice beer).
- **Zutho (Nagaland)**:
  - Fermented rice drink prepared traditionally by the **Angami and Sema Nagas**.
- **Judima (Assam)**:
  - Traditional rice wine brewed by the **Dimasa tribe** of Assam; received a **Geographical Indication (GI) Tag**.
- **Yu (Manipur)**:
  - Traditional fermented rice beverage brewed by the Meitei and tribal communities in Manipur.
- **Chhang (Ladakh, Sikkim & Himalayan Belts)**:
  - Warm Himalayan fermented beverage brewed from barley, millet, or buckwheat grains.

---

### 3. Analysis of Other Options

- **Soma**:
  - An intoxicating, sacred ritual elixir mentioned in ancient **Vedic literature (Rigveda - 9th Mandala / Soma Mandala)**, extracted from the stalks of the *Soma* plant; has no connection with the tribal Solung festival.
- **Thukpa**:
  - A popular Tibetan/Himalayan hot **noodle soup** served with vegetables or minced meat; it is a food dish, not a traditional beverage.
- **Chhang**:
  - Fermented millet/barley beer indigenous to the **Trans-Himalayan** regions of **Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh, Sikkim, and Tibet**.
