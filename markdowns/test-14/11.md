# 📌 First Integrated Aqua Park in Nagaland & PMMSY

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Nagaland**.
- **The Project**: Prime Minister Narendra Modi virtually laid the foundation stone for the **first Integrated Aqua Park** of Nagaland at **Tzudikong** in **Mokokchung district**.
- **Scheme**: Sanctioned under the flagship scheme **Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)**.
- **Core Objectives**:
  - Enhance local **fish and quality fish seed production**.
  - Establish a comprehensive resource center and forward market linkages.
  - Generate sustainable livelihoods and self-employment avenues for rural youth.
- **Nodal Ministry**: Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying, Government of India.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Integrated Aqua Parks under PMMSY

*Concept, infrastructure components, and developmental significance:*

- **Concept**: An Integrated Aqua Park functions as a holistic "One-Stop Centre" bringing together multiple elements of the aquaculture value chain into a centralized multi-functional facility.
- **Key Infrastructure Components**:
  - Modern fish broodstock development and high-quality fish seed hatcheries.
  - Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS) and Biofloc technology units.
  - Feed manufacturing mills, training facilities, and quality testing labs.
  - Post-harvest management units (cold storage, ice plants, and processing lines).
  - Direct wholesale and retail distribution market platforms with digital traceability.

---

### 2. Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY) Overview

- **Launch Date**: Formally launched on **10 September 2020** under the *Aatmanirbhar Bharat* package.
- **Investment Outlay**: An estimated record total investment of **₹20,050 crore** over a period of 5 years (FY 2020–21 to FY 2024–25 / extended).
- **Key Targets**:
  - Enhance national fish production to **22 million metric tonnes (MMT)**.
  - Double fisheries export earnings to **₹1,00,000 crore**.
  - Increase aquaculture productivity from 3 tonnes/hectare to 5 tonnes/hectare.
  - Reduce post-harvest losses from 20–25% to around 10%.
  - Generate direct and indirect employment opportunities for 55 lakh fishers and fish farmers.
