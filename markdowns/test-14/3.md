# 📌 'Hind Swaraj' (1909) & Key Literary Works of Modern Indian Leaders

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Mahatma Gandhi**.
- **'Hind Swaraj' (Indian Home Rule)**: Written in **1909** by **Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi** in his native **Gujarati** language aboard the ship *SS Kildonan Castle* during his voyage from London to South Africa.
- **Central Theme**: Critiques modern Western industrial civilization, outlines Gandhi's philosophy of **Swaraj** (self-rule / self-control), and introduces **Satyagraha** (soul force / truth force) as the primary tool of non-violent resistance.
- **Banned by Britishers**: The Gujarati edition was banned by the British colonial authorities in 1910 as a "seditious text", prompting Gandhi to translate it into English himself.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Literary Works and Newspapers of Mahatma Gandhi

- **Major Books**:
  - ***Hind Swaraj*** (1909) – Philosophical manifesto of non-violence and self-reliance.
  - ***The Story of My Experiments with Truth*** (1927) – Autobiography originally written in Gujarati; translated into English by Mahadev Desai.
  - ***Satyagraha in South Africa*** (1928) – Chronicle of the struggle against racial discrimination in Transvaal/Natal.
  - ***From Yeravda Mandir*** (1932) – Letters written from prison exploring moral vows (Ashram observances).
  - ***India of My Dreams*** – Compilation of his visionary essays on ideal societal structure.
- **Major Newspapers / Periodicals**:
  - *Indian Opinion* (South Africa, 1903)
  - *Young India* (1919)
  - *Navajivan* (Gujarati, 1919)
  - *Harijan* (1933; also *Harijan Bandhu* and *Harijan Sevak*)

---

### 2. Major Works & Journals of Other Leaders in Options

*Important publications of prominent national leaders:*

- **Bal Gangadhar Tilak ("Lokmanya")**:
  - **Books**: *Gita Rahasya* (written in Mandalay Jail), *The Arctic Home in the Vedas*, *The Orion*.
  - **Newspapers**: *Kesari* (in Marathi) and *Mahratta* (in English).
- **Gopal Krishna Gokhale (Political Guru of Gandhi)**:
  - Founded the **Servants of India Society** (1905).
  - Founded the English daily newspaper ***The Hitavada*** in 1911 in Nagpur.
  - Associated with the journal *Sudharak*.
- **Jawaharlal Nehru**:
  - **Books**: *The Discovery of India*, *Glimpses of World History*, *An Autobiography (Toward Freedom)*.
  - **Newspaper**: Founded the ***National Herald*** (1938).
