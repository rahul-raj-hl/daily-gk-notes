# 📌 Metallurgy & Metals in the Indus Valley Civilisation (Harappan Culture)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Iron**.
- **Bronze Age Civilisation**: The Indus Valley Civilisation (IVC, $\sim 2600\text{--}1900\text{ BCE}$) belongs strictly to the **Bronze Age** (Chalcolithic/Bronze Phase).
- **Metals Known to Harappans**: **Copper, Bronze, Gold, Silver, Lead, and Electrum**.
- **Unknown Metal**: **Iron** was completely unknown to Harappan people. Iron was introduced to the Indian subcontinent much later during the **Later Vedic Period** ($\sim 1000\text{ BCE}$, found at Atranjikhera, UP).
- **Bronze Making**: Harappans manufactured bronze by alloying **Copper with Tin** ($\sim 90:10$ ratio) and occasionally with **Arsenic**.
- **Lost-Wax Technique (*Cire Perdue*)**: Used to cast famous metallic masterpieces like the bronze **"Dancing Girl" of Mohenjo-daro**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Metallurgy of the Indus Valley Civilisation

*How Harappans sourced, processed, and utilized different metals:*

- **1. Copper ($Cu$) & Bronze**:
  - **Most Widely Used Metal**: Copper was the primary functional metal used for making tools, utensils, axes, saws, chisels, and weapons (spears, daggers).
  - **Sources**: Copper was imported primarily from the **Khetri mines in Rajasthan** and Oman (Magan) in West Asia. Tin was sourced from **Afghanistan** and **Hazaribagh (Jharkhand)**.
- **2. Gold ($Au$) & Silver ($Ag$)**:
  - Used predominantly for making ornaments (beads, bangles, necklaces, brooches) and luxury vessels.
  - **Sources**: Gold was sourced from the **Kolar gold fields (Karnataka)**, Persia, and Afghanistan. Silver was sourced from **Afghanistan, Persia (Iran)**, and Rajasthan.
  - Silver was more abundant in Harappan sites than gold.
- **3. Absence of Iron ($Fe$)**:
  - Iron smelting requires high furnace temperatures ($> 1500^\circ\text{C}$) and specialized reducing conditions, which were not developed until the **Iron Age** ($\sim 1000\text{ BCE}$, associated with Painted Grey Ware / PGW culture).
  - In Rigvedic texts, copper/bronze was referred to as *'Ayas'*, while in Later Vedic texts, iron was specifically termed *'Shyama Ayas'* or *'Krishna Ayas'*.

---

### 2. Major Metals & Their Prehistoric Trade Links

- **Khetri (Rajasthan)** $\rightarrow$ Copper
- **Kolar (Karnataka)** $\rightarrow$ Gold
- **Badakhshan (Afghanistan)** $\rightarrow$ Lapis Lazuli (deep-blue semi-precious stone)
- **Oman (Magan)** $\rightarrow$ Copper
- **Baluchistan / Iran** $\rightarrow$ Silver and Lead
- **Saurashtra / Gujarat** $\rightarrow$ Carnelian, Agate, Shells
