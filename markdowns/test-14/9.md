# 📌 Geographical Distribution of the 16 Mahajanapadas (West to East Sequence)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Avanti, Chedi, Vatsa, Magadha, Anga**.
- **Historical Context**: In the 6th century BCE (Second Urbanisation), ancient India saw the rise of **16 major territorial states (Mahajanapadas)**.
- **Literary Sources**: Listed comprehensively in the Buddhist text ***Anguttara Nikaya*** (part of Sutta Pitaka) and the Jain text ***Bhagavati Sutra***.
- **West-to-East Alignment**:
  $$\text{Avanti (Malwa/MP)} \longrightarrow \text{Chedi (Bundelkhand)} \longrightarrow \text{Vatsa (Prayagraj/UP)} \longrightarrow \text{Magadha (Patna-Gaya/Bihar)} \longrightarrow \text{Anga (Bhagalpur-Munger/Bihar)}$$

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Geographical Location of the 5 Mahajanapadas (West $\rightarrow$ East)

*Step-by-step spatial progression across Central and Eastern India:*

1. **Avanti (Farthest West among the given)**:
   - **Region**: Modern Malwa region of Western Madhya Pradesh (Ujjain and Narmada river valley).
   - **Capitals**: Split into two parts by the Vindhyas:
     - Northern Avanti: **Ujjayini (Ujjain)**
     - Southern Avanti: **Mahishmati**
   - **Famous King**: King Pradyota (*Chanda Pradyota*).
2. **Chedi**:
   - **Region**: Eastern part of modern **Bundelkhand** region (along the Ken River) spanning Madhya Pradesh and southern Uttar Pradesh.
   - **Capital**: **Suktimati / Sotthivati**.
   - **Famous King**: Shishupala (mentioned in the Mahabharata).
3. **Vatsa**:
   - **Region**: Situated around the confluence of the Ganga and Yamuna near modern **Prayagraj (Allahabad)**, Uttar Pradesh.
   - **Capital**: **Kaushambi** (modern Kosam).
   - **Famous King**: King Udayana (the romantic protagonist of Bhasa's play *Svapnavasavadatta*).
4. **Magadha**:
   - **Region**: Patna, Gaya, and Nalanda districts of Southern Bihar (bounded by the Son River on the west and the Ganga on the north).
   - **Capitals**: **Girivraja / Rajagriha** (surrounded by 5 hills), later shifted to **Pataliputra** by Udayin.
   - **Famous Dynasties**: Haryanka, Shishunaga, Nanda, and Maurya.
5. **Anga (Farthest East among the given)**:
   - **Region**: Bhagalpur and Munger districts of Eastern Bihar, extending to the borders of Bengal (west of Rajmahal hills).
   - **Capital**: **Champa** (ancient *Malini*, situated at the confluence of the Ganga and Champa rivers).
   - **Annexation**: Annexed into Magadha by **Bimbisara**, marking Magadha's first major imperial expansion.
