# 📌 Chotanagpur Plateau & Peninsular Drainage System

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Verdict**: **Both A and R are true, but R is NOT the correct explanation of A**.
- **Assertion (A) is TRUE**: The **Chotanagpur Plateau** forms the northeastern extension of the **Peninsular Plateau** of India, situated primarily in **Jharkhand** and extending into parts of Odisha, West Bengal, Bihar, and Chhattisgarh.
- **Reason (R) is TRUE**: The plateau is drained by major radial/dendritic rivers like the **Damodar, Subarnarekha, North Koel, South Koel, and Barakar**.
- **Why R does not explain A**: The classification of the Chotanagpur Plateau as part of the Peninsular Plateau is based on **geological origin and lithology** (ancient Precambrian crystalline igneous and metamorphic rocks of Gondwanaland), not on its surface river drainage.
- **"Ruhr of India"**: Known as the mineral heartland of India due to rich reserves of **coal, iron ore, mica, bauxite, copper, and limestone**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Physiography & Geology of the Chotanagpur Plateau

- **Geological Composition**: Formed of ancient **Archean granite and gneiss** along with Gondwana sedimentary coal-bearing strata.
- **Sub-divisions of the Plateau**:
  1. **Ranchi Plateau**: The largest part, characterized by rolling topography and undulating "Pats" (flat-topped laterite mesas).
  2. **Hazaribagh Plateau**: Lies north of the Damodar trough.
  3. **Koderma Plateau**: Famous for rich mica deposits (*"Mica Capital of India"*).
  4. **Rajmahal Hills**: Forms the northeastern edge of the plateau, composed of basaltic lava traps.
- **The Malda Gap (Garo-Rajmahal Gap)**: A tectonic depression separating the Chotanagpur Plateau from the **Shillong (Meghalaya) Plateau** and Karbi Anglong Hills in the Northeast.

---

### 2. Major River Systems Draining the Plateau

*Key rivers originating from or flowing through the Chotanagpur Plateau:*

- **1. Damodar River**:
  - **Origin**: Near Chandwa (Tori), Latehar district in the Chotanagpur Plateau.
  - **Rift Valley**: Flows through a prominent down-faulted rift valley containing rich Gondwana coal seams (Jharia, Raniganj, Bokaro, Giridih).
  - **Historical Nickname**: Known as the *"Sorrow of Bengal"* due to historical devastating floods before the construction of the **Damodar Valley Corporation (DVC, 1948)** multi-purpose dams (Tilaiya, Maithon, Panchet, Konar).
  - **Tributaries**: Barakar, Konar, Jamunia, Bokaro.
- **2. Subarnarekha River**:
  - **Origin**: Nagri village near **Ranchi**.
  - **Course**: Flows through Jharkhand, West Bengal, and Odisha to empty directly into the **Bay of Bengal** without joining any other major river.
  - **Key Landmark**: Forms the magnificent **Hundru Falls** (98 m) on the Ranchi plateau; passes through the industrial city of **Jamshedpur** (Tatanagar).
- **3. North Koel & South Koel**:
  - **North Koel**: Rises on the Ranchi Plateau and flows north to join the **Son River** (a right-bank tributary of the Ganga).
  - **South Koel**: Rises near Ranchi, flows south into Odisha, and joins the **Sankh River** at Vedvyas (Rourkela) to form the **Brahmani River**.
