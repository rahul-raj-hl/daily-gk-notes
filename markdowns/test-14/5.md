# 📌 Indian National Congress (INC) Sessions in Uttar Pradesh

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **1905 Banaras Session**: Presided over by **Gopal Krishna Gokhale**; marked the formal proclamation of the **Swadeshi Movement** against the Partition of Bengal.
- **1916 Lucknow Session**: Presided over by **Ambica Charan Mazumdar**; witnessed the **reunion of Moderates and Extremists** and the signing of the historic **Lucknow Pact** between the INC and the All India Muslim League.
- **1925 Kanpur Session**: Presided over by **Sarojini Naidu**, making her the **first Indian woman President** of the INC.
- **1946 Meerut Session**: Presided over by **J. B. Kripalani**; the last INC session held prior to Indian Independence (Kripalani served as INC President at the time of Independence on August 15, 1947).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### Detailed Analysis of Landmark Sessions Held in Uttar Pradesh

#### 1. 1905 – Banaras Session
- **President**: Gopal Krishna Gokhale.
- **Key Outcome**: Expressed deep resentment against Lord Curzon's **Partition of Bengal (1905)** and formally endorsed the **Swadeshi and Boycott Movement**.

#### 2. 1916 – Lucknow Session
- **President**: Ambica Charan Mazumdar.
- **Historic Milestones**:
  - **Reunion**: Moderates and Extremists (led by Bal Gangadhar Tilak) reunited after the bitter 1907 Surat Split.
  - **Lucknow Pact (1916)**: Joint declaration between Congress and the All-India Muslim League (negotiated by Tilak and Muhammad Ali Jinnah), wherein Congress accepted the principle of separate communal electorates for Muslims.
  - **Gandhian Milestone**: Raj Kumar Shukla met Mahatma Gandhi during this session to invite him to investigate the plight of indigo farmers in Champaran.

#### 3. 1925 – Kanpur Session
- **President**: Sarojini Naidu ("Nightingale of India").
- **Key Distinction**: First **Indian** woman to preside over an INC session (*Note: Annie Besant was the overall first woman president at the 1917 Calcutta session*).

#### 4. 1946 – Meerut Session
- **President**: Acharya J. B. Kripalani.
- **Significance**: The 54th and final pre-independence session of the INC. Kripalani continued in the presidency through August 1947.
