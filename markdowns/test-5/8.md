# 📌 Important Himalayan Ranges

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- The Himalayas consist of several important mountain ranges, each with distinct geographical features.
- Questions are commonly asked about their location, famous peaks, glaciers, valleys, and passes.
- Karakoram belongs to the Trans-Himalayan system and contains India's largest glacier.

---

## 🏔️ Pir Panjal Range

*One-liner:* The largest range of the Lesser Himalayas.

- Part of the **Lesser (Inner) Himalayas**.
- Separates the **Kashmir Valley** from the outer Himalayan region.
- Lies south of the Greater Himalayas.
- Rich in forests and tourism.

### Important Passes

- Banihal Pass
- Pir Panjal Pass
- Rohtang Pass

---

## 🏔️ Karakoram Range

*One-liner:* India's highest and most rugged mountain range.

- Part of the **Trans-Himalayas**.
- Located in the northernmost region of India.
- Extends across India, Pakistan and China.
- Extremely cold and glacier-rich.

### Important Facts

- K2 (Mount Godwin-Austen) – World's 2nd highest peak.
- Siachen Glacier – India's largest glacier.
- Karakoram Pass connects Ladakh with Xinjiang (China).

---

## 🏔️ Ladakh Range

*One-liner:* A major Trans-Himalayan range known for its cold desert.

- Located north of the Zanskar Range.
- Famous for barren landscapes and high-altitude passes.

### Important Passes

- Khardung La
- Chang La

---

## 🏔️ Dhauladhar Range

*One-liner:* Southern branch of the Lesser Himalayas in Himachal Pradesh.

- Located mainly in Kangra district.
- Forms the backdrop of Dharamshala and McLeod Ganj.
- Popular trekking destination.

### Important Trek

- Triund Trek

> **Explanation:** K2, the world's second-highest mountain, is situated in the Karakoram Range.
