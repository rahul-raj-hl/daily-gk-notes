# 📌 Essential Plant Nutrients

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Plants require essential nutrients for growth, photosynthesis, and reproduction.
- Nitrogen is the most important macronutrient for vegetative growth.
- Deficiency symptoms help identify which nutrient is lacking.
- Mobile nutrients show deficiency first in older leaves.

---

## 🌱 Nitrogen (N)

*One-liner:* Essential for green leafy growth and protein formation.

### Functions

- Major component of chlorophyll.
- Required for photosynthesis.
- Forms amino acids and proteins.
- Essential for DNA and RNA synthesis.
- Promotes vegetative growth and cell division.

### Deficiency Symptoms

- Stunted plant growth.
- Yellowing of leaves (Chlorosis).
- Older leaves affected first (Nitrogen is mobile).

---

## 🟣 Phosphorus (P)

*One-liner:* Promotes strong roots and energy transfer.

### Functions

- Root development.
- Flowering and seed formation.
- Energy transfer (ATP).

### Deficiency Symptoms

- Dark green or purplish leaves.
- Poor root growth.
- Delayed maturity.

---

## 🍁 Potassium (K)

*One-liner:* Improves disease resistance and water regulation.

### Functions

- Regulates water balance.
- Improves disease resistance.
- Strengthens stems.
- Enhances crop quality.

### Deficiency Symptoms

- Yellowing or browning of leaf margins.
- Weak stems.
- Poor disease resistance.

---

## 🦴 Calcium (Ca)

*One-liner:* Essential for healthy cell walls and growing tissues.

### Functions

- Strengthens cell walls.
- Supports root and shoot growth.
- Required for cell division.

### Deficiency Symptoms

- Weak cell walls.
- Poor root and shoot tip development.
- Death of growing points.

---

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Nutrient | Major Function | Deficiency Symptom |
|----------|----------------|--------------------|
| Nitrogen | Chlorophyll & proteins | Yellow leaves (Chlorosis) |
| Phosphorus | Root growth & ATP | Purple leaves |
| Potassium | Water balance & disease resistance | Brown leaf margins |
| Calcium | Cell walls | Poor root & shoot growth |

---

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Nutrient | Remember |
|----------|----------|
| Nitrogen | Mobile → Older leaves affected |
| Calcium | Immobile → Younger leaves affected |
| Chlorosis | Yellowing of leaves |
| Necrosis | Death of plant tissue |

---

## 🎯 Memory Trick

👉 **NPK = Growth, Roots, Strength**

- 🌿 **N** → Green Growth
- 🌱 **P** → Powerful Roots
- 💪 **K** → Keeps Plants Strong

**Extra:**

- **N = New Green** → Less Nitrogen = Less Chlorophyll = Yellow Leaves

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Nitrogen is an essential component of:

- [ ] Cell wall
- [x] Chlorophyll
- [ ] Starch
- [ ] Cellulose

> **Explanation:** Nitrogen is a major component of chlorophyll, which is essential for photosynthesis.

---

### Q2

Nitrogen deficiency first appears in:

- [x] Older leaves
- [ ] Younger leaves
- [ ] Flowers
- [ ] Fruits

> **Explanation:** Nitrogen is a mobile nutrient, so it moves from older to younger leaves when deficient.

---

### Q3

Which nutrient deficiency causes purplish leaves?

- [ ] Potassium
- [ ] Calcium
- [x] Phosphorus
- [ ] Nitrogen

> **Explanation:** Phosphorus deficiency often results in dark green or purplish leaves.

---

### Q4

Browning of leaf margins ("leaf scorch") is caused by deficiency of:

- [ ] Nitrogen
- [ ] Calcium
- [x] Potassium
- [ ] Magnesium

> **Explanation:** Potassium deficiency commonly causes yellowing and browning along leaf edges.

---

### Q5

Which nutrient is essential for strong cell walls?

- [ ] Nitrogen
- [ ] Potassium
- [ ] Phosphorus
- [x] Calcium

> **Explanation:** Calcium is required for the formation and stability of plant cell walls.
