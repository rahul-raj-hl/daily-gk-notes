# 📌 EXIM Bank (Export-Import Bank of India)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Apex financial institution for promoting India's international trade.
- Established on **1 January 1982** under the Export-Import Bank of India Act, 1981.
- Started operations on **1 March 1982**.
- Wholly owned by the Government of India.

---

## 🔑 Key Points

### 🏦 What is EXIM Bank?

*One-liner:* India's premier export finance institution.

- Provides financial assistance to exporters and importers.
- Promotes India's foreign trade.
- Supports Indian companies expanding overseas.
- Coordinates export financing institutions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- 💰 Export and import financing.
- 🌍 Promote international trade.
- 🤝 Support overseas investment by Indian companies.
- 📈 Improve India's global trade competitiveness.

---

## 👥 Governance

- Headed by a **Chairman & Managing Director (CMD)**.
- Board consists of nominees from:
  - RBI
  - IDBI
  - ECGC
  - Government of India

---

## 📍 Headquarters

- Mumbai, Maharashtra.
