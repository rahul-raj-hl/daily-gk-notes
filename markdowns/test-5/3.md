# 📌 Charter Act, 1813

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Renewed the East India Company's charter for another 20 years.
- Ended the Company's trade monopoly except for tea and trade with China.
- Allowed Christian missionaries to work in India.
- First Act to officially support education in India.

---

## 🔑 Key Provisions

### 🏛️ Renewal of Charter

*One-liner:* Extended the East India Company's rule for another 20 years.

- Company's political control continued.
- Charter renewed till 1833.

---

### 🚢 Trade Monopoly

*One-liner:* Monopoly ended, but not completely.

- Monopoly over Indian trade abolished.
- Exceptions:
  - Tea trade
  - Trade with China

- British merchants were allowed to trade freely with India.

---

### ✝️ Christian Missionaries

*One-liner:* Missionaries were officially permitted to enter India.

- Allowed to spread Christianity.
- Established schools and educational institutions.

---

### 🎓 Promotion of Education

*One-liner:* First Charter Act to recognize the responsibility of promoting education.

- ₹1 lakh per year allocated for education.
- Encouraged the growth of schools and colleges.

---

## ⭐ Significance

- Beginning of free trade in India.
- Reduced the commercial dominance of the East India Company.
- Increased British cultural and educational influence.
- Opened India to missionaries and private traders.

| Charter Act, 1833 | Monopoly completely abolished |
