# 📌 Important National Days (August)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- August contains several important national and international observances.
- Questions are frequently asked on dates, purpose, and related personalities/events.
- Focus on the association rather than just memorizing the date.

---

## 🗓️ August Timeline

### 📅 7 August — National Handloom Day

*One-liner:* Celebrates India's rich handloom heritage and the Swadeshi Movement.

- Started in **2015** by PM Narendra Modi.
- Commemorates the **Swadeshi Movement (1905)**.
- Promotes indigenous handloom products and weavers.

---

### 📅 12 August — International Youth Day

*One-liner:* Recognizes the role of youth in nation-building.

- Declared by the **United Nations (1999)**.
- First observed in **2000**.
- Promotes youth participation in achieving the **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**.

---

### 📅 23 August — National Space Day

*One-liner:* Celebrates India's achievement in lunar exploration.

- Declared in **2023**.
- Marks the successful landing of **Chandrayaan-3** near the Moon's south pole.
- Recognizes India's achievements in space science and innovation.

---

### 📅 29 August — National Sports Day

*One-liner:* Honours hockey legend Major Dhyan Chand.

- Celebrated on the birth anniversary of **Major Dhyan Chand**.
- Promotes sports culture and physical fitness.
- National Sports Awards are presented around this occasion.

---

## 📚 Related Facts

### 🧵 Swadeshi Movement (1905)

- Launched after the **Partition of Bengal (1905)**.
- Encouraged the use of indigenous goods.
- Boycotted foreign products.

---

### 🌕 Chandrayaan-3

- First successful soft landing near the Moon's south pole.
- Made India the **4th country** to achieve a soft landing on the Moon.
- ISRO mission launched in 2023.

---

### 🏑 Major Dhyan Chand

- Known as the **"Wizard of Hockey."**
- Won **3 Olympic Gold Medals** (1928, 1932, 1936).

---

### 🌍 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

- Adopted by the **United Nations in 2015**.
- Consist of **17 Goals** to be achieved by **2030**.
