# 📌 Cabinet Mission (1946)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Arrived in India on **24 March 1946**.
- Sent by British Prime Minister **Clement Attlee**.
- Proposed a united India and rejected immediate partition.
- Recommended the formation of the Constituent Assembly and an Interim Government.
- Its failure paved the way for the Partition of India.

---

## 🔑 Background

*One-liner:* The Cabinet Mission was Britain's final major effort to transfer power peacefully while keeping India united.

- Sent by the British Government in 1946.
- Aimed to resolve the constitutional deadlock between the Congress and the Muslim League.
- Tried to find a constitutional framework acceptable to all parties.

---

## 👥 Members of the Cabinet Mission

- Lord Pethick-Lawrence
- Sir Stafford Cripps
- A. V. Alexander

> **Remember:** The Mission consisted of **three British Cabinet Ministers**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Frame a Constitution for independent India.
- Establish the Constituent Assembly.
- Form an Interim Government with participation of major political parties.
- Ensure a peaceful transfer of power.

---

## 📜 Cabinet Mission Plan (16 May 1946)

*One-liner:* Proposed a federal structure while keeping India united.

### Main Features

- No immediate partition of India.
- Proposed a **Union of India**.
- Provinces grouped into **Group A, B and C**.
- Centre to control only:
  - Defence
  - Foreign Affairs
  - Communications
- Provinces to enjoy maximum autonomy.
- Constituent Assembly to draft the Constitution.

---

## ❌ Why Did It Fail?

- Congress wanted a **strong Central Government**.
- Muslim League demanded **Pakistan** and stronger safeguards for Muslims.
- Disagreement over compulsory provincial grouping.
- Political differences made implementation impossible.

---

## ⭐ Significance

- Led to the formation of the Constituent Assembly.
- Led to the formation of the Interim Government.
- Marked the last serious British attempt to preserve a united India.
- Its failure accelerated the Partition of India.
