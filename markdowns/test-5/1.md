# 📌 Ajanta Caves

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Located on the banks of the Waghora River in Maharashtra.
- Rock-cut Buddhist cave complex built between the 2nd Century BCE and 6th Century CE.
- Developed in two phases under the Satavahana and Vakataka dynasties.
- Famous for Buddhist murals, sculptures, Chaityas and Viharas.
- UNESCO World Heritage Site (1983).

---

## 📅 Development Phases

### Phase I (2nd–1st Century BCE)

*One-liner:* Early Buddhist phase under the Satavahanas.

- Patron: Satavahana Dynasty.
- Associated with Hinayana Buddhism.
- Stupa worship was prominent.
- Buddha was represented through symbols rather than idols.

---

### Phase II (5th–6th Century CE)

*One-liner:* Flourished under the Vakatakas with magnificent paintings.

- Patron: Vakataka Dynasty (King Harisena).
- Associated with Mahayana Buddhism.
- Buddha idols became common.
- Famous murals and sculptures were created.
