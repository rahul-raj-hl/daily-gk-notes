# 📌 National Sports Governance Framework: National Sports Board (NSB) & Tribunal Architecture

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Total Composition of NSB**: The **National Sports Board (NSB)** comprises a **Chairperson and two Members** (a total of 3 individuals).
- **Appointment Authority**: Appointed directly by the **Central Government** based on recommendations from a dedicated **Search-cum-Selection Committee**.
- **Apex Regulatory Mandate**:
  - Serves as the central authority responsible for granting, renewing, or revoking formal recognition of **National Sports Bodies (NSBs / National Sports Federations)**.
  - Enforces statutory compliance with strict governance parameters, financial transparency, election integrity, and ethical standards across sports federations.
- **Dedicated Adjudication Mechanism**: Operationalizes the **National Sports Tribunal (NST)** as an autonomous quasi-judicial/adjudicatory forum to fast-track sports dispute resolution.
- **Techno-Legal Integration**: Mandates digital compliance via an **online portal** for dispute filings and virtual hearings, systematically curbing the multiplicity of litigation in traditional civil courts.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Dual Institutional Architecture of Sports Governance

| Institutional Body | Composition & Head | Core Mandate & Jurisdiction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **National Sports Board (NSB)** | **Chairperson + 2 Members** (Total: 3) | • Regulatory, accreditation, and administrative oversight.<br>• Recognition and derecognition of National Sports Federations (NSFs).<br>• Monitoring compliance with tenure, age caps, and good governance norms. |
| **National Sports Tribunal (NST)** | Dedicated Adjudicatory Panel | • Dedicated appellate and dispute adjudication body for athletes, federations, and coaches.<br>• Decides on selection grievances, disciplinary actions, and governance deadlocks, bypassing prolonged civil litigation. |

---

### 2. Major Pillars of India's National Sports Governance Framework

| Governance Pillar | Statutory Mandate / Prescribed Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Age & Tenure Ceilings** | Enforces the National Sports Development Code of India (e.g., maximum age limit of **70 years** for office bearers; cooling-off periods and consecutive term restrictions). |
| **Athlete Representation** | Mandates representation of active/former sportspersons (with voting rights) in the executive management of all recognized federations (minimum $25\%$). |
| **Gender Inclusion** | Mandatory induction of female administrators and establishment of Internal Complaints Committees (ICC) under the PoSH Act. |
| **Anti-Doping Compliance** | Direct statutory alignment with the **National Anti-Doping Act, 2022** and the **National Anti-Doping Agency (NADA)**. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Body / Feature | Structural Detail | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **National Sports Board (NSB)** | **Chairperson + 2 Members** | Guessing larger board sizes (Chairperson + 3, 4, or 5 Members) |
| **NSB vs. NST** | • **NSB**: Regulatory & Administrative recognition<br>• **NST**: Quasi-judicial dispute resolution | Mixing the regulatory functions of NSB with the adjudicatory role of NST |
| **Sports Authority of India (SAI)** | **Executive training & infrastructure wing** (est. 1984) | Confusing policy governance (NSB) with athlete training (SAI) |
| **IOA (Indian Olympic Association)** | **Autonomous Olympic body** under IOC charter | Treating IOA as a statutory department of the Ministry |
