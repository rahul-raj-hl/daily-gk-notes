# 📌 World Asthma Day: GINA Mandate, Observance & Respiratory Health Matrix

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Statement Evaluation**:
  - **Statement I is CORRECT**: World Asthma Day is observed globally every year on the **first Tuesday of May** (e.g., May 5 in 2026).
  - **Statement II is INCORRECT**: The day was initiated in **1998** by the **Global Initiative for Asthma (GINA)** (a medical collaborative organization founded in 1993), *not* solely by the WHO (though GINA collaborates closely with WHO).
  - **Statement III is CORRECT**: The specific theme highlights *"Access to anti-inflammatory inhalers for everyone with asthma, still an urgent need."*
  - **Verdict**: **Only I and III** are correct.
- **Pathological Definition**: Asthma is a chronic non-communicable inflammatory disease affecting the **airways (bronchi and bronchioles)** of the lungs, characterized by bronchospasms, mucosal swelling, and excess mucus secretion.
- **Standard First-Line Treatment**: **Inhaled Corticosteroids (ICS)** combined with bronchodilators (e.g., Short-Acting Beta-Agonists / Salbutamol).

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Health Observance / Term | Schedule / Nature | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **World Asthma Day** | **First Tuesday of May** (GINA) | Marking fixed dates like May 1 or May 5 |
| **GINA vs. WHO** | **GINA is the primary organizer** in collaboration with WHO | Assuming WHO alone founded and organizes World Asthma Day |
| **Asthma vs. Bronchitis/Emphysema (COPD)** | • **Asthma**: Largely **reversible** airway obstruction.<br>• **COPD**: Largely **irreversible / progressive** airflow limitation. | Treating asthma as an infectious or irreversible condition |
| **Inhaler Types** | • **Reliever**: Short-acting bronchodilator (Salbutamol)<br>• **Controller**: Anti-inflammatory corticosteroid | Assuming inhalers only contain temporary muscle relaxants |
