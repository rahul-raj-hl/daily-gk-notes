# 📌 'Spice and Heal' Initiative: AYUSHEXCIL & Spices Board of India Collaboration

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Proposed Branding Campaign**: The **'Spice and Heal Initiative'** was formally launched under a strategic Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) signed between **AYUSHEXCIL** (Ayush Export Promotion Council) and the **Spices Board of India**.
- **Core Vision**: To globally position India as the premier destination and leader in **holistic health, plant-based nutraceuticals, and natural healing systems**.
- **Signatories & Ministerial Presence**:
  - Signed by **Dr. Anurag Sharma** (Chairman, AYUSHEXCIL) and **Shri M. S. Manivannan** (Secretary, Spices Board of India).
  - Executed in the presence of **Shri Prataprao Jadhav**, Union Minister of State (Independent Charge), Ministry of Ayush.
- **Key Strategic Pillars**:
  - Enhancement of product quality, end-to-end farm-to-shelf traceability, and rigorous scientific validation.
  - Active engagement with the **Codex Alimentarius Commission** to harmonize international standards for herbal functional foods and spice-derived nutraceuticals.
  - Joint global outreach through international trade expos to support farmers, MSMEs, startups, and exporters.
