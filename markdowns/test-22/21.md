# 📌 Sardar Patel Apex University: Mirzapur Approval & UP Development Matrix

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Approved Location**: The Uttar Pradesh State Cabinet, chaired by Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath, approved the issuance of a Letter of Intent (LoI) for establishing the **"Sardar Patel Apex University"** in **Mirzapur district**.
- **Specific Site**: Planned to be established in **Samaspur village**, located within the **Chunar tehsil** of Mirzapur district.
- **Accompanying Regional Clearances**:
  - **Power Sector (Mirzapur)**: Clearance of a ₹2,799.47 crore transmission project for setting up a $765/400\text{ kV}$ power substation to reinforce grid stability in the Vindhya region.
  - **Metro Expansion**: Approval of an MoU for the ₹5,801 crore **Charbagh to Vasant Kunj East-West Metro Corridor** in **Lucknow**, and land transfer for Corridor 2 in **Agra**.
  - **Healthcare Infrastructure**: Land transfer for the expansion of **Swaroop Rani Nehru Hospital** in **Prayagraj** and sanction of a 1,100-bed emergency care facility at **Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Institute of Medical Sciences (RMLIMS)** in **Lucknow**.
