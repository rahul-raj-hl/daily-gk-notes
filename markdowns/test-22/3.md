# 📌 Chandragupta II: 'Vikramaditya', Conquests & Cultural Zenith

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Title of 'Vikramaditya'**: **Chandragupta II** (reigned c. $375\text{--}415\text{ CE}$) assumed the prestigious title of **'Vikramaditya'** (*Sun of Prowess*) after completely annihilating the Western Kshatrapas (Sakas) in western India.
- **Other Imperial Titles**: *Sakari* (destroyer/enemy of the Sakas), *Simhavikrama*, *Devagupta*, *Devaraja*, and *Paramabhagavata*.
- **Mehrauli Iron Pillar Inscription**: The rust-resistant iron pillar situated at Mehrauli (Delhi) eulogizes a king named **'Chandra'**, widely identified by historians as Chandragupta II, commemorating his victories across Bengal (Vanga) and the north-western frontiers (Vahlika/Bactria).
- **First Silver Coins (*Rupaka*)**: Chandragupta II was the **first Gupta monarch to issue silver coins** (*Rupaka*), minted following his conquest of the silver-rich Saka territories of Gujarat and Saurashtra.
- **Nine Jewels (*Navaratnas*)**: His royal court (at Ujjain, his second capital) was renowned for housing nine celebrated scholars, including **Kalidasa**, **Varahamihira**, **Amarasimha**, and **Dhanvantari**.
- **Chinese Pilgrim Account**: The Chinese Buddhist monk **Faxian (Fa-Hien)** traveled through India during his reign ($399\text{--}412\text{ CE}$), documenting peace, economic prosperity, and mild governance.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Titles and Achievements of Prominent Gupta Rulers

| Gupta Ruler | Most Famous Titles / Epithets | Landmark Historical Milestone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sri Gupta** | *Maharaja* | **Founder** of the Gupta Dynasty (c. late 3rd century CE). |
| **Ghatotkacha** | *Maharaja* | Succeeded Sri Gupta; father of Chandragupta I. |
| **Chandragupta I** | *Maharajadhiraja* | Real founder of Gupta imperial power; founded the **Gupta Era (319–320 CE)**; married Licchavi princess Kumaradevi. |
| **Samudragupta** | *Kaviraja*, *Sarva-raj-ochchetta*, *Ashvamedha-parakrama*, *Vikramanka* | Termed the **"Indian Napoleon"** by Vincent Smith; military campaigns recorded on the **Prayag Prashasti** by court poet Harisena. |
| **Chandragupta II** | **Vikramaditya**, *Sakari*, *Simhavikrama*, *Paramabhagavata* | Conquered Western Sakas; court adorned by **Navaratnas**; issued first Gupta silver coins; erection of **Mehrauli Iron Pillar**. |
| **Kumaragupta I** | *Mahendraditya*, *Shakraditya* | Founded the world-renowned **Nalanda Mahavihara (University)**; introduced peacock-style coins. |
| **Skandagupta** | *Kramaditya*, *Vikramaditya* | Successfully repulsed the fierce **Huna invasion**; restored the **Sudarshana Lake** embankment (Junagadh Rock Inscription). |

### 2. The Nine Jewels (*Navaratnas*) in Chandragupta II's Court

| Scholar / Luminary | Field of Expertise | Famous Literary / Scientific Contribution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kalidasa** | Classical Sanskrit Literature & Drama | *Abhijnanashakuntalam*, *Meghaduta*, *Raghuvamsha*, *Kumarasambhava* |
| **Varahamihira** | Astronomy, Astrology & Mathematics | *Pancha-Siddhantika*, *Brihat Samhita*, *Brihat Jataka* |
| **Amarasimha** | Sanskrit Lexicography & Poetry | *Amarakosha* (celebrated Sanskrit thesaurus) |
| **Dhanvantari** | Medicine & Ayurveda | Known as the physician to the gods; developed medical treatises |
| **Vararuchi** | Sanskrit Grammar & Linguistics | *Prakrita Prakasha* (first formal grammar of Prakrit) |
| **Ghatakarpara** | Poetry & Sculpture | Classical Sanskrit lyrics and architectural treatises |
| **Kshapanaka** | Astrology | Astrological treatises (*Jyotishashastra*) |
| **Shanku** | Architecture & Civil Engineering | *Shilpa Shastra* texts |
| **Vetala Bhatta** | Occult Sciences & Magic | Attributed with the *Mantra Shastra* / folklore collections |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Historical Monarch | Imperial Title | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chandragupta II** | **Vikramaditya** | Confusing Chandragupta II with Chandragupta I or Chandragupta Maurya |
| **Chandragupta I** | *Maharajadhiraja* | Assuming Chandragupta I adopted 'Vikramaditya' |
| **Samudragupta** | *Kaviraja* / *Indian Napoleon* | Selecting Samudragupta for the title 'Vikramaditya' |
| **Skandagupta** | *Kramaditya* / *Vikramaditya* | Forgetting that Skandagupta's primary title in exams is associated with repulsing Hunas |
| **Hemu (Hemchandra)** | *Raja Vikramaditya* | Confusing medieval 16th-century Hemu with ancient Gupta Vikramaditya |
