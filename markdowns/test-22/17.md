# 📌 National Policy for Rare Diseases (NPRD): Financial Aid, CoEs & Management Framework

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Evaluation of All Three Statements**:
  - **Statement I is CORRECT**: The **National Policy for Rare Diseases (NPRD)**, formulated in **2021** by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW), categorizes rare diseases into **three distinct groups** based on clinical feasibility, treatment modalities, and cost requirements.
  - **Statement II is CORRECT**: In **August 2022**, the Central Government significantly enhanced financial assistance under the *Rashtriya Arogya Nidhi (RAN)* umbrella from **₹20 lakh to up to ₹50 lakh per patient** for treatment at any designated Centre of Excellence (CoE).
  - **Statement III is INCORRECT**: The network of designated **Centres of Excellence (CoEs)** for rare disease diagnosis and treatment was expanded from **8 to 15 premier tertiary healthcare institutes** (including two in North-East India), *not* 20.
  - **Correct Answer**: **Only I and II** (Tricky question with only 3% pass rate).
- **UMMID Initiative**: Spearheaded by the Department of Biotechnology (DBT), the **Unique Methods of Management of Inherited Disorders (UMMID)** initiative operates **NIDAN Kendras** (*National Inherited Diseases Administration Kendras*) to provide prenatal screening and genetic counseling.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Classification of Rare Diseases under NPRD 2021

| Disease Group | Clinical & Economic Criteria | Illustrative Disease Examples | Financial & Policy Support Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Group 1** | Disorders amenable to **one-time curative treatment** (e.g., stem cell transplantation, organ transplantation). | • Lysosomal Storage Disorders (amenable to HSCT)<br>• Severe Combined Immunodeficiency (SCID)<br>• Osteopetrosis<br>• Fanconi Anemia | Direct financial support up to **₹50 Lakh** provided through designated CoEs. |
| **Group 2** | Diseases requiring **long-term / lifelong therapy** where cost is relatively low, and disability is managed with regular dietary or hormonal supplements. | • Phenylketonuria (PKU)<br>• Maple Syrup Urine Disease (MSUD)<br>• Congenital Adrenal Hyperplasia<br>• Galactosemia | Managed via state governments and national health programs (special medical formulas, dietary supplements). |
| **Group 3** | Diseases for which **definitive treatment is available, but involves lifelong high costs** with variable clinical outcomes (Enzyme Replacement Therapies). | • Gaucher Disease<br>• Pompe Disease<br>• Spinal Muscular Atrophy (SMA)<br>• Cystic Fibrosis | Crowdfunding digital portal platform supported by voluntary corporate CSR donations and central CoE coordination. |

---

### 2. Institutional Architecture for Rare Diseases Management

| Institutional Wing | Design / Setup | Primary Operational Mandate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Centres of Excellence (CoEs)** | **15 Premier Institutes** *(e.g., AIIMS New Delhi, PGIMER Chandigarh, NIMHANS, JIPMER, SGPGI Lucknow)* | Specialized tertiary care hospitals designated for diagnostic validation, specialized treatment, genetic workups, and administering the ₹50 lakh financial grant. |
| **NIDAN Kendras (UMMID)** | DBT-supported genetic centers in government hospitals | Diagnostic testing, genetic counseling for expecting mothers, and carrier screening. |
| **National Rare Disease Portal** | Digital Crowdfunding & Case Registration Platform | Connects corporate donors and philanthropists with Group 3 patients requiring expensive lifelong therapies. |
| **ICMR Initiatives** | Indian Council of Medical Research | Active clinical research on **6 repurposed off-patent formulations** and frontier advanced biologics like **CAR-T cell therapy**. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Parameter | Policy Fact | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Financial Assistance Ceiling** | **₹50 Lakh** | Assuming the old limit of ₹20 Lakh is still operational |
| **Total Number of CoEs** | **15 Institutes** | Selecting inflated numbers like 20, 25, or 50 |
| **UMMID vs. NIDAN** | • **UMMID**: The overarching scheme/initiative<br>• **NIDAN Kendra**: The physical diagnostic/counseling center | Treating UMMID and NIDAN as competing schemes |
| **Eligibility for ₹50 Lakh Aid** | **Any patient** receiving treatment at a designated CoE (not restricted to BPL families alone under revised guidelines) | Assuming financial assistance is strictly restricted to BPL cardholders |
