# 📌 Sikkim Police: 1897 Foundation, President's Police Colour & Security Milestones

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Year of Establishment**: The **Sikkim Police** was formally established in **1897** during the reign of the Chogyal (under the administrative supervision of the British Political Officer, John Claude White).
- **President's Honour**: Conferred with the prestigious **‘President of India’s Police Colour’** by President **Droupadi Murmu** in recognition of over 125 years of exceptional, community-friendly, and professional service.
- **President’s Police Colour Significance**: The highest honour that can be bestowed upon any military, paramilitary, or state police force in the Republic of India (*Nishan*).
- **Evolution of Sikkim's Polity**:
  - **1897**: Formed with a modest strength of 1 Head Constable and a few constables under the Namgyal Dynasty / British Protectorate.
  - **1975**: Following the **36th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1975**, Sikkim formally merged into the Indian Union as the **22nd State**, integrating the Sikkim Police into the Indian police framework under Article 371F.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Chronology of Sikkim's Political & Police Evolution

| Year / Milestone | Historical Event & Institutional Transition | Key Governance Aspect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1897** | **Formal Founding of Sikkim Police** | Created with a small outpost at Aritar near Rhenock to secure border trade routes connecting British India with Tibet. |
| **1950** | **Indo-Sikkim Peace Treaty** | Sikkim became an Indian Protectorate; India assumed responsibility for external defense, diplomacy, and strategic communication. |
| **1974** | **35th Constitutional Amendment Act** | Sikkim was conferred the unique status of an **'Associate State'** of India (Article 2A added). |
| **1975** | **36th Constitutional Amendment Act** | Fully repealed Associate status and admitted Sikkim as the **22nd full-fledged State** of the Indian Union; inserted **Article 371F** with special provisions. |
| **Recent** | **Conferment of President’s Police Colour** | Presented by President Droupadi Murmu, recognizing modern community policing, border safety, and crime prevention. |
