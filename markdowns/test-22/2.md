# 📌 RBI Shareholding Approvals: The 9.99% Investment Cap & Regulatory Framework

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Approved Holding Limit**: The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) granted approval to **Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited** (including subsidiaries and managed funds under the Kotak Mahindra Group) to acquire an aggregate holding of up to **9.99%** of the paid-up share capital or voting rights in **AU Small Finance Bank** and **Federal Bank**.
- **Statutory Provision**: Under **Section 12B of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949**, prior approval of the RBI is mandatory for acquiring $5\%$ or more of the paid-up share capital or voting rights in any banking company.
- **Strategic Ceiling**: The **9.99% cap** is standard for non-promoter institutional / non-financial entities to prevent hostile takeovers and ensure diversified ownership in private banks without granting operational control.
- **Fit and Proper Requirement**: Any entity acquiring a major shareholding ($\ge 5\%$) must continuously satisfy the RBI's stringent *"Fit and Proper"* criteria.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. RBI Shareholding & Ownership Caps in Indian Private Banks

| Category of Shareholder | Maximum Permissible Long-Term Voting / Shareholding Cap | Applicable Regulatory Conditions |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Non-Promoter: Natural Persons / Non-Financial Entities** | **Up to 10% (Commonly capped at 9.99%)** | Prior approval required from RBI if crossing the $\mathbf{5\%}$ threshold under Section 12B. |
| **Non-Promoter: Financial Institutions, Regulated Funds, Insurance/Banks** | **Up to 15%** | Permitted for institutional investors (e.g., LIC, mutual funds) subject to RBI scrutiny. |
| **Bank Promoters (Long-Term after 15 Years)** | **Up to 26%** | Raised from 15% to **26%** based on the Internal Working Group (IWG) recommendations chaired by P.K. Mohanty. |
| **Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) in Private Banks** | **Up to 74%** | Up to $49\%$ via automatic route; beyond $49\%$ up to $74\%$ via government approval route. |
| **FDI in Public Sector Banks (PSBs)** | **Up to 20%** | Governed by statutory public sector banking acts via government approval route. |

---

### 2. Statutory Pillars under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949

| Section | Statutory Mandate / Provision |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Section 12B** | **Prior Approval for Acquisition of Major Shareholding**: Mandatory prior RBI approval for acquiring $\ge 5\%$ of paid-up capital or voting rights. |
| **Section 12(2)** | **Cap on Voting Rights**: Restricts the maximum voting rights of any single shareholder (currently up to $26\%$ in private banks, regardless of higher actual economic ownership). |
| **Section 22** | **Licensing of Banking Companies**: Mandates banking license from RBI to conduct banking business in India. |
| **Section 35A** | **Power to Give Directions**: Empowers RBI to issue binding directions in public interest or to prevent bank affairs from harming depositors. |
