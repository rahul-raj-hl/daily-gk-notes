# 📌 World War II: Axis Powers vs. Allied Powers & Major Blocs

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Non-Axis Power**: **France** was a major constituent of the **Allied Powers** (alongside Great Britain, the USSR, the USA, and China), not the Axis.
- **The Core Axis Powers ("Rome–Berlin–Tokyo Axis")**:
  - **Germany** (led by Nazi dictator *Adolf Hitler*)
  - **Italy** (led by Fascist dictator *Benito Mussolini*)
  - **Japan** (led by *Emperor Hirohito* and military leader *General Hideki Tojo*)
- **Tripartite Pact (27 September 1940)**: Formalized the military alliance of Germany, Italy, and Japan in Berlin, establishing their mutual defense and expansionist vision (*New Order* in Europe and Greater East Asia).
- **Other Secondary Axis Partners / Co-belligerents**: Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, and Finland (co-belligerent against USSR).
- **France's Role**: Despite falling to German Blitzkrieg in June 1940 and the installation of the puppet Vichy regime, the **Free French Forces** led by **General Charles de Gaulle** fought continuously as a principal Allied nation.

### 2. Major Treaties & Turning Point Events of World War II

| Historical Milestone | Date / Year | Significance & Impact |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Invasion of Poland** | **1 September 1939** | Germany invades Poland; Britain and France declare war on Germany, triggering WWII. |
| **Tripartite Pact** | **27 September 1940** | Germany, Italy, and Japan officially forge the military Axis alliance in Berlin. |
| **Operation Barbarossa** | **22 June 1941** | Nazi Germany breaks the Molotov–Ribbentrop Non-Aggression Pact and invades the Soviet Union, bringing the USSR into the Allies. |
| **Attack on Pearl Harbor** | **7 December 1941** | Japan attacks the US naval base in Hawaii; the United States formally enters the war on the Allied side. |
| **Battle of Stalingrad** | **1942 – 1943** | Catastrophic German defeat on the Eastern Front; major turning point in Europe. |
| **D-Day (Operation Overlord)** | **6 June 1944** | Allied amphibious landings on the beaches of Normandy, leading to the liberation of France and Western Europe. |
| **Hiroshima & Nagasaki Bombing** | **6 & 9 August 1945** | US drops atomic bombs (*Little Boy* and *Fat Man*), leading to Japan's unconditional surrender and the end of WWII. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Alliance / Conflict | Axis / Central Powers | Allied Powers | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **World War I (1914–1918)** | **Central Powers**: Germany, Austria-Hungary, Ottoman Empire, Bulgaria | **Allied Powers**: Britain, France, Russia, Italy, USA, Japan | Mixing up WWI alliances with WWII alliances (Italy and Japan fought with Allies in WWI!) |
| **World War II (1939–1945)** | **Axis Powers**: Germany, Italy, Japan | **Allied Powers**: Britain, France, USSR, USA, China | Assuming Italy and Japan were on the same side in both World Wars |
| **Vichy France vs. Free France** | • **Vichy**: German collaborationist regime (Petain)<br>• **Free France**: Official Allied government-in-exile (De Gaulle) | Classifying all of France under the Axis alliance |
