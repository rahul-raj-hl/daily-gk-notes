# 📌 Tri-Services Rank Structure: Equivalent Ranks Across Army, Navy & Air Force

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Equivalent Rank for Major General**: The rank of **Major General** in the Indian Army is equivalent to **Rear Admiral** in the Indian Navy and **Air Vice Marshal** in the Indian Air Force.
- **Star Category**: Major General / Rear Admiral / Air Vice Marshal is a **Two-Star (2-Star) Flag Officer rank**.
- **Breakdown of the Incorrect Options**:
  - **Air Marshal** (IAF) $\rightarrow$ 3-Star rank (equivalent to *Lieutenant General* in Army / *Vice Admiral* in Navy).
  - **Air Commodore** (IAF) $\rightarrow$ 1-Star rank (equivalent to *Brigadier* in Army / *Commodore* in Navy).
  - **Commodore** (Navy) $\rightarrow$ 1-Star rank (equivalent to *Brigadier* in Army / *Air Commodore* in IAF).

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Complete Equivalent Rank Matrix (Commissioned Officers)

| Star Level / Category | Indian Army | Indian Navy | Indian Air Force (IAF) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5-Star** *(Honorary / Wartime)* | **Field Marshal** | **Admiral of the Fleet** *(Never held)* | **Marshal of the IAF** |
| **4-Star** *(Chief of Staff)* | **General** | **Admiral** | **Air Chief Marshal** |
| **3-Star** *(Vice Chief / Army Commander)* | **Lieutenant General** | **Vice Admiral** | **Air Marshal** |
| **2-Star** *(Divisional Commander)* | **Major General** | **Rear Admiral** | **Air Vice Marshal** |
| **1-Star** *(Brigade Commander)* | **Brigadier** | **Commodore** | **Air Commodore** |
| **Field Rank** *(Battalion / Regimental Cdr)* | **Colonel** | **Captain** | **Group Captain** |
| **Field Rank** *(2nd-in-Command)* | **Lieutenant Colonel** | **Commander** | **Wing Commander** |
| **Company Commander** | **Major** | **Lieutenant Commander** | **Squadron Leader** |
| **Subaltern / Junior Officer** | **Captain** | **Lieutenant** | **Flight Lieutenant** |
| **Entry Level Commissioned Officer** | **Lieutenant** | **Sub Lieutenant** | **Flying Officer** |

### 2. Five-Star Rank Holders in Indian Military History

| Personality | Service Branch | 5-Star Rank Conferred | Historical Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sam Manekshaw** | **Indian Army** | **Field Marshal (1973)** | Chief of Army Staff during the 1971 Indo-Pak War (Liberation of Bangladesh). First Indian officer to receive this rank. |
| **K. M. Cariappa** | **Indian Army** | **Field Marshal (1986)** | First Indian Commander-in-Chief of the Indian Army (15 January 1949). |
| **Arjan Singh** | **Indian Air Force** | **Marshal of the IAF (2002)** | Chief of the Air Staff during the 1965 Indo-Pak War. Only Air Force officer to be conferred the 5-star rank. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Rank Term | Correct Service Assignment & Level | Common Exam Trap / Confusion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Captain** | • **Army**: Junior Subaltern (3 stars on shoulder).<br>• **Navy**: Senior Officer (Equivalent to Colonel / Group Captain). | Assuming 'Captain' is the same rank across Army and Navy |
| **Commodore vs. Rear Admiral** | • **Commodore**: 1-Star (Equivalent to Brigadier).<br>• **Rear Admiral**: 2-Star (Equivalent to Major General). | Mixing the 1-star and 2-star naval ranks |
| **Air Marshal vs. Air Vice Marshal** | • **Air Marshal**: 3-Star (Equivalent to Lt General).<br>• **Air Vice Marshal**: 2-Star (Equivalent to Major General). | Inverting the seniority of Air Marshal and Air Vice Marshal |
