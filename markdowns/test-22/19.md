# 📌 ICMR Grant at Chandigarh University: Gut Microbiota & Diabetic Nephropathy

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Core Focus of the Project**: **Early detection of diabetic kidney disease (Diabetic Nephropathy) using gut microbiota analysis**.
- **Funding & Leadership**: Supported by a **₹56 lakh grant** from the **Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)**, led by **Dr. Deepak Kukkar** at Chandigarh University.
- **Scientific Rationale**: Employs high-throughput genomic sequencing and computational bioinformatics to identify specific microbial dysbiosis signatures in the human gut that precede renal tissue damage in diabetic patients.
- **Related Parallel Grant**: A separate **₹60 lakh grant** from the **Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF)** under the *Prime Minister Early Career Research Grant (PM-ECRG)* was awarded to Dr. Anil Kumar to harness beneficial soil microbes for enhancing mustard (*Brassica*) crop productivity.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Gut-Kidney Axis in Diabetic Kidney Disease (DKD)

| Biological Dimension | Mechanism / Operational Workflow | Clinical / Diagnostic Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pathology Targeted** | **Diabetic Nephropathy (DKD)**: Progressive deterioration of kidney filtration units (glomeruli) induced by chronic hyperglycemia. | Leading cause of End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) globally. |
| **Diagnostic Novelty** | **Gut Microbiota Profiling**: Mapping shifts in gut bacterial populations (dysbiosis) and altered short-chain fatty acid (SCFA) metabolites. | Enables non-invasive early detection before standard microalbuminuria or serum creatinine spikes occur. |
| **Tools Deployed** | Metagenomic 16S rRNA sequencing, metabolic profiling, and machine learning/AI diagnostic algorithms. | Establishes reference datasets for AI-driven diagnostic kits. |
