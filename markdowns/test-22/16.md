# 📌 Defense Pioneer: Squadron Leader Saanya & Cat-A QFI Qualification

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **First Woman Cat-A QFI**: **Squadron Leader Saanya** became the **first woman officer in the history of the Indian Air Force (IAF)** to earn the elite **Category-A (Cat-A) Qualified Flying Instructor** qualification.
- **Significance of Cat-A Status**: Category-A is the **highest instructional grading** awarded by the Flying Instructors School (FIS). Officers holding this certification are master aviation instructors authorized to train, evaluate, and test other flying instructors in advanced combat maneuvers and tactical flight envelopes.
- **Service Career Progression**:
  - Commissioned on **20 June 2015** as part of the **42 SSC(W) FP** (Short Service Commission - Women - Flying Pilot) Course.
  - Promoted to **Flight Lieutenant** in **2017**.
  - Elevated to the rank of **Squadron Leader** in **2021**.
