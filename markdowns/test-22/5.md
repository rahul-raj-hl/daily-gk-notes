# 📌 Bulgaria: Prime Ministerial Transition & Political Profile

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Newly Appointed Prime Minister**: **Rumen Radev**, a 62-year-old former President and former Bulgarian Air Force commander, was approved by the Bulgarian parliament as the **Prime Minister**.
- **Parliamentary Vote**: The National Assembly approved his appointment by a vote of **124–70** (with 36 abstentions) to end prolonged political deadlock and lead economic reforms.
- **Political Transition**: Radev previously served as the President of Bulgaria (a largely ceremonial head of state role since 2017) before resigning to seek the executive office of the Prime Minister.
- **Military Background**: A former fighter pilot and Major General who served as the **Commander of the Bulgarian Air Force** (2014–2016) and earned a Master of Strategic Studies from the **US Air War College** (2003).
