# 📌 Madrid Open: Women's Singles Title & Tennis Honours

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Winner**: Ukrainian tennis star **Marta Kostyuk** won the Women's Singles title at the **Madrid Open**.
- **Runner-Up**: Defeated teenage prodigy **Mirra Andreeva** with a straight-set score of **7–5, 6–3** in the final.
- **Career Milestone**: Marked Kostyuk's **maiden WTA 1000-level crown** and her **third career WTA singles title**, following a title run at Rouen.
- **Tournament Details**: The Madrid Open (*Mutua Madrid Open*) is a premier **WTA 1000 and ATP Masters 1000** event contested annually on outdoor **red clay courts** at the *Caja Mágica* (Magic Box) in Madrid, Spain.
