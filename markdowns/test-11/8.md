# 📌 UNESCO World Heritage Sites in India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Non-UNESCO Site in Option**: **Group of Monuments at Mandu** (Madhya Pradesh) is **NOT** a UNESCO World Heritage Site (it remains on the UNESCO tentative list).
- **First UNESCO Sites in India (1983)**: Ajanta Caves, Ellora Caves, Agra Fort, and Taj Mahal.
- **Sun Temple, Konark**: Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in **1984**.
- **Fatehpur Sikri**: Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in **1986**.
- **Agra Fort**: Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in **1983**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Analysis of Options

- **Fatehpur Sikri (Uttar Pradesh)**:
  - Built by Mughal Emperor Akbar in the 16th century.
  - Declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in **1986**.
- **Sun Temple, Konark (Odisha)**:
  - Built by King Narasimhadeva I of the Eastern Ganga Dynasty in the 13th century.
  - Declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in **1984** (also known as Black Pagoda).
- **Agra Fort (Uttar Pradesh)**:
  - Key seat of Mughal governance near the Yamuna river.
  - Declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in **1983** (among India's first 4 sites).
- **Group of Monuments at Mandu (Madhya Pradesh)**:
  - Famous for Afghan architecture including Jahaz Mahal, Hindola Mahal, and Rani Roopmati Pavilion.
  - It is protected by the ASI, but **NOT** officially inscribed on the main UNESCO World Heritage List.

---

### 2. Landmark UNESCO World Heritage Sites in India

*Key milestones and recent additions.*

- **First Inscribed Sites (1983)**:
  - Ajanta Caves (Maharashtra)
  - Ellora Caves (Maharashtra)
  - Agra Fort (Uttar Pradesh)
  - Taj Mahal (Uttar Pradesh)
- **Recent Inscriptions**:
  - **Kakatiya Rudreshwara (Ramappa) Temple** (Telangana) – Inscribed in 2021.
  - **Dholavira** (Harappan City, Gujarat) – Inscribed in 2021.
  - **Santiniketan** (West Bengal) – Inscribed in 2023.
  - **Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas** (Belur, Halebidu & Somnathapura, Karnataka) – Inscribed in 2023.
  - **Moidams** (Ahom Dynasty Mound-Burial System, Assam) – Inscribed in 2024.
