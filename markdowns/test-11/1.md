# 📌 Khajuraho Group of Monuments & Chandela Dynasty

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Builders**: Constructed by the **Chandela Dynasty** between **950 AD and 1050 AD**.
- **Location**: Situated in Chhatarpur district, **Madhya Pradesh**.
- **Architecture Style**: Premium example of the **Nagara style** (specifically the *Panchayatana* temple layout).
- **Religious Harmony**: Monuments belong to two major Indian religions: **Hinduism** (Shaivism & Vaishnavism) and **Jainism**.
- **UNESCO Status**: Recognized as a **UNESCO World Heritage Site** in **1986**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Chandela Dynasty (Jejakabhukti)

*Founders and rulers who built Khajuraho.*

- **Founder**: **Nannuka** founded the Chandela dynasty in the 9th century in Central India.
- **Kingdom Region**: Ruled the **Bundelkhand** region, which was historically known as **Jejakabhukti**.
- **Religious Capital**: **Khajuraho** served as their primary religious capital.
- **Prominent Rulers**:
  - **Yashovarman**: Built the famous **Lakshmana Temple** at Khajuraho.
  - **Dhanga**: Built the **Vishvanatha Temple** and **Parshvanatha Temple**.
  - **Vidyadhara**: Built the grandest temple, the **Kandariya Mahadeva Temple**.

---

### 2. Architectural Features of Khajuraho Temples

*Distinct Nagara style temple elements.*

- **Panchayatana Style**: Main shrine surrounded by four smaller subsidiary shrines on a single raised platform (*jagati*).
- **Curved Towers (Shikhara)**: Features miniature towers (*Urushringas*) clinging to the main central tower.
- **Kandariya Mahadeva Temple**: The largest and most famous Hindu temple in Khajuraho, dedicated to Lord Shiva.
- **Jain Temples at Khajuraho**: Includes the **Parshvanatha Temple**, **Adinatha Temple**, and **Ghantai Temple**.
- **Sculptural Art**: Famous for detailed outer wall carvings depicting daily human life, court scenes, musicians, and erotic art.
