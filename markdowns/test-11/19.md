# 📌 Rovers Cup & Famous Indian Football Trophies

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Rovers Cup**: Related to **Football**. It was the second oldest football tournament in India, started in 1891 in Bombay.
- **Inaugural Winner**: 1st Worcester Regiment won the first title in 1891.
- **Abolition**: Abolished in **2001** due to high costs and sponsorship challenges following the launch of the National Football League (NFL).
- **Other Major Football Trophies**: Durand Cup (oldest in Asia), Santosh Trophy, Subroto Cup, IFA Shield, Bandodkar Gold Trophy, and Merdeka Cup.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Facts About Rovers Cup

*Historical background and highlights.*

- **Origin**: Established in **1891** by British football enthusiasts in Bombay (now Mumbai) as a form of entertainment.
- **Status in India**: It was the **second oldest tournament in India** after the Durand Cup.
- **Final Winner**: **Mohun Bagan AC** won the last edition (2000–01 season) by defeating Churchill Brothers SC 2–0.
- **Discontinuation**: Faced financial and operational issues after the inception of the National Football League (NFL) and was officially stopped in 2001.

---

### 2. Major Football Trophies & Cups in India

*High-frequency sports trophies frequently asked in exams.*

- **Durand Cup**: Founded in 1888 in Shimla; **oldest football tournament in Asia** and third oldest in the world.
- **Santosh Trophy**: Premier Indian national state-level football championship (started in 1941).
- **Subroto Cup**: Prestigious international inter-school football tournament named after Air Marshal Subroto Mukerjee.
- **IFA Shield**: Annual tournament organized by the Indian Football Association (West Bengal); Mohun Bagan became the first Indian club to win it in 1911.
- **Bandodkar Gold Trophy / DCM Trophy / Scindia Gold Cup**: Prominent historical domestic football competitions in India.
