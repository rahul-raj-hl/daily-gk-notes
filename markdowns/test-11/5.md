# 📌 Sutlej River & Major Mountain Passes of India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Sutlej Entry Point**: The Sutlej River enters India from Tibet through **Shipki La** pass in Himachal Pradesh.
- **Origin of Sutlej**: Originates near **Rakas Lake** (Rakshas Tal) near Mansarovar in Tibet.
- **Tributary Status**: Longest of the five major tributaries of the Indus River (Panjnad).
- **Major Dam**: The famous **Bhakra Nangal Dam** is constructed on the Sutlej River.
- **Key Mountain Passes**:
  - **Shipki La**: Himachal Pradesh (Sutlej entry)
  - **Nathu La**: Sikkim (Indo-China trade route)
  - **Zoji La**: Ladakh (connects Srinagar to Leh)
  - **Lipulekh**: Uttarakhand (Mansarovar Yatra route)

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Sutlej River Profile

*Essential geographical details.*

- **Sanskrit Name**: *Shatadru*.
- **Course**: Originates in Tibet as *Langqên Zangbo*, enters India at Kinnaur district (Himachal Pradesh) via Shipki La, flows through Punjab plains, and joins the Indus River in Pakistan.
- **Major Hydroelectric Projects**:
  - **Bhakra Nangal Project** (Himachal Pradesh/Punjab)
  - **Nathpa Jhakri Dam** (Himachal Pradesh)
  - **Koldam Dam** (Himachal Pradesh)

---

### 2. Major Mountain Passes Asked in Exams

*High-frequency mountain passes and their connecting regions.*

- **Shipki La**: Located in Himachal Pradesh; connects Kinnaur district with Tibet.
- **Zoji La**: Located in the Zaskar Range (Ladakh); connects Srinagar with Kargil and Leh.
- **Nathu La**: Located in Sikkim; ancient Silk Route pass connecting India with Tibet.
- **Lipulekh**: Located at the tri-junction of India (Uttarakhand), China (Tibet), and Nepal; main route for Kailash Mansarovar Yatra.
- **Rohtang Pass**: Located in Himachal Pradesh (Pir Panjal Range); connects Kullu Valley with Lahaul and Spiti Valleys.
- **Bara-lacha La**: Located in Himachal Pradesh; source of the Chenab River (Chandra and Bhaga streams).
- **Bani Hal Pass**: Located in Jammu & Kashmir; houses the Jawahar Tunnel connecting Jammu to Srinagar.
