# 📌 Major Gandhian Movements in Indian Freedom Struggle

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Champaran Satyagraha (1917)**: Gandhi's first major involvement in Indian politics; addressed grievances of indigo farmers against British planters in Bihar.
- **Non-Cooperation Movement (1920)**: Launched in response to the Jallianwala Bagh massacre; involved a nationwide boycott of British goods and institutions.
- **Salt March / Dandi March (1930)**: A 240-mile march to the Arabian Sea to break the British salt tax and launch Civil Disobedience.
- **Quit India Movement (1942)**: Launched during World War II with the call *"Do or Die"*, demanding an immediate end to British rule.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Chronology of Core Movements

*Detailed breakdown of key movements led by Mahatma Gandhi.*

- **Champaran Satyagraha (1917)**:
  - **Issue**: Oppressive **Tinkathia System** (mandatory cultivation of indigo on 3/20th of land) imposed by European planters.
  - **Significance**: Gandhi's **first Civil Disobedience** in India; successfully forced the British to abolish the system and refund illegal extractions.

- **Non-Cooperation Movement (1920–1922)**:
  - **Trigger**: Jallianwala Bagh massacre (1919), Rowlatt Act, and the Khilafat Movement.
  - **Strategy**: Boycott of foreign cloth, courts, government titles, and schools.
  - **Withdrawal**: Suspended in February 1922 after the **Chauri Chaura incident** (violent burning of a police station by a crowd).

- **Salt March / Civil Disobedience Movement (1930)**:
  - **Event**: Gandhi along with 78 volunteers marched **240 miles** from Sabarmati Ashram to Dandi from March 12 to April 6, 1930.
  - **Action**: Picked up a handful of salt from the coast to break the British monopoly on salt tax.

- **Quit India Movement (1942)**:
  - **Context**: Failure of Cripps Mission and threat of Japanese invasion during World War II.
  - **Resolution**: Passed on **August 8, 1942**, at Gowalia Tank Maidan, Bombay; Gandhi gave the famous slogan ***"Do or Die"*** (*Karo ya Maro*).
