# 📌 Thyroxin Synthesis & Thyroid Gland Hormones

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Essential Element**: **Iodine** is required for the synthesis of thyroxin hormones.
- **Thyroid Hormones**: The thyroid gland uses iodine and the amino acid **tyrosine** to synthesize **Thyroxin ($\text{T}_4$)** and **Triiodothyronine ($\text{T}_3$)**.
- **Deficiency Disease**: Deficiency of iodine leads to reduced thyroxin synthesis, resulting in **Goiter** (swollen neck) and **Hypothyroidism**.
- **Regulation**: Thyroid hormone secretion is controlled by **Thyroid-Stimulating Hormone (TSH)** produced by the **pituitary gland**.
- **Primary Function**: Regulates basal metabolic rate (BMR), physical growth, and tissue differentiation.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Mechanism of Thyroxin Synthesis

*How the thyroid gland produces hormones.*

- **Thyroid Gland Location**: Butterfly-shaped endocrine gland located in the neck, in front of the larynx and trachea.
- **Synthesis Process**:
  - The thyroid gland extracts **iodine** from blood plasma.
  - It combines iodine with the amino acid **tyrosine** to form two main hormones:
    1. **Thyroxin ($\text{T}_4$)**: Tetraiodothyronine (contains 4 iodine atoms).
    2. **Triiodothyronine ($\text{T}_3$)**: Contains 3 iodine atoms.
- **Role of Pituitary Gland**: Secretes **TSH** (Thyroid-Stimulating Hormone) to stimulate the thyroid gland whenever $\text{T}_3/\text{T}_4$ levels drop in blood.

---

### 2. Thyroid Disorders & Minerals

*High-yield exam facts on endocrine deficiency.*

- **Goiter**: Abnormal enlargement of the thyroid gland due to iodine deficiency in diet.
- **Cretinism**: Severe congenital hypothyroidism in children leading to stunted growth and mental retardation.
- **Myxedema**: Severe hypothyroidism in adults causing lethargy and swelling.
- **Hyperthyroidism (Grave's Disease)**: Overproduction of thyroxin leading to weight loss and bulging eyes (Exophthalmic Goiter).
