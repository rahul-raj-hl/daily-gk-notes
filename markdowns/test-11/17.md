# 📌 Major Festivals of North-East India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Nongkrem Dance Festival**: Celebrated by the Khasi tribe in **Meghalaya** to offer thanks for a bountiful harvest and pray for peace.
- **Chapchar Kut**: The spring harvest festival celebrated with joy and traditional *Cheraw* (bamboo) dance in **Mizoram**.
- **Kharchi Puja**: A royal 7-day festival worshiping 14 deities (*Chaturdasa Devata*) celebrated in **Tripura**.
- **Dree Festival**: An agricultural harvest festival celebrated by the Apatani tribe in Ziro valley, **Arunachal Pradesh**.
- **Hornbill Festival**: Known as the *"Festival of Festivals"*, celebrated by all Naga tribes in **Nagaland** (Dec 1–10).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key North-Eastern Festivals & Associated Tribes

*Cultural traditions, tribes, and state connections.*

- **Meghalaya**:
  - **Nongkrem Festival**: Held at Smit by the Khasi tribe to invoke divine blessings for good crops and prosperity.
  - **Wangla Festival**: Famous 100-Drums festival celebrated by the Garo tribe in honor of Saljong (Sun God).
  - **Shad Suk Mynsiem**: Traditional Thanksgiving dance festival of the Khasi people.

- **Mizoram**:
  - **Chapchar Kut**: Celebrated in March after clearing forests for Jhum (shifting) cultivation. Features the famous **Cheraw (Bamboo) Dance**.
  - **Mim Kut & Pawl Kut**: Harvest-related festive occasions.

- **Tripura**:
  - **Kharchi Puja**: Celebrated at Agartala in July; involves cleansing the earth and worshiping 14 ancestral gods.
  - **Garia Puja**: Tribal festival celebrating deity Garia for livestock and crop safety.

- **Arunachal Pradesh**:
  - **Dree Festival**: Celebrated in July by the **Apatani tribe** for agricultural protection against pests and famine.
  - **Losar**: Tibetan New Year celebrated by the Monpa tribe.
  - **Solung & Mopin**: Major agricultural festivals celebrated by the Adi and Galo tribes.

---

### 2. Overview of Other High-Yield North-East Festivals

*Repeatedly tested in competitive SSC and State exams.*

- **Assam**: **Bihu** (Bohag, Kati, Kongali), **Ambubachi Mela** (Kamakhya Temple), **Dehing Patkai Festival**, **Baishagu**.
- **Manipur**: **Yaoshang** (Holi of Manipur), **Cheiraoba** (Manipuri New Year), **Chavang Kut**, **Lui-Ngai-Ni** (Seed-sowing festival).
- **Nagaland**: **Hornbill Festival** (Kisama Village), **Moatsu Festival** (Ao tribe), **Sekrenyi** (Angami tribe).
- **Sikkim**: **Losoong** (Namsoong), **Saga Dawa** (Buddhist festival), **Pang Lhabsol**.
