# 📌 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act & Panchayati Raj System

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Articles Inserted**: **Articles 243 to 243-O** were inserted regarding Panchayats under the **73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992**.
- **Part Added**: **Part IX** (titled *"The Panchayats"*) was added to the Constitution.
- **Schedule Added**: **11th Schedule**, containing **29 functional subjects** assigned to Panchayati Raj Institutions.
- **Enforcement Date**: Came into force on **24 April 1993** (celebrated annually as *National Panchayati Raj Day*).
- **74th Amendment**: Municipalities (Urban Local Bodies) were inserted under **Part IX-A** covering **Articles 243-P to 243-ZG** and the **12th Schedule** (18 subjects).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992

*Constitutionalizing rural local self-government in India.*

- **Part & Articles**: Inserted **Part IX** containing **Articles 243 to 243-O**.
- **Schedule**: Added the **Eleventh Schedule** consisting of **29 functional items** (e.g., agriculture, rural housing, drinking water, roads).
- **Three-Tier System**: Mandated a uniform 3-tier structure across all states:
  - Gram Panchayat (Village level)
  - Panchayat Samiti (Block/Intermediate level)
  - Zilla Parishad (District level)
  - *(Note: States with a population under 20 lakhs may choose not to constitute the intermediate level).*
- **Reservations**: Mandatory minimum **1/3rd reservation for women** across all levels (including chairpersons).
- **Tenure**: Fixed **5-year term** for each Panchayat; re-elections must be held within 6 months of dissolution.

---

### 2. Key Articles Under Part IX (Panchayats)

*Frequently queried specific articles in competitive exams.*

- **Article 243**: Definitions.
- **Article 243A**: Gram Sabha.
- **Article 243B**: Constitution of Panchayats.
- **Article 243C**: Composition of Panchayats.
- **Article 243D**: Reservation of seats (including 1/3rd reservation for women).
- **Article 243E**: Duration of Panchayats (5 years).
- **Article 243I**: State Finance Commission (to review financial position).
- **Article 243K**: State Election Commission (for conducting local body elections).
