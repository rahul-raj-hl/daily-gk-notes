# 📌 Ellora Caves & Rashtrakuta Dynasty Architecture

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Patron Dynasty**: The famous **Ellora Caves** were primarily patronized and constructed under the **Rashtrakuta Dynasty** (8th to 10th centuries CE).
- **Kailasa Temple (Cave 16)**: The world's largest monolithic rock-cut structure, built by Rashtrakuta King **Krishna I** in the 8th century CE.
- **Location**: Located in Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar (formerly Aurangabad) district of **Maharashtra**.
- **Total Caves**: **34 major rock-cut caves** representing three major religions:
  - **Buddhist**: Caves 1–12 (12 caves)
  - **Hindu**: Caves 13–29 (17 caves)
  - **Jain**: Caves 30–34 (5 caves)
- **UNESCO Recognition**: Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in **1983**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Architectural Highlights of Ellora Caves

*Religious harmony and rock-cut engineering.*

- **Religious Convergence**: Represents secular tolerance, housing Buddhist, Hindu, and Jain monuments side-by-side.
- **Kailasa Temple (Cave 16)**:
  - Carved top-down from a single basalt cliff-face.
  - Mimics Mount Kailash, the divine abode of Lord Shiva.
  - Commissioned by Rashtrakuta ruler **Krishna I**.
- **Notable Caves**:
  - **Cave 10 (Vishvakarma Cave)**: A famous Buddhist Chaitya hall with a large seated Buddha in front of a Stupa.
  - **Cave 15 (Dashavatara Cave)**: Depicts ten avatars of Lord Vishnu.
  - **Cave 32 (Indra Sabha)**: The most detailed two-story Jain cave.

---

### 2. The Rashtrakuta Dynasty

*Rulers, literary works, and achievements.*

- **Founder**: **Dantidurga** founded the Rashtrakuta Empire in 753 CE after overthrowing the Chalukyas of Badami.
- **Capital**: **Manyakheta** (Malkhed in modern Karnataka).
- **Greatest Ruler**: **Amoghavarsha I**, a great patron of literature who authored ***Kavirajamarga*** (the earliest available work on Kannada poetics).
- **Other Major Monuments**: Elephanta Caves (Trimurti idol) were also developed under Rashtrakuta patronage.
