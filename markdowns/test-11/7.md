# 📌 Removal of Permanent Hardness of Water & Sodium Compounds

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Permanent Hardness Removal**: **Sodium carbonate** ($\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$, common name: **Washing Soda**) is used to permanently remove the hardness of water.
- **Chemical Formula of Washing Soda**: $\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3 \cdot 10\text{H}_2\text{O}$ (Sodium Carbonate Decahydrate).
- **Cause of Permanent Hardness**: Presence of soluble **chlorides** and **sulfates** of calcium and magnesium ($\text{CaCl}_2$, $\text{MgCl}_2$, $\text{CaSO}_4$, $\text{MgSO}_4$).
- **Cause of Temporary Hardness**: Presence of soluble **bicarbonates** of calcium and magnesium ($\text{Ca(HCO}_3)_2$, $\text{Mg(HCO}_3)_2$), which can be removed simply by boiling or adding lime (Clark's process).
- **Working Mechanism**: Sodium carbonate reacts with soluble calcium and magnesium salts to form insoluble carbonates ($\text{CaCO}_3$ and $\text{MgCO}_3$), which precipitate out.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Types of Hardness of Water & Removal Methods

*Understanding temporary vs. permanent hardness.*

- **Temporary Hardness**:
  - Caused by: Bicarbonates of Calcium and Magnesium.
  - Removal Methods: 
    - **Boiling**: Converts soluble bicarbonates into insoluble carbonates.
    - **Clark's Process**: Adding calculated amount of slaked lime ($\text{Ca(OH)}_2$).
- **Permanent Hardness**:
  - Caused by: Chlorides and Sulfates of Calcium and Magnesium.
  - Removal Methods:
    - **Washing Soda Method**: Adding **Sodium Carbonate** ($\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3$).
    - **Calgon's Process**: Using Sodium Hexametaphosphate [$\text{Na}_6\text{P}_6\text{O}_{18}$].
    - **Ion-Exchange / Permutit Process**: Using Zeolites (Hydrated Sodium Aluminum Silicate).

---

### 2. Common Sodium Compounds & Uses

*High-yield sodium compounds asked in SSC & Railway exams.*

- **Sodium Carbonate ($\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3 \cdot 10\text{H}_2\text{O}$)**:
  - Common Name: **Washing Soda**.
  - Uses: Water softening, glass manufacturing, soap industry.
- **Sodium Hydrogen Carbonate ($\text{NaHCO}_3$)**:
  - Common Name: **Baking Soda** (or Sodium Bicarbonate).
  - Uses: Antacid, baking powder, fire extinguishers.
- **Sodium Hydroxide ($\text{NaOH}$)**:
  - Common Name: **Caustic Soda**.
  - Uses: Soap making, paper manufacturing, petroleum refining.
- **Sodium Chloride ($\text{NaCl}$)**:
  - Common Name: **Common Salt** (Table Salt).
  - Uses: Food preservative, raw material for producing $\text{NaOH}$, $\text{NaHCO}_3$, and $\text{Cl}_2$.

What is the chemical formula of Washing Soda?

- [ ] $\text{NaOH}$
- [ ] $\text{CaOCl}_2$

> Washing soda is hydrated sodium carbonate with ten molecules of water of crystallization ($\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3 \cdot 10\text{H}_2\text{O}$).

- [ ] Chlorides of calcium and magnesium
- [ ] Sulfates of calcium and magnesium

- [ ] Nitrates of calcium and magnesium

> Soluble hydrogen carbonates (bicarbonates) of calcium and magnesium cause temporary hardness, which can be removed simply by boiling.
