# 📌 Energy Conversion Devices & Battery Operation

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Battery**: Converts **chemical energy into electrical energy** via spontaneous electrochemical oxidation-reduction (redox) reactions.
- **Electric Generator**: Converts **mechanical energy into electrical energy** using electromagnetic induction.
- **Electric Motor**: Converts **electrical energy into mechanical energy**.
- **Moving Coil Galvanometer / Meter**: Converts **electrical energy into mechanical deflection** to measure current.
- **Solar Cell (Photovoltaic)**: Converts **light energy directly into electrical energy**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Working Mechanism of a Battery

*Electrochemical energy conversion.*

- **Definition**: A battery consists of one or more electrochemical cells connected together.
- **Core Principle**: Stores chemical energy and converts it into direct current (DC) electrical energy through controlled **redox (oxidation-reduction) reactions**.
- **Key Components**:
  - **Anode (-)**: Oxidation occurs here (loss of electrons).
  - **Cathode (+)**: Reduction occurs here (gain of electrons).
  - **Electrolyte**: Allows movement of ions between electrodes while electrons flow through the external circuit to perform electrical work.

---

### 2. Common Energy Conversion Devices

*High-yield device conversions frequently asked in exams.*

- **Electric Motor**: Converts Electrical Energy $\rightarrow$ Mechanical Energy.
- **Generator / Dynamo**: Converts Mechanical Energy $\rightarrow$ Electrical Energy.
- **Microphone**: Converts Sound Energy $\rightarrow$ Electrical Energy.
- **Loudspeaker**: Converts Electrical Energy $\rightarrow$ Sound Energy.
- **Thermocouple**: Converts Thermal (Heat) Energy $\rightarrow$ Electrical Energy.
- **Solar Cell**: Converts Light (Solar) Energy $\rightarrow$ Electrical Energy.

| :--- | :--- |
