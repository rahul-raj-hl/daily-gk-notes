# 📌 Digital India Bhashini Mission & Tripura Partnership

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **First North-Eastern State**: **Tripura** is the first state in North-East India (and the 8th state nationally) to partner with the **Digital India Bhashini Division (DIBD)** for AI-driven multilingual e-governance.
- **MOU Venue**: Signed at Pragna Bhavan, Agartala, during the *'Bhashini Rajyam'* State Level Workshop inaugurated by Tripura IT Minister Pranajit Singha Roy.
- **Core Technology**: Enables AI-driven real-time speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and voice-to-voice translation across **22 scheduled Indian languages**.
- **Governance Integration**: Integrated with CM Helpline, eVidhan, Kisan Sahayata, e-District services, and police CCTNS FIR translations.
- **Nodal Ministry**: Operating under the **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Bhashini Integration in Tripura

*Key aspects of the partnership and local implementation.*

- **Pioneering Role**: Tripura became the first North-Eastern state to adopt Bhashini to improve digital literacy and governance accessibility in tribal and rural areas.
- **Key Use Cases**:
  - **Citizen Services**: Multilingual access in local dialects/languages for CM Helpline, Kisan Sahayata, and e-District applications.
  - **Law Enforcement**: Automated FIR translations and voice-based data entries for the Crime and Criminal Tracking Network & Systems (CCTNS).
  - **Legislative Functioning**: Supporting real-time language options in Assembly proceedings via eVidhan.

---

### 2. Overview of Digital India Bhashini Mission

*National AI language translation platform.*

- **Full Name**: National Language Translation Mission (NLTM) – **BHASHINI** (BHASha INterface for India).
- **Primary Objective**: Break language barriers by leveraging AI, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and open-source speech models across all 22 scheduled languages of India.
- **Key Technical Milestones**:
  - Supports **22+ Indian languages** with **300+ AI models**.
  - Processes over **100 million+ monthly inferences**.
  - Onboarded key national portals and stakeholders including NPCI, RBIH, Lok Sabha, and Rajya Sabha.
