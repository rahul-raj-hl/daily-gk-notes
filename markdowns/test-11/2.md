# 📌 Major Indian Festivals & Fairs (Odisha & Other States)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Bali Trutiya**: A traditional festival celebrated in **Odisha** (observed on the 3rd day of the bright fortnight in the month of Bhadrapada).
- **Hornbill Festival**: Known as the *"Festival of Festivals"*, celebrated in **Nagaland** (December 1 to 10).
- **Bonalu**: A major Hindu festival dedicated to Goddess Mahakali, celebrated in **Telangana** (Hyderabad and Secunderabad).
- **Chappar Mela**: A famous fair held in the Ludhiana district of **Punjab** in honor of Gugga Pir.
- **Other Odisha Festivals**: Raja Parba, Nuakhai, Bali Jatra, Dhanu Yatra, and Jagannath Rath Yatra.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Festivals of Odisha

*Culture, rituals, and seasonal celebrations.*

- **Bali Trutiya**:
  - Celebrated by married women in Odisha for the well-being and long life of their husbands.
  - Married women build small umbrellas out of palm leaves and offer prayers to Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati.
- **Nuakhai**:
  - The premier agricultural/harvest festival of Western Odisha where people eat the newly harvested rice (*Nua* = new, *Khai* = food).
- **Raja Parba**:
  - A 3-day festival celebrating womanhood and the onset of the monsoon (Earth's menstruation period).
- **Bali Jatra**:
  - Celebrated in Cuttack on the banks of the Mahanadi river to honor the ancient maritime trade glory (*Baitas*) of Odisha with Southeast Asia.
- **Dhanu Yatra**:
  - Held in Bargarh; considered the **world's largest open-air theater**, depicting the story of Krishna and King Kamsa.

---

### 2. Major Festivals from Other States in the Question

*Repeated static GK questions in competitive exams.*

- **Hornbill Festival (Nagaland)**:
  - Held annually from **1 to 10 December** at Kisama Heritage Village, Kohima.
  - Showcases the rich cultural heritage and dances of all Naga tribes.
- **Bonalu (Telangana)**:
  - Celebrated during the Hindu month of *Ashada* (July/August).
  - Women carry decorated brass or clay pots (*Bonam*) filled with rice, milk, and jaggery topped with neem leaves and a lamp to Mahakali temples.
- **Chappar Mela (Punjab)**:
  - Annual fair held at Chappar village in Ludhiana district.
  - Organized to worship **Gugga Pir** (Snake God) to seek protection against snake bites.
