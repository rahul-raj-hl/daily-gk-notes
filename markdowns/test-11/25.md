# 📌 "Untouchable" & Major Works of Mulk Raj Anand

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Author**: The novel *"Untouchable"* was written by **Mulk Raj Anand**.
- **Publication Year**: Published in **1935**.
- **Protagonist**: Centers on **Bakha**, a young toilet-cleaner (sweeper), detailing a single day in his life and depicting the oppressive social system of caste discrimination.
- **Foreword**: Features a famous foreword written by **E. M. Forster**.
- **Trio of Indian English Literature**: Mulk Raj Anand, R. K. Narayan, and Raja Rao are considered the founding fathers of Indian English fiction.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. About the Novel "Untouchable" (1935)

*Themes, literary importance, and plot overview.*

- **Plot Focus**: Captures a single day in the life of **Bakha**, a 18-year-old sweeper living in the cantonment town of Bulandshahr.
- **Social Impact**: Highlighted the harsh realities of caste discrimination, social exclusion, and untouchability in pre-independence India.
- **Influence**: Written under the strong influence of Mahatma Gandhi's philosophy and stay at Sabarmati Ashram.
- **E. M. Forster's Contribution**: E. M. Forster wrote the preface/foreword for the novel, helping it secure publication in England after several rejections.

### 2. Famous Literary Works by Authors in Options

*High-yield books by Indian literary figures frequently tested in exams.*

- **Mulk Raj Anand**:
  - ***Untouchable* (1935)**
  - ***Coolie* (1936)** (Follows Munoo, a young hill boy)
  - ***Two Leaves and a Bud* (1937)** (Set in Assam tea plantations)
  - ***The Village* (1939)**
  - ***Across the Black Waters* (1939)**
  - ***The Sword and the Sickle* (1942)**
  - ***The Private Life of an Indian Prince* (1953)**
- **Munshi Premchand**:
  - *Godaan*, *Gaban*, *Nirmala*, *Seva Sadan*, *Rangbhoomi*, *Karmabhoomi*, *Idgah*, *Mansarovar*.
- **Sarojini Naidu** (*Nightingale of India*):
  - *The Golden Threshold*, *The Bird of Time*, *The Broken Wing*, *The Feather of the Dawn*.
- **K. Natwar Singh**:
  - *One Life Is Not Enough*, *Walking with Lions*.
