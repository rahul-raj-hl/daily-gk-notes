# 📌 Major Government of India Acts (1909, 1919, 1935, 1947)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Indian Councils Act 1909**: Also known as **Morley-Minto Reforms**; introduced **separate electorates** for Muslims.
- **Government of India Act 1919**: Known as **Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms**; introduced **Dyarchy in Provinces** and bicameralism at the Centre.
- **Government of India Act 1935**: Introduced **Provincial Autonomy**, Dyarchy at the Centre, Federal Court, and 3 Legislative Lists (Federal, Provincial, Concurrent).
- **Indian Independence Act 1947**: Partitioned British India into **India and Pakistan**; Royal Assent given on **18 July 1947**.
- **Longest Act**: The Government of India Act 1935 was the longest UK Parliament Act ever enacted at the time.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Indian Councils Act, 1909 (Morley-Minto Reforms)

*First major expansion of legislative councils.*

- **Key Names**: Lord Morley (Secretary of State) and Lord Minto (Viceroy).
- **Communal Electorate**: Introduced separate representation for Muslims; Lord Minto came to be known as the *"Father of Communal Electorate"*.
- **Executive Council Entry**: **Satyendra Prasad Sinha** became the first Indian to join the Viceroy's Executive Council (as Law Member).

---

### 2. Government of India Act, 1919 (Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms)

*Introduction of responsible government at the provincial level.*

- **Key Names**: Edwin Montagu (Secretary of State) and Lord Chelmsford (Viceroy).
- **Dyarchy in Provinces**: Transferred subjects (administered by Governor with ministers) and Reserved subjects (administered by Governor with executive council).
- **Bicameral Central Legislature**: Replaced the Council of India with a Council of State (Upper House) and Legislative Assembly (Lower House).
- **Public Service Commission**: Provided for establishing a Central Public Service Commission (set up in 1926).

---

### 3. Government of India Act, 1935

*The backbone and largest structural source of the current Indian Constitution.*

- **Provincial Autonomy**: Abolished Dyarchy in provinces and introduced full responsible government in states.
- **Dyarchy at Centre**: Proposed dyarchy at the central level (though never actually implemented).
- **Division of Powers**: Distributed powers into 3 Lists:
  - **Federal List**
  - **Provincial List**
  - **Concurrent List**
- **Federal Court**: Provided for establishing a Federal Court (established in 1937 in Delhi).
- **Reserve Bank of India**: Recommended setting up RBI to control currency and credit.

---

### 4. Indian Independence Act, 1947

*Termination of British rule in India.*

- **Royal Assent**: Granted on **18 July 1947**.
- **Creation of Dominions**: Formally separated British India into two independent dominions—**India** and **Pakistan**—effective from **15 August 1947**.
- **Abolition of Offices**: Abolished the post of Secretary of State for India and office of Viceroy (replaced by Governor-General for each dominion).
