# 📌 Plan Holidays (1966–1969) & Five-Year Plans of India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Plan Holiday Period**: **1966 to 1969** (3 Annual Plans: 1966–67, 1967–68, and 1968–69).
- **Primary Cause**: Severe failure of the **Third Five-Year Plan (1961–1966)** due to the **Sino-Indian War (1962)**, **Indo-Pak War (1965)**, severe droughts, and hyperinflation.
- **Key Milestone**: Implementation of the new agricultural strategy, laying the foundation for the **Green Revolution** (HYV seeds, fertilizers, irrigation).
- **Fourth Plan Start**: The Fourth Five-Year Plan was launched in **1969**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Context & Causes of Plan Holidays (1966–1969)

*Why five-year planning was temporarily suspended.*

- **Sino-Indian War (1962) & Indo-Pak War (1965)**: Diverted huge national capital and administrative resources toward defense spending.
- **Severe Droughts (1965–1966)**: Triggered food shortages and high inflation across the country.
- **Devaluation of Rupee (1966)**: Forced economic restructuring under external economic pressure.
- **Solution**: Instead of a full 5-year plan, government made **three Annual Plans** (1966–67, 1967–68, 1968–69) prioritizing agriculture, soil conservation, and irrigation.

---

### 2. Timeline of Five-Year Plans & Key Gaps

*Complete breakdown of planning gaps in Indian history.*

- **First Plan (1951–1956)**: Based on Harrod-Domar model; focused on Agriculture.
- **Second Plan (1956–1961)**: Based on Mahalanobis model; focused on Heavy Industries.
- **Third Plan (1961–1966)**: Known as Gadgil Yojana; faced severe economic crisis.
- 🔴 **Plan Holidays (1966–1969)**: 3 Annual Plans; Green Revolution introduced.
- **Fourth Plan (1969–1974)**: Growth with Stability & Self-Reliance; 14 Banks Nationalized (1969).
- **Fifth Plan (1974–1978)**: Focused on *Garibi Hatao* (Poverty Alleviation); terminated 1 year early by Janata Party.
- 🔴 **Rolling Plan (1978–1980)**: Introduced by Morarji Desai government.
- **Sixth Plan (1980–1985)**: Re-launched by Congress Government; focus on poverty reduction & infrastructure.
- 🔴 **Annual Plans (1990–1992)**: 2 Annual Plans due to political instability & BoP Forex crisis (leading to LPG Reforms of 1991).
