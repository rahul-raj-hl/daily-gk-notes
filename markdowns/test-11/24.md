# 📌 World Environment Day 2025 & UNEP Initiatives

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Theme for 2025**: **"Putting an End to Plastic Pollution"** (supporting the UNEP-led *#BeatPlasticPollution* campaign).
- **Date**: Celebrated annually on **5 June**.
- **Official Host Nation (2025)**: **Republic of Korea (South Korea)**.
- **First Celebration**: Held in **1973** with the theme *"Only One Earth"*.
- **Establishment**: Established by the UN General Assembly in **1972** during the Stockholm Conference on the Human Environment.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Highlights of World Environment Day 2025

*Global focus and hosting details.*

- **Date of Observance**: **5 June** every year.
- **Theme 2025**: **"Putting an End to Plastic Pollution"**.
- **Focus Area**: Eliminating single-use plastics and promoting global circular economy frameworks.
- **Global Host Nation**: **South Korea** hosted the global celebrations for 2025.
- **Nodal Agency**: United Nations Environment Programme (**UNEP**), headquartered in **Nairobi, Kenya**.

---

### 2. Historical Background & Origin

*Milestones in international environmental governance.*

- **Stockholm Conference (1972)**: The UN Conference on the Human Environment held in Stockholm, Sweden, led to the creation of UNEP.
- **Inception**: Officially designated by the UN General Assembly (UNGA) in **June 1972**.
- **First Observance**: First celebrated in **1973** under the tagline ***"Only One Earth"***.
