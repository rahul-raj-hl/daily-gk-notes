# 📌 Vitamins, Chemical Names & Deficiency Diseases

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Pernicious Anemia**: Caused by the deficiency of **Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$** (Cyanocobalamin).
- **Intrinsic Factor (IF)**: Lack of intrinsic factor in stomach secretions prevents absorption of Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$, leading to pernicious anemia.
- **Vitamin K**: Essential for **blood clotting/coagulation**; deficiency causes non-clotting of blood.
- **Vitamin E**: Known as **Tocopherol**; deficiency causes reduced fertility and muscle weakness.
- **Vitamin $\text{B}_6$**: Known as **Pyridoxine**; deficiency causes microcytic anemia, skin lesions, and convulsions.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$ (Cyanocobalamin)

*Key facts about the Cobalt-containing vitamin.*

- **Chemical Name**: **Cyanocobalamin** (contains the metal Cobalt).
- **Function**: Essential for Red Blood Cell (RBC) maturation and maintaining nervous system health.
- **Deficiency Disease**: **Pernicious Anemia** (body produces abnormally large, immature RBCs that cannot function properly).
- **Sources**: Mainly non-vegetarian foods such as red meat, fish, eggs, and dairy products.

---

### 2. Complete List of Vitamins, Chemical Names & Deficiency Diseases

*High-frequency table tested across SSC and State Exams.*

- **Fat-Soluble Vitamins (A, D, E, K)**:
  - **Vitamin A (Retinol)**: Night Blindness, Xerophthalmia.
  - **Vitamin D (Calciferol)**: Rickets (in children), Osteomalacia (in adults).
  - **Vitamin E (Tocopherol)**: Reduced fertility, muscle weakness.
  - **Vitamin K (Phylloquinone)**: Failure of blood clotting / excessive bleeding.

- **Water-Soluble Vitamins (B-Complex, C)**:
  - **Vitamin $\text{B}_1$ (Thiamine)**: Beriberi.
  - **Vitamin $\text{B}_2$ (Riboflavin)**: Ariboflavinosis (cracked lips / Cheilosis).
  - **Vitamin $\text{B}_3$ (Niacin)**: Pellagra (4D disease: Dermatitis, Diarrhea, Dementia, Death).
  - **Vitamin $\text{B}_5$ (Pantothenic Acid)**: Paresthesia (burning feet syndrome).
  - **Vitamin $\text{B}_6$ (Pyridoxine)**: Anemia, skin lesions, nervous system disorders.
  - **Vitamin $\text{B}_7$ (Biotin)**: Hair loss, dermatitis.
  - **Vitamin $\text{B}_9$ (Folic Acid)**: Megaloblastic Anemia.
  - **Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$ (Cyanocobalamin)**: **Pernicious Anemia**.
  - **Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)**: Scurvy (bleeding gums).

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic for B-Complex Names**: **"THE ROMAN NIECES PLAYED PERIPHERAL FLUTE CREATIVELY"**
> - **THE** $\rightarrow$ **Th**iamine ($\text{B}_1$)
> - **ROMAN** $\rightarrow$ **R**iboflavin ($\text{B}_2$)
> - **NIECES** $\rightarrow$ **N**iacin ($\text{B}_3$)
> - **PLAYED** $\rightarrow$ **P**yridoxine ($\text{B}_6$)
> - **FLUTE** $\rightarrow$ **F**olic Acid ($\text{B}_9$)
> - **CREATIVELY** $\rightarrow$ **C**yanocobalamin ($\text{B}_{12}$)

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Deficiency of which of the following Vitamins causes pernicious anaemia?

- [ ] Vitamin K
- [ ] Vitamin E
- [x] Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$
- [ ] Vitamin $\text{B}_6$

> Pernicious anemia is a specific type of anemia caused by the body's inability to absorb Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$.

---

### PYQ 2

Which metal ion is present in the molecular structure of Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$?

- [ ] Iron
- [x] Cobalt
- [ ] Zinc
- [ ] Magnesium

> Vitamin $\text{B}_{12}$ (Cyanocobalamin) contains a central Cobalt atom.

---

### PYQ 3

Which of the following vitamins helps in blood coagulation (clotting)?

- [ ] Vitamin A
- [ ] Vitamin C
- [ ] Vitamin E
- [x] Vitamin K

> Vitamin K is essential for the synthesis of prothrombin and other clotting factors required for blood coagulation.
