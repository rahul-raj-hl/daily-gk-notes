# 📌 Global Innovation Index (GII) 2025

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **India's Rank (GII 2025)**: **38th** globally among 139 evaluated economies.
- **Regional & Income Group Rank**: Ranked **#1** among lower-middle-income economies and in Central & Southern Asia.
- **Publishing Body**: Published annually by the **World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO)**.
- **Top 3 Countries**: 1. **Switzerland**, 2. Sweden, 3. United States of America.
- **Knowledge & Tech Outputs**: India performs exceptionally well in this sub-category, securing **22nd** position.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Performance Highlights of GII 2025

*India's trajectory and sub-index rankings.*

- **Global Position**: India secured the **38th rank**, reflecting sustained progress in its research, innovation ecosystem, and tech-driven growth.
- **Leading Performance**:
  - Ranked **1st** in the **Central and Southern Asia** region.
  - Ranked **1st** among **lower-middle-income** group economies.
  - Ranked **22nd** in **Knowledge and Technology Outputs** sub-pillar.
- **Core Strengths**: High performance in Knowledge & Technology Outputs, Human Capital & Research, and IT service exports.
- **Areas of Improvement**: Infrastructure, Business Sophistication, and Institutional Frameworks.

---

### 2. About Global Innovation Index (GII)

*Framework, pillars, and publishing agency.*

- **Released By**: World Intellectual Property Organization (**WIPO**), headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland.
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Assesses economies using **over 80 indicators** across **7 key pillars**:
  1. Institutions
  2. Human Capital & Research
  3. Infrastructure
  4. Market Sophistication
  5. Business Sophistication
  6. Knowledge & Technology Outputs
  7. Creative Outputs
