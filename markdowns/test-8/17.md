# 📌 Gallantry Awards in India & Shaurya Chakra

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Republic Day 2025 (Phase-I) Update**: A total of **33 Shaurya Chakras** were approved in the Phase-I (January 2025) gallantry list.
- **Nodal Announcing Authority**: Announced by the **Ministry of Defence (MoD)** and approved/conferred by the **President of India** (NOT the PMO).
- **Gallantry Classification**:
  - **Wartime**: Param Vir Chakra (PVC), Maha Vir Chakra (MVC), Vir Chakra (VrC).
  - **Peacetime**: Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra, **Shaurya Chakra**.
- **Shaurya Chakra Hierarchy**: **3rd highest peacetime gallantry award** (after Ashoka Chakra and Kirti Chakra).
- **Conferment Schedule**: Announced **twice a year** — on Republic Day (26th January) and Independence Day (15th August).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Classification & Hierarchy of Gallantry Awards

| Category | Order of Precedence | Highest Award | Second Highest | Third Highest |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wartime Gallantry** | In the face of the enemy | **Param Vir Chakra (PVC)** | Maha Vir Chakra (MVC) | Vir Chakra (VrC) |
| **Peacetime Gallantry** | Otherwise than in face of enemy | **Ashoka Chakra** | Kirti Chakra | **Shaurya Chakra** |

---

### 2. Operational Framework & Authorities

| Stage of Process | Responsible Authority / Body | Key Mandate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nomination & Recommendation** | Armed Forces / Central Armed Police Forces / State Police | Initiates recommendations for brave acts |
| **Scrutiny & Review** | Central Honours & Awards Committee (Ministry of Defence) | Evaluates and shortlists nominations |
| **Final Approval & Order** | **President of India** | Approves and formally confers the awards |
| **Administrative Body** | **Ministry of Defence (MoD)** | Publishes gazette notifications and lists |

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Dimension / Parameter | Detail / Fact | Key Exam Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Instituted (Wartime)** | **26th January 1950** (with effect from 15th August 1947) | Param Vir, Maha Vir, Vir Chakra |
| **First Instituted (Peacetime)** | **4th January 1952** (as Class I, II, III) | Renamed Ashoka, Kirti, Shaurya in 1967 |
| **Frequency of Announcement** | **Twice a Year** | Republic Day & Independence Day |
| **Eligibility** | **Military Personnel & Civilians** | Open to all ranks of forces & civilians |
| **Highest Civilian Award vs Gallantry** | Bharat Ratna (Civilian) vs PVC / Ashoka Chakra | Do not confuse civilian with gallantry |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Parameter | Shaurya Chakra | Vir Chakra |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Type of Award** | Peacetime Gallantry Award | Wartime Gallantry Award |
| **Operational Context** | Counter-terrorism, insurgency, security & non-combat bravery | Direct combat operational bravery against enemy forces |
| **Precedence Rank** | 3rd Highest Peacetime Award | 3rd Highest Wartime Award |
| **Announcing Agency** | Ministry of Defence (MoD) | Ministry of Defence (MoD) |

Which of the following is the highest peacetime gallantry award in India?

- [ ] Param Vir Chakra

> **Explanation**: Ashoka Chakra is India's highest peacetime gallantry award, followed by Kirti Chakra and Shaurya Chakra. Param Vir Chakra is the highest *wartime* gallantry award.

Who among the following has the authority to formally approve and confer Gallantry Awards in India?

- [ ] Prime Minister of India
- [ ] Union Home Minister
- [x] President of India
- [ ] Chief of Defence Staff

> **Explanation**: The President of India is the Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces and formally approves and confers all wartime and peacetime gallantry awards.

- [ ] 1952

> **Explanation**: Instituted in 1952 as Ashoka Chakra Class I, Class II, and Class III, they were officially renamed Ashoka Chakra, Kirti Chakra, and Shaurya Chakra respectively in 1967.
