# 📌 Lancet Global Estimates on Violence (GBD Study)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Core Finding**: Global estimates from *The Lancet* (based on the Global Burden of Disease Study) revealed that **over 1 billion individuals** aged 15+ experienced sexual violence during childhood.
- **Key Discrepancy / Incorrect Claim**: Stating that *only 15% of women globally* experienced childhood sexual violence contradicts the finding that **over 1 billion survivors** exist worldwide, along with country-specific estimates (e.g., **>30% in India**).
- **Intimate Partner Violence (IPV)**: An estimated **608 million females** aged 15+ globally have been exposed to IPV in their lifetime.
- **India-Specific Data**: In India, the prevalence of IPV among women aged 15+ is **~23%**, while childhood sexual violence affects **>30% of females** and **~13% of males**.

## 🎯 Key Definitions

- **Intimate Partner Violence (IPV)**: Physical, sexual, or psychological harm by a current or former partner.
- **Childhood Sexual Violence (SVAC)**: Any forced or non-consensual sexual act/contact experienced prior to turning 18 years of age.
- **Disability-Adjusted Life Years (DALY)**: A measure of overall disease burden, expressed as the number of years lost due to ill-health, disability, or early death.
