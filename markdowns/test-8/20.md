# 📌 Devices of Parliamentary Proceedings: Question Hour

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Question Hour**: The **first hour** of every parliamentary sitting dedicated to members asking questions to ministers (and private members).
- **Types of Questions**:
  1. **Starred Question**: Answered **orally**; marked with an asterisk ($*$); **supplementary questions CAN follow**.
  2. **Unstarred Question**: Answered in **written** format; **NO supplementary questions allowed**.
  3. **Short Notice Question**: Asked on an urgent matter giving **fewer than 10 days' notice**; answered **orally**.
- **Non-Existent Category**: **Long Notice Question** (does NOT exist in Indian parliamentary procedure).

---

## 📖 Parliamentary Questions Comparison Matrix

| Type of Question | Notice Period | Mode of Answer | Supplementary Questions Allowed? | Distinguishing Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starred Question** | Minimum 10 clear days (up to 21 days) | **Oral** | **YES** | Differentiated in lists by an **asterisk ($*$)** and green color (Lok Sabha). |
| **Unstarred Question** | Minimum 10 clear days | **Written** | **NO** | Printed on white paper (Lok Sabha); no oral discussion follows. |
| **Short Notice Question** | **Fewer than 10 days** | **Oral** | **YES** | Addressed on matters of **urgent public importance** with Speaker's consent. |
| **Question to Private Member** | Minimum 10 clear days | Oral / Written | Depends | Directed to a non-minister regarding bills, resolutions, or matters they are responsible for. |

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Parliamentary Device | Timing / Description | Key Exam Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Question Hour** | First hour of a sitting (**11:00 AM – 12:00 PM**) | Explicitly mentioned in the Rules of Procedure of the House. |
| **Zero Hour** | Immediately follows Question Hour (**12:00 PM – 1:00 PM**) | An **informal Indian innovation** (since 1962); **NOT** in the rulebook. |
| **Half-an-Hour Discussion** | For matters of sufficient public importance needing clarification | Allocated by Speaker on 3 days a week; no formal motion or voting. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Term / Device | Rulebook Status | Key Characteristic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Question Hour** | Mentioned in Rules of Procedure | Formal mechanism to hold the Executive accountable every day. |
| **Zero Hour** | **Not** mentioned in Rules | Unofficial tool created by Indian parliamentarians to raise urgent matters without prior notice. |
| **Calling Attention Motion** | Mentioned in Rules | Indian innovation (since 1954) to call a minister's attention to urgent public importance. |

---

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "STAR = SPOKEN, UNSTARRED = UNSPOKEN (WRITTEN)"**
> - **Star ($*$)** $\rightarrow$ **S**poken orally $\rightarrow$ **S**upplementary questions permitted.
> - **Unstarred** $\rightarrow$ **U**nspoken (Written) $\rightarrow$ **Zero** supplementary questions.

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which of the following is NOT a recognized type of question asked during the Question Hour in the Indian Parliament?

- [ ] Starred Question
- [ ] Unstarred Question
- [x] Long Notice Question
- [ ] Short Notice Question

> **Explanation**: "Long Notice Question" is not a parliamentary term. The three official types of questions are Starred, Unstarred, and Short Notice Questions.

---

### PYQ 2

Which type of question in Parliament requires an oral answer and allows members to ask supplementary questions?

- [ ] Unstarred Question
- [x] Starred Question
- [ ] Short Notice Question
- [ ] Private Member Question

> **Explanation**: Starred questions are distinguished by an asterisk mark, require oral answers on the floor, and allow follow-up supplementary questions.

---

### PYQ 3

The 'Zero Hour' in the Indian Parliamentary system is best described as:

- [ ] The first hour of a sitting reserved for ministers.
- [x] An informal device starting immediately after Question Hour not mentioned in the rules.
- [ ] A voting hour for financial budgets.
- [ ] The last hour before adjournment for the day.

> **Explanation**: Zero Hour starts right after Question Hour (around 12 noon) and is a unique Indian parliamentary innovation introduced in 1962, not mentioned in the rulebook.

---

### PYQ 4

A 'Short Notice Question' can be asked in the House of Parliament by giving a notice of:

- [ ] More than 30 days
- [ ] Exactly 15 days
- [x] Fewer than 10 days
- [ ] At least 21 days

> **Explanation**: A Short Notice Question relates to a matter of urgent public importance and is admitted with a notice period shorter than 10 days.

---

### PYQ 5

During Parliamentary proceedings, an 'Unstarred Question' results in which form of response?

- [ ] An oral answer by the Minister followed by debate.
- [x] A written answer laid on the table of the House.
- [ ] Immediate voting by secret ballot.
- [ ] Referred automatically to a Parliamentary Standing Committee.

> **Explanation**: Unstarred questions demand a written reply laid on the table by the minister; hence, no supplementary questions can be asked on the spot.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Consider the following statements regarding Parliamentary proceedings in India:
1. Statement 1: Starred questions require a written answer and do not allow supplementary questions.
2. Statement 2: Zero Hour is an informal device that is not explicitly mentioned in the Rules of Procedure.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- [ ] 1 only
- [x] 2 only
- [ ] Both 1 and 2
- [ ] Neither 1 nor 2

> **Explanation**: Statement 1 is incorrect because Starred questions require *oral* answers and *do* allow supplementary questions. Statement 2 is correct.

---

### Q2

Which among the following parliamentary procedures is an Indian innovation introduced in 1954?

- [ ] Question Hour
- [ ] Adjournment Motion
- [x] Calling Attention Motion
- [ ] Motion of Thanks

> **Explanation**: Calling Attention Motion is an original Indian innovation introduced in 1954 to draw a minister's attention to an urgent public matter.

---

### Q3

Which color of paper is used for printing Starred Questions in the Lok Sabha for easy identification?

- [ ] White
- [x] Green
- [ ] Pink
- [ ] Yellow

> **Explanation**: In Lok Sabha, Starred questions are printed on Green paper, Unstarred on White, Short Notice on Light Pink, and Questions to Private Members on Yellow paper.

---

### Q4

What is the maximum limit of Starred Questions allowed for oral answers in a single day's list in Lok Sabha?

- [ ] 10
- [x] 20
- [ ] 50
- [ ] No limit

> **Explanation**: In the Lok Sabha, maximum 20 questions are listed for oral answers (Starred) per day, whereas in the Rajya Sabha the limit is 15.
