# 📌 Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam: "Wings of Fire" & Key Contributions

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **"Wings of Fire"**: Autobiography of **Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam** (co-authored with **Arun Tiwari**), published in **1999**.
- **Audiobook Voice**: The English audiobook edition of "Wings of Fire" was narrated by the acclaimed Kannada playwright and actor **Girish Karnad**.
- **11th President of India**: Served from **2002 to 2007**; widely known as the **"People's President"** and **"Missile Man of India"**.
- **Key Technological Feat**: Project Director for **SLV-III**, India's first indigenously designed Satellite Launch Vehicle (placed Rohini satellite in orbit, 1980).
- **Highest Civilian Honor**: Awarded the **Bharat Ratna in 1997**.

### 1. Major Books Authored by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

| Book Title | Co-Author / Collaborator | Core Theme / Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wings of Fire** (1999) | Arun Tiwari | Autobiography tracing early life, ISRO/DRDO journey, and missile development. |
| **India 2020: A Vision for the New Millennium** (1998) | Y. S. Rajan | Action plan to transform India into a developed nation by 2020. |
| **Ignited Minds** (2002) | — | Unleashing the power within India's youth and nation-building. |
| **Guiding Souls** (2005) | Arun Tiwari | Dialogues on the purpose of life, spirituality, and morality. |
| **Turning Points** (2012) | — | Sequel to *Wings of Fire*, detailing his presidential tenure. |
| **Target 3 Billion** (2011) | Srijan Pal Singh | Rural development through PURA (Providing Urban Amenities to Rural Areas). |

### 2. Major Missiles & Space Projects Associated

| Project / Missile | Significance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SLV-III** | India's first indigenous satellite launch vehicle (ISRO). |
| **IGMDP (1983)** | Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme architect (**Agni**, **Prithvi**, **Nag**, **Akash**, **Trishul**). |
| **Pokhran-II (1998)** | Served as Chief Scientific Adviser to PM and Director of DRDO during nuclear tests (**Operation Shakti**). |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Term / Personality | Context | Key Distinguishing Detail |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Co-author of *Wings of Fire*** | **Arun Tiwari** | Written jointly by Dr. Kalam and his colleague **Arun Tiwari**. |
| **Audiobook Voice of *Wings of Fire*** | **Girish Karnad** | Famous Jnanpith laureate and actor who **narrated** the official English audiobook. |
| **Missile Man vs Missile Woman** | Science & Tech | Dr. Kalam is the **Missile Man**; **Dr. Tessy Thomas** is known as the **Missile Woman of India** (Agni-IV Director). |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic for IGMDP Missiles: "P-A-T-N-A"**
> - **P**rithvi (Surface-to-surface)
> - **A**gni (Intermediate-range ballistic missile)
> - **T**rishul (Short-range surface-to-air)
> - **N**ag (Anti-tank guided missile)
> - **A**kash (Medium-range surface-to-air)
