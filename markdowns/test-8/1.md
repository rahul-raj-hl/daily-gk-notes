# 📌 Indian Independence Act, 1947

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Legislative Basis**: Based on the **Mountbatten Plan (3rd June Plan)**; passed by the British Parliament on **July 5, 1947**, and received Royal Assent on **July 18, 1947**.
- **Creation of Dominions**: Provided for the partition of British India into two independent dominions: **India** and **Pakistan** (effective August 15, 1947).
- **Constituent Assemblies**: Empowers the Constituent Assemblies of both dominions to **frame and adopt their own Constitutions** without any interference from the British Parliament. *(Hence, the Act did NOT lay down a Constitution by British legislative will).*
- **Lapse of Paramountcy**: Declared the **lapse of British Paramountcy** over Indian Princely States and tribal areas (leaving them free to join India, Pakistan, or remain independent).
- **Interim Governance**: Empowered Constituent Assemblies to act as interim legislatures, governing each dominion under the **Government of India Act, 1935** (with modifications) until new constitutions were framed.

---

## 📖 Key Constitutional Provisions Matrix

### 1. Salient Features of the Indian Independence Act, 1947

| Provision | Details / Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sovereignty & Power** | Abolished the office of **Secretary of State for India**; transferred functions to Secretary of State for Commonwealth Affairs. |
| **Governor-General** | Provided a Governor-General for each dominion, appointed by the King on the advice of the Dominion cabinet. |
| **Princely States Freedom** | Granted freedom to join India, join Pakistan, or stay independent (British paramountcy lapsed). |
| **Boundary Determination** | Established two Boundary Commissions headed by **Sir Cyril Radcliffe** for Punjab and Bengal. |
| **Veto Power** | Deprived the British Monarch of the right to veto bills or reserve them for royal approval. |

---

### 2. Territorial Division Framework

| Dominion | Key Territories Included |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Dominion of India** | Remaining territory of British India, including East Punjab, West Bengal, and princely states acceding to India. |
| **Dominion of Pakistan** | West Punjab, East Bengal, Sindh, NWFP, Baluchistan, and Sylhet district of Assam (via referendum). |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Incorrect Claim / Misconception | Actual Historical Fact |
| :--- | :--- |
| ❌ *The Act drafted/imposed a Constitution for India and Pakistan.* | ✅ The Act **empowered Constituent Assemblies** to frame their own Constitutions independently. |
| ❌ *The British Monarch retained veto power over Indian laws after Aug 15, 1947.* | ✅ The right to veto or reserve bills by the British Monarch was **completely abolished**. |
| ❌ *Princely states were legally forced to join either India or Pakistan.* | ✅ They were given the option to join India, join Pakistan, or **remain independent**. |

> **Mnemonic for Key Dates of 1947 Act: "3-5-18-15"**
> - **3 June**: Mountbatten Plan announced
> - **5 July**: Introduced/Passed in British Parliament
> - **18 July**: Royal Assent received
> - **15 August**: Partition and Independence enacted
