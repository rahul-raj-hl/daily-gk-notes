# 📌 Congress Socialist Party (CSP) - 1934

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Formation Year & Place**: Founded in **May 17, 1934** at **Patna** (All India Conference held in Bombay, Oct 1934).
- **Key Founders**: **Jayaprakash Narayan** (General Secretary), **Acharya Narendra Dev** (President), **Minoo Masani** (Joint Secretary), **Ram Manohar Lohia**, Achyut Patwardhan, and Yusuf Meherally.
- **Nature of Party**: A **socialist caucus within the Indian National Congress (INC)** that remained loyal to the parent organization while attempting to push INC towards radical socio-economic policies.
- **Key Publications**:
  - *Why Socialism?* – Book written by **Jayaprakash Narayan**.
  - *Congress Socialist* – Official journal published/edited by **Ram Manohar Lohia** and **Asoka Mehta**.

---

## 📖 CSP Ideology & Programmatic Matrix

### 1. Ideological Standpoints & Goals

| Dimension | CSP Position / Strategy |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ideological Blends** | Combined **Marxism, Democratic Socialism, and Gandhian Principles** (Anti-imperialism). |
| **Relation with INC** | Functioned *within* INC; aimed to transform Congress into a broad anti-imperialist mass front. |
| **Key Economic Demands** | Abolition of **Zamindari system**, abolition of **Capitalism**, and integration of **Princely States**. |
| **Mass Mobilization** | Linked the national struggle to industrial workers' and peasants' movements (Kisan Sabhas). |
| **Industry & Land** | Favored state control over key industries, land redistribution, and cooperative farming. |

---

### 2. Prominent CSP Leaders & Contributions

| Leader | Primary Contribution / Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Jayaprakash Narayan (JP)** | Founding General Secretary; wrote *Why Socialism?* (1936). |
| **Acharya Narendra Dev** | Founding President; drafted early party manifestos and policies. |
| **Ram Manohar Lohia** | Edited *Congress Socialist* journal; foreign affairs voice of CSP. |
| **Minoo Masani** | Joint Secretary; organized early student and youth wings in Bombay. |
| **Yusuf Meherally** | Coined iconic slogans like *"Simon Go Back"* and *"Quit India"*. |

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Strategic Point | Fact / Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Origins in Prison** | The idea of forming the CSP was conceived by young leaders while lodged in **Nasik Central Jail** during the Civil Disobedience Movement (1932–33). |
| **Banning/Dissolution** | Disbanded in **1948** when the INC amended its constitution to ban dual party memberships, leading socialists to form the **Socialist Party of India**. |
| **Faizpur Session (1936)** | CSP influenced INC to adopt a radical **Agrarian Programme** at the Faizpur Congress Session. |
| **Quit India Movement (1942)** | CSP leaders like JP Narayan, Ram Manohar Lohia, and Aruna Asaf Ali ran the **underground resistance** when senior Congress leaders were jailed. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Myth / Misconception | Actual Historical Fact |
| :--- | :--- |
| ❌ *CSP was a separate rival party to the INC from 1934.* | ✅ CSP was a **group/caucus within the INC**; members were required to be INC members. |
| ❌ *Jawaharlal Nehru & Subhas Chandra Bose were CSP founders.* | ✅ They **sympathized** with socialist ideas, but **never formally joined** the CSP. |
| ❌ *CSP rejected Marxism completely.* | ✅ Early CSP heavily adopted **Marxist analysis** alongside Gandhian methods. |
