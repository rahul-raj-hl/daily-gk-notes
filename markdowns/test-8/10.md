# 📌 Bal Gangadhar Tilak & The Swaraj Slogan

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Famous Slogan**: *"Swaraj is my birthright and I shall have it!"* (Marathi: *"स्वराज्य हा माझा जन्मसिद्ध हक्क आहे आणि तो मी मिळवणारच!"*).
- **Leader**: **Bal Gangadhar Tilak** (referred to as **'Lokmanya'** meaning "Accepted by the people" and titled *"Father of the Indian Unrest"* by Valentine Chirol).
- **Faction**: Key leader of the **Extremist Phase (1905–1919)** of the Indian National Congress alongside Lala Lajpat Rai and Bipin Chandra Pal (**Lal-Bal-Pal** trio).
- **Major Contributions**:
  - Founded the **Indian Home Rule League** in April 1916 at Belgaum (Karnataka).
  - Started newspapers **Kesari** (in Marathi) and **Mahratta** (in English).
  - Popularized public celebrations of **Ganesh Utsav (1893)** and **Shivaji Jayanti (1895)** to build national consciousness.

### 1. Moderates vs. Extremists Phase

| Feature | Moderates (1885–1905) | Extremists (1905–1919) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prominent Leaders** | Dadabhai Naoroji, W.C. Bonnerjee, Gopal Krishna Gokhale | Bal Gangadhar Tilak, Lala Lajpat Rai, Bipin Chandra Pal, Aurobindo Ghosh |
| **Methods** | "3 Ps" — Prayers, Petitions, and Protests within constitutional limits | Swadeshi, Boycott of foreign goods, Passive Resistance, National Education |

## 🧠 Exam Booster

| Strategic Point | Fact / Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lucknow Pact (1916)** | Tilak played a pivotal role in bridging the Moderates-Extremists rift (Surat Split 1907) and forming the **INC-Muslim League Alliance**. |
| **First to Demand Swaraj** | Grand Old Man **Dadabhai Naoroji** first declared 'Swaraj' as the official INC goal at the **1906 Calcutta Session**, inspired by Tilak's movement. |
| **Home Rule Area Distribution** | Tilak's League operated in **Maharashtra (excluding Bombay), Karnataka, Central Provinces, and Berar**. (Annie Besant covered the rest of India). |
