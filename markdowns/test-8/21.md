# 📌 Environmental Protection in the Indian Constitution

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Article 48A (DPSP)**: Mandates that the **State** shall endeavor to protect and improve the environment and safeguard forests and wildlife. Added by the **42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976**.
- **Article 51A(g) (Fundamental Duty)**: Mandates that it shall be the duty of **every citizen** to protect and improve the natural environment (forests, lakes, rivers, wildlife) and have compassion for living creatures. Also added by the **42nd Amendment, 1976**.
- **Article 21 (Fundamental Right)**: The Supreme Court expanded the **Right to Life** to include the right to a clean, safe, and healthy environment (e.g., *Subhash Kumar v. State of Bihar*, *M.C. Mehta* cases).

---

## 📖 Constitution & Environment Provisions Matrix

| Constitutional Head | Article / Provision | Primary Responsibility | Enforcement Status | Added By |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Directive Principles (DPSP)** | **Article 48A** | Obligation on the **State** | Non-justiciable directly (enforceable indirectly via Art 21) | 42nd Amendment, 1976 |
| **Fundamental Duties** | **Article 51A(g)** | Moral duty of **Every Citizen** | Non-justiciable | 42nd Amendment, 1976 |
| **Fundamental Rights** | **Article 21** | Guaranteed to **Every Person** | Directly Enforceable (Writ jurisdiction) | Original Constitution (expanded by Judiciary) |
| **Seventh Schedule** | Concurrent List (List III) | **Center & States** (Forests, Wildlife) | Legislative power over environment | 42nd Amendment, 1976 (transferred Forests/Wildlife from State to Concurrent) |

| **42nd Amendment Act** | 1976 | Introduced **Article 48A** and **Part IV-A (Article 51A)** into the Constitution. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused Concepts

| Provision | Targeted Entity | Key Wording |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Article 48A** | **The State** | "The **State shall endeavor** to protect and improve..." |
| **Article 51A(g)** | **Every Citizen** | "It shall be the duty of **every citizen** to protect..." |
| **Article 48** | **The State** (Agriculture) | Organize agriculture and animal husbandry; ban slaughter of cows/calves (Socialist/Gandhian DPSP). |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "48A = STATE, 51A(g) = CITIZEN GREEN"**
> - **48A** $\rightarrow$ Part IV (DPSP) $\rightarrow$ Duty of the **State**
> - **51A(g)** $\rightarrow$ Part IV-A (Duties) $\rightarrow$ **(g)** for **G**reen environment duty of **Citizens**

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Under which Article of the Indian Constitution is it mandated that the State shall endeavor to protect and improve the environment and safeguard the country's forests and wildlife?

- [ ] Article 39A
- [x] Article 48A
- [ ] Article 43A
- [ ] Article 51A(g)

> **Explanation**: Article 48A was added to Part IV (Directive Principles of State Policy) by the 42nd Amendment in 1976, placing an explicit obligation on the State to protect the environment and wildlife.

### PYQ 2

Which Constitutional Amendment Act added provisions regarding the protection of the environment under both Directive Principles of State Policy and Fundamental Duties?

- [ ] 44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978
- [x] 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976
- [ ] 86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002
- [ ] 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992

> **Explanation**: The 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976 added both Article 48A (DPSP) and Article 51A(g) (Fundamental Duty) to promote environmental protection.

### PYQ 3

Which sub-clause of Article 51A prescribes the duty of every citizen to protect and improve the natural environment including forests, lakes, rivers, and wildlife?

- [ ] Article 51A(a)
- [ ] Article 51A(e)
- [x] Article 51A(g)
- [ ] Article 51A(k)

> **Explanation**: Article 51A(g) explicitly states that it is the duty of every citizen of India to protect and improve the natural environment and have compassion for living creatures.

### PYQ 4

The right to clean drinking water and a pollution-free environment has been recognized by the Supreme Court of India as an integral part of which Fundamental Right?

- [ ] Article 14 (Right to Equality)
- [ ] Article 19 (Right to Freedom)
- [x] Article 21 (Right to Life and Personal Liberty)
- [ ] Article 32 (Right to Constitutional Remedies)

> **Explanation**: In landmark cases such as *Subhash Kumar v. State of Bihar (1991)*, the Supreme Court held that the Right to Life under Article 21 includes the right to enjoy pollution-free water and air.

### PYQ 5

By which Constitutional Amendment were 'Forests' and 'Protection of Wild Animals and Birds' shifted from the State List to the Concurrent List?

- [ ] 24th Amendment Act, 1971

- [ ] 52nd Amendment Act, 1985

> **Explanation**: The 42nd Amendment Act, 1976 transferred five subjects from the State List to the Concurrent List, including Forests and Protection of Wild Animals and Birds.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Consider the following statements regarding constitutional provisions for environmental protection in India:
1. Statement 1: Article 48A imposes a duty on citizens, while Article 51A(g) imposes a directive on the State.
2. Statement 2: Both Article 48A and Article 51A(g) were inserted into the Constitution by the same Amendment Act in 1976.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- [ ] 1 only
- [x] 2 only
- [ ] Both 1 and 2
- [ ] Neither 1 nor 2

> **Explanation**: Statement 1 is incorrect because Article 48A directs the *State*, while Article 51A(g) imposes a duty on *citizens*. Statement 2 is correct as both were added by the 42nd Amendment in 1976.

---

### Q2

Which of the following landmark Supreme Court cases explicitly established that the Right to Life under Article 21 encompasses the right to a clean and healthy environment?

- [ ] Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala
- [x] Subhash Kumar v. State of Bihar
- [ ] Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India
- [ ] Minerva Mills v. Union of India

> **Explanation**: In *Subhash Kumar v. State of Bihar (1991)*, the Supreme Court held that Article 21 includes the right to wholesome environment, pollution-free water, and air for full enjoyment of life.

### Q3

Which of the following legislation was enacted by the Parliament under Article 253 of the Constitution to implement decisions made at the 1972 Stockholm UN Conference on Human Environment?

- [ ] Forest Conservation Act, 1980
- [x] Environment (Protection) Act, 1986
- [ ] Public Liability Insurance Act, 1991

> **Explanation**: The Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 was enacted under Article 253 (legislation for giving effect to international agreements) to implement decisions taken at the 1972 Stockholm Conference.
