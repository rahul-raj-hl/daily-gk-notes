# 📌 Teentaal & Rhythmic Structure in Hindustani Music

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Devotional Composition**: The famous bhajan **"Guru Bin Kaun Bataaye"** is typically set to **Teentaal**.
- **Teentaal Profile**: It is the most fundamental and widely used Tala in Hindustani classical music, consisting of **16 Matras (beats)** divided equally into **4 Vibhags (sections)** of 4 beats each.
- **Structure**: 
  - **Tali (Claps)**: Beats 1, 5, and 13.
  - **Khali (Wave)**: Beat 9.
  - **Sam (First Beat)**: Beat 1 ($\mathbf{\times}$).

## 🧠 Technical Features of Teentaal

| Feature | Description / Representation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Theka (Bols)** | `Dha Dhin Dhin Dha \| Dha Dhin Dhin Dha \| Dha Tin Tin Ta \| Ta Dhin Dhin Dha` |
| **Vibhags** | 4 equal divisions of 4 matras each |
| **Notation Signs** | **$\mathbf{\times}$** = Sam (Beat 1), **2** = 2nd Tali (Beat 5), **$\mathbf{0}$** = Khali (Beat 9), **3** = 3rd Tali (Beat 13) |
| **Universality** | Serves as the standard reference rhythm for both beginner exercises and complex solo improvisations on Tabla. |

---

## 🎯 Key Musical Concepts

- **Matra**: The basic unit of time (beat) in a Tala cycle.
- **Vibhag**: A subgroup or section of Matras within a Tala cycle, separated by vertical bars (`|`).
- **Sam**: The starting beat (Beat 1) of any Tala cycle, which carries the strongest dynamic emphasis.
- **Khali**: A unaccented section represented by a wave of the hand, used to balance the rhythmic structure.
