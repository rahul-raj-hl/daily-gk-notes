# 📌 Book Breakdown: *'Maine Cancer Ko Jeet Liya'*

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Book Title**: *'Maine Cancer Ko Jeet Liya'* (Hindi translation of *"I Am a Survivor"*).
- **Author**: **Dr. Palkonda Vijay Anand Reddy** (Renowned Oncologist & Director, Apollo Cancer Center, Hyderabad).
- **Translators (Hindi)**: Dr. R. Suman Latha and Dr. T. C. Vasanta.
- **Core Theme**: A collection of **108 real-life cancer survivor stories** highlighting courage, hope, early detection, and personal battles against cancer.
- **Publisher**: Penguin Random House India.

---

## 📖 Key Takeaways & Fact-Check

| Question / Parameter | Fact Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Original Language vs Translation** | Originally written in **English** (*"I Am a Survivor"*) and translated into **Hindi** (*"Maine Cancer Ko Jeet Liya"*) and **Telugu**. |
| **Author Profile** | **Dr. P. Vijay Anand Reddy** (Senior Consultant & Director, Apollo Cancer Center). |
| **Primary Objective** | To inspire newly diagnosed cancer patients and caregivers through 108 authentic survivor journeys and mental resilience. |
| **Key Message** | Early diagnosis, mental strength, medical intervention, and lifestyle adaptation are crucial to conquering cancer. |
