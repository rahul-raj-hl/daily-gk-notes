# 📌 All India Forward Bloc & Subhas Chandra Bose

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Founding Date & Founder**: The All India Forward Bloc was formed on **May 3, 1939**, by **Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose**.
- **Political Ideology**: Established as a left-wing nationalist political faction within the Indian National Congress (INC).
- **Reason for Formation**: Formed after Netaji resigned as Congress President following ideological differences with Mahatma Gandhi after the **Tripuri Session (1939)**.
- **First All India Conference**: Conducted at **Nagpur in June 1940**, where the resolution titled **"All Power to the Indian People"** was passed.
- **Azad Hind Fauj (INA)**: Bose later took command of the Indian National Army (INA) in **Singapore in 1943** to fight against British rule.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Formation of the Forward Bloc

*Key events around May 1939.*

- **Tripuri Crisis (1939)**: Netaji defeated Pattabhi Sitaramayya (Gandhi's candidate) to become INC President, but resigned due to working committee conflicts.
- **Establishment**: Founded on **May 3, 1939**, in West Bengal as a radical left-wing faction inside Congress.
- **Nagpur Session (1940)**: The first party conference was held in Nagpur in June 1940.
- **Key Demand**: Urged immediate and militant mass agitation against British rule.

---

### 2. Profile of Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose

*Essential facts frequently tested in SSC exams.*

- **Birth**: Born on **23rd January 1897** in Cuttack, Odisha.
- **Political Mentor**: **Chittaranjan Das** (Deshbandhu), who founded the newspaper **'Forward'** (edited by Bose).
- **Congress Leadership**: Elected President of **Haripura Session (1938)** and **Tripuri Session (1939)**.
- **Azad Hind Government**: Formed the Provisional Government of Free India (Arzi Hukumat-e-Azad Hind) in **Singapore in October 1943**.
- **Famous Slogans**: *"Give me blood, and I shall give you freedom!"*, *"Jai Hind"*, and *"Dilli Chalo"*.
