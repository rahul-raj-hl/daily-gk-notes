# 📌 Gautam Buddha, Sarnath & Triratna of Buddhism

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Birthplace of Dhamma & Sangha**: **Sarnath** (near Varanasi) is known as the birthplace of Dhamma and Sangha.
- **First Sermon**: Gautam Buddha delivered his first sermon at the Deer Park in **Sarnath**, an event called **Dharmachakra Parivartan** (Turning of the Wheel of Law).
- **The Three Jewels (Triratna)**: Consists of **Buddha** (The Enlightened One), **Dhamma** (The Teachings), and **Sangha** (The Monastic Order).
- **Enlightenment**: Attained under a Peepal tree at **Bodh Gaya** on the banks of the **Niranjana River** at the age of 35.
- **Mahaparinirvana**: Gautam Buddha passed away at the age of 80 in **Kushinagar** (Kushinara).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Life Events of Gautam Buddha

*Key biographical milestones and technical terms used in Buddhist texts.*

- **Birth & Childhood**: Born in **Lumbini** (near Kapilavastu, Nepal) as Siddhartha to King Shuddhodana and Queen Mayadevi; raised by stepmother Prajapati Gautami.
- **Four Sights**: Saw an old man, a sick person, a dead body, and a peaceful meditating monk, leading to worldly detachment.
- **Renunciation (Mahabhinishkramana)**: Left home at the age of **29** in search of truth.
- **Gurus**: Learned Sankhya philosophy from **Alara Kalama** (Vaishali) and later received guidance from **Udraka Ramaputra** (Rajgir).
- **Enlightenment (Nirvana)**: Attained supreme knowledge under a Peepal tree in Uruvela (**Bodh Gaya**) on the full moon night of Vaishakh.
- **First Sermon (Dharmachakra Parivartan)**: Preached to his 5 former ascetic companions (Kaundinya, Vappa, Bhaddiya, Mahanama, Assaji) at **Sarnath**.
- **Death (Mahaparinirvana)**: Passed away at **Kushinagar** after accepting a meal offered by Chunda.

---

### 2. Triratna (The Three Jewels) of Buddhism

*The core pillars of the Buddhist faith.*

- **Buddha**: Represents the teacher/enlightened master who discovered the path to liberation.
- **Dhamma (Dharma)**: Represents the core doctrine and spiritual teachings preached by Buddha.
- **Sangha**: Represents the monastic community of monks and nuns who practice and preserve the Dhamma.
