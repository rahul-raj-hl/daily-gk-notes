# 📌 Constitutional Bodies & Corresponding Articles

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **All are correct**.
- **Article 324 $\rightarrow$ Election Commission of India**: Responsible for conducting elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, and the offices of President & Vice-President.
- **Article 280 $\rightarrow$ Finance Commission**: Recommends the distribution of net tax proceeds between the Union and the States.
- **Article 148 $\rightarrow$ Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG)**: Guardian / Watchdog of the Public Purse.
- **Article 76 $\rightarrow$ Attorney General for India**: The highest law officer / first legal advisor to the Government of India.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Constitutional Bodies & Functions

*Core provisions, appointments, and mandates under the Constitution:*

- **1. Election Commission of India (Article 324)**:
  - **Function**: Directs, superintends, and controls elections to the Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, State Legislative Assemblies, State Legislative Councils, President, and Vice President (*Note: Panchayat & Municipality elections are conducted by State Election Commissions under Art 243K/243ZA*).
  - **Tenure**: Appointed by the President for a term of **6 years or up to 65 years of age**, whichever is earlier.
- **2. Finance Commission of India (Article 280)**:
  - **Function**: Constituted every 5 years (or earlier) by the President to recommend vertical and horizontal devolution of central taxes to states, and grants-in-aid from the Consolidated Fund of India.
  - **Composition**: A Chairman and 4 other members.
- **3. Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) (Article 148)**:
  - **Function**: Audits all receipts and expenditures of the Government of India and State Governments. Known as the **"Watchdog of the Public Purse"** and friend, philosopher, and guide of the Public Accounts Committee (PAC).
  - **Reports**: CAG reports are scrutinized by the **Public Accounts Committee (PAC)**, which has 22 members (15 from Lok Sabha, 7 from Rajya Sabha).
- **4. Attorney General for India (Article 76)**:
  - **Function**: Chief legal advisor to the Union Government; holds the right of audience in all courts in India and can participate in Parliament proceedings without the right to vote (Article 88).
  - **Tenure**: Holds office during the **pleasure of the President**.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Who is known as the "Guardian and Watchdog of the Public Purse" in India?

- [ ] Finance Minister
- [ ] Governor of RBI
- [x] Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG)
- [ ] Chairman of Finance Commission

> The CAG is designated as the custodian of public money and ensures financial accountability of the executive to the legislature.

---

### Q2

The recommendations made by the Finance Commission under Article 280 are:

- [ ] Legally binding on the Central Government
- [x] Advisory in nature
- [ ] Binding on State Governments only
- [ ] Subject to presidential veto

> The recommendations of the Finance Commission are advisory in nature and not legally binding on the Government of India.

---

### Q3

The Public Accounts Committee (PAC), which examines the audit reports of the CAG, has how many members in total?

- [ ] 15 members
- [ ] 20 members
- [x] 22 members
- [ ] 30 members

> The PAC consists of 22 members (15 elected from the Lok Sabha and 7 from the Rajya Sabha).

---

### Q4

Which constitutional body is responsible for conducting elections to Municipalities and Panchayats in India?

- [ ] Election Commission of India (Art 324)
- [x] State Election Commission (Art 243K)
- [ ] Union Ministry of Panchayati Raj
- [ ] District Collectorate

> Elections to local self-governing bodies (Panchayats and Municipalities) are exclusively conducted by the respective State Election Commissions under Articles 243K and 243ZA.
