# 📌 Famous Indian Temples & Monuments

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Matching**: **1 – b, 2 – d, 3 – a, 4 – c**.
- **Brihadeswara Temple**: Located in **Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu** (*built by Chola King Raja Raja I*).
- **Dilwara Temple**: Located in **Mount Abu, Rajasthan** (*famous Jain temple complex made of white marble*).
- **Lingaraja Temple**: Located in **Bhubaneswar, Odisha** (*Kalinga style architecture dedicated to Shiva/Harihara*).
- **Hampi Group of Monuments**: Located in **Karnataka** (*capital of Vijayanagara Empire, UNESCO World Heritage Site*).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Match Explanation

*Detailed mapping of each temple to its respective location:*

- **1. Brihadeswara Temple $\rightarrow$ b. Tamil Nadu**:
  - Located at Thanjavur on the banks of the Kaveri river.
  - Built by Chola Emperor **Raja Raja I** in 1010 CE. Part of the UNESCO site *"Great Living Chola Temples"*.
- **2. Dilwara Temple $\rightarrow$ d. Rajasthan**:
  - Located at Mount Abu in Rajasthan.
  - Constructed by **Vimal Shah** and **Tejpala** between the 11th and 13th centuries CE. World-renowned for intricate white marble carvings dedicated to Jain Tirthankaras.
- **3. Lingaraja Temple $\rightarrow$ a. Odisha**:
  - Located at Bhubaneswar, Odisha.
  - Built by Somavamsi King **Jajati Keshari** in the 11th century CE in classic Kalinga architecture (*Deula style*).
- **4. Hampi Group of Monuments $\rightarrow$ c. Karnataka**:
  - Located in Vijayanagara district along the Tungabhadra River in Karnataka.
  - Serves as the ancient capital ruins of the **Vijayanagara Empire**.
