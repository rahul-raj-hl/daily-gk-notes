# 📌 World's Highest War Memorial at Galwan & Bharat Ranbhoomi Darshan Initiative

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Bharat Ranbhoomi Darshan initiative**.
- **Memorial Location**: Built near the **KM-120 post** along the strategic **Durbuk–Shyok–Daulat Beg Oldie (DS-DBO) Road** in Ladakh.
- **Global Distinction**: Recognized as the **world's highest war memorial**.
- **Dedicated To**: Honors the **20 Indian Army bravehearts** (led by Colonel B. Santosh Babu) who laid down their lives during the hand-to-hand Galwan Valley clash on **June 15, 2020**.
- **Inauguration**: Inaugurated by Defence Minister **Rajnath Singh**.
- **Architecture & Symbolism**: Made of **red and black granite** (representing sacrifice and valor), shaped with motifs of a **Trishul** and **Damru**, housing an eternal flame, 20 bronze statues, and a digital war museum.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Bharat Ranbhoomi Darshan Initiative

*Promoting military heritage and patriotic border tourism:*

- **Objective**: An initiative conceptualized by the Indian Armed Forces and the Ministry of Defence to develop and open historic battlegrounds and frontier outposts to Indian citizens.
- **Purpose**:
  - Educate visitors and students on India's border history and frontline defense sacrifices.
  - Boost local border economy and sustainable high-altitude tourism in remote regions.
  - Upgrade civil-military tourist amenities along high-altitude routes (such as traveler cafés and viewpoint hubs along the DS-DBO road).

---

### 2. Key Details of the Galwan War Memorial Complex

*Features of the high-altitude monument:*

- **Strategic Setting**:
  - Situated in Eastern Ladakh at an altitude exceeding 14,000 feet.
  - Located on the vital DS-DBO highway connecting Leh to the Daulat Beg Oldie post near the Karakoram Pass.
- **Memorial Elements**:
  - **20 Bronze Statues**: Individual lifelike tributes to each fallen soldier.
  - **Eternal Flame (*Amar Jawan Jyoti*)**: Positioned beneath the National Flag at the central axis.
  - **Digital Gallery & Museum**: Showcases the history of Ladakh's defense campaigns, wartime artifacts, and interactive displays.
