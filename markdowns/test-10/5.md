# 📌 16 Mahajanapadas - Kuru, Vatsa, Kosala & Chedi

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Primary Historical Sources**: The 16 Mahajanapadas of the 6th century BCE are listed in the Buddhist text ***Anguttara Nikaya*** and the Jain text ***Bhagavati Sutra***.
- **Kuru**: Located around modern **Delhi, Meerut, and Thanesar**; capital was **Indraprastha**; epicenter of the *Mahabharata* war.
- **Vatsa**: Located around modern **Prayagraj (Allahabad)**; capital was **Kaushambi** (on the banks of the Yamuna); famous for its fine cotton textiles and its popular ruler **King Udayana**.
- **Kosala**: Located in modern **Awadh (Eastern UP)**; bifurcated by the **Saryu River** into Northern Kosala (capital: **Shravasti**) and Southern Kosala (capital: **Saket / Ayodhya**); famous ruler was **King Prasenjit** (contemporary of Gautama Buddha).
- **Chedi**: Located in the **Bundelkhand** region; capital was **Suktimati (Sothivati)**; ruled by King **Shishupala** as mentioned in the epic *Mahabharata*.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Detailed Profile of the Four Mahajanapadas

*Geography, capitals, prominent kings, and economic significance.*

- **Kuru Mahajanapada**:
  - **Location**: Extended across modern Meerut, Delhi, and Thanesar (Haryana).
  - **Capitals**: **Indraprastha** (modern-day Delhi) and **Hastinapur**.
  - **Literary Context**: Central setting of the epic *Mahabharata*, chronicling the conflict between the Kauravas and Pandavas (two branches of the Kuru clan).
- **Vatsa Mahajanapada**:
  - **Location**: Situated around modern Prayagraj on the banks of the Yamuna River.
  - **Capital**: **Kaushambi** (located near the confluence of the Ganga and Yamuna).
  - **Famous King**: **King Udayana**, celebrated in Sanskrit plays such as Bhasa's *Svapnavasavadattam* and Harsha's *Ratnavali* and *Priyadarsika*.
  - **Economy**: Prominent center for trade, commerce, and **fine cotton textiles**.
- **Kosala Mahajanapada**:
  - **Location**: Modern Awadh region of Uttar Pradesh.
  - **Two Divisions**: The **Saryu River** divided the realm into two parts:
    - **Northern Kosala**: Capital at **Shravasti** (Sahet-Mahet).
    - **Southern Kosala**: Capital at **Saket** (Ayodhya).
    - Also included the tribal republican territory of the **Sakyas of Kapilavastu** (Buddha's birthplace).
  - **Famous King**: **Prasenjit** (*Pasenadi*), a close follower and contemporary of Gautama Buddha.
- **Chedi Mahajanapada**:
  - **Location**: Situated in the modern **Bundelkhand** area (border of UP and MP).
  - **Capital**: **Suktimati** / Sotthivatinagara (near modern Banda, UP).
  - **Epic Reference**: King **Shishupala** was killed by Lord Krishna at the Rajasuya sacrifice.
