# 📌 Uniform Circular Motion & Direction of Acceleration

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Direction perpendicular to velocity** (acts along the radius toward the center).
- **What is Uniform Circular Motion (UCM)?**: When an object moves in a circle at a **constant speed**.
- **Velocity vs Speed**:
  - **Speed** is fixed (scalar value).
  - **Velocity** changes at every point because the **direction of motion keeps turning**.
- **Direction of Velocity**: Always along the **tangent** to the circle.
- **Direction of Acceleration (Centripetal Acceleration)**: Always points **inwards along the radius toward the center**.
- **Angle between Velocity and Acceleration**: Since radius and tangent meet at a right angle, they are **perpendicular ($90^\circ$)** to each other.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Understanding the Directions in Circular Motion

- **Velocity ($\vec{v}$)**:
  - The instantaneous path the body wants to fly off along if released.
  - It always points **tangentially** along the circular edge.
- **Centripetal Acceleration ($\vec{a}_c$)**:
  - Because velocity keeps changing direction, an inward pull/acceleration is required to keep the body on the curved path.
  - This acceleration points **straight toward the center** (radially inward).
- **Perpendicular Relationship**:
  - The tangent (velocity) and the radius (acceleration) are always at **$90^\circ$**.
  - Therefore, the acceleration is strictly **perpendicular to the velocity**.

---

### 2. What Changes and What Stays Constant in UCM?

- **Things that stay CONSTANT**:
  - **Speed** (magnitude of velocity)
  - **Kinetic Energy** ($KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$)
  - **Magnitude** of Centripetal Acceleration ($a_c = \frac{v^2}{r}$)
  - **Angular Velocity** ($\omega$)
- **Things that CHANGE at every moment** (because direction changes):
  - **Linear Velocity**
  - **Linear Momentum**
  - **Direction of Acceleration**
  - **Direction of Centripetal Force**

| **Speed** | None (Scalar) | **Constant** |

| **Acceleration** | **Towards the center ($\perp$ to velocity)** | **Variable** (Direction changes) |

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

The direction of acceleration in uniform circular motion is along the:

- [ ] direction of motion
- [ ] tangent to the circle at the point of observation
- [ ] direction of velocity
- [x] direction perpendicular to velocity

> Acceleration acts toward the center of the circle, which is perpendicular to the tangent (direction of velocity).

---

### PYQ 2

When a body moves in a circular path with uniform speed, the work done on it by the centripetal force is:

- [ ] Positive
- [ ] Negative
- [x] Zero
- [ ] Infinite

> Since centripetal force and displacement are perpendicular to each other ($90^\circ$), work done is zero ($W = F \cdot d \cdot \cos 90^\circ = 0$).

---

### PYQ 3

Which one of the following quantities remains constant when an object performs uniform circular motion?

- [ ] Linear velocity
- [ ] Momentum
- [ ] Acceleration vector
- [x] Kinetic Energy

> Kinetic energy is a scalar quantity depending only on speed and mass, both of which are constant in uniform circular motion.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

What is the angle between the velocity vector and the acceleration vector in uniform circular motion?

- [ ] $0^\circ$
- [ ] $45^\circ$
- [x] $90^\circ$
- [ ] $180^\circ$

> The velocity is tangential and the acceleration is radial, making the angle between them exactly $90^\circ$.

---

### Q2

What is the formula for the centripetal acceleration ($a_c$) of an object moving with speed $v$ in a circle of radius $r$?

- [ ] $\frac{v}{r}$
- [x] $\frac{v^2}{r}$
- [ ] $v \cdot r$
- [ ] $\frac{r}{v^2}$

> The magnitude of centripetal acceleration is $a_c = \frac{v^2}{r}$.

---

### Q3

If a stone tied to a string is whirled in a horizontal circle and the string suddenly breaks, the stone will fly off in which direction?

- [ ] Towards the center of the circle
- [ ] Radially outwards
- [x] Tangentially to the circular path
- [ ] Vertically downwards instantly

> Once the inward centripetal force is removed, the stone continues in the direction of its instantaneous velocity, which is tangential.

---

### Q4

Why is uniform circular motion called an 'accelerated motion' even though the speed is constant?

- [ ] Because the radius keeps changing
- [x] Because the direction of velocity changes continuously
- [ ] Because mass decreases over time
- [ ] Because friction is zero

> Any change in the direction of velocity creates an acceleration, making circular motion always an accelerated motion.
