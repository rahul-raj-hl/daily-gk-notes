# 📌 Paika Rebellion (1817) & Peasant-Tribal Uprisings

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Leader**: **Bakshi Jagabandhu** (full name: *Bakshi Jagabandhu Bidyadhara Mohapatra Bhramarabar Rai*).
- **Year & Region**: Broke out in **1817** in **Khurda, Odisha**.
- **Role of Bakshi Jagabandhu**: He was the hereditary **military commander (*Bakshi*)** of the King of Khurda (Raja Mukunda Deva II).
- **Who Were the Paikas?**: The traditional **peasant-militia of Odisha** who held rent-free lands (*Nish-kar Jagirs*) in exchange for hereditary military policing services.
- **Suppression**: The rebellion spread across Banapur, Puri, Cuttack, and Sambalpur before being crushed by British forces by **1818**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Causes of the Paika Rebellion (1817)

*The socio-economic and political fallout of the British takeover of Odisha (1803):*

- **Resumption of Rent-Free Lands**: The East India Company confiscated the hereditary rent-free agricultural lands (*Paikali Jagirs*) of the Paikas following the dethronement of the Raja of Khurda.
- **Extortionate Revenue Settlement**: Imposition of heavy land revenue taxes collected ruthlessly in silver currency rather than traditional cowrie shells (*kaudi*).
- **Monopoly on Salt**: British monopoly on salt manufacture and trade led to an artificial scarcity and an exorbitant increase in salt prices along coastal Odisha.
- **Dispossession of Bakshi Jagabandhu**: British officials fraudulently dispossessed Bakshi Jagabandhu of his ancestral estate of *Killa Rorang*, rendering him destitute.

---

### 2. Major Tribal & Peasant Leaders in the Options

*High-yield early uprisings against British colonial rule:*

- **Sidhu and Kanhu Murmu**:
  - **Revolt**: **Santhal Hool / Santhal Rebellion (1855–56)** in the *Damin-i-Koh* region (modern-day Jharkhand & West Bengal) against oppressive moneylenders (*Mahajans*), zamindars, and British revenue police.
- **Baba Ram Singh**:
  - **Revolt**: **Kuka Movement / Namdhari Movement (1871–72)** in Punjab; transformed from a religious reform movement into a political campaign advocating non-cooperation and boycott of British goods.
- **Birsa Munda**:
  - **Revolt**: **Munda Rebellion / *Ulgulan* (The Great Tumult, 1899–1900)** in the Chota Nagpur plateau against the erosion of the traditional joint landholding system (*Khuntkatti*), missionaries, and *Dikus* (outsiders).
- **Bakshi Jagabandhu**:
  - **Revolt**: **Paika Rebellion (1817)** in Odisha.
