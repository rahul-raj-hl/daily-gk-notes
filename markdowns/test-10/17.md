# 📌 Official Tournament Ambassador for ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Rohit Sharma**.
- **Appointed Role**: Former Indian captain **Rohit Sharma** has been appointed as the **Official Tournament Ambassador** for the **ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026**.
- **Host Nations**: The 2026 tournament is jointly hosted by **India and Sri Lanka**.
- **Two-Time Champion**: Rohit Sharma is a two-time T20 World Cup champion (won as a player in **2007** and as captain in **2024**).
- **T20 World Cup Record**: He is the only player to have participated in **all 9 editions** of the ICC Men's T20 World Cup from 2007 to 2024.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Rohit Sharma's T20 World Cup Achievements

*Key milestones of the 2026 Tournament Ambassador:*

- **2007 Inaugural Edition**:
  - Made his T20I debut during the tournament.
  - Scored crucial unbeaten knocks—$50^*$ against South Africa in the Super Eights and $30^*$ off 16 balls in the final against Pakistan.
- **2024 Championship Edition**:
  - Captained India to win their second Men's T20 World Cup title, ending an 11-year ICC trophy drought.
  - Finished as India's highest run-scorer in the tournament with **257 runs** (strike rate: 156.70).
  - Notable knocks included **92 off 41 balls** vs Australia and **57 off 39 balls** vs England in the semi-final.
- **Career T20I Record**:
  - Scored **4,231 runs** in T20Is at an average of 32.01 with a strike rate of 140.89.
  - Retired from T20 Internationals following the 2024 World Cup victory.

---

### 2. ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026 Overview

*Key details about the 10th edition of the tournament:*

- **Edition**: 10th edition of the ICC Men's T20 World Cup.
- **Hosts**: India and Sri Lanka.
- **Format**: 20 participating national teams.
