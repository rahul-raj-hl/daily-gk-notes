# 📌 Dharmendra - Cinematic Legacy & Padma Bhushan Award (2012)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **2012**.
- **Padma Bhushan**: Veteran Bollywood icon Dharmendra was conferred with the **Padma Bhushan** (India's third-highest civilian award) in **2012** for his distinguished contribution to Indian Cinema.
- **Popular Nickname**: Widely celebrated as Bollywood's **"He-Man"** and one of Hindi cinema's most handsome leading stars.
- **Career Span**: Over six decades (60+ years) with appearances in more than 300 films.
- **Film Debut**: Made his acting debut in **1960** with the movie *"Dil Bhi Tera Hum Bhi Tere"*.
- **Iconic Roles**: Renowned for classic films including *Sholay* (as Veeru), *Satyakam*, *Anupama*, *Chupke Chupke*, *Dharam Veer*, and *Phool Aur Patthar*.
- **Final On-Screen Appearance**: Featured in the biographical war drama *"Ikkis"*.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Life & Cinematic Contributions of Dharmendra

- **Early Journey**:
  - Born as Dharmendra Singh Deol in Sahnewal, Punjab in 1935.
  - Won the Filmfare New Talent Contest and moved to Mumbai to pursue acting.
- **Versatility Across Genres**:
  - **Action & Macho Roles**: Established the archetype of the romantic-action hero (*Phool Aur Patthar*, *Mera Gaon Mera Desh*, *Dharam Veer*).
  - **Sensibility & Drama**: Delivered critically acclaimed artistic performances in Hrishikesh Mukherjee's films like *Satyakam* and *Anupama*.
  - **Comedy**: Displayed impeccable comic timing in classics like *Chupke Chupke* and *Sholay*.
- **Political Career**:
  - Served as a Member of Parliament (Lok Sabha) representing the Bikaner constituency in Rajasthan from 2004 to 2009.

---

### 2. Major Honours & Recognition

- **Padma Bhushan (2012)**: India's 3rd highest civilian honor for his lifetime contribution to arts and cinema.
- **Filmfare Lifetime Achievement Award (1997)**: Honored for his lasting impact on Hindi cinema.
- **National Film Award**: Won as producer for *Ghayal* (1990) under the Best Popular Film Providing Wholesome Entertainment category.
