# 📌 Alauddin Khalji & Military Expeditions into Southern India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Alauddin Khalji**.
- **First Southern Expedition**: Alauddin Khalji oversaw the first Turkish/Sultanate expedition into southern India in **AD 1294**, during which they pillaged **Devagiri** (while he was still a commander/governor under his uncle).
- **Key Commander for Later Southern Campaigns**: **Malik Kafur**, Alauddin's trusted general, led extensive imperial campaigns deeper into the Deccan and South India (Warangal, Hoysalas, Pandyas).
- **Dynastic Foundation**: The Khalji dynasty was founded by **Jalaluddin Khalji** in **1290**, whom Alauddin subsequently slew in July 1296 to seize the throne.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Southern Expansion and Deccan Policy

*Strategic shift from northern conquests to southern plunder and suzerainty:*

- **Motivation**: Unlike northern territories which were directly annexed, Alauddin's southern campaigns aimed primarily at extracting immense wealth, gold, elephants, and annual tribute while leaving the local Hindu dynasties as tributary rulers.
- **Key Southern Kingdoms Targeted**:
  - **Devagiri** (Yadavas)
  - **Warangal** (Kakatiyas)
  - **Dwarasamudra** (Hoysalas)
  - **Madurai** (Pandyan Kingdom)

---

### 2. Major Rulers in the Options

*Comparative achievements of Delhi Sultanate monarchs:*

- **Ghiyasuddin Balban (1266–1287 AD)**:
  - Restored crown dignity, introduced strict court protocols like ***Sijda*** (prostration) and ***Paibos*** (kissing the monarch's feet), established a spy network (*Barids*), and created a separate police force (*Kotwali*).
- **Alauddin Khalji (1296–1316 AD)**:
  - First Sultan to launch expeditions into South India; instituted market control regulations (*Price Control*), permanent standing army, cash salaries, and *Daag* (branding horses)/- *Chehra* (descriptive roll of soldiers).
- **Muhammad bin Tughluq (MBT)**:
  - Noted for experimental administrative measures such as the **relocation of the capital from Delhi to Daulatabad (Devagiri)** and the introduction of **token currency**.
- **Shamsuddin Iltutmish (1210–1236 AD)**:
  - Regarded as the **effective founder of the Delhi Sultanate**, introduced the silver *Tanka* and copper *Jittal*, organized the **Iqta system**, and formed the elite group of 40 Turkish slaves (*Turkaan-e-Chahalgani*).
