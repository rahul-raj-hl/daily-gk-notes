# 📌 Central Council of Ministers & Constitutional Provisions

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Neither 1 nor 2** (Both statements are incorrect).
- **Statement 1 is Incorrect**: Under **Article 75(5)**, a person who is not a member of either House can be appointed as a minister, but they must become a member of either House of Parliament within **six consecutive months**—either by **election OR nomination** (to the Rajya Sabha). It is **not mandatory** to become an *elected* member exclusively.
- **Statement 2 is Incorrect**: The salaries and allowances of Union Ministers are **determined by Parliament by law** under **Article 75(6)** (*Salaries and Allowances of Ministers Act, 1952*). They are **NOT** listed in the **Second Schedule** of the Constitution.
- **Second Schedule**: Deals with emoluments, allowances, and privileges of the President, Governors, Speaker/Deputy Speaker of Lok Sabha, Chairman/Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha, Supreme Court/High Court Judges, and the Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG).

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Constitutional Provisions Governing the Council of Ministers

*Key articles defining the executive authority of the Union:*

- **Article 74(1)**: Mandates a **Council of Ministers with the Prime Minister at the head** to aid and advise the President, who shall, in the exercise of their functions, act in accordance with such advice (42nd and 44th Constitutional Amendment Acts).
- **Article 75(1)**: The Prime Minister is appointed by the **President**, and other Ministers are appointed by the President on the **advice of the Prime Minister**.
- **Article 75(1A)**: The total number of ministers, including the Prime Minister, in the Council of Ministers shall not exceed **15%** of the total strength of the Lok Sabha (*inserted by the 91st Amendment Act, 2003*).
- **Article 75(2)**: Ministers hold office during the **pleasure of the President** (*Individual Responsibility*).
- **Article 75(3)**: The Council of Ministers is **collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha** (*Collective Responsibility*).
- **Article 75(5)**: A minister who for any period of six consecutive months is not a member of either House of Parliament shall at the expiration of that period cease to be a minister.
- **Article 75(6)**: The salaries and allowances of ministers shall be such as **Parliament may from time to time by law determine**.

---

### 2. Dignitaries Covered Under the Second Schedule

*Who is included versus who is excluded:*

- **Included in the Second Schedule**:
  - The President of India & Governors of States.
  - Speaker and Deputy Speaker of the Lok Sabha.
  - Chairman and Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
  - Speaker and Deputy Speaker of State Legislative Assemblies.
  - Chairman and Deputy Chairman of State Legislative Councils.
  - Judges of the Supreme Court and High Courts.
  - Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG).
- **EXCLUDED from the Second Schedule**:
  - **Prime Minister and Union Ministers** (Determined under Article 75(6)).
  - **Chief Ministers and State Ministers** (Determined under Article 164(5)).
  - **Members of Parliament (MPs)** (Determined under Article 106).
  - **Attorney General of India** (Determined by the President under Article 76(6)).

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Constitutional Aspect | Individual Responsibility | Collective Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Article** | **Article 75(2)** | **Article 75(3)** |
| **Responsible To** | **President of India** | **Lok Sabha** (House of the People) |
| **Legal Meaning** | Minister can be dismissed anytime by President on PM's advice | Entire ministry falls if a No-Confidence Motion is passed |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "MINISTERS SWIM & SINK TOGETHER IN LOK SABHA"**
> - **Swim & Sink Together** $\rightarrow$ Collective Responsibility (**Article 75(3)** to Lok Sabha).
> - **6 Months Grace** $\rightarrow$ Can be **Elected OR Nominated** to either House.
> - **No 2nd Schedule** $\rightarrow$ Parliament fixes their pay, not the Constitution.

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Consider the following statements with reference to the Central Council of Ministers:
1. A nominated member can be appointed as a minister, but within six months he must become an elected member of either House of Parliament.
2. The salaries and allowances of ministers are given in the Second Schedule of the Indian Constitution.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- [ ] 1 only
- [ ] 2 only
- [ ] Both 1 and 2
- [x] Neither 1 nor 2

> Statement 1 is false because membership can be obtained via election or nomination; Statement 2 is false because minister salaries are determined by Parliament by law, not in the Second Schedule.

---

### PYQ 2

Under Article 75(3) of the Indian Constitution, the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to:

- [ ] The President of India
- [ ] The Prime Minister
- [x] The Lok Sabha (House of the People)
- [ ] Both Houses of Parliament

> The Council of Ministers is collectively responsible only to the directly elected popular house, the Lok Sabha.

---

### PYQ 3

Which Constitutional Amendment Act capped the total number of ministers, including the Prime Minister, at 15% of the total strength of the Lok Sabha?

- [ ] 42nd Amendment Act, 1976
- [ ] 44th Amendment Act, 1978
- [ ] 86th Amendment Act, 2002
- [x] 91st Amendment Act, 2003

> The 91st Amendment Act of 2003 introduced Article 75(1A), restricting the Council of Ministers to 15% of the Lok Sabha's strength.

- [ ] President of India
- [ ] Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG)
- [ ] Judges of the Supreme Court
- [x] Prime Minister and Union Ministers

> The salaries of the Prime Minister and Union Ministers are regulated by parliamentary enactments under Article 75(6), not the Second Schedule.

### Q2

If a person who is not a member of either House of Parliament is appointed as the Prime Minister, within what timeframe must they secure membership in either the Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha?

- [ ] 3 months
- [x] 6 months
- [ ] 9 months
- [ ] 1 year

> Under Article 75(5), any minister (including the PM) must obtain membership of either House within 6 consecutive months.

---

### Q3

Under which Article of the Constitution does a Union Minister hold office during the pleasure of the President?

- [ ] Article 74(1)
- [ ] Article 75(1)
- [x] Article 75(2)
- [ ] Article 75(3)

> Article 75(2) outlines the doctrine of pleasure for individual ministers.

### Q4

Can a nominated member of the Rajya Sabha be appointed as a Union Minister?

- [ ] No, only directly elected Lok Sabha members can become Ministers
