# 📌 Arya Samaj & Swami Dayanand Saraswati

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Founding Year & Place**: Founded on **April 7, 1875**, in **Bombay** by **Swami Dayanand Saraswati** (original name: *Mool Shankar*).
- **Core Philosophy**: A **monotheistic Hindu reform movement** upholding the conviction that the **Vedas are infallible** and contain pure, uncorrupted knowledge.
- **Iconic Slogan**: *"Go Back to the Vedas"* (*Vedo ki or lauto*).
- **Magnum Opus**: Authored the seminal book ***Satyarth Prakash*** (The Light of Truth) in Hindi in 1875.
- **Headquarters Shift**: A second founding/headquarters was established at **Lahore in 1877**, transforming it into a widespread socio-cultural movement across Punjab and North India.
- **Key Reforms**: Introduced the **Shuddhi Movement** (reconversion / proselytization), founded **Gurukuls / DAV Schools**, and championed the abolition of child marriage and untouchability.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Tenets & Principles of Arya Samaj

*Revivalist yet progressive social reforms rooted in Vedic authority:*

- **Infallibility of Vedas**: Rejected post-Vedic texts like the Puranas as corrupted interpolations, advocating pure monotheism (*Ekeshwarism*).
- **Opposition to Dogmas**: Vehemently condemned **idol worship, polytheism, ritualistic priesthood, animal sacrifices, and caste distinctions based on birth**.
- **Vedic Education**:
  - Founded Gurukuls (the first Vedic school was founded at **Farrukhabad in 1869**) to promote ancient Vedic values alongside contemporary knowledge, creating an alternative to British Macaulay-style education.
- **Shuddhi Movement**:
  - First organized socio-religious campaign to reconvert individuals who had converted from Hinduism to other religions (Islam or Christianity).
- **First Call for Swaraj**:
  - Swami Dayanand Saraswati was among the earliest thinkers to declare: *"Good government is no substitute for self-government"* and gave the first call for **"India for Indians" (Swaraj)** in 1876.

---

### 2. The Split in Arya Samaj (1893)

*Divergence over educational policy and diet:*

- **College Party (Cultured / Moderate Faction)**:
  - **Leaders**: Lala Hansraj, Lala Lajpat Rai.
  - **Philosophy**: Favored English-medium modern education alongside Vedic studies; non-vegetarian diet was permitted.
  - **Institution**: Established the first **Dayanand Anglo-Vedic (DAV) College** at Lahore in **1886**.
- **Gurukul Party (Mahatma / Orthodox Faction)**:
  - **Leaders**: Swami Shraddhanand (Munshi Ram), Lekh Ram.
  - **Philosophy**: Advocated strict adherence to ancient Gurukul tradition, Sanskrit-medium instruction, and strict vegetarianism.
  - **Institution**: Founded **Gurukul Kangri** at Haridwar in **1902**.
