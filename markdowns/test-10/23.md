# 📌 Ramakrishna Mission (1897) & Swami Vivekananda

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Both 1 and 2**.
- **Founding Date & Founder**: Founded on **May 1, 1897**, by **Swami Vivekananda** (original name: *Narendranath Datta*).
- **Core Mission & Ideology**: Established to spread the universal teachings of **Vedanta** as exemplified in the life and spiritual realizations of his guru, **Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa** (Gadadhar Chattopadhyay).
- **Dual Organs**: 
  - **Ramakrishna Math**: The monastic brotherhood dedicated to spiritual practice and training.
  - **Ramakrishna Mission**: The philanthropic and educational wing dedicated to humanitarian service (*"Service to mankind is service to God"* / *Shiva Jnane Jiva Seva*).
- **Headquarters**: **Belur Math**, located on the banks of the Hooghly (Ganga) River in Howrah, near Kolkata, West Bengal.
- **Global Presence**: The **Vedanta Society of New York**, founded in 1894 and incorporated in 1898, is the oldest branch of the Ramakrishna Order in the United States.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Life & Philosophy of Swami Vivekananda

*Synthesizing Indian spirituality with modern humanitarian action:*

- **Early Life**:
  - Born on **January 12, 1863** in Calcutta (celebrated nationwide as **National Youth Day**).
  - Disciple of the mystic saint of Dakshineswar, Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa.
- **Neo-Vedanta / Practical Vedanta**:
  - Emphasized that true spirituality lies in serving the poor, hungry, and marginalized (*Daridra Narayana*).
  - Advocated a balance between Western scientific/material progress and Eastern spiritual wisdom.
- **World's Parliament of Religions (1893)**:
  - Addressed the historic Parliament in **Chicago** on **September 11, 1893**, famously opening with *"Sisters and Brothers of America!"*.
  - Introduced Vedanta and Hinduism to the Western world as a universal faith of tolerance and acceptance.
- **Patriot-Saint of Modern India**:
  - Subhash Chandra Bose described him as the *"Maker of Modern India"*.
  - Passed away at the age of 39 on **July 4, 1902** at Belur Math.

---

### 2. Major Socio-Religious Movements Comparison

*Quick chronological overview of key 19th-century organizations:*

- **Brahmo Samaj (1828)**: Founded by Raja Ram Mohan Roy in Calcutta (Monotheism & Anti-Sati).
- **Prarthana Samaj (1867)**: Founded by Atmaram Pandurang in Bombay (Social reforms & Bhakti tradition).
- **Satyashodhak Samaj (1873)**: Founded by Jyotirao Phule in Pune (Anti-caste movement & education for Dalits/women).
- **Arya Samaj (1875)**: Founded by Swami Dayanand Saraswati in Bombay (Vedic revivalism & Shuddhi movement).
- **Ramakrishna Mission (1897)**: Founded by Swami Vivekananda near Calcutta (Practical Vedanta & humanitarian service).
