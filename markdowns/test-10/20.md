# 📌 Chronology of Dynasties in Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526 AD)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Slave Dynasty – Khilji Dynasty – Tughlaq Dynasty – Lodi Dynasty**.
- **Complete Chronological Order (All 5 Dynasties)**:
  1. **Slave (Mamluk) Dynasty** (1206–1290 AD)
  2. **Khilji Dynasty** (1290–1320 AD)
  3. **Tughlaq Dynasty** (1320–1414 AD)
  4. **Sayyid Dynasty** (1414–1451 AD)
  5. **Lodi Dynasty** (1451–1526 AD)
- **First Dynasty**: Slave / Mamluk Dynasty (founded by **Qutb-ud-din Aibak**).
- **Last Dynasty**: Lodi Dynasty (founded by **Bahlul Lodi**; ended with the defeat of **Ibrahim Lodi** by Babur in the First Battle of Panipat, 1526).
- **Longest Ruling Dynasty**: **Tughlaq Dynasty** (~94 years).
- **Shortest Ruling Dynasty**: **Khilji Dynasty** (~30 years).

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Five Dynasties of Delhi Sultanate

*Detailed timeline, prominent rulers, and contributions of each dynasty:*

- **1. Slave (Mamluk / Ilbari) Dynasty (1206–1290 AD)**:
  - **Founder**: **Qutb-ud-din Aibak** (known as *Lakh Baksh* or "giver of lakhs").
  - **Prominent Rulers**: Shamsuddin Iltutmish (real consolidator, introduced *Iqta* system), Razia Sultana (first and only female Muslim monarch of Delhi), and Ghiyasuddin Balban (introduced *Sijda* and *Paibos*).
  - **Capital**: Lahore (under Aibak), later shifted to Delhi (by Iltutmish).
- **2. Khilji Dynasty (1290–1320 AD)**:
  - **Founder**: **Jalaluddin Khalji**.
  - **Greatest Ruler**: **Alauddin Khalji** (market/price control regulations, permanent standing army, *Daag* and *Chehra* systems, and first military expeditions to South India).
  - **Significance**: Marked the zenith of Sultanate military imperialism in northern and southern India.
- **3. Tughlaq Dynasty (1320–1414 AD)**:
  - **Founder**: **Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq** (Ghazi Malik), who founded the city and fort of Tughlaqabad.
  - **Prominent Rulers**: 
    - **Muhammad bin Tughluq** (known for ambitious experiments: shifting capital to Daulatabad, token copper currency, and creation of agriculture department *Diwan-i-Amir-Kohi*).
    - **Firoz Shah Tughlaq** (built extensive canal irrigation networks, founded cities like Firozabad and Hisar, established *Diwan-i-Khairat* and *Diwan-i-Bandagan*).
  - **Major Turning Point**: Invasion of **Timur (Taimur)** in **1398 AD** dealt a fatal blow to the dynasty.
- **4. Sayyid Dynasty (1414–1451 AD)**:
  - **Founder**: **Khizr Khan** (appointed as Governor of Multan by Timur).
  - **Significance**: A transitional and weak dynasty with limited territorial control centered around the Delhi region.
  - **Last Ruler**: Alauddin Alam Shah voluntarily abdicated and surrendered the throne to Bahlul Lodi.
- **5. Lodi Dynasty (1451–1526 AD)**:
  - **Founder**: **Bahlul Lodi** (first Afghan / Pashtun dynasty to rule Delhi).
  - **Prominent Rulers**: 
    - **Sikandar Lodi** (founded the city of **Agra** in 1504 and introduced the land measurement yard *Gaz-i-Sikandari*).
    - **Ibrahim Lodi** (defeated by Babur in the **First Battle of Panipat on 21 April 1526**, ending the Delhi Sultanate and establishing the Mughal Empire).
