# 📌 Lord Canning & The Revolt of 1857 (Sepoy Mutiny)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Governor-General during 1857 Revolt**: **Lord Canning** (served as Governor-General from **1856 to 1858** and subsequently as the **first Viceroy of India from 1858 to 1862**).
- **Dual Distinction**: He was the **last Governor-General of the East India Company** and the **first Viceroy under the British Crown**.
- **Queen’s Proclamation & GOI Act 1858**: Read Queen Victoria’s Proclamation at the **Allahabad Durbar on 1 November 1858**, transferring Indian governance from the Company to the British Crown.
- **Key Legislative Milestones**:
  - **Hindu Widows' Remarriage Act (1856)**: Drafted under Lord Dalhousie, passed by Canning.
  - **General Service Enlistment Act (1856)**: Made it mandatory for recruits to serve overseas if ordered.
  - **Establishment of Universities (1857)**: Founded the first three modern universities at **Calcutta, Bombay, and Madras** based on Wood’s Despatch (1854).
  - **Indian Penal Code (IPC, 1860)** and **Indian High Courts Act (1861)**.
  - **Indian Councils Act (1861)**: Introduced the **Portfolio System** and executive council decentralization.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Lord Canning's Tenure (1856–1862)

*Navigating the mutiny, Crown takeover, and institutional reforms.*

- **Outbreak of the Revolt (1857)**:
  - Triggered by military grievances, economic exploitation, and the immediate spark of greased cartridges in the Enfield rifle.
  - Suppressed through British military commanders (Colin Campbell, Hugh Rose, John Nicholson, James Outram).
- **Administrative Transition (1858)**:
  - Abolished the Board of Control and Court of Directors; created the post of **Secretary of State for India** assisted by a 15-member Council of India.
  - **Withdrawal of the Doctrine of Lapse**: Reassured Indian princely states that their adoption rights would be honored and no further annexation would take place.
- **Introduction of the Portfolio System (1861)**:
  - Under the *Indian Councils Act, 1861*, members of the Governor-General’s Executive Council were assigned specific government departments (foundations of the modern Cabinet system).
- **Financial Reforms**:
  - Appointed **James Wilson** as India's first Finance Member, who introduced India’s **first Paper Currency Act** and the **first Income Tax system (1860)**.

---

### 2. Viceroys & Governors-General in the Options

*Chronological placement and distinctive historical associations.*

- **Lord Hardinge I (1844–1848)**: First Anglo-Sikh War (1845–46), Treaty of Lahore (1846), and prohibition of female infanticide/human sacrifice (*Meriah* among Khonds).
- **Lord Dalhousie (1848–1856)**: Annexed states via the **Doctrine of Lapse** (Satara, Sambalpur, Jhansi, Nagpur); annexed Awadh on grounds of misgovernance (1856); introduced the first passenger railway (1853), electric telegraph, and postal reforms.
- **Lord Lytton (1876–1880)**: Infamous for the **Vernacular Press Act (1878)**, Arms Act (1878), Royal Titles Act (1876 - *Kaiser-i-Hind* to Queen Victoria), and the Great Famine of 1876–78.
