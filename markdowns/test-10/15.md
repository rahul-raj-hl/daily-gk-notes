# 📌 Chalukya Dynasty Capitals & Architecture (Aihole & Badami)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Aihole** (Served as the early capital and mercantile hub of the Early Chalukyas before Pulakeshin I shifted the royal seat to **Badami / Vatapi**).
- **Cradle of Indian Temple Architecture**: **Aihole** (located in the Bagalkot district of Karnataka along the Malaprabha River) is known as the *"Cradle of Indian Temple Architecture"* due to over 100 stone and cave temples showcasing architectural experimentation.
- **Aihole Inscription (634 CE)**: Composed in Sanskrit by the Jain poet **Ravikirti**, glorifying the achievements of **Pulakeshin II** and his victory over Emperor **Harshavardhana** on the banks of the Narmada River.
- **Key Temples at Aihole**: **Durga Temple** (apsidal plan), **Lad Khan Temple**, **Huchappayyagudi Temple**, and the **Meguti Jain Temple**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Evolution of Chalukyan Capitals

*From early experimental center to fortified royal seat:*

- **Aihole (Aryapura)**:
  - Acted as the **first capital** and chief commercial trading center of the early Chalukya rulers.
  - Home to the famous merchant guild **Ayyavole 500** (*Ainurruvar*), which controlled extensive maritime and inland trade networks across South and Southeast Asia.
- **Badami (Vatapi)**:
  - Founded and fortified by **Pulakeshin I** around **543 CE**, shifting the political capital from Aihole to the rugged sandstone hills of Vatapi.
  - Renowned for its four magnificent rock-cut cave temples (dedicated to Shiva, Vishnu, and Jain Tirthankaras).
- **Pattadakal (Raktapura)**:
  - Located close to Aihole and Badami; served as the **ceremonial coronation center** (*Pattam* = Crown) and reached the zenith of Chalukyan art.
  - Declared a **UNESCO World Heritage Site** (features the famous **Virupaksha Temple** built by Queen Lokamahadevi).

---

### 2. Historical Associations of the Options

*Origins and dynastic links of ancient cities:*

- **Madurai (Tamil Nadu)**:
  - Historic capital of the **Pandya Dynasty**; located on the banks of the Vaigai River; celebrated host of the ancient **Tamil Sangams** and site of the Meenakshi Sundareswarar Temple.
- **Amaravati (Andhra Pradesh)**:
  - Situated on the banks of the Krishna River; prominent Mahayana Buddhist art center and secondary/eastern capital of the **Satavahana Dynasty** (also known as *Dhanyakataka*).
- **Bharuch (Bhrigukachha / Barygaza, Gujarat)**:
  - Ancient seaport and maritime trade emporium at the mouth of the Narmada River; prominently documented in the Greco-Roman text *Periplus of the Erythraean Sea*.
- **Aihole (Karnataka)**:
  - Early capital of the **Chalukyas of Badami**.
