# 📌 Tiger Mortality Data 2025 & NTCA Conservation Statistics

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Madhya Pradesh**.
- **Highest Tiger Deaths in 2025**: **Madhya Pradesh** recorded the highest number of tiger deaths in India, with **55 fatalities**.
- **Total National Tiger Deaths**: India recorded a total of **166 tiger deaths in 2025** (including 31 cubs), an increase of 40 compared to 126 in 2024.
- **State-wise Tally in 2025**:
  - **1st**: Madhya Pradesh — **55 deaths**
  - **2nd**: Maharashtra — **38 deaths**
  - **3rd**: Kerala — **13 deaths**
  - **4th**: Assam — **12 deaths**
- **Primary Cause**: Wildlife experts identified **territorial infighting due to limited space/habitat saturation** as a leading cause of mortality.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Madhya Pradesh – The "Tiger State of India"

*Population growth, density, and conservation pressures:*

- **Top Tiger Population**: Madhya Pradesh holds the largest tiger population in India, with **785 tigers** according to the 2022 All-India Tiger Estimation report.
- **Unprecedented Growth**: The state's tiger numbers rose steadily from **308 (2014)** to **526 (2018)** and reached **785 (2022)**, representing nearly a 60% surge.
- **Territorial Competition**: Higher density in core areas and fragmented corridors have intensified territorial clashes among male tigers and younger felines.
- **Prominent Tiger Reserves in MP**:
  - Bandhavgarh Tiger Reserve (Highest tiger density)
  - Kanha Tiger Reserve
  - Pench Tiger Reserve
  - Panna Tiger Reserve
  - Satpura Tiger Reserve
  - Sanjay-Dubri Tiger Reserve
  - Veerangana Durgavati Tiger Reserve (7th in MP)

---

### 2. National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA)

*Mandate, legal basis, and standard operating procedures:*

- **Statutory Status**: Established in **December 2005** following recommendations of the Tiger Task Force, under the **Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972** (as amended in 2006).
- **Parent Ministry**: Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC).
- **Core Functions**:
  - Oversees and monitors the implementation of **Project Tiger** (launched in **1973** at Jim Corbett National Park).
  - Coordinates the All-India Tiger Estimation every **4 years**.
  - Tracks tiger mortality and poaching cases across the country.
- **Investigation Rule**: The NTCA's standard operating procedure mandates treating every tiger death as a **case of poaching until proven otherwise**.
