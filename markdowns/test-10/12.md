# 📌 Human Diseases - Bacterial Diseases vs Non-Bacterial Disorders

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Asthma** (It is a non-infectious, chronic inflammatory respiratory condition/allergic disorder, NOT a bacterial disease).
- **Typhoid**: Caused by the bacterium ***Salmonella typhi*** (confirmed by the **Widal Test**).
- **Plague**: Caused by the bacterium ***Yersinia pestis*** (transmitted via rat fleas, *Xenopsylla cheopis*).
- **Diphtheria**: Caused by the bacterium ***Corynebacterium diphtheriae*** (prevented via the **DPT / Pentavalent vaccine**; diagnosed by the **Schick Test**).
- **Asthma Characterization**: A chronic condition where airways narrow, swell, and produce extra mucus, leading to wheezing, coughing, chest tightness, and shortness of breath.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Classification & Mechanism of Asthma

*A non-communicable, hypersensitive inflammatory disorder.*

- **Mechanism**: Triggered by allergens (pollen, dust mites, pet dander), cold air, respiratory infections, or physical exertion, causing bronchospasm and swelling of the bronchial lining.
- **Key Symptoms**:
  - Wheezing (a high-pitched whistling sound during exhalation).
  - Shortness of breath (*dyspnea*) and chronic coughing (worse at night/early morning).
  - Chest tightness or pain.
- **Types of Asthma**:
  - **Allergic (Extrinsic) Asthma**: Triggered by environmental allergens.
  - **Non-allergic (Intrinsic) Asthma**: Triggered by stress, smoke, cold air, or viral infections.
  - **Occupational Asthma**: Triggered by workplace fumes, gases, or dust.

---

### 2. High-Yield Overview of Bacterial Pathogens in the Options

*Causative agents, modes of transmission, and diagnostic tests.*

- **Typhoid (Enteric Fever)**:
  - **Causative Agent**: *Salmonella typhi* (Gram-negative bacterium).
  - **Transmission**: Ingestion of contaminated food and water.
  - **Target Organ**: Small intestine (can cause intestinal perforation in severe cases).
  - **Diagnostic Test**: **Widal Test**.
- **Plague (Black Death)**:
  - **Causative Agent**: *Yersinia pestis* (formerly *Pasteurella pestis*).
  - **Transmission**: Vector-borne through infected rat fleas (*Xenopsylla cheopis*).
  - **Forms**: Bubonic (swollen lymph nodes/buboes), Septicemic, and Pneumonic (airborne).
- **Diphtheria**:
  - **Causative Agent**: *Corynebacterium diphtheriae*.
  - **Target**: Upper respiratory tract (forms a thick, grey pseudo-membrane over the throat/tonsils, causing suffocation).
  - **Diagnostic Test**: **Schick Test**.

## 📚 Related Previous Year Questions

### PYQ 1

Which of the following is not a bacterial disease?

- [ ] Typhoid
- [ ] Plague
- [ ] Diphtheria
- [x] Asthma

> Asthma is a chronic non-infectious inflammatory disorder of the airways, whereas Typhoid, Plague, and Diphtheria are bacterial infections.

---

### PYQ 2

The 'Widal Test' is widely employed for the clinical diagnosis of which of the following diseases?

- [ ] Tuberculosis
- [x] Typhoid
- [ ] Cholera
- [ ] Malaria

> The Widal test detects agglutinating antibodies against *Salmonella typhi* in suspected typhoid patients.

---

### PYQ 3

The DPT vaccine is administered to infants to provide immunization against which three infectious diseases?

- [ ] Diphtheria, Polio, Tetanus
- [x] Diphtheria, Pertussis (Whooping Cough), Tetanus
- [ ] Diarrhea, Plague, Typhoid
- [ ] Dengue, Polio, Tuberculosis

> DPT stands for Diphtheria, Pertussis, and Tetanus toxoid vaccine.
