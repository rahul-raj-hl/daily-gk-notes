# 📌 Vedic Rivers & Ancient Rigvedic Nomenclature (Sapta Sindhu)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Shutudri**.
- **Sutlej**: Vedic name is **Shutudri** (or *Shatadru*). It originates from **Lake Rakshastal** near Mount Kailash in Tibet (enters India through **Shipki La Pass**).
- **Ravi**: Vedic name is **Purushni** (or *Iravati*). Battle of Ten Kings (*Dasrajan War*) was fought on its banks.
- **Chenab**: Vedic name is **Asikni**. It originates from the confluence of Chandra and Bhaga near **Bara-lacha La Pass**.
- **Jhelum**: Vedic name is **Vitasta** (Greek: *Hydaspes*). It originates from **Verinag Spring** in Jammu & Kashmir.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Sapta Sindhu Region & Rigvedic Nadistuti Sukta

*Geography of the Early Vedic settlement described in the Rigveda:*

- **Nadistuti Sukta**: Found in the **10th Mandala** of the Rigveda, which praises the major rivers of the Indian subcontinent from east to west.
- **Sapta Sindhu ("Land of Seven Rivers")**: The primary geographical zone inhabited by Early Vedic Aryans, comprising:
  1. **Sindhu** (Indus) — Most frequently mentioned river.
  2. **Saraswati** — Most sacred/revered river (*Naditama*, *Devitama*, *Matetama*).
  3. **Vitasta** (Jhelum)
  4. **Asikni** (Chenab)
  5. **Purushni** (Ravi)
  6. **Vipasa** (Beas)
  7. **Shutudri** (Sutlej)

---

### 2. Modern vs. Vedic River Names & Origins

*Comprehensive mapping of Indus river system tributaries:*

- **Sutlej (Shutudri)**:
  - Originates from **Rakshastal**, flows through Himachal Pradesh and Punjab.
  - Site of the famous multipurpose **Bhakra Nangal Dam**.
- **Ravi (Purushni)**:
  - Originates near **Bara Bhangal** (Kangra district, Himachal Pradesh).
  - Epicenter of the Vedic *Battle of the Ten Kings* (King Sudas defeated a confederacy of ten tribes).
- **Chenab (Asikni)**:
  - Largest tributary of the Indus River by volume, formed by **Chandra** and **Bhaga** streams in Lahaul-Spiti.
- **Jhelum (Vitasta)**:
  - Flows through the Kashmir Valley and Wular Lake; site of the **Battle of Hydaspes** (326 BCE) between Alexander and King Porus.
- **Beas (Vipasa)**:
  - Originates from **Beas Kund** near Rohtang Pass; marks the easternmost limit of Alexander’s Indian invasion.
