# 📌 Rubber

> ✅ **Answer:** Rubber is an **equatorial crop**.

---

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Rubber grows best in a **hot, humid climate**.
- Requires **high temperature (>25°C)** and **heavy rainfall (>200 cm)**.
- Sensitive to **frost** and **dry weather**.

---

## 🔑 Key Points

### 🌱 Ideal Climate
*One-liner:* Rubber thrives in equatorial conditions.

- 🌡️ Temperature → Above **25°C**
- 🌧️ Rainfall → More than **200 cm**, well distributed
- 💧 Humidity → High throughout the year
- ❄️ Frost → Harmful to the crop

---

### 🇮🇳 Major Rubber Producing States
*One-liner:* Kerala is India's leading rubber producer.

- Kerala
- Tamil Nadu
- Karnataka
- Andaman & Nicobar Islands

---

### 🥛 Latex
*One-liner:* Natural rubber is obtained from latex.

- Latex is the milky sap extracted from the rubber tree.
- Yield is highest when rainfall is evenly distributed.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

- Rubber → **Equatorial crop**
