# 📌 Shri Narayana Guru

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Shri Narayana Guru was a renowned social reformer, philosopher, and spiritual leader from Kerala.
- He fought against caste discrimination and promoted social equality.
- His teachings emphasized unity, education, and social justice.
- Founder and inspiration behind the SNDP movement for the upliftment of backward communities.

---

## 🔑 Key Points

### 👤 Who was Shri Narayana Guru?

*One-liner:* A pioneer of Kerala's social reform movement.

- Born in **1856** at Chempazhanthy, Kerala.
- Social reformer, philosopher, and spiritual leader.
- Worked for the upliftment of oppressed communities.
- Opposed caste-based discrimination.

---

### 💬 Famous Slogan

> **"One Caste, One Religion, One God for Mankind."**

*(Malayalam: Oru Jati, Oru Matham, Oru Daivam Manushyanu)*

Meaning:

- Equality among all human beings.
- Universal brotherhood.
- Rejection of caste hierarchy.

---

### 🌟 Major Contributions

- Campaigned against untouchability.
- Promoted social equality and education.
- Established temples open to all communities.
- Inspired the formation of the **Sree Narayana Dharma Paripalana (SNDP) Yogam**.
