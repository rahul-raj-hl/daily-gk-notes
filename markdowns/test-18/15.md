# 📌 Census 2011: Bihar Demographics & Decadal Growth

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Madhepura** (Recorded the highest decadal population growth rate in Bihar during 2001–2011 at **$31.12\%$**).
- **Total Population of Bihar (2011)**: **$10,40,99,452$** (~10.41 crore), making Bihar the **3rd most populous state** in India (after Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra).
- **Decadal Growth Rate (2001–2011)**: **$25.42\%$** (a decrease from $28.43\%$ in the 1991–2001 decade).
- **Share of National Population**: Bihar accounts for **$8.60\%$** of India’s total population (up from $8.07\%$ in 2001).
- **Most Populous District**: **Patna** ($57,72,804$).
- **Least Populous District**: **Sheikhpura** ($6,36,342$).
