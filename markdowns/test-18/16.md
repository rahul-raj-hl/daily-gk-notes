# 📌 The Vijayanagara Empire: Key Rulers, Literature & Decline

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **All three** statements are correct.
- **Statement 1 (Correct)**: The Vijayanagara Empire was founded in **1336 CE** on the banks of the **Tungabhadra River** by brothers **Harihara I** and **Bukka Raya I** of the Sangama dynasty under the spiritual guidance of saint **Swami Vidyaranya**.
- **Statement 2 (Correct)**: Emperor **Krishnadevaraya** (reigned 1509–1529 CE), the most illustrious ruler of the **Tuluva dynasty**, authored the renowned epic poem on statecraft, ***Amuktamalyada***, in **Telugu**, and the play ***Jambavati Kalyanam*** in **Sanskrit**.
- **Statement 3 (Correct)**: The capital city of **Hampi (Vijayanagara)** was devastated, plundered, and sacked following the disastrous **Battle of Talikota** (also known as *Rakshasi-Tangadi*) on **23 January 1565**.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Chronology of the Four Dynasties of Vijayanagara

*The empire was ruled in succession by four distinct dynasties from 1336 to 1646 CE:*

| Dynasty | Period | Founder | Prominent Monarchs & Legacy |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Sangama Dynasty** | 1336–1485 CE | **Harihara I & Bukka I** | • **Deva Raya II (Gajabetekara)**: Greatest Sangama ruler.<br>• Visited by Persian envoy **Abdur Razzaq** (1443) and Italian traveler **Nicolo de Conti** (1420). |
| **Saluva Dynasty** | 1485–1505 CE | **Saluva Narasimha** | • Shortest-lived dynasty; restored administrative stability. |
| **Tuluva Dynasty** | 1505–1570 CE | **Vira Narasimha** | • **Krishnadevaraya (1509–1529)**: Golden age of literature and military expansion.<br>• **Achyuta Deva Raya**: Visited by Fernao Nuniz.<br>• **Sadashiva Raya**: Puppet king during the Battle of Talikota (power held by Rama Raya). |
| **Aravidu Dynasty** | 1570–1646 CE | **Tirumala Deva Raya** | • Shifted capital south to **Penukonda** and later to **Chandragiri** after Hampi was sacked. |

---

### 2. The Golden Reign of Krishnadevaraya (1509–1529 CE)

*Key cultural, literary, and architectural achievements:*

- **Literary Works**:
  - ***Amuktamalyada*** (Telugu): Masterpiece on statecraft and the devotion of Andal (an Alvar saint).
  - ***Jambavati Kalyanam*** & ***Ushaparinayam*** (Sanskrit).
  - Adopted titles like ***Abhinava Bhoja***, ***Andhra Bhoja***, and ***Yavanaraja Sthapanacharya***.
- **The Ashtadiggajas**: His royal court (*Bhuvana Vijayam*) was adorned by eight eminent Telugu literary scholars, led by **Allasani Peddana** (*Andhra Kavita Pitamaha*, author of *Manucharitam*) and **Tenali Ramakrishna** (*Panduranga Mahatmyam*).
- **Architecture**:
  - Built the **Vijaya Vittala Temple** (famous for its Stone Chariot and musical pillars), **Hazara Rama Temple**, and grand entrance gateways (*Rayagopurams*) across South Indian temples.
  - Founded the suburb town of **Nagalapuram** in memory of his mother Nagala Devi.
- **Foreign Visitors**: Portuguese travelers **Domingo Paes** and **Duarte Barbosa** visited his court and left detailed descriptions of his wealth and governance.

---

### 3. Battle of Talikota (1565 CE) & Fall of Hampi

- **Also Known As**: Battle of **Rakshasi-Tangadi** / Battle of **Bannihatti** (fought on 23 January 1565).
- **The Contenders**:
  - **Vijayanagara Army**: Commanded de facto by the chief minister **Aliya Rama Raya**.
  - **Combined Deccan Sultanates**: Alliance of **Bijapur, Golconda, Ahmadnagar, and Bidar** (Berar did not participate).
- **Aftermath**: Rama Raya was captured and executed. The grand imperial capital of Hampi was systematically vandalized, sacked, and abandoned, reducing it to ruins.

| ***Amuktamalyada*** | Written in **Telugu** | Mistakenly assuming it was written in Sanskrit or Kannada |
| ***Jambavati Kalyanam*** | Written in **Sanskrit** | Confusing with Krishnadevaraya's Telugu works |
| **Battle of Talikota Year** | **1565 CE** | Confusing with First Battle of Panipat (1526) or Khanwa (1527) |
| **Dynasty of Krishnadevaraya** | **Tuluva Dynasty** | Confusing with the founding Sangama Dynasty |
| **Berar Sultanate** | **Did NOT join the anti-Vijayanagara alliance** | Assuming all five Deccan Sultanates united |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "S-S-T-A SANG TO KRISHNA AT TALIKOTA"**
> - **S** $\rightarrow$ **S**angama Dynasty (Founding: Harihara & Bukka, 1336)
> - **S** $\rightarrow$ **S**aluva Dynasty
> - **T** $\rightarrow$ **T**uluva Dynasty (Krishnadevaraya)
> - **A** $\rightarrow$ **A**ravidu Dynasty (Post-Talikota shift to Penukonda)
> - **Talikota** $\rightarrow$ Sacking of Hampi in 1565
