# 📌 Andaman and Nicobar Islands: Geography, Channels & Subgroups

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **$10^\circ\text{ N}$ latitude** (The **Ten Degree Channel**).
- **Geographic Divide**: The **$10^\circ\text{ N}$ parallel** serves as the maritime boundary separating the **Andaman Group** (to the north) from the **Nicobar Group** (to the south).
- **Coordinates**: The archipelago extends between **$6^\circ\text{ N}$ and $14^\circ\text{ N}$ Latitude** and **$92^\circ\text{ E}$ and $94^\circ\text{ E}$ Longitude** in the Bay of Bengal.
- **Island Breakdown**:
  - **Andaman Group**: Over **300 islands**; key clusters include **Great Andaman** (North, Middle, and South Andaman), Little Andaman, Ritchie's Archipelago, and Sentinel Islands.
  - **Nicobar Group**: Comprises **19 islands**; divided into **Car Nicobar** (north), **Camorta, Katchall, and Nancowry** (central), and **Great Nicobar** (south).
- **International Boundary**: The southern tip of Great Nicobar lies about **90 miles (~145 km)** from the northwestern tip of **Sumatra, Indonesia**, across the Great Channel.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Spatial Structure: Andaman vs. Nicobar Groups

| Island Cluster | Primary Islands & Groups | Key Highlights & Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Great Andaman** | North Andaman, Middle Andaman, South Andaman, Baratang, Rutland | • **North Andaman**: Contains **Saddle Peak ($732\text{ m}$)**, the highest point.<br>• **Middle Andaman**: Largest island in India by area.<br>• **South Andaman**: Houses the capital city **Port Blair** and Cellular Jail. |
| **Ritchie's Archipelago & Sentinel** | Swaraj Dweep (Havelock), Shaheed Dweep (Neil), North Sentinel Island | • Major tourism hub (Radhanagar Beach).<br>• North Sentinel is home to the protected, uncontacted **Sentinelese** tribe. |
| **Little Andaman** | Little Andaman Island | • Southernmost island of the Andaman subgroup.<br>• Separated from South Andaman/Rutland by **Duncan Passage**.<br>• Separated from Nicobar by the **$10^\circ\text{ N}$ Channel** (~145 km wide). |
| **Northern Nicobars** | Car Nicobar | • Northernmost island of the Nicobar subgroup, immediately south of 10° N. |
| **Central Nicobars** | Camorta, Katchall, Nancowry, Teressa | • Known for sheltered natural harbours and rich coconut plantations. |
| **Southern Nicobars** | Great Nicobar, Little Nicobar | • Houses **Mount Thullier ($642\text{ m}$)**.<br>• Contains **Indira Point ($6^\circ 45'\text{ N}$)**, the southernmost tip of India.<br>• Designated UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. |

### 2. Major Channels & Water Passages Around India

| Channel / Passage | Latitude / Coordinates | Water Body / Division Separated |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **8° Channel (Eight Degree Channel)** | **$8^\circ\text{ N}$** | **Minicoy Island (India)** $\longleftrightarrow$ **Maldives** |
| **9° Channel (Nine Degree Channel)** | **$9^\circ\text{ N}$** | **Minicoy Island** $\longleftrightarrow$ **Main Lakshadweep Archipelago** |
| **10° Channel (Ten Degree Channel)** | **$10^\circ\text{ N}$** | **Little Andaman** $\longleftrightarrow$ **Car Nicobar** (Andamans vs Nicobars) |
| **Duncan Passage** | — | **South Andaman / Rutland** $\longleftrightarrow$ **Little Andaman** |
| **Great / Grand Channel** | **$6^\circ\text{ N}$** | **Great Nicobar (Indira Point)** $\longleftrightarrow$ **Sumatra (Indonesia)** |
| **Coco Channel** | — | **North Andaman (Landfall Island)** $\longleftrightarrow$ **Coco Islands (Myanmar)** |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Boundary / Passage | Exact Separation Line | Common Trap / Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **10° North Latitude** | **Andaman Group $\longleftrightarrow$ Nicobar Group** | Confusing with Duncan Passage |
| **Duncan Passage** | **South Andaman $\longleftrightarrow$ Little Andaman** | Assuming it divides Andaman from Nicobar |
| **8° Channel** | **Minicoy $\longleftrightarrow$ Maldives** | Confusing with Minicoy $\longleftrightarrow$ Lakshadweep |
| **9° Channel** | **Lakshadweep $\longleftrightarrow$ Minicoy** | Confusing with the 8° Channel |
| **Great Channel** | **Great Nicobar $\longleftrightarrow$ Sumatra (Indonesia)** | Confusing with Coco Channel (Myanmar) |
