# 📌 Battle of Talikota (1565): Rama Raya & the Fall of Vijayanagara

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Ramrai (Rama Raya / Aliya Rama Raya)**.
- **De Facto vs. De Jure Rule**:
  - **De Jure (Titular) Monarch**: **Sadashiva Raya** (the last formal emperor of the Tuluva dynasty).
  - **De Facto Ruler (Real Power)**: **Aliya Rama Raya** (son-in-law of Krishnadevaraya), who served as the Prime Minister/Regent and controlled complete military and civil administration.
- **The Battle of Talikota (23 January 1565)**:
  - Also known as the **Battle of Rakshasi-Tangadi** or **Battle of Bannihatti**.
  - Fought between the Vijayanagara Empire (led by Rama Raya) and a unified coalition of four Deccan Sultanates (**Bijapur, Golconda, Ahmadnagar, and Bidar**; Berar was absent).
- **Outcome & Aftermath**: Rama Raya was captured and executed during the battle; the capital city of **Hampi** was plundered and destroyed; Rama Raya's brother **Tirumala Deva Raya** fled south with Sadashiva Raya to establish the fourth dynasty (**Aravidu Dynasty**) at **Penukonda**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. The Political Setting Before the Battle (1542–1565)

| Figure / Entity | Role / Political Stature | Key Historical Actions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sadashiva Raya** | Puppet / Titular Emperor | Placed on the throne in 1542 but kept under close confinement with zero administrative power. |
| **Aliya Rama Raya (Ramrai)** | *De Facto* Ruler & Prime Minister | • Married Krishnadevaraya's daughter (*Aliya* means son-in-law).<br>• Controlled foreign policy, revenue, and military appointments.<br>• Pursued a "divide-and-rule" policy among the rival Deccan Sultanates. |
| **The Deccan Confederacy** | United Sultanates Coalition | Formed an alliance sealed through matrimonial ties (e.g., Chand Bibi of Ahmadnagar married Ali Adil Shah I of Bijapur) to eliminate Vijayanagara hegemony. |
| **Tirumala & Venkatadri** | Rama Raya's Brothers | Commanded army wings at Talikota; Tirumala subsequently salvaged the state treasury and founded the Aravidu Dynasty. |
| **Gilani Brothers** | Mercenary Commanders | Commanded large Muslim contingents in the Vijayanagara army; their last-minute betrayal/defection shifted the battle in favor of the Sultanates. |

---

### 2. Key Factors Behind the Vijayanagara Defeat

- **Geopolitical Overreach**: Rama Raya continuously played one Deccan sultan against another (intervening in wars between Bijapur and Ahmadnagar), which eventually forced them to bury their differences and unite against him.
- **Battlefield Tactical Shifts**: Despite Vijayanagara's numerical superiority, the Sultanates utilized superior, coordinated **artillery (cannons under Rumi Khan)** and cavalry maneuvers.
- **Defection of Key Divisions**: The sudden defection of key commander divisions (including the Gilani brothers) during the peak of battle led to the capture and beheading of Rama Raya on the field.

> - **Ramrai in Fact** $\rightarrow$ De facto ruler and supreme commander
> - **Sadashiv on Paper** $\rightarrow$ Titular/De jure emperor on the throne
> - **'65** $\rightarrow$ Battle fought in 1565 CE
