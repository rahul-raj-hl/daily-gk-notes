# 📌 Global Rice Production & Agricultural Milestones

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **China**.
- **Global Production Dynamic**: Historically, **China** held the position of the world's largest producer of rice, followed closely by India. In recent crop assessment cycles, India surged to the forefront driven by expanded gross cultivated acreage, record output, enhanced irrigation networks, and high-yielding climate-resilient varieties.
- **Top Exporter Status**: India has consistently remained the **world's largest exporter of rice** (commanding ~40% of the global rice trade), surpassing traditional exporters like **Thailand** and **Vietnam**.
- **Key Agronomic Drivers**: Rapid adoption of high-yielding varieties (HYVs), expansion of micro-irrigation, improved agronomic practices, and government release of climate-smart crop seed cultivars.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Rice Cultivation: Agronomic & Geographic Conditions in India

*Geographical requirements and features of India's principal food grain:*

| Agronomic Parameter | Ideal Requirement | Exam Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Crop Season** | Primarily **Kharif** (sown in June–July, harvested in Autumn/Winter) | Grown year-round (*Aus, Aman, Boro*) in West Bengal & Assam |
| **Temperature** | High temperature (**$> 25^\circ\text{C}$**) | Requires warmth throughout germination and vegetative phases |
| **Rainfall** | High annual rainfall (**$> 100\text{ cm}$**) | Grown in lower rainfall areas (e.g., Punjab, Haryana) via canal/tube-well irrigation |
| **Soil Type** | Deep clayey / loamy soils | Requires soils with high water-retention capacity |
| **Leading Indian States** | **1. West Bengal**, **2. Uttar Pradesh**, **3. Punjab** | West Bengal is the highest total rice producer in India |

---

### 2. Major Rice Producing & Exporting Nations

*Global dynamics of the world's leading rice economies:*

- **Top Producers**: **India** and **China** account for over 50% of total global rice production combined, followed by Indonesia, Bangladesh, and Vietnam.
- **Top Exporters**:
  1. **India** (Dominates non-basmati and basmati international markets).
  2. **Thailand** (Known for Jasmine rice).
  3. **Vietnam** (Key supplier to Southeast and East Asia).
  4. **Pakistan** (Key exporter of aromatic Basmati rice).
