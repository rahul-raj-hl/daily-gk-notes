# 📌 Fundamental Duties (Article 51A, Part IV-A)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Constitutional Location**: Enshrined in **Part IV-A** of the Constitution under a single article: **Article 51A**.
- **Historical Incorporation**: Added by the **42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976** based on the recommendations of the **Sardar Swaran Singh Committee** (originally 10 duties).
- **Source of Inspiration**: Borrowed from the **USSR Constitution** (former Soviet Union). India is one of the few democratic nations (along with Japan) to include duties in its constitution.
- **Current Total Count**: Currently, there are **11 Fundamental Duties** in the Constitution. The 11th duty was added by the **86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002**.
- **Legal Obligation vs. Duty**: Paying taxes and family planning were recommended by the Swaran Singh Committee but were **not accepted**; paying taxes remains a legal/statutory obligation, not a constitutional Fundamental Duty.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Evolution and Recommendations

- **Swaran Singh Committee (1976)**: Appointed during the Internal Emergency to suggest amendments regarding fundamental duties. It recommended 8 duties, including:
  - Duty to pay taxes (Rejected by the government).
  - Penalty/punishment for non-compliance (Rejected).
- **Verma Committee (1999)**: Identified the existence of legal provisions for the enforcement of some Fundamental Duties (e.g., *Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971*, *Wildlife Protection Act, 1972*, *Representation of the People Act, 1951*).

---

### 2. Complete List of 11 Fundamental Duties (Article 51A)

*It shall be the duty of every citizen of India:*

| Clause | Duty Description | Key Theme |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **51A(a)** | To abide by the Constitution and respect its ideals and institutions, the **National Flag** and the **National Anthem** | National Symbols |
| **51A(b)** | To cherish and follow the noble ideals that inspired the national struggle for freedom | Freedom Struggle |
| **51A(c)** | To uphold and protect the **sovereignty, unity, and integrity of India** | Sovereignty & Integrity |
| **51A(d)** | To defend the country and render national service when called upon to do so | National Defense |
| **51A(e)** | To promote harmony and common brotherhood; to renounce practices derogatory to the dignity of women | Brotherhood & Women's Dignity |
| **51A(f)** | To value and preserve the rich heritage of the country's composite culture | Composite Culture |
| **51A(g)** | To protect and improve the natural environment (forests, lakes, rivers, wildlife) and have compassion for living creatures | Environment & Compassion |
| **51A(h)** | To develop **scientific temper**, humanism, and the spirit of inquiry and reform | Scientific Temper |
| **51A(i)** | To safeguard public property and to abjure violence | Public Property |
| **51A(j)** | To strive towards excellence in all spheres of individual and collective activity | Excellence & Progress |
| **51A(k)** | To provide opportunities for education to one's child/ward between the age of **6 and 14 years** *(Added by 86th CAA, 2002)* | Education of Children |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Feature / Concept | Actual Status | Common Pitfall / Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duty to Pay Taxes** | **Statutory/Legal Obligation only** | Mistakenly considering it an Article 51A duty |
| **Duty to Vote in Elections** | **Statutory Right / civic responsibility** | Assuming voting is listed in Article 51A |
| **National Anthem vs. National Song** | **National Anthem is explicitly protected** | Assuming *Vande Mataram* is explicitly named in Art 51A(a) |
| **Target Applicability** | **Indian Citizens Only** | Assuming Fundamental Duties apply to foreign tourists/residents |
