# 📌 Chief Justice of High Court: Appointment, Oath & Sikkim High Court

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Justice A. Muhamed Mustaque**.
- **Appointment & Oath**: Sworn in as the **24th Chief Justice of the Sikkim High Court** on January 4, 2026, at Lok Bhawan (Gangtok).
- **Oath Administrator**: Administered by the **Governor of Sikkim**, **Om Prakash Mathur** (as per **Article 219** of the Constitution).
- **Prior Judicial Service**: Previously served as a Senior Judge and **Acting Chief Justice of the Kerala High Court**, with extensive expertise in constitutional, civil, and criminal jurisprudence.
- **Sikkim High Court Establishment**: Established in **1975** following the merger of Sikkim with the Indian Union via the **36th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1975**; it is one of the smallest High Courts in India by sanctioned judge strength.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Constitutional Framework Governing High Courts (Articles 214–231, Part VI)

*Key constitutional mechanisms governing the State Judiciary in India:*

| Constitutional Dimension | Governing Article | Operational Rule / Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Establishment of High Courts** | **Article 214** | There shall be a High Court for each State (Parliament may establish a common High Court under Article 231). |
| **High Court as a Court of Record** | **Article 215** | High Courts have power to punish for contempt of themselves and maintain records of evidentiary value. |
| **Appointment of Chief Justice** | **Article 217(1)** | Appointed by the **President of India** after consultation with the **Chief Justice of India (CJI)** and the **Governor of the concerned State**. |
| **Appointment of Other HC Judges** | **Article 217(1)** | Appointed by the President in consultation with the CJI, the State Governor, and the **Chief Justice of that High Court**. |
| **Oath or Affirmation** | **Article 219** | Subscribed before the **Governor of the State** (or a person appointed by him) according to the **Third Schedule**. |
| **Retirement Age** | **Article 217(1)** | Holds office until the age of **62 years** (raised from 60 to 62 by the 15th CAA, 1963). |
| **Transfer of Judges** | **Article 222** | President may transfer a Judge from one High Court to another after consultation with the CJI. |
| **Writ Jurisdiction** | **Article 226** | Power to issue writs (*Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Quo-Warranto, Certiorari*) for Fundamental Rights and ordinary legal rights. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Constitutional Action | High Court Judges | Supreme Court Judges | Common Pitfall / Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Appointed By** | **President of India** | **President of India** | Assuming State Governor appoints High Court Judges |
| **Oath Administered By** | **Governor of the State** | **President of India** | Confusing Appointing authority with Oath administrator |
| **Resignation Submitted To** | **President of India** | **President of India** | Assuming HC Judges resign to the State Governor |
| **Retirement Age** | **62 Years** | **65 Years** | Mixing up 62 years (HC) and 65 years (SC) |
| **Pension Charged On** | **Consolidated Fund of India** | **Consolidated Fund of India** | Salaries of HC judges are charged on State funds, but pensions on India's fund |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "PRESIDENT APPOINTS, GOVERNOR SWEARS, 62 RETIRES"**
> - **President Appoints** $\rightarrow$ Warrant issued by President (Art 217)
> - **Governor Swears** $\rightarrow$ Oath administered by State Governor (Art 219)
> - **62 Retires** $\rightarrow$ Maximum age limit for High Court Judges
