# 📌 72nd National Volleyball Tournament & Sports Milestones

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Varanasi** (Uttar Pradesh).
- **Inauguration**: Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the **72nd National Volleyball Tournament** in January from **Varanasi**, Uttar Pradesh, via video conferencing.
- **Participation**: Teams representing **28 states and Union Territories** competed in this apex national-level sports championship.
- **Varanasi Connection**: Varanasi is the parliamentary constituency (Lok Sabha) of Prime Minister Narendra Modi and a prominent cultural, spiritual, and emerging sports hub in eastern Uttar Pradesh.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Key Highlights of the 72nd National Volleyball Tournament

- **Broad Representation**: Brought together premier male and female volleyball athletes from 28 states, fostering grassroots sports talent and inter-state sporting camaraderie.
- **Core Message**: Emphasized the core sporting values of teamwork, agile on-court coordination, mental resilience, and shared collective responsibility under the broader vision of *Khelo India* and *Fit India*.
- **Infrastructure Push**: Highlighted modern sports complex development across Uttar Pradesh, including multi-purpose indoor halls, synthetic courts, and rural mini-stadiums.
