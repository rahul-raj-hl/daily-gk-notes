# 📌 Gallantry Awards of India: Sena Medals, Ashoka Chakra & Kirti Chakra

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Sena Medal (Gallantry)**: A total of **44 Sena Medals (Gallantry)** were approved, of which **5 were awarded posthumously**.
- **Total Posthumous Gallantry Honors**: A combined total of **6 posthumous awards** were conferred across the gallantry categories (**1 Shaurya Chakra + 5 Sena Medals**).
- **Ashoka Chakra (1)**: Awarded to **Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla** (Indian Air Force – Flying/Pilot).
- **Kirti Chakra (3)**: Conferred upon:
  1. **Major Arshdeep Singh** (Indian Army – 1 Assam Rifles)
  2. **Naib Subedar Doleshwar Subba** (Indian Army – 2 PARA SF)
  3. **Group Captain Prashanth Balakrishnan Nair** (Indian Air Force – Flying/Pilot)

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Classification & Hierarchy of Gallantry Awards in India

*Gallantry awards in India are broadly divided into Wartime and Peacetime decorations:*

| Category | Highest Decoration (Rank 1) | Second Highest (Rank 2) | Third Highest (Rank 3) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wartime Gallantry Awards** *(Conspicuous bravery in the face of the enemy)* | **Param Vir Chakra (PVC)** | **Maha Vir Chakra (MVC)** | **Vir Chakra (VrC)** |
| **Peacetime Gallantry Awards** *(Courage/self-sacrifice other than in the face of enemy)* | **Ashoka Chakra** | **Kirti Chakra** | **Shaurya Chakra** |
| **Service-Specific Gallantry Medals** | • **Sena Medal** (Indian Army)<br>• **Nao Sena Medal** (Indian Navy)<br>• **Vayu Sena Medal** (Indian Air Force) | — | — |
