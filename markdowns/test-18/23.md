# 📌 Sources of the Indian Constitution: Canadian vs. Irish Features

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Nomination of Members to Rajya Sabha** (Borrowing from the **Irish Constitution**, not Canada).
- **Provisions Borrowed from Canada**:
  - Federation with a strong Centre (*quasi-federal structure*).
  - Vesting of **residuary powers** in the Centre (**Article 248**).
  - **Appointment of State Governors** by the Centre (**Article 155**).
  - **Advisory jurisdiction of the Supreme Court** (**Article 143**).
- **Provisions Borrowed from Ireland**:
  - **Nomination of 12 members to Rajya Sabha** by the President for literature, science, art, and social service (**Article 80**).
  - **Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP)** (Part IV, Articles 36–51).
  - Method of **election of the President** (**Article 55**).

## 📖 Main Concept

*Major features borrowed from foreign constitutions by the Constituent Assembly:*

| **USA** | • **Fundamental Rights** (Part III)<br>• **Independence of the Judiciary & Judicial Review**<br>• **Impeachment of the President**<br>• **Removal of Supreme Court & High Court Judges**<br>• **Preamble** | • Articles 12–35<br>• Article 13, 32, 226<br>• Article 61<br>• Article 124(4) |

### 2. Deep Dive: Rajya Sabha Composition Provisions

*Crucial distinction regarding the upper house of Parliament:*

| Provision | Borrowed Source Country | Governing Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nomination of 12 Members to Rajya Sabha** | **Ireland** | 12 members nominated by the President having special knowledge in **Literature, Science, Art, and Social Service** (Article 80). |
| **Election of Members to Rajya Sabha** | **South Africa** | Elected by the elected members of the Legislative Assemblies of States via proportional representation with single transferable vote (Article 80(4)). |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Constitutional Feature | Correct Source | Common Trap / Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nomination to Rajya Sabha** | **Ireland** | Confusing with South Africa (which gave the *Election* of RS members) |
| **Election of Rajya Sabha** | **South Africa** | Confusing with Ireland |
| **Appointment of Governor** | **Canada** | Confusing with Government of India Act 1935 (which gave *Office of Governor*) |
| **Residuary Powers** | **Canada (Centre)** | Confusing with USA/Australia (where residuary powers rest with States) |
| **Concurrent List** | **Australia** | Confusing with Canada |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "CANADA CENTERS THE GOVERNOR'S RESIDUAL ADVICE"**
> - **Centers** $\rightarrow$ Strong Centre
> - **Governor** $\rightarrow$ Appointment of State Governors by Centre
> - **Residual** $\rightarrow$ Residuary powers with Centre
> - **Advice** $\rightarrow$ Advisory Jurisdiction of the Supreme Court

### PYQ 1

Which of the following provision is not borrowed by the Indian Constitution from the Canadian Constitution?

- [ ] Federal system with fortified center
- [ ] Appointment of state governors by the center
- [ ] Advisory adjudication of supreme court
- [x] Nomination of Members to Rajya Sabha

> The nomination of 12 distinguished members to the Rajya Sabha was borrowed from the Irish Constitution, whereas the other three features were borrowed from Canada.

The concept of 'Concurrent List' in the Seventh Schedule of the Indian Constitution is borrowed from the Constitution of:

- [ ] Canada
- [x] Australia

> The Concurrent List and the provision for a Joint Sitting of both Houses of Parliament were adopted from the Australian Constitution.

- [ ] United States

> The advisory jurisdiction of the apex court is an established Canadian constitutional convention incorporated under Article 143 of the Indian Constitution.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

The method of electing the members of the Rajya Sabha by state MLAs through proportional representation is borrowed from the constitution of:

- [ ] Ireland
- [x] South Africa
- [ ] Canada
- [ ] United States

> While the *nomination* of members to Rajya Sabha is from Ireland, the *procedure for election* of Rajya Sabha members was borrowed from the Constitution of South Africa.

---

### Q2

In the Indian Constitution, the vesting of residuary legislative powers with the Union Parliament (Article 248) follows the model of:

- [ ] The United States
- [ ] Switzerland
- [x] Canada
- [ ] Australia

> In the USA and Australia, residuary powers belong to the constituent states, whereas the Indian framers adopted the Canadian model of vesting residuary powers exclusively in the central Parliament.

---

### Q3

The procedure established by law (Article 21) in the Indian Constitution is borrowed from which nation's legal system?

- [ ] United Kingdom
- [ ] United States
- [x] Japan
- [ ] Germany

> The phrase "procedure established by law" in Article 21 is derived from the Japanese Constitution, distinct from the American concept of "due process of law."

---

### Q4

Which of the following features was borrowed from the Weimar Constitution of Germany?

- [ ] Emergency powers of the President
- [x] Suspension of Fundamental Rights during an Emergency
- [ ] Financial Emergency provisions
- [ ] Impeachment of the President

> The provision for the suspension of Fundamental Rights during a National Emergency was borrowed from the Weimar Constitution of Germany (while the emergency provisions themselves were adapted from the GoI Act 1935).
