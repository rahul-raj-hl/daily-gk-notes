# 📌 Regional Classification of the Himalayas (River Boundaries)

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Nepal Himalayas**.
- **River Demarcation**: The **Nepal Himalayas** stretch across an east-west span of approximately **800 km** between the **Kali River** (in the west) and the **Teesta River** (in the east).
- **Highest Elevation**: This east-central section is the highest and most elevated part of the entire Himalayan mountain system, hosting the world's tallest peaks including **Mount Everest (8,848.86 m)**, **Kanchenjunga (8,586 m)**, **Makalu**, **Dhaulagiri**, **Annapurna**, and **Manaslu**.
- **Classification Pioneer**: The regional division of the Himalayas from west to east on the basis of river valleys was first formulated by the eminent geologist and surveyor **Sir Sidney Burrard**.

---

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. West-to-East Regional Divisions of the Himalayas (Sir Sidney Burrard)

*Complete river demarcation and characteristics across all four traditional divisions:*

| Regional Division | River Demarcation (West $\to$ East) | Approx. Length | Major Mountain Peaks | Key Features / Passes |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Punjab / Kashmir / Himachal Himalayas** | Between **Indus** and **Sutlej** rivers | ~560 km | Nanga Parbat, Nun Kun | • Zaskar, Ladakh, Pir Panjal ranges.<br>• *Karewa* formations (famous for Zafran/Saffron cultivation).<br>• Rohtang, Zoji La, Banihal passes. |
| **Kumaon Himalayas** | Between **Sutlej** and **Kali** rivers | ~320 km | Nanda Devi, Kamet, Trishul, Badrinath, Kedarnath | • Located predominantly in **Uttarakhand**.<br>• Sources of sacred rivers (Ganga, Yamuna).<br>• Famous for *Duns* (Dehradun) and lakes (*Tals* like Nainital, Bhimtal). |
| **Nepal Himalayas** | Between **Kali** and **Teesta** rivers | ~800 km | **Mt. Everest**, **Kanchenjunga**, Makalu, Dhaulagiri, Annapurna | • **Longest and highest section**.<br>• Lesser Himalayas termed **Mahabharat Lekh**.<br>• Intermontane lacustrine basins: **Kathmandu & Pokhara valleys**. |
| **Assam / Eastern Himalayas** | Between **Teesta** and **Dihang (Brahmaputra)** rivers | ~720 km | **Namcha Barwa**, Kula Kangri, Chomo Lhari | • Narrow outer Himalayas (Shiwaliks almost merge).<br>• Drained by Dibang, Lohit, Subansiri, Manas.<br>• Home to ethnic tribal communities (Apatani, Mishmi, Dafla). |

---

### 2. Prominent Features of the Nepal Himalayas

- **Transverse River Systems**: Traversed and dissected by three major antecedent river systems:
  - **Ghaghara (Karnali)**
  - **Gandak (Narayani)**
  - **Kosi (Saptakoshi)**
- **Lacustrine Valleys**: Features the famous flat-bottomed lacustrine valleys of **Kathmandu** and **Pokhara** (former ancient glacial/lake basins filled with rich lacustrine sediments).
- **Lesser Himalayas & Shiwaliks**:
  - The Middle/Lesser Himalayan range is locally known as the **Mahabharat Lekh**.
  - Towards the eastern part of Nepal, the **Shiwalik range** narrows significantly, hugging the Lesser Himalayas, and becomes discontinuous beyond the Narayani (Gandak) river.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Regional Division | Defining River Pair | Common Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nepal Himalayas** | **Kali to Teesta** | Confusing with Kumaon (Sutlej to Kali) |
| **Kumaon Himalayas** | **Sutlej to Kali** | Confusing with Punjab Himalayas (Indus to Sutlej) |
| **Assam Himalayas** | **Teesta to Dihang (Brahmaputra)** | Assuming it begins from Kali instead of Teesta |
| **Pir Panjal Range** | **Punjab/Kashmir Himalayas** | Mistakenly assigning it to Kumaon or Nepal |

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic for River Boundaries (West to East): "I SEE KALI TASTING DRINKS"**
> - **I** $\to$ **I**ndus
> - **S**ee $\to$ **S**utlej (Indus to Sutlej = *Punjab/Kashmir*)
> - **K**ali $\to$ **K**ali (Sutlej to Kali = *Kumaon*)
> - **T**asting $\to$ **T**eesta (Kali to Teesta = *Nepal*)
> - **D**rinks $\to$ **D**ihang (Teesta to Dihang = *Assam*)

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

Which of the following mountain peaks is NOT situated in the Nepal Himalayas?

- [ ] Makalu
- [ ] Annapurna
- [x] Nanda Devi
- [ ] Dhaulagiri

> Nanda Devi (7,816 m) is situated in the Garhwal/Kumaon section of the Himalayas in Uttarakhand, India, whereas Makalu, Annapurna, and Dhaulagiri are in the Nepal Himalayas.

- [x] Kashmir / Punjab Himalayas

- [ ] Nepal Himalayas
- [ ] Assam Himalayas

> *Karewas* are lacustrine deposits found exclusively in the Valley of Kashmir within the Kashmir Himalayas.
