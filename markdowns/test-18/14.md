# 📌 Enzymes and Their Essential Inorganic Metal Cofactors

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Nitrogenase — Mo** (Molybdenum).
- **Core Role of Nitrogenase**: Biological nitrogen-fixing enzyme that converts atmospheric nitrogen ($\text{N}_2$) into ammonia ($\text{NH}_3$) in diazotrophs (e.g., *Rhizobium*, *Azotobacter*, cyanobacteria) utilizing an essential iron-molybdenum cofactor (**FeMo-co**).
- **Corrections for Incorrect Pairs**:
  - **Carbonic anhydrase**: Uses **Zinc ($\text{Zn}^{2+}$)** (essential for $\text{CO}_2 / \text{HCO}_3^-$ buffering in blood), *not $\text{Fe}^{2+}$*.
  - **Salivary amylase (Ptyalin)**: Activated by **Chloride ions ($\text{Cl}^-$)**, *not $\text{Zn}^{2+}$*.
  - **Urease**: Requires **Nickel ($\text{Ni}^{2+}$)** (the first enzyme ever crystallized by J.B. Sumner), *not $\text{Cu}^{2+}$*.

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Classification of Enzyme Components

*Non-protein auxiliary components required for biocatalytic activity:*

| Component Type | Chemical Nature | Association with Apoenzyme | Prominent Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Apoenzyme** | Pure protein component | Inactive on its own | Catalytic protein backbone |
| **Cofactor (Inorganic / Metal Ions)** | Inorganic metal cations or anions | Coordinates with active site residues | $\text{Zn}^{2+}, \text{Mg}^{2+}, \text{Mo}, \text{Ni}^{2+}, \text{Fe}^{2+}/\text{Fe}^{3+}, \text{Cl}^-$ |
| **Coenzyme (Organic)** | Organic molecules (mostly vitamin-derived) | Transiently/loosely bound | $\text{NAD}^+, \text{NADP}^+$ (Niacin), $\text{FAD}$ (Riboflavin), Coenzyme A |
| **Prosthetic Group** | Organic or inorganic complex | Tightly / covalently bound | **Heme** in Catalase & Peroxidase, **Biotin** |
| **Holoenzyme** | Complete active catalytic complex | $\text{Apoenzyme} + \text{Cofactor} = \text{Holoenzyme}$ | Fully functional enzyme unit |

### 2. High-Frequency Enzyme-Cofactor Pairs

*Standard biological enzymes and their indispensable metal ion activators/cofactors:*

| Enzyme | Metal Ion / Cofactor | Biological Function / Reaction Catalyzed |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Nitrogenase** | **$\text{Mo}$ (Molybdenum) & $\text{Fe}$ (Iron)** | Biological $\text{N}_2$ fixation: $\text{N}_2 + 8\text{H}^+ + 8\text{e}^- + 16\text{ATP} \to 2\text{NH}_3 + \text{H}_2 + 16\text{ADP} + 16\text{P}_i$ |
| **Carbonic Anhydrase** | **$\text{Zn}^{2+}$ (Zinc)** | Rapid interconversion: $\text{CO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O} \rightleftharpoons \text{H}_2\text{CO}_3 \rightleftharpoons \text{H}^+ + \text{HCO}_3^-$ |
| **Carboxypeptidase** | **$\text{Zn}^{2+}$ (Zinc)** | Cleavage of peptide bonds at the C-terminal of proteins |
| **Alcohol Dehydrogenase** | **$\text{Zn}^{2+}$ (Zinc)** | Oxidation of alcohols to aldehydes/ketones |
| **Urease** | **$\text{Ni}^{2+}$ (Nickel)** | Hydrolysis of urea into carbon dioxide and ammonia |
| **DNA / RNA Polymerase** | **$\text{Mg}^{2+}$ (Magnesium)** | Nucleic acid synthesis and polymerization of nucleotides |
| **Hexokinase / Kinases** | **$\text{Mg}^{2+}$ (Magnesium)** | Phosphorylation of glucose during glycolysis |
| **Cytochrome Oxidase** | **$\text{Fe}^{2+}/\text{Fe}^{3+} \text{ and } \text{Cu}^{2+}$** | Terminal electron transfer to $\text{O}_2$ in mitochondrial ETC |
| **Catalase & Peroxidase** | **$\text{Fe}^{2+}/\text{Fe}^{3+}$ (Heme)** | Breakdown of toxic hydrogen peroxide ($\text{H}_2\text{O}_2 \to \text{H}_2\text{O} + \frac{1}{2}\text{O}_2$) |
| **Salivary Amylase** | **$\text{Cl}^-$ (Chloride ion)** | Hydrolysis of dietary starch into maltose |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Enzyme | Correct Cofactor | Common Incorrect Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Carbonic Anhydrase** | **$\text{Zn}^{2+}$ (Zinc)** | Confusing with Iron ($\text{Fe}$) because it works inside RBCs |
| **Salivary Amylase** | **$\text{Cl}^-$ (Chloride)** | Confusing with Zinc ($\text{Zn}$) or Calcium ($\text{Ca}$) |
| **Urease** | **$\text{Ni}^{2+}$ (Nickel)** | Confusing with Copper ($\text{Cu}$) or Iron ($\text{Fe}$) |
| **Nitrogenase** | **$\text{Mo}$ & $\text{Fe}$** | Confusing with Manganese ($\text{Mn}$) |
| **Water-Splitting Complex in Photosynthesis** | **$\text{Mn}^{2+}$ & $\text{Cl}^-$** | Confusing with Magnesium ($\text{Mg}$) |

> **Mnemonic: "MOLY FIXES NITROGEN, ZINC BLOWS BUBBLES IN CARBONIC, NICKEL EATS UREA"**
> - **Moly Fixes Nitrogen** $\rightarrow$ **Mo** is the cofactor for **Nitrogenase**
> - **Zinc in Carbonic** $\rightarrow$ **$\text{Zn}^{2+}$** is the cofactor for **Carbonic Anhydrase**
> - **Nickel eats Urea** $\rightarrow$ **$\text{Ni}^{2+}$** is the cofactor for **Urease**

- [ ] Urease — $\text{Cu}^{2+}$

Which metal ion is present as a catalytic cofactor in the fastest known enzyme, Carbonic Anhydrase?

- [ ] Iron ($\text{Fe}$)
- [ ] Copper ($\text{Cu}$)
- [x] Zinc ($\text{Zn}$)
- [ ] Magnesium ($\text{Mg}$)

> Carbonic anhydrase contains a coordinated $\text{Zn}^{2+}$ ion at its active site, enabling it to hydrate up to $10^6$ molecules of $\text{CO}_2$ per second.

---

### PYQ 3

Which of the following ions is required as an activator for the optimal catalytic activity of human salivary amylase (ptyalin)?

- [ ] Sodium ions ($\text{Na}^+$)
- [x] Chloride ions ($\text{Cl}^-$)
- [ ] Potassium ions ($\text{K}^+$)
- [ ] Calcium ions ($\text{Ca}^{2+}$)

> Chloride ions ($\text{Cl}^-$) act as allosteric activators that bind specifically to salivary and pancreatic amylase, facilitating starch hydrolysis.

- [ ] Cobalt

An apoenzyme combined with its specific non-protein cofactor forms the complete, catalytically active enzyme known as a:

- [ ] Zymogen
- [ ] Isoenzyme
- [x] Holoenzyme
- [ ] Coenzyme

> A holoenzyme represents the intact, catalytically active enzyme-cofactor complex ($\text{Holoenzyme} = \text{Apoenzyme} + \text{Cofactor}$).

### Q3

Which of the following enzymes responsible for cellular respiration contains BOTH Iron ($\text{Fe}$) and Copper ($\text{Cu}$) metal cofactors?

- [ ] Hexokinase
- [x] Cytochrome c Oxidase (Complex IV)
- [ ] Pyruvate Dehydrogenase
- [ ] Lactate Dehydrogenase

> Cytochrome c Oxidase (Complex IV of the mitochondrial electron transport chain) contains two heme iron centers ($\text{a}$ and $\text{a}_3$) and two copper centers ($\text{Cu}_A$ and $\text{Cu}_B$).

### Q4

Which metal ion is mandatory for the catalytic activity of all kinase enzymes that transfer phosphate groups from ATP (such as Hexokinase and DNA Polymerase)?

- [ ] Calcium ($\text{Ca}^{2+}$)
- [x] Magnesium ($\text{Mg}^{2+}$)
- [ ] Manganese ($\text{Mn}^{2+}$)
- [ ] Iron ($\text{Fe}^{2+}$)

> $\text{Mg}^{2+}$ forms an essential chelate complex with the negatively charged triphosphate chain of ATP ($\text{Mg-ATP}^{2-}$), enabling kinases and polymerases to attack and transfer phosphate groups.
