# 📌 Buddhist Patronage of Emperor Ashoka & Sects of Buddhism

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Hinayana** (specifically the orthodox **Theravada / Sthaviravada** lineage).
- **Ashoka's Historical Patronage**: Emperor Ashoka embraced and patronized early Buddhism following the devastating **Kalinga War (261 BCE)**, adhering strictly to the original ethical teachings of Siddhartha Gautama (Hinayana/Theravada) using the **Pali language**.
- **Third Buddhist Council**: Convened by Ashoka at **Pataliputra (250 BCE)** under the presidency of **Moggaliputta Tissa** to purge the Sangha of corruption, compile the ***Abhidhamma Pitaka***, and dispatch Buddhist missions across Sri Lanka (led by his son Mahendra and daughter Sanghamitra) and Southeast Asia.
- **Why Not "All of the Above"**: **Mahayana** developed formally around the 1st century CE during the Fourth Buddhist Council under Kushan Emperor **Kanishka**, while **Vajrayana** (Tantric Buddhism) originated much later around the 5th–8th century CE under the **Pala dynasty**. Ashoka lived in the 3rd century BCE and therefore only patronized early orthodox Buddhism (Hinayana).

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Comparative Analysis of the Three Main Sects of Buddhism

*Key theological, linguistic, and philosophical differences across the branches of Buddhism:*

| Parameter | **Hinayana (Lesser Vehicle / Theravada)** | **Mahayana (Greater Vehicle)** | **Vajrayana (Vehicle of Thunderbolt / Diamond)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meaning / Conception** | "Lesser Vehicle" / School of the Elders (*Sthaviravada*) | "Greater Vehicle" / Universal salvation | "Vehicle of Thunderbolt" / Tantric esoteric path |
| **View of Buddha** | Revered as a **great human teacher** and historical guide | Worshipped as a **divine God / deity** | Worshipped via deities, female energies (*Taras*), and mantras |
| **Salvation / Goal** | **Arhatship** (Individual self-liberation / Nirvana through strict self-discipline) | **Bodhisattva** (Postponing own Nirvana to liberate all living beings) | Attainment of Buddhahood within a single lifetime through esoteric rituals |
| **Sacred Language** | **Pali** | **Sanskrit / Hybrid Sanskrit** | **Sanskrit & Tibetan** |
| **Idol Worship** | **Rejected** (Represented via symbols: Stupa, Footprints, Wheel, Lotus, Tree) | **Practiced extensively** (First human statues of Buddha made in Gandhara/Mathura art) | **Extensive use of mandalas, yantras, mudras, and deities** |
| **Key Royal Patrons** | **Ashoka** (Mauryan Empire) | **Kanishka** (Kushan Empire), **Harshavardhana** | **Pala Kings of Bengal & Bihar** (Dharmapala, Devapala) |
| **Geographical Spread** | Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Thailand, Cambodia, Laos (Southern Buddhism) | China, Japan, Korea, Vietnam (Northern Buddhism) | Tibet, Bhutan, Mongolia, Ladakh (Tantric Buddhism) |

### 2. The Four Buddhist Councils

*Key historical assemblies defining the evolution of Buddhist doctrine and schisms:*

| Council | Year & Venue | Royal Patron | Chairman / President | Core Outcome & Significance |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Council** | **483 BCE**<br>Sattapani Cave, Rajgriha | **Ajatashatru** (Haryanka Dynasty) | **Mahakassapa** | Compilation of **Sutta Pitaka** (recited by Ananda) and **Vinaya Pitaka** (recited by Upali). |
| **Second Council** | **383 BCE**<br>Vaishali | **Kalasoka** (Shishunaga Dynasty) | **Sabbakami** | First major schism into **Sthaviravadins** (elders/orthodox) and **Mahasanghikas** (reformists). |
| **Third Council** | **250 BCE**<br>Pataliputra | **Ashoka** (Mauryan Dynasty) | **Moggaliputta Tissa** | Compilation of the third pitaka: **Abhidhamma Pitaka**; dispatch of Dhamma missionaries abroad. |
| **Fourth Council** | **72 CE**<br>Kundalvana (Kashmir) | **Kanishka** (Kushan Dynasty) | **Vasumitra** (Dep: Asvaghosha) | Formal split of Buddhism into **Hinayana** and **Mahayana**; compilation of *Mahavibhasha Sastra*. |

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Historical Figure | Patronized Sect | Common Trap / Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Emperor Ashoka** | **Hinayana / Theravada** | Assuming Ashoka patronized Mahayana or all sects |
| **Emperor Kanishka** | **Mahayana** | Confusing Kanishka's Mahayana Fourth Council with Ashoka's Third Council |
| **Pala Kings (Dharmapala)** | **Vajrayana (Tantric)** | Assuming ancient kings only patronized Hinayana |
| **Buddha's Teachings Language** | **Pali / Magadhi Prakrit** | Confusing early Pali with later Mahayana classical Sanskrit |

- [ ] Sthaviravada
