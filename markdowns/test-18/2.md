# 📌 Major Multipurpose River Valley & Canal Projects in India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Damodar Valley Project (Jharkhand & West Bengal)**: India's **first multipurpose river valley project** (established via the DVC Act in **1948**), modeled on the *Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA)* of the USA to provide flood control, irrigation, and power generation in the Damodar basin.
- **Nagarjuna Sagar Project (Telangana & Andhra Pradesh)**: Built across the **Krishna River**; one of the world's largest and tallest masonry dams, supplying vital irrigation to Nalgonda, Guntur, Khammam, and Prakasam districts.
- **Indira Gandhi Canal (Rajasthan)**: Originating at the **Harike Barrage** (Punjab) at the confluence of the **Beas and Sutlej rivers**, it is the **longest canal network in India** (~650 km), transforming the arid Thar Desert into green, productive cropland.
- **Hirakud Dam Project (Odisha)**: Built across the **Mahanadi River** near Sambalpur; holds the distinction of being the **longest earthen dam in the world** (total length: ~25.8 km; main dam: 4.8 km).

### 2. Strategic Importance to Indian Agriculture & Economy

- **Taming "Rivers of Sorrow"**:
  - The Damodar River was tamed by DVC dams to prevent catastrophic flooding in lower Bengal.
  - The Mahanadi River flood surges across the coastal plains of Odisha were brought under control by the Hirakud reservoir.
- **Desert Transformation (Green Revolution in the Arid West)**:
  - The **Indira Gandhi Canal** (originally called *Rajasthan Canal*, renamed in 1984) brought assured water to arid zones, creating productive agricultural belts for wheat, mustard, and cotton in western Rajasthan.
- **Hydropower & Rural Electrification**:
  - All four projects provide base-load hydroelectric capacity, supporting regional industrial clusters and rural tubewell electrification.

## ⚠️ Frequently Confused

| Dam / Project | River | Common Trap |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nagarjuna Sagar Dam** | **Krishna River** | Confusing with Godavari River (Pochampad/Sriram Sagar is on Godavari) |
| **Hirakud Dam** | **Mahanadi River** | Confusing with Godavari or Brahmani rivers |
| **Indira Gandhi Canal Origin** | **Harike Barrage (Beas + Sutlej confluence)** | Assuming it originates directly at Bhakra Dam |
| **Highest vs. Longest Dam** | • **Highest**: Tehri Dam (Bhagirathi)<br>• **Longest**: Hirakud Dam (Mahanadi) | Confusing highest elevation with longest crest length |
