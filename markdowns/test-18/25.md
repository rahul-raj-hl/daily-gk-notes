# 📌 Major Mountain Passes of India: State-wise Distribution

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Correct Answer**: **Niti — Uttarakhand**.
- **Corrections for Other Options**:
  - **Mana Pass**: Located in **Uttarakhand** (connects Uttarakhand with Tibet), *not Himachal Pradesh*.
  - **Diphu Pass**: Located in **Arunachal Pradesh** (tri-junction of India, China, and Myanmar), *not Ladakh*.
  - **Aghil Pass**: Located in **Ladakh** (Karakoram Range, connects Ladakh with Xinjiang, China), *not Arunachal Pradesh*.
- **Strategic Importance of Niti Pass**: Situated at an elevation of $5,800\text{ m}$ in the Chamoli district of Uttarakhand, connecting the Garhwal region with Tibet; historically used for trade and pilgrimage.

*Comprehensive distribution of key Himalayan mountain passes:*

| State / UT | Major Mountain Passes | Connecting Regions / Strategic Details |

| **Jammu & Kashmir (UT)** | • **Banihal Pass**<br>• **Pir Panjal Pass**<br>• **Burzil Pass** | • **Banihal**: Connects Jammu with Srinagar (Jawahar Tunnel).<br>• **Burzil**: Connects Srinagar with Gilgit. |

| Pass Pair | Correct States / Locations | Common Trap / Pitfall |

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

The 'Palghat Gap' (Palakkad Gap) is a prominent mountain pass in the Western Ghats that provides connectivity between:

- [ ] Maharashtra and Gujarat
- [x] Kerala and Tamil Nadu
- [ ] Karnataka and Goa
- [ ] Andhra Pradesh and Odisha

> The Palakkad Gap is a low mountain pass break in the Western Ghats between the Nilgiri Hills and Anaimalai Hills connecting Palakkad (Kerala) to Coimbatore (Tamil Nadu).

### Q2

Which of the following passes is situated at the tri-junction of the international borders of India, China, and Myanmar?

- [ ] Bom Di La
- [x] Diphu Pass
- [ ] Jelep La
- [ ] Lipulekh

> Diphu Pass is a mountain pass located on the MacMahon Line at the tri-point borders of Arunachal Pradesh (India), China, and Myanmar.

### Q3

Nathu La Pass, an ancient offshoot of the historic Silk Route reopened for border trade in 2006, is located in which Indian state?

- [ ] Himachal Pradesh
- [ ] Uttarakhand
- [x] Sikkim
- [ ] Arunachal Pradesh

> Nathu La is situated at an elevation of 4,310 meters in East Sikkim district, connecting Sikkim with Tibet's Chumbi Valley.

Bara-lacha La pass in the Zanskar range connects which of the following two destinations?
