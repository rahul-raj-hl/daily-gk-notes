# 📌 Office, Removal & Election of the Vice-President of India

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- **Term of Office**: Holds office for a term of **five years from the date** on which he enters upon his office (Article 67).
- **Resignation**: Addressed to the **President of India** at any time.
- **Removal Procedure (Article 67b)**:
  - **No formal impeachment** is required (unlike the President under Article 61).
  - Can only be initiated in the **Rajya Sabha** (Council of States).
  - Must give at least **14 days' advance notice**.
  - Passed by the Rajya Sabha by an **effective majority** (a majority of all the then members) and agreed to by the Lok Sabha by a **simple majority**.
  - **No grounds for removal** are specified in the Constitution.
- **Electoral Body & Re-eligibility**:
  - State legislative assemblies (**MLAs**) do **NOT** participate in Vice-Presidential elections.
  - Eligible for **re-election for any number of terms** (e.g., Dr. S. Radhakrishnan, Mohammad Hamid Ansari).

## 📖 Main Concept

### 1. Comparative Analysis: President vs. Vice-President of India

*Key constitutional contrasts between the two highest executive offices:*

| Constitutional Dimension | President of India (Part V) | Vice-President of India (Part V) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Constitutional Article** | **Article 52** (Office) | **Article 63** (Office), **Article 64** (*Ex-officio* Chairman of RS) |
| **Electoral College Composition** | • Elected members of **both Houses of Parliament** (MPs).<br>• Elected members of **State Legislative Assemblies** (MLAs).<br>• Elected MLAs of **Delhi and Puducherry** (70th CAA). | • **Elected and Nominated members** of both Houses of Parliament (all MPs).<br>• **State MLAs do NOT participate**. |
| **Nominated Members' Role** | Cannot vote in the presidential election (can vote in impeachment). | **Can vote** in the election and removal. |
| **Term & Continuity** | 5 years; holds office until successor enters upon office. | 5 years; holds office until successor enters upon office. |
| **Resignation Submitted To** | **Vice-President** (communicated to Lok Sabha Speaker). | **President of India**. |
| **Removal / Impeachment Motion** | • Can originate in **either House** (LS or RS).<br>• Requires **Special Majority of total membership** ($2/3^{\text{rd}}$ of total House strength). | • Can originate **ONLY in Rajya Sabha**.<br>• Requires **Effective Majority in RS** + **Simple Majority in LS**. |
| **Grounds for Removal** | Specified: **"Violation of the Constitution"** (Article 61). | **No ground mentioned** in the Constitution. |
| **Advance Notice Period** | **14 days** | **14 days** |

### 2. Major Constitutional Articles Related to the Vice-President

*Key articles governing the office under Part V of the Constitution:*

- **Article 63**: There shall be a Vice-President of India.
- **Article 64**: Vice-President to be the *ex-officio* Chairman of the Council of States (Rajya Sabha).
- **Article 65**: Vice-President to act as President or discharge his functions during casual vacancies/absence.
- **Article 66**: Election of the Vice-President (Proportional Representation via Single Transferable Vote).
- **Article 67**: Term of office, resignation, and removal procedures.
- **Article 68**: Time of holding elections to fill a vacancy in the office.
- **Article 69**: Oath or affirmation by the Vice-President (administered by the President).

## 🎯 Memory Trick

> **Mnemonic: "VICE-PRESIDENT RUNS ON RS: RESIGNS TO PRESIDENT, REMOVED BY RS, RULES FOR 5 YEARS"**
> - **Runs on RS** $\rightarrow$ Initiated in Rajya Sabha by Effective Majority
> - **Resigns to President** $\rightarrow$ Resignation submitted to President
> - **Rules for 5 Years** $\rightarrow$ Standard 5-year tenure from assuming office

### PYQ 1

A resolution for the removal of the Vice-President of India can be initiated only in:

- [ ] Lok Sabha
- [x] Rajya Sabha
- [ ] Either House of Parliament
- [ ] A Joint Sitting of Parliament

> Because the Vice-President serves as the *ex-officio* Chairman of the Council of States, any motion for removal must originate exclusively in the Rajya Sabha under Article 67(b).

Who among the following participates in the election of the Vice-President of India?

- [ ] Only elected members of Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha
- [x] Both elected and nominated members of both Houses of Parliament
- [ ] Elected members of Parliament and State Legislative Assemblies
- [ ] Elected members of Lok Sabha only

> The Electoral College for the Vice-President consists of all members (both elected and nominated) of both the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.

- [ ] Article 61
- [ ] Article 63
- [x] Article 64
- [ ] Article 72

> Article 64 explicitly stipulates that the Vice-President of India shall be the *ex-officio* Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.

## 📝 Practice Questions

### Q1

What majority is required in the Lok Sabha to pass a resolution for the removal of the Vice-President after it has been approved by the Rajya Sabha?

- [ ] Special Majority under Article 368
- [ ] Two-thirds majority of total membership
- [x] Simple Majority (Majority of members present and voting)
- [ ] Absolute Majority

> Under Article 67(b), the Lok Sabha is only required to agree to the Rajya Sabha's removal resolution by a standard Simple Majority.

---

### Q2

What is the constitutional ground mentioned in the Constitution of India for the removal of the Vice-President?

- [ ] Proven misbehavior or incapacity
- [ ] Violation of the Constitution
- [ ] Insolvency and bankruptcy
- [x] No ground is mentioned in the Constitution

> Unlike the President (who can be impeached for "violation of the Constitution"), the Constitution does not mention any specific grounds for removing the Vice-President.

---

### Q3

Who administers the oath of office to the Vice-President of India under Article 69?

- [ ] Chief Justice of India
- [ ] Speaker of the Lok Sabha
- [x] President of India (or some person appointed by him)
- [ ] Prime Minister of India

> Under Article 69, every Vice-President takes and subscribes to an oath or affirmation before the President of India or someone appointed by the President.
