# 📌 Shivaji & the Deshmukhs

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Deshmukhs were powerful local feudal chiefs with administrative and military authority.
- Many Deshmukhs initially opposed Shivaji because they supported the Adil Shahi Sultanate of Bijapur.
- Shivaji aimed to establish a centralized Maratha administration based on Swarajya.
- Over time, Shivaji subdued or won over many Deshmukhs, strengthening the Maratha state.

---

## 🔑 Key Points

### 👥 Who were the Deshmukhs?

*One-liner:* Local hereditary chiefs responsible for administration, revenue collection, and military leadership.

- Controlled villages and large territories.
- Collected land revenue.
- Maintained local armed forces.
- Enjoyed considerable regional autonomy.

---

### ⚔️ Why did many Deshmukhs oppose Shivaji?

*One-liner:* They feared losing their traditional powers.

- Loyal to the Adil Shahi rulers of Bijapur.
- Opposed Shivaji's policy of centralized administration.
- Wanted to preserve their hereditary privileges.
- Many were reluctant to support the idea of Swarajya.

> **Note:** Not all Deshmukhs opposed Shivaji permanently. Many later accepted his authority and became part of the Maratha administration.

---

## 🏛️ Shivaji's Objective

*One-liner:* Establish an independent and well-organized Maratha kingdom.

- Build a centralized administration.
- Reduce the influence of feudal chiefs.
- Strengthen direct control over territories.
- Promote the idea of Swarajya (self-rule).
