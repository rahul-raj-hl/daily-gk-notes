# 📌 Finance Commission

## ⚡ Quick Summary

- Constitutional body established under Article 280.
- Recommends distribution of tax revenue between the Centre and States.
- Constituted by the President every 5 years.
- Recommendations are advisory in nature.

---

## 🔑 Key Points

### 📖 What is Finance Commission?
A constitutional body that recommends sharing of financial resources between the Union and States.

- Established under Article 280.
- Constituted by the President.
- Formed every 5 years or earlier if required.
- Ensures fiscal balance between Centre and States.

---

## 👥 Composition

- Chairperson + 4 Members
- Appointed by the President
- Qualifications prescribed by Parliament

---

## 🛠️ Functions

- Recommend distribution of net tax proceeds.
- Recommend Grants-in-Aid from Consolidated Fund of India.
- Recommend measures to strengthen finances of Panchayats.
- Recommend measures to strengthen finances of Municipalities.
- Advise on matters referred by the President.
